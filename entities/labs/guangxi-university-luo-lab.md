# Guangxi University — Luo Jijing Lab

## Summary

The Luo lab (PI: Professor Luo Jijing, 罗继景) at Guangxi University's College of Life Science and Technology and the State Key Laboratory for Conservation and Utilization of Subtropical Agro-bioresources is a rice functional genomics group focused on mining natural variation for abiotic stress tolerance. The lab's geographic location in Guangxi — the center of diversity for wild rice (*Oryza rufipogon*) in China — provides unique access to unexplored genetic variation. Their research program spans cold tolerance, drought tolerance, and bacterial leaf streak resistance, with a signature 2026 paper in *Plant Communications* characterizing the **OsMAPK3 → OsbHLH150 → OsNCED3 → ABA** cold tolerance cascade and a natural V471L SNP that explains japonica/indica chilling tolerance divergence. The lab has published 30+ SCI papers including in *Nature Genetics*, *The Plant Cell*, and *Nature Communications*.

---

## Research Focus

### Cold Tolerance in Rice — Primary Program

The lab's primary focus is the molecular genetics of rice chilling tolerance, leveraging natural variation from Guangxi wild rice collections.

**Key Discovery (2026) — OsMAPK3-OsbHLH150-OsNCED3 Cascade:**
- Map-based cloning from a CSSL population (Koshihikari × Nona Bokra) identified **OsbHLH150** as the causal gene for QTL CSS12b
- OsbHLH150 is phosphorylated by **OsMAPK3** at S669/S674/S734 under cold stress, stabilizing the protein
- Phosphorylated OsbHLH150 directly binds the E-box (CACATG) in the **OsNCED3** promoter, driving ABA biosynthesis
- A natural SNP causes **V471L**: Val (japonica) → strong transactivation + cold tolerance; Leu (indica) → weak transactivation + cold sensitivity
- Full genetic validation: KO, OE, complementation, double-mutant epistasis

Evidence: [[rice-chilling-tolerance]] — Luo et al. (2026), *Plant Communications*

### Drought Tolerance

Molecular genetic studies on drought tolerance genes from wild rice. Specific genes and mechanisms not yet detailed in public domain.

### Bacterial Leaf Streak Resistance

Genetic mapping and functional characterization of bacterial leaf streak (*Xanthomonas oryzae* pv. *oryzicola*) resistance genes from Guangxi wild rice.

## Techniques and Approaches

### Genetic Mapping and Cloning
- Map-based cloning from CSSL (chromosome segment substitution line) populations
- QTL mapping for abiotic stress tolerance traits
- Natural variation mining from Guangxi wild rice (*O. rufipogon*) collections

### Functional Genomics
- CRISPR/Cas9 knockout and overexpression in rice
- Complementation and double-mutant epistasis analysis

### Molecular Biology and Biochemistry
- Yeast two-hybrid (Y2H) for protein-protein interaction
- In vitro and in vivo phosphorylation assays
- Chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP)
- Luciferase (LUC) reporter assays for transcriptional activation
- Site-directed mutagenesis for functional variant characterization

### Population Genetics
- Haplotype analysis across japonica and indica accessions
- Natural variant (SNP) association with cold tolerance phenotypes

### Physiological Assays
- Seedling and booting stage cold tolerance phenotyping
- ABA quantification under cold stress

## Research Significance

### Scientific Impact
- **Cold tolerance mechanism**: Identified a complete kinase-TF-enzyme cascade (OsMAPK3 → OsbHLH150 → OsNCED3 → ABA) for cold tolerance — one of the most thoroughly characterized cold signaling pathways in rice.
- **Natural variation**: The V471L SNP provides a clear molecular explanation for the classic japonica/indica cold tolerance divergence.
- **Breeding potential**: The identified SNP and the OsbHLH150 allele are direct targets for marker-assisted breeding of cold-tolerant rice.

### Geographic and Strategic Value
- Guangxi wild rice represents an underexploited genetic reservoir for stress tolerance alleles.
- The lab's systematic mining of this resource could yield additional tolerance genes beyond cold.

## Open Questions

### Question 1: What other cold tolerance genes are in the lab's pipeline?
- **Why It Matters**: The OsbHLH150 cascade likely represents one of multiple cold tolerance pathways active in Guangxi wild rice.
- **Missing Evidence**: Additional QTLs and cloned genes beyond CSS12b/OsbHLH150.
- **Expected**: Further publications from the lab's ongoing wild rice screening program.

### Question 2: How generalizable is the OsbHLH150 allele across rice germplasm?
- **Why It Matters**: Determines the practical breeding utility of the V471L SNP.
- **Missing Evidence**: Haplotype analysis across diverse indica and japonica accessions beyond the CSSL parents; effect validation in elite breeding lines.
- **Suggested Experiment**: Genotype V471L across 500+ rice accessions; field cold tolerance phenotyping of near-isogenic lines.

### Question 3: What are the lab's specific drought and disease resistance findings?
- **Why It Matters**: The lab lists drought and bacterial leaf streak as research programs, but specific genes and mechanisms are not yet in this knowledge base.
- **Missing Evidence**: Publications or preprints describing drought/disease resistance gene cloning.
- **Expected**: Future publications from these ongoing programs.
