### 核心发现
1. We show that the device can preserve protoplast viability for at least two weeks, allowing cell expansion, cell wall formation, and cell division for both Arabidopsis and maize.
2. We found that maize cells in more crowded conditions divided more fre- quently than cells with single or fewer than four cells per cup.
3. The gap between adjacent microposts is 5 μm, which we found enables protoplast trapping while allowing continuous perfusion of culture medium through and around the trapped cells.
4. To assess whether the microﬂuidic device could support visu- alization of cell wall reconstitution, root protoplasts of Arabidopsis were cultured in the device, and calcoﬂuor white (5 μg/mL), which binds to the cellulosic polymers of the cell wall, was added to W5++ medium for imaging under UV excitation.
5. This consistent ﬂow of media is critical for maintaining viability and promoting the growth of protoplasts (Yu et al., 2018).
