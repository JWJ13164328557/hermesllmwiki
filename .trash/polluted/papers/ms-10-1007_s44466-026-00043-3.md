### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these findings suggest a therapeutic rationale for targeting ­PGE2 or IL-1β to disrupt the feed-forward loop between inflam- matory PDAC cell reprogramming and IL-1β+ TAMs.
2. Finally, we summarize the recent progress in cancer immunotherapy—including immune checkpoint blockade, engineered T-cell and macrophage therapies, and personalized cancer vaccines—and discuss emerging strategies that target specific TME components to over- come primary and acquired resistance.
3. Moreover, longitudinal profiling under therapeutic pres- sure has elucidated dynamic immune adaptations asso- ciated with resistance to ICB [18], providing a rational basis for combination strategies that target both immune cells and stromal components of the TME.
4. Collectively, CXCL13⁺ Th1-like CD4⁺ T cells serve as a pivotal bridge coordinating innate and adaptive antitu- mor immunity.
5. In certain set- tings, Th17-polarized or Th17-derived CD4⁺ T cells can acquire granzyme B–dependent cytotoxicity and directly kill MHC-II⁺ target cells, including tumor cells in experi- mental models [62, 63].
6. These findings suggest that Th17- driven responses can either enhance or impair antitumor immunity depending on the cytokine balance and tumor immunogenicity.
7. Collectively, these mechanisms create a potent immuno- suppressive niche that enables tumor persistence.
8. Collectively, within the TME, T cells exhibit strik- ing heterogeneity and functional specialization.
