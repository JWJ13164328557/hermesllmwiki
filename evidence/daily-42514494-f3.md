# While 2,4-D is essential for callus induction and the acquisition of embryogenic …

## Claim
While 2,4-D is essential for callus induction and the acquisition of embryogenic competence, excessive concentrations can maintain cells in a highly proliferative and undifferentiated state, thereby delaying their progression toward embryo maturation and shoot formation and increasing the abnormalities in developing somatic embryos [ ].
