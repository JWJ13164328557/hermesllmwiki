# Management can shift cultivars along this yield–protein–NUE spectrum, with intensive …

## Claim
Management can shift cultivars along this yield–protein–NUE spectrum, with intensive fertiga tion with modest compost (F4O1) generating the highest grain protein for Sids-14 and Misr-1, whereas intensive fertigation with high compost (F4O3) maximizes NUE and carbohydrate yield for Sakha-95 and Misr-1.
