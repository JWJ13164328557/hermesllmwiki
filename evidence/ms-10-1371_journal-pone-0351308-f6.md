# Syncytin-1 and syncytin-2, encoded by the HERV-W and HERV-FRD families, respectively, possess fusoge

## Claim
Syncytin-1 and syncytin-2, encoded by the HERV-W and HERV-FRD families, respectively, possess fusogenic properties that are crucial for trophoblast fusion and placental development.
