# In plants with crassulacean acid metabolism, PHS1 also plays a critical role in starch degradation i

## Claim
In plants with crassulacean acid metabolism, PHS1 also plays a critical role in starch degradation in the chloroplast, and compared to the hydrolytic breakdown of starch (amylase) into maltose and glucose, this phosphorolytic degradation of starch (glucan phosphorylase) offers energy advantages [15].
