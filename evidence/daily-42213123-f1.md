# Our data suggest that (i) proteolytic cleavages in both legumins and vicilins occur at …

## Claim
Our data suggest that (i) proteolytic cleavages in both legumins and vicilins occur at conserved sequence motifs; (ii) legumin processing is catalyzed by an asparaginyl endopeptidase, whereas vicilin processing is mediated by legumains, a family of cysteine endopeptidases; and (iii) one chickpea vicilin is N-glycosylated and carries a high-mannose oligosaccharide side chain.
