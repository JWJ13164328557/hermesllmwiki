## Claim
In summary, we conclude that in young ovaries, gonadotropins syn- chronize follicle cohorts to coordinate progression towards ovulation or atresia, yielding immature antral follicles at estrus.

## Biological Context
Aging disrupts spatiotemporal coordination in the cycling murine ovary
