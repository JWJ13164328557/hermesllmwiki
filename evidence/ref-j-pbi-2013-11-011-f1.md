# Importantly, expression of both PIN1 and DR5 diminishes in lfy mutant ﬂoral primordia, increases aft

## Claim
Importantly, expression of both PIN1 and DR5 diminishes in lfy mutant ﬂoral primordia, increases after inducible LFY overexpression and additionally, LFY directly promotes expression of the PINOID (PID) kinase that controls PIN1 activity [37,38].

## Biological Context
Signaling in shoot and flower meristems of Arabidopsis thaliana.
