# Together with the binding and uptake measurements, these data show that TfR1 is not required for ini

## Claim
Together with the binding and uptake measurements, these data show that TfR1 is not required for initial virion attachment but must be engaged for subsequent internalization.

## Biological Context
Transferrin receptor 1 binds human parvovirus B19 VP1u to facilitate entry
