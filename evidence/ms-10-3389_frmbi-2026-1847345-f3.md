# This hormone then travels to the liver, where it binds to its receptor and suppresses the liver-spec

## Claim
This hormone then travels to the liver, where it binds to its receptor and suppresses the liver-speciﬁc 7a hydroxylase (CYP7A1) synthesis, thereby establishing a feedback loop that regulates and reduces bile acid production (Joyce and Gahan, 2016).
