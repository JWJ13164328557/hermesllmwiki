# Notably, nanovaccines constructed from lipid-polymer hybrids, biomimetic membranes, and stimulus-res

## Claim
Notably, nanovaccines constructed from lipid-polymer hybrids, biomimetic membranes, and stimulus-responsive materials enable codelivery of neoantigens and immunostimulatory agonists, promoting enhanced lymph node targeting, dendritic cell activation, and antigen cross-presentation.
