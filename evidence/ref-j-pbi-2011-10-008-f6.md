# This study reveals that a transient increase in WUS levels within the CZ leads to a sequential expan

## Claim
This study reveals that a transient increase in WUS levels within the CZ leads to a sequential expansion of the stem cell identity and an increase in cell division rates in stem cell progenitors.

## Biological Context
