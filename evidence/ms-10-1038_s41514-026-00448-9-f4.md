# As higher plasma polyamine levels are associated with increased risk of poststroke cognitive impairm

## Claim
As higher plasma polyamine levels are associated with increased risk of poststroke cognitive impairment (PSCI) among patients with ischemic stroke, plasma polyamines may be implicated in the pathophysiologic processes of PSCI and represent potential intervention targets for PSCI.

## Biological Context
Geroprotective insights into the natural metabolite spermidine in aging and age-related diseases
