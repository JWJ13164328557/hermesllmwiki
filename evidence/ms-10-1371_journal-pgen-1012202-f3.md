# Together, these findings reveal Rv3839-Rv3840 as proteins involved in the downregulation of heme bio

## Claim
Together, these findings reveal Rv3839-Rv3840 as proteins involved in the downregulation of heme biosynthesis under NO stress and iron limitation, and highlight the link between Mtb growth control in response to NO/ low iron and endogenous heme biosynthesis.

## Biological Context
Rv3839-Rv3840 links the endogenous heme biosynthesis pathway with Mycobacterium tuberculosis adaptation to nitric oxide and iron limitation stress
