# We analyze material-based strategies for modulating senescence-associated phenotypes, including mech

## Claim
We analyze material-based strategies for modulating senescence-associated phenotypes, including mechanobiological approaches, such as tunable stress relaxation to attenuate pathological mechanical memory, and biochemical interventions that target reactive oxygen species via nanozyme catalysis and modulate SASP signaling through spatiotemporal delivery control.

## Biological Context
