# More interestingly, both MYB44 and ENAP1 preferred binding to their shared target genes over binding

## Claim
More interestingly, both MYB44 and ENAP1 preferred binding to their shared target genes over binding to the genes that were uniquely targeted by MYB44 or ENAP1 (Fig 4B and 4C).

## Biological Context
MYB44-ENAP1/2 restricts HDT4 to regulate drought tolerance in Arabidopsis.
