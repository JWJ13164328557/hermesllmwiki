# Previous structural work on Ube2S has shown that Ube2S conjugated to an Ub molecule can interact wit

## Claim
Previous structural work on Ube2S has shown that Ube2S conjugated to an Ub molecule can interact with a free Ub molecule is such a way that it orients Lys11 of the free Ub toward the catalytic cysteine of Ube2S (Wickliffe et al., 2011).
