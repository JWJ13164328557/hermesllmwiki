# Taken together, this study shows that WUS performs both short-range function of maintaining stem cel

## Claim
Taken together, this study shows that WUS performs both short-range function of maintaining stem cell number, and long-range functions of restricting cell division rates and differentiation of stem cell progenitors.

## Biological Context
Stem cell maintenance in shoot apical meristems.
