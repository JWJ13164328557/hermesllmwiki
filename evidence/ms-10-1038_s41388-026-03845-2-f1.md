# Importantly, pharmacological inhibition of CDK1 with RO-3306 or AZD5438, as well as siRNA- mediated 

## Claim
Importantly, pharmacological inhibition of CDK1 with RO-3306 or AZD5438, as well as siRNA- mediated CDK1 knockdown, signiﬁcantly reduced caspase-7 cleavage and DNA damage, demonstrating that CDK1 activation is necessary for the apoptotic response.

## Biological Context
DNA replication stress and translational repression converge to drive CDK1- and caspase-dependent apoptosis in Ewing sarcoma
