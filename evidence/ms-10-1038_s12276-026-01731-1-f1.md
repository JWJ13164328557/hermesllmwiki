# Taken together, while TfR1 levels and Tf uptake appear to be compensated in iRhom KO cells, ADAM10/A

## Claim
Taken together, while TfR1 levels and Tf uptake appear to be compensated in iRhom KO cells, ADAM10/ADAM17 KOs have a profound effect on TfR1 total protein levels, TfR1 surface levels and, importantly, Tf uptake, which cannot be compensated by downregulation of TFRC gene expression.

## Biological Context
Transferrin receptor 1 shedding by the pro-inflammatory iRhom–ADAM17 complex and ADAM10 regulates cellular iron uptake and ferroptosis
