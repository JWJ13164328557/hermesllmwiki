# In this study, we found that the phenotypes of 5-year-old ginkgo trees from low-altitude (LA) Pizhou

## Claim
In this study, we found that the phenotypes of 5-year-old ginkgo trees from low-altitude (LA) Pizhou and high-altitude (HA) Qinghai-Tibet Plateau (Nyingchi) differed significantly, and that ginkgo leaves from HA-grown trees had significantly higher contents of total flavonoids and flavonol glycosides than did leaves from LA-grown trees.
