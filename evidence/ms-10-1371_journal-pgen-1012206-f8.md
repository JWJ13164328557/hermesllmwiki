# We found that while the wild-type tti1 integrant was resistant to HU and MMS, two separate colonies 

## Claim
We found that while the wild-type tti1 integrant was resistant to HU and MMS, two separate colonies from the N18 integration were sensitive to HU and MMS (Fig 2C, top panels).

## Biological Context
A tti1 mutation in the Tel2-Tti1-Tti2 complex specifically eliminates the cellular function of Rad3ATR, but not that of other PIKKs in fission yeast
