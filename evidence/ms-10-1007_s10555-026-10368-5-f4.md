# These findings support evaluating measurable metabolic dependencies as therapeu- tic targets alongsi

## Claim
These findings support evaluating measurable metabolic dependencies as therapeu- tic targets alongside, or instead of, direct inhibition of core EMT transcription factors; however, candidate dependencies should be validated longitudinally and with sufficient cell- type resolution to distinguish carcinoma cell requirements from those of stromal and immune populations.

## Biological Context
Epithelial–mesenchymal transition in carcinoma: navigating phenotypic states to target resistance and metastasis
