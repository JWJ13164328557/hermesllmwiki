# These genes, which are the target of PbrNSC, are known to be involved in the lignin and cellulose bi

## Claim
These genes, which are the target of PbrNSC, are known to be involved in the lignin and cellulose biosynthesis in stone cells (Figure S2b; Wang et al., 2021), and the reduced expression of these genes after NAA treatment was conﬁrmed by quantitative real-time polymerase chain reaction (qRT-PCR) analyses (Figure 1g).

## Biological Context
