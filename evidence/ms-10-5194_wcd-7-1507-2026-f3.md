# Surface winds associated with oceanic cyclones are crucial for air-sea interactions …

## Claim
Surface winds associated with oceanic cyclones are crucial for air-sea interactions, generating ocean waves which help determine surface roughness (Donelan et al., 1993), and controlling turbulent fluxes of energy (Hsiung, 1986) and CO2 (Wanninkhof and McGillis, 1999) between the atmosphere and the ocean.
