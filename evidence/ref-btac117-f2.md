# We show that the Inferelator 3.0 is a state-of-the-art method by testing against SCENIC and CellOrac

## Claim
We show that the Inferelator 3.0 is a state-of-the-art method by testing against SCENIC and CellOracle on model organisms with reliable ground truth networks, and show that the Inferelator 3.0 can generate a mouse neuronal GRN from a publicly available dataset containing 1.3 million cells.
