# To determine whether DPML@OVA-CpGs could effec- tively target the PPs, we fabricated a carboxymethyl

## Claim
To determine whether DPML@OVA-CpGs could effec- tively target the PPs, we fabricated a carboxymethyl cellulose-modiﬁed nanovaccine (CPML@OVA-CpG) as a control and compared its tar- geting performance with those of PML@OVA-CpGs and DPML@OVA- CpGs.

## Biological Context
A dual-site oral nanovaccine programs anti-colorectal tumor immunity from small intestinal priming to large intestinal boosting
