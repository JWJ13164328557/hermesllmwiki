# Another target gene of WUS is CLV1, but its expression is repressed by WUS, not activated as predict

## Claim
Another target gene of WUS is CLV1, but its expression is repressed by WUS, not activated as predicted by the CLV–WUS feedback model; therefore, this regulation is likely only for the fine-tuning of CLV1 levels (15).
