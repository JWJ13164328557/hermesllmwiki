# Nuclear IL-33 interacts with the NF-kB p65 subunit and functions as a key transcriptional co-activat

## Claim
Nuclear IL-33 interacts with the NF-kB p65 subunit and functions as a key transcriptional co-activator, enhancing p65 occupancy at the promoters of the neutrophil-recruiting chemokines CXCL1 and IL-8.

## Biological Context
Nuclear IL-33 amplifies NF-κB signaling to drive neutrophil recruitment and activation in the endocervical epithelium during Mycoplasma genitalium infection
