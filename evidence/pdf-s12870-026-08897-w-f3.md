# These results suggest that key downstream flavonoid pathway genes (e.g., CHS2, F3H, HST, CHS1A, BEAT

## Claim
These results suggest that key downstream flavonoid pathway genes (e.g., CHS2, F3H, HST, CHS1A, BEATH, CHIL2, F3'5'H, FGGT1, and U79B6) may be regulated by miR156a-StSPL9 module, potentially contributing to the increased accumulation of hesperetin, kaempferol, and quercetin in potato tubers.

## Biological Context
miR156-StSPL9 regulating the biosynthesis of flavonoids and secondary metabolites in potato tubers
