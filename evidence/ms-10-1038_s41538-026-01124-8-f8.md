# In summary, this study systematically delineates the dynamic land- scape of ﬂavor-related VOCs in CR

## Claim
In summary, this study systematically delineates the dynamic land- scape of ﬂavor-related VOCs in CRC ﬂavedo across the commercial harvest cycle, identiﬁes the S1-to-S2 transition as the critical window for aroma formation, and proposes potential transcriptional regulatory mechanisms underlying fatty acid-derived VOC biosynthesis.

## Biological Context
Volatilomic and transcriptomic analyses uncover the mechanisms underlying flavor-related VOC changes during the harvest period of Citrus reticulata ‘Chachi’
