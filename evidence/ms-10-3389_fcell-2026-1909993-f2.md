# Based primarily on in vitro expression models, it is proposed that ZAKβ can bind to ZAKα through het

## Claim
Based primarily on in vitro expression models, it is proposed that ZAKβ can bind to ZAKα through heterodimerization and promote the downregulation of ZAKα protein levels, thereby acting as an endogenous attenuator of its signaling output (Fu et al., 2016).
