# Found that the reason for white appearance of ‘Jinba’ at the blooming stage was that the …

## Claim
Found that the reason for white appearance of ‘Jinba’ at the blooming stage was that the R3 MYB transcription factor CmMYB#7, which can interact with CmbHLH2, disrupted the MBW protein complex: CmMYB6-CmbHLH2, which is necessary for the anthocyanin biosynthesis of chrysanthemum, thus blocking the anthocyanin biosynthesis in white petals [15].
