# In PFAS-contaminated systems, HAs may interact with PFAS through competing mechanisms …

## Claim
In PFAS-contaminated systems, HAs may interact with PFAS through competing mechanisms: PFAS-DOM complexation can reduce the freely available fraction through hydrophobic and electrostatic associations, while competition for sorption sites may alter adsorption-desorption dynamics and mobility in the soil matrix (Mei et al., 2021; Qi et al., 2022).
