# These results indicate that the blue light-CRYs module acts as a dual regulator in the hypocotyl, fu

## Claim
These results indicate that the blue light-CRYs module acts as a dual regulator in the hypocotyl, functioning as a suppressor in seedlings and as an activator in adult hypocotyls depending on the developmental stages in the context of SCW formation.
