# For instance, nano-formulations can facilitate the targeted delivery of agrochemicals, reducing requ

## Claim
For instance, nano-formulations can facilitate the targeted delivery of agrochemicals, reducing required dosages and minimizing off-target effects, while certain nanoparticles themselves can directly scavenge reactive oxygen species or stimulate antioxidant defenses to alleviate abiotic stress [57, 153].

## Biological Context
Enhancing crop stress resilience and agricultural sustainability with nanoparticle applications
