# In this context, NADPH is essential for antioxidant defence through the regeneration of reduced glut

## Claim
In this context, NADPH is essential for antioxidant defence through the regeneration of reduced glutathione (GSH) from oxidized gluta­ thione (GSSG), and disruption of this axis has been shown to critically affect cellular responses to ionizing radiation.

## Biological Context
Isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH) as target for anticancer therapy in brain and gastrointestinal cancers
