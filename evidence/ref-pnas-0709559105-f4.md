# After standardizing the analysis of all experiments and setting liberal criteria to detect as many g

## Claim
After standardizing the analysis of all experiments and setting liberal criteria to detect as many genes as possible [dChip normalization, t test filter (P  0.05), FDR analysis (q  5%)], we found that cell sorting uncovers a much larger response than the 1,000–2,000 nitrogen-responsive genes reported in previous studies or found in our own whole-root experiments.

## Biological Context
Cell-specific nitrogen responses mediate developmental plasticity.
