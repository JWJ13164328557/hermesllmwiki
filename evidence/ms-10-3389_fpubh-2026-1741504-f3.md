# Interventions to address mental health lit­ eracy can thus include those aiming to increase awarenes

## Claim
Interventions to address mental health lit­ eracy can thus include those aiming to increase awareness and education (proximal outcomes) as well as those improving access to care such as behaviour therapy and counselling though self- help, telemedicine or peer-to-peer programmes, which could more directly target distal outcomes (improving mental health functioning) (33).

## Biological Context
Public health interventions to improve the mental health and mental health literacy of young people in resource-limited settings: a systematic review
