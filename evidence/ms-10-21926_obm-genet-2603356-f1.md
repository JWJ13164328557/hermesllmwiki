# DREB proteins bind to Dehydration-Responsive Element/C-Repeat (DRE/CRT) cis-elements in the promoter

## Claim
DREB proteins bind to Dehydration-Responsive Element/C-Repeat (DRE/CRT) cis-elements in the promoters of stress-responsive genes, activating their expression under drought and salinity stress [22, 27].
