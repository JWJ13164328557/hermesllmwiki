# Notably, Fc-disabled soluble HVEM fusion proteins may offer a safer modulation strategy by selective

## Claim
Notably, Fc-disabled soluble HVEM fusion proteins may offer a safer modulation strategy by selectively disrupting BTLA/HVEM interactions without engaging FcgR-mediated effector functions, thereby reducing the risk of cytokine release syndrome, thereby avoiding direct killing of HVEM-expressing hepatocytes or stromal cells and reducing the risk of cytokine release syndrome (36–38).

## Biological Context
The BTLA/HVEM axis as a key regulator for stratified functional cure in chronic hepatitis B
