# Consistent with this, we found that, although the absolute cellular levels of Tsix and …

## Claim
Consistent with this, we found that, although the absolute cellular levels of Tsix and Linx were correlated between different cells (Figure S5J), in fact Linx and Tsix were slightly, but significantly, anticorrelated in their expression levels in cis (Figure S5K), with Tsix being more transcribed on the allele showing lower Linx transcription and vice versa.
