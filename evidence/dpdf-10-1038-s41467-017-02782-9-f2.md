# Our results suggest not only that different modes of pectin breakdown and associated textural change

## Claim
Our results suggest not only that different modes of pectin breakdown and associated textural changes occur in different tissues, but that canonical ripening-related pectin disassembly initiates in internal tissues at a stage that is ostensibly “pre-ripe” based on external pericarp coloration.

## Biological Context
High-resolution spatiotemporal transcriptome mapping of tomato fruit development and ripening
