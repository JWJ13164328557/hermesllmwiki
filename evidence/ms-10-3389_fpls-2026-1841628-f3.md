# Under the screening condition of 400 ng·mL⁻¹ Aureobasidin A (AbA), yeast cells co-transformed with S

## Claim
Under the screening condition of 400 ng·mL⁻¹ Aureobasidin A (AbA), yeast cells co-transformed with SmWRKY4 and ProSmAPX2 grew normally, whereas the negative control did not (Figure 7C), indicating that SmWRKY4 could bind to the tested SmAPX2 promoter fragment.

## Biological Context
Integrated physiological and multi-omics analyses identify a SmWRKY4–SmAPX2 regulatory module associated with cold adaptation in Sapindus mukorossi
