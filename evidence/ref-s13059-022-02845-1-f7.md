# Among the HD-ZIP III TFs in Arabidopsis, AtHB8 is sufficient to induce the func- tion of organizer c

## Claim
Among the HD-ZIP III TFs in Arabidopsis, AtHB8 is sufficient to induce the func- tion of organizer cells derived from vascular cambium to determine xylem identity [8], and HD-ZIP III was reported to participate in interfascicular cambium development in Populus [51].

## Biological Context
Single-cell transcriptomics unveils xylem cell development and evolution.
