# Here we show that RNF220 ablation triggers severe synapsis disruption at pachytene and germ cell apo

## Claim
Here we show that RNF220 ablation triggers severe synapsis disruption at pachytene and germ cell apoptosis, thereby recapitulating the phenotype of Syce1 haploinsufficient mice [13].

## Biological Context
RNF220 safeguards meiotic synapsis and male fertility by antagonizing SYCE1 ubiquitination
