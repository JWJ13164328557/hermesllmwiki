# Here we show that the ADP/ATP-activated Gq-coupled receptor P2Y1 drives astrocyte-mediated neurotoxi

## Claim
Here we show that the ADP/ATP-activated Gq-coupled receptor P2Y1 drives astrocyte-mediated neurotoxicity in experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis (EAE), a model of multiple sclerosis (MS).

## Biological Context
Neuroglial P2Y1 receptor signalling differentially contributes to inflammatory neurodegeneration
