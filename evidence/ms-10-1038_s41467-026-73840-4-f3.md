# The mouse liver circadian proteome is enriched for well-characterized secreted proteins6,7 and a rec

## Claim
The mouse liver circadian proteome is enriched for well-characterized secreted proteins6,7 and a recent study showed that at the transcript and protein level, the clock protein BMAL1 specifically targets and regulates proteins annotated as secreted8.

## Biological Context
Timed secreted proteomes reveal regulation of hepatokines by the liver circadian clock
