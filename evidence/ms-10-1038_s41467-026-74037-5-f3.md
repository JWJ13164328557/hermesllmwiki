# Here, we show that CD31, a recognized endothelial marker, is predominantly expressed in microglia bu

## Claim
Here, we show that CD31, a recognized endothelial marker, is predominantly expressed in microglia but not in neurons or astrocytes, and it is significantly elevated in the brains of AD patients and mouse models.

## Biological Context
Microglial CD31 suppresses Aβ clearance and promotes Alzheimer pathology in 5×FAD mice
