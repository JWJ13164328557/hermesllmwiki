# Yet, it emerges that the impact of 3′ UTR APA on miRNA-mediated regulation of mR- NAs is …

## Claim
Yet, it emerges that the impact of 3′ UTR APA on miRNA-mediated regulation of mR- NAs is more complicated than mere inclusion/exclusion of miRNA target sites in/from the 3′ UTR, as the efficiency of mRNA targeting is also affected by the location of the miRNA target site––sites located at the start or end of the 3′ UTRs are more efficient than those located in the middle.
