# Specifically, AtNAP, a well-characterized NAC transcription factor, can directly bind to the promote

## Claim
Specifically, AtNAP, a well-characterized NAC transcription factor, can directly bind to the promoter of AAO3 (ALDEHYDE OXIDASE 3), a key enzyme in ABA biosynthesis, thereby enhancing the transcription of chlorophyll-degrading genes and forming a regulatory loop that amplifies ABA-mediated senescence signals (Yang et al.
