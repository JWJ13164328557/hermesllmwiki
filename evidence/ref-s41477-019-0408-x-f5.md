# Our results suggest that a dynamic JA wave cooperates with histone methylation to upregulate a pulse

## Claim
Our results suggest that a dynamic JA wave cooperates with histone methylation to upregulate a pulse of auxin production and promote DNRR in response to wounding.

## Biological Context
Jasmonate-mediated wound signalling promotes plant regeneration.
