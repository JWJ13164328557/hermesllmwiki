# Consequently, PD-1 blockade reinvigorates the progenitor pool but cannot rescue terminally exhausted

## Claim
Consequently, PD-1 blockade reinvigorates the progenitor pool but cannot rescue terminally exhausted cells, whereas BTLA blockade targets the terminal subset and may interrupt the TPEX-to-TEX differentiation trajectory, preserving the progenitor pool that PD-1 blockade relies upon.

## Biological Context
