# Here we report the half-life extension of LSN and LSN-containing chimeric lysins by fusion to a huma

## Claim
Here we report the half-life extension of LSN and LSN-containing chimeric lysins by fusion to a human immunoglobulin G1 (IgG1) Fc, using a previously described chassis which incorporates a helical linker (Badarau et al., 2026).

## Biological Context
A chimeric, half-life extended lysin with a unique mode of action
