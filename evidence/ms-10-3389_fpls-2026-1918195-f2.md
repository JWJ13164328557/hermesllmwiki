# Collectively, these ﬁndings outline a biphasic auxin response: an initial broad suppression followed

## Claim
Collectively, these ﬁndings outline a biphasic auxin response: an initial broad suppression followed by the speciﬁc activation of key transporters and PhARF5, which is critical for regeneration.

## Biological Context
Cytokinin-supplemented medium drives de novo shoot regeneration in petunia (Petunia hybrida) by bypassing the root-meristem program
