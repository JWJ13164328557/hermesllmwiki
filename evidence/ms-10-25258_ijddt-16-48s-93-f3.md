# Overall, the present study provides strong experimental evidence that oxidative stress plays a centr

## Claim
Overall, the present study provides strong experimental evidence that oxidative stress plays a central role in fluoride- induced renal injury and that Curcuma longa effectively mitigates this damage through multiple mechanisms, including antioxidant enhancement, inhibition of lipid peroxidation, membrane stabilization, and restoration of enzymatic function.
