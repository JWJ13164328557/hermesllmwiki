# Luciferase reporter and ChIP-qPCR assays further revealed that HDAC5 interacts with myocyte enhancer

## Claim
Luciferase reporter and ChIP-qPCR assays further revealed that HDAC5 interacts with myocyte enhancer factor 2A (MEF2A) and restrains its binding to the Smad7 promoter, thereby suppressing Smad7 transcription (66).

## Biological Context
Histone modifications in skin fibrosis: linking immune dysregulation, metabolic reprogramming, and persistent fibrotic remodeling
