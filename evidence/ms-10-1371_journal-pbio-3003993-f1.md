# We found that the progression of maturation from the tip of the leaf downward was delayed in jaw-D r

## Claim
We found that the progression of maturation from the tip of the leaf downward was delayed in jaw-D relative to wild type based on several markers of maturation, in agreement with the role of TCP transcription factors in pro­ moting maturation.

## Biological Context
Growth-rate coordination across the width of a leaf preserves its flatness
