# Using a hollow-fibre infection model to replicate human plasma and urinary pharmacokinetics, we show

## Claim
Using a hollow-fibre infection model to replicate human plasma and urinary pharmacokinetics, we show that plasma-like exposures rapidly select for pre-existing resistant subpopulations; whereas, urinary exposures produce sustained bactericidal activity without resistance emergence.

## Biological Context
Molecular pharmacodynamics of amoxicillin-clavulanic acid for urinary tract infections caused by Escherichia coli
