# Rather than directly activating the receptor, PAMs bind to distinct allosteric sites and selectively

## Claim
Rather than directly activating the receptor, PAMs bind to distinct allosteric sites and selectively enhance the activity of endogenous opioid peptides, thereby preserving natural spatiotemporal signalling patterns while potentially circumventing some limitations inherent to orthosteric agonists30.

## Biological Context
Structure-guided allosteric modulation of the delta opioid receptor
