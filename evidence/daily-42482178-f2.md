# Upon perceiving extracellular cues, MAPKKKs phosphorylate and activate MAPKKs; the latter …

## Claim
Upon perceiving extracellular cues, MAPKKKs phosphorylate and activate MAPKKs; the latter then dually phosphorylate conserved threonine and tyrosine residues within the TXY motif of the MAPK activation loop, thereby triggering downstream MAPK activity [ ].
