# In addition to the known regulators of the leaf vein, we also found novel marker genes, such as HEAV

## Claim
In addition to the known regulators of the leaf vein, we also found novel marker genes, such as HEAVY METAL ASSOCIATED ISOPRENYLATED PLANT PROTEIN 36 (HIPP36), ARABIDOPSIS THALIANA HISTIDINE-CONTAINING PHOSPHOTRANSMITTER 1 (AHP1), TET6 and PHL12 for CC, ARABINOGALACTAN PROTEIN 31 (AGP31) and CELLULASE 1 (CEL1) for XP, DOF2.4 for SE and IRX7, CDF4, SULTR2;1 and BZIP9 for PP (Figures 1 and S4).
