# The Arabidopsis ge- nome encodes several SWEET paralogs, includ- ing the closely related transporter

## Claim
The Arabidopsis ge- nome encodes several SWEET paralogs, includ- ing the closely related transporters AtSWEET10, 13, 14, and 15, which we show here to function as sucrose transporters.

## Biological Context
Sucrose efflux mediated by SWEET proteins as a key step for phloem transport.
