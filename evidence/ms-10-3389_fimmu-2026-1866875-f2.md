# In the later stages of clear cell RCC, second-line treatment options include VEGFR TKIs, mechanistic

## Claim
In the later stages of clear cell RCC, second-line treatment options include VEGFR TKIs, mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR) inhibitors, and belzutifan, a hypoxia inducible factor-2a (HIF-2a) inhibitor (8).

## Biological Context
Obesity-driven metabolic reprogramming and immune dysfunction in renal cancer
