# The upregulation of terpenoid backbone biosynthesis and pyruvate metabolism does not …

## Claim
The upregulation of terpenoid backbone biosynthesis and pyruvate metabolism does not necessarily contradict the reduced carotenoid accumulation, because the terpenoid backbone pathway supplies precursors not only for carotenoids but also for other terpenoids, including the phytol tail of chlorophyll, sterols, and various phytohormones.
