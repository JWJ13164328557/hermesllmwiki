# Notably, although these formulations differed and achieved different organoid formation efficiencies

## Claim
Notably, although these formulations differed and achieved different organoid formation efficiencies, they all included Noggin and RSPO1, growth factors that are essential to maintain stemness.

## Biological Context
An empirically refined workflow for generating patient-derived organoids from epithelial ovarian cancer
