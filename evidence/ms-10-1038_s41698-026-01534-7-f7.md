# However, to broaden the scope beyond binary identification of expression-based prognostic markers an

## Claim
However, to broaden the scope beyond binary identification of expression-based prognostic markers and toward the identification of therapeutic targets as well, here, we used the Z-score-based statistical approach and identified a gene expression-based candidate prognostic marker, BEN domain-containing protein 4 (BEND4).

## Biological Context
Discovery of BEND4 as a novel single-gene prognostic marker and therapeutic target for adverse AML
