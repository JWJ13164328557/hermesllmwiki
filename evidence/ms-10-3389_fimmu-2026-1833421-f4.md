# For example, in colorectal cancer (CRC), circ_0075829 targets miR- 330-5p to upregulate TCF4, simult

## Claim
For example, in colorectal cancer (CRC), circ_0075829 targets miR- 330-5p to upregulate TCF4, simultaneously inhibiting ferroptosis (via GPX4/xCT upregulation) and increasing PD-L1, impairing CD8+ T cell function and promoting immune escape (19).

## Biological Context
Circular RNAs as molecular bridges: dual regulation of ferroptosis and immunity in cancer
