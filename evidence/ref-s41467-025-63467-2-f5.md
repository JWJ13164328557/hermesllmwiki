# In agreement with this hypothesis, we found that drought recovery-induced activation of immune syste

## Claim
In agreement with this hypothesis, we found that drought recovery-induced activation of immune system genes leads to reduced disease severity and bacterial load in infected leaves of both Arabidopsis and two tomato species.

## Biological Context
Drought recovery in plants triggers a cell-state-specific immune activation.
