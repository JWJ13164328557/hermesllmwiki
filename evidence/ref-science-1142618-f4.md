# To determine if AtRHD6 and AtRSL1 are required for the development of the only other …

## Claim
To determine if AtRHD6 and AtRSL1 are required for the development of the only other tip-growing cell in flowering plants, the pollen tube, we characterized the phenotypes of pollen tubes in Atrhd6-3, Atrsl1-1, and Atrhd6-3 Atrsl1-1 mutants both in vitro and in vivo.
