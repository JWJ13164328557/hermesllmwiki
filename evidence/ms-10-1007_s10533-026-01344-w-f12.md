# Our results show that the observed shift in microbial guilds likely favoured decomposer groups adapt

## Claim
Our results show that the observed shift in microbial guilds likely favoured decomposer groups adapted to resource limitation, where efficient growth and substrate uptake were promoted (Roller and Schmidt 2015).

## Biological Context
Wildfire and post-fire management reshape soil microbial guilds and carbon dynamics at a boreal forest site in Sweden
