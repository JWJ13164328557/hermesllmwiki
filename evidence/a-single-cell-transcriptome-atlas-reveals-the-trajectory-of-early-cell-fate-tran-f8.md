# QC-like cells are undifferentiated pluripotent acquired cells that appear in the early stages of cal

## Claim
QC-like cells are undifferentiated pluripotent acquired cells that appear in the early stages of callus formation and play a critical role in later callus development and organ regeneration.

## Biological Context
A single-cell transcriptome atlas reveals the trajectory of early cell fate transition during callus induction in Arabidopsis
