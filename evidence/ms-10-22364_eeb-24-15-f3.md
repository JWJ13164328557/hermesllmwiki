# The practical choice of a macroalgal product should be determined not only by positive biological ef

## Claim
The practical choice of a macroalgal product should be determined not only by positive biological effects but also by the target soil problem, required processing infrastructure, and the degree of product standardization needed for field use.

## Biological Context
Marine macroalgae for soil fertility enhancement and remediation: a review with focus on the Black Sea
