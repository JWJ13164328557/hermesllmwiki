# Taken together, these histo-immunochemical analyses of the pock­ ets formed during the gummosis peri

## Claim
Taken together, these histo-immunochemical analyses of the pock­ ets formed during the gummosis period correspond to sites of gum Arabic accumulation, thus justifying the term “gum pockets”.

## Biological Context
Understanding the histological and histochemical mechanisms involved in gum arabic formation in Senegalia senegal, a key species of the Sahelian region
