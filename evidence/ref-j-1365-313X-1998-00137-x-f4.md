# Taken together, these results indicate that the scr and shr mutations cause the loss of a normal end

## Claim
Taken together, these results indicate that the scr and shr mutations cause the loss of a normal endodermis that, in wild-type plants, contains sedimenting amyloplasts in both the inﬂorescence stem and the hypocotyl.

## Biological Context
Genetic evidence that the endodermis is essential for shoot gravitropism in Arab
