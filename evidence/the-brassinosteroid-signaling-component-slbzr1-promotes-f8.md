# Activated BSK1 binds to and activates the phosphatase BRI1‐suppressor1 (BSU1), which leads to dephos

## Claim
Activated BSK1 binds to and activates the phosphatase BRI1‐suppressor1 (BSU1), which leads to dephosphorylation and inhibition of the kinase Brassinosteroid‐insensitive2 (BIN2) (Tang et al., 2008).
