# Early hypotheses proposing clonal relationships between MNs and their muscle targets have not been s

## Claim
Early hypotheses proposing clonal relationships between MNs and their muscle targets have not been supported by lineage-tracing studies, which demonstrate that individual progenitors generate diverse neuronal and glial cell types (Moody and Jacobson, 1983; Leber et al., 1990).

## Biological Context
Defining spinal motor neuron subtypes across development: from embryonic specification to postnatal maturation
