# Interestingly, we found that depletion of mitochondrial PI in both yeast and mammalian systems resul

## Claim
Interestingly, we found that depletion of mitochondrial PI in both yeast and mammalian systems resulted in a stark respiratory defect accompanied by a loss of mitochondrial protein content and a lack of assembled OxPhos machinery, respectively, that could not be rescued by CoQ supplementation.

## Biological Context
Fmp30p is a mitochondrial phosphatidylinositol hydrolase that modulates CoQ biosynthesis
