# At the pre-target stage (Galluzzi et al., 2014), resistance arises from reduced drug uptake, such as

## Claim
At the pre-target stage (Galluzzi et al., 2014), resistance arises from reduced drug uptake, such as downregulation of copper transporter 1/2 (CTR1/2), and increased efflux, such as overexpression of copper-transporting ATPases 7A/7B (ATP7A/ 7B), leading to decreased intracellular drug concentrations (Yoshida et al., 2013; Lukanović et al., 2020; Li et al., 2017).
