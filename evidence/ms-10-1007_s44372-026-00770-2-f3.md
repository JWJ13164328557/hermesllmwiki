## Claim
However, comparative evi­ dence suggests that, although terpenoid metabolism plays a central role in hormonal sig­ naling and rapid stress responses, its contribution to drought adaptation is more variable across genotypes than phenylpropanoid-driven structural protection, indicating path­ way-specific specialization in stress resilience strategies.
