# Through in situ hybridization, RTqPCR, tissue staining, transient dual-LUC reporter …

## Claim
Through in situ hybridization, RTqPCR, tissue staining, transient dual-LUC reporter assays, and EMSA, we identified key TFs, including IAA29 (which directly regulates endodermis suberization), MYB2 and MYB78 (that positively regulate ginsenoside biosynthesis), and WRKY75 and AT1G16060 (which mediate the expression of the FAD gene family).
