# During callus formation, plants are reprogrammed to acquire pluripotent competency in the middle lay

## Claim
During callus formation, plants are reprogrammed to acquire pluripotent competency in the middle layer of callus, which is required for shoot regeneration ( 3 , 5 , 6 ).

## Biological Context
Histone deacetylation regulates de novo shoot regeneration.
