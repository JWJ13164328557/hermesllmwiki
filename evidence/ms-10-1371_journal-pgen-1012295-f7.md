# The sequencing results showed that all knockout lines carried a 1 bp deletion at the target site, re

## Claim
The sequencing results showed that all knockout lines carried a 1 bp deletion at the target site, resulting in a frameshift mutation of OsDUF1223 and leading to premature translation termination.

## Biological Context
OsDUF1223 negatively regulates seedling drought tolerance and modulates nitrate-dependent root growth in rice
