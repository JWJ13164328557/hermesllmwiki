# Collectively, these findings further suggest that GBA1 overexpression reduces α-syn aggregation path

## Claim
Collectively, these findings further suggest that GBA1 overexpression reduces α-syn aggregation pathology, which is mediated by KAT8-driven lysosomal biogenesis, in an α-syn PFF mouse model.

## Biological Context
Lysine acetyltransferase 8-mediated histone acetylation, regulated by GBA1, is associated with lysosomal function related to α-Synuclein pathology
