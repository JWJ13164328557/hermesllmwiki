# GCRs are composed primarily of protons (~87%) and helium ions (~12%), as well as high-energy, high-c

## Claim
GCRs are composed primarily of protons (~87%) and helium ions (~12%), as well as high-energy, high-charge (HZE) nuclei (~1-2%), including oxygen (16O), carbon, and iron ions, which readily penetrate shielding and interact with biological tissue, including the central nervous system (CNS)1.

## Biological Context
Long-term molecular effects of 16O-ion exposure in rat brain and implications for space radiation risk
