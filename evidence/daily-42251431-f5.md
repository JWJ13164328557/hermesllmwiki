# Similarly, the expression levels of TaPDI in the five salttolerant lines were 7.43 …

## Claim
Similarly, the expression levels of TaPDI in the five salttolerant lines were 7.43 (S008), 11.98 (S022), 9.61 (S037), 6.38 (S044), and 12.84 (S066), respectively, all significantly higher than that in the wild type Shi 4185 (3.61), Notably, the expression levels in all five lines were not significantly different from that in Dekang 961 (9.69).
