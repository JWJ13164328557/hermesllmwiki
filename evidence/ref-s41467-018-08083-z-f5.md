## Claim
Based on previously identiﬁed phytohormone-responsive genes16, we found that transcripts from genes whose expression is activated by a phytohormone, including abscisic acid, auxin, brassinoster- oids, ethylene, gibberellin, and jasmonic acid, were enriched in the abaxial domain.

A gene expression map of shoot domains reveals regulatory mechanisms.
