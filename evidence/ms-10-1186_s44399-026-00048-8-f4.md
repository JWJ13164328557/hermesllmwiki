# Notably, sugar-specific effects on aphid feeding are pronounced: in Myzus persicae, sorbose stimulat

## Claim
Notably, sugar-specific effects on aphid feeding are pronounced: in Myzus persicae, sorbose stimulates ingestion without being metabo­ lized, whereas mannose, sorbitol, xylose, and ribose nei­ ther promote feeding nor support utilization; rhamnose, arabinose, lactose, and cellobiose inhibit fluid uptake and reduce survival [24].

The nutritional interface between plants and aphids: mechanisms, constraints, adaptations and knowledge gaps
