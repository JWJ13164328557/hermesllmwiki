# Thus far, the only described direct target of this family of transcription factors is ERF1 (ETHYLENE

## Claim
Thus far, the only described direct target of this family of transcription factors is ERF1 (ETHYLENE RESPONSE FACTOR1) (24), a member of a large family of AP2-like DNA binding transcription factors known as EREBPs.

## Biological Context
Genome-wide insertional mutagenesis of Arabidopsis thaliana.
