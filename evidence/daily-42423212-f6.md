# K⁺ is essential for enzyme activation, protein synthesis, membrane potential, osmotic …

## Claim
K⁺ is essential for enzyme activation, protein synthesis, membrane potential, osmotic balance, stomatal movement and phloem transport, and loss of K⁺ may also affect growth when accumulation of Na⁺ is partly controlled., Therefore, salt tolerance depends on preserving a favorable K⁺/Na⁺ balance rather than simply reducing Na⁺ concentration.
