# Our findings suggest that Corylin did not affect appetite or feeding behavior in aged mice, and thus

## Claim
Our findings suggest that Corylin did not affect appetite or feeding behavior in aged mice, and thus the observed lifespan extension is unlikely to result from an aversive response to the supplemented diet.

## Biological Context
Corylin promotes healthy aging via RAGA–mTOR suppression and sex-dependent activation of SIRT3
