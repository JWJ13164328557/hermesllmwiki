# Interestingly, we found that for prolonged chilling and salinity stress, the presence of malonic aci

## Claim
Interestingly, we found that for prolonged chilling and salinity stress, the presence of malonic acid (VIP score > 2) increased in both stress condi­ tions, whereas for iminodiacetic acid whose level was high under chilling stress at any time, raised slightly only upon prolonged and high salt concentration.
