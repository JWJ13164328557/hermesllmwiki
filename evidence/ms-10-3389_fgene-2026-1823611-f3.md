# Functionally, many circRNAs act as competitive endogenous RNAs (ceRNAs) or miRNA sponges; by sequest

## Claim
Functionally, many circRNAs act as competitive endogenous RNAs (ceRNAs) or miRNA sponges; by sequestering specific microRNAs (miRNAs), they alleviate the post-transcriptional repression of target genes, thereby promoting their expression (Hansen et al., 2013).
