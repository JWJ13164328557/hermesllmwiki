# Long- read sequencing of 23 human blood samples and the HepG2 cell line achieved an average on-targe

## Claim
Long- read sequencing of 23 human blood samples and the HepG2 cell line achieved an average on-target coverage of ~40× and a read N50 of ~61 kb, enabling haplotype-resolved detection of structural and small variants.

## Biological Context
Resolving the complex CES1 genomic locus with Cas9-directed targeted long-read sequencing
