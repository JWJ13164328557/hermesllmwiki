# Collectively, 0.05 mg·L⁻¹ EBR mainly acted at the Podu stage, promoting hypocotyl expansion by incre

## Claim
Collectively, 0.05 mg·L⁻¹ EBR mainly acted at the Podu stage, promoting hypocotyl expansion by increasing cambium cell layers, cambium thickness, and thickening xylem and phloem, while 1 mg·L⁻¹ EBR had no significant effect on radial growth and only caused a transient, significant thinning of the cambium at the early pre-Podu stage.
