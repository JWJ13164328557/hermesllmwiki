# This study establishes an optimized protocol for indirect shoot organogenesis from leaf lamina expla

## Claim
This study establishes an optimized protocol for indirect shoot organogenesis from leaf lamina explants through callus culture, followed by shoot induction, rooting, and acclimatization.

## Biological Context
Indirect organogenesis and photoautotrophic acclimatization for scalable propagation of Ficus lyrata Warb. (fiddle-leaf fig), a popular indoor ornamental
