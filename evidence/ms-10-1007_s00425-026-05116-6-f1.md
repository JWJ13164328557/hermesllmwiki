# Here we report for the first time stable and shifted DNA methylation differences between clonal Norw

## Claim
Here we report for the first time stable and shifted DNA methylation differences between clonal Norway spruce CE and WE epitype trees that have retained different bud phenology traits after more than a decade in common garden conditions.

## Biological Context
Stable and temporally shifted DNA methylation differences between 14-year-old Norway spruce epitypes under common garden conditions
