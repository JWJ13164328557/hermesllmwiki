# Taken together, these results show that in the presence of p53H179R and p53H179Y mutants, both neutr

## Claim
Taken together, these results show that in the presence of p53H179R and p53H179Y mutants, both neutral lipid levels and the number of lipid droplets are increased, with robust effects in lung and ovarian cancer cells but more variable effects in prostate cancer cells.

## Biological Context
TP53 mutation at codon 179 metabolically reprograms cancer cells to promote invasion
