## Claim
Here, by comprehensively tracking limb tissues marked by an inte- grated green fluorescent protein (GFP)–transgene in the salamander Ambystoma mexicanum (the axolotl), we show that cells do not become pluripotent during limb regeneration and retain a strong memory of their tissue or embryonic origin.

## Biological Context
Cells keep a memory of their tissue origin during axolotl limb regeneration.
