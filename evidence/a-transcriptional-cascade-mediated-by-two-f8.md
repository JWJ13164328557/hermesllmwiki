# Knockout of SlAP2c in two tomato cultivars resulted in greater accumulation of carotenoids, particul

## Claim
Knockout of SlAP2c in two tomato cultivars resulted in greater accumulation of carotenoids, particularly lycopene, while overexpression of SlAP2c driven by the ripening‐ speciﬁc promoter E8 led to the formation of orange‐yellow fruit with lower lycopene and higher β‐carotene contents than the WT.
