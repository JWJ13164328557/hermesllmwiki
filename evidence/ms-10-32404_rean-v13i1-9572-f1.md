# Accordingly, although the combined application at growth stage 6 resulted in an increase of 12.8 bag

## Claim
Accordingly, although the combined application at growth stage 6 resulted in an increase of 12.8 bags ha⁻¹, which was sufficient to cover the application costs, the same was not observed for the application at growth stage 2, where the increase was only 4.91 bags ha⁻¹, insufficient to offset the cost of the products.
