# Analyses of small RNA and mRNA transcriptomes indicated that miRNA:target interactions primarily rep

## Claim
Analyses of small RNA and mRNA transcriptomes indicated that miRNA:target interactions primarily repress TFs belonging to the ARF, GRAS, HD-ZIP, MADS-box, MYB, NAC, SBP, and TCP domain families.

## Biological Context
Developmental and genomic architecture of plant embryogenesis: from model plant
