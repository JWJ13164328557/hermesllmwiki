## Claim
In summary, our findings support a model in which Corylin binds ESR1 to induce Sirt3 expression, thereby enhancing mitochondrial resilience and antioxidant defenses in a sex-dependent manner and contributing to longevity benefits observed in females.

## Biological Context
Corylin promotes healthy aging via RAGA–mTOR suppression and sex-dependent activation of SIRT3
