# To exert its transcriptional regulatory effects, PPARα forms a functional complex with PGC-1α, which

## Claim
To exert its transcriptional regulatory effects, PPARα forms a functional complex with PGC-1α, which then binds specifically to PPAR response elements (PPREs) in the promoter regions of target genes (94).

## Biological Context
Mechanisms of energy metabolism reprogramming and homeostasis maintenance in overwintering hibernating animals
