# Together, these findings suggest sympatric marine turtles may partition resources through difference

## Claim
Together, these findings suggest sympatric marine turtles may partition resources through differences in resource use, fine-scale location of hotspots, or foraging behavior and highlights the importance of incorporating habitat charac­ teristics and prey availability into studies of marine turtle foraging ecology to further explain these patterns.

## Biological Context
Diet selection of sympatric marine turtles reveals the importance of diverse benthic habitats
