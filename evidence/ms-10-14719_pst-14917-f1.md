# The microbial community that exists in the soil and interacts with plant roots either nearby (rhizos

## Claim
The microbial community that exists in the soil and interacts with plant roots either nearby (rhizospheric microbiome) or far away (non-rhizospheric microbiome) is referred to as the “belowground microbiome.” The phyllosphere, endosphere and spermosphere microbiomes that circulate above the soil in plant components and interact with plant functions are included in the “aboveground microbiome” (10)

## Biological Context
Crop microbiome: A way for climate-resilience agriculture
