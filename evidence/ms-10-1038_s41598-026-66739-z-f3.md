# The 16-month sampling allowed assessment of long-term persistence of colonization after a full rainy

## Claim
The 16-month sampling allowed assessment of long-term persistence of colonization after a full rainy and dry season cycle, which is critical for evaluating functional stability of AMF symbiosis in slow-growing species such as shea.

## Biological Context
Enhancing growth and graft success of shea (Vitellaria paradoxa C.F. Gaertn) through targeted inoculation with indigenous arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi
