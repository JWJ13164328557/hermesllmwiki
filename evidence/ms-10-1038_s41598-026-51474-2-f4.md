# Target DNA was amplified by Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) in a Thermal Cycler 2720 (Applied Biosys

## Claim
Target DNA was amplified by Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) in a Thermal Cycler 2720 (Applied Biosystems, Foster city, CA, USA) using a 25-µL reaction mixture containing 12.5 µL of Gene RED PCR Mix Plus (Nippon Gene, Tokyo, Japan) and the primer set ITS1F/ ITS420,21.

## Biological Context
Differential inhibition of bark endophytes and a wood-decay fungus by inner bark of Sorbus commixta
