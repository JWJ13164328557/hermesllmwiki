# At broader spatial and temporal scales, crab grazing and bioturbation can interact with inundation a

## Claim
At broader spatial and temporal scales, crab grazing and bioturbation can interact with inundation and sea-level rise to alter vegetation patterns, tidal-creek development, and salt-marsh geomorphology (Crotty et al., 2020; Wilson et al., 2012; Hughes et al., 2024).

## Biological Context
Top-down control by crabs along tidal creeks in a salt marsh: vegetation suppression and variable sediment deposition
