# We found that Tenascin C expression was normal in both MGCD0103-treated (Figure 6A,B) and chd4a MO-i

## Claim
We found that Tenascin C expression was normal in both MGCD0103-treated (Figure 6A,B) and chd4a MO-injected fins (see Additional file 1: Figure S12), suggesting that the hdac1 and chd4a do not influence mesenchymal remodeling during blastema formation.

## Biological Context
Specific NuRD components are required for fin regeneration in zebrafish.
