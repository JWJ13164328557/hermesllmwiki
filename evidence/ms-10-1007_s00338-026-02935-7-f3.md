# Grains displaying edge characteristics consistent with fish predation exhibit sharper, more angular 

## Claim
Grains displaying edge characteristics consistent with fish predation exhibit sharper, more angular edges and are predominantly located towards the left of each chart Coral Reefs Discussion This study provides the first direct evidence of the role of invertivore fishes as producers of carbonate sediment on coral reefs by integrating fish ecology, invertebrate analysis, and sedimentology.

## Biological Context
Invertivore fish predation is an overlooked source of carbonate sediment on a turbid coral reef
