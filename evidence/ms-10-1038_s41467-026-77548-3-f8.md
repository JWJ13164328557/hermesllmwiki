# In the context of oral vaccination, the MLNs serve as the principal draining lymph nodes responsible

## Claim
In the context of oral vaccination, the MLNs serve as the principal draining lymph nodes responsible for initiating anti-tumor immunity, whereas the spleen plays a central role in orchestrating systemic immune responses23.

## Biological Context
A dual-site oral nanovaccine programs anti-colorectal tumor immunity from small intestinal priming to large intestinal boosting
