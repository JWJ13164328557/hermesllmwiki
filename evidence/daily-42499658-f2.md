# Collectively, these results indicate that MWS28 enhances chilling tolerance in apple stem …

## Claim
Collectively, these results indicate that MWS28 enhances chilling tolerance in apple stem cuttings through coordinated activation of antioxidant defenses, reduction of lipid peroxidation, and regulation of endogenous phytohormones, consistent with previous reports on PGPR-mediated cold stress tolerance (Zhang et al., 2023).
