# In tea (Camellia sinensis), CAMTA binds to a G-box (CACGTC) cis-element in the promoter of the Fluor

## Claim
In tea (Camellia sinensis), CAMTA binds to a G-box (CACGTC) cis-element in the promoter of the Fluoride Export Gene 1 (FEX1) to inhibit its transcription, while Calmodulin 1 (CAM1) physically interacts with CAMTA to create a repressor complex.
