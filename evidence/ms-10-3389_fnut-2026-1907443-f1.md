# In summary, mHFD reshapes the intrauterine adverse microenvironment through two independent pathways

## Claim
In summary, mHFD reshapes the intrauterine adverse microenvironment through two independent pathways: one is to induce persistent systemic inﬂammation and oxidative stress in the mother and construct a circulating inﬂammatory signal library; the second is to damage the placental structure, material transport and barrier function, and to amplify the cross- generational transmission of inﬂammation a

Research progress on the association of maternal high-fat diet with offspring neurodevelopment and susceptibility to Parkinson's disease
