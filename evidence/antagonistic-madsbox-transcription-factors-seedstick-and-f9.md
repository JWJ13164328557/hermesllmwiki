# We found that sep3‐cas9#1 and sep3‐ca9#23 produced larger and heavier seeds and greater size of embr

## Claim
We found that sep3‐cas9#1 and sep3‐ca9#23 produced larger and heavier seeds and greater size of embryos than wild‐type plants, while seeds and embryos of Col‐0 35:SEP3– GFP overexpression lines were much smaller and lighter than those of wild‐type plants (Figure 4C, D, F–I).
