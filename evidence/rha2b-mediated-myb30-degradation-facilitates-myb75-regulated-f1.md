## Claim
In this study, we found that MYB30 can form a complex with MYB75, but it does not physically interact with other MBW components such as TT8 and EGL3, suggesting that the function of MYB30 in sucrose-induced anthocyanin biosynthesis relies on MYB75.

## Biological Context
RHA2b-介导的 MYB30 degradation facilitates MYB75-regulated, sucrose-induced 花青素 生物合成 in Arabidopsis seedlings
