# To isolate single cells it was necessary to ablate at least three contigu- ous neighbour cells on ea

## Claim
To isolate single cells it was necessary to ablate at least three contigu- ous neighbour cells on each side to ensure that all contacts were lost during subsequent expansion of the isolated cell.
