# Under T1 treatment, the DEGs in ‘Zhenong 117’ were predominantly enriched in pathways …

## Claim
Under T1 treatment, the DEGs in ‘Zhenong 117’ were predominantly enriched in pathways such as amino sugar and nucleotide sugar metabolism and galactose metabolism, suggesting that these pathways may play a central role in the response of this cultivar to organic Se under T1 conditions (Figure 4C).
