# Furthermore, we reveal that modulation of mitochondrial PI content enhances CoQ metabolon formation 

## Claim
Furthermore, we reveal that modulation of mitochondrial PI content enhances CoQ metabolon formation and biosynthetic efficiency, and show that this relationship between PI and CoQ is conserved in mammals.

## Biological Context
Fmp30p is a mitochondrial phosphatidylinositol hydrolase that modulates CoQ biosynthesis
