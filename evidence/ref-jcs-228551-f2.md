# Simultaneously, kinases of the shaggy- like kinase family such as BRASSINOSTEROID-INSENSITIVE 2 (BIN

## Claim
Simultaneously, kinases of the shaggy- like kinase family such as BRASSINOSTEROID-INSENSITIVE 2 (BIN2, also known as ASK7), which are connected to brassinosteroid (BR) signaling, also target and downregulate SPCH protein (Gudesblat et al., 2012).
