# Based on the data shown here, we hypothesize that UBE2F regulates the degradation of the pro-apoptot

## Claim
Based on the data shown here, we hypothesize that UBE2F regulates the degradation of the pro-apoptotic protein NOXA, and that SDH-deficient cells are uniquely hypersensitive to elevated NOXA levels.19 We show that NOXA levels fluctuate significantly in response to changes in UBE2F levels (Figures 2D and 3F).

## Biological Context
Unbiased CRISPR synthetic lethal screening for genetic vulnerabilities in a succinate dehydrogenase-loss model of paraganglioma.
