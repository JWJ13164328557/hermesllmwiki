# Consistent with these findings, key mitochondrial biogenesis regulators such as p-AMPK …

## Claim
Consistent with these findings, key mitochondrial biogenesis regulators such as p-AMPK, SIRT1, PGC1-α and the transcription factors NRF1, NRF2, along with mitochondrial transcription factor A (TFAM), which act as ultimate effectors, are downregulated in primary human OA chondrocytes.79 Overall, these data suggest a failure of mitochondrial biogenesis in OA chondrocytes.
