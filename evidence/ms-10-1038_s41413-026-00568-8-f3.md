# Cbfβ preserves genome stability in BMACs through chromatin accessibility-dependent transcriptional r

## Claim
Cbfβ preserves genome stability in BMACs through chromatin accessibility-dependent transcriptional regulation To investigate how Cbfβ regulates DNA damage and senescence, we performed Cleavage Under Targets and Tagmentation sequencing (CUT&Tag-seq) to map Cbfβ binding and Transposase-Accessible Chromatin sequencing (ATAC-seq) to assess chromatin accessibility.
