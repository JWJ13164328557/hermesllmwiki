# Aldo-keto reductase (AKR4B) has been associated to ASA generation, since overexpression of this gene

## Claim
Aldo-keto reductase (AKR4B) has been associated to ASA generation, since overexpression of this gene resulted in a two-fold ASA content increase in Arabidopsis thaliana (Agius et al., 2003).
