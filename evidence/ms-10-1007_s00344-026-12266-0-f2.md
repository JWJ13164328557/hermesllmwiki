# Interestingly, while novel_47 (targeting the DNA repair gene BRCA2) was upregulated in both genotype

## Claim
Interestingly, while novel_47 (targeting the DNA repair gene BRCA2) was upregulated in both genotypes, its silencing effect appeared specific to the sensitive cultivar, implying that Perola possesses down­ stream buffering mechanisms—potentially via target site masking or ceRNA decoys—that prevent this miRNA from compromising genomic integrity.
