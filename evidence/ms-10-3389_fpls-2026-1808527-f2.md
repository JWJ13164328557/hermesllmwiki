# Importantly, the mycorrhizal treatment resulted in a comparatively stronger stimulatory effect in K2

## Claim
Importantly, the mycorrhizal treatment resulted in a comparatively stronger stimulatory effect in K2 drought-sensitive plants than in K1 drought-tolerant plants.

## Biological Context
Funneliformis mosseae enhances drought tolerance in maize inbred lines through root transcriptomic reprogramming
