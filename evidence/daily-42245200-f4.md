# Strains CNPSo 2374, CNPSo 2376, CNPSo 2343 and CNPSo 2391 of Enterobacter, Klebsiella …

## Claim
Strains CNPSo 2374, CNPSo 2376, CNPSo 2343 and CNPSo 2391 of Enterobacter, Klebsiella CNPSo 2359, Stenotrophomonas CNPSo 2326 and Burkholderia CNPSo 2334 were outstanding in the synthesis of indolic compounds in the presence of tryptophan, most of them producing equivalent or above the positive control with Azospirillum brasilense Ab-V5, which yielded 16.04±2.72 µg ml−1.
