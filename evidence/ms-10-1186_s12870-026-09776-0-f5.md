# Notably, rhizo­ bacteria alone raised chlorophyll by 64% and carotenoids by 48%, while nano-NPK alon

## Claim
Notably, rhizo­ bacteria alone raised chlorophyll by 64% and carotenoids by 48%, while nano-NPK alone gave lower increases— demonstrating clear synergy.
