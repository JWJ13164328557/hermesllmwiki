# For instance, the MaKAN4 protein in the root endodermis can interact with the promoters …

## Claim
For instance, the MaKAN4 protein in the root endodermis can interact with the promoters of autoinhibited Ca 2+ -ATPase 7 ( MaACA7 ) and Alcohol Dehydrogenase 3 ( MaADH3 ) from Musa acuminata and promote their expression, revealing that MaKAN4 plays a critical role in banana disease resistance by regulating the Zn 2+ -dependent MaACA7/MaADH3 module [ ].
