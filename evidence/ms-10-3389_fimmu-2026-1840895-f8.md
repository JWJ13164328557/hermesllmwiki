# Importantly, by fusing both MHC-I- and MHC-II-restricted epitopes within the same recombinant protei

## Claim
Importantly, by fusing both MHC-I- and MHC-II-restricted epitopes within the same recombinant protein, this approach allows simultaneous stimulation of CD8+ cytotoxic and CD4+ helper T cells, two arms of the immune system that act synergistically to generate strong and durable immune responses.

## Biological Context
Soluble multi−epitope protein vaccination leverages antigen availability to drive CD8+ T cell immunity and tumor control
