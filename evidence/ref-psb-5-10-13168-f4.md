# With ABA treatment, hda19-1 displayed a lower expression of ABA-responsive genes compared with wild-

## Claim
With ABA treatment, hda19-1 displayed a lower expression of ABA-responsive genes compared with wild-type, suggesting that HDA19 is required for full induction of ABA-responsive genes.

## Biological Context
Role of histone deacetylases HDA6 and HDA19 in ABA and abiotic stress response.
