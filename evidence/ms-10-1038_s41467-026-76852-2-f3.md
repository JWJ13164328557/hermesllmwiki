# Notably, DCTPP1 knockout resulted in higher immune response pathways, including TNF-α signaling, and

## Claim
Notably, DCTPP1 knockout resulted in higher immune response pathways, including TNF-α signaling, and inﬂammatory and interferon-γ response; while DCTPP1 over- expression resulted in upregulation of hedgehog and Wnt signaling pathway genes.

## Biological Context
Triptolide sensitizes cancer cells to nucleoside DNA methyltransferase inhibitors through inhibition of DCTPP1-mediated cell-intrinsic resistance
