# Most importantly, foliar spray of the investigated microorganisms, particularly the consortium treat

## Claim
Most importantly, foliar spray of the investigated microorganisms, particularly the consortium treatment, helped alleviate the negative effects of water stress by improving chlorophyll, carotenoids, RWC, TSS, while reducing proline accumulation compared to stress controls.

## Biological Context
Foliar spraying of Bradyrhizobium and Anabaena improve stress tolerance under deficit irrigation of Cowpea
