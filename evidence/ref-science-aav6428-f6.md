# By comparing these transitions to chromosome cytology, we found that the first occurs within leptote

## Claim
By comparing these transitions to chromosome cytology, we found that the first occurs within leptotene, whereas the second is at the boundary of prezygotene, a cytology stage in maize that occurs just before the onset of chro- mosome pairing (11).
