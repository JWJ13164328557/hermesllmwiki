# Together, our results suggest that aging impairs the integration of external cues that guide oocyte 

## Claim
Together, our results suggest that aging impairs the integration of external cues that guide oocyte maturation, providing a potential mechanistic basis for the age-related decline in oocyte quality.

## Biological Context
Aging disrupts spatiotemporal coordination in the cycling murine ovary
