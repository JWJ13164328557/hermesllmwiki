# Recruitment of AtGCN5 to target promoters is likely to be mediated by direct or indirect interaction

## Claim
Recruitment of AtGCN5 to target promoters is likely to be mediated by direct or indirect interaction with DNA-binding transcription factors and/or by interaction with acetylated histone lysine residues on the targets.
