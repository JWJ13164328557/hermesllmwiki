# Mycorrhizal helper bacteria (MHB) are necessary for promoting ecological fitness and establishing AM

## Claim
Mycorrhizal helper bacteria (MHB) are necessary for promoting ecological fitness and establishing AM symbiosis by influenc­ ing spore germination, mycelial growth, and root colonization.

Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi support plant nutrition soil health and ecosystem resilience
