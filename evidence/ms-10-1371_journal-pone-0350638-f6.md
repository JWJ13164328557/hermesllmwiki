# Through this integrated strategy, we aim to: (1) identify key molecular targets of NP/OP in regulati

## Claim
Through this integrated strategy, we aim to: (1) identify key molecular targets of NP/OP in regulating prostate cancer; (2) elucidate the regulatory axis of “pollutant exposure-metabolic reprogramming-oncogenic signaling activation”; and (3) provide a theoretical basis for the precise prevention and treatment of environment-associated prostate cancer.

## Biological Context
Integrating network toxicology, machine learning, and single-cell sequencing to reveal the FASN-mediated role of phenolic endocrine disruptors in water in promoting prostate cancer
