# For example, PEA binds to PPARα, which suppresses NF-κB activity, reducing inflammatory media­ tors 

## Claim
For example, PEA binds to PPARα, which suppresses NF-κB activity, reducing inflammatory media­ tors like TNF-α, IL-1β, iNOS, and COX-2, and increasing the activity of antioxidant enzymes, such as catalase and superoxide dismutase (SOD), thus modulating intestinal inflammation [36, 37].
