# Our results indicate that GmSHMT05 sustains overall soybean growth and development in the …

## Claim
Our results indicate that GmSHMT05 sustains overall soybean growth and development in the absence of GmSHMT08; however, GmSHMT08’s gain-of-function in SCN resistance negatively influences pod and root growth, highlighting a potential trade-off between soybean defense and development that may impact yield when breeding with GmSHMT08 to develop SCN-resistant varieties.
