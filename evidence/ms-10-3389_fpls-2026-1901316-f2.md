## Claim
Notably, the 75% NPK dose resulted in the highest signiﬁcant values of PH (51.13 and 53.30 cm), NB (11.93 and 12.80 branches/plant), AL (46.14 and 47.73 cm2), CI (17.67 and 18.22 SPAD units), FWS (95.76 and 96.20 g/plant), DWS (24.00 and 24.70 g/plant), FWR (40.01 and 41.05 g/ plant), and DWR (12.41 and 12.70 g/plant) (P ≤0.05) in both seasons, respectively.
