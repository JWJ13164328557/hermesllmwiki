## Claim
With regard to the unexpected upregulation of miR-1247-5p expression upon antagomiR administration, one expla­ nation could be an indirect derepression mechanism: if the miRNA targets a repressor of its own expression, silencing the miRNA may release and upregulate its expression via a feedback loop [39].
