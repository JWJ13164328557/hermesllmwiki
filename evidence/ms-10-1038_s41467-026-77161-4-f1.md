# Importantly, neutralizing ele- vated miR-29a-3p in maternal sEVs ameliorated metabolic dysfunction i

## Claim
Importantly, neutralizing ele- vated miR-29a-3p in maternal sEVs ameliorated metabolic dysfunction in offspring of dams with obesity, suggesting that this pathway may represent a candidate target for limiting obesity-associated inter- generational metabolic programming.

## Biological Context
Maternal obesity programs offspring metabolic dysfunction via small extracellular vesicle-mediated epigenetic remodeling
