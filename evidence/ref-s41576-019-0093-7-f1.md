# Catalytically inactivated versions of Cas9 could be fused to different effector domains …

## Claim
Catalytically inactivated versions of Cas9 could be fused to different effector domains, such as histone modifiers, transcriptional activators or repressors, and DNA methyltransferases, in order to assess the effect of epigenetic modifications on gene expression57–61 and could target combinations of epigenetic effectors with gene knockouts62.
