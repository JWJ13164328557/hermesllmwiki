# Subsequently, target prediction was performed for all known and novel miRNAs using the plant-speciﬁc

## Claim
Subsequently, target prediction was performed for all known and novel miRNAs using the plant-speciﬁc tool psRobot, to predict putative targets among mRNAs, lncRNAs, and circRNAs, and to infer their potential regulatory interactions with miRNAs.
