# We found that the OBE1 ortho- logs from higher land plants bound to H3K4me2/3 and to a lesser to H3K

## Claim
We found that the OBE1 ortho- logs from higher land plants bound to H3K4me2/3 and to a lesser to H3K4me1, and that the OBE1 ortholog from the lower land plant Physcomitrium patens did not bind to any H3 peptides (Fig 7D).
