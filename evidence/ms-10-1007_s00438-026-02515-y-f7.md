# Notably, the early-flow­ ering genotypes Westar and No2127 were always grouped within the same clade

## Claim
Notably, the early-flow­ ering genotypes Westar and No2127 were always grouped within the same clades for both genes, a pattern that was not observed from the other genomes.

## Biological Context
Genome-wide cyclin gene evolution in Arabidopsis and Brassica reveals polyploidization-driven duplication and flowering-time associations
