# Overexpression of PbrARF13 decreased stone cell content in pear fruit and secondary cell wall (SCW) 

## Claim
Overexpression of PbrARF13 decreased stone cell content in pear fruit and secondary cell wall (SCW) thickness in transgenic Arabidopsis plants.

## Biological Context
Auxin 抑制 木质素 and 纤维素 生物合成 in stone cells of pear fruit via the PbrARF13‐PbrNSC‐PbrMYB132 transcriptional regulatory cascade
