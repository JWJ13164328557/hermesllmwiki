# By analyzing the transcriptional activation activity and overexpression of FtMYB31 in transgenic Tar

## Claim
By analyzing the transcriptional activation activity and overexpression of FtMYB31 in transgenic Tartary buckwheat roots, we found that FtMYB31 as a transcript factor enhances rutin synthesis not only in tobacco leaves but also in the roots of transgenic TB.
