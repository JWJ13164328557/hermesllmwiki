# We discover that Fmp30p (Found in mitochondrial proteome 30; Ypl103c), a mitochondrial intermembrane

## Claim
We discover that Fmp30p (Found in mitochondrial proteome 30; Ypl103c), a mitochondrial intermembrane space- localized protein belonging to the metallo-b-lactamase superfamily, is a lipid hydrolase with specificity toward phosphatidylinositol (PI).

## Biological Context
Fmp30p is a mitochondrial phosphatidylinositol hydrolase that modulates CoQ biosynthesis
