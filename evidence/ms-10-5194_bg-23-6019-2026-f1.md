# Biofilms interact with DOC through diffusion and adsorption of DOC from the water column into the bi

## Claim
Biofilms interact with DOC through diffusion and adsorption of DOC from the water column into the biofilm matrix, where it may be decomposed and utilised for energy and biosynthesis (Battin et al., 2008; Logue et al., 2016; Findlay and Sinsabaugh, 2003).

## Biological Context
Stability of stream biofilm community composition to transient shifts in dissolved organic carbon characteristics
