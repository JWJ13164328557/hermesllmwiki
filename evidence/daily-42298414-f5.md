# The tissue expression data suggests a spatial division of defensin-mediated immunity in …

## Claim
The tissue expression data suggests a spatial division of defensin-mediated immunity in soybean, with GlyPDF2 members providing frontline protection at pathogen entry points (roots and seeds), GlyPDF3 / GlyPDF4 members contributing to reproductive tissue defense, and GlyPDF1 members potentially serving constitutive, housekeeping roles in systemic immunity.
