# PbbHLH164 could directly bind to the promoter of 1-aminocyclopropane-1- carboxylate synthase, PbACS1

## Claim
PbbHLH164 could directly bind to the promoter of 1-aminocyclopropane-1- carboxylate synthase, PbACS1b, to enhance the expression, leading to the increase of ethylene production and the acceleration of fruit ripening.

## Biological Context
The transcription factor PbbHLH164 is destabilized by PbRAD23C/D.1 and mediates ethylene 生物合成 过程中 pear fruit ripening
