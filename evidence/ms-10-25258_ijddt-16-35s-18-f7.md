# The presence of nutrient imbalances will interrupt how plants interact with beneficial soil microorg

## Claim
The presence of nutrient imbalances will interrupt how plants interact with beneficial soil microorganisms, which results in more plant health problems (Vessey, 2003; Chen et al., 2020).
