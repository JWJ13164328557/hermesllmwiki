# The enhanced tolerance of idr1–1 mutant seedlings to osmotic stress may stem from marked …

## Claim
The enhanced tolerance of idr1–1 mutant seedlings to osmotic stress may stem from marked changes in DEG profiles at the early and middle stages of osmotic stress, especially at the middle stage, when compared to those from wild-type IAPAR9 seedlings, and these changes may strengthen the capacity of idr1–1 mutant seedlings to cope better with severe osmotic stress.
