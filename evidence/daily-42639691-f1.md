## Claim
Unlike previous work documenting negative effects linked to sd1 in Asian germplasm (Murai et al.,; Vikram et al., ), we show that a specific upstream haplotype linked to historical sd1 deployment is associated with a consistent yield penalty in US tropical japonica germplasm and that closely related haplotypes predominate in elite global indica semidwarf material.
