# Signal strength (i.e., amplitude and peak power density) was positively influenced by …

## Claim
Signal strength (i.e., amplitude and peak power density) was positively influenced by both pathway phase and xylem ingestion, but strength was the highest when leafhopper mouthparts were located in the xylem; signal strength was similar while nonprobing and ingesting phloem.
