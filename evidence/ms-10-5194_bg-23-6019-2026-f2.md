# Three DNA metabarcoding regions were amplified from each sample, 16S rRNA V4 variable gene region, 2

## Claim
Three DNA metabarcoding regions were amplified from each sample, 16S rRNA V4 variable gene region, 23S rRNA fragment and ITS1-2 internal transcribed spacer region, to target the bacterial, algal and fungal components of the biofilm communities, respectively (primer sequences provided in Table S4).

## Biological Context
Stability of stream biofilm community composition to transient shifts in dissolved organic carbon characteristics
