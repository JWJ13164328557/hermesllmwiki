# Here, we show that the response of Mtb to NO stress is augmented in the simultaneous presence of iro

## Claim
Here, we show that the response of Mtb to NO stress is augmented in the simultaneous presence of iron limitation and vice versa, and reveal an important role of Rv3839-Rv3840 in the regulation of heme biosynthesis and the adaptive response of the bacterium to iron limitation and NO stress.

## Biological Context
Rv3839-Rv3840 links the endogenous heme biosynthesis pathway with Mycobacterium tuberculosis adaptation to nitric oxide and iron limitation stress
