# These DFT results show that Ti 3 C 2 T x has an electronic structure well-suited for …

## Claim
These DFT results show that Ti 3 C 2 T x has an electronic structure well-suited for pseudocapacitive energy storage: a metallic Ti–C backbone ensures rapid electron transport, O/F terminations enable reversible proton-coupled redox reactions, and the Ti-3d d-band center position (−0.72 eV) supports favorable reaction kinetics.
