# These findings suggest that KAT5 and KAT7 also participate in GBA1- mediated regulation of lysosomal

## Claim
These findings suggest that KAT5 and KAT7 also participate in GBA1- mediated regulation of lysosomal biogenesis, but with less efficacy than KAT8.

## Biological Context
Lysine acetyltransferase 8-mediated histone acetylation, regulated by GBA1, is associated with lysosomal function related to α-Synuclein pathology
