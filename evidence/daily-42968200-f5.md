# The histone acetyltransferase IDM1 is required for the DNA demethylase ROS1 to remove DNA …

## Claim
The histone acetyltransferase IDM1 is required for the DNA demethylase ROS1 to remove DNA methylation in some genomic regions [, ]; histone deacetylase 6 (HDA6) interacts with MET1, which methylates DNA [, ]; alterations in other histone marks (including H3K36me3, H3K27me1, H3K4me3, and H3K27me3) do not have a notable impact on DNA methylation [ ].
