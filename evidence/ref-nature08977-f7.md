# In summary, our data indicate that miR165/6 post-transcriptionally restricts PHB within the root mer

## Claim
In summary, our data indicate that miR165/6 post-transcriptionally restricts PHB within the root meristem to the stele centre for proper xylem patterning and that SHR regulates this process by promoting miR165/6 activity in the stele periphery and endodermis.

## Biological Context
Cell signalling by microRNA165/6 directs gene dose-dependent root cell fate.
