# The ethogram revealed whale sharks forage using different feeding types and movements, and may feed 

## Claim
The ethogram revealed whale sharks forage using different feeding types and movements, and may feed in a cost-effective manner across a very broad range of prey densities, in contrast to other large filter feeders such as baleen whales and basking sharks, which target and limit feeding to dense aggregations of prey.

## Biological Context
An ethogram for the whale shark (Rhincodon typus) with insights into foraging plasticity
