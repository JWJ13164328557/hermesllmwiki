# Additionally, functional groups on the biochar surface interact with soil active acids, buffering so

## Claim
Additionally, functional groups on the biochar surface interact with soil active acids, buffering soil pH (Nkoh et al., 2021); consequently, the treatment combining chemical fertilizer with biochar showed the highest pH.

## Biological Context
Effect of partial substitution of chemical fertilizer with organic manure combined with biochar on lettuce yield, soil properties and leaf metabolism
