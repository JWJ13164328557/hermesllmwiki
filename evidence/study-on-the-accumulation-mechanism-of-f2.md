# AATC encodes aspartate aminotransferase which is important for the metabolism of Asp and Asp-derived

## Claim
AATC encodes aspartate aminotransferase which is important for the metabolism of Asp and Asp-derived amino acids as well as Krebs-cycle related organic acids.51 As a member of the malate-aspartate shuttle, aspartate plays a key role in nitrogen metabolism as well as in aspects of carbon and energy metabolisms.
