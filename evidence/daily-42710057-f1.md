# By analyzing our previously reported SIEC-Arabidopsis interactome in more detail, we …

## Claim
By analyzing our previously reported SIEC-Arabidopsis interactome in more detail, we found that SIECs interact with more host proteins associated with abiotic stress compared with pathogen effectors and possess unique domain architecture (eg alpha/beta hydrolases, metalloproteases, serine proteases; ), which may represent a symbiont specific fingerprint.
