# Interestingly, sequestra­ tion of miR165/miR166 by ZLL is essential for shoot meri­stem maintenance 

## Claim
Interestingly, sequestra­ tion of miR165/miR166 by ZLL is essential for shoot meri­stem maintenance in the Arabidopsis accession Landsberg erecta (Ler) and generally correlates with higher expression of the AGO1 cofactor SQUINT (SQN; also known as CYP40) compared to other acces­ sions104,105.

## Biological Context
Plant and animal stem cells: similar yet different.
