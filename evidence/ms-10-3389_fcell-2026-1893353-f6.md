# Taken together, these lineage-tracing results indicate that embryonic KSP+ cells function as progeni

## Claim
Taken together, these lineage-tracing results indicate that embryonic KSP+ cells function as progenitor cells during kidney development, differentiating into epithelial cells of multiple major nephron segments in the adult mouse kidney.

## Biological Context
Lineage tracing reveals KSP+ cells as multipotent progenitors driving postnatal renal development and injury repair via notch signaling
