# Our data show that ZNF217, an oncogenic zinc finger transcription factor, promotes CRPC resistance t

## Claim
Our data show that ZNF217, an oncogenic zinc finger transcription factor, promotes CRPC resistance to ferroptosis by transcriptionally activating prominin-2 (PROM2) and thereby modulating iron homeostasis and lipid peroxidation.

## Biological Context
Disruption of the AR/ZNF217/PROM2 axis sensitizes prostate cancer to ferroptosis and enzalutamide therapy
