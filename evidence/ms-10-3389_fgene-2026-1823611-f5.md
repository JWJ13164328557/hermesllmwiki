# Some circRNAs contain miRNA response elements and can act as competitive endogenous RNAs (ceRNAs) by

## Claim
Some circRNAs contain miRNA response elements and can act as competitive endogenous RNAs (ceRNAs) by binding miRNAs and serving as miRNA sponges in cells, thereby relieving the inhibition of target genes by miRNAs and upregulating target gene expression (Hansen et al., 2013; Shang et al., 2019).
