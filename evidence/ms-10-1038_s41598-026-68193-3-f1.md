# NFAT expression is upregulated by microenvironmental stimuli and is elevated in cases with high CLL-

## Claim
NFAT expression is upregulated by microenvironmental stimuli and is elevated in cases with high CLL-PD score.32 Interestingly, higher levels of miR-155 have also been associated with a shorter time to relapse during Ibrutinib treatment in CLL.23 Altogether, these findings suggest that miR-155 may be an interesting target in CLL therapeutics.
