# We found that callus-like cells induced from XVE-WIND1 root explants by 17b-estradiol …

## Claim
We found that callus-like cells induced from XVE-WIND1 root explants by 17b-estradiol redifferentiated into both roots and shoots after transfer to 17b-estradiol-free MS medium (Figures S2K and S2L), indicating that WIND1-overexpressing cells have reacquired pluripotency, the competence to develop into more than one type of mature organs.
