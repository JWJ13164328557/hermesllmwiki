## Claim
In this study, we found that the targeted mutation of all Bnatts except Bnatt18 resulted in consistent modiﬁcation of FA composition, including increases in C18:2 and C18:3 and decreases in C18:1 relative to those in WT seeds (Figure 5c–e).
