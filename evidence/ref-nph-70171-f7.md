# Although SERK1’s contribution to plant immunity is unclear, it interacts with EFR- and FLS2-containi

## Claim
Although SERK1’s contribution to plant immunity is unclear, it interacts with EFR- and FLS2-containing PRR complexes potentially serving as a signaling node relaying immune signal to regeneration process (Roux et al., 2011).
