# TET2 plays a critical role by catalyzing the demethylation of DNA at loci associated with inﬂammator

## Claim
TET2 plays a critical role by catalyzing the demethylation of DNA at loci associated with inﬂammatory genes, and its functional loss during clonal hematopoiesis has been implicated in triggering severe inﬂammatory responses following myocardial infarction (42).
