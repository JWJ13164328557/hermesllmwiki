# PIP3 recruits AKT to the plasma mem­ brane, where it is partially activated by phosphorylation at Th

## Claim
PIP3 recruits AKT to the plasma mem­ brane, where it is partially activated by phosphorylation at Thr308 by phosphoinositide-dependent kinase 1 (PDK1) and fully activated at Ser473 by mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 2 (mTORC2) [3].
