# Notably, the WT accumulated much higher anthocyanin under blue light than the other light treatments

## Claim
Notably, the WT accumulated much higher anthocyanin under blue light than the other light treatments, while the cry1 mutants abolished the anthocyanin accumulation under blue light (Figure 6b,f), indicating that blue light played a major role in light‐ induced anthocyanin biosynthesis and PagCRY1 was essential for this process.
