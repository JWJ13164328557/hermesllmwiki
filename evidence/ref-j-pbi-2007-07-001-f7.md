# Consistent with a role in transcriptional repression, tpl-1 was suppressed by mutation of HAG1 (hist

## Claim
Consistent with a role in transcriptional repression, tpl-1 was suppressed by mutation of HAG1 (histone acetyltransferase, HAT, which promotes expression of target genes) and enhanced by mutation in HDA19 (histone deacetylase, HDAC, which represses target genes).
