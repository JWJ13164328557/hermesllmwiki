# Notably, sequence variations in UGT84A13 promoters led to different trans-activating activities of W

## Claim
Notably, sequence variations in UGT84A13 promoters led to different trans-activating activities of WRKY32/59, explaining the different expression patterns of UGT84A13 among the three species.

Genomic basis of the distinct 生物合成 of β‐glucogallin, a biochemical marker for hydrolyzable tannin production, in three oak species
