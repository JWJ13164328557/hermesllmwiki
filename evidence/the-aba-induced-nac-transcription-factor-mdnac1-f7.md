# Specifi- cally, MdNAC1 enhances anthocyanin production by interacting with the basic leucine zipper 

## Claim
Specifi- cally, MdNAC1 enhances anthocyanin production by interacting with the basic leucine zipper (bZIP) family member MdbZIP23 to form the MdNAC1–MdbZIP23 complex that significantly increases the transcription of downstream target genes, especially in the presence of ABA, implying MdNAC1 is highly responsive to ABA.

## Biological Context
The ABA-induced NAC transcription factor MdNAC1 interacts with a bZIP-type transcription factor to promote 花青素 synthesis in red-fleshed apples
