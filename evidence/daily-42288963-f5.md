# We found that no single SERAT isoform accounts for the full induction of OAS cluster …

## Claim
We found that no single SERAT isoform accounts for the full induction of OAS cluster genes; instead, the loss of any of the 3 major isoforms (SERAT1;1, SERAT2;1, SERAT2;2) reduced induction, with SERAT2;2 playing a predominant role.
