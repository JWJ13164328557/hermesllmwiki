# The auxin signaling pathway directly upre- gulates euAINTEGUMENTAb (SkeuANTb), and then SkeuANTb dir

## Claim
The auxin signaling pathway directly upre- gulates euAINTEGUMENTAb (SkeuANTb), and then SkeuANTb directly promotes the expression of SkeuANTa and the WUSCHEL-RELATED HOMEOBOX13b (SkWOX13b) for RAM maintenance, partially similar to the molecular pathway involving the euANT-branch PLETHORA (AtPLT) genes and AtWOX5 in root initiation in the seed plant Arabidopsis thaliana.

## Biological Context
Genome and transcriptome of Selaginella kraussiana reveal evolution of root apical meristems in vascular plants
