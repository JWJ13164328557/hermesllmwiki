# Cambium identity Cambium is a type of cell that is essential for the growth and development of tree 

## Claim
Cambium identity Cambium is a type of cell that is essential for the growth and development of tree stems, and WOX4 and PXY are considered to be cambium cell markers.

## Biological Context
Combining single-cell RNA sequencing with spatial transcriptome analysis reveals dynamic molecular maps of cambium differentiation in the primary and secondary growth of trees
