# Artificial Doublets Provide a Valid Training Set for Doublet Classification

## Claim

DoubletFinder demonstrates that artificial doublets, generated by averaging the expression profiles of randomly selected real cell pairs, provide a valid training set for supervised doublet classification, enabling doublet detection without prior knowledge of ground-truth doublets.

## Methodological Context

Doublets (two cells captured in one droplet) are a common artifact in droplet-based scRNA-seq, occurring at rates proportional to the number of cells loaded. Identifying doublets typically requires prior knowledge of cell-type-specific markers. DoubletFinder circumvents this by creating synthetic doublets from the real data and using them to train a classifier that distinguishes real singlets from artificial doublets based on gene expression features.

**Established** — Artificial doublet generation is the standard approach for training doublet classifiers.

## Alternative Models

Scrublet, DoubletDetection, and Solo (deep learning) use similar artificial doublet strategies.

## Open Questions

- At what doublet rate does the synthetic approach fail?
- Can the approach detect heterotypic doublets involving rare cell types?

## Next Critical Experiment

Systematic benchmark of DoubletFinder, Scrublet, and Solo against ground-truth doublets from cell hashing or genetic multiplexing experiments.
