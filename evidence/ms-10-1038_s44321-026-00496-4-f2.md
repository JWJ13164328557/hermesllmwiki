# We show that treatment of established TNBC metastases with miR-342 or the CDK4/6 inhibitor palbocicl

## Claim
We show that treatment of established TNBC metastases with miR-342 or the CDK4/6 inhibitor palbociclib reduces E2F signalling and speciﬁcally limits metastatic outgrowth.

## Biological Context
Metastasis of triple negative breast cancer is regulated by a targetable miR-342-E2F network
