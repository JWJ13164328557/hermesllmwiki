# Interestingly, among the constant R: B treatments, the lower proportions of red light in LT5 to LT7 

## Claim
Interestingly, among the constant R: B treatments, the lower proportions of red light in LT5 to LT7 were associated with reduced lignin levels, indicating that a minimal proportion of red light is required to effectively stimulate lignin biosynthesis.

## Biological Context
Strategic modulation of blue and red light ratios during seedling phase ameliorated cold tolerance of Viola × wittrockiana
