# Maize ZmABI4 can directly bind to the promoters of drought-responsive genes Zmrab17 and …

## Claim
Maize ZmABI4 can directly bind to the promoters of drought-responsive genes Zmrab17 and ZmADH1 to activate their transcription, and it can rescue the ABA-insensitive phenotype of the Arabidopsis abi4 mutant, suggesting functional conservation of ZmABI4 in plant drought adaptation [ ].
