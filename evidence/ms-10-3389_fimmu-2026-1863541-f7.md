# RT promotes the secretion of CSF-1 by tumor cells (27), which binds to CSF-1R on the surface of macr

## Claim
RT promotes the secretion of CSF-1 by tumor cells (27), which binds to CSF-1R on the surface of macrophages, triggering receptor dimerization and autophosphorylation, activating downstream signaling cascades to regulate macrophage migration, proliferation, and survival (28).

## Biological Context
The role of macrophages in radiation-induced lung injury: from pathological mechanisms to therapeutic targets
