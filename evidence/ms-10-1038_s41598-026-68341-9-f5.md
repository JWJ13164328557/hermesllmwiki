# Relative mRNA expression levels of the target gene PSMD14 were quantified using the 2<sup>−ΔΔCt</sup

## Claim
Relative mRNA expression levels of the target gene PSMD14 were quantified using the 2<sup>−ΔΔCt</sup> method, with GAPDH adopted as the internal reference gene for normalization.

## Biological Context
PSMD14 reduced microglial M2 polarization to promote ferroptosis and aggravate traumatic brain injury through the induction of BMP6/SMAD3 pathway
