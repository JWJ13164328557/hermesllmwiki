# Stress-responsive transcription factors from the MYB, WRKY, bZIP, AP2/ERF, NAC and basic helix-loop-

## Claim
Stress-responsive transcription factors from the MYB, WRKY, bZIP, AP2/ERF, NAC and basic helix-loop-helix families may recruit histone deacetylases to target promoters, where the removal of histone acetylation marks can repress negative regula- tors or reset previously activated genes (Hussein and El-Anssary, 2019; Lee and Seo, 2019; Jan et al., 2021; Guo et al., 2023).

## Biological Context
Integrating stress responses and secondary metabolism: the regulatory functions of histone deacetylases in medicinal plants
