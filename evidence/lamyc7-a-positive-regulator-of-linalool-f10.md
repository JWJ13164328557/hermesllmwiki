# Notably, the LaMYC7 promoter contained hormone and stress-responsive regulatory elements and respond

## Claim
Notably, the LaMYC7 promoter contained hormone and stress-responsive regulatory elements and responded to various treatments, including ultraviolet, low temperature, salt, drought, methyl jasmonate, and P.

## Biological Context
LaMYC7, a positive regulator of linalool and caryophyllene 生物合成, confers plant resistance to Pseudomonas syringae
