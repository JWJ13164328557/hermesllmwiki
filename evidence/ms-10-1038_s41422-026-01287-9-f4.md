# Activation signals such as K+ efﬂux drive NLRP3 into liquid-like condensates, initiating inﬂammasome

## Claim
Activation signals such as K+ efﬂux drive NLRP3 into liquid-like condensates, initiating inﬂammasome assembly.98 Collectively, these ﬁndings suggest that there is a dynamic interplay between organelle trafﬁcking and NLRP3 activation in response to some stimuli.
