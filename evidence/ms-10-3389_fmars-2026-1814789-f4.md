# Together, these results show a transition from a simple two-region structure in the upper bathyal zo

## Claim
Together, these results show a transition from a simple two-region structure in the upper bathyal zone to a more differentiated eight-region pattern in the mid bathyal zone, followed by broader and more spatially extensive regional units in the lower bathyal and abyssal zones.

Molecular phylogeny and fossil records reveal the origin and evolutionary history of deep-sea Ophiuroidea
