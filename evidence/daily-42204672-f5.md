# 1C), the changes in the scopoletin content of the four overexpression-positive lines were consistent

## Claim
1C), the changes in the scopoletin content of the four overexpression-positive lines were consistent with the trend of changes in their expression of McF6'H1, which were all significantly higher than that of the wild-type lines, indicating that overexpression of McF6'H1 was associated with increased scopoletin content in noni leaves.
