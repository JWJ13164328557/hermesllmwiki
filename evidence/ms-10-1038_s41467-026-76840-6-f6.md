# These results suggest that the difference in drought resistance is unlikely to be explained by NLR8 

## Claim
These results suggest that the difference in drought resistance is unlikely to be explained by NLR8 transcript abundance under drought or by the effects of the promoter variants on reporter activity.

## Biological Context
An LRR-truncated NLR natural variant confers dual resistance to drought and blast in rice
