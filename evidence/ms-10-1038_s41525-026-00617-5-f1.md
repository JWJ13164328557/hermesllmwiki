# Effective sequencing depth should therefore be interpreted in terms of usable transcript-level evide

## Claim
Effective sequencing depth should therefore be interpreted in terms of usable transcript-level evidence, including full-length/non-chi- meric read counts for whole-transcriptome experiments and on-target enrichment for targeted designs, rather than total platform output alone.
