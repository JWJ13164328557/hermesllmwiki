# By fusing catalytically inactive dCas9 with histone methyltransferases or demethylases …

## Claim
By fusing catalytically inactive dCas9 with histone methyltransferases or demethylases, it is now possible to specifically modulate H3K4me3 or H3K27me3 at individual stress-responsive loci without altering the underlying DNA sequence, thereby enabling the engineering of salt-tolerant crops with minimal risk of off-target genetic changes.
