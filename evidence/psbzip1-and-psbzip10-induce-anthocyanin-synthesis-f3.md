# In tomato, SlHY5 can bind to promoters of SlCHS1, SlCHS2, and SlDFR at sites containing G-box and AC

## Claim
In tomato, SlHY5 can bind to promoters of SlCHS1, SlCHS2, and SlDFR at sites containing G-box and ACE motifs to promote anthocyanin biosynthesis (Liu et al., 2018).
