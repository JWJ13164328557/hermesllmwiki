# Our results reveal that the transcriptional state of a cell is largely a reaction to environmental c

## Claim
Our results reveal that the transcriptional state of a cell is largely a reaction to environmental conditions that are regulated by a smaller core set of genes that stably determines cell identity.

## Biological Context
Cell identity mediates the response of Arabidopsis roots to abiotic stress.
