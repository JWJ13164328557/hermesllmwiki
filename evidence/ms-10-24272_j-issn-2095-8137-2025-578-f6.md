# RNA-seq and bioinformatics analysis revealed that the target genes of miR-184 are enriched in the TG

## Claim
RNA-seq and bioinformatics analysis revealed that the target genes of miR-184 are enriched in the TGF-β pathway, with TGF-β1 being feedback-induced by miR-184.

## Biological Context
A novel positive feedback regulatory loop, TGF-β1/SMAD4/miR-184, controls the follicular development in sows.
