# Consistently, HMGB1 inhibition attenuates I/R-induced liver injury by suppressing Kupffer cell pyrop

## Claim
Consistently, HMGB1 inhibition attenuates I/R-induced liver injury by suppressing Kupffer cell pyroptosis and downstream inflammatory escalation, supporting HMGB1-centered pyroptosis as a tractable therapeutic target in sterile hepatic injury (Hua et al., 2019).

## Biological Context
Involvement of the pyroptosis–HMGB1 axis in systemic diseases
