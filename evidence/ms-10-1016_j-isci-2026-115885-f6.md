# The neddylation pathway, required for ubiquitin-mediated selective protein degradation, plays a crit

## Claim
The neddylation pathway, required for ubiquitin-mediated selective protein degradation, plays a critical role in Sdhb-deficient imCC growth and survival: loss of the neddylation regulator Ube2m led to increased proliferation, while loss of Ube2f suppressed growth of Sdhb-deficient imCCs.

## Biological Context
Unbiased CRISPR synthetic lethal screening for genetic vulnerabilities in a succinate dehydrogenase-loss model of paraganglioma.
