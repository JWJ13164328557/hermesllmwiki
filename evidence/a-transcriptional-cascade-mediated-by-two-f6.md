# Knockout of SlAP2c in both the “MicroTom” and “Ailsa Craig” backgrounds resulted in …

## Claim
Knockout of SlAP2c in both the “MicroTom” and “Ailsa Craig” backgrounds resulted in greater lycopene accumulation, whereas overexpression of this gene led to orangeripe fruit with significantly lower lycopene contents than the wild type.
