# In summary, among NA-experienced, HBeAg-positive patients with CHB and baseline HBsAg <3,000 IU/mL, 

## Claim
In summary, among NA-experienced, HBeAg-positive patients with CHB and baseline HBsAg <3,000 IU/mL, Peg- IFNα-2b add-on therapy was associated with a rapid early decline in qHBsAg followed by a modest rebound, whereas continued NAs alone showed minimal change.

qHBsAg Trajectories with Peg-IFNα-2b Add-on Therapy in Nucleos(t)ide Analog-experienced HBeAg-positive Chronic Hepatitis B
