# Overexpression of NtMYB1 and NtMYB1 / NtbZIP53 in transgenic narcissus upregulated the …

## Claim
Overexpression of NtMYB1 and NtMYB1 / NtbZIP53 in transgenic narcissus upregulated the expression of upstream genes NtKS1 and NtGA20ox in the GA biosynthesis pathway; however, NtMYB1 and/or NtbZIP53 directly targeted the promoters of NtGA3ox2 and NtGA2ox8 and antagonistically controlled NtGA3ox2 and NtGA3ox8 transcription, respectively, and both decreased GA 1 accumulation.
