# Hepatic LDLR, a transcriptional target of SREBP228 that mediates uptake of LDL particles and apoE-ri

## Claim
Hepatic LDLR, a transcriptional target of SREBP228 that mediates uptake of LDL particles and apoE-rich chylomicron remnants, was significantly higher in formula-fed piglets than in sow-fed controls, consistent with the observed increase in hepatic SREBF2 expression.

## Biological Context
Lower-cholesterol formula feeding alters the early-life cholesterol-bile acid axis in neonatal piglets
