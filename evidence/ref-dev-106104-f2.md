# Our data show elevated expression of genes encoding photosystem I and II light-harvesting complexes,

## Claim
Our data show elevated expression of genes encoding photosystem I and II light-harvesting complexes, along with other accessory proteins of the photosynthetic machinery, in the L3 cell population; this provides molecular support for the presence of the functional chloroplast machinery in SAMs.
