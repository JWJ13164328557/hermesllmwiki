# Interestingly, MsNanA contains more than twice the number of lysines (34) as the chap- eronin-depend

## Claim
Interestingly, MsNanA contains more than twice the number of lysines (34) as the chap- eronin-dependent EcNanA and DapA proteins (16 and 13, respectively) and less than half the number of arginines (5 compared to 12 and 13, respectively).
