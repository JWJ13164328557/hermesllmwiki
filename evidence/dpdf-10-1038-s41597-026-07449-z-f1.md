# To further develop ATK as a new vertebrate model for cardiac aging, in this study, we report the fir

## Claim
To further develop ATK as a new vertebrate model for cardiac aging, in this study, we report the first scRNA-seq dataset generated from young (8 weeks-old; week 8 hereafter) and old (16 weeks-old; week 16 hereafter) female GRZ strain ATK hearts (equivalent to 30 and 60 years of age in humans, respectively).

## Biological Context
A single-cell RNA sequencing dataset of cardiac aging in African Turquoise Killifish
