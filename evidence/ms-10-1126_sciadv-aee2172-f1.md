# Collectively, these find- ings uncover a regulatory module for lignin metabolites, PtoCPK3-­ ERF72-­

## Claim
Collectively, these find- ings uncover a regulatory module for lignin metabolites, PtoCPK3-­ ERF72-­WOX13b-­UGT72AZ2, providing a mechanistic basis and genetic targets for engineering drought-­resilient tree varieties.

## Biological Context
Natural variation in <i>PtoCPK3</i> governs drought tolerance by orchestrating xylem remodeling and lignin metabolism in <i>Populus</i>
