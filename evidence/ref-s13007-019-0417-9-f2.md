# For shorter term imaging, we found that a 1% slab is the lowest concen- tration that could be handle

## Claim
For shorter term imaging, we found that a 1% slab is the lowest concen- tration that could be handled without falling apart (although this concentration is more prone to drying out over 1-week imaging periods).

## Biological Context
Week-long imaging of cell divisions in the Arabidopsis root meristem.
