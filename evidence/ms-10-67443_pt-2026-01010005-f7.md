# Since these compounds target physiological pathways unique to insects, they provide a selective mean

## Claim
Since these compounds target physiological pathways unique to insects, they provide a selective means of pest control and generally exert lower impacts on non-target organisms than conventional insecticides (Bligh Toms, 2023; Chen and Palli, 2022; Kurogi et al., 2024).
