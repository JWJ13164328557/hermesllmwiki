## Claim
We found that the Kd values for PpCHIL binding to PpCHS (for example, 19.0 µM by MST and 37.0 µM by BLI; Supplementary Tables 3 and 4) were significantly higher than the Michaelis constants (Km) of PpCHS for its ester-CoA substrates p-coumaroyl-CoA (3.8 µM) and malonyl-CoA (1.46 µM).
