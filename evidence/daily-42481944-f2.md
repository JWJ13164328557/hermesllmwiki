# Collectively, these findings suggest that ZmACO2 plays a dual and stage-dependent role …

## Claim
Collectively, these findings suggest that ZmACO2 plays a dual and stage-dependent role during ear development: at early developmental stages, moderate ZmACO2 expression enhances ethylene biosynthesis and activates ZmEREB147, facilitating the establishment of IM activity and ear length, with ZmEREB147 serving as a key regulator balancing IM maintenance and ear axis elongation.
