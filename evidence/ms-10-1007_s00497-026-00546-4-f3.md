# In this study, we report the isolation and characterization of the Sbtdr male sterile mutant in sorg

## Claim
In this study, we report the isolation and characterization of the Sbtdr male sterile mutant in sorghum, whose causal gene is orthologous to the rice TDR gene.

## Biological Context
The SbTDR gene is required for anther development and male fertility in Sorghum bicolor
