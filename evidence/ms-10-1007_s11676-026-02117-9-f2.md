# Collectively, studies across Quercus species converge on a general pattern of high within population

## Claim
Collectively, studies across Quercus species converge on a general pattern of high within population diversity and low nuclear differentiation (Kremer and Petit 1993), a pattern commonly interpreted as reflecting extensive pollen-mediated gene flow.

## Biological Context
Genetic and genomic improvement of Quercus alba productivity and resilience: a synthesis of breeding systems in white oaks (Quercus sect. Quercus)
