# While animal extracellular matrix is composed of fibrous proteins that serve as substrates for plasm

## Claim
While animal extracellular matrix is composed of fibrous proteins that serve as substrates for plasmin-mediated degradation and tissue invasion, plant cell walls are instead composed of cellulose, hemicellulose, pectin, and lignin - polysaccharide-based structures that do not interact with the plasminogen system66.

## Biological Context
Heterodera schachtii enolase does not elicit canonical immune responses in Arabidopsis thaliana
