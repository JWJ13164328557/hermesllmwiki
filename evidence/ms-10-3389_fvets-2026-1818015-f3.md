# Under the low-temperature environment of hibernation, the nuclear receptor PPARα is specifi­ cally a

## Claim
Under the low-temperature environment of hibernation, the nuclear receptor PPARα is specifi­ cally activated, and the activated PPARα binds to the response ele­ ments in the PDK4 gene promoter to substantially induce PDK4 upregulation.

## Biological Context
Mechanisms of energy metabolism reprogramming and homeostasis maintenance in overwintering hibernating animals
