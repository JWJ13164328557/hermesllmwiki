# Endodermally produced microRNA165/6 then acts to degrade its target mRNAs encoding class III homeodo

## Claim
Endodermally produced microRNA165/6 then acts to degrade its target mRNAs encoding class III homeodomain-leucine zipper transcription factors in the endodermis and stele periphery.

## Biological Context
Cell signalling by microRNA165/6 directs gene dose-dependent root cell fate.
