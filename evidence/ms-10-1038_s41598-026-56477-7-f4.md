# The biological significance of the FRK1 and NHL10 downregulation, and whether it reflects a specific

## Claim
The biological significance of the FRK1 and NHL10 downregulation, and whether it reflects a specific response to nematode-derived enolase, a generic protein-exposure effect, or a response to enolase as a conserved protein class, cannot be resolved with the present data and represents a target for future investigation using non-enolase protein controls and plant-derived enolase comparisons.

## Biological Context
Heterodera schachtii enolase does not elicit canonical immune responses in Arabidopsis thaliana
