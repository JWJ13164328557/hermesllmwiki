# Overexpression of NtMADS91 significantly increased the number and length of tobacco axillary buds, i

## Claim
Overexpression of NtMADS91 significantly increased the number and length of tobacco axillary buds, indicating that it acts as a positive regulator of axillary bud growth.

## Biological Context
Genome-wide characterization of MADS-box genes and their roles in axillary bud development in tobacco
