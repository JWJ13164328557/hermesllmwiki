# The receptor-binding domain (RBD) of SARS-CoV-2 binds to its cellular receptor angiotensin-convertin

## Claim
The receptor-binding domain (RBD) of SARS-CoV-2 binds to its cellular receptor angiotensin-converting enzyme 2 (ACE2) and is a key molecule for the efficient entry of the virus into the host cell (Saito et al., 2022; Xia et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2022).

## Biological Context
Recombinant RBD-based subunit vaccines incorporating high-frequency mutation sites elicit cross-immunity and robust protection against SARS-CoV-2
