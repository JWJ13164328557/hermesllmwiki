# To overcome the limitations imposed by data heterogeneity and technical discrepancies across studies

## Claim
To overcome the limitations imposed by data heterogeneity and technical discrepancies across studies, it is essential to establish a unified, high-resolution cortical transcriptome framework to lay the foundation for systematic research in cortical transcriptomics.
