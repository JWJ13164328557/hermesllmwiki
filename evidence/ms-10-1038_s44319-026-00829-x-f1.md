# Here, we report that lactate-induced cholesterol accumulation activates mammalian target of rapamyci

## Claim
Here, we report that lactate-induced cholesterol accumulation activates mammalian target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1) signalling under hypoxic conditions, thereby promoting hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) progression.

## Biological Context
Lactate-mediated cholesterol uptake promotes liver cancer progression via the SCARB1-autophagy axis
