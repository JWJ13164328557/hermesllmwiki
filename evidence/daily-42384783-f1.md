# Using an integrated approach combining biosynthetic pathway elucidation, regulatory …

## Claim
Using an integrated approach combining biosynthetic pathway elucidation, regulatory mechanism dissection, evolutionary analysis, and field trials, we show that the rice MYC2-JAMYB transcriptional cascade directly regulates the BSMT1 (benzoic acid/SA carboxyl methyltransferase 1)–MSOMT (methyl salicylate O -methyltransferase) enzyme cascade, driving DMSA biosynthesis in response to BPH infestation.
