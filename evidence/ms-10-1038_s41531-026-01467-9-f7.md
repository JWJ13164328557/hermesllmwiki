# Taken together, APCDD1 emerges as an attractive candidate for flow cytometry-based QC and purificati

## Claim
Taken together, APCDD1 emerges as an attractive candidate for flow cytometry-based QC and purification of DA progenitors, offering a tool to enhance the consistency and efficacy of cell replacement therapies for PD in clinical trials.

## Biological Context
APCDD1 shows high specificity for ventral midbrain dopaminergic progenitors in a cell surface marker benchmarking study
