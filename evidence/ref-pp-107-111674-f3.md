# Here, we show that an HDAC is involved in the repression of embryonic properties upon germination, b

## Claim
Here, we show that an HDAC is involved in the repression of embryonic properties upon germination, based on the inhibition of HDAC activity in germinated Arabidopsis seeds following treatment with tricho- statin A (TSA), an HDAC inhibitor.

## Biological Context
The Arabidopsis histone deacetylases HDA6 and HDA19 contribute to the repression
