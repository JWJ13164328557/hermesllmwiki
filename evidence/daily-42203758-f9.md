# BDG proteins also contain six completely conserved, novel sequence motifs within the α/β hydrolase d

## Claim
BDG proteins also contain six completely conserved, novel sequence motifs within the α/β hydrolase domain: two corresponded to the structurally conserved α-helix (Motif 9) and β-sheet (Motif 10) following the nucleophilic elbow, and four situated in a potential lid domain (Motifs 11–14).
