# We found that the transcript levels of senescence-related genes SAG12, SAG13, and SGR1 were strongly

## Claim
We found that the transcript levels of senescence-related genes SAG12, SAG13, and SGR1 were strongly induced in CA-ROP2 plants, but remained largely unchanged in the rop2/4/6 triple mutant, compared to WT (Figure 2B).

## Biological Context
Active ROP2 triggers leaf senescence and orchestrates the growth-senescence trade-off under nitrogen starvation
