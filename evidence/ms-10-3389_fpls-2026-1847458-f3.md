# In practice, single-gene-resistant cultivars that target only speciﬁc physiological races often lose

## Claim
In practice, single-gene-resistant cultivars that target only speciﬁc physiological races often lose their resistance after 4–5 years of cultivation (Strelkov et al., 2016; Strelkov, 2018; Hollman et al., 2020).

## Biological Context
Identification of candidate genes involved in root gall formation during early infection of Plasmodiophora brassicae in B.napus
