# In summary, the high expression of ﬁve GPATs, one LPAT, one LPCAT and two PLA2s from S1~S2 were sync

## Claim
In summary, the high expression of ﬁve GPATs, one LPAT, one LPCAT and two PLA2s from S1~S2 were synchronized with the rapid accumulation of oil (Figure 1D), speculating that they were jointly involved in the rapid accumulation of oil at these early stages.
