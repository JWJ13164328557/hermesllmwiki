# Emerging evidence shows that the mitophagy receptor FUNDC1 interacts with glutathione peroxidase 4 (

## Claim
Emerging evidence shows that the mitophagy receptor FUNDC1 interacts with glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4), facilitating GPX4 entry into mitochondria and its subsequent autophagic degradation, thereby promoting ferroptosis (Bi et al., 2024).
