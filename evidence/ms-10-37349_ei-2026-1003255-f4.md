# In contrast, effector immune cells—including Th1, Th17, cytotoxic T cells, and inflammatory macropha

## Claim
In contrast, effector immune cells—including Th1, Th17, cytotoxic T cells, and inflammatory macrophages—adopt glycolytic metabolism characterized by rapid glucose uptake, lactate production, and activation of mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1) and hypoxia-inducible factor-1 alpha (HIF-1α) signaling pathways [82, 85].

## Biological Context
Epigenetic and metabolic reprogramming in autoimmune rheumatology: toward immune tolerance reprogramming
