# Our findings suggest that DEX is a promising therapeutic option for severe complications associated 

## Claim
Our findings suggest that DEX is a promising therapeutic option for severe complications associated with acute METH intoxication, particularly hyperthermia and agitation.

## Biological Context
Dexmedetomidine attenuates methamphetamine-induced hyperthermia and hyperlocomotion in male rats and modulates monoamine dynamics in the anterior hypothalamus
