# During the early divisions of the pre-implantation embryo, passive demethylation occurs in the mater

## Claim
During the early divisions of the pre-implantation embryo, passive demethylation occurs in the maternally inherited genome during replication, except at imprinted sites that are essential for controlling the expression of imprinted genes at later developmental stages and in adulthood [34,37–39].
