# Here, we report the morphological characterization of anther development in wild-type sorghum BTx623

## Claim
Here, we report the morphological characterization of anther development in wild-type sorghum BTx623 and the Sorghum bicolor tapetal degeneration retardation (Sbtdr) mutant, as well as identification of the causal gene SbTDR.

## Biological Context
The SbTDR gene is required for anther development and male fertility in Sorghum bicolor
