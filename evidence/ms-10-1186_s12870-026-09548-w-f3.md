# Preliminary experiments confirmed that extraction beyond three hours yielded less than 0.5% addition

## Claim
Preliminary experiments confirmed that extraction beyond three hours yielded less than 0.5% additional oil, validating that a three‑hour Soxhlet extraction was sufficient for near‑complete oil recovery.
