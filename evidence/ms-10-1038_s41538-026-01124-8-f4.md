# We found that their total content peaked at the S2 and S3 stages and subsequently declined, a patter

## Claim
We found that their total content peaked at the S2 and S3 stages and subsequently declined, a pattern that closely mirrored the expression dynamics of four core LOX-pathway genes (CrLOX2, CrLOX4, CrLOX7, and CrADH4).

## Biological Context
Volatilomic and transcriptomic analyses uncover the mechanisms underlying flavor-related VOC changes during the harvest period of Citrus reticulata ‘Chachi’
