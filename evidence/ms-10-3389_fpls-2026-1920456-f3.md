# NPs, because of their high surface area and small size, are more likely to interact with cell walls,

## Claim
NPs, because of their high surface area and small size, are more likely to interact with cell walls, apoplastic spaces, endocytic routes, and membrane-associated transport processes (Taylor et al., 2020; Spanò et al., 2022; Zytowski et al., 2025).

## Biological Context
Brassinosteroids as phytohormonal shields against micro- and nanoplastic stress in plants
