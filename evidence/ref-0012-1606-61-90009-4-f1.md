# On the- premise that DNA synthesis is an important characteristic of cells transforming into blastem

## Claim
On the- premise that DNA synthesis is an important characteristic of cells transforming into blastema cells, these data suggest that the blastema originates from the internal tissues, and not from the inactive apical epidermis.

## Biological Context
Origin of the blastema in regenerating limbs of the newt Triturus viridescens
