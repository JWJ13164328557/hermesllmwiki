# However, GA also interacts with other hormones; for instance, it inhibits crown root growth in Oryza

## Claim
However, GA also interacts with other hormones; for instance, it inhibits crown root growth in Oryza sativa by binding to the OsGER4 protein, which negatively interacts with JA (Meng et al., 2024).

## Biological Context
Molecular mechanisms of root expansion in medicinal plants
