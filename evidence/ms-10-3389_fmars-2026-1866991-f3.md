# Marine invertebrate tissues often harbor diverse microbial commu- nities, and co-cultured microeukar

## Claim
Marine invertebrate tissues often harbor diverse microbial commu- nities, and co-cultured microeukaryotes can outcompete host cells, becoming the predominant cell type in culture that can be mistak- enly identiﬁed as target cells (Nowotny et al., 2021; Walsh et al., 2025).
