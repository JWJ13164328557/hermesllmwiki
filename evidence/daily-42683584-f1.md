# Through small RNA and messenger RNA (mRNA) sequencing, nascent RNA-sequencing, and …

## Claim
Through small RNA and messenger RNA (mRNA) sequencing, nascent RNA-sequencing, and Cleavage Under Targets and Tagmentation (CUT&Tag), we found that Piwi6 mediates transcriptional gene silencing of long terminal repeat (LTR) retrotransposons, including a full-length LTR/Gypsy-like endogenous retrovirus that we named Aedes albopictus Endogenous Retrovirus-1 ( AalERV1 ).
