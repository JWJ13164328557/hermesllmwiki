# b-Cyclocitral-induced ROS may enhance activation of MAPK cascades (MPK3/MPK6), which could phosphory

## Claim
b-Cyclocitral-induced ROS may enhance activation of MAPK cascades (MPK3/MPK6), which could phosphorylate MYC2, MYC3, and MYC4, the central JA- responsive transcription factors, thereby increasing their transcrip- tional activity and stability.
