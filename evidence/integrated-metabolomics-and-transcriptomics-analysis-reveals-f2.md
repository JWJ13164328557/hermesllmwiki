# In the present study, we found that GABA significantly reduced the wheat yield loss induced by eleva

## Claim
In the present study, we found that GABA significantly reduced the wheat yield loss induced by elevated O3, which attributed to GABA’s role in enhancing accumulation of antioxidants, including antioxidant en­ zymes, proline, as well as flavonoids, and thus inhibiting H2O2 and MDA.
