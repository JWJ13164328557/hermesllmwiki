# Notably, the highest capsule number per plant (93.33) and 1000-seed weight (3.80 g) were documented 

## Claim
Notably, the highest capsule number per plant (93.33) and 1000-seed weight (3.80 g) were documented under the interactive effects of full irrigation and a 75% nitrogen fertigation, which consequently maximized seed yield (1570.51 kg·ha⁻¹).

## Biological Context
Deficit drip irrigation and nitrogen fertigation synergistically modulate sesame performance: morpho-physiological, agronomic, and quality traits
