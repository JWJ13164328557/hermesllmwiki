# We found that GCS inhibition, or silencing, in several cancer cell lines results in cell death due t

## Claim
We found that GCS inhibition, or silencing, in several cancer cell lines results in cell death due to dysregulation of autophagy and lysosome turnover, which were not rescued by inhibition of ceramide accumulation.

## Biological Context
Targeting glucosylceramide generation induces cancer cell death through lysosomal dysfunction independent of ceramide accumulation
