# Through gene expression analysis and physiological experiments, we found that OsDUF1223 modulates dr

## Claim
Through gene expression analysis and physiological experiments, we found that OsDUF1223 modulates drought responses through the hormone abscisic acid signaling and nitrogen response pathways.

## Biological Context
OsDUF1223 negatively regulates seedling drought tolerance and modulates nitrate-dependent root growth in rice
