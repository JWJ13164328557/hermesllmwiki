# These transcriptome-wide methods revealed that the majority of human proteincoding genes …

## Claim
These transcriptome-wide methods revealed that the majority of human proteincoding genes contain more than one 3′ untranslated region (3′ UTR) pA site, indicating alternative polyadenylation (APA) as a widespread regulatory layer that generates transcript isoforms with alternative 3′ ends.
