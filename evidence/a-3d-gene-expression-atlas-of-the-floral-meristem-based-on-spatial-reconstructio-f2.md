# In summary, the presented data demonstrates that our method can be used to create a genome-wide 3D g

## Claim
In summary, the presented data demonstrates that our method can be used to create a genome-wide 3D gene expression atlas of a plant organ, and to correctly predict gene expression and gene fold-change expression of particular morphological regions that was not possible with the snRNA-seq data alone.

## Biological Context
A 3D gene expression atlas of the floral meristem based on spatial reconstruction of single nucleus RNA sequencing data
