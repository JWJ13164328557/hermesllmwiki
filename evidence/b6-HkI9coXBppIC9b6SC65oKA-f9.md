# Mechanistically, we propose that changes in CO2 availability are sensed via the capacity of the phot

## Claim
Mechanistically, we propose that changes in CO2 availability are sensed via the capacity of the photorespiratory flux, with the amount of the photorespiration-specific entrance metabolite 2-PG in chloroplasts serving as a key determinant.

## Biological Context
Guard cell photorespiration controls stomata behavior and development.
