# Importantly, these nuclei lacked canonical neuroendocrine prostate cancer (NEPC) markers such as CHG

## Claim
Importantly, these nuclei lacked canonical neuroendocrine prostate cancer (NEPC) markers such as CHGA and SYP (mean normalized CHGA expression in c4: 0.005, mean normalized SYP expression in c4: 0.05).

## Biological Context
RNAlater-compatible protocol for nuclei isolation from radical prostatectomy prostate cancer resections, enabling single-nucleus resolution RNA-seq
