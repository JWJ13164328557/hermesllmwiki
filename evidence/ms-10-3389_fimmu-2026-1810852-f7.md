# OLFML3 destabilizes RIG-I and interacts with E3 ubiquitin ligase tripartite motif protein 21 In orde

## Claim
OLFML3 destabilizes RIG-I and interacts with E3 ubiquitin ligase tripartite motif protein 21 In order to validate the action of OLFML3 on vRNA sensor proteins, we analyzed the cellular protein levels of RIG-I and MDA5 in the absence and presence of OLFML3.

## Biological Context
OLFML3 negatively regulates RIG-I signaling in RNA virus infection
