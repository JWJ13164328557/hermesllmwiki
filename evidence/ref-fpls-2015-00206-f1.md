## Claim
Here we report that, by labeling dividing stem cells with 5-ethynyl-2′- deoxyuridine (EdU), the number and distribution of their labeled progeny can be studied so that the division rate of stem cells can be measured quantitatively and in addition, that the progression of stem cell progeny differentiation can be assessed in combination with Lugol’s staining.
