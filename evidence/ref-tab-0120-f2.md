# Overexpression of CDC6 or CDT1, E2F targets encoding subunits of the pre-replication complex are suf

## Claim
Overexpression of CDC6 or CDT1, E2F targets encoding subunits of the pre-replication complex are sufficient to increase ploidy level in trichomes (Castellano et al., 2001; Castellano et al., 2004), revealing a direct action of these, and possibly other, DNA replication components in regulating the transition from the cell cycle to the endoreplication cycle.

## Biological Context
The Arabidopsis cell division cycle.
