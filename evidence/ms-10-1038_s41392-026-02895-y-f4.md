# Oncolytic viruses could target the hypoxic fraction of tumors owing to their selective replication, 

## Claim
Oncolytic viruses could target the hypoxic fraction of tumors owing to their selective replication, induction of cell lysis, and cell-to-cell spread, thereby reaching hypoxic areas that may otherwise be inaccessible to other therapeutic platforms due to poor tumor perfusion.

## Biological Context
Hypoxia increases the activity of oncolytic adenoviruses through HIF-2α-stimulated E1A expression
