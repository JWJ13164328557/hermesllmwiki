## Claim
Despite the fact that virus identiﬁcation was achieved primarily at the order level, our results support the conclusion that plant-associated viral diversity is greater in regions with lower levels of human disturbance, while viral prevalence remains broadly similar across landscapes.

## Biological Context
Composition of plant-associated RNA virus communities in two Brazilian regions with different levels of human intervention
