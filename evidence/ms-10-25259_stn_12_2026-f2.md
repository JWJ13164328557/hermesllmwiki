# Among these, bacteria are the most abundant, alongside archaea, protists, fungi, and viruses.[36] Wi

## Claim
Among these, bacteria are the most abundant, alongside archaea, protists, fungi, and viruses.[36] Within this ecosystem, microbial communities actively interact with the host by metabolising dietary components, producing a wide range of bioactive metabolites, and generating molecules that can inhibit pathogen colonisation or, in some contexts, contribute to host toxicity.
