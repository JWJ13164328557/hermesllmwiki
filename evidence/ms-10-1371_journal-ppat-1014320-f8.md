## Claim
And in this study, we found that dioctyldiethylenetriamine provided a higher control effect on diseases caused by the mutants, of which the key gene in the QS system were deleted, than the wild-type.

## Biological Context
A chemical bactericide dioctyldiethylenetriamine (Xinjunan) exerts a non-lethal effect by inhibiting RpfG activity to regulate the quorum sensing system
