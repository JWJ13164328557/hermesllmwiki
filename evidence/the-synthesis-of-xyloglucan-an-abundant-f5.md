# The CSLC proteins have striking functional similarities in that they synthesize β-1,4–linked backbon

## Claim
The CSLC proteins have striking functional similarities in that they synthesize β-1,4–linked backbones and interact with glycosyl- transferases from the GT34 family to add sugars to the 6-position of the sugars in the backbone (12).

## Biological Context
The synthesis of xyloglucan, an abundant plant cell wall polysaccharide, requires CSLC function
