# Collectively, these findings indicate that apocrine morphology is associated with distinct, ER- depe

## Claim
Collectively, these findings indicate that apocrine morphology is associated with distinct, ER- dependent mutational patterns—characterized by increased genomic instability in ER-positive disease and a context-dependent relationship with PIK3CA mutations in ER-negative versus ER-positive breast cancer.

## Biological Context
The clinical relevance of apocrine morphology in breast cancer depends on the estrogen receptor status
