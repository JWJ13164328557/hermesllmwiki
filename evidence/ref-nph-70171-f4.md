# Under hypoxic conditions created by limited gas exchange in developing calli, the …

## Claim
Under hypoxic conditions created by limited gas exchange in developing calli, the hypoxia-activated RELATED TO APETALA 2.12 (RAP2.12) directly binds to the promoter of the ICS1 gene, activating SA biosynthesis, which in turn hinders the acquisition of pluripotency and de novo shoot regeneration in calli (Koo et al., 2024).
