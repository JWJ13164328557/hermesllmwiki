# Notably, BIN2 phosphorylates ICE1, thereby pro- moting its physical interaction with HOS1 and accele

## Claim
Notably, BIN2 phosphorylates ICE1, thereby pro- moting its physical interaction with HOS1 and accelerating its proteasomal degradation (Ye et al., 2019); however, the functional crosstalk between phosphorylation and SUMOylation on ICE1 remains unclear.

## Biological Context
Post-translational modifications play indispensable roles in cold stress responses of plants
