# These results suggest that the decrease in the number of MCs moderates SAH- induced microglia activa

## Claim
These results suggest that the decrease in the number of MCs moderates SAH- induced microglia activation and also ameliorates SAH- induced damage to the astrocyte boundary.

## Biological Context
Simultaneous activation of border-associated immune cells and glial cells at the CNS-meningeal interface after subarachnoid haemorrhage in rats
