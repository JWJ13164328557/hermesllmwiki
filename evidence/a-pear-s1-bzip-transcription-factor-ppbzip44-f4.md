# Interestingly, down- or up-regulation of SDH genes, as well as S6PDH and A6PR, modulates sorbitol co

## Claim
Interestingly, down- or up-regulation of SDH genes, as well as S6PDH and A6PR, modulates sorbitol contents, leading to stamen development, pollen tube growth, and other stress responses [34–37].
