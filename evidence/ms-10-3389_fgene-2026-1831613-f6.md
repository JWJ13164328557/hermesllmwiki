# Many circRNAs are expressed at relatively low copy numbers, which may be insufficient to sequester a

## Claim
Many circRNAs are expressed at relatively low copy numbers, which may be insufficient to sequester a significant fraction of abundant miRNAs.In such cases, subcellular co-localization of circRNAs and their target miRNAs at specific functional sites (e.g.,P-bodies, stress granules) may facilitate effective competition despite low global abundance.
