# Surprisingly, isolates, such as Chicken1 and Cattle1, categorized as having a stronger host preferen

## Claim
Surprisingly, isolates, such as Chicken1 and Cattle1, categorized as having a stronger host preference for other species, were also detected in the rectal content of most animals in all treatment groups.

## Biological Context
Intestinal colonization of weaner pigs by extended-spectrum-β-lactamase-encoding Escherichia coli classified for differential host-association using a phylogenetics-based approach
