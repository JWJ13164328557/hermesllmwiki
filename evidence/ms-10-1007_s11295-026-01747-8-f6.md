# These data suggest that within developing sec­ ondary xylem, EgrARF10 is co-opted with core SCW bio­

## Claim
These data suggest that within developing sec­ ondary xylem, EgrARF10 is co-opted with core SCW bio­ synthetic and transcriptional regulatory machinery, while across a broader set of tissues and conditions, EgrARF10 has a negative co-expression relationship with these genes.

## Biological Context
Eucalyptus grandis Auxin Response Factor 10 (EgrARF10) is associated with the modulation of secondary xylem cell wall chemistry
