# Collectively, these findings indicate a recoveryassociated transcriptional transition …

## Claim
Collectively, these findings indicate a recoveryassociated transcriptional transition within the first 12 h of recovery, characterized by downregulation of photosynthesis and primary carbohydrate metabolismassociated genes and enrichment of genes associated with glyoxysomal and fatty acid βoxidation processes.
