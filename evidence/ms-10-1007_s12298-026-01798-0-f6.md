# Moreover, MYBs themselves can recruit histone deacetylases and methyltransferases to regulate histon

## Claim
Moreover, MYBs themselves can recruit histone deacetylases and methyltransferases to regulate histone acetylation (Ac) and histone methylation (Me), respectively, thereby remodeling chromatin states at target loci and coordinately regulating cold responses.
