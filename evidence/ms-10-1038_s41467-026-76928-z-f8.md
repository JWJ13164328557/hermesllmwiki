# DNA lesions, such as DSBs and SSBs, activate the phosphoinositide- 3-kinase-related protein (PIKK) k

## Claim
DNA lesions, such as DSBs and SSBs, activate the phosphoinositide- 3-kinase-related protein (PIKK) kinases, which phosphorylate the H2AX variant of histone H2A at serine 139, leading to the formation of γH2AX.

## Biological Context
TTC33 forms a complex with WDR61 and PHF5A to recruit factors relevant to genomic integrity
