# In this study, we report that ZmSPL12 also acts as a central regulator of droughtinduced …

## Claim
In this study, we report that ZmSPL12 also acts as a central regulator of droughtinduced root plasticity in maize by directly repressing the expression of the auxin biosynthesis gene ZmYUC5, thereby filling in an important gap connecting DS and auxin responses in modulating maize root development and providing a novel genetic target for breeding droughtresilient maize varieties.
