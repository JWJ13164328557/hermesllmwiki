# grandiflorus increased saponin content, while silencing of PgbHLH28 significantly inhibited saponin 

## Claim
grandiflorus increased saponin content, while silencing of PgbHLH28 significantly inhibited saponin synthesis, suggesting that PgbHLH28 acts as a positive regulator of saponin biosynthesis.

## Biological Context
