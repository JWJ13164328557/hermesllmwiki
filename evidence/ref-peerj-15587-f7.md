# Interestingly, the leaf area of cotton decreased earlier than that of photosynthesis under low potas

## Claim
Interestingly, the leaf area of cotton decreased earlier than that of photosynthesis under low potassium stress, which was attributed to the effect of potassium deficiency on mesophyll cells before the chloroplast (Hu et al., 2020).

## Biological Context
Response of <i>in situ</i> root phenotypes to potassium stress in cotton.
