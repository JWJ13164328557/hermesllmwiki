# Se NPs + biochar co-application under combined stress (T8) produced the highest gene …

## Claim
Se NPs + biochar co-application under combined stress (T8) produced the highest gene expression for all six target genes, with the following fold-changes: TaSOD1 (8.64-fold), TaCAT1 (7.86-fold), TaAPX (7.24-fold), TaDREB2 (9.38-fold), TaHSP70 (12.74-fold), and TaNHX1 (7.92-fold).
