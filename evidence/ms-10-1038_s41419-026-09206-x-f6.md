## Claim
To definitively determine whether HCK kinase activity is required for M1 polarization, we reconstituted HCK‑knockout macrophages with either wild-type HCK (HCK-WT) or kinase-dead HCK (HCK-K288E).

## Biological Context
Targeting HCK in M1 macrophages alleviates postoperative abdominal adhesions by suppressing NF-κB/IL6 axis-driven mesothelial-to-mesenchymal transition
