# Based on published resequencing data for wild and cultivated soybeans, including landraces and culti

## Claim
Based on published resequencing data for wild and cultivated soybeans, including landraces and cultivars, we found that 7 eQTL hotspots at 14 DAF and 5 eQTL hotspots at 21 DAF experienced strong artiﬁcial selection during the domestication of soybean (Figure S3b, Supporting Information).
