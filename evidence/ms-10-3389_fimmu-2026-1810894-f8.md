# Collectively, these data indicate that although RSTRs remain invisible to our current arsenal of TB 

## Claim
Collectively, these data indicate that although RSTRs remain invisible to our current arsenal of TB diagnostics, RSTRs elicit detectable IgG responses to a distinct subset of Mtb proteins than do individuals with LTBI.
