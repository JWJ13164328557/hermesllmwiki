# We found that genes speciﬁcally expressed in the primary vascular cells of cotton, which are the mai

## Claim
We found that genes speciﬁcally expressed in the primary vascular cells of cotton, which are the main cell type for callus initiation, were generally enriched in Arabidopsis CIM1 cells, and the largest proportion of these genes were co-expressed with Arabidopsis XPP cells.

## Biological Context
A single-cell transcriptome atlas reveals the trajectory of early cell fate transition during callus induction in Arabidopsis
