# Towards the differentiation pathway, MP directly regulates LEAFY (LFY), AINTEGUMENTA (ANT) and ANTEG

## Claim
Towards the differentiation pathway, MP directly regulates LEAFY (LFY), AINTEGUMENTA (ANT) and ANTEGUMENTA-LIKE6 (AIL6) transcription factors, which are required for ﬂoral fate acquisition and ﬂower outgrowth [45,47,48].
