# AGO2 and EIF4A3 were the common RNA- binding proteins predicted to interact with these five circRNAs

## Claim
AGO2 and EIF4A3 were the common RNA- binding proteins predicted to interact with these five circRNAs, which is consistent with previous studies reporting that AGO2 and EIF4A3 participate in hypoxic regulation (Hale et al., 2014; Li et al., 2020).
