# Our data reveal Ht1a and Gg1a as promising candidates for the development of novel varroa mite treat

## Claim
Our data reveal Ht1a and Gg1a as promising candidates for the development of novel varroa mite treatments of honeybee hives.

## Biological Context
Spider venom peptides Ht1a and Gg1a are toxic to honeybee parasite <i>Varroa destructor</i> by topical application.
