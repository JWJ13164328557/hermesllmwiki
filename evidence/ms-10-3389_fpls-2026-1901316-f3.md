## Claim
Notably, plants sprayed with 3 g L-1 ADY + 1.5 ml L-1SW + 0.5 g L-1 GT had the highest TCS (13.44 and 13.45%), N (2.80 and 2.88%), P (0.67 and 0.70%), and K (2.11 and 2.51%) compared with the other natural extract treatments in both seasons.
