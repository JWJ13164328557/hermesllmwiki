# By examining the distribution of the 424 single-cell-type marker genes across the 16 annotated soybe

## Claim
By examining the distribution of the 424 single-cell-type marker genes across the 16 annotated soybean root clusters, we found that these genes were restricted to 9 clusters (i.e., root hair cluster 3, root cap 4, dividing cells 6, cortex 7, endodermis 10 and 11, xylem 14, phloem ﬁber 15, and phloem 16; Supplemental Table 1), likely reﬂecting the biological specialization of these cell types.
