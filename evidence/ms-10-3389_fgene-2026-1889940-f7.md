# Collectively, these data demonstrate that TPI1 is frequently overexpressed in HNSCC at both the mRNA

## Claim
Collectively, these data demonstrate that TPI1 is frequently overexpressed in HNSCC at both the mRNA and protein levels, and that elevated TPI1 expression correlates with poor clinical outcome, supporting its potential as a prognostic biomarker for HNSCC.

## Biological Context
TPI1 promotes malignant progression and ferroptosis resistance in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma
