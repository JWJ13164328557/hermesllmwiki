# (iv) PpERF9 interacts with the co-repressor PpTOPLESS1 via EAR motifs to form a complex that removes

## Claim
(iv) PpERF9 interacts with the co-repressor PpTOPLESS1 via EAR motifs to form a complex that removes the acetyl group on histone H3, maintaining low levels of acetylated histone H3 in the promoter regions of PpMYB114 and PpRAP2.4.

## Biological Context
The ethylene-responsive transcription factor PpERF9 抑制 PpRAP2.4 and PpMYB114 via histone deacetylation to inhibit 花青素 生物合成 in pear
