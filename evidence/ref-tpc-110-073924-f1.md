# CLV3 is active as a 13–amino acid arabinosylated peptide that diffuses until it binds to its recepto

## Claim
CLV3 is active as a 13–amino acid arabinosylated peptide that diffuses until it binds to its receptors: the LRR receptor kinase CLV1, the receptor-like kinase CLV2, and the Ser-Thr kinase CORYNE (Rojo et al., 2002; Lenhard and Laux, 2003; Kondo et al., 2006; Muller et al., 2008; Ohyama et al., 2009).

## Biological Context
How a plant builds leaves.
