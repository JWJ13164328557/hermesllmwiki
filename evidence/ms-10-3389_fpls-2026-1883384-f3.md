# Nanoencapsulation of plasma-generated nitrogen is essential for reducing nitrogen losses, addressing

## Claim
Nanoencapsulation of plasma-generated nitrogen is essential for reducing nitrogen losses, addressing signiﬁcant risks, and increasing yields, as nanoparticles offer improved physico- chemical properties of fertilizers and biological properties, such as a large surface area-to-volume ratio (Abdel-aziz et al., 2021).

## Biological Context
Exploring slow-release plasma nitrogen nano-fertilizer for improved maize production and nutrient use efficiency
