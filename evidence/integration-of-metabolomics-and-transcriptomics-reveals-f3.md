# Here, we found that the glandular heads of type VI trichomes were clearly distinguishable between AC

## Claim
Here, we found that the glandular heads of type VI trichomes were clearly distinguishable between AC and SH under cryo-scanning electron microscopy, the difference indicating that SH could secrete more anti-insect metabolites than AC.
