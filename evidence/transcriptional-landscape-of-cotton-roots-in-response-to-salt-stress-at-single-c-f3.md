# Some genes participate in the biological synthesis of regulatory factors such as abscisic acid (ABA)

## Claim
Some genes participate in the biological synthesis of regulatory factors such as abscisic acid (ABA) and ethylene, thus activating the expression of TFs that bind speciﬁcally to the promoter sequences of target genes to further activate or inhibit the expression of downstream functional genes, ultimately playing major roles in response to abiotic stress (Zhang et al., 2022a, 2022b, 2022c).
