# Our results suggest that prenatal exposure to low-dose cesium-137 enhances susceptibility to HFD-dri

## Claim
Our results suggest that prenatal exposure to low-dose cesium-137 enhances susceptibility to HFD-driven tumorigenesis in males, potentially through IR, mediated epigenomic reprogramming of metabolic progenitor cells during development, a mechanism previously demonstrated in mice 49.

## Biological Context
Prenatal low-dose cesium-137 exposure combined with a lipid-enriched diet promotes late-onset hepatic tumorigenesis in male mouse offspring
