# Across these crops, one recurring theme is that short-wavelength–enriched spectra tend to shift carb

## Claim
Across these crops, one recurring theme is that short-wavelength–enriched spectra tend to shift carbon towards secondary metabolism and stress‑related pathways, whereas red and far‑red light more strongly modulates sink strength, fruit growth, and ripening dynamics.

## Biological Context
Postharvest preservation of fruits and vegetables by light-emitting diodes
