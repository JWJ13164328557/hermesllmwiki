# We found that, although SOAT1 is a key enzyme in cholesterol esterification, its expression was not 

## Claim
We found that, although SOAT1 is a key enzyme in cholesterol esterification, its expression was not significantly correlated with circulating lipid profiles (TC, TG, HDL- C, LDL-C, Apo-A1, Apo-B, Lp(a)).

## Biological Context
SOAT1 expression associates with prognosis, immune environment and biochemical indicators in glioma
