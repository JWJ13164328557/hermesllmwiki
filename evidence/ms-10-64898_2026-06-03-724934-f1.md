## Claim
Altogether, these findings suggest that both USP7 and RING1B might be contributing to the maintenance of the oncogenic transcriptional programs in FP-RMS by regulating PAX3::FOXO1 target genes, while their depletion promotes myogenic differentiation.

## Biological Context
USP7 sustains PAX3::FOXO1 enhancer reprogramming and represents a therapeutic vulnerability in Rhabdomyosarcoma
