# NSC, composed primarily of soluble sugars and starch, repre- sents the carbon storage form in plants

## Claim
NSC, composed primarily of soluble sugars and starch, repre- sents the carbon storage form in plants and are critical for plant growth, metabolism and stress responses (Signori−Müller et al., 2021).

## Biological Context
Stage-dependent shifts in non-structural carbohydrates and C, N stoichiometry in Setaria viridis along a salinity gradient in the Xiaokai River irrigation area of the Yellow River Delta, China
