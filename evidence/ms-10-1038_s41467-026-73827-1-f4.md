# Here, we show that inhibition of sphingosine kinase-2 (SphK2), the enzyme generating S1P in MDSCs, r

## Claim
Here, we show that inhibition of sphingosine kinase-2 (SphK2), the enzyme generating S1P in MDSCs, reduces the suppressive activity of monocytic MDSCs (M-MDSCs) while promoting their differentiation toward a mature, immunogenic phenotype characterized by enhanced antigen presentation.

## Biological Context
Sphingosine kinase-2 inhibition promotes immunogenic differentiation of myeloid-derived suppressor cells through an Acetyl-CoA carboxylase-phosphatidylcholine axis
