# Notably, the results of the Y1H and LUC assays showed that LaMYC7 can only bind to the LaTPS76 promo

## Claim
Notably, the results of the Y1H and LUC assays showed that LaMYC7 can only bind to the LaTPS76 promoter, indicating that LaMYC7 regulates caryophyllene synthesis by binding to the LaTPS76 promoter.

## Biological Context
LaMYC7, a positive regulator of linalool and caryophyllene 生物合成, confers plant resistance to Pseudomonas syringae
