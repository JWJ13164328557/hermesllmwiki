# The mechanism behind the peptide's ability to enhance generative components is related to its role a

## Claim
The mechanism behind the peptide's ability to enhance generative components is related to its role as a biological elicitor that modifies the balance of endogenous hormones, particularly the cytokinin and gibberellin groups, and stimulates the expression of target genes encoding flowering initiation (Franzoni et al., 2023).
