# These results confirm that soaking seeds with germination stimulants plays a crucial role in overcom

## Claim
These results confirm that soaking seeds with germination stimulants plays a crucial role in overcoming physiological dormancy caused by the hard structure of Arabica coffee bean hulls, which are not very permeable to water.

## Biological Context
The Effect of Soaking Medium Type on the Viability, Vigor, and Early Growth of Arabica Coffee Seeds (Coffea arabica L.)
