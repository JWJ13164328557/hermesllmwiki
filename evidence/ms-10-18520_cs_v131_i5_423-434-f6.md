# Water A steep gradient of water vapour potential between the stem and the environment …

## Claim
Water A steep gradient of water vapour potential between the stem and the environment enclosing it is sufficient to lead to water entry from the atmosphere into the stem; the periderm and rhytidome also form a protective layer that minimises water loss, facilitating more water conservation for photosynthesis in the cortical region2.
