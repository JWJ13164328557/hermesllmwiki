# Among physical factors, light plays a key role in regulating cellular reprogramming through its effe

## Claim
Among physical factors, light plays a key role in regulating cellular reprogramming through its effects on auxin stability, hormonal balance, and primary metabolism, thereby influencing dedifferentiation efficiency (Cioć et al., 2022; Miguel, 2025).

## Biological Context
Evaluation of the efficiency of the optimal combination of  hormonal, physical  and biological stimuli in the induction and growth of callus in Lavandula angustifolia Mill
