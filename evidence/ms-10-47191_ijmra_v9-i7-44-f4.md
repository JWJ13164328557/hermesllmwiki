# Root hairs are extensions of epidermal cells that play a crucial role in water and nutrient uptake; 

## Claim
Root hairs are extensions of epidermal cells that play a crucial role in water and nutrient uptake; the better development of root hairs in treatments B1 and B2 indicates that the seedlings in these two treatments are physiologically better prepared to absorb water and nutrients from the growing medium immediately after germination.

## Biological Context
The Effect of Soaking Medium Type on the Viability, Vigor, and Early Growth of Arabica Coffee Seeds (Coffea arabica L.)
