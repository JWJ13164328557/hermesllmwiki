# We found that N assimilation and AA metabolism in roots were dominated by the Glu-Gln/Thea- Orn-Arg 

## Claim
We found that N assimilation and AA metabolism in roots were dominated by the Glu-Gln/Thea- Orn-Arg pathway, while the long-distance root-leaf transport of N occurs mainly in the form of Arg, Glu, Gln, and Thea.
