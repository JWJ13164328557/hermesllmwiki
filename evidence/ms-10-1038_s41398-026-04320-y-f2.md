# Our findings reveal a significant reduction in low-frequency coupling within the sensorimotor networ

## Claim
Our findings reveal a significant reduction in low-frequency coupling within the sensorimotor network (SMN) in patients with schizophrenia compared to healthy controls, and notably, this low-frequency coupling is significantly correlated with the severity of delusional symptoms.

## Biological Context
Frequency-specific structure-function decoupling underlies core symptoms in schizophrenia
