# This paradigm posits that clonal neoantigens repre­ sent optimal immunotherapeutic targets because T

## Claim
This paradigm posits that clonal neoantigens repre­ sent optimal immunotherapeutic targets because T cells recognising these antigens can theoretically eliminate the entire tumour mass, whereas responses against subclonal neoantigens target only tumour subpopulations [10, 11].
