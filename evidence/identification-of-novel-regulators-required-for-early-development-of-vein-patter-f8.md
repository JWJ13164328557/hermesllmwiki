# The phloem SE-like cells and xylem tracheary elements can be induced by VISUAL, and genetic analysis

## Claim
The phloem SE-like cells and xylem tracheary elements can be induced by VISUAL, and genetic analysis has conﬁrmed that ALTERED PHLOEM DEVELOPMENT (APL) plays a central role in SE differenti- ation via VISUAL (Kondo et al., 2015).
