# Our data suggest that putative NuRD components are induced in the blastema during fin regeneration, 

## Claim
Our data suggest that putative NuRD components are induced in the blastema during fin regeneration, and are involved in the maintenance of blastema cell proliferation and in rediffer- entiation during the regenerative outgrowth phase.

## Biological Context
Specific NuRD components are required for fin regeneration in zebrafish.
