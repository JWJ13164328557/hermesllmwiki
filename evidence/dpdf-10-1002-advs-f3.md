# Notably, only 0.16–0.29% (50/17020, 14 DAF; 28/17020, 21 DAF) of lncRNAs exhibited expression across

## Claim
Notably, only 0.16–0.29% (50/17020, 14 DAF; 28/17020, 21 DAF) of lncRNAs exhibited expression across all 238 accessions, whereas ≈13–28% (14776/52872, 14 DAF; 7082/52872, 21 DAF) of PCGs were universally expressed in all accessions (Figure 1c; Table S3, Supporting Information).
