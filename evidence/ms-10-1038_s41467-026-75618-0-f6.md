# Using rice lines with OsPIMT overexpression, RNAi-mediated suppression, and genome editing, we show 

## Claim
Using rice lines with OsPIMT overexpression, RNAi-mediated suppression, and genome editing, we show that PIMT enhances tolerance to sheath blight (ShB) caused by Rhizoctonia solani.

## Biological Context
Rice PROTEIN L-ISOASPARTYL METHYLTRANSFERASES provides tolerance against sheath blight disease and repairs ALDH and PBZ1
