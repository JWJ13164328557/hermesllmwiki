# Consistent with increased miR-1247-5p activity, we observed a trend towards downregulation of Trapc9

## Claim
Consistent with increased miR-1247-5p activity, we observed a trend towards downregulation of Trapc9 and Synj2bp—miR-1247-5p target candidates identified by Ago2 CLIP-seq [36]—in matching organs (S9b Fig).

## Biological Context
A microRNA atlas of CD4+ T cell subsets in experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis identifies regulators of disease pathogenesis
