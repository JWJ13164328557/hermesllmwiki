# Based on a high-quality 39 genome assembly, mRNA sequencing (mRNA-seq) data, and single-cell RNA-seq

## Claim
Based on a high-quality 39 genome assembly, mRNA sequencing (mRNA-seq) data, and single-cell RNA-seq data for 40 the lycophyte Selaginella kraussiana, here we show that the two root origin events in 41 lycophytes and euphyllophytes adopted partially similar molecular modules in the 42 regulation of root apical meristem (RAM) development.

## Biological Context
An improved de novo assembly and annotation of the tomato reference genome using single-molecule sequencing, Hi-C proximity ligation and optical maps
