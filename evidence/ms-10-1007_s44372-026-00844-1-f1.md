# Collectively, these examples span editing targets from guard cells to vascular tissue to root merist

## Claim
Collectively, these examples span editing targets from guard cells to vascular tissue to root meristems, illustrating the value of a multi-target precision editing strategy though, as the OsNAC2 case shows, each target’s evidence base should be evaluated on its own terms rather than assumed to be uniformly strong.
