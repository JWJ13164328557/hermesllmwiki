# It does not simply mean that a fungicide has a known molecular mode of action, because many conventi

## Claim
It does not simply mean that a fungicide has a known molecular mode of action, because many conventional fungicides already act on defined targets such as sterol biosynthesis, mitochondrial respiration, tubulin polymerization, or cell-wall biosynthesis.
