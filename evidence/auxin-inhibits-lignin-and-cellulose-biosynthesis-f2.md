## Claim
In conclusion, our study shows that the ‘PbrARF13- PbrNSC-PbrMYB132’ regulatory cascade mediates the biosynthesis of lignin and cellulose in stone cells of pear fruit in response to auxin signals and also provides new insights into plant SCW formation.
