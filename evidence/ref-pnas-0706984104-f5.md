# The fact that the suberin-associated enzymes GPAT5 and CYP86A1 are required to function …

## Claim
The fact that the suberin-associated enzymes GPAT5 and CYP86A1 are required to function together to produce typical suberin monomers in the transgenic cuticle and that both CYP86A2 and GPAT4/8 are required for endogenous cutin monomer synthesis highlights the existence of conserved features in the biosynthetic machineries of cutin and suberin.
