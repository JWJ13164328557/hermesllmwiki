# Nutritional interventions including calorie restriction (CR) and rapamycin extend healthspan via mol

## Claim
Nutritional interventions including calorie restriction (CR) and rapamycin extend healthspan via molecular mechanisms converging on suppression of the mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1) 74, 75, a molecule that antagonizes autophagy induction 76, 77.

## Biological Context
Geroprotective insights into the natural metabolite spermidine in aging and age-related diseases
