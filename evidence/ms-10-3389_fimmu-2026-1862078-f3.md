# In a stimulus- and context-dependent manner, TIFA Thr9 phosphorylation can be mediated by distinct u

## Claim
In a stimulus- and context-dependent manner, TIFA Thr9 phosphorylation can be mediated by distinct upstream kinases: ALPK1 directly phosphorylates TIFA in ADP-heptose sensing (11), Aurora A promotes Thr9 phosphorylation in AML/ TNF-a-associated signaling (12), and AKT-associated signaling has also been implicated in TNFR-induced Thr9 phosphorylation (13).

## Biological Context
TIFA: a signaling hub linking inflammation, innate immunity, and human disease
