# These findings suggest that squalene may exert a vasculo-protective effect, potentially through enha

## Claim
These findings suggest that squalene may exert a vasculo-protective effect, potentially through enhanced endothelial nitric oxide bioavailability and reduced oxidative vascular stress, mechanisms that have been described for antioxidant interventions in cardiovascular populations [23,29].

## Biological Context
Dose-dependent effects of squalene on blood pressure and cellular energy biomarkers in patients with stable ischemic heart disease
