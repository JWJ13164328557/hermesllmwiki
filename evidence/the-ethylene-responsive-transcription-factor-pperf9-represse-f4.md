# Furthermore, PpERF9 interacts with the co-repressor PpTOPLESS1 (PpTPL1) via EAR motifs to form a com

## Claim
Furthermore, PpERF9 interacts with the co-repressor PpTOPLESS1 (PpTPL1) via EAR motifs to form a complex that removes the acetyl group on histone H3 and maintains low levels of acetylated H3 in the PpMYB114 and PpRAP2.4 promoter regions.

## Biological Context
The ethylene-responsive transcription factor PpERF9 抑制 PpRAP2.4 and PpMYB114 via histone deacetylation to inhibit 花青素 生物合成 in pear
