# After SA treatment, the conformation of SmNPR4 protein bind to SA is changed, which releases its rep

## Claim
After SA treatment, the conformation of SmNPR4 protein bind to SA is changed, which releases its repression of SmTGA5, thereby inducing the transcriptional upregulation of SmTAT1 and fostering the accumulation of phenolic acids.

## Biological Context
The SmNPR4-SmTGA5 模块 调控 SA-介导的 phenolic acid 生物合成 in Salvia miltiorrhiza hairy roots
