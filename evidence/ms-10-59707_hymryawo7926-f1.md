# Other candidate genes in acquired TTP were discovered to be targets for drug discov- ery, such as HL

## Claim
Other candidate genes in acquired TTP were discovered to be targets for drug discov- ery, such as HLAs DRB1-11 and DQB1-03 which were found to be susceptibility alleles, and DRB1-04 which had a protective effect against the development of TTP (66).
