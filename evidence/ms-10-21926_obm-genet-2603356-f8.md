# Auxin, cytokinin, and gibberellin signaling generally decline during osmotic stress and interact wit

## Claim
Auxin, cytokinin, and gibberellin signaling generally decline during osmotic stress and interact with ABA, SA, ET, and JA as resources are redirected from growth toward defense.
