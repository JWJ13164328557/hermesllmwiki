# Further, we found that constitutively active ROP2 (CA-ROP2) highly upregulates the expres­ sion of k

## Claim
Further, we found that constitutively active ROP2 (CA-ROP2) highly upregulates the expres­ sion of key leaf senescence-promoting transcription factors (e.g., ORE1, NAP, ANAC005), while strongly suppressing the expression of leaf senescence-inhibitory transcription factors (e.g., HASTY, NIGT1, WRKY53) under prolonged N starvation.

## Biological Context
Active ROP2 triggers leaf senescence and orchestrates the growth-senescence trade-off under nitrogen starvation
