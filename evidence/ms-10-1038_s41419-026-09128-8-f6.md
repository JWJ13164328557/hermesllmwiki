# Peanut agglutinin (PNA) binds to the acrosome and can be used to identify the stages of sperm morpho

## Claim
Peanut agglutinin (PNA) binds to the acrosome and can be used to identify the stages of sperm morphogenesis and classify the corresponding cell types during the seminiferous epithelial cycle.
