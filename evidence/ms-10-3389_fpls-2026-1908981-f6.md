# Iron in particular is essential for chlorophyll synthesis, and HA can increase iron availability, in

## Claim
Iron in particular is essential for chlorophyll synthesis, and HA can increase iron availability, indirectly promote chlorophyll formation, and thus increase SPAD values (Turan et al., 2022).
