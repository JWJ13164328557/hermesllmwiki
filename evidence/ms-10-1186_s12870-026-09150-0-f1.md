# Citric acid (CA) is a naturally occurring organic acid that plays a central role in plant energy met

## Claim
Citric acid (CA) is a naturally occurring organic acid that plays a central role in plant energy metabolism as part of the tricarboxylic acid (Krebs) cycle [41].

## Biological Context
Integrative co-application of citric acid and halotolerant/halophilic citrate-utilizing PGPR enhances leaf ionic homeostasis, productivity, and quality of Vitis vinifera L. in saline-calcareous soils
