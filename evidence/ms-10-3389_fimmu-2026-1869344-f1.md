# Molecularly, mAb 3D11 infusion led to a downmodulation of CD28 expression on porcine T cells in vivo

## Claim
Molecularly, mAb 3D11 infusion led to a downmodulation of CD28 expression on porcine T cells in vivo with the remaining CD28 molecules blocked from binding natural ligand proteins CD80 and CD86, as we show in this publication.

## Biological Context
Treatment with saturating dose of conventional anti-CD28 monoclonal antibody well tolerated in pig acute myocardial infarction model
