# Isoxazole derivatives readily interact with diverse enzymes and receptors, and their incorporation i

## Claim
Isoxazole derivatives readily interact with diverse enzymes and receptors, and their incorporation into drug scaffolds can modulate activities such as antiviral, anticancer, immunomodulatory, anti-inflammatory, analgesic (Sysak and Obmińska-Mrukowicz, 2017), antioxidant (Hawash et al., 2022), antibacterial, and antidiabetic effects.

## Biological Context
Design and in silico evaluation of Thiazole–Isoxazole hybrids as PPARγ-Targeted antidiabetic agents
