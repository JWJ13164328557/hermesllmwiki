# Despite the intrinsic diversity of murine BCR repertoires (77), we found that FMT induced a concerte

## Claim
Despite the intrinsic diversity of murine BCR repertoires (77), we found that FMT induced a concerted immune response, marked by the emergence of highly stereotypic BCR clonotypes in both the LG and spleen of the NOD-FMT group.

## Biological Context
Gut microbiota transfer from autoimmune dry eye mice imprints stereotypic B cell receptor repertoires in the lacrimal gland and induces disease
