# Notably, TfR1 preferentially binds to pro-inﬂammatory iRhom2 over iRhom1, with the cytosolic N termi

## Claim
Notably, TfR1 preferentially binds to pro-inﬂammatory iRhom2 over iRhom1, with the cytosolic N terminus of iRhom serving as a critical binding determinant.

## Biological Context
Transferrin receptor 1 shedding by the pro-inflammatory iRhom–ADAM17 complex and ADAM10 regulates cellular iron uptake and ferroptosis
