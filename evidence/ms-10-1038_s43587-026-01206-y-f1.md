# In summary, our results show that cGAS functions to maintain heterochromatin organization in the nuc

## Claim
In summary, our results show that cGAS functions to maintain heterochromatin organization in the nucleus, independent of its cytoplasmic role as a DNA sensor or its catalytic activity, with implications for geroprotective strategies targeting this pathway.

## Biological Context
cGAS-deficient mice display premature aging associated with derepression of LINE1 elements and inflammation
