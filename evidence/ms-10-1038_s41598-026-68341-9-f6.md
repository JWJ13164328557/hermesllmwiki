# With bregma set as the coordinate origin, a small ~1 mm × 1 mm craniotomy was drilled over target br

## Claim
With bregma set as the coordinate origin, a small ~1 mm × 1 mm craniotomy was drilled over target brain regions (AcbC, VTA) per standard brain atlas coordinates, without damaging the underlying dura mater.

## Biological Context
PSMD14 reduced microglial M2 polarization to promote ferroptosis and aggravate traumatic brain injury through the induction of BMP6/SMAD3 pathway
