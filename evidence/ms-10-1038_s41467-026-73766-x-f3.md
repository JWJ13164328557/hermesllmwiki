# Here, we discover that the inner mitochondrial membrane protein Fmp30p, a member of the metallo-b-la

## Claim
Here, we discover that the inner mitochondrial membrane protein Fmp30p, a member of the metallo-b-lactamase superfamily, displays phospholipase type D activity toward phosphatidylinositol (PI)—a notable mitochondrial membrane component with unclear functional roles.

## Biological Context
Fmp30p is a mitochondrial phosphatidylinositol hydrolase that modulates CoQ biosynthesis
