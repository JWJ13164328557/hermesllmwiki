# These results suggest that the accumulation of umami- and sweet-tasting amino acids during yellowing

## Claim
These results suggest that the accumulation of umami- and sweet-tasting amino acids during yellowing contributes substantially to the characteristic taste of MDBYT 12, while the increase in bitter amino acids during later stages may enhance thickness and flavor complexity.
