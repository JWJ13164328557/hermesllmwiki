# When sensing JA sig- naling, COI1 binds to SKP1‐LIKE PROTEIN1/2, CULLIN 1, and RING‐box PROTEIN 1 to

## Claim
When sensing JA sig- naling, COI1 binds to SKP1‐LIKE PROTEIN1/2, CULLIN 1, and RING‐box PROTEIN 1 to form the SCFCOI1 complex; the SCFCOI1 complex initiates the JA signaling response by tar- geting ubiquitination and degradation of JAZ proteins.

## Biological Context
MdbHLH162 connects the gibberellin and jasmonic acid signals to regulate 花青素 生物合成 in apple
