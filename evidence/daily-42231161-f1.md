# Stable overexpression of PavNHX37 in Nicotiana benthamiana significantly improved salt …

## Claim
Stable overexpression of PavNHX37 in Nicotiana benthamiana significantly improved salt tolerance: transgenic lines exhibited lower Na + accumulation, higher K + retention, and a reduced Na + /K + ratio, along with better growth, higher proline accumulation, elevated SOD, POD and CAT activities, and reduced MDA content under 150 mM NaCl compared to wildtype plants.
