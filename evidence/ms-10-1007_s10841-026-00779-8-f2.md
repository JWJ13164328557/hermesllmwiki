# Effects of PSHB-infested trees on beetle assemblages We found higher beetle species richness in plot

## Claim
Effects of PSHB-infested trees on beetle assemblages We found higher beetle species richness in plots with higher proportions of PSHB-infested trees.

## Biological Context
Native Scolytinae and Platypodinae beetle assemblages in indigenous South African forests and their co-occurrence with the invasive PSHB beetle
