# In hybrid aspen, photoperiod is the major environmental factor to induces dormancy in cambial merist

## Claim
In hybrid aspen, photoperiod is the major environmental factor to induces dormancy in cambial meristem (Baba et al., 2011), and photoperiod also plays a central role in early wood and late wood formation (Déjardin et al., 2010).
