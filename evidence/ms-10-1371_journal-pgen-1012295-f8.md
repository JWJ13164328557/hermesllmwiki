# Collectively, overexpression of OsDUF1223 modulates the expression of genes associated with stress r

## Claim
Collectively, overexpression of OsDUF1223 modulates the expression of genes associated with stress response, hormone signal transduction, and nitrogen metabolism.

## Biological Context
OsDUF1223 negatively regulates seedling drought tolerance and modulates nitrate-dependent root growth in rice
