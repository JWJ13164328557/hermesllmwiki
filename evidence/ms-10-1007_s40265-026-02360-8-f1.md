# Intracellularly, BAFF bind- ing to BAFF-R strongly activates the non-canonical nuclear factor kappa 

## Claim
Intracellularly, BAFF bind- ing to BAFF-R strongly activates the non-canonical nuclear factor kappa B subunit 2 (NF-κB2) pathway and weakly activates the classical NF-κB1 pathway, while also engag- ing phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)/protein kinase B (AKT)/mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR) signal- ling to support protein synthesis and metabolic fitness [42].
