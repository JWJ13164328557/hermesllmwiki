# Interestingly, in tomatoes and kiwifruit, CPPU-induced fruit set is partially dependent on GA and au

## Claim
Interestingly, in tomatoes and kiwifruit, CPPU-induced fruit set is partially dependent on GA and auxin biosynthesis; exogenous application of paclobutrazol (PAC) inhibits CPPU-induced parthenocarpy, but this can be rescued by exogenous GA application [21, 25].
