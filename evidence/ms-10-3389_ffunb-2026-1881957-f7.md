# In yeast, Atg1 has been shown to phosphorylate Atg4 at the site of autophagosome biogenesis, thereby

## Claim
In yeast, Atg1 has been shown to phosphorylate Atg4 at the site of autophagosome biogenesis, thereby inhibiting Atg4 protease activ- ity and facilitating autophagosome formation (Sánchez-Wandelmer et al., 2017).

## Biological Context
Hydrogen peroxide as a multifaceted regulator of the Atg4 protease and autophagy in the pathogenic fungus Alternaria alternata
