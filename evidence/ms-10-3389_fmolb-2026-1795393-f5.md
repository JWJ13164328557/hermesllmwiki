# Understanding the metabolic shifts during the transition from healthy wood to highly resinous tissue

## Claim
Understanding the metabolic shifts during the transition from healthy wood to highly resinous tissue is crucial for identifying the molecular markers of high-quality “Dragon’s Blood.” To capture the complexity of the Dracaena metabolome, high- resolution analytical strategies are required.

## Biological Context
Integrative metabolomics and molecular networking reveal the progressive metabolic continuum of resin formation in Dracaena cochinchinensis wood
