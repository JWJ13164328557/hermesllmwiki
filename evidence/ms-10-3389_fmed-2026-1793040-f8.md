# The increased biosynthesis of kynurenine from tryptophan played an important role in maintaining cel

## Claim
The increased biosynthesis of kynurenine from tryptophan played an important role in maintaining cellular energetics through de novo synthesis of pyridine nucleotide NAD+, which is essential for cellular metabolism and function (13, 26).

## Biological Context
Metabolic biomarkers differentiate extrapulmonary tuberculosis from pulmonary TB and non-TB pleural effusions
