# 5 Conclusion This study demonstrates that NaCl-induced salinity stress markedly impairs the growth a

## Claim
5 Conclusion This study demonstrates that NaCl-induced salinity stress markedly impairs the growth and anatomical development of Spinacia oleracea, causing reductions in fresh and dry biomass, shoot and root length, plant height, stem area, cortex cell area, epidermal thickness, epidermal cell area, and vascular region area.

## Biological Context
Foliar-applied salicylic acid mitigates NaCl-induced morphological, anatomical, and biochemical damages in Spinacia oleracea
