# To assess whether the microﬂuidic device could support visu- alization of cell wall reconstitution, 

## Claim
To assess whether the microﬂuidic device could support visu- alization of cell wall reconstitution, root protoplasts of Arabidopsis were cultured in the device, and calcoﬂuor white (5 μg/mL), which binds to the cellulosic polymers of the cell wall, was added to W5++ medium for imaging under UV excitation.

## Biological Context
Aggregates-on-chip: a microfluidic device for crowding plant cells in culture
