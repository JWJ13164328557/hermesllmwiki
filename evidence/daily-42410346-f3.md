# Transient overexpression of wild-type versions enhanced salt tolerance, lowering …

## Claim
Transient overexpression of wild-type versions enhanced salt tolerance, lowering malondialdehyde (MDA) and reactive oxygen species (ROS) while elevating proline, chlorophyll, and antioxidant enzyme activities; these effects were lost in acetylation-defective mutants.
