# Compared with glucose or amino-acid metabolism, these pathways are tightly connected to membrane arc

## Claim
Compared with glucose or amino-acid metabolism, these pathways are tightly connected to membrane architecture, signaling platforms, energy storage, redox homeostasis, and cell-fate control; importantly, the same lipid pathway can produce opposite consequences in tumor cells and different immune-cell subsets (13–15, 17).

## Biological Context
Lipid metabolism and the immune microenvironment in gastric cancer
