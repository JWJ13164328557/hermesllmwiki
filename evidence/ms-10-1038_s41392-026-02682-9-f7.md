# HGF binds to MET, leading to MET homodimerization and autophosphorylation, activating various downst

## Claim
HGF binds to MET, leading to MET homodimerization and autophosphorylation, activating various downstream pathways such as MAPK, PI3K/AKT, FAK, and STAT3, promoting cell proliferation, survival, migration, invasion, and metastasis.
