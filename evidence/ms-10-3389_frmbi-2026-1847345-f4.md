# This is consistent with the observation that mTORC1/S6K1 hyperactivation directly phosphorylates IRS

## Claim
This is consistent with the observation that mTORC1/S6K1 hyperactivation directly phosphorylates IRS-1 at inhibitory serine residues, thereby uncoupling the insulin receptor from its down- stream PI3K/Akt effectors (Veilleux et al., 2010).
