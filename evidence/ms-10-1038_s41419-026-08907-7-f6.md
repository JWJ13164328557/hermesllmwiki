# Notably, HCC demonstrates reduced infiltration densities of CD8+ cytotoxic T lymphocytes and natural

## Claim
Notably, HCC demonstrates reduced infiltration densities of CD8+ cytotoxic T lymphocytes and natural killer (NK) cells, while displaying disproportionate enrichment of regulatory T cells (Tregs) and myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSCs).

## Biological Context
CFTR-driven immune microenvironment reprogramming synergizes with anti-PD-L1 antibody in hepatocellular carcinoma
