# We found considerable DE-APA genes even between the two healthy samples, especially in the cell type

## Claim
We found considerable DE-APA genes even between the two healthy samples, especially in the cell types ‘Immature Ery 2ʹ, ‘B’ and ‘T’ (grey points and line in Figure 2(a, c)), because cells of these three types accounted for relatively large portions in the healthy samples (Figure S2), resulting in more genes captured.
