# These findings suggest that, in GBM tissues, increased T-cell infil­ tration after neoBev treatment 

## Claim
These findings suggest that, in GBM tissues, increased T-cell infil­ tration after neoBev treatment may be partly associated with vascular/BBB-related remodeling and the modulation of the myeloid/endothelial compartment.
