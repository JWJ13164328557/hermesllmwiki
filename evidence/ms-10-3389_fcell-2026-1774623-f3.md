# Research indicates that RNA methylation plays a key role in the progression of gastrointestinal tumo

## Claim
Research indicates that RNA methylation plays a key role in the progression of gastrointestinal tumors by simultaneously regulating tumor and immune cell functions: it promotes tumor growth by reshaping the TIME, thereby becoming a crucial link between genetic information and immune traits (Yang et al., 2023).

## Biological Context
Advances in research on RNA methylation and its role in the immune microenvironment of gastrointestinal tumors
