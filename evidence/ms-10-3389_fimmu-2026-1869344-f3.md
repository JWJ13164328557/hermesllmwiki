# This means that conventional anti-CD28 mAb harbour clones which bind to or close to the binding site

## Claim
This means that conventional anti-CD28 mAb harbour clones which bind to or close to the binding site of CD80 and CD86 on the tip of the CD28 molecule and are, thus, capable of blocking ligand binding to CD28.

## Biological Context
Treatment with saturating dose of conventional anti-CD28 monoclonal antibody well tolerated in pig acute myocardial infarction model
