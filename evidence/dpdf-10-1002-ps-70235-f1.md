## Claim
We found: (i) Nano-Se applying (especially 10 mg L−1) signiﬁcantly inhibited tomato leaf mold and reduced disease severity of seedlings (52.84%, 14 days after inoculation).

## Biological Context
Nano selenium enhanced tomato resistance to leaf mold via activating salicylic acid defense pathways and regulating metabolic profiles as well as improved plant growth
