# We show that this patterning occurs through crosstalk between the vascular cylinder and the surround

## Claim
We show that this patterning occurs through crosstalk between the vascular cylinder and the surrounding endodermis mediated by cell-to-cell movement of a transcription factor in one direction and microRNAs in the other.

## Biological Context
Cell signalling by microRNA165/6 directs gene dose-dependent root cell fate.
