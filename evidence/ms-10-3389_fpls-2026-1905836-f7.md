# sativa is mediated by photoperiod and GA: short-day conditions trigger a decrease in bioactive GA4 a

## Claim
sativa is mediated by photoperiod and GA: short-day conditions trigger a decrease in bioactive GA4 at the shoot apex that is required for compact inﬂorescence formation, whereas exogenous GA3 prevents condensation.
