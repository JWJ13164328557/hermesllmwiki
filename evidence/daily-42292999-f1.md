# Our results suggest that the UV-B threshold negatively affecting kratom growth lies …

## Claim
Our results suggest that the UV-B threshold negatively affecting kratom growth lies between 0.9 kJ·m−2·day−1 (0.3 µmol·m−2·s−1 for 2 h, as used in this study) and 10.2 kJ·m−2·day−1 (0.5 µmol·m−2·s−1 for 14 h, based on unpublished preliminary trials).
