# In the root, we found a significant enrichment of di- cistronic CK1-TLS transcript compared with mon

## Claim
In the root, we found a significant enrichment of di- cistronic CK1-TLS transcript compared with mono-cistronic CK1, in the procambium cluster 16a (P-value 0.031), PPP cluster 20b (P-value 0.044) and endodermis cluster 31 (P- value 0.027) (Figure 6, B).
