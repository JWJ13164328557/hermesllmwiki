# These results suggest that wounding induces callus formation through pathways different from those e

## Claim
These results suggest that wounding induces callus formation through pathways different from those employing the lateral root differentiation program and that resulting callus possesses different levels of cell differentiation and/or dedif- ferentiation compared to callus at the nonwounded site.
