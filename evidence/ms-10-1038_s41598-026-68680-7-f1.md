# From a farmer's perspective, improving nutrient-use efficiency while reducing dependence on chemical

## Claim
From a farmer's perspective, improving nutrient-use efficiency while reducing dependence on chemical fertilizers is essential for lowering input costs, maintaining soil fertility, and enhancing long-term productivity.

## Biological Context
Biopriming with liquid biofertilizers enhances productivity, seed quality, soil health, and resource use efficiency in soybean under semi-arid conditions
