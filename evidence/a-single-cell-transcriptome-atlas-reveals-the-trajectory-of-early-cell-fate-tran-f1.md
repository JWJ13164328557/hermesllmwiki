# Taken together, our results suggest that QC-like cells that develop from LRPI-like cells are in a we

## Claim
Taken together, our results suggest that QC-like cells that develop from LRPI-like cells are in a weakly differentiated state and exhibit enhanced pluripotency and a range of tran- scriptional activities that regulate callus development, indicating the potential for unique roles in subsequent callus development.

## Biological Context
A single-cell transcriptome atlas reveals the trajectory of early cell fate transition during callus induction in Arabidopsis
