# Notably, ceramide induced by GH01 interacted with mitochondria, thus releasing cytochrome c, which i

## Claim
Notably, ceramide induced by GH01 interacted with mitochondria, thus releasing cytochrome c, which in turn activated caspase-9 and caspase-3, ultimately triggering cellular apoptosis.

## Biological Context
A novel prenylflavonoid with efficacy in cancer therapy by targeting ceramide-orchestrated signaling
