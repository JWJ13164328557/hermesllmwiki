# Raw inflorescences must undergo controlled moisture reduction to equilibrium relative humidity level

## Claim
Raw inflorescences must undergo controlled moisture reduction to equilibrium relative humidity levels between 55% and 62% to maintain structural terpene profiles while eliminating target microbial proliferation risks.
