# To map dCA2 outputs, we unilaterally injected a Cre-dependent synaptophysin virus (AAV1-hSyn- Flex-2

## Claim
To map dCA2 outputs, we unilaterally injected a Cre-dependent synaptophysin virus (AAV1-hSyn- Flex-2A-GFP-Synaptophysin-mRuby) into Amigo2-Cre mice and used a combination of fluorescence and confocal microscopy to identify projection targets and synaptic terminal 120 locations.

## Biological Context
Hippocampal CA2 modulates trace fear conditioning through circuit-specific control of CA1
