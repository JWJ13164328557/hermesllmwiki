# We show that seqFISH+ can image mRNAs for 10,000 genes in single cells—with high accuracy and sub-di

## Claim
We show that seqFISH+ can image mRNAs for 10,000 genes in single cells—with high accuracy and sub-diffraction-limit resolution—in the cortex, subventricular zone and olfactory bulb of mouse brain, using a standard confocal microscope.

## Biological Context
Transcriptome-scale super-resolved imaging in tissues by RNA seqFISH.
