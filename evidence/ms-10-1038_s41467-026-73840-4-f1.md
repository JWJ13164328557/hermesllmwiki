# Analysis of epididymal white adipose tissue (eWAT), a previously identified target of endostatin, re

## Claim
Analysis of epididymal white adipose tissue (eWAT), a previously identified target of endostatin, revealed that endostatin lowered the expression of several genes that are critical to the metabolic activities of adipocytes, including the Pparg2 isoform that regulates adipogenesis44 (Pparg2, Srebf1, Lipe, Mgll, Fabp4).
