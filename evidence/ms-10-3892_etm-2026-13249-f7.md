# These findings suggest that Hspa9 deficiency perturbs NK cell development and altering the expressio

## Claim
These findings suggest that Hspa9 deficiency perturbs NK cell development and altering the expression of critical surface molecules such as CD117, CD127 and Ly49C/I.

## Biological Context
Hematopoietic cell‑specific knockout Hspa9 impairs natural killer cell function and alters the Akt/mTOR axis in mice
