# Using LORAX-seq we show that DIS3 degrades backtracked RNA, and by analyzing catalytically inactive 

## Claim
Using LORAX-seq we show that DIS3 degrades backtracked RNA, and by analyzing catalytically inactive mutants of DIS3 we provide direct evidence that its exoribonucleolytic activity is required for efficient transcription elongation.

## Biological Context
RNA degradation by DIS3 is a necessary step in the resolution of backtracked transcription complexes
