# Collectively, these findings demonstrate that chronic smoke exposure combined with ethanol intervent

## Claim
Collectively, these findings demonstrate that chronic smoke exposure combined with ethanol intervention successfully induced a stable oligoasthenozoospermia phenotype characterized by reduced sperm count, impaired motility, structural damage to seminiferous tubules, and decreased germ cell proliferation.
