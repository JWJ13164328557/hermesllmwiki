# Mechanistically, SYCE1, a core component of the synaptonemal complex central element essential for s

## Claim
Mechanistically, SYCE1, a core component of the synaptonemal complex central element essential for synapsis, was identified as a testicular target of RNF220.

## Biological Context
RNF220 safeguards meiotic synapsis and male fertility by antagonizing SYCE1 ubiquitination
