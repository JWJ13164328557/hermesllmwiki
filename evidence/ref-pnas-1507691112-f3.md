# Strikingly, ectopic ex- pression of MYB36 is sufficient to reprogram cells to both ex- press the gen

## Claim
Strikingly, ectopic ex- pression of MYB36 is sufficient to reprogram cells to both ex- press the genetic machinery required to synthesize Casparian strips and to locate and assemble this machinery, such that the strips develop in the correct cellular location, even though they are in cell types that do not normally form Casparian strips.

## Biological Context
The MYB36 transcription factor orchestrates Casparian strip formation.
