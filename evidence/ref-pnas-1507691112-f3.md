# Strikingly, ectopic expression of MYB36 is sufficient to reprogram cells to both express …

## Claim
Strikingly, ectopic expression of MYB36 is sufficient to reprogram cells to both express the genetic machinery required to synthesize Casparian strips and to locate and assemble this machinery, such that the strips develop in the correct cellular location, even though they are in cell types that do not normally form Casparian strips.
