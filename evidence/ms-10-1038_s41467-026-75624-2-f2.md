# Our data support that IRX9 genes have a specialized role in SCW biosynthesis in dicots, with a clear

## Claim
Our data support that IRX9 genes have a specialized role in SCW biosynthesis in dicots, with a clear peak during SCW formation, while IRX9L genes exhibited a more general expression pattern during xylogenesis, similar to that of pectin and xyloglucan biosynthetic genes (Figure 4C).

## Biological Context
Comparative regulomics of wood formation across dicot and conifer trees
