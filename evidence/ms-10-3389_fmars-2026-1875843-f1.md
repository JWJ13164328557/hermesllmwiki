# Here we show, through direct ﬁeld measurement, that plant abundance in crab-exclusion plots was 2.2-

## Claim
Here we show, through direct ﬁeld measurement, that plant abundance in crab-exclusion plots was 2.2- fold higher than in crab-access plots, and vegetation cover also diverged markedly through time, indicating strong suppression of creek-edge vegetation establishment by crab herbivory.

## Biological Context
Top-down control by crabs along tidal creeks in a salt marsh: vegetation suppression and variable sediment deposition
