# Fine roots (diameter < 2 mm), which are highly sensitive to changes in soil moisture, play a central

## Claim
Fine roots (diameter < 2 mm), which are highly sensitive to changes in soil moisture, play a central role in water uptake and may therefore serve as sensitive indicators of forest ecosystem adaptation and resilience potential [2].

## Biological Context
Fine-root biomass in relation to soil properties in regenerating Norway spruce stands following large-scale bark beetle outbreaks in mountain and lowland forests
