# Our results suggest that ant communities and/or composition are influenced by pre­ dictor variables 

## Claim
Our results suggest that ant communities and/or composition are influenced by pre­ dictor variables such as geographic distances, which in turn reflect a multi-scale process of species turnover.

## Biological Context
Altitudinal distribution of epigeous ants (Hymenoptera: Formicidae) along the slopes of mountain ranges adjacent to the Cauca River valley, Colombia
