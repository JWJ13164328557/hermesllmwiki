# Importantly, low viremia does not preclude transmission: Culex mosquitoes can acquire enzootic VEEV 

## Claim
Importantly, low viremia does not preclude transmission: Culex mosquitoes can acquire enzootic VEEV strains from vertebrate hosts with titers as low as 10³–104 PFU/mL (Cupp et al., 1986, 1981), and comparable levels have been observed in natural reservoir hosts experimentally infected with subtype ID strains.

## Biological Context
Río Negro virus (Venezuelan equine encephalitis virus complex) shows pathogenic potential in a permissive murine model
