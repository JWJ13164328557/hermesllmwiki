# Previous studies have shown that SREBP1 can directly bind to the SRE element (−475 to −483 bp) in th

## Claim
Previous studies have shown that SREBP1 can directly bind to the SRE element (−475 to −483 bp) in the ACSS2 promoter to drive its transcription47; and that ChREBP activates the target gene Acss2 to regulate hepatic acetate metabolism48.

## Biological Context
ACSS2-KAT5 complex-driven histone crotonylation orchestrates a pro-inflammatory program to promote the transition from MASLD to MASH
