# The upstream IR-NF- κB inﬂammatory axis is dominant, and its activation directly inhibits Parkin tra

## Claim
The upstream IR-NF- κB inﬂammatory axis is dominant, and its activation directly inhibits Parkin transcription and induces its oxidative inactivation, thereby downstream blocking PINK1/Parkin-mediated mitophagy; autophagy disorder leads to the accumulation of damaged mitochondria and the outbreak of ROS, which in turn strongly activates NF-κB and forms a closed-loop ampliﬁcation (83, 84).

## Biological Context
Research progress on the association of maternal high-fat diet with offspring neurodevelopment and susceptibility to Parkinson's disease
