# In conclusion, PSMD14 suppresses microglial M2 polarization during TBI progression by promoting mito

## Claim
In conclusion, PSMD14 suppresses microglial M2 polarization during TBI progression by promoting mitochondrial oxidation-induced ferroptosis through the activation of the BMP6/SMAD3 pathway.

## Biological Context
PSMD14 reduced microglial M2 polarization to promote ferroptosis and aggravate traumatic brain injury through the induction of BMP6/SMAD3 pathway
