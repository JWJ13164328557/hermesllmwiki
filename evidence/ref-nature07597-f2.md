# These results show that stem cell-like properties that mediate complete organ regeneration are dispe

## Claim
These results show that stem cell-like properties that mediate complete organ regeneration are dispersed in plant meristems and are not restricted to niches, which nonetheless appear necessary for indeterminate growth.

## Biological Context
Organ regeneration does not require a functional stem cell niche in plants.
