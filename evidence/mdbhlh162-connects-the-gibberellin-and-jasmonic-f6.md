# When sensing JA signaling, COI1 binds to SKP1LIKE PROTEIN1/2, CULLIN 1, and RINGbox …

## Claim
When sensing JA signaling, COI1 binds to SKP1LIKE PROTEIN1/2, CULLIN 1, and RINGbox PROTEIN 1 to form the SCFCOI1 complex; the SCFCOI1 complex initiates the JA signaling response by targeting ubiquitination and degradation of JAZ proteins.
