# Our findings show that QoQ-Med3 trained with DRPO achieves an overall balanced accuracy of 71.3% and

## Claim
Our findings show that QoQ-Med3 trained with DRPO achieves an overall balanced accuracy of 71.3% and a F1 of 0.349, a 14.7 percentage point improvement over the strongest baseline, with particularly pronounced gains in understudied modalities such as ultrasound and mammography.
