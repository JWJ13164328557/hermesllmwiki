# Our results showed that GoHSFA4a directly binds to the detected HSF binding motifs in the promoter r

## Claim
Our results showed that GoHSFA4a directly binds to the detected HSF binding motifs in the promoter regions of the MVA pathway gene HMGS and the gossypol pathway gene CYP736A196 (Figure 3I and Supplemental Figure 13C), whereas GoNAC42 binds to NAC binding motifs in the promoter regions of the MVA pathway gene HMGR and the gossypol pathway gene DH1 (Figure 3J and Supplemental Figure 13D).
