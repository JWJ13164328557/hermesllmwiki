# However, incomplete recoveries, extrapolated values outside the established calibration ranges, and,

## Claim
However, incomplete recoveries, extrapolated values outside the established calibration ranges, and, most importantly, the lack of demonstrated molecular selectivity limit their use for the absolute and specific quantification of IAA, GA₃, and kinetin in plantain tissues.
