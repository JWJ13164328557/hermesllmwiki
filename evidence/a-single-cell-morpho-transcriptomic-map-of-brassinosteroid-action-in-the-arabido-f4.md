# Their interaction with brassinolide triggers a phospho-transfer cascade, which eventually increases 

## Claim
Their interaction with brassinolide triggers a phospho-transfer cascade, which eventually increases the nu- clear abundance of downstream transcriptional effectors and thereby modulates the expression of their target genes (Wang et al., 2002; Yin et al., 2002; Tang et al., 2016; Kim and Russinova, 2020).
