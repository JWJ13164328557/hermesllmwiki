# It is a modular scaffold in the nucleus: its 5′ region binds PRC2 (through EZH2) to deposit repressi

## Claim
It is a modular scaffold in the nucleus: its 5′ region binds PRC2 (through EZH2) to deposit repressive H3K27me3, while its 3′ region engages the LSD1/CoREST/REST complex to strip activating H3K4me2, jointly silencing target loci, including the posterior HOXD cluster (Rinn et al., 2007; Tsai et al., 2010).
