# Notably, when stem cells of the columella root cap (CSCs) are ablated, cells of the quiescent center

## Claim
Notably, when stem cells of the columella root cap (CSCs) are ablated, cells of the quiescent center (QC) can undergo rapid transverse/anticlinal divisions to replace the dead CSCs [37].

## Biological Context
A single-cell view of tissue regeneration in plants.
