# The zinc finger domain of PARP1 quickly recognizes and binds to the lesion, triggering conformationa

## Claim
The zinc finger domain of PARP1 quickly recognizes and binds to the lesion, triggering conformational activation and catalyzing the transfer of ADP-ribose units from nico- tinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD)+ to PARP1 and sur- rounding proteins, a process known as PARylation (Figure 1).
