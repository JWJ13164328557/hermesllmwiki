# Collectively, these findings identify LSD1 as an upstream epigenetic regulator that promotes Ang II-

## Claim
Collectively, these findings identify LSD1 as an upstream epigenetic regulator that promotes Ang II-induced atrial remodeling by independently activating Wnt/β-catenin-dependent hypertrophic signaling and oxidative stress- driven inflammatory pathways, including NLRP3 inflammasome activation, thereby contributing to increased AF susceptibility.
