# Compared to homogenous chains, branched conjugates syn- thesized by the APC/C increase the efﬁciency

## Claim
Compared to homogenous chains, branched conjugates syn- thesized by the APC/C increase the efﬁciency of proteasomal substrate recognition and, accordingly, are required for the degradation of cell-cycle regulators at times of limited APC/C activity.

Enhanced Protein Degradation by Branched Ubiquitin Chains
