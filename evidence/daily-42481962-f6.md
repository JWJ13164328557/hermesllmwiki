# The T15 treatment also increased lodging resistance, but its effects on stalk puncture …

## Claim
The T15 treatment also increased lodging resistance, but its effects on stalk puncture strength and lignin content were markedly weaker than those of T10 (Tables, ), likely because the V15 stage coincides with the transition from vegetative to reproductive growth, when internode elongation has already progressed and cell wall thickening is less responsive to gibberellin inhibitors.
