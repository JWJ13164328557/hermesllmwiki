# They do so by disabling the antigen processing and presentation machinery (e.g., via loss of β2-micr

## Claim
They do so by disabling the antigen processing and presentation machinery (e.g., via loss of β2-microglobulin expression or downregulation of histocompatibility antigens), impairing interferon (IFN)-γ signaling, losing expression of target antigens, limiting T-cell recognition, and blunting cytotoxic T-cell activity (Galassi et al., 2024).

## Biological Context
Leveraging ZBP1 signaling for cancer immunotherapy
