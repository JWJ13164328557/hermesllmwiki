# Discussion A sulfur (S) deficiency constitutes one of the primary limitations to crop …

## Claim
Discussion A sulfur (S) deficiency constitutes one of the primary limitations to crop production due to soil depletion and present-day farming systems since S is a vital component in the formation of sulfurous amino acids, proteins, enzymes, and antioxidant molecules that play a key role in regulating photosynthesis and metabolism processes (Zandalinas et al., 2018).
