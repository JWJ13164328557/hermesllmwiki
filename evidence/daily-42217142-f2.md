# Soil dehydrogenase values in soil inoculated with KF23, regardless of NaCl level …

## Claim
Soil dehydrogenase values in soil inoculated with KF23, regardless of NaCl level, remained at a similar level (5.49 µg TPF g⁻¹ h⁻¹, 5.65 µg TPF g⁻¹ h⁻¹, and 7.2 µg TPF g⁻¹ h⁻¹), whereas in variants C, CS150, and CS300 dehydrogenase values were clearly more variable (7.49 µg TPF g⁻¹ h⁻¹, 4.03 µg TPF g⁻¹ h⁻¹, and 6.48 µg TPF g⁻¹ h⁻¹).
