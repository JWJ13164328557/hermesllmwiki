# Collectively, this promoter analysis revealed that NtMADS-box family genes are extensively involved 

## Claim
Collectively, this promoter analysis revealed that NtMADS-box family genes are extensively involved in mediating plant hormonal responses, potentially through abscisic acid and methyl jasmonate signaling pathways.

## Biological Context
Genome-wide characterization of MADS-box genes and their roles in axillary bud development in tobacco
