# Overexpression of HD2C, a HD2-type histone deacetylase, resulted in a higher germination rate and lo

## Claim
Overexpression of HD2C, a HD2-type histone deacetylase, resulted in a higher germination rate and longer root length compared with wild-type when treated with ABA ( Sridha and Wu, 2006 ).
