# Taken together, these results indi­ cate that the overall metabolic burden associated with surfactin

## Claim
Taken together, these results indi­ cate that the overall metabolic burden associated with surfactin production under the tested conditions repre­ sents roughly 19.57% of the potential growth capacity of the cell, with the expression of the large NRPS machin­ ery accounting for the majority of the observed growth penalty.

## Biological Context
Exploring the metabolic burden of surfactin biosynthesis and the metabolic costs of srfA operon expression in Bacillus subtilis
