## Claim
In summary, these bioinformatics analyses support an increased di- versification of CSLC genes including the recent evolution of CSLC4 genes in eudicots, most likely from an ancestral CSLC group, and important roles of XyG in plant reproduction.

## Biological Context
The synthesis of xyloglucan, an abundant plant cell wall polysaccharide, requires CSLC function
