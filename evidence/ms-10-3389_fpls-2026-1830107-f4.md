# In Pueraria lobata, at a biochemical level of control, IAA regulates the tryptophan metabolism pathw

## Claim
In Pueraria lobata, at a biochemical level of control, IAA regulates the tryptophan metabolism pathway (ko00380), which targets genes YUCCA FLAVIN MONOOXYGENASE10 (YUCCA10) and COBRA and induces expansion genes CELL DIVISION CYCLE48 (CDC48) and EXPANSIN (EXP) (Liangdeng et al., 2024).

## Biological Context
Molecular mechanisms of root expansion in medicinal plants
