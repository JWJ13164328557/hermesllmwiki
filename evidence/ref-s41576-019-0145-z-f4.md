# Subcomplexes interact with the indicated sequence motifs (‘AAUAAA’ being the canonical poly(A) signa

## Claim
Subcomplexes interact with the indicated sequence motifs (‘AAUAAA’ being the canonical poly(A) signal) in nascent RNAs to dynamically determine the site of cleavage and polyadenylation, located preferentially after a cytosine (indicated by ‘C’).

## Biological Context
Alternative cleavage and polyadenylation in health and disease.
