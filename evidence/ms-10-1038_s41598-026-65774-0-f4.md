# Moreover, mvCKM-associated genetic signals show significant and specific enrichment in pancreatic ce

## Claim
Moreover, mvCKM-associated genetic signals show significant and specific enrichment in pancreatic cells and immune cells—particularly macrophages—directly implicating dysregulated insulin secretion and chronic inflammation as two core biological pathways mediating CKM genetic risk, and thereby highlighting plausible cellular targets for mechanistic investigation.

## Biological Context
Integrative genomic dissection of cardio-kidney-metabolic syndrome reveals novel loci, causal genes, and tissue-specific pathways in European populations
