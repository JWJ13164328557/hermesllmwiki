# Collectively, these data indicate that PNPLA3 I148M homozygotes exhibit a distinct metabolic phenoty

## Claim
Collectively, these data indicate that PNPLA3 I148M homozygotes exhibit a distinct metabolic phenotype, marked by impaired lipid droplet turnover, altered mitochondrial function, and modified hepatic lipid flux, that may blunt expected therapeutic responses to some steatosis-targeting strategies.

## Biological Context
Incretin-based therapies: a new era in metabolic liver disease management
