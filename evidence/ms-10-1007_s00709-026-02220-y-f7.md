# Using Yariv reagent, which is specific to arabino­ galactan-protein (AGP) epitopes in general, and t

## Claim
Using Yariv reagent, which is specific to arabino­ galactan-protein (AGP) epitopes in general, and the JIM4 monoclonal antibody, which specifically targets epitopes of AGPs in gum arabic, we mapped the spatial and temporal dynamics of gum formation and identified the cell types initiating this process.

## Biological Context
Understanding the histological and histochemical mechanisms involved in gum arabic formation in Senegalia senegal, a key species of the Sahelian region
