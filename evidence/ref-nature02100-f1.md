# We conclude that APL is required for the asymmetric cell divisions as well as for SE and CC differen

## Claim
We conclude that APL is required for the asymmetric cell divisions as well as for SE and CC differentiation during phloem development, and that the absence of APL function results in differentiation of TE-like cells at the site of the phloem pole.

## Biological Context
APL regulates vascular tissue identity in Arabidopsis.
