# The targeted mutations of each BnaTT were identiﬁed by Hi-TOM sequencing analysis of the target site

## Claim
The targeted mutations of each BnaTT were identiﬁed by Hi-TOM sequencing analysis of the target sites, and the editing efﬁciency ranged from 22.24% to 62.41% in the T0 generation (Table S2).
