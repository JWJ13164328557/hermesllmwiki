# We found that treatment with BSO significantly decreased the number of cells with de novo amyloplast

## Claim
We found that treatment with BSO significantly decreased the number of cells with de novo amyloplast formation at 18 hpc and that co-treatment with GSH rescued amyloplast formation to the level of untreated roots (Figures 5E and 5F), consistent with regeneration defects caused by diminished levels of GSH post-injury.

## Biological Context
Glutathione accelerates the cell cycle and cellular reprogramming in plant regen
