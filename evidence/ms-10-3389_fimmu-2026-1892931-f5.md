# Sustained TGF-b1 stimulation, in concert with YAP/ TAZ mechanosignaling, directly binds to the EN1 e

## Claim
Sustained TGF-b1 stimulation, in concert with YAP/ TAZ mechanosignaling, directly binds to the EN1 enhancer region through SMAD2/3 and TEAD complexes, transcriptionally activating En1 expression and locking ﬁbroblasts into an EPF identity charac- terized by high expression of a−SMA and type I collagen (122).

## Biological Context
Reprogramming the wound microenvironment: identity remodeling strategies for fibroblasts, keratinocytes, and macrophages
