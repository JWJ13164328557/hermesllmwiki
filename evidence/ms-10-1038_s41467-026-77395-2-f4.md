# S-Gboxin binds to ATP synthase to disrupt mitochondrial function, while galunisertib inhibits SMAD2/

## Claim
S-Gboxin binds to ATP synthase to disrupt mitochondrial function, while galunisertib inhibits SMAD2/3 phosphorylation, thereby downregulating TSP-1 expression, suppressing tumor MT formation, reducing mitochondrial transfer, and concurrently activating antitumor immunity.

## Biological Context
ROS-autocatalytic nanoplatform interfering astrocyte-cancer crosstalk and enhancing glioblastoma therapy
