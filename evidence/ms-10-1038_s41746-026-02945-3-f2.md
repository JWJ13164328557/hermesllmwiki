# Our results show that QoQ-Med3 pretrained on non-EHR data (CLIMB) achieves 3.4-fold higher BioBERT s

## Claim
Our results show that QoQ-Med3 pretrained on non-EHR data (CLIMB) achieves 3.4-fold higher BioBERT similarity and 8.1% higher accuracy than the base model on MIMIC-IV in a zero-shot setting, with further gains when combined with MIMIC-IV finetuning.

## Biological Context
QoQ-Med3: a multimodal reasoning foundation model for clinical analysis
