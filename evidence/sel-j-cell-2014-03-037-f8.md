# Strikingly, depletion of Ube2S caused a strong increase in the abundance of Nek2A kinase during prom

## Claim
Strikingly, depletion of Ube2S caused a strong increase in the abundance of Nek2A kinase during prometa- phase, a cell-cycle stage when the APC/C is partially inhibited by the spindle checkpoint (Figure 1A; Figure S1A available online).
