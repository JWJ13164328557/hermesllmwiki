# Collectively, the abrupt changes at 60% mutation load are consistent with a reductive shift that may

## Claim
Collectively, the abrupt changes at 60% mutation load are consistent with a reductive shift that may inhibit NAD⁺- dependent dehydrogenases and favours NADH-driven reductions, suggesting that rising mtDNA mutation load and worsening OXPHOS deficiency progressively disrupt the NADH/NAD⁺ balance, driving the observed metabolic abnormalities.

## Biological Context
Cross-species urinary metabolomics identifies 2-hydroxyisovalerate as a candidate biomarker for mtDNA-based disorders
