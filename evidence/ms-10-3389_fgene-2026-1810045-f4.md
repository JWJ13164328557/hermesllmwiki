## Claim
Collectively, these findings support the hypothesis that PPOs may function not only as stress-associated markers, but also as active contributors to plant defence responses, although definitive mechanistic evidence remains limited in many species (Li and Steffens, 2002; Constabel and Barbehenn, 2008).
