# Carotenoids are a specific class of tetraterpenoids which play a crucial role in protecting photosys

## Claim
Carotenoids are a specific class of tetraterpenoids which play a crucial role in protecting photosystems from photoinhibition [41], by dissipating excess light energy generated during drought.

## Biological Context
Adaptive roles of plant secondary metabolites under drought stress conditions
