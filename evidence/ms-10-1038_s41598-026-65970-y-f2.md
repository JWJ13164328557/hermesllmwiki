# For instance, specific microRNAs have been shown to target the 3′ untranslated regions (UTRs) of Ppa

## Claim
For instance, specific microRNAs have been shown to target the 3′ untranslated regions (UTRs) of Ppargc1a (PGC1a) and TFAM, fine-tuning mitochondrial biogenesis and consequently influencing energy availability, metabolic disorders, and neurodegenerative diseases [50].

## Biological Context
Effect of six weeks of environmental enrichment on oxidative metabolism and hypothalamic mitochondrial markers of adult male mice
