# We found also, a dose-dependent enhancement of Rutin, the major flavonoid (27.69±0.09 µg/g (control)

## Claim
We found also, a dose-dependent enhancement of Rutin, the major flavonoid (27.69±0.09 µg/g (control) to 32.27±0.16 µg/g in 100 mM) indicating a further effect of H2O2 on flavonoid glycoside pathways.
