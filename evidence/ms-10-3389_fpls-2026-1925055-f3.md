# These proteins contain a highly conserved MADS domain of approx- imately 60 amino acids, which funct

## Claim
These proteins contain a highly conserved MADS domain of approx- imately 60 amino acids, which functions as a cis-acting element by binding to the promoter or enhancer regions of target genes to regulate their transcriptional expression (Smaczniak et al., 2012; Ning et al., 2023).

## Biological Context
Chromosome-level genome assembly of Hamlin sweet orange and transcriptomic insights into MADS-box gene regulation of secondary fruit development
