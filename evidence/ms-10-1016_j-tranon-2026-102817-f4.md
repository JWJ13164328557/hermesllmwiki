# In summary, these results demonstrate that TRIP6 is a direct functional target of TDP-43, whose expr

## Claim
In summary, these results demonstrate that TRIP6 is a direct functional target of TDP-43, whose expression and mRNA stability are positively regulated by TDP-43 through an interaction requiring its RRM1 and RRM2 domains.

## Biological Context
HIF1-driven TDP-43 stabilizes TRIP6 mRNA to drive angiogenesis and colorectal cancer progression under hypoxia
