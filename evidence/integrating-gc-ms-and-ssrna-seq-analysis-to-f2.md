# Many target genes of differentially expressed lncRNAs (DELs) were enriched in terpenoid biosynthesis

## Claim
Many target genes of differentially expressed lncRNAs (DELs) were enriched in terpenoid biosynthesis pathways, and 17 DELs were related to monoterpenoid and sesquiterpenoid biosynthesis.

## Biological Context
Integrating GC-MS and ssRNA-Seq 分析 to identify long non-coding RNAs related to 萜类 生物合成 in Cinnamomum camphora
