# These results suggest that TRB proteins coordinate PRC2 and JMJ14 activities to repress target genes

## Claim
These results suggest that TRB proteins coordinate PRC2 and JMJ14 activities to repress target genes via H3K27me3 deposition and H3K4me3 removal.

## Biological Context
Arabidopsis TRB proteins function in H3K4me3 demethylation by recruiting JMJ14.
