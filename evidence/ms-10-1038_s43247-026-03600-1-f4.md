# To improve understanding of the role of macroalgae and seagrasses in the removal of atmospheric CO2,

## Claim
To improve understanding of the role of macroalgae and seagrasses in the removal of atmospheric CO2, it is necessary to empirically quantify carbon sequestration via DOC derived from these habitats.

## Biological Context
Macroalgal and seagrass species generate variable amounts of recalcitrant dissolved organic carbon in coastal Japan
