# Here we found that a translational fusion of two KoNifH or AvNifH monomers, forming KoNifHH or AvNif

## Claim
Here we found that a translational fusion of two KoNifH or AvNifH monomers, forming KoNifHH or AvNifHH synthetic dimers, produced a soluble protein when targeted to plant mitochondria and co-expressed with the putative peptidyl-prolyl cis–trans isomerase NifM.

## Biological Context
Use of translational fusions to express functional Klebsiella oxytoca dinitrogenase reductase in plant mitochondria
