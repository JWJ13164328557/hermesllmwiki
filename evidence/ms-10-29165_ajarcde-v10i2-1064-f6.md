# Fertilization or exogenous auxin/GA application triggers large-scale transcriptional reprogramming i

## Claim
Fertilization or exogenous auxin/GA application triggers large-scale transcriptional reprogramming in maternal ovary tissues, activating genes related to the cell cycle, cell wall modification, and sink strength, which collectively are sufficient to initiate fruit growth without zygote formation [17][18][19].

## Biological Context
