# We found that HAAO expression regulated extracellular QA levels, with overexpression in LNCaP cells 

## Claim
We found that HAAO expression regulated extracellular QA levels, with overexpression in LNCaP cells significantly increasing QA secretion, whereas HAAO knockdown in C4-2B cells markedly reduced QA levels in the supernatant (Figure S3E).

## Biological Context
HAAO‑derived quinolinic acid fuels FDPS‑dependent AR signaling and sensitizes prostate cancer to combination therapy
