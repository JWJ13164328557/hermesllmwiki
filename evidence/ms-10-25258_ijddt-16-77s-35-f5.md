# Oxidative stress is the most reproducible physiologic response to RF-EMF investigations, however une

## Claim
Oxidative stress is the most reproducible physiologic response to RF-EMF investigations, however uneven and in need of further validation.50-52 These data suggest that oxidative stress is the major molecular link between chemically initiated, metabolically driven and environmental linked exposures in mammary carcinogenesis.
