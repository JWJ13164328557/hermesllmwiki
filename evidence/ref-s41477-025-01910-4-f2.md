# Our findings suggest that the biphasic effect of auxin results from the biphasic response of hypocot

## Claim
Our findings suggest that the biphasic effect of auxin results from the biphasic response of hypocotyl cells to decreasing apoplastic pH.

## Biological Context
The apoplastic pH is a key determinant in the hypocotyl growth response to auxin
