# These findings suggest spermidine as not only a "caloric restriction mimetic" with its supplementati

## Claim
These findings suggest spermidine as not only a "caloric restriction mimetic" with its supplementation mimicking the beneficial effects of nutrient deprivation but also a downstream effector of the possibly geroprotective effects of fasting and rapamycin.

## Biological Context
Geroprotective insights into the natural metabolite spermidine in aging and age-related diseases
