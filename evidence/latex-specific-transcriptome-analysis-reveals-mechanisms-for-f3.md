# There were also another two latex-specific com­ plexes, Skp-cullin-F-box protein (SCF) and cullin 3 

## Claim
There were also another two latex-specific com­ plexes, Skp-cullin-F-box protein (SCF) and cullin 3 (Cul3), that shared the RING box protein 1 (RBX1) in many transcripts, but had different target recognizing proteins F-BOX and DDB1- and CUL4-associated factor (DCAF) with latex-specific expression.
