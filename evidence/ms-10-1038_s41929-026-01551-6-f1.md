# Importantly, the presence of bound CoA seems to inhibit the accommodation of T-i in PpCHS–CoA; steri

## Claim
Importantly, the presence of bound CoA seems to inhibit the accommodation of T-i in PpCHS–CoA; steric hindrance from CoA’s thiol moiety limits the conformational change in T-i that is required for its entry into the CLC-binding pocket.

## Biological Context
Transient dynamics of flavonoid metabolons tune chalcone synthase specificity
