# Short-chain peptides (oligopeptides) applied through the leaf canopy are readily absorbed through st

## Claim
Short-chain peptides (oligopeptides) applied through the leaf canopy are readily absorbed through stomata and then bind to kinase receptors on the cell membrane to activate endogenous growth hormones such as auxins and brassinosteroids (Albert et al., 2022).

## Biological Context
Effect of Peptide Concentration On New Shoot Growth and Flowering of Arabica and Robusta Coffee
