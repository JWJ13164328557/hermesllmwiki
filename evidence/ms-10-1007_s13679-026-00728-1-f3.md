# At the intracellular level, ghrelin stimulates intracellular Ca2⁺ mobilization and activates the sir

## Claim
At the intracellular level, ghrelin stimulates intracellular Ca2⁺ mobilization and activates the sirtuin-1 (SIRT1)/p53 pathway, AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK), and the mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR), ultimately pro­ moting appetite stimulation in ARC neurons [82–85].
