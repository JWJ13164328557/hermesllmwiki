# Conclusions: These findings confirm that each altitudinal gradient is home to specific ant communiti

## Claim
Conclusions: These findings confirm that each altitudinal gradient is home to specific ant communities that respond to the environmental, historical, and biogeographical conditions of each mountain range.

## Biological Context
Altitudinal distribution of epigeous ants (Hymenoptera: Formicidae) along the slopes of mountain ranges adjacent to the Cauca River valley, Colombia
