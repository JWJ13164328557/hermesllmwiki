# In fact, AtSKP2 recruits CDKA-dependent phosphorylated AtE2Fc but not the nonphosphorylated form, su

## Claim
In fact, AtSKP2 recruits CDKA-dependent phosphorylated AtE2Fc but not the nonphosphorylated form, suggesting that this phosphorylation is necessary to target AtE2Fc for proteolysis.

## Biological Context
Arabidopsis E2Fc functions in cell division and is degraded by the ubiquitin-SCF
