# Altering BRC1 levels through over- expression or decapitation leads to dose-dependent changes in H3K

## Claim
Altering BRC1 levels through over- expression or decapitation leads to dose-dependent changes in H3K27me3 deposition at FOD gene loci, enabling tunable expression of these targets in axillary buds.

## Biological Context
BRANCHED1 acts as a rheostat of Nuclear Factor Y-dependent epigenetic regulation in axillary bud development
