# Importantly, their pairwise meta-analysis revealed no signiﬁcant difference between GLP-1RAs and SGL

## Claim
Importantly, their pairwise meta-analysis revealed no signiﬁcant difference between GLP-1RAs and SGLT2is for liver-related events (pooled HR 0.94, 95% CI 0.85-1.04), aligning closely with our observation of comparable but directionally favor- able effects for SGLT2is on liver enzymes (18).

## Biological Context
Effects of SGLT-2 inhibitors and GLP-1 receptor agonists on liver function in patients with non-alcoholic fatty liver disease and type 2 diabetes
