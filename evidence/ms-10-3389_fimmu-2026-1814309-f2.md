# Thymocytes bearing TCRs that can bind to any of the self pMHCs of the individual host are ‘positivel

## Claim
Thymocytes bearing TCRs that can bind to any of the self pMHCs of the individual host are ‘positively selected’ and upregulate CCR4 and CCR7 to reach the CCL17, CCL19 and CCL21-rich thymic medulla (19).

## Biological Context
Multifaceted regulation of thymic tolerance by pattern recognition cascades
