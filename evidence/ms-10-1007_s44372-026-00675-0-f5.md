# Importantly, bypassing the agar rooting stage reduced the overall production cycle by approximately 

## Claim
Importantly, bypassing the agar rooting stage reduced the overall production cycle by approximately four weeks under the conditions tested, a practical advantage aligning with Kozai’s argument that photoautotroph system reduces labor intensity and acclimatization bottlenecks.

## Biological Context
Indirect organogenesis and photoautotrophic acclimatization for scalable propagation of Ficus lyrata Warb. (fiddle-leaf fig), a popular indoor ornamental
