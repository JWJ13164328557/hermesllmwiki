# Because the primary intention was to identify overlapping iPSC targets that correspond to the “groun

## Claim
Because the primary intention was to identify overlapping iPSC targets that correspond to the “ground-truth” (i.e., human CSF post-ketamine), and because of limited assay coverage of DEGs in the Olink platform (see Discussion), differential CSF protein expression was FDR-corrected for signiﬁcance, while DEGs from the iPSC conditions were kept uncorrected (p < 0.05).

## Biological Context
Time-dependent effects of rapid-acting antidepressants in iPSC-derived neurons from treatment-resistant depression and healthy volunteers
