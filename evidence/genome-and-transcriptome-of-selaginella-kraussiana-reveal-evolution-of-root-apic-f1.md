# Based on a high-quality genome assembly, mRNA sequencing (mRNA-seq) data, and single-cell RNA-seq da

## Claim
Based on a high-quality genome assembly, mRNA sequencing (mRNA-seq) data, and single-cell RNA-seq data for the lycophyte Selaginella kraussiana, we show that the two root origin events in lycophytes and euphyllophytes adopted partially similar molecular modules in the regulation of root apical meristem (RAM) development.

## Biological Context
Genome and transcriptome of Selaginella kraussiana reveal evolution of root apical meristems in vascular plants
