# Collectively, this study provides a comprehensive dynamic atlas of ﬂavor-related VOCs across the CRC

## Claim
Collectively, this study provides a comprehensive dynamic atlas of ﬂavor-related VOCs across the CRC commercial harvest cycle, identiﬁes key candidate genes underlying aroma biosynthesis, and offers both a chemical basis for precise harvest timing optimization and candidate targets for future ﬂavor quality improvement in CRC.

## Biological Context
Volatilomic and transcriptomic analyses uncover the mechanisms underlying flavor-related VOC changes during the harvest period of Citrus reticulata ‘Chachi’
