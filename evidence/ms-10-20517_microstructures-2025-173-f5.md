# For example, PROTACs typically possess large molecular weights (> 800 Da), violating Lipinski’s Rule

## Claim
For example, PROTACs typically possess large molecular weights (> 800 Da), violating Lipinski’s Rule of Five, which leads to poor membrane permeability, low oral bioavailability, off-target toxicity, and the potential for a “hook effect”[13-16].

## Biological Context
Nano-TPD for precision protein degradation via endogenous or exogenous triggered release
