# Among these coexpressed genes, we identified several genes known to be related to Cd stress, such as

## Claim
Among these coexpressed genes, we identified several genes known to be related to Cd stress, such as MsACA12, MsACA13, MsCaN2, and MsCML (associated with the Ca²⁺ signaling pathway); MsGST11, MsGST25, MsGSTF9, MsGSTT2, and MsGSTU19 (related to glutathione S-transferase); and MsHIPP20, MsHIPP21, MsHIPP22, MsHMA2, MsHMA47, MsMT2A, and MsMTP10 (heavy metal-related genes).
