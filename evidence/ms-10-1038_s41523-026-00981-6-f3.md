# Although ER-negativity was long considered one of the diagnostic criteria for carcinoma with apocrin

## Claim
Although ER-negativity was long considered one of the diagnostic criteria for carcinoma with apocrine differentiation, we found that clinical and biological relevance of apocrine morphology are opposite by the ER status.

## Biological Context
The clinical relevance of apocrine morphology in breast cancer depends on the estrogen receptor status
