# Experimental validation of miRNA sponging activity typically requires a combination of approaches, i

## Claim
Experimental validation of miRNA sponging activity typically requires a combination of approaches, including: (i) Argonaute-2 RNA immunoprecipitation (AGO2-RIP) to confirm circRNA- miRNA binding in the RISC complex; (ii) luciferase reporter assays to demonstrate functional derepression of target mRNAs; and (iii) MS2-tagging systems to map circRNA-miRNA interactions.

## Biological Context
CircRNAs in hepatic lipid metabolism: regulatory mechanisms and clinical implications
