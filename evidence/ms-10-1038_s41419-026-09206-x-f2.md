# Overall, we demonstrated that targeting HCK in M1 macrophages alleviates postoperative abdominal adh

## Claim
Overall, we demonstrated that targeting HCK in M1 macrophages alleviates postoperative abdominal adhesions by suppressing NF-κB/IL-6 axis-driven MMT, providing a new target and available strategy for the prevention of PAAs.

## Biological Context
Targeting HCK in M1 macrophages alleviates postoperative abdominal adhesions by suppressing NF-κB/IL6 axis-driven mesothelial-to-mesenchymal transition
