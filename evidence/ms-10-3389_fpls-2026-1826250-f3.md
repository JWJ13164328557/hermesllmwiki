# Notably, recent chemical analyses have revealed chamazulene contents exceeding 50%, markedly higher 

## Claim
Notably, recent chemical analyses have revealed chamazulene contents exceeding 50%, markedly higher than those reported for most other blue essential oil sources (Rizvi et al., 2018; Mailänder et al., 2022).

## Biological Context
Optimized extraction, odor modulation, and antioxidant and antimicrobial activities of blue essential oil from Artemisia umbrosa
