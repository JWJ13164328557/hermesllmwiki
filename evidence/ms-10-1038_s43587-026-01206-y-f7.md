# We found that the AIM2 inflammasome may be partially involved in compensating for the loss of cGAS; 

## Claim
We found that the AIM2 inflammasome may be partially involved in compensating for the loss of cGAS; however, AIM2 knockdown did not fully reduce inflammation in cGAS KO cells, indicating that other DNA or RNA sensing pathways are likely active.

## Biological Context
cGAS-deficient mice display premature aging associated with derepression of LINE1 elements and inflammation
