# Although knockdown of RBBP6 leads to global 3ʹ UTR lengthening, RBBP6 also appears to be …

## Claim
Although knockdown of RBBP6 leads to global 3ʹ UTR lengthening, RBBP6 also appears to be important specifically for the accumulation of its target mRNAs, including those with AUrich elements in their 3ʹ UTRs, suggesting that there is a connection between mRNA stability and 3ʹ end processing54.
