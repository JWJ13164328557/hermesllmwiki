## Claim
Interestingly, although knockdown of RBBP6 leads to global 3ʹ UTR lengthening, RBBP6 also appears to be important specifically for the accumulation of its target mRNAs, including those with AU‑rich elements in their 3ʹ UTRs, suggesting that there is a connection between mRNA stability and 3ʹ end processing54.

## Biological Context
Alternative polyadenylation of mRNA precursors.
