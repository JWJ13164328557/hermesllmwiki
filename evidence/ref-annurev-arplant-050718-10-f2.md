# However, the best characterized interaction partners of Aux/IAAs, such as AXR3/IAA17, are auxin-resp

## Claim
However, the best characterized interaction partners of Aux/IAAs, such as AXR3/IAA17, are auxin-response transcription factors (ARFs), raising the possibility of still unidentified intracellu- lar targets of the auxin-dependent TIR-Aux/IAA interaction.
