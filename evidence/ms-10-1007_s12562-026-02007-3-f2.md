# Although the clusters in the live holding group were cooked for 30 s longer than their wild counterp

## Claim
Although the clusters in the live holding group were cooked for 30 s longer than their wild counterparts to reach the same target temperature (91 °C) in the walking leg core, it is likely that, in the wild crabs, the leg meat located in proximity to the exoskeleton was subjected to higher thermal exposure.

## Biological Context
Sensory properties of cooked leg meat of commercial-size red king crab (Paralithodes camtschaticus) after 3 months of live holding with feeding
