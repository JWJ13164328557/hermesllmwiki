# Then, ampliﬁed siRNAs join with the AGO protein to form RISC, these siRNAs being used by RISC to pre

## Claim
Then, ampliﬁed siRNAs join with the AGO protein to form RISC, these siRNAs being used by RISC to precisely connect with homologous RNA in the cell, triggering endo-nucleolytic cleavage and translational inhibition of the cognate target mRNA, resulting in PTGS.
