# CLDN18.2 is a tight-junction target rather than an immune checkpoint biomarker, but CLDN18.2-directe

## Claim
CLDN18.2 is a tight-junction target rather than an immune checkpoint biomarker, but CLDN18.2-directed antibodies such as zolbetuximab are designed to induce tumor-cell killing through mechanisms that include antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity and complement-dependent cytotoxicity (44).

## Biological Context
Spatial immune ecology of immunotherapy resistance in gastric and gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma
