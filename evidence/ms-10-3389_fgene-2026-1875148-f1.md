# Collectively, these data demonstrate that filgotinib forms a stable, high-affinity complex with CENP

## Claim
Collectively, these data demonstrate that filgotinib forms a stable, high-affinity complex with CENPM, characterized by favorable H-bonding, low flexibility, and a thermodynamically favorable conformational basin, confirming robust target engagement.

## Biological Context
CENPM as a biomarker and therapeutic target for lymph node metastasis in thyroid carcinoma
