# scRNA-seq Identifies Cell-Type Diversity in Developing Maize Ears

## Claim

Single-cell RNA-seq of developing maize ears (1-5 mm immature ears) resolves cell-type diversity including inflorescence meristem (IM), spikelet pair meristem (SPM), spikelet meristem (SM), and floral meristem (FM) domains, providing a transcriptomic map of early reproductive development in a major crop.

## Biological Context

Maize ear development involves a series of meristem transitions that determine kernel row number and yield. Understanding the transcriptional programs of each meristem domain is essential for crop improvement and developmental biology.
