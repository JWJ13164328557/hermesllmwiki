# SnRK2 kinases in the ABA pathway, such as OST1, can directly phosphorylate ICE1 …

## Claim
SnRK2 kinases in the ABA pathway, such as OST1, can directly phosphorylate ICE1, enhancing its stability and transcriptional activity, while ICE1 can in turn interact with transcription factors such as ABI5 to suppress excessive ABA responses, forming a negative feedback loop (Ding et al., 2015).
