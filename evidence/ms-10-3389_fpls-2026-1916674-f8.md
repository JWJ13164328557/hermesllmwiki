# CMLs have simple structures and mainly act as early responders to calcium signals; CDPKs combine cal

## Claim
CMLs have simple structures and mainly act as early responders to calcium signals; CDPKs combine calcium-binding and kinase activities and can directly phosphorylate downstream target proteins (Schulz et al., 2013); CBLs must interact with CIPKs to form complexes to become functional (Weinl and Kudla, 2009).

## Biological Context
Regulatory effects of exogenous calcium on sweet cherry fruit cracking and functional analysis of the PavCML42 gene
