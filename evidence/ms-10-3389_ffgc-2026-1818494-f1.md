# These results suggest that the trade- off between photosynthetic capacity loss associated with leaf 

## Claim
These results suggest that the trade- off between photosynthetic capacity loss associated with leaf area removal and improvements in stem quality and structure may be species dependent, likely driven by differences in light requirements, crown plasticity, and apical dominance control (Niinemets, 2010; Alcorn et al., 2013).

## Biological Context
Early stem and form pruning effects on short-term tree growth and stem quality in hardwood plantations: differing results in Nothofagus dombeyi and Nothofagus alpina in south-central Chile
