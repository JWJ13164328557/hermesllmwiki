# Notably, the enzymes responsible for the synthesis and transfer of AATGal are shared with the teicho

## Claim
Notably, the enzymes responsible for the synthesis and transfer of AATGal are shared with the teichoic and lipoteichoic acid biosynthetic pathways, both of which play essential roles in cell wall architecture and integrity (Behr et al., 1992; Fischer et al., 1993).

## Biological Context
Investigation of the essential role of capsular polysaccharide in serotype 1 Streptococcus pneumoniae
