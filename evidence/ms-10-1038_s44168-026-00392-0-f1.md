# Interestingly, it was non-financial companies that were more likely to have adopted Scope 3 targets,

## Claim
Interestingly, it was non-financial companies that were more likely to have adopted Scope 3 targets, had targets validated by SBTi, incorporated climate-related measures into executive pay plans and undertaken substantive engagement with suppliers in relation to decarbonisation.

## Biological Context
Preparedness of medium-sized and small UK listed companies to publish a climate transition plan
