# Hormonal regulation associated with apical dominance—particularly auxin–cytokinin–strigolactone inte

## Claim
Hormonal regulation associated with apical dominance—particularly auxin–cytokinin–strigolactone interactions—also plays a central role in re-establishing a domi­ nant leader and suppressing competing shoots after pruning (Ferguson and Beveridge, 2009; Beveridge et al., 2023).

## Biological Context
Early stem and form pruning effects on short-term tree growth and stem quality in hardwood plantations: differing results in Nothofagus dombeyi and Nothofagus alpina in south-central Chile
