## Claim
While it has been shown that SHR-SCR can bind to JACKDAW and MAGPIE (and may be required to do so for SHR-SCR DNA-binding function), the expression domains of JACKDAW and MAGPIE are restricted to immature meristem tissue, while SHR and SCR are expressed throughout the root endodermis [38,39].
