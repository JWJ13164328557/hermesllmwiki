# To ensure efficient editing of the CcPDS gene, sgRNA target sequences 20 bp long and situated just u

## Claim
To ensure efficient editing of the CcPDS gene, sgRNA target sequences 20 bp long and situated just upstream of a 5′-NGG-3′ PAM site (5′- GATCATATTCAGTCCTTGGG-3’) were picked employing CRISPR RGENE software (www.rgenome.net, accessed on 11 March 2024).
