# Interestingly, OST1 in turn phosphorylates PP2CG1 at Ser365 to inhibit its phosphatase activity, con

## Claim
Interestingly, OST1 in turn phosphorylates PP2CG1 at Ser365 to inhibit its phosphatase activity, constituting a positive feedback loop to enhance OST1 activity in response to cold stress (Lv et al., 2021).

## Biological Context
Post-translational modifications play indispensable roles in cold stress responses of plants
