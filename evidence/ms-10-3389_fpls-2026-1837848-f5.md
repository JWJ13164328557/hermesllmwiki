# Taken together, the evidence indicates a fundamental distinction between PGRs and osmoprotectants: P

## Claim
Taken together, the evidence indicates a fundamental distinction between PGRs and osmoprotectants: PGRs primarily regulate stress responses at the signaling and developmental level, whereas osmoprotectants act at the cellular and biochemical level to stabilize plant function.

## Biological Context
Biostimulants and their role in increasing cereal crops’ resistance to drought stress
