## Claim
From previous experiments, it can be concluded that, 1.0 g of ­NiCl2 .6H2O as a catalyst using the microwave irradiation method is sufficient to produce cellulose di-acetate with a percentage yield of 89.85% in 8 min in the presence of 200 mL of ­Ac2O.

## Biological Context
Fabrication of dialysis membrane from cotton Giza 86 cellulose di-acetate prepared using Ac2O and NiCl2 as a new catalyst
