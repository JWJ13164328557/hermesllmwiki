# Conclusion: Altogether, our data suggest that specialized NuRD components are induced in the blastem

## Claim
Conclusion: Altogether, our data suggest that specialized NuRD components are induced in the blastema during fin regeneration and are involved in blastema cell proliferation and redifferentiation of osteoblast precursor cells.

## Biological Context
Specific NuRD components are required for fin regeneration in zebrafish.
