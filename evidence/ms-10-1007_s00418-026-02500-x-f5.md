# Methotrexate inhibits dihydro- folate reductase (DHFR), thereby blocking the conversion of dihydro- 

## Claim
Methotrexate inhibits dihydro- folate reductase (DHFR), thereby blocking the conversion of dihydro- folate (DHF) to tetrahydrofolate (THF), which is essential for the syn- thesis of deoxythymidine monophosphate (dTMP) from deoxyuridine monophosphate (dUMP).

## Biological Context
Replication stress induced exposure to methotrexate in root meristem cells of Vicia faba
