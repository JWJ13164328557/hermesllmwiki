# Consistent with the biochemical binding data, we found that AgenetFMRP exhibited higher afﬁnity for 

## Claim
Consistent with the biochemical binding data, we found that AgenetFMRP exhibited higher afﬁnity for histone H3 carrying lysine methylation mimics, including H3Kc79me2 (Kd 135 ± 28 nM; Figure S4B) and H3Kc27me1 (Kd 102 ± 11 nM; Figure S4C) as compared with unmethylated H3 (Kd 1063 ± 136 nM; Figure S4D).
