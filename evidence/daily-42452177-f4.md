# In gain-of-function mutants of the H3K4 demethylase JMJ15, most differentially expressed …

## Claim
In gain-of-function mutants of the H3K4 demethylase JMJ15, most differentially expressed genes were downregulated under control conditions, yet selected salt-responsive genes ( RD29A, RD29B, RD22, COR15A, COR47, P5CS1, and P5CS2 ) were actually hyper-induced after salt treatment compared to the wild type, suggesting they may not be direct targets of JMJ15 [ ].
