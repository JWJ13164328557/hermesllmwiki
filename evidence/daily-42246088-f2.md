# Collectively, non-cigarette tobacco products are used by approximately 1.7 million adults …

## Claim
Collectively, non-cigarette tobacco products are used by approximately 1.7 million adults in England, many of whom do not smoke cigarettes so will not be captured by surveys that focus only on cigarette smoking (eg, the Annual Population Survey ) or by tobacco cessation services and outreach programs.
