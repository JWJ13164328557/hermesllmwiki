# Our data support the model that molecular reprogramming occurs between tassel lengths of …

## Claim
Our data support the model that molecular reprogramming occurs between tassel lengths of 1.0 and 1.5 cm, which coincides with morphological changes in the tassel, such as the termination of BMs, SPMs, and SMs, and the initiation of florets containing stamen primordia.
