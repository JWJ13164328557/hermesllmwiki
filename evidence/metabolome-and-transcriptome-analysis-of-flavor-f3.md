# The bHLH family members are associated with anthocyanin biosynthesis in fruit trees and have been sh

## Claim
The bHLH family members are associated with anthocyanin biosynthesis in fruit trees and have been shown to interact with MYB TFs to regulate fruit color.

## Biological Context
Metabolome and transcriptome 分析 of flavor components and 类黄酮 生物合成 in fig female flower tissues (Ficus carica L.) after bagging
