# Therefore, integrating phenology-based irrigation with a combined soil-foliar Zn strategy is essenti

## Claim
Therefore, integrating phenology-based irrigation with a combined soil-foliar Zn strategy is essential for maximising wheat productivity and nutritional output, especially under conditions where either water or Zn may be limiting (39, 41, 42).

## Biological Context
Optimising irrigation and zinc management improves wheat yield, quality and resource use-efficiency in the Indo-Gangetic Plains
