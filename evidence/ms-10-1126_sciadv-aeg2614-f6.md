# Loss of PRDM16 is sufficient to induce senescence-­associated phenotypes and aging-­like transcripti

## Claim
Loss of PRDM16 is sufficient to induce senescence-­associated phenotypes and aging-­like transcriptional programs in human CMs, while restoration of PRDM16 in aged hearts attenuates age-­associated functional decline and partially re- verses aging-­related transcriptional states.

## Biological Context
Life-span–dependent transcriptional dynamics of the human heart
