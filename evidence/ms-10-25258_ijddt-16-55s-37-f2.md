# Thymol, a phenolic monoterpene, has perhaps been the most well studied of the compounds for its powe

## Claim
Thymol, a phenolic monoterpene, has perhaps been the most well studied of the compounds for its powerful bioactivity, structurally being a positional isomer of carvacrol and both having the capacity to interact with cellular membranes, ion channels and transcription factor complexes.
