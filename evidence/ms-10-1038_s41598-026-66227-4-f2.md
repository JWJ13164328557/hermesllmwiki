# Conclusion Taken together, Zn deficiency altered the composition of mucus, including the quantity an

## Claim
Conclusion Taken together, Zn deficiency altered the composition of mucus, including the quantity and types of mucins present, their N-glycosylation as well as tendencies toward altered cellular O-glycosylation, supporting the idea that intestinal symptoms associated with Zn deficiency are also attributable to impaired mucus production by goblet cells.

## Biological Context
Transcriptome, glycome, and mucinome analysis reveal zinc is essential for the composition of mucus in the human goblet cell model HT-29-MTX
