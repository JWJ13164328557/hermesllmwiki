# This study identifies ferroptosis as a critical pathological feature in PVAT of atherosclerotic arte

## Claim
This study identifies ferroptosis as a critical pathological feature in PVAT of atherosclerotic artery, establishing a previously unrecognized link between PVAT dysfunction and ferroptosis.

## Biological Context
CL-316243 facilitates stable atherosclerotic plaque phenotypes in association with suppression of perivascular adipose tissue ferroptosis via upregulating C/EBPβ
