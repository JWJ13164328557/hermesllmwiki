# Downstream of auxin signaling, MP directly regulates AHP6 expression, which allows the spatio-tempor

## Claim
Downstream of auxin signaling, MP directly regulates AHP6 expression, which allows the spatio-temporal establishment of cytokinin signaling and thereby stabilizes the plastochron.

## Biological Context
O cell, where art thou? The mechanisms of shoot meristem patterning.
