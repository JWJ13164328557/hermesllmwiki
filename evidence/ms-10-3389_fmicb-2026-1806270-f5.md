# RBD is the main target of neutralizing antibodies (NAbs) and is an ideal antigen for vaccine develop

## Claim
RBD is the main target of neutralizing antibodies (NAbs) and is an ideal antigen for vaccine development due to its immunodominance (Dai and Gao, 2021).

## Biological Context
Recombinant RBD-based subunit vaccines incorporating high-frequency mutation sites elicit cross-immunity and robust protection against SARS-CoV-2
