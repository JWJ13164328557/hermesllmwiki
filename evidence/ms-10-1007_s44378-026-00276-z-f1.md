# 2.4 Production of plant growth regulators (PGRs) PGPR have the ability to produce various phytohormo

## Claim
2.4 Production of plant growth regulators (PGRs) PGPR have the ability to produce various phytohormones such as Indole-3-acetic acid (IAA), which regulates cell elongation and root development; Abscisic acid (ABA), which plays a key role in stress tolerance; Cytokinins (CKs), which promote cell division and shoot formation; Gibberellins (GAs), which stimulate seed germination and stem elongation;

## Biological Context
Plant growth promoting rhizobacteria enhance resilience productivity and quality of high value spice crops
