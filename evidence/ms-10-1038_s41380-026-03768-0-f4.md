# iPSC studies of ketamine found increased neural progenitor proliferation via cAMP-IGF2 signaling [13

## Claim
iPSC studies of ketamine found increased neural progenitor proliferation via cAMP-IGF2 signaling [13] and enhanced structural plasticity via AMPAR-driven brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) and mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR) signaling [12], paralleling observations in animal models.

## Biological Context
Time-dependent effects of rapid-acting antidepressants in iPSC-derived neurons from treatment-resistant depression and healthy volunteers
