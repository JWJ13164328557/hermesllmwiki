# Interestingly, we found that the transcriptomes of CA-ROP2 and WT were nearly identical under N-suff

## Claim
Interestingly, we found that the transcriptomes of CA-ROP2 and WT were nearly identical under N-sufficient conditions; they became clearly separated over time under N starvation (Supplementary Figure 2A).

## Biological Context
Active ROP2 triggers leaf senescence and orchestrates the growth-senescence trade-off under nitrogen starvation
