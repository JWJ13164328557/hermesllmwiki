# In this study, we found that the synthesis and efﬂux of citric acid played an important role in pige

## Claim
In this study, we found that the synthesis and efﬂux of citric acid played an important role in pigeon pea plants in response to Al stress, which provided a certain methodology for analysis of the molecular mechanism of metabolites in plant stress resistance.

## Biological Context
CcNFYB3‐CcMATE35 and LncRNA CcLTCS‐CcCS modules jointly regulate the efflux and synthesis of citrate to enhance aluminium tolerance in pigeon pea
