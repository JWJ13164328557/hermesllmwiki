# Notably, human tumor-associated neutro- phils (TANs) have also been demonstrated to display elevated

## Claim
Notably, human tumor-associated neutro- phils (TANs) have also been demonstrated to display elevated expression of ARG-1 mRNA (Cane et al, 2025; Si et al, 2019; Ugolini et al, 2020), as well as to express (Si et al, 2019; Ugolini et al, 2020) and release (Rotondo et al, 2009) high levels of ARG-1 protein.
