# ABI2 and ABI1 represent highly homologous phosphatases that exert negative regulation …

## Claim
ABI2 and ABI1 represent highly homologous phosphatases that exert negative regulation over SnRK2 kinases and their target substrates via dephosphorylation; however, only ABI2 has been identified as a regulator of flowering time.
