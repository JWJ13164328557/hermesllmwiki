# As expected, overexpression or knockdown of PbrMYB132 increased or decreased stone cell content in p

## Claim
As expected, overexpression or knockdown of PbrMYB132 increased or decreased stone cell content in pear fruit and SCW thickness in Arabidopsis transgenic plants.
