# TFs co-enriched with LEC2 had significantly greater phytohormone-dependent effects on …

## Claim
TFs co-enriched with LEC2 had significantly greater phytohormone-dependent effects on LEC2 targets compared to non-target genes (Wilcoxon rank sum test; P <2.2e −3; ) and were strongly differentially expressed between hormone treatments at the onset of dedifferentiation (FDR < 0.05; ).
