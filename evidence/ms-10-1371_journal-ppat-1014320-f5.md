# We found that the abundance of transposon insertions in the rpfB, rpfF, and rpfC was reduced while r

## Claim
We found that the abundance of transposon insertions in the rpfB, rpfF, and rpfC was reduced while rpfG was increased, with log2FC values of -4.09, -0.68, -0.99, and 1.37, respectively (Fig 1D).

## Biological Context
A chemical bactericide dioctyldiethylenetriamine (Xinjunan) exerts a non-lethal effect by inhibiting RpfG activity to regulate the quorum sensing system
