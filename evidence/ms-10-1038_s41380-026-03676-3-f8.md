# Notably, the rare missense variant PLCG2 p.P522R (rs72824905 C > G) confers protection against AD an

## Claim
Notably, the rare missense variant PLCG2 p.P522R (rs72824905 C > G) confers protection against AD and other neurodegenerative diseases, consistent with functional studies showing that the P522R substitution produces a mildly hypermorphic enzyme that enhances microglial phagocytic responses and resilience to inﬂammatory stress [18, 19].
