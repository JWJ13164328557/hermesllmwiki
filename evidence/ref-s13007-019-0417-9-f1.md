# We show that initials in direct contact with the QC do exhibit a distinctly slower rate of division 

## Claim
We show that initials in direct contact with the QC do exhibit a distinctly slower rate of division com- pared to their daughters, which, in turn, showed a slightly lower rate of division than their daughters, forming a short, steep gradient over four cells.

## Biological Context
Week-long imaging of cell divisions in the Arabidopsis root meristem.
