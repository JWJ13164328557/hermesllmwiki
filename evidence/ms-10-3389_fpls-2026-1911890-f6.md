# This apparent contradiction is rationalized by the intensity and duration of the stress: while a rad

## Claim
This apparent contradiction is rationalized by the intensity and duration of the stress: while a radical decline in CK signaling is essential for survival during extreme, life-threatening drought, a moderate increase in CK signaling is more effective for maintaining productivity and cellular homeostasis during manageable, moderate stress episodes (reviewed by Savelieva et al., 2026).

## Biological Context
Cytokinin derivatives as tools to enhance plant stress resilience
