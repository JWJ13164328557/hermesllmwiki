# Asperulosidic acid, asperulosidic acid methyl ester, dictamnoside I, tricalysioside M …

## Claim
Asperulosidic acid, asperulosidic acid methyl ester, dictamnoside I, tricalysioside M, and globulusin B were specifically elevated in AAT1-treated plants, whereas other terpene glycosides, including verbenabraside A, dendroside C, dendronobiloside B, and eurostoside, showed increased abundance in both POT1- and AAT1-inoculated plants.
