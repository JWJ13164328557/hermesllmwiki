# While some transitions to white flowers involve gene loss or loss of function in …

## Claim
While some transitions to white flowers involve gene loss or loss of function in anthocyanin pathway genes (Coburn et al., 2015; Lin et al., 2022), others seem to involve primarily regulatory changes (Ho & Smith, 2016; Gates et al., 2018), suggesting that some losses of anthocyanins may be more easily reversible.
