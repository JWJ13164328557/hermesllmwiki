# Lastly, in both Beta vul- garis and Portulaca grandiﬂora, trans-acting Promoter of Anthocyanin Pigme

## Claim
Lastly, in both Beta vul- garis and Portulaca grandiﬂora, trans-acting Promoter of Anthocyanin Pigmentation 1 (PAP1) homologs have been found to lose their ability to interact with canonical bHLH partners in heterologous assays.
