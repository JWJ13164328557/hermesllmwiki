# These results confirm that co-existing conifers differ in the timing of cli­ matic control over radi

## Claim
These results confirm that co-existing conifers differ in the timing of cli­ matic control over radial growth, suggesting species-spe­ cific physiological strategies instead of a common response to environmental variability.

## Biological Context
Contrasting stem NSC dynamics in evergreen and deciduous conifers of a Siberian mixed forest
