# In Phalaenopsis, nitrate proportions of at least 50% are required for satisfactory vegetative growth

## Claim
In Phalaenopsis, nitrate proportions of at least 50% are required for satisfactory vegetative growth and flowering, and a 75:25 NO3 –-N: NH4 +-N ratio has been recommended [20].

## Biological Context
Nitrate-to-ammonium ratio shapes vegetative growth, nutrient status, and physiology of Vanilla planifolia in soilless culture
