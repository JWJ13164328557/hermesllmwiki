# We found that mid-life administration of Corylin, a flavonoid derived from Psoralea corylifolia, imp

## Claim
We found that mid-life administration of Corylin, a flavonoid derived from Psoralea corylifolia, improves metabolic function, muscle integrity, and physical performance in mice maintained on a standard diet.

## Biological Context
Corylin promotes healthy aging via RAGA–mTOR suppression and sex-dependent activation of SIRT3
