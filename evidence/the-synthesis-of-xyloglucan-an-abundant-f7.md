# S2), indicate that CSLC12, likely in combination with CSLC4, is required for proper growth of root h

## Claim
S2), indicate that CSLC12, likely in combination with CSLC4, is required for proper growth of root hairs and interaction of pollen with the tissues of the pistil.

## Biological Context
The synthesis of xyloglucan, an abundant plant cell wall polysaccharide, requires CSLC function
