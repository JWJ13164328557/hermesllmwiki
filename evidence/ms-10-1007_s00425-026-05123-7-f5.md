# In the absence of DEX, GR-fused TF is bound by the HSP90 protein, which prevents TF translocation to

## Claim
In the absence of DEX, GR-fused TF is bound by the HSP90 protein, which prevents TF translocation to the nucleus and thus blocks transcription of its target genes.

## Biological Context
Dexamethasone-inducible LhGR/pOp system: simple and flexible spatiotemporal control of gene expression
