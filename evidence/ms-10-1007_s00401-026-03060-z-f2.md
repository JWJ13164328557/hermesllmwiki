# Thus, our data show that PrP in F198S retina is composed of full- 348 length, detergent-insoluble gl

## Claim
Thus, our data show that PrP in F198S retina is composed of full- 348 length, detergent-insoluble glycosylated PrP with a glycosylation pattern distinct from that of control retina.

## Biological Context
Distinctive properties of the prion protein in the brain and retina in the amyloidosis associated with the PRNP F198S mutation
