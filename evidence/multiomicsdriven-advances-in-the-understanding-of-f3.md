# In cotton, overexpression of GhBCCP1, GhBC1, or GhCTb resulted in increased CSO content …

## Claim
In cotton, overexpression of GhBCCP1, GhBC1, or GhCTb resulted in increased CSO content by 17% to 22%, whereas overexpression of GhCTa2 in transgenic cotton seeds showed no significant change in oil content (Cui et al., 2017).
