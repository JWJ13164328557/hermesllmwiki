# Remarkably, CYCD3;1 appears to be also the target of cytokinin signaling and triple mutants for all 

## Claim
Remarkably, CYCD3;1 appears to be also the target of cytokinin signaling and triple mutants for all three CYCD3 subfamily members in Arabidopsis showed a reduced shoot branching resembling plants with reduced cytoki- nin levels.
