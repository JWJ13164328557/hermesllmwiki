# The activities of these nucleases are tightly controlled by the Polo-like kinase Cdc5, which directl

## Claim
The activities of these nucleases are tightly controlled by the Polo-like kinase Cdc5, which directly phosphorylates Mus81-Mms4 to stimulate its nuclease activity at the prophase I-to-metaphase I transition 24 and indirectly regulates Yen1 through Cdc14-dependent dephosphorylation during anaphase 26,27.

## Biological Context
Distinct and overlapping roles of MutLγ, Mus81-Mms4, and STR in meiotic Holliday junction processing
