# At the start of this circuit, the transcription factor proteins Circadian Locomotor Output Cycles Ka

## Claim
At the start of this circuit, the transcription factor proteins Circadian Locomotor Output Cycles Kaput (Clock) and Aryl hydrocarbon receptor nuclear translocator-like (Arntl, also known as Bmal) form a heterodimer that binds to E-box promoter elements across the genome.

## Biological Context
Convergent latitudinal erosion of circadian systems in a rapidly diversifying order of fishes
