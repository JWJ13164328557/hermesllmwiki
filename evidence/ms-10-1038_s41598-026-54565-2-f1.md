# Notably, the total sugars, NRS, and NRS/RS ratio in both the shoot and taproot organs of sugar beet,

## Claim
Notably, the total sugars, NRS, and NRS/RS ratio in both the shoot and taproot organs of sugar beet, in addition to the TPs in the shoot, at MMNPs2 were statistically comparable to those obtained at MMNPs1 treatment.

## Biological Context
Physiological homeostasis, yield, and sugar profile of salt-stressed sugar beet plants as influenced by nano-structured mixture of zinc, boron, and molybdenum application
