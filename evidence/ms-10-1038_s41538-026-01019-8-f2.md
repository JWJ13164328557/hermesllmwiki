# Here, through RNA-seq-based transcriptome analysis of the liver and small intestine, we found a sign

## Claim
Here, through RNA-seq-based transcriptome analysis of the liver and small intestine, we found a significant higher expression of cholesterol biosynthesis genes in formula-fed piglets compared to their sow-fed counterparts (Figure 1).

## Biological Context
Lower-cholesterol formula feeding alters the early-life cholesterol-bile acid axis in neonatal piglets
