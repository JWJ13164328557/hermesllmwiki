# 3.2 Mechanism of action Several bacteriocins produced by lactic acid bacteria isolated from milk kef

## Claim
3.2 Mechanism of action Several bacteriocins produced by lactic acid bacteria isolated from milk kefir grains, kill target cells by creating pores in the membrane, although alternative mechanisms, such as inhibition of cell wall synthesis and interference with nucleic acid or protein synthesis, have also been described (Hassan et al., 2012; Kumariya et al., 2019; Ríos Colombo et al., 2018).
