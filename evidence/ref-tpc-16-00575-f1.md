# Here, we show that LZY1, LZY2, and their paralog AtDRO1/AtNGR2/LZY3 are redundantly involved in both

## Claim
Here, we show that LZY1, LZY2, and their paralog AtDRO1/AtNGR2/LZY3 are redundantly involved in both shoot and root gravitropism and that their corre- sponding proteins play a key role in controlling lateral auxin ﬂow after the reorientation of statocytes.
