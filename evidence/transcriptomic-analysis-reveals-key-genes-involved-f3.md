# The high expression of FA de novo biosynthesis-related genes, encompassed ACCase, MAT, KAS, KAR, EAR

## Claim
The high expression of FA de novo biosynthesis-related genes, encompassed ACCase, MAT, KAS, KAR, EAR, HAD, and TAG biosynthesis-related genes, including GPAT, LPAT, LPCAT, PLA2 during S1~S2, which was crucial for the rapid accu- mulation of oil in the early seed development stages.
