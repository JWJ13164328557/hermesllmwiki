# Identifying the VP1uR is essential for understanding the fundamental mechanism that governs B19V tro

## Claim
Identifying the VP1uR is essential for understanding the fundamental mechanism that governs B19V tropism and viral entry, both in the target erythroid progenitors and in transplacental transmission.

## Biological Context
Transferrin receptor 1 binds human parvovirus B19 VP1u to facilitate entry
