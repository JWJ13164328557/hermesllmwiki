# In this study, we show that loss-of-function mutations in MLO2, MLO6, and MLO12 result in …

## Claim
In this study, we show that loss-of-function mutations in MLO2, MLO6, and MLO12 result in a near-complete failure of PM pathogenesis, even in eds1/pad4/sid2 triple and eds1/pad4/sid2/pen1/pen2/pen3 sextuple mutant backgrounds, both of which are severely compromised in immune signaling and defense against various (potential) pathogens.
