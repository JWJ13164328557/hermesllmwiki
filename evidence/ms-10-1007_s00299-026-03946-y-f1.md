# Functional analysis demonstrated that overexpression of NtMADS91 significantly promoted axillary bud

## Claim
Functional analysis demonstrated that overexpression of NtMADS91 significantly promoted axillary bud growth, increasing both the number and length of axillary buds.

## Biological Context
Genome-wide characterization of MADS-box genes and their roles in axillary bud development in tobacco
