# We found that the fluorescence intensity of the rpfG-GFP-PXO99A strain decreased with the increasing

## Claim
We found that the fluorescence intensity of the rpfG-GFP-PXO99A strain decreased with the increasing con­ centration of dioctyldiethylenetriamine (Fig 2B), while that of the GFP-PXO99A strain showed no significant change (S4 Fig).

## Biological Context
A chemical bactericide dioctyldiethylenetriamine (Xinjunan) exerts a non-lethal effect by inhibiting RpfG activity to regulate the quorum sensing system
