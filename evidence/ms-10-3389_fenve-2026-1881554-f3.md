# Importantly, the CO2 loading at the end of adsorption does not exactly match the loading at the star

## Claim
Importantly, the CO2 loading at the end of adsorption does not exactly match the loading at the start of the subsequent vacuum stage (t = 0), indicating that the system does not fully return to an identical state after a single cycle.

## Biological Context
Cycle-resolved dynamic modeling of atmospheric CO2 separation systems under time-varying ambient and electricity conditions
