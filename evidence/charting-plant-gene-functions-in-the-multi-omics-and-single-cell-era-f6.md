# Aside from genes known to be essential in anaerobic metabolism and hypoxia response, such as the PCO

## Claim
Aside from genes known to be essential in anaerobic metabolism and hypoxia response, such as the PCO family, genes not well associated with submergence, such as the PYR/PYL family, were identiﬁed and might pose novel targets to enhance ﬂooding tolerance in susceptible crops.
