# Overall, we show that GSH acts as a signal in regeneration where, upon wounding, GSH enters the nucl

## Claim
Overall, we show that GSH acts as a signal in regeneration where, upon wounding, GSH enters the nucleus, prompting a rapid exit from G1, a fast cell cycle, and cell-fate reprogramming.

Glutathione accelerates the cell cycle and cellular reprogramming in plant regen
