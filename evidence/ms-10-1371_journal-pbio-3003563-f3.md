# Using RT-qPCR, we found that the heat-shock promoter induced similar mRNA expression of sept-1(N2) a

## Claim
Using RT-qPCR, we found that the heat-shock promoter induced similar mRNA expression of sept-1(N2) and sept-1(XZ1516) (S5A Fig), but western blot results indicated that the same heat-shock condition resulted in higher expres­ sion of SEPT-1(XZ1516) proteins than SEPT-1(N2) (both are fused with HA-tags; S5B Fig).
