# We show that high DCTPP1 expression mediates cell-intrinsic resistance to nucleoside DNMTi, and that

## Claim
We show that high DCTPP1 expression mediates cell-intrinsic resistance to nucleoside DNMTi, and that triptolide and its analogs could overcome this resistance.

## Biological Context
Triptolide sensitizes cancer cells to nucleoside DNA methyltransferase inhibitors through inhibition of DCTPP1-mediated cell-intrinsic resistance
