# Notably, a shared subset of antibody targets was enriched in LTBI, RSTRs and IV-BCG-immunized NHPs—p

## Claim
Notably, a shared subset of antibody targets was enriched in LTBI, RSTRs and IV-BCG-immunized NHPs—populations with enhanced control over Mtb infection and validated biophysical and functional antibody responses to selected antigens.

## Biological Context
A proteome-wide atlas of humoral immunity to Mycobacterium tuberculosis across the spectrum of disease
