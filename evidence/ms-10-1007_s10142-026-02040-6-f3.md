# Mechanistically, we found that NFS1 selectively activates the PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling cascade at the

## Claim
Mechanistically, we found that NFS1 selectively activates the PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling cascade at the phosphorylation level without altering total protein abun­ dance, leading to the upregulation of GPX4 expression, the central ferroptosis suppressor, and thereby enhancing fer­ roptosis resistance.

## Biological Context
NFS1 activates PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling to upregulate GPX4 expression and enhance ferroptosis resistance in osteosarcoma
