# MYBA1 and MYBA2 trigger the expression of target genes such as VviUFGT, which in turn encodes the UD

## Claim
MYBA1 and MYBA2 trigger the expression of target genes such as VviUFGT, which in turn encodes the UDP- glucose:flavonoid 3-O-glucosyltransferase enzyme that limits anthocyanin production (Kobayashi et al., 2002; Walker et al., 2007).
