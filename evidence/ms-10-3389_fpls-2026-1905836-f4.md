# Activated ethylene signaling can also reduce levels of bioactive GAs by inhibiting GA biosynthesis e

## Claim
Activated ethylene signaling can also reduce levels of bioactive GAs by inhibiting GA biosynthesis enzymes such as GA20ox (Achard et al., 2007), and the ethylene-regulated transcription factor EIN3 can physically interact with the JAZ-family repressors, which themselves interact with DELLAs (Colebrook et al., 2014; Davière and Achard, 2013).

## Biological Context
Gibberellin pathway remodeling accompanies ethylene-driven sex reversal in Cannabis sativa
