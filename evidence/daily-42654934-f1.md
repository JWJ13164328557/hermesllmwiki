# The MEturquoise and MEmagenta modules were specifically associated with aborted pistils …

## Claim
The MEturquoise and MEmagenta modules were specifically associated with aborted pistils from hermaphroditic and male flowers, respectively, indicating that these two forms of pistil abortion are governed by distinct transcriptional programs.
