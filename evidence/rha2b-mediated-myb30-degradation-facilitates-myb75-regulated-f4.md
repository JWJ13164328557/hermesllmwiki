# Importantly, we found that expression of MYB75 was much lower in rha2b-1 plants but much higher in m

## Claim
Importantly, we found that expression of MYB75 was much lower in rha2b-1 plants but much higher in myb30-2 and myb30-2 rha2b-1 plants than in Col-0 plants (Figure 6F), supporting the conclusion that the RHA2b–MYB30 module regulates MYB75 at the transcriptional level.

## Biological Context
RHA2b-介导的 MYB30 degradation facilitates MYB75-regulated, sucrose-induced 花青素 生物合成 in Arabidopsis seedlings
