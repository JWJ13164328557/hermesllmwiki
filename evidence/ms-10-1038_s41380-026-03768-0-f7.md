# Pathways governing metabolism, growth, and proliferation, including mTORC1 signaling, oxidative phos

## Claim
Pathways governing metabolism, growth, and proliferation, including mTORC1 signaling, oxidative phosphorylation, fatty acid metabo- lism, glycolysis, and MYC target pathways (V1 and V2) were signiﬁcantly upregulated in the excitatory EX_Mature_1 cluster and simultaneously downregulated in the inhibitory IN_Mid_2 cluster.

## Biological Context
Time-dependent effects of rapid-acting antidepressants in iPSC-derived neurons from treatment-resistant depression and healthy volunteers
