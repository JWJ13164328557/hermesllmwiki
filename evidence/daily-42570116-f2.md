# SynDsbAB shows structural similarities to proteins involved in disulfide bond formation …

## Claim
SynDsbAB shows structural similarities to proteins involved in disulfide bond formation in non-photosynthetic prokaryotes and eukaryotes where disulfide bridges in the substrate proteins are formed by electron transfer from two target cysteines in these proteins through cysteine pairs in SynDsbA, SynDsbB to a quinone acceptor (Inaba and Ito ).
