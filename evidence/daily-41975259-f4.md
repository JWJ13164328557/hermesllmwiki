# In summary, most of the proteins highly expressed during the rapid growth phase (MG) have …

## Claim
In summary, most of the proteins highly expressed during the rapid growth phase (MG) have been confirmed to be involved in cancer initiation and progression by affecting processes such as cell proliferation and signal transduction, whereas the proteins highly expressed during the ossification phase (LG) are primarily associated with chondrogenesis and differentiation.
