# STM directly activates transcription of the cytokinin biosynthetic enzyme gene IPT7 in the shoot mer

## Claim
STM directly activates transcription of the cytokinin biosynthetic enzyme gene IPT7 in the shoot meristem, and exogenous application of cytokinins or expression of a cytokinin biosynthetic enzyme from the STM promoter can rescue the stm mutant phenotype.
