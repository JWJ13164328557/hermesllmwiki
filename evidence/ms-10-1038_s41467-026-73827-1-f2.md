# Collectively, these findings suggest that SphK2 inhibition drives the differentiation of suppressive

## Claim
Collectively, these findings suggest that SphK2 inhibition drives the differentiation of suppressive monocytic MDSCs into an immunostimulatory, mature macrophage-like phenotype, thereby unleashing T-cell activity.

## Biological Context
Sphingosine kinase-2 inhibition promotes immunogenic differentiation of myeloid-derived suppressor cells through an Acetyl-CoA carboxylase-phosphatidylcholine axis
