# We found both Pf12 and Pf41 exhibited low nucleotide diversity and limited haplotype variation, indi

## Claim
We found both Pf12 and Pf41 exhibited low nucleotide diversity and limited haplotype variation, indicating substantial sequence conservation compared to highly polymorphic invasion ligands such as PfEBA-175 and PfAMA1, as well as the merozoite surface antigen PfMSP128,33,34.

## Biological Context
Conserved genetic and antigenic profiles of Pf12 and Pf41 in asymptomatic Plasmodium falciparum carriers in Tanzania
