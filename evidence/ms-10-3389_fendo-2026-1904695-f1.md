# In summary, these results suggest that the lipid metabolism network is primarily affected by differe

## Claim
In summary, these results suggest that the lipid metabolism network is primarily affected by differential lipid metab- olites along two functional axes: membrane lipid remodeling, which is characterized by glycerophospholipid and sphingolipid metabo- lism, and polyunsaturated fatty acid metabolism, which is centered on alpha–linolenic acid metabolism.

## Biological Context
Amniotic-fluid metabolomics identifies phospholipid remodeling as a metabolic signature of intrauterine exposure in pregnancies with polycystic ovary syndrome
