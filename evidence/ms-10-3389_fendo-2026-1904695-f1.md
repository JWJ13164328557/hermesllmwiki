# In summary, these results suggest that the lipid metabolism network is primarily affected …

## Claim
In summary, these results suggest that the lipid metabolism network is primarily affected by differential lipid metabolites along two functional axes: membrane lipid remodeling, which is characterized by glycerophospholipid and sphingolipid metabolism, and polyunsaturated fatty acid metabolism, which is centered on alpha–linolenic acid metabolism.
