# Notably, the maximal tension rule confers superior robustness against stochastic ﬂuctuations in divi

## Claim
Notably, the maximal tension rule confers superior robustness against stochastic ﬂuctuations in division orientation compared to the least area rule, because the sequential formation of division walls continuously updates the tension distribution to guide subsequent orientation.

## Biological Context
3D geometry and mechanics of a single apical stem cell ensure helically symmetric plant body in multicellular models
