# Microbiota-modified bile acids interact with host receptors, most notably TGR5, also known as GPBAR1

## Claim
Microbiota-modified bile acids interact with host receptors, most notably TGR5, also known as GPBAR1 (G protein–coupled bile acid receptor 1).

## Biological Context
Loss of TGR5-activating bile acids is associated with disease activity in inflammatory bowel disease
