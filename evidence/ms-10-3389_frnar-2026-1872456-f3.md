# Expanding beyond modification site catalogues, RMBase v3.0 now integrates over 1,880 high-throughput

## Claim
Expanding beyond modification site catalogues, RMBase v3.0 now integrates over 1,880 high-throughput datasets to annotate 73 distinct modification types across 62 species, with modules linking modification sites to RNA-binding protein interactomes, miRNA target overlaps, GWAS- derived disease variants, and histone modification co-occurrence patterns (Xuan et al., 2024).

Reading between the bases: technological advances in RNA modification detection
