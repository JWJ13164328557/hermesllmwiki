# RNF220 functions through mediating either K48-linked polyubiquitination and proteasomal degradation 

## Claim
RNF220 functions through mediating either K48-linked polyubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of its target or K63-linked ubiquitination or monoubiquitination[17–22] of the substrates to regulate their localization or activity.

## Biological Context
RNF220 safeguards meiotic synapsis and male fertility by antagonizing SYCE1 ubiquitination
