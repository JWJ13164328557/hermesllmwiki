# DNA Affinity Purification Sequencing (DAP-seq) analy- sis revealed that GhSBP1 binds to the GTAC mot

## Claim
DNA Affinity Purification Sequencing (DAP-seq) analy- sis revealed that GhSBP1 binds to the GTAC motif and directly activates the expression of downstream targets, including GhNRT1.5 (a nitrate transporter), GhEzrA (a cytoskeleton-associated protein), and GhSH3P2 (involved in membrane trafficking).
