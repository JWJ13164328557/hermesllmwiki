# Neuropeptides released from sensory nerves, such as CGRP, substance P, and vasoactive intestinal pep

## Claim
Neuropeptides released from sensory nerves, such as CGRP, substance P, and vasoactive intestinal peptide, bind to their respec- tive receptors and either suppress effector immune cell functions or promote regulatory phenotypes, thereby shaping antitumor immune responses (35).
