# Specifically, we introduced mutations in three target genes MsALS1, MsALS2 and MsACC1 to …

## Claim
Specifically, we introduced mutations in three target genes MsALS1, MsALS2 and MsACC1 to confer herbicide resistance: mutations in MsALS1 and MsALS2 were designed to confer resistance to ALSinhibiting herbicides (based on evidence from their rice homologs), while those in MsACC1 were targeted to confer resistance to aryloxyphenoxypropionate (APP) herbicides.
