# Taken together, our findings demonstrate that MSC resistance stems from overlapping survival strateg

## Claim
Taken together, our findings demonstrate that MSC resistance stems from overlapping survival strategies, including slow proliferation and attenuated apoptotic priming, and underscore that selecting an appropriate “normal cell” comparator is critical when evaluating therapeutic windows in drug discovery platforms.
