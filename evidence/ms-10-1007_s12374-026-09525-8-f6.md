# By generating introgression lines in which a functional AN2 allele produces anthocy­ anin-pigmented 

## Claim
By generating introgression lines in which a functional AN2 allele produces anthocy­ anin-pigmented purple flowers and a non-functional allele yields white flowers, they demonstrated that manipulating this single gene was sufficient to shift pollinator preference between bumblebees and hawkmoths.
