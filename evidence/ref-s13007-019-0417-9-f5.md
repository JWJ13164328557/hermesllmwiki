# We found that the cell cycle was indeed sped up in the presence of light, with a median of 12.57 h i

## Claim
We found that the cell cycle was indeed sped up in the presence of light, with a median of 12.57 h in the TA zone (Additional file 16), showing that the on-stage growth system allows for growth rates typically observed in other experimental systems [17].

## Biological Context
Week-long imaging of cell divisions in the Arabidopsis root meristem.
