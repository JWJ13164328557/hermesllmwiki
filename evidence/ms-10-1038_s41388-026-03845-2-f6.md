# Collectively, these features create a dependency on replication stress response pathways, rendering 

## Claim
Collectively, these features create a dependency on replication stress response pathways, rendering Ewing sarcoma cells particularly vulnerable to agents that either amplify replication stress or inhibit its resolution [13–20].

## Biological Context
DNA replication stress and translational repression converge to drive CDK1- and caspase-dependent apoptosis in Ewing sarcoma
