# THBS2 knockdown inhibited the PI3K/AKT pathway phosphorylation, declined c-MYC expressions, weakened

## Claim
THBS2 knockdown inhibited the PI3K/AKT pathway phosphorylation, declined c-MYC expressions, weakened the MYC/MAX dimerization and the binding ability of MYC to target gene promoters.

## Biological Context
Down-regulation of THBS2 inhibits the malignant progression of Burkitt lymphoma by blocking the PI3K/AKT/c-MYC pathway
