# In addition, AR directly activates the expression of the cystine transporter subunits SLC7A11 and SL

## Claim
In addition, AR directly activates the expression of the cystine transporter subunits SLC7A11 and SLC3A2, as well as GPX4, thereby enhancing glutathione-dependent antioxidant defenses and facilitating the clearance of lipid peroxides[12- 14].

## Biological Context
Disruption of the AR/ZNF217/PROM2 axis sensitizes prostate cancer to ferroptosis and enzalutamide therapy
