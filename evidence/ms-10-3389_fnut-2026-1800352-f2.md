# The clock-speciﬁc pattern we observed is biologically coherent: epigenetic clocks diﬀer in their tra

## Claim
The clock-speciﬁc pattern we observed is biologically coherent: epigenetic clocks diﬀer in their training targets and therefore in sensitivity to social and environmental exposures, with mortality- or risk- informed clocks plausibly more responsive to material adversity than ﬁrst-generation chronological-age clocks (76, 77).

## Biological Context
Food insecurity, epigenetic age acceleration, and depression in middle-aged and older adults: a longitudinal cohort study
