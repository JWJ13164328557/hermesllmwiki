# Induced by chemotherapy and radiation therapy, SASP paradoxically remodels the brain microenvironmen

## Claim
Induced by chemotherapy and radiation therapy, SASP paradoxically remodels the brain microenvironment, promoting glioma cell stemness, angiogenesis, and immune evasion, thereby emerging as a critical target for overcoming therapeutic resistance and preventing relapse.
