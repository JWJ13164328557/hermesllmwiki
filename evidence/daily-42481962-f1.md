# In conclusion, under high planting density, early PGR application at the 10leaf stage …

## Claim
In conclusion, under high planting density, early PGR application at the 10leaf stage significantly reduces lodging by 37.2% through enhancing stalk mechanical strength and root anchorage, whereas application at the 15leaf stage significantly increases grain yield by 5.8% by extending the active grainfilling period and improving thousandkernel weight.
