# Mechanistically, CUL7 interacts with Kelch-like ECH-associated protein 1 (KEAP1) and catalyzes K29- 

## Claim
Mechanistically, CUL7 interacts with Kelch-like ECH-associated protein 1 (KEAP1) and catalyzes K29- and K48-linked polyubiquitination to promote its proteasomal degradation, which is crucial for NRF2 signaling.

## Biological Context
CUL7-mediated KEAP1 ubiquitination promotes the progression of colon cancer via NRF2 signaling
