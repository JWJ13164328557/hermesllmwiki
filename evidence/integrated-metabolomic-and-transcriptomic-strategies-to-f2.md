# We found the expression levels of 7 CsPAL genes were significantly down-regulated with a range of 2.

## Claim
We found the expression levels of 7 CsPAL genes were significantly down-regulated with a range of 2.10–3.98-fold compared with control group, while CsCHS, CsANS, CsFLS, CsANR and CsLAR genes showed over a 2-fold reduction, and two 4CL genes were decreased by over 3-fold.
