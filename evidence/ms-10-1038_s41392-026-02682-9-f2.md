# C-MET, the receptor for hepatocyte growth factor (HGF), plays a crucial role in the HGF/C-MET signal

## Claim
C-MET, the receptor for hepatocyte growth factor (HGF), plays a crucial role in the HGF/C-MET signaling pathway, which is involved in various processes such as tumor cell growth, invasion, migration, angiogenesis, epithelial-mesenchymal transition, tumor microenvironment remodeling and therapeutic resistance.
