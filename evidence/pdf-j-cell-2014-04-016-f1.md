# Interestingly, we found the Gata6 locus to have silencing chromatin modiﬁcations (H3K27me3) in BMDMs

## Claim
Interestingly, we found the Gata6 locus to have silencing chromatin modiﬁcations (H3K27me3) in BMDMs and inﬂammatory macrophages, whereas Gata6 locus in LPMs has an H3K4me3 mark associated with active chromatin (Figures 6C and S5D).
