# As previously noted, NKCC2, NCC, and ENaC are the targets of loop, thiazide, and K+-sparing diuretic

## Claim
As previously noted, NKCC2, NCC, and ENaC are the targets of loop, thiazide, and K+-sparing diuretics, common medications for hypertension (Arumugham and Shahin, 2024).

## Biological Context
Circadian rhythms in glomerular filtration govern natriuresis and diuretic responsiveness
