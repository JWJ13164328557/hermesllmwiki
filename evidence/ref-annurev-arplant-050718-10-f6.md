# Inhibition of auxin signaling via expression of a stabilized AXR3/INDOLE-3-ACETIC ACID17 …

## Claim
Inhibition of auxin signaling via expression of a stabilized AXR3/INDOLE-3-ACETIC ACID17 (IAA17) isoform in the expanding epidermal cells renders root growth partially resistant to auxin, suggesting that more downstream components of canonical, transcriptional auxin signaling are involved in this nontranscriptional pathway.
