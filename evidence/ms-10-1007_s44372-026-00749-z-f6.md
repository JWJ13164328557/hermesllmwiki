# These elicitors interact with plant root cells and initiate a cascade of cellular signalling that ac

## Claim
These elicitors interact with plant root cells and initiate a cascade of cellular signalling that acti­ vates defense-related genes leading to accumulation of the defense enzymes such as per­ oxidases, phenylalanine ammonia-lyase (PAL), chitinases, and β-1,3-glucanases in distal plant tissues.
