# The 10 most affected TF families were zf-C2H2, TF-bZIP, Homeobox, bHLH, ZBTB, HMG, ETS, THR-like, MB

## Claim
The 10 most affected TF families were zf-C2H2, TF-bZIP, Homeobox, bHLH, ZBTB, HMG, ETS, THR-like, MBD, and NGFIB-like (Figure 6B), forming a regulatory network that orchestrates the key downstream targets involved in insulin secretion, glucose sensing, and cellular stress responses (Figure 6C; Supplementary Table 3).

## Biological Context
Acute glucose stimulation drives coordinated translational reprogramming in primary pancreatic islets: from global remodeling to fine-tuned insulin synthesis
