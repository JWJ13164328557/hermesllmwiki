# Viral entry is mediated by the GP, which binds to host cell surface receptors including C-type lecti

## Claim
Viral entry is mediated by the GP, which binds to host cell surface receptors including C-type lectins (e.g., DC-SIGN, Dendritic Cell-Specific Intercellular adhesion molecule-3-Grabbing Non-integrin) and TIM-1 (T-cell immunoglobulin and mucin domain-containing protein 1) receptors [13–24], thereby facilitating internalization via macropinocytosis [25–27].

## Biological Context
Antibody Therapy Targeting GP in Treatment of Ebola Virus Infection
