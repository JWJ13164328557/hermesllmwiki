# Importantly, iNK cells maintained high expression of activating and cytotoxic molecules, including N

## Claim
Importantly, iNK cells maintained high expression of activating and cytotoxic molecules, including NKp46, CD69, and perforin, and did not exhibit significant upregulation of exhaustion-associated markers such as CD57 or TIGIT following stimulation with K562 cells (Figure 2A; Supplementary Figure S4).

## Biological Context
From iPSC to manufactured iNK cells using CombiCult® screening platform
