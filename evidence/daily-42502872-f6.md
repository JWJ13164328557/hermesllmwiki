# The enhanced fruity and woody attributes in ORG green tea were mainly attributed to the …

## Claim
The enhanced fruity and woody attributes in ORG green tea were mainly attributed to the presence of unique odor-active compounds, including 3,7-dimethylocta-1,5,7-trien-3-ol, δ-dodecalactone, and α-farnesene, which were detected exclusively in ORG samples.
