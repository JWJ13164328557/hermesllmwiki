# Our results suggest that the observed paleodiversity patterns were likely influenced by abiotic fact

## Claim
Our results suggest that the observed paleodiversity patterns were likely influenced by abiotic factors, and that Flagellicaudata and Rebbachisauridae may have been subject to distinct climatic constraints, potentially reflecting different ecological strategies.
