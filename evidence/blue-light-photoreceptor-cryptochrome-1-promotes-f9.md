# The target sequences (∼150 bp) of the UFGT1, CHS1 and C3H3 promoters were amplified and ligated into

## Claim
The target sequences (∼150 bp) of the UFGT1, CHS1 and C3H3 promoters were amplified and ligated into the SalⅠand KpnⅠ digestion sites of the pLacZi2μ vector to generate ProUFGT1‐lacZ, ProCHS1‐lacZ or ProC3H3‐lacZ, respectively (Lin et al., 2007).
