# Collectively, these results indicate that at E14.5, RFP expression is driven by Ksp promoter and str

## Claim
Collectively, these results indicate that at E14.5, RFP expression is driven by Ksp promoter and strictly confined to KSP+ cells in RFPKC mice, establishing that RFP serves as a faithful reporter for lineage tracing of KSP+ cell progeny throughout kidney development.

## Biological Context
Lineage tracing reveals KSP+ cells as multipotent progenitors driving postnatal renal development and injury repair via notch signaling
