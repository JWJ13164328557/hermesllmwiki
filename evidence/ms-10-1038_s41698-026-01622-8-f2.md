# In particular, we highlight how spatial transcriptomics has shifted TAM research from phenotype-base

## Claim
In particular, we highlight how spatial transcriptomics has shifted TAM research from phenotype-based classification toward niche-based functional interpretation, revealing how TAMs interact with cancer cells, T cells, CAFs, endothelial cells and B cells in defined anatomical regions such as hypoxic cores, invasive margins, perivascular areas and tertiary lymphoid structures.

## Biological Context
Spatiotemporal multi-omics reveals lineage, spatial and functional heterogeneity of tumor-associated macrophages and therapeutic vulnerabilities in the tumor microenvironment
