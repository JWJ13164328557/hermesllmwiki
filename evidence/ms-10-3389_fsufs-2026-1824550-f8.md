# Due to the limited seed numbers per progeny line, the plants could not be harvested at the immature 

## Claim
Due to the limited seed numbers per progeny line, the plants could not be harvested at the immature (R6) stage, and mature seeds (F5 generation) were required for advancement to the next selection cycle of the breeding programme.

## Biological Context
Physio-biochemical and morphological screening of F4 vegetable-type soybean progeny lines under water-limited stress conditions
