# We found no signiﬁcant difference in the transcription levels of three collision gases (CADs) betwee

## Claim
We found no signiﬁcant difference in the transcription levels of three collision gases (CADs) between TW and OW or between NW, but their translation levels were signiﬁcantly downregulated in TW, suggesting post-transcriptional control for CAD.
