# The combined Tau + Spd + Nd treatment elicited the greatest amelioration, significantly …

## Claim
The combined Tau + Spd + Nd treatment elicited the greatest amelioration, significantly increasing the root length, shoot fresh weight, and leaf area by 59.13%, 67.95%, and 54.20%, respectively, relative to sole Nd stress, demonstrating a strong synergistic protective effect.
