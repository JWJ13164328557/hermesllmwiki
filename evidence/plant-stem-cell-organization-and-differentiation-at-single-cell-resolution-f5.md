# As expected, we found that epidermal cell differentiation correlates with up-regulation of the OUTER

## Claim
As expected, we found that epidermal cell differentiation correlates with up-regulation of the OUTER CELL LAYER (OCL) homeodomain leucine zipper IV transcription factor- encoding genes that promote epidermal cell identity (28).
