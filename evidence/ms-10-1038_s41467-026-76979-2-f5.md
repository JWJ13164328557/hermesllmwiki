# However, ﬁrm inference of the timing of ρ-WGD and the mode of polyploidization depends on the evolut

## Claim
However, ﬁrm inference of the timing of ρ-WGD and the mode of polyploidization depends on the evolutionary relationships of Streptochaeta and Anomochloa; while conserved target capture sequence data support their monophyly39, a high-quality genome of Anomochloa would be useful to conﬁrm these results.

## Biological Context
Chromosome-level Streptochaeta genome elucidates the ancestral karyotype and allopolyploid origin of grasses
