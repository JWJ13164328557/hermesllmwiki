# We found that the truncated LZY3 protein, for which transcript was detected, did not rescue the grav

## Claim
We found that the truncated LZY3 protein, for which transcript was detected, did not rescue the gravitropic phenotype of lzy1 lzy2 lzy3 roots, and nor did the truncated LZY2 (Figure 4B; Supplemental Figures 15 and 16A).
