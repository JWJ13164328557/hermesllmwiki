# Here we report how state-of-the-art omics methods not only accelerate gene discovery …

## Claim
Here we report how state-of-the-art omics methods not only accelerate gene discovery through the high-resolution spatial resolution of gene expression but also how complementary omics data facilitates the construction of a more holistic view of the genome encapsulating genes, gene regulation in 2D (linear) and 3D (chromatin) space, and genic end products, that is, metabolites.
