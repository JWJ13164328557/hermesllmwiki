# Notably, a recent study showed that the AP2a‐DWARF‐BZR1 module positively regulates lycopene biosynt

## Claim
Notably, a recent study showed that the AP2a‐DWARF‐BZR1 module positively regulates lycopene biosynthesis (Sang et al., 2022); however, DWARF and BZR1, which are both downstream targets of AP2a, act as positive regulators of fruit softening (Sang et al., 2022; Meng et al., 2023), which is inconsistent with the ﬁnding that AP2a negatively regulates fruit softening.
