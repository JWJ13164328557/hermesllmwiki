# Understanding how EVs contribute to the infection process, including their role in modulating the ho

## Claim
Understanding how EVs contribute to the infection process, including their role in modulating the host immune system is crucial, as they represent both key determinants of pathogenesis, potential therapeutic targets and promising tools for the development of EV-based vaccines or immunomodulatory strategies.

## Biological Context
Staphylococcus aureus derived extracellular vesicles modulate osteoblast-like cell immune responses independently of vesicle internalization
