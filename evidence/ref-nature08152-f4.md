# Taken together, our dermis tracking indicates that dermis cells do not enter the myo- genic or the S

## Claim
Taken together, our dermis tracking indicates that dermis cells do not enter the myo- genic or the Schwann cell lineage but do form cartilage, connective tissue and tendons.

## Biological Context
Cells keep a memory of their tissue origin during axolotl limb regeneration.
