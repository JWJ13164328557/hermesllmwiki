# In this timeframe, inactivation of the necroptotic stimulus can result in dephosphorylation of MLKL,

## Claim
In this timeframe, inactivation of the necroptotic stimulus can result in dephosphorylation of MLKL, complete reversal of phosphatidyl serine exposure on the outer leaflet of the plasma membrane, and, remarkably, a return to robust growth in culture of the resuscitated cell (Gong et al., 2017).

## Biological Context
Leveraging ZBP1 signaling for cancer immunotherapy
