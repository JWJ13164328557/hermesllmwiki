# Conclusion We showed how wildfire and post-fire management affected and rearranged soil microbial gu

## Claim
Conclusion We showed how wildfire and post-fire management affected and rearranged soil microbial guilds, by decreasing the growth of bacteria and ectomycorrhizal fungi while stimulating the activity of saprotrophic fungi, particularly after salvage-logging live trees.

## Biological Context
Wildfire and post-fire management reshape soil microbial guilds and carbon dynamics at a boreal forest site in Sweden
