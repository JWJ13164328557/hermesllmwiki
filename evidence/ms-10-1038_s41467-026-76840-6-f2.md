# Here, we report the identiﬁcation of NLR8-Hap2, a naturally occurring NLR allele that is signiﬁcantl

## Claim
Here, we report the identiﬁcation of NLR8-Hap2, a naturally occurring NLR allele that is signiﬁcantly associated with drought resistance in rice through a genome-wide association study (GWAS).

## Biological Context
An LRR-truncated NLR natural variant confers dual resistance to drought and blast in rice
