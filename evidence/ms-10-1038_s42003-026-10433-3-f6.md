# Cytoplasmic phospholipase cPLA2α (PLA2G4A) is the rate-limiting enzyme in the production of eicosano

## Claim
Cytoplasmic phospholipase cPLA2α (PLA2G4A) is the rate-limiting enzyme in the production of eicosanoid compounds and plays a key role in inflammatory responses and cancer development through the arachidonic acid cascade14.

## Biological Context
cPLA2α inhibition potentiates anti-PD-1 immunotherapy in lung adenocarcinoma via remodeling tumor microenvironment
