# After optimization of each of the key reaction parameters, we found that chlorocyclopropanation of 1

## Claim
After optimization of each of the key reaction parameters, we found that chlorocyclopropanation of 1 proceeded efficiently using 4 equiv CHCl3, 5 mol % Vitamin B12, 5 equiv of Zn as the stoichiometric reductant and 1 equiv NH4Cl in DMA under 525 nm LED irradiation for 8 h, generating cyclopropyl adduct 2 in 64% NMR yield (entry 1).
