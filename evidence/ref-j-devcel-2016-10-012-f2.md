# When comparing sorted cells with unsorted whole roots, we found 4% of all detected genes (1,113 gene

## Claim
When comparing sorted cells with unsorted whole roots, we found 4% of all detected genes (1,113 genes) and 1.5% of all detected isoforms (977 isoforms) changed signiﬁcantly (FDR <0.01, log2 fold change >2), indi- cating only a small effect of cell sorting on both gene expression and alternative splicing.
