# In diabetic foot ulcers, increased phenylpyr- uvate enters macrophages via the CD36 scavenger recept

## Claim
In diabetic foot ulcers, increased phenylpyr- uvate enters macrophages via the CD36 scavenger receptor and binds to palmitoyl-protein thioesterase 1 (PPT1) at residues Lys229 and Gly245, inhibiting its depalmitoylase activity.
