# Importantly, we assessed transcriptional changes within our etrolizumab- and adalimumab-treated UC p

## Claim
Importantly, we assessed transcriptional changes within our etrolizumab- and adalimumab-treated UC patients within the HIBISCUS studies by leveraging UC single-cell datasets to deconvolve the on-treatment changes, especially within the myeloid compartment to assess cellular changes on treatment and relationships to response outcomes.
