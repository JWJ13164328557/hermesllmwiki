# Collectively, these results indicate that ethylene inhibits anthocyanin biosynthesis by a mechanism 

## Claim
Collectively, these results indicate that ethylene inhibits anthocyanin biosynthesis by a mechanism that involves PpERF9-PpTPL1 complex- mediated histone deacetylation of PpMYB114 and PpRAP2.4.

## Biological Context
The ethylene-responsive transcription factor PpERF9 抑制 PpRAP2.4 and PpMYB114 via histone deacetylation to inhibit 花青素 生物合成 in pear
