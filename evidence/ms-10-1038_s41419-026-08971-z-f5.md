# Our data support these observations by showing that female neurons express higher levels of Ddx3x th

## Claim
Our data support these observations by showing that female neurons express higher levels of Ddx3x than male neurons in culture and that DDX3X forms nuclear puncta in neurons from human Alzheimer’s disease brains, with more pronounced accumulation in females.

## Biological Context
Sex-linked helicases DDX3X and DDX3Y regulate G-quadruplex-associated stress in neurons
