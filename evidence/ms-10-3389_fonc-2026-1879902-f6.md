# Conclusion: Knockdown of THBS2 inhibited the malignant phenotype, EMT and stem cell-like characteris

## Claim
Conclusion: Knockdown of THBS2 inhibited the malignant phenotype, EMT and stem cell-like characteristics via inhibiting the PI3K/AKT/c-MYC signaling axis, and blocked the cell cycle.

## Biological Context
Down-regulation of THBS2 inhibits the malignant progression of Burkitt lymphoma by blocking the PI3K/AKT/c-MYC pathway
