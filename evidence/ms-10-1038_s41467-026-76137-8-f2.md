# Notably, we found that BRCA1-deficient cancer cells are highly sensitive to TRDMT1i 16, suggesting t

## Claim
Notably, we found that BRCA1-deficient cancer cells are highly sensitive to TRDMT1i 16, suggesting that homologous recombination deficiency (HRD) is a cause of TRDMT1 dependency in cancer and the consequent TRDMT1i sensitivity.

## Biological Context
Suppression of transcription-replication conflicts by sequence-coordinated actions of TRDMT1 and MutLα
