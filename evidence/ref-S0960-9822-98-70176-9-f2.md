# In conclusion, the maintenance of positional information and cell fate did not depend on …

## Claim
In conclusion, the maintenance of positional information and cell fate did not depend on a single communication pathway between living epidermal cells (within or between files) nor between epidermal and cortical cells, although redundant and combinatorial use of these pathways could not be excluded.
