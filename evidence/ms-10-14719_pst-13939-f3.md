# Further, ABA triggers cytosolic Ca2+ elevations by activating Ca2+- permeable plasma membrane and to

## Claim
Further, ABA triggers cytosolic Ca2+ elevations by activating Ca2+- permeable plasma membrane and tonoplast-located channels, which activate Ca2+-dependent protein kinases (CDPKs) that phosphorylate SLAC1 and regulate guard cell ion fluxes (25, 26).

## Biological Context
Interaction between stomatal, mesophyll and chloroplast regulation for plant photosynthetic acclimation to drought
