# Hormone Pathways Map to Specific Rice Root Cell Types

## Claim

scRNA-seq of rice roots reveals that phytohormone biosynthesis and signaling pathway components are expressed in cell-type-specific patterns, with distinct hormone pathways mapping to particular root cell types and developmental zones.

## Methodological Context

Plant hormones (auxin, cytokinin, gibberellin, ABA, ethylene, brassinosteroids) coordinate root development, but their sites of synthesis and action have traditionally been studied using reporter lines in Arabidopsis. scRNA-seq in rice provides genome-wide resolution of hormone pathway expression across all root cell types in a crop species.
