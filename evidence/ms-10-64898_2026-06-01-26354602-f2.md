# De novo HM450K methylation analysis revealed HAMP as the dominant epigenetic target in the iron netw

## Claim
De novo HM450K methylation analysis revealed HAMP as the dominant epigenetic target in the iron network, exhibiting the strongest hypomethylation signal (Δβ=-0.265, P=1.4×10⁻⁴⁸), while NEO1 and TFRC showed constitutively low baseline methylation (β<0.05).
