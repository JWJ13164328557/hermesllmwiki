# RHD6 action requires RSL4 (root hair defective6-like 4), which is a direct target of RHD6 and regula

## Claim
RHD6 action requires RSL4 (root hair defective6-like 4), which is a direct target of RHD6 and regulates root hair growth by modifying cell wall composition (Feng et al., 2017).

## Biological Context
Shaping the root system architecture in plants for adaptation to drought stress.
