# Moreover, spermidine may have beneficial effects, which can be attributed to its metabolites, since 

## Claim
Moreover, spermidine may have beneficial effects, which can be attributed to its metabolites, since spermidine supplementation significantly enhances plasma spermine levels in healthy adults without affecting the concentration of spermidine per se or putrescine 59.

## Biological Context
Geroprotective insights into the natural metabolite spermidine in aging and age-related diseases
