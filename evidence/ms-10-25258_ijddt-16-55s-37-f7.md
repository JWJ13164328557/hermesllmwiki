# Carvacrol similarly inhibits NF-κB, and has additionally been shown to interact with the TRPV1 recep

## Claim
Carvacrol similarly inhibits NF-κB, and has additionally been shown to interact with the TRPV1 receptor — a cation channel whose activation can amplify NF-κB-dependent neurogenic inflammation — positioning it as a dual inhibitor of both receptor-mediated and oxidative NF-κB activation (Gupta et al., 2020).
