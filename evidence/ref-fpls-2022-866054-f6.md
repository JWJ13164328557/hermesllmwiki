# We found that the genes with a longer 3′ UTR in Arabidopsis were significantly enriched in GO terms 

## Claim
We found that the genes with a longer 3′ UTR in Arabidopsis were significantly enriched in GO terms related to salt stress, including response to salt stress and cation transport; such enrichment was not detected in Eutrema.

## Biological Context
Divergence in the Regulation of the Salt Tolerant Response Between <i>Arabidopsi
