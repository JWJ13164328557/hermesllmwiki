# When ETH is not present, ETH receptors trigger the Raf-like Ser/Thr protein kinase …

## Claim
When ETH is not present, ETH receptors trigger the Raf-like Ser/Thr protein kinase CONSTITUTIVE TRIPLE RESPONSE1 (CTR1) (Guo and Ecker, 2004; Huang et al., 2003), which phosphorylates the C-terminal end of the ER-localised membrane protein EIN2 (EIN2-CEND), thereby blocking it.
