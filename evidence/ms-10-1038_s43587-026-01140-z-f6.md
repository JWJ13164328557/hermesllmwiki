# Together, these findings suggest that successful atresia requires coordinated transition into an apo

## Claim
Together, these findings suggest that successful atresia requires coordinated transition into an apoptotic, immune-active state for follicular clearance, and that aging impairs this process, potentially promoting unresolved atresia and accumulation of follicular debris.

## Biological Context
Aging disrupts spatiotemporal coordination in the cycling murine ovary
