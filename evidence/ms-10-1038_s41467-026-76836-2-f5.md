# TaJAZ1 interacts with TaEIL3 and represses TaEIL3, binding to the TaNPF7.6-A1mod promoter, inﬂuencin

## Claim
TaJAZ1 interacts with TaEIL3 and represses TaEIL3, binding to the TaNPF7.6-A1mod promoter, inﬂuencing allele expression and causing phenotypic differences.

## Biological Context
An elite allele TaNPF7.6-A1mod enhances nitrogen use efficiency and grain yield in wheat
