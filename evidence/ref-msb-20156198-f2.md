# By pooling data from single cells of the three different stem cell populations, we found evidence fo

## Claim
By pooling data from single cells of the three different stem cell populations, we found evidence for the use of longer 30 UTR isoforms in neural stem cells, and, interestingly, in ESC-FCS compared to ESCs maintained in 2i medium (Fig 7A).
