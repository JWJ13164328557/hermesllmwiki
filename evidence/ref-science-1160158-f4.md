# These observations suggest that ACR4 is required to coordinate pericycle cell divisions during later

## Claim
These observations suggest that ACR4 is required to coordinate pericycle cell divisions during lateral root initiation, where it functions mainly to prevent surrounding cells from dividing but also seems to be involved in the initiation event itself.

## Biological Context
Receptor-like kinase ACR4 restricts formative cell divisions in the Arabidopsis
