# EMBRYONIC FLOWER 2 (EMF2), a core component of Polycomb Repressive Complex 2, mediates transcription

## Claim
EMBRYONIC FLOWER 2 (EMF2), a core component of Polycomb Repressive Complex 2, mediates transcriptional repression through H3K27me3 and is essential for root regeneration in Arabidopsis.
