# Collectively, these findings demonstrate that Ehmt2- mediated H3K9me2 deposition acts as a global ep

## Claim
Collectively, these findings demonstrate that Ehmt2- mediated H3K9me2 deposition acts as a global epigenetic constraint on NK cell gene programs, and its loss results in coordinated derepression of functionally relevant transcriptional networks.

## Biological Context
Targeting EHMT2 enhances NK cell maturation and antitumor activity
