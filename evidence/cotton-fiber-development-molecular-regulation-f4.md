# Interestingly, the regulation of fiber initiation versus elongation involves specific interactions; 

## Claim
Interestingly, the regulation of fiber initiation versus elongation involves specific interactions; for instance, MYB16 (GhMML4_D12), a MIXTA-like TF, does not interact with a bHLH but with a divergent WD40 protein TRANSPARENT TESTA GLABRA 1 (GhWDR) to influence fiber development, highlight- ing a variation from the canonical Arabidopsis trichome R2R3MYB-bHLH-WDR (MBW) complex (Tian et al.
