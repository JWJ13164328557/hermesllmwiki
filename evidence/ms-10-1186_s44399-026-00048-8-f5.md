# Notably, it remains unclear whether aphid performance is driven primarily by the total amino acid co

## Claim
Notably, it remains unclear whether aphid performance is driven primarily by the total amino acid concentration in phloem sap or by the ratio of essen­ tial to non-essential amino acids, which is a distinc­ tion with practical implications for breeding crops with reduced aphid suitability.
