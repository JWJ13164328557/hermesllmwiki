# While white grapes and wines tend to display more flowery and white-fruit notes …

## Claim
While white grapes and wines tend to display more flowery and white-fruit notes (Petronilho et al., 2020), additional systematic analyses are required to determine whether their characteristic flavour properties are related to the absence of anthocyanins or are just due to independent varietal features selected for the production of different types of wine.
