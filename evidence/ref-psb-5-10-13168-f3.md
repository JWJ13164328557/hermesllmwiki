# Arabidopsis AtERF4 and AtERF7 bind to the promoters of the target genes and recruits AtSin3, a co-re

## Claim
Arabidopsis AtERF4 and AtERF7 bind to the promoters of the target genes and recruits AtSin3, a co-repressor, which in turn may interact with histone deacetylases, HDA6 and HDA19.

## Biological Context
Role of histone deacetylases HDA6 and HDA19 in ABA and abiotic stress response.
