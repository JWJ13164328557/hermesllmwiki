# Among the putative direct targets of SlMYB11, we noticed the MYB transcription factor gene SlMYB111,

## Claim
Among the putative direct targets of SlMYB11, we noticed the MYB transcription factor gene SlMYB111, a homolog of SlMYB12 (Fernandez‐Moreno et al., 2016); SlMYB111 was bound by SlBEL11 at its “TYTTTTTCTTTYT” motif (Figure 7D) and was downregulated in SlBEL11‐RNAi lines based on the RNA‐seq data (Table S1).

## Biological Context
SlBEL11 调控 类黄酮 生物合成, thus fine‐tuning auxin efflux to prevent premature fruit drop in tomato
