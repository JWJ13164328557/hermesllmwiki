# Among the putative direct targets of SlMYB11, we noticed the MYB transcription factor …

## Claim
Among the putative direct targets of SlMYB11, we noticed the MYB transcription factor gene SlMYB111, a homolog of SlMYB12 (FernandezMoreno et al., 2016); SlMYB111 was bound by SlBEL11 at its “TYTTTTTCTTTYT” motif (Figure 7D) and was downregulated in SlBEL11RNAi lines based on the RNAseq data (Table S1).
