# Our data suggest that brassinosteroid signaling affects neither cell proliferation nor cellular grow

## Claim
Our data suggest that brassinosteroid signaling affects neither cell proliferation nor cellular growth ca- pacity per se, but rather enforces accurate cell division plane orientation and cellular anisotropy (i.e., an increased ratio be- tween longitudinal and radial cell expansion).
