# XANTHOMONAS RESISTANCE 21-binding protein 3 (XB3), which includes both ankyrin (ANK) repeats and a R

## Claim
XANTHOMONAS RESISTANCE 21-binding protein 3 (XB3), which includes both ankyrin (ANK) repeats and a RING finger/U-box domain, has been reported to be a RING-type (truly Interesting New Gene) E3 ligase that plays a role in the ubiquitination-mediated degradation of target proteins.

## Biological Context
Genome-wide characterization of XBMD3 genes in apple and the role of XBMD32 in response to drought stress
