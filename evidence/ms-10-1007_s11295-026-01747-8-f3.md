# Since PB1 domains of classes B and C PB1 are thought to have a reduced capacity, to interact with Au

## Claim
Since PB1 domains of classes B and C PB1 are thought to have a reduced capacity, to interact with Aux/IAAs (Guilfoyle & Hagen, 2012), they are generally regarded as auxin-independent or non-canonical with some documented exceptions.

## Biological Context
Eucalyptus grandis Auxin Response Factor 10 (EgrARF10) is associated with the modulation of secondary xylem cell wall chemistry
