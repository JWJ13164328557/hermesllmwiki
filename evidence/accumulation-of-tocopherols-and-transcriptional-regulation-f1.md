# Collectively, results suggest that the concentration of tocopherols at harvest may play a function i

## Claim
Collectively, results suggest that the concentration of tocopherols at harvest may play a function in the natural tolerance of mandarin fruit to CI, and that changes in the expression of genes during storage appear to be cold-regulated responses, rather than involved in CI tolerance.

## Biological Context
