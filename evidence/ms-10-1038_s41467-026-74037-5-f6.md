# In the Morris water maze (MWM) test, Cx3cr1CreCD31fl/+5xFAD mice exhibited decreased latency in loca

## Claim
In the Morris water maze (MWM) test, Cx3cr1CreCD31fl/+5xFAD mice exhibited decreased latency in locating the hidden platform during the training phase (Fig.1O), and they demonstrated reduced latency (Fig.1P), increased platform crossings during the probe trial (Fig.1Q) and extended time spent in the target quadrant (Fig.1R), suggesting enhanced spatial learning and memory abilities.

## Biological Context
Microglial CD31 suppresses Aβ clearance and promotes Alzheimer pathology in 5×FAD mice
