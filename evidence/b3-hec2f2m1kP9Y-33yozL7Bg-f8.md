# CUPSHAPED COTYLEDON 2 (CUC2), a shootpromoting transcription factor, activates the …

## Claim
CUPSHAPED COTYLEDON 2 (CUC2), a shootpromoting transcription factor, activates the expression of the cell wallloosening enzymeencoding gene XYLOGLUCAN ENDOTRANSGLUCOSYLASE/HYDROLASE 9 (XTH9) in nonprogenitor cells, which is critical for shoot regeneration (Varapparambath et al., 2022).
