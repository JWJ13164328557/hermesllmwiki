# Both arms of the cellular immune response play complementary roles: CD8+ cytotoxic T cells directly 

## Claim
Both arms of the cellular immune response play complementary roles: CD8+ cytotoxic T cells directly kill target cells, while CD4+ T helper cells provide essential support for sustaining and amplifying immune responses, including CD8+ T cell priming and memory formation.

## Biological Context
Soluble multi−epitope protein vaccination leverages antigen availability to drive CD8+ T cell immunity and tumor control
