# The homologues of these candidate transcription factors have been already reported to be correlated 

## Claim
The homologues of these candidate transcription factors have been already reported to be correlated with anthocyanin biosynthesis and regulation; in particular, WRKY44 was found to be a target gene of the MWB complex regulating the deposition of anthocyanins.32,33 However, their functional roles in potato require further investigation.
