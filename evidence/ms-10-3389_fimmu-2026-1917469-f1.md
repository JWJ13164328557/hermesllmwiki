# Homeostatic licensing, by contrast, describes a permissive signal that does not necessarily drive pr

## Claim
Homeostatic licensing, by contrast, describes a permissive signal that does not necessarily drive proliferation or differentiation but is required to maintain the target cell in a quiescent, baseline state; its withdrawal triggers latent activation programs.

## Biological Context
From support to homeostatic licensing: the bidirectional fibroblast–macrophage circuit and its central role in tissue homeostasis and fibrosis
