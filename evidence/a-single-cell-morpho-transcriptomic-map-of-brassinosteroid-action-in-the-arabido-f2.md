# Moreover, we found that the cell-aligning effects of brassinosteroid signaling can propagate to norm

## Claim
Moreover, we found that the cell-aligning effects of brassinosteroid signaling can propagate to normalize the anatomy of both adjacent and distant brassinosteroid-blind cells through non-cell-autonomous functions, which are sufﬁcient to restore growth vigor.

## Biological Context
A single-cell morpho-transcriptomic map of brassinosteroid action in the Arabidopsis root
