# CRK, a protein kinase closely related to CDPK, also senses intracellular Ca2+ changes and participat

## Claim
CRK, a protein kinase closely related to CDPK, also senses intracellular Ca2+ changes and participates in calcium signal transduction through protein phosphorylation.69 Calcium signaling can play a key role in initiating SA biosynthesis by regulating ICS1 and EDS1 transcription, linking it to plant disease resistance.70 In this study, Nano-Se (10 mg L−1 and 25 mg L−1) signiﬁcantly upregulated CDPK2, CDPK1, and CRK expression compared to healthy control.

## Biological Context
Nano selenium enhanced tomato resistance to leaf mold via activating salicylic acid defense pathways and regulating metabolic profiles as well as improved plant growth
