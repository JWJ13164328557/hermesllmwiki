# More in-depth studies found that WUS directly regulates additional cytokinin- and auxin-related gene

## Claim
More in-depth studies found that WUS directly regulates additional cytokinin- and auxin-related genes, possibly con- trolling the balance between these two hormones, and also regulates a class of transcriptional corepressors involved in auxin signaling (15).
