# We have recently reported that the concentration of total carotenoids in the flavedo of …

## Claim
We have recently reported that the concentration of total carotenoids in the flavedo of ‘Nadorcott’ mandarin is substantially higher than that of ‘Fortune’ and ‘Nova’ (Rey et al., 2020), and also of other traditional mandarin (Lado et al., 2019b), suggesting an enhanced synthesis and accumulation of isoprenoids-derived compounds in fruit of this mandarin cultivar.
