# The ABA synthesised in the plant root and shoot system under drought is transported via xylem to gua

## Claim
The ABA synthesised in the plant root and shoot system under drought is transported via xylem to guard cells, where it binds to a family of ABA receptors, i.e., pyrabactin resistance/PYR-like/regulatory component of ABA receptor (PYR/PYL/RCAR) (22).

## Biological Context
Interaction between stomatal, mesophyll and chloroplast regulation for plant photosynthetic acclimation to drought
