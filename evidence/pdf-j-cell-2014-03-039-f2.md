# Taken together, these results suggest that REV-ERBa controls TH gene expression by recruiting HDAC3 

## Claim
Taken together, these results suggest that REV-ERBa controls TH gene expression by recruiting HDAC3 complexes, which negatively regulate permissive histone acetylation to sup- press transcriptional activation of the TH gene promoter.
