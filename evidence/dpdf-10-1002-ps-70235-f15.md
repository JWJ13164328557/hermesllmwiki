# In summary, Nano-Se enhanced tomato disease resistance by upregulating SA signaling pathway, antioxi

## Claim
In summary, Nano-Se enhanced tomato disease resistance by upregulating SA signaling pathway, antioxidant enzymes, defense-related genes and photosynthesis, with 10 mg L−1 Nano-Se showing the most signiﬁcant effects.

## Biological Context
Nano selenium enhanced tomato resistance to leaf mold via activating salicylic acid defense pathways and regulating metabolic profiles as well as improved plant growth
