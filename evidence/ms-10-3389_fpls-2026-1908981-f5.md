# In summary, HA- mediated increases in maize seedling biomass were associated with coordinated change

## Claim
In summary, HA- mediated increases in maize seedling biomass were associated with coordinated changes in root functional morphology, whole-plant nutrient status, and leaf photosynthetic capacity, and according to variance partitioning analysis, root functional traits made the largest independent contribution to the observed biomass variation.

## Biological Context
Root functional traits dominate maize seedling growth responses to different humic acids
