# To ensure biological relevance and specificity of the screening, the following inclusion and exclusi

## Claim
To ensure biological relevance and specificity of the screening, the following inclusion and exclusion criteria were applied: Only compounds with a clearly documented unidirectional regulatory effect on the target gene in the CTD, and where this effect direction was consistent with the gene’s dysregulation pattern observed in PAH, were retained.

## Biological Context
Integrating multi-omics and reverse network toxicology to identify pollutant risks and potential intervention targets in pulmonary arterial hypertension
