# Notably, evidence of reduced morbidity has been reported primarily in studies involving copper or co

## Claim
Notably, evidence of reduced morbidity has been reported primarily in studies involving copper or copper-alloy high-touch surfaces, whereas studies on copper-impregnated textiles and coatings show more heterogeneous and less conclusive results (Marcus et al., 2017; Marik et al., 2020; Albarqouni et al., 2020).
