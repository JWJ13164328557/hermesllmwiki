# Importantly, the recent report of a clinical apparent and molecularly conﬁrmed human case of RNV inf

## Claim
Importantly, the recent report of a clinical apparent and molecularly conﬁrmed human case of RNV infection in Bolivia (Mafayle et al., 2023) challenges the long-standing perception of this virus as clinically insigniﬁcant and underscores the need for experimental evaluation of its pathogenic capacity.

## Biological Context
Río Negro virus (Venezuelan equine encephalitis virus complex) shows pathogenic potential in a permissive murine model
