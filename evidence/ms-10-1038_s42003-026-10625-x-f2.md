## Claim
Collectively, our study not only provides the first T2T genome of a hibernating mammal but also uncovers an arachidonic acid–mediated lipid metabolic regulatory mechanism, offering evolutionary and biomedical insights into metabolic plasticity during hibernation.

## Biological Context
A telomere-to-telomere genome reveals arachidonic acid as a key regulator of hepatic lipid metabolism during hibernation
