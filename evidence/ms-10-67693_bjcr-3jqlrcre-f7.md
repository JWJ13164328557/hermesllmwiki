# Elaeocarpus grandiflorus callus cultures exhibited differential growth responses on MS medium supple

## Claim
Elaeocarpus grandiflorus callus cultures exhibited differential growth responses on MS medium supplemented with varying concentrations of plant growth regulators.The study demonstrated that optimization of auxin and cytokinin levels is essential for enhancing callus proliferation, providing valuable insights for In Vitro propagation and conservation of Elaeocarpus species (Habibah et al., 2019).
