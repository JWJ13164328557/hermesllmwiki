# Collectively, our findings uncover the previously unrecognized oncogenic role of TPI1 in HNSCC progr

## Claim
Collectively, our findings uncover the previously unrecognized oncogenic role of TPI1 in HNSCC progression and establish a novel functional link between TPI1 and ferroptosis regulation, providing a promising prognostic biomarker and potential therapeutic target for HNSCC.

## Biological Context
TPI1 promotes malignant progression and ferroptosis resistance in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma
