# However, accumulating evidence suggests that early exposure to, or preconditioning with, EE before t

## Claim
However, accumulating evidence suggests that early exposure to, or preconditioning with, EE before the onset of adverse challenges—such as exposure to toxins, anxiety, depression, and breast cancer— plays a pivotal role in regulating key pathophysiological mechanisms, including inflammation, apoptosis, autophagy, and oxidative stress.

## Biological Context
Effect of six weeks of environmental enrichment on oxidative metabolism and hypothalamic mitochondrial markers of adult male mice
