# Through KEGG, GO, and GSEA analyses, we found that overexpression of POU2F2 is associated with the J

## Claim
Through KEGG, GO, and GSEA analyses, we found that overexpression of POU2F2 is associated with the JAK/STAT pathway.

## Biological Context
POU2F2 promotes the progression of clear cell renal cell carcinoma by regulating the JAK/STAT signaling pathway through SPI1
