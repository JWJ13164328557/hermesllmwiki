# The most common infectious pathogens that trigger GBS include Campylobacter jejuni, Mycoplasma pneum

## Claim
The most common infectious pathogens that trigger GBS include Campylobacter jejuni, Mycoplasma pneumoniae and cyto- megalovirus (10, 66, 67); surprisingly, these pathogens were not detected in the stool samples from GBS subjects in this study.
