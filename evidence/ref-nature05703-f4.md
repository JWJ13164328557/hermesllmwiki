# Collectively, our data indicate that WOX5 expression depends on the induction in the root pole by MP

## Claim
Collectively, our data indicate that WOX5 expression depends on the induction in the root pole by MP-mediated auxin signalling and on SHR/SCR activity, whereas PLT1 and PLT2 have only a minor role in confining WOX5 expression to the quiescent centre.
