# Notably, while yield predictions based on traits from earlier stages provided only coarse estimates,

## Claim
Notably, while yield predictions based on traits from earlier stages provided only coarse estimates, predictions at the maturity stage achieved the highest accuracy, highlighting that phenotypic traits and environmental conditions during this period exert the greatest influence on final yield.
