# Generally, the initial step in PHY signal transduction involves PHYA perceiving FR light …

## Claim
Generally, the initial step in PHY signal transduction involves PHYA perceiving FR light or PHYB-PHYE perceiving red light, leading to conforma tional changes between Pr and Pfr, upon photoactivation, phytochromes translocate from the cytoplasm to the nucleus [23], where they interact with PIFs, triggering the phosphorylation and subsequent 26 S proteasome-medi ated degradation of PIFs.
