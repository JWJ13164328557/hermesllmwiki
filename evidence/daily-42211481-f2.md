# Collectively, the enriched GO terms induced by idr1–1 mutation appear to be concentrated …

## Claim
Collectively, the enriched GO terms induced by idr1–1 mutation appear to be concentrated on alterations of photosynthetic systems, responses to water deficiency, heat as well as phytohormones (ABA, JA and GA), alterations of antioxidation process, regulation of stomatal actions, which are somewhat different from those found in the wild-type IAPAR9 seedlings under the same treatment conditions.
