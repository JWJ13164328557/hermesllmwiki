# The tran- scription factor RcMYC2 interacts with Mediator subunit RcMED25 and recruits histone deace

## Claim
The tran- scription factor RcMYC2 interacts with Mediator subunit RcMED25 and recruits histone deacetylase RcHDA8 to repress expansin gene RcEXPA8 transcription via histone deacetylation, thereby restricting petal cell expansion and reducing flower diameter.

## Biological Context
Histone deacetylation links jasmonic acid signaling to flower size control in rose
