# OsbHLH002/OsICE1 directly activates the transcription of OsTPP1 which encodes a key enzyme for treha

## Claim
OsbHLH002/OsICE1 directly activates the transcription of OsTPP1 which encodes a key enzyme for trehalose biosynthesis, thereby increasing the levels of trehalose to enhance the cold resistance of rice (Zhang et al., 2017).

## Biological Context
Post-translational modifications play indispensable roles in cold stress responses of plants
