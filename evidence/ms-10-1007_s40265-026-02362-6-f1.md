# This article highlights the dissonance between drug development for ‘at-risk’ MASH that targets both

## Claim
This article highlights the dissonance between drug development for ‘at-risk’ MASH that targets both active disease and fibrosis relying on strict histological cri- teria, and current NITs that have been proposed for patient selection for pharmacotherapy and monitoring in clinical practice but largely reflect fibrosis.
