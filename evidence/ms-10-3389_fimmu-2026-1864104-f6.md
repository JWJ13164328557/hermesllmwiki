# Collectively, TGF-b plays three distinct roles in cancer: (1) at very low levels it promotes Th17 di

## Claim
Collectively, TGF-b plays three distinct roles in cancer: (1) at very low levels it promotes Th17 differentiation and a pro-inﬂam- matory environment that drives cancer onset; (2) at higher levels it suppresses early tumor development through its cytostatic effects; and (3) once tumor cells acquire resistance to TGF-b–mediated growth inhibition, persistently high TGF-b promotes tumor pro- gression
