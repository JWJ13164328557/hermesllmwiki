# In the current study, we found that four pairs of paralogous genes developed through segmental dupli

## Claim
In the current study, we found that four pairs of paralogous genes developed through segmental duplication, whereas no evidence of tandem duplication was detected for any gene pair, indi- cating that segmental duplication rather than tandem duplication has played a prominent role in the expansion of the tomato ZHD gene family.
