# We found that consecutive rotational divisions emerge across a broader parameter space of the initia

## Claim
We found that consecutive rotational divisions emerge across a broader parameter space of the initial geometry (e.g., the polar angle ≥40° and the cell height > 0.2 in Figures 1B, C; Supplementary Video S1), while the periclinal divisions occur in slender initial AC geometries (e.g., the polar angle < 40° in Figure 1C).

## Biological Context
3D geometry and mechanics of a single apical stem cell ensure helically symmetric plant body in multicellular models
