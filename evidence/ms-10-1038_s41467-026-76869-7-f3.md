# To compare the performance of different enzymes, we found it helpful to visualise the collected data

## Claim
To compare the performance of different enzymes, we found it helpful to visualise the collected data along two aggregated dimen- sions: the maximum activity found across all tested substrate combi- nations (activity score), and the number of positive substrate combinations (generality score).

## Biological Context
Data-driven construction of an imine reductase library capable of broad-scope reductive amination at equimolar substrate concentrations
