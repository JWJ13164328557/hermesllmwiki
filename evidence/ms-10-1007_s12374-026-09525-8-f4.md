# Conventional CRISPR-based editing still depends on stable transformation and tissue-culture regenera

## Claim
Conventional CRISPR-based editing still depends on stable transformation and tissue-culture regeneration, which remain inefficient or unavailable in most wild spe­ cies, and can still produce mosaic or off-target edits.
