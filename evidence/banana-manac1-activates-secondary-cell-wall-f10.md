## Claim
Since the promoters of MaCESA6A, MaCESA6B, MaCESA6F, MaCESA7 and MaCESA8 contained one or more SNBE sites (Figure 5a), we then puriﬁed the recombinant protein of GST- MaNAC1 (Figure S6) and used EMSA to conﬁrm whether MaNAC1 speciﬁcally binds to SNBEs within the promoters MaCESA6B and MaCESA7 (Figure 5b).
