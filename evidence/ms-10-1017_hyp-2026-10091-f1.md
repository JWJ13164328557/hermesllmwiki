# While altered abilities to engage with, and move towards, possibilities are important aspects of som

## Claim
While altered abilities to engage with, and move towards, possibilities are important aspects of some disenfranchised grief, we argue that this characterisation glosses over important distinctions that are key to characterising the phenomenology and intentionality of disenfranchised grief.
