# Notably, truncation of 15 amino acids resulted in complete loss of detectable α-terpineol production

## Claim
Notably, truncation of 15 amino acids resulted in complete loss of detectable α-terpineol production, whereas truncation of 13 amino acids yielded the highest activity.

## Biological Context
Synergistic engineering of monoterpenoid synthase and metabolic pathways to enhance α-terpineol production in Saccharomyces cerevisiae
