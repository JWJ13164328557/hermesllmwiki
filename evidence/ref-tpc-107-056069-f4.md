# We found that sieve plates in 2-d-old stems (4 to 5 cm long) were signif- icantly (P ¼ 0.0001) small

## Claim
We found that sieve plates in 2-d-old stems (4 to 5 cm long) were signif- icantly (P ¼ 0.0001) smaller than sieve plates in stems 1 week after bolting: On average, the ﬁrst sieve plates had a surface of 20 mm2 (n ¼ 10) (Figure 4B), while sieve plates at later stages (n ¼ 11, stems 10 to 15 cm long) had an average surface of 31 mm2 (Figure 4F; see Supplemental Figure 3 online).
