# Solvent polarity also plays a critical role in polyphenol extraction eﬃciency; methanolic extracts o

## Claim
Solvent polarity also plays a critical role in polyphenol extraction eﬃciency; methanolic extracts of Nigella sativa exhibited superior bioactivity compared to ethanolic extracts, owing to methanol’s high polarity and hydrogen-bonding capacity, which enhance the solubilization of plant phenolics (Karkar et al., 2023).
