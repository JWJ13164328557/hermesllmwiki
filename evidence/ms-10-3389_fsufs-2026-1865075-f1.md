# This study provides two key implications for sustainable intercropping design: From a carbon-budget 

## Claim
This study provides two key implications for sustainable intercropping design: From a carbon-budget perspective, maize-legume intercropping systems, particularly maize/pea, achieve higher carbon assimilation per unit area with lower GHG intensity, making them more carbon-eﬃcient in semi-arid regions.

## Biological Context
Resource utilization and maize competitiveness across maize-based intercropping treatment in semi-arid Northwestern China
