# Using genetic loss-of-function experiments and pharmacological inhibition of FAO both in vitro and i

## Claim
Using genetic loss-of-function experiments and pharmacological inhibition of FAO both in vitro and in vivo, we show that blocking this metabolic pathway disrupts EpSC differentiation, thereby impairing epidermal renewal and compromising stratiﬁcation.

## Biological Context
Fatty acid oxidation fuels mitochondrial respiration to drive epidermal stem cell fate and differentiation
