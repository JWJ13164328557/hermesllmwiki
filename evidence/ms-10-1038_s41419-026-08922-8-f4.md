# These results confirm that OAS1 is up-regulated in malignant pancreatic cells and serves as an indep

These results confirm that OAS1 is up-regulated in malignant pancreatic cells and serves as an independent marker for predicting poor prognosis in PDAC patients.

## Biological Context
The OAS1-FASN axis promotes pancreatic cancer by coordinating lipogenic stress and the unfolded protein response
