# Upon oxidative stress or DNA damage, p53 undergoes phosphorylation and activation, leading to upregu

## Claim
Upon oxidative stress or DNA damage, p53 undergoes phosphorylation and activation, leading to upregulation of its downstream target p21WAF1 (CDKN1A), which inhibits cyclin- dependent kinase (CDK) activity and induces G1/S phase arrest (Yan et al., 2024).

## Biological Context
Sodium butyrate attenuates D-galactose-induced cellular senescence in WI-38 fibroblasts in vitro via modulation of HDAC activity, Nrf2/ARE pathway, and p53/p21/p16 signaling
