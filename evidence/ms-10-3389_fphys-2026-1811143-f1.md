# Upon oxidative stress or DNA damage, p53 undergoes phosphorylation and activation …

## Claim
Upon oxidative stress or DNA damage, p53 undergoes phosphorylation and activation, leading to upregulation of its downstream target p21WAF1 (CDKN1A), which inhibits cyclindependent kinase (CDK) activity and induces G1/S phase arrest (Yan et al., 2024).
