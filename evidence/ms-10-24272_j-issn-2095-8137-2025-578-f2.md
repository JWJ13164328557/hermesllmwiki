# Interestingly, detection of the expression pattern of the TGF-β pathway members revealed that in add

## Claim
Interestingly, detection of the expression pattern of the TGF-β pathway members revealed that in addition to SMAD3, a validated positive target of miR- 184, the transcript level of TGF-β1 was notably decreased following miR-184 knockdown (Figure 4C).

## Biological Context
A novel positive feedback regulatory loop, TGF-β1/SMAD4/miR-184, controls the follicular development in sows.
