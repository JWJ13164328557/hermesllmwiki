# The most widely used methods for assessing histone modifications in recent years include Chromatin I

## Claim
The most widely used methods for assessing histone modifications in recent years include Chromatin Immunoprecipitation followed by sequencing (ChIP-seq) (Kumar et al., 2025), Cleavage Under Targets and Tagmentation (CUT&Tag)/ Cleavage Under Targets and Release Using Nuclease (CUT&RUN) (Hinte and von Meyenn, 2025), and Mass Spectrometry (Verhelst et al., 2022).

## Biological Context
Live monitoring uncovers divergent epigenetic remodeling during osteogenic and adipogenic differentiation of mesenchymal stem cells
