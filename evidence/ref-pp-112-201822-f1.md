# Collectively, these results suggest that sep- arate acyltransferases are required for the synthesis 

## Claim
Collectively, these results suggest that sep- arate acyltransferases are required for the synthesis of each respective class of alkyl hydroxycinnamate (cou- marate versus caffeate versus ferulate) and that a bi- osynthetic overlap exists between suberin and root wax biosynthesis.

## Biological Context
Identification of an Arabidopsis fatty alcohol:caffeoyl-Coenzyme A acyltransfera
