## Claim
Based on the transcriptome data in the previous studies [32,33], we found that 125 bHLH genes were expressed in developing and ripening fruits of pear cultivars, while 73 bHLH genes were undetectable (RPKM < 0.2; Table S1).

## Biological Context
The transcription factor PbbHLH164 is destabilized by PbRAD23C/D.1 and mediates ethylene 生物合成 过程中 pear fruit ripening
