# Compared with non-targeted nanoparticles, this active targeting approach markedly diminishes non-spe

## Claim
Compared with non-targeted nanoparticles, this active targeting approach markedly diminishes non-specific sequestration by the reticuloendothelial system (e.g., liver and spleen), reduces systemic toxicity, and significantly enhances both the local effective drug concentration and the therapeutic index.

## Biological Context
Advances in the application of nanoparticles for the diagnosis and treatment of diabetic cardiomyopathy
