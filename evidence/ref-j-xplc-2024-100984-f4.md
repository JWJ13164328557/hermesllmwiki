# Specifically, we report that different transcriptomic programs are specifically activated …

## Claim
Specifically, we report that different transcriptomic programs are specifically activated in three different sub-populations of rhizobial-infected soybean cells: those not actively fixing atmospheric dinitrogen, those fixing and assimilating atmospheric nitrogen, and those already engaged in senescence.
