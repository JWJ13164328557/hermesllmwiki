# Interestingly, addition of romidepsin within the first day of WIND1 activation was sufficient to nea

## Claim
Interestingly, addition of romidepsin within the first day of WIND1 activation was sufficient to nearly abolish somatic embryogenesis (Supplemental Figures 2C and 2E), indicating that HDAC activity is essential during the early phase of WIND1 induction.

WIND1 controls cell fate transition through coordinately integrating histone ace
