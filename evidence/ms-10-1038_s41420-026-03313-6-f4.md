# We found that inhibition of GCS by eliglustat, an FDA-approved GCS inhibitor, or other pharmacologic

## Claim
We found that inhibition of GCS by eliglustat, an FDA-approved GCS inhibitor, or other pharmacological inhibitors (Genz123346 and miglustat), or genetic silencing of GCS induced cell death in several cancer cell lines which was not rescued by prevention of ceramide generation, suggesting that the cell death may be independent of ceramide accumulation.

## Biological Context
Targeting glucosylceramide generation induces cancer cell death through lysosomal dysfunction independent of ceramide accumulation
