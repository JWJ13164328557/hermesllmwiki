# Results and discussion: We found that plant NSC content was consistently higher in aboveground parts

## Claim
Results and discussion: We found that plant NSC content was consistently higher in aboveground parts than in belowground parts across growth stages, indicating an aboveground−prioritized carbon strategy.

## Biological Context
Stage-dependent shifts in non-structural carbohydrates and C, N stoichiometry in Setaria viridis along a salinity gradient in the Xiaokai River irrigation area of the Yellow River Delta, China
