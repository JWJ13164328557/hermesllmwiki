# Plants sense various innate and exogenous molecules through pattern recognition recep- tors (PRRs), 

## Claim
Plants sense various innate and exogenous molecules through pattern recognition recep- tors (PRRs), and signals arise when microbial molecules interact with the extracellular ligand-binding domains of PRRs (Zipfel 2008).

## Biological Context
Microbial partnerships and molecular mechanisms in plant stress physiology for climate-resilient and sustainable farming
