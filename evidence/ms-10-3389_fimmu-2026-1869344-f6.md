## Claim
From data generated in mice, it is known that intact anti-CD28 mAb is required to increase the proportion of Treg amongst CD4+ T cells and that mere inhibition of CD28 - ligand interaction with a Fab fragment of a ligand binding-blocking anti-CD28 mAb is not sufﬁcient (30).

## Biological Context
Treatment with saturating dose of conventional anti-CD28 monoclonal antibody well tolerated in pig acute myocardial infarction model
