# Because mutations in MED18 and MED20 result in downregulation of jasmonate-associated genes and upre

## Claim
Because mutations in MED18 and MED20 result in downregulation of jasmonate-associated genes and upregulation of salicylic acid–associated pathogenesis- related genes [30], these findings suggest that AtCAN1 is regulated predominantly via the salicylic acid signaling pathway.
