# In summary, early after SCI, microglial polarization toward the M1 phenotype creates a temporal wind

## Claim
In summary, early after SCI, microglial polarization toward the M1 phenotype creates a temporal window that inhibits angiogenic sprouting through the release of anti-angiogenic factors, exacerba- tion of oxidative stress, and maintenance of inﬂammation.
