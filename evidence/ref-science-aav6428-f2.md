# Pr1 markers (Rmf and C3h3) were expressed at low levels during premeiotic interphase and early lepto

## Claim
Pr1 markers (Rmf and C3h3) were expressed at low levels during premeiotic interphase and early leptotene, up-regulated in 7 of 17 florets (41%) in the “leptotene with a central nucleolus” stage, and strongly expressed at all later cytological stages; we conclude that the Pr1 transition occurred within the leptotene with a central nucleolus stage.

## Biological Context
Defining the developmental program leading to meiosis in maize.
