# Transcriptional regulation is principally con- trolled by transcription factors (TFs) that bind to D

## Claim
Transcriptional regulation is principally con- trolled by transcription factors (TFs) that bind to DNA and effect chromatin remodeling (Zaret, 2020) or directly modulate the output of RNA polymerases (Kadonaga, 2004).
