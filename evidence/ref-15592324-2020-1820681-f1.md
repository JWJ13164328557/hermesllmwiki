# Combination of 0.5 µM TSA and 200 µM SB had no apparent effect on wheat regeneration efficiency Acco

## Claim
Combination of 0.5 µM TSA and 200 µM SB had no apparent effect on wheat regeneration efficiency According to the above experiment data, we found that 0.5 µM TSA increased the embryonic calli frequency and plantlet regeneration frequency; Addition of 200 µM SB increased the regeneration efficiency on CB037, Fielder, and CS to some extent.

## Biological Context
Trichostatin A and sodium butyrate promotes plant regeneration in common wheat.
