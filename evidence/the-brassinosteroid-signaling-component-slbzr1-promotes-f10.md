# When BRs are present at high levels, BZR1 and BES1 are dephosphorylated by PP2A and are transported 

## Claim
When BRs are present at high levels, BZR1 and BES1 are dephosphorylated by PP2A and are transported into the nucleus; they then bind to the promoters of their target genes, resulting in transcrip- tional activation or repression (Mao and Li, 2020).
