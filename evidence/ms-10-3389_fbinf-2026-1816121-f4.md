# Importantly, the inferred components are sensitive to modeling assumptions, including the predefined

## Claim
Importantly, the inferred components are sensitive to modeling assumptions, including the predefined number of cell types, the degree of transcriptional separability among cellular populations, and the transformation of microarray intensities into pseudo- counts.

## Biological Context
Transcriptome-informed metabolic modeling reveals astrocyte-specific vulnerabilities in mild cognitive impairment and Alzheimer’s disease progression
