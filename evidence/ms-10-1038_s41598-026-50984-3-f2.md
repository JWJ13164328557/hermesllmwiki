# The application of arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) as a biofertilizer significantly enhances plan

## Claim
The application of arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) as a biofertilizer significantly enhances plant physiological performance under both optimal and stress conditions.

## Biological Context
Carbon quantum dots and mycorrhizal fungi improve cadmium stress tolerance and essential oil production in grapefruit mint (Mentha suaveolens × piperita)
