# We found that LZY1, LZY2, and LZY3 exhibited different expression patterns that partially overlapped

## Claim
We found that LZY1, LZY2, and LZY3 exhibited different expression patterns that partially overlapped and that all three genes were active mainly in the endodermis of both inﬂorescence stems and hypocotyls.

## Biological Context
The Arabidopsis LAZY1 Family Plays a Key Role in Gravity Signaling within Statoc
