# In this study, we show that inhibition of the ATR-CHK1- WEE1 pathway in S-phase-arrested Ewing sarco

## Claim
In this study, we show that inhibition of the ATR-CHK1- WEE1 pathway in S-phase-arrested Ewing sarcoma cells triggers rapid apoptosis within 2–4 h, without widespread mitotic entry.

## Biological Context
DNA replication stress and translational repression converge to drive CDK1- and caspase-dependent apoptosis in Ewing sarcoma
