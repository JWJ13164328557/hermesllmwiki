# Importantly, we found that LINC00152 expression correlated with shorter time-to-treatment, in contra

## Claim
Importantly, we found that LINC00152 expression correlated with shorter time-to-treatment, in contrast to previous reports Page 8/20 suggesting no clinical impact.27 Our analysis of a large CLL cohort confirmed its poor prognostic value independent of Binet stage and other clinical or biological factors, underscoring its functional relevance.

## Biological Context
LINC00152 is a lncRNA modulated by microenvironmental stimuli that has pathogenetic and clinical relevance in CLL
