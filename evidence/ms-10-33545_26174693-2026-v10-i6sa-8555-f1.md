# Nanoparticles interact with cellular organelles (mitochondria, chloroplasts, and peroxisomes) to inf

## Claim
Nanoparticles interact with cellular organelles (mitochondria, chloroplasts, and peroxisomes) to influence the production of reactive oxygen species (ROS), including O₂⁻, H₂O₂, •OH, NO, and ONOO⁻.

## Biological Context
Nano priming effects on seed vigour and disease resistance in cereal crops
