# For ISS staging, the proportion of stage III patients was signiﬁcantly higher in the UAP1 high-expre

## Claim
For ISS staging, the proportion of stage III patients was signiﬁcantly higher in the UAP1 high-expression group, and these results suggest that high UAP1 expression was associated with a poorer prognosis.

## Biological Context
UAP1 as a prognostic biomarker regulating malignant biological functions in multiple myeloma cells
