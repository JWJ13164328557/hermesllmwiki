# The most clinically mature example is serial monitoring of plasma or serum 2-HG in IDH-mutant AML tr

## Claim
The most clinically mature example is serial monitoring of plasma or serum 2-HG in IDH-mutant AML treated with ivosidenib or enasidenib: a sustained ≥ 90% decline by cycle 1, day 15 confirms on-target enzymatic inhibition, while subsequent 2-HG re-elevation may signal pharmacodynamic failure or emergence of resistance mechanisms prior to morphologic relapse, enabling earlier therapeutic reassessme
