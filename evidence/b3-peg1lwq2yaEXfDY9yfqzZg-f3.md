# We found two YABBY TF genes as markers for FMab: HvCRC ( 4HG0396510 ), the orthologue of …

## Claim
We found two YABBY TF genes as markers for FMab: HvCRC ( 4HG0396510 ), the orthologue of DROOPING LEAF (OsDL) in rice, DL2 in maize and CRABS CLAW in Arabidopsis; and HvTOB1 ( 2HG0184460 ), the orthologue of ZmYABBY14 / TOB1 / ABNORMAL FLOWER ORGANS, all related to leaf-like organs such as the lemma in barley –.
