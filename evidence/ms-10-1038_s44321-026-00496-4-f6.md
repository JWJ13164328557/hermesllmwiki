# Several miRNAs have been shown to inﬂuence breast cancer metastasis through inhibition of speciﬁc ta

## Claim
Several miRNAs have been shown to inﬂuence breast cancer metastasis through inhibition of speciﬁc target genes, including miR-10b (RHOC), miR-335 (SOX4, TNC), miR-9 (CDH1, LIFR), miR-200 (ZEB1/2, SEC23A, MSN), miR-126 (CXCL12) and miR-21 (PDCD4, SERPINB5) among others (reviewed in (Pencheva and Tavazoie, 2013)).

## Biological Context
Metastasis of triple negative breast cancer is regulated by a targetable miR-342-E2F network
