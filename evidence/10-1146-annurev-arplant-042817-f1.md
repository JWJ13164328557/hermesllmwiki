# Indeed, energy supply in the form of sucrose and perception of light signals from outside of the sho

## Claim
Indeed, energy supply in the form of sucrose and perception of light signals from outside of the shoot meristem are required to stimulate WUS expression, with both factors acting through TARGET OF RAPAMYCIN (TOR) kinase, a conserved integrator of nutrients, energy, and growth factors (89).
