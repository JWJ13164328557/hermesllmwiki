# While both TBP and cycloprodigiosin settlement cues were first identified in the genus Pseudoalterom

## Claim
While both TBP and cycloprodigiosin settlement cues were first identified in the genus Pseudoalteromonas, our results suggest marine biofilms likely contain additional taxa capable of producing these settlement-inducing compounds.

## Biological Context
Metagenomic insights into mechanisms of coral larval settlement induction and inhibition by marine biofilms
