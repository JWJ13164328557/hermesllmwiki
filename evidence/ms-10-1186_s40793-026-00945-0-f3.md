# However, we found that genes encoding L-arginine synthesis were 17% higher in mean abundance in dark

## Claim
However, we found that genes encoding L-arginine synthesis were 17% higher in mean abundance in dark biofilms compared to light 1M and 2M, indicating the potential for larger amounts of L-arginine in dark biofilms (Figure 2C).

## Biological Context
Metagenomic insights into mechanisms of coral larval settlement induction and inhibition by marine biofilms
