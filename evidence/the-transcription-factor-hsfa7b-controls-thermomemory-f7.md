# Interestingly, we found that expression of HSFA7b, but not HSFA7a, is mediated by ethylene during HS

## Claim
Interestingly, we found that expression of HSFA7b, but not HSFA7a, is mediated by ethylene during HS, as evidenced by reduced expression of HSFA7b in plants treated with AIB or in PT acs mutants.
