# Fine roots, in addition to understory organic deposition, play a role in carbon cycling and in the a

## Claim
Fine roots, in addition to understory organic deposition, play a role in carbon cycling and in the accumulation of soil organic matter, which is crucial for the regeneration of soil properties after disturbances [23,24].

## Biological Context
Fine-root biomass in relation to soil properties in regenerating Norway spruce stands following large-scale bark beetle outbreaks in mountain and lowland forests
