# These results reveal that microglia-specific knockdown of CD31 not only decreases brain Aβ depositio

## Claim
These results reveal that microglia-specific knockdown of CD31 not only decreases brain Aβ deposition but also enhances microglial engagement around plaques, indicating improved microglial recruitment and plaque surveillance.

## Biological Context
Microglial CD31 suppresses Aβ clearance and promotes Alzheimer pathology in 5×FAD mice
