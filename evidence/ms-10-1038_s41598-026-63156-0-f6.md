# Studies using gene-ablated mice have shown that FXR and TGR5 are essential for maintaining a tolerog

## Claim
Studies using gene-ablated mice have shown that FXR and TGR5 are essential for maintaining a tolerogenic immune phenotype in the intestine, and their deletion leads to the polarization of intestinal T cells and macrophages toward a pro-inflammatory profile18.

## Biological Context
Loss of TGR5-activating bile acids is associated with disease activity in inflammatory bowel disease
