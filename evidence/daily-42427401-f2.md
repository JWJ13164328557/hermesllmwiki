# The use of metal oxide NPs, such as zinc oxide or magnesium oxide, synthesized by …

## Claim
The use of metal oxide NPs, such as zinc oxide or magnesium oxide, synthesized by endophytes as a beneficial application, can adversely affect non-target soil microorganisms and have lasting implications on the diversity of the microbial community, with negative effects on soil fertility; this will further alter crop health due to minor alterations in natural ecosystems.
