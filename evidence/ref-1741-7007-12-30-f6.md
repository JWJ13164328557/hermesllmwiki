# We found that the levels of acetylated histones H3 and H4 were significantly increased in fin regene

## Claim
We found that the levels of acetylated histones H3 and H4 were significantly increased in fin regenerates treated with 5 μM MGCD0103 for 4 days compared with fins treated with DMSO (Figure 4A), demonstrating that MGCD0103 effectively blocks Hdac1 activity in the caudal fin dur- ing regeneration.

## Biological Context
Specific NuRD components are required for fin regeneration in zebrafish.
