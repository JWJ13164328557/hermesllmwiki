# Notably, scRNA-seq identiﬁed clusters of progenitor T cells that signiﬁcantly expanded and innate-li

## Claim
Notably, scRNA-seq identiﬁed clusters of progenitor T cells that signiﬁcantly expanded and innate-like cytotoxic T cells, which emerged in SE- challenged birds at 2 dpi, indicating rapid mobilization of an innate-like cytotoxic response.

## Biological Context
Single-cell transcriptomic profiling reveals innate-like cytotoxic intraepithelial lymphocyte expansion during Salmonella Enteritidis infection in chickens
