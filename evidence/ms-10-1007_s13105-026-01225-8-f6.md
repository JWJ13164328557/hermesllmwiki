# These findings suggest that CB1-mediated signaling varies depending on the ligand (e.g., AEA e 2-AG)

## Claim
These findings suggest that CB1-mediated signaling varies depending on the ligand (e.g., AEA e 2-AG) and may also be influenced by the pathophysiological state of the intestine, the level of eCBs tone, and CB1 polariza­ tion between the apical and basolateral domains of epi­ thelial cells, thereby activating distinct signaling pathways and producing differential effects on barrier function [35].
