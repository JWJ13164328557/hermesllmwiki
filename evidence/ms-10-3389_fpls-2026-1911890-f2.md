# (2010) demonstrated that the CK-activated transcription factor ARR2 interacts with the SA response f

## Claim
(2010) demonstrated that the CK-activated transcription factor ARR2 interacts with the SA response factor TGA3 in an NPR1-dependent manner, thereby inducing the defense gene PR1 and the SA biosynthesis gene ICS1, and thus promoting resistance to Pseudomonas syringae.
