# Adequately spaced onion plants in the field are essential to maximize the number of plants per unit 

## Claim
Adequately spaced onion plants in the field are essential to maximize the number of plants per unit area while simultaneously improving the quality of the seeds produced.

## Biological Context
Paclobutrazol and planting density improved onion growth, reduced lodging and enhanced seed yield and quality in mid-hills of Himachal Pradesh
