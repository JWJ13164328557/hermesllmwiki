# Our findings suggest that thicker stems conferred by 400 elevated GhTALE expression or downregulated

## Claim
Our findings suggest that thicker stems conferred by 400 elevated GhTALE expression or downregulated GhOFP1/GhXTHB enhance 401 the structural integrity of cotton stalks, reducing lodging risk, and potentially 402 enhancing cotton yield (Supplemental Table 4) (Ookawa et al., 2010; Bisht et 403 al., 2022; Niu et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2024).

GhOFP1-GhTALE-GhXTHB transcriptional cascade regulates cotton stem vascular camb
