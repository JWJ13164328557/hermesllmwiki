# This study reveals a streamlined, root- meristem-independent route to shoot regeneration, underscori

## Claim
This study reveals a streamlined, root- meristem-independent route to shoot regeneration, underscoring the plasticity of plant regenerative programs.

## Biological Context
Cytokinin-supplemented medium drives de novo shoot regeneration in petunia (Petunia hybrida) by bypassing the root-meristem program
