# Taken together, these results suggest that the observed genetic patterns in pf12 are more consistent

## Claim
Taken together, these results suggest that the observed genetic patterns in pf12 are more consistent with recent population expansion, although a contribution from purifying selection cannot be fully excluded.

## Biological Context
Conserved genetic and antigenic profiles of Pf12 and Pf41 in asymptomatic Plasmodium falciparum carriers in Tanzania
