# In summary, THBS2 knockdown declined c-MYC protein via suppressing the PI3K/AKT axis, while weakenin

## Claim
In summary, THBS2 knockdown declined c-MYC protein via suppressing the PI3K/AKT axis, while weakening the formation of MYC/MAX dimers and the binding ability of c-MYC to bind to target gene promoters, thereby inhibiting c-MYC transcriptional activity.

## Biological Context
Down-regulation of THBS2 inhibits the malignant progression of Burkitt lymphoma by blocking the PI3K/AKT/c-MYC pathway
