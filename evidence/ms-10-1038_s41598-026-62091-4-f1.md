# We show here that the infection with the pathogen induces significant transcription of cytokine gene

## Claim
We show here that the infection with the pathogen induces significant transcription of cytokine genes such as il1β, il6 and tnf but not their secretion.

## Biological Context
Chlamydia trachomatis serovar D replicates in uroepithelial T24/83 cells in the absence of overt inflammation
