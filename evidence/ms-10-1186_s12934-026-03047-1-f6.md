# Understanding the metabolic costs associated with surfactin production, specifically the ATP demand 

## Claim
Understanding the metabolic costs associated with surfactin production, specifically the ATP demand and precursor diversion resulting from the expression of the large srfAA-AD operon (srfA operon), is crucial for optimizing production strains.

## Biological Context
Exploring the metabolic burden of surfactin biosynthesis and the metabolic costs of srfA operon expression in Bacillus subtilis
