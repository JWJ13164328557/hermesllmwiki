# We show that both lights expedite the progress of skin coloration in tomato fruit by promoting chlor

## Claim
We show that both lights expedite the progress of skin coloration in tomato fruit by promoting chlorophyll degradation, stimulating carotenoid biosynthesis, acceler­ ating plastid conversion, and enhancing plant hormone (i.e., abscisic acid and ethylene) biosynthesis.
