# Knockdown of ODC, SRM and SMOX reduced α-synuclein toxicity, while inhibition of SAT1 and SMS exacer

## Claim
Knockdown of ODC, SRM and SMOX reduced α-synuclein toxicity, while inhibition of SAT1 and SMS exacerbated the toxicity, underscoring the critical role of polyamine pathways in regulating the effect of α-synuclein and offering novel therapeutic targets for Parkinson's Disease (PD) 52.

## Biological Context
Geroprotective insights into the natural metabolite spermidine in aging and age-related diseases
