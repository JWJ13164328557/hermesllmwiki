# To this end, a leader robot lays a phosphorescent trail by exciting the arena surface with ultraviol

## Claim
To this end, a leader robot lays a phosphorescent trail by exciting the arena surface with ultraviolet light; the brightness of the trail decays over time, and its initial intensity encodes the target group size.

## Biological Context
Robotic group formation with phosphorescent trails
