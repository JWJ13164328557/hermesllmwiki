# Collectively, the results demonstrate that increasing fertilizer concentration is the primary driver

## Claim
Collectively, the results demonstrate that increasing fertilizer concentration is the primary driver of structural growth, whereas the addition of Moringa extract selectively enhances physiological and biochemical attributes, with the most effective treatment varying according to the targeted trait.

## Biological Context
Qualitative attributes, nutritional traits and yield components of Calendula officinalis L.: response to liquid NPK fertilizer, humic acid, and moringa leaf extract
