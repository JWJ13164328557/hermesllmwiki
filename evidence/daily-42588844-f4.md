# The methylation levels of mCHH in barley positively correlated with gene density …

## Claim
The methylation levels of mCHH in barley positively correlated with gene density (correlation coefficient r = 0.72; p < 2.2 × 10 −16 ) and negatively correlated with LTR-RT density (r = −0.54; p < 2.2 × 10 −16 ) ( ), a pattern also observed in rice and sorghum.
