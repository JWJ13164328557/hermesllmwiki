# Alter- native polyadenylation is a crucial and ubiquitous way to modulate gene expression, mRNA meta

## Claim
Alter- native polyadenylation is a crucial and ubiquitous way to modulate gene expression, mRNA metabolism and func- tion at the post-transcriptional level by regulating the inclu- sion or exclusion of a speciﬁc segment of a mRNA sequence, such as target sites for microRNAs (miRNAs) or RNA-binding proteins (RBPs) (Xing and Li, 2011; Mayr, 2016; Tian and Manley, 2017).

## Biological Context
Alternative Polyadenylation Contributes to Fibroblast Senescence in Pulmonary Fibrosis
