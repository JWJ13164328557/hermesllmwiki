# These results suggest that tumours with higher mutation load and neoanti­ gen abundance recruit more

## Claim
These results suggest that tumours with higher mutation load and neoanti­ gen abundance recruit more activated CD4 T cells but fewer Tregs, whereas the quality of clonal neoantigens (binder-weighted VAF) is specifically linked to T-cell dys­ function rather than broad immune infiltration.

## Biological Context
Inverse relationship between neoantigen clonality and T-cell activity reveals distinct immune phenotypes in HNSCC
