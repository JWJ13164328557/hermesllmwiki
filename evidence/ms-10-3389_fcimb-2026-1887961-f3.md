## Claim
Taken together, these ﬁndings indicate that metabolic alterations associated with comorbid T2DM in PTB patients are mainly characterized by changes in arginine and proline metabolism, steroid hormone biosynthesis, cAMP signaling, taurine and hypotaurine metabolism.
