# Together, our ﬁndings reveal a dynamic regulatory mechanism in which BRC1 functions as a mole- cular

## Claim
Together, our ﬁndings reveal a dynamic regulatory mechanism in which BRC1 functions as a mole- cular rheostat to modulate NF-Y-dependent epigenetic regulation of target genes in a dose-dependent manner, thereby integrating diverse branching signals in the control of developmental plasticity of axillary buds.

## Biological Context
BRANCHED1 acts as a rheostat of Nuclear Factor Y-dependent epigenetic regulation in axillary bud development
