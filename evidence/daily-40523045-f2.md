# The N-terminus of the bZIP domain, known as the basic region, interacts with the major …

## Claim
The N-terminus of the bZIP domain, known as the basic region, interacts with the major groove of the DNA helix, while the C-terminus, or leucine zipper region, stabilizes the α-helical structure through dimer formation, a structure referred to as the leucine zipper domain.
