# (p)ppGpp binds to target proteins, such as RNA polymerase and primase, reprograms transcription, dow

## Claim
(p)ppGpp binds to target proteins, such as RNA polymerase and primase, reprograms transcription, downregulates macromolecular synthesis, and ultimately induces dormancy (Amato and Brynildsen, 2015; Bokinsky et al., 2013; Germain et al., 2013; Hauryliuk et al., 2015; Korch et al., 2003; Semanjski et al., 2018).
