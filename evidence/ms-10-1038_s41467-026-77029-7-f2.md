# Mechanistically, the heterotrimeric Nuclear Factor Y (NF-Y) complex is required for transcriptional 

## Claim
Mechanistically, the heterotrimeric Nuclear Factor Y (NF-Y) complex is required for transcriptional activation of representative FOD genes, functioning through chromatin looping and H3K27me3 demethylation to establish active chromatin states at target loci.

## Biological Context
BRANCHED1 acts as a rheostat of Nuclear Factor Y-dependent epigenetic regulation in axillary bud development
