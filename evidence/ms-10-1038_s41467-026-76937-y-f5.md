# Collectively, in this mouse model, the genetic depletion of Tfh cells reduced lymphoid aggregate for

## Claim
Collectively, in this mouse model, the genetic depletion of Tfh cells reduced lymphoid aggregate formation and partially rescued pregnancy outcomes, providing experimental evidence for a causal contribution of Tfh-dependent lymphoid aggregates to compromised establishment and maintenance of pregnancy in mice.

An endometrial immune atlas across the female menstrual cycle reveals lymphoid aggregates compromise pregnancy
