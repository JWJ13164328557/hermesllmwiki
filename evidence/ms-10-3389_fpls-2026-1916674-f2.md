# Transient overexpression of PavCML42 signiﬁcantly increased cell wall hydrolase activities and induc

## Claim
Transient overexpression of PavCML42 signiﬁcantly increased cell wall hydrolase activities and induced cracking, whereas VIGS silencing reduced these enzyme activities and alleviated membrane lipid peroxidation damage.

## Biological Context
Regulatory effects of exogenous calcium on sweet cherry fruit cracking and functional analysis of the PavCML42 gene
