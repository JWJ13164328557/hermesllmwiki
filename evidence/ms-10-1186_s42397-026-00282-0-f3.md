# Adequate photosynthetic carbohydrates produced by cotton leaves are essential to attain fiber qualit

## Claim
Adequate photosynthetic carbohydrates produced by cotton leaves are essential to attain fiber quality approaching genetic maximums (Petti- grew 2001; Zhao and Oosterhuis 1998).

## Biological Context
Effects of planting density and topping time on the source-sink tributes and photosynthetic carbon production in short-fruiting branch cotton
