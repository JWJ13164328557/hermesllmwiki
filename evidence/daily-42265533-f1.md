# Utilizing lipid analyses, confocal and transmission electron microscopy, and viral …

## Claim
Utilizing lipid analyses, confocal and transmission electron microscopy, and viral propagation surveys in plants impaired in LD biogenesis, we show that potyvirus turnip mosaic virus (TuMV) infection induces a significant accumulation of NLs and a proliferation of LDs in Nicotiana benthamiana or Arabidopsis thaliana infected leaves, which are often located in close proximity to VRCs.
