# Previous research showed that irrigation applied at critical growth stages such as tillering, bootin

## Claim
Previous research showed that irrigation applied at critical growth stages such as tillering, booting and grain filling, is essential for achieving optimum yield and improving water use efficiency (WUE), whereas excessive irrigation may negatively affect grain yield and reduce input use efficiency (7–9).

## Biological Context
Optimising irrigation and zinc management improves wheat yield, quality and resource use-efficiency in the Indo-Gangetic Plains
