# Combined Se NPs + biochar application produced superior synergistic amelioration relative …

## Claim
Combined Se NPs + biochar application produced superior synergistic amelioration relative to either amendment alone, restoring net photosynthetic rate and grain yield to approximately 89%–90% and 88% of control values, respectively, alongside significant reductions in oxidative stress indicators.
