# Target gene regions were further purified by using agarose gel electrophoresis with a Monarch DNA Ge

## Claim
Target gene regions were further purified by using agarose gel electrophoresis with a Monarch DNA Gel Extraction Kit (Monarch, NEB, USA), and fragment distribution and size were verified by using an Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer (Agilent Technologies, Santa Clara, CA, USA).

## Biological Context
Comparative analysis of soil prokaryotic community structure and function in Ethiopian Church Forests and adjacent habitats
