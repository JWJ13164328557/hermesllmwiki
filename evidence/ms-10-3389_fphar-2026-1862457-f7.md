# Interestingly, GH01 administration displayed a negligible impact on ceramide metabolism in the liver

## Claim
Interestingly, GH01 administration displayed a negligible impact on ceramide metabolism in the liver and plasma of healthy mice (Supplementary Figures S2A,B) and normal L02 cells (Supplementary Figure S3A).

## Biological Context
A novel prenylflavonoid with efficacy in cancer therapy by targeting ceramide-orchestrated signaling
