# Our data suggest that OsLESV and OsESV1 are crucial for transitory starch biosynthesis in …

## Claim
Our data suggest that OsLESV and OsESV1 are crucial for transitory starch biosynthesis in rice leaves and that OsLESV and OsESV1 may have partially overlapping functions, as the double mutant displayed significantly greater impairment in its ability to accumulate transitory starch in leaves.
