# Resistance to conjugation-target­ ing interventions is likely to emerge, but key targets such as rel

## Claim
Resistance to conjugation-target­ ing interventions is likely to emerge, but key targets such as relaxases and ATPases perform essential and highly conserved functions within the conjugation machinery (Costa et al., 2021; de la Cruz et al., 2010; Patkowski et al., 2023), limiting the evolutionary space for escape and potentially constraining the emergence of resistance to inhibition.

## Biological Context
Conjugation as an evolutionary bottleneck in antimicrobial resistance spread
