# Specifically, we show that TET1-mediated DNA demeth­ ylation is associated with MRPS17 upregulation 

## Claim
Specifically, we show that TET1-mediated DNA demeth­ ylation is associated with MRPS17 upregulation and that the TET1/MRPS17 axis promotes malignant phenotypes of LUAD cells.

## Biological Context
TET1-mediated DNA demethylation and transcription activation of MRPS17 induces lung adenocarcinoma through PI3K-AKT-mTOR pathway
