# In summary, cell wall thickening and compositional changes, decline in intercellular airspace and ch

## Claim
In summary, cell wall thickening and compositional changes, decline in intercellular airspace and chloroplast retraction are among the drought-induced modifications that constitute a coordinated, balanced structural strategy in plants, allowing them to conserve water while still carrying out some photosynthetic activity.

## Biological Context
Interaction between stomatal, mesophyll and chloroplast regulation for plant photosynthetic acclimation to drought
