## Claim
Collectively, these findings suggest that POU2F2 promotes the activation of the JAK/STAT pathway, thereby enhancing cell viability, reducing apop­ tosis, and increas-ing invasiveness in clear cell renal cell carcinoma.

## Biological Context
POU2F2 promotes the progression of clear cell renal cell carcinoma by regulating the JAK/STAT signaling pathway through SPI1
