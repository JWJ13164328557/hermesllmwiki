# By performing histone pull-down assays using the OBE1 and OBE2 mixture, we found that …

## Claim
By performing histone pull-down assays using the OBE1 and OBE2 mixture, we found that OBE2 enhanced the binding affinity of OBE1 with H3K4me1/2/3 peptides and that OBE1 enhanced the binding affinity of OBE2 with histone peptides non-specifically (Fig 7C), suggesting that the two OBE proteins in the WRKY-OBE complex cooperate to bind to histone.
