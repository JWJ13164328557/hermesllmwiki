# Interestingly, we found 10,280 overlapping peaks (group A&N) between wild-type WRKY11 and WRKY11-CCD

## Claim
Interestingly, we found 10,280 overlapping peaks (group A&N) between wild-type WRKY11 and WRKY11-CCD, which accounted for 90.1 and 53.0% of wild-type WRKY11 and WRKY11-CCD peaks, respectively (Fig 6A), indicating that WRKY11-CCD binds to numerous target genomic loci that are not bound by wild-type WRKY11.
