# This trend indicates that IAA plays a critical role in regulating tiller initiation and growth in ve

## Claim
This trend indicates that IAA plays a critical role in regulating tiller initiation and growth in vetiver cuttings and follows the characteristic bell-shaped auxin response curve reported in many plant species (Rout, 2006; Wang et al., 2022).

## Biological Context
Optimization of Indole-3-Acetic Acid and Cutting Type for Enhanced Vegetative Propagation of Vetiver (Vetiveria zizanioides L.)
