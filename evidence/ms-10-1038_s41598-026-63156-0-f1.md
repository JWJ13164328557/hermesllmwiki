# Our findings support the importance of microbial 7α-dehydroxylation for maintaining the secondary bi

## Claim
Our findings support the importance of microbial 7α-dehydroxylation for maintaining the secondary bile acid pool but further suggest that, in humans, this metabolic capacity is primarily determined by uncultivated bai-carrying taxa.

## Biological Context
Loss of TGR5-activating bile acids is associated with disease activity in inflammatory bowel disease
