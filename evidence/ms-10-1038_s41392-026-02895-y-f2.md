# Mechanistic studies revealed that the hypoxia-inducible factor (HIF)-2α and HIF-1β heterodimers bind

## Claim
Mechanistic studies revealed that the hypoxia-inducible factor (HIF)-2α and HIF-1β heterodimers bind to this HRE, transactivating E1A.

## Biological Context
Hypoxia increases the activity of oncolytic adenoviruses through HIF-2α-stimulated E1A expression
