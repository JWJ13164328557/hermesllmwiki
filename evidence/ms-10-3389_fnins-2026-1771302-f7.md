# Abnormally elevated ceramides specifically activate death-associated protein kinase 1 (DAPK1), causi

## Claim
Abnormally elevated ceramides specifically activate death-associated protein kinase 1 (DAPK1), causing it to phosphorylate the Ser350 site of N-myc downstream-regulated gene 2 (NDRG2), thereby initiating and amplifying the caspase-dependent apoptotic pathway (You et al., 2017; Chalfant et al., 1999).

## Biological Context
Exploring ceramide as a novel biomarker and therapeutic target for Alzheimer’s disease
