# As a necessary step in gaining a deeper understanding of the regulatory mechanisms controlling plant

## Claim
As a necessary step in gaining a deeper understanding of the regulatory mechanisms controlling plant gene expression, we report the use of single-nucleus RNA sequencing (sNucRNA-seq) and single-nucleus assay for transposase accessible chromatin sequencing (sNucATAC- seq) technologies on Arabidopsis roots.

## Biological Context
Single-nucleus RNA and ATAC sequencing reveals the impact of chromatin accessibility on gene expression in Arabidopsis roots
