# These results indicate that SlEIL2 directly regulates the expression of SlERF.G6 and SlERF.H30, thus

## Claim
These results indicate that SlEIL2 directly regulates the expression of SlERF.G6 and SlERF.H30, thus affecting SlLCYB2 transcript levels, which in turn influences the accumulation of β-carotene in tomato fruit.

## Biological Context
ETHYLENE-INSENSITIVE 3-LIKE 2 调控 β-carotene and ascorbic acid 积累 in tomatoes 过程中 ripening
