# Remarkably, 27 phenylpropanoids and flavonoids were significantly accumulated in resistant cultivars

## Claim
Remarkably, 27 phenylpropanoids and flavonoids were significantly accumulated in resistant cultivars but were largely undetected or showed no substantial variation in vulnerable cultivars.

## Biological Context
Comprehensive metabolomic characterization of defense reprogramming in Castanea mollissima under Oligonychus ununguis feeding stress
