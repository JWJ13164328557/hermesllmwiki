# While it is expected that the variance on abundance estimates should increase approximately linearly

## Claim
While it is expected that the variance on abundance estimates should increase approximately linearly with abundance13, our results show that there is high variability in uncertainty of estimates as a result of the complex structure of similarity among transcripts, especially multiple isoforms of genes.
