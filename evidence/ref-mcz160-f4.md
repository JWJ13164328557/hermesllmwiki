# Briefly, ABA binds to intercellular Pyrabactin Resistance/ Regulatory Component of ABA …

## Claim
Briefly, ABA binds to intercellular Pyrabactin Resistance/ Regulatory Component of ABA Receptor (PYR/RCAR) receptors to inhibit the PP2C.A type protein phosphatases, which leads to release of SNF1-Related Protein Kinase 2 (SnRK2) family members; these activate ion channels, transcription factors and other targets (Hauser et al., 2017).
