# In a two-step assay, the lipid transport function of the allergen was investigated by offering unlab

## Claim
In a two-step assay, the lipid transport function of the allergen was investigated by offering unlabelled liposomes and measuring the integration of lipids into the FRET-sensitive target membrane.

## Biological Context
Lipid ligand binding and membrane interactions of a novel food-derived lipid transfer protein enhance basophil allergic responses
