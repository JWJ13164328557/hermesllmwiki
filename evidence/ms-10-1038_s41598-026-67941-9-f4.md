# Taken together, 2 weeks of glucocorticoid administration to C57BL/6 mice did not cause any obvious c

## Claim
Taken together, 2 weeks of glucocorticoid administration to C57BL/6 mice did not cause any obvious changes in islet morphology but significantly altered the quality of insulin granules in pancreatic -cells.

## Biological Context
Glucocorticoids directly inhibit insulin biosynthesis via the glucocorticoid receptor in pancreatic β-cells
