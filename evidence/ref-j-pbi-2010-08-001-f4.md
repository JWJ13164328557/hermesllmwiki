# The set of WUS targets included CLV1 and was enriched for genes involved in hormone responses and me

## Claim
The set of WUS targets included CLV1 and was enriched for genes involved in hormone responses and meristem de- velopment, in addition to genes involved in pollen and ovule development, reﬂecting later functions of WUS in reproductive organs.

Plant stem cell niches: from signalling to execution.
