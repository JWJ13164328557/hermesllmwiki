# A close examination of individual hairs in older individuals shows that this is actually …

## Claim
A close examination of individual hairs in older individuals shows that this is actually a modular process, with the amount of visual greying not being determined by a uniform degree of whitening in all hair shafts, but rather by the number of individual binary hairs that no longer contain any pigment.
