# In summary, overexpression of GbNAC072 C in combination with knockout of GhDEAH12 …

## Claim
In summary, overexpression of GbNAC072 C in combination with knockout of GhDEAH12 significantly enhances LP, while overexpression of GbDEAH12 G in combination with knockout of GhNAC072 markedly reduces LP, suggesting an antagonistic regulatory relationship between GbNAC072 and GbDEAH12 in the modulation of cotton lint yield.
