# Interestingly, reduced promoter methylation correlates with decreased transcription of mtDNA-encoded

## Claim
Interestingly, reduced promoter methylation correlates with decreased transcription of mtDNA-encoded oxidative phosphorylation genes and modest reductions in mtDNA copy number, implying that, unlike in the nucleus, mtDNA methylation may support transcription.
