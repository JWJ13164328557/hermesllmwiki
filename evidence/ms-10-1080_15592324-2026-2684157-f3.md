# Interestingly, the ascorbic acid content was higher under RBFR than RB in Group 2 (by ~9% in red and

## Claim
Interestingly, the ascorbic acid content was higher under RBFR than RB in Group 2 (by ~9% in red and 3%–4% in yellow fruits), suggesting that, in an interlighting context where FR supplements rather than replace PAR, photosynthetic enhancement may offset spectral trends that otherwise decrease ascorbate.

## Biological Context
Postharvest preservation of fruits and vegetables by light-emitting diodes
