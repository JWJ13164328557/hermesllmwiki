# Inspired by the helical nanoarchitecture of cherry bark19,20, we report a bioinspired nano-orientati

## Claim
Inspired by the helical nanoarchitecture of cherry bark19,20, we report a bioinspired nano-orientation engineering strategy to fabricate ultra-tough regenerated cellulose fibers with a biaxially oriented configuration via a scalable microfluidic spinning system.

## Biological Context
Bioinspired nano-architecture for cellulose fibers with spider silk–like toughness
