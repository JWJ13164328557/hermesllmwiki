# Collectively, these findings support the transition of Sargassum from a coastal nuisance to a viable

## Claim
Collectively, these findings support the transition of Sargassum from a coastal nuisance to a viable/valuable agricultural input, positioning biostimulants within an emerging framework of seaweed-based valorization strategies.

## Biological Context
Valorization of pelagic Sargassum into a biostimulant (OJA1) for improved tomato yields and protection against bacterial and fungal phytopathogens
