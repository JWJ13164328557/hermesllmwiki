# We propose that SbIMP-4 represents a prime target for functional validation through CRISPR/Cas9-medi

## Claim
We propose that SbIMP-4 represents a prime target for functional validation through CRISPR/Cas9-mediated knockout, transgenic overexpression, or base editing approaches (Peng et al., 2025) in sorghum, coupled with phenotypic characterization under salt stress and biochemical assays of AsA content and ROS scavenging capacity.

## Biological Context
Comparative genomics and stress-responsive expression of the IMP gene family in Sorghum bicolor
