# Bioinformatics analysis identified three potential target genes for CEP, namely glutathione peroxida

## Claim
Bioinformatics analysis identified three potential target genes for CEP, namely glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4), tyrosyl‑DNA phosphodiesterase 1 and carbonic anhydrase 4, among which CEP significantly suppressed the expression of GPX4 in LUAD cells.

## Biological Context
Cepharanthine suppresses lung adenocarcinoma through targeting glutathione peroxidase 4
