# Notably, tumor angiogenesis, stimulated by hypoxia-induced factors like VEGF, is essential for suppl

## Claim
Notably, tumor angiogenesis, stimulated by hypoxia-induced factors like VEGF, is essential for supplying nutrients and oxygen, thereby facilitating tumor growth and metastatic spread.

## Biological Context
HIF1-driven TDP-43 stabilizes TRIP6 mRNA to drive angiogenesis and colorectal cancer progression under hypoxia
