# We found that, due to the “metabolic bottleneck” imposed by low Mpi expression in macrophages, D-man

## Claim
We found that, due to the “metabolic bottleneck” imposed by low Mpi expression in macrophages, D-mannose not only inhibited glycolysis and the TCA cycle but also markedly constrained the PPP, leading to a concurrent reduction in intracellular ATP and NADPH levels.

## Biological Context
D-mannose blocks S-adenosylmethionine generation to suppress macrophage IL-1β expression
