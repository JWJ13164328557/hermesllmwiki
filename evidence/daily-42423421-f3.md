# Sucrose synthase (SuSy) and vacuolar acid invertase (AINV) cleave sucrose into UDPglucose …

## Claim
Sucrose synthase (SuSy) and vacuolar acid invertase (AINV) cleave sucrose into UDPglucose or hexoses (glucose and fructose), and their activity plays a central role in determining TSS and sugar accumulation in tomato fruits (Beckles et al.,; Qin et al., ).
