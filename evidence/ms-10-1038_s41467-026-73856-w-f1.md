# Using murine and human models, we show that hepatic polarity and bile canaliculi (BC) formation init

## Claim
Using murine and human models, we show that hepatic polarity and bile canaliculi (BC) formation initiate at sites of cell division during the hepatoblast stage, and that BC elongation is temporally associated with oriented cell division during hepatocyte differentiation.

## Biological Context
PAR3-mediated coordination of hepatocyte proliferation, maturation, and architecture in liver development and regeneration
