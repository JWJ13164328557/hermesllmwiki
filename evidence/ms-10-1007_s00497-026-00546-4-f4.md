# We found two mutant lines designated ARS-67 and ARS-84 that carried point mutations in the sorghum o

## Claim
We found two mutant lines designated ARS-67 and ARS-84 that carried point mutations in the sorghum orthologue of the rice TDR gene (gene ID: Sorbi_3004G017500).

## Biological Context
The SbTDR gene is required for anther development and male fertility in Sorghum bicolor
