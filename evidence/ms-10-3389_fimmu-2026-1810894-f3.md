# Collectively, these data provide an atlas of antibody protein binding across the spectrum of Mtb inf

## Claim
Collectively, these data provide an atlas of antibody protein binding across the spectrum of Mtb infection, and further point to a handful of promising Mtb protein candidates to guide the design of antibody-based TB interventions aimed at mitigating disease globally.
