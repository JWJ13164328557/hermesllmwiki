# Interestingly, PbrMYB24 not only directly activates the tran- scription of lignin and cellulose bios

## Claim
Interestingly, PbrMYB24 not only directly activates the tran- scription of lignin and cellulose biosynthesis genes but also directly activates the expression of the TF-encoding genes PbrNSC (first layer of cascade regulation) and PbrMYB169 (third layer of cascade regulation).

## Biological Context
The transcription factor PbrMYB24 调控 木质素 and 纤维素 生物合成 in stone cells of pear fruits
