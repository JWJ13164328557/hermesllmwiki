# The activated SOS2 kinase then phosphorylates SOS1, thereby enhancing the activity of Na+/H+ antipor

## Claim
The activated SOS2 kinase then phosphorylates SOS1, thereby enhancing the activity of Na+/H+ antiporters in the plasma membrane and facilitating the efflux of excess Na+ from the cytoplasm to the extracellular space [25,28] (Figure 1).
