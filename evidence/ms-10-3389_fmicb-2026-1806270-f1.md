## Claim
By analyzing the mutation sites in the RBD fragments of existing SARS-CoV-2 vari­ ants, we found that nearly all variants have mutations at five amino acid sites (417, 452, 478, 484, and 501), which have a certain impact on viral transmission and immune evasion.

## Biological Context
Recombinant RBD-based subunit vaccines incorporating high-frequency mutation sites elicit cross-immunity and robust protection against SARS-CoV-2
