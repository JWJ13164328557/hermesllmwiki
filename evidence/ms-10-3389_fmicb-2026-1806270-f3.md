# Studies have shown that the immune epitopes of RBD are capable of inducing neutralizing antibodies a

## Claim
Studies have shown that the immune epitopes of RBD are capable of inducing neutralizing antibodies and T-cell immune responses, making it an ideal target as a means of developing effective vaccines (Anzai et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024).

## Biological Context
Recombinant RBD-based subunit vaccines incorporating high-frequency mutation sites elicit cross-immunity and robust protection against SARS-CoV-2
