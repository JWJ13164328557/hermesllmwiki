# Collectively, these results demonstrate that TRB-ZFs can target H3K27me3 deposition and H3K4me3 remo

## Claim
Collectively, these results demonstrate that TRB-ZFs can target H3K27me3 deposition and H3K4me3 removal at many sites in the genome.

## Biological Context
Arabidopsis TRB proteins function in H3K4me3 demethylation by recruiting JMJ14.
