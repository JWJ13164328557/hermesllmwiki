# Collectively, these results suggest that TRB- ZFs can recruit JMJ14 to regions with low …

## Claim
Collectively, these results suggest that TRB- ZFs can recruit JMJ14 to regions with low or medium levels of preexisting H3K4me3 to remove methylation, and in addition, TRB-ZFs might also recruit WDR5A and associated H3K4 methyltransferases to regions with high levels of pre-existing H3K4me3 to add methylation.
