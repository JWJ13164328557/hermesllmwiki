# Surprisingly, we found that AP2m3 activated expression of the reporter gene driven by the MYBL2 prom

## Claim
Surprisingly, we found that AP2m3 activated expression of the reporter gene driven by the MYBL2 promoter by 3.5-fold but did not activate expression of the other 24 genes (Figure 3A).

## Biological Context
The APETALA2–MYBL2 模块 抑制 原花青素 生物合成 by affecting formation of the MBW complex in seeds of Arabidopsis thaliana
