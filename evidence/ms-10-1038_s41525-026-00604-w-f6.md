# A critical component of the nCATS protocol is the design of guide RNAs (gRNAs), which determine Cas9

## Claim
A critical component of the nCATS protocol is the design of guide RNAs (gRNAs), which determine Cas9 cleavage sites and direct the ligation of sequencing adapters to target regions.

## Biological Context
Resolving the complex CES1 genomic locus with Cas9-directed targeted long-read sequencing
