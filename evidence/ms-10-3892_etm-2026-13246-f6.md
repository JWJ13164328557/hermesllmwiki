# In PM2.5‑ and silica‑induced PF models, ALKBH5 expression is downregulated in lung epithelial cells 

## Claim
In PM2.5‑ and silica‑induced PF models, ALKBH5 expression is downregulated in lung epithelial cells and macro­ phages, leading to increased m6A modification of target mRNAs such as autophagy related 13 and SLAM family member 7 (SLAMF7), thereby promoting autophagy dysfunction, inflam­ mation and extracellular matrix (ECM) deposition (19,20).
