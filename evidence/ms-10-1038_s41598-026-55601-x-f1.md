# Taken together, these results show that CHR5 does not regulate the amount or activity of immune MAPK

## Claim
Taken together, these results show that CHR5 does not regulate the amount or activity of immune MAPKs, favouring the hypothesis that CHR5 is indeed solely downstream of MPK3.

## Biological Context
Genetic analyses of autoimmune mutants uncover the MPK3/6-CHR5-SNC1 module in Arabidopsis
