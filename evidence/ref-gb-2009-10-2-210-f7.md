# Auxin is required for meristem cell division: application of exogenous auxin increases root meristem

## Claim
Auxin is required for meristem cell division: application of exogenous auxin increases root meristem size, for example, whereas cytokinin reduces it [2,3].

## Biological Context
Cytokinin and auxin intersection in root meristems
