# Recent evidence indicates that a high dietary fructose intake promotes insulin resistance, hepatic l

## Claim
Recent evidence indicates that a high dietary fructose intake promotes insulin resistance, hepatic lipid accumulation, oxidative stress, chronic inflammation and activation of oncogenic pathways, including the phosphatidylinositol-3 kinase/protein kinase B/mammalian target of rapamycin (PI3K/AKT/mTOR) signaling axis.
