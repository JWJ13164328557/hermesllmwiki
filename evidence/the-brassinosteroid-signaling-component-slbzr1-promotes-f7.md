# BRI1 and BAK1 trans‐phosphorylate each other, increasing BRI1 activity that results in phos- phoryla

## Claim
BRI1 and BAK1 trans‐phosphorylate each other, increasing BRI1 activity that results in phos- phorylation of Brassinosteroid signaling kinase1 (BSK1), which is then released from the receptor complex (Wang et al., 2008; Jaillais et al., 2011).
