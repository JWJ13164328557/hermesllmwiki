# Remarkably, the DEGs identified in any of the cultivars and tissues overlapping with the specific he

## Claim
Remarkably, the DEGs identified in any of the cultivars and tissues overlapping with the specific hemizygous intervals of TB compared to TT did not include any known regulator of metabolism pathways, apart from the phenylpropanoid regu- lators VviMYBA1 and VviMYBA2 (Vitvi02g01015), and the pseudogene VvMYBA8 (Vitvi02g01310) resulting from the frameshift InDel in VviMYBA2 (Supplementary Table S4).
