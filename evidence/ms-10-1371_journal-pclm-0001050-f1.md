## Claim
Unlike an earlier MODIS-based study in Los Angeles and Seoul [60], which reported that incorporating LST observations from additional days did not necessarily improve Ta estimation, our findings suggest that expanding the temporal diversity of the training data is an important factor in enhancing model robustness, particularly when it comes to creating maps for future dates.
