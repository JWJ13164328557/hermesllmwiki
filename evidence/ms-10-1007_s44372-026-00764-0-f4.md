# Importantly, work on liquid Sargassum stimulants promotes its use in agriculture by demonstrating in

## Claim
Importantly, work on liquid Sargassum stimulants promotes its use in agriculture by demonstrating increased soil macronutrient availability to the plant and no detectable short-term accumulation of metals of concern (As, Cd, Pb, Ni, Cr) in the soil–plant-fruit system [48].

## Biological Context
Valorization of pelagic Sargassum into a biostimulant (OJA1) for improved tomato yields and protection against bacterial and fungal phytopathogens
