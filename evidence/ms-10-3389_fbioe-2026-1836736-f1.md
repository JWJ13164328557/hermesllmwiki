# These results suggest that the Ch/FM/SeNPs nanocomposite, similar to glibenclamide, may be used eith

## Claim
These results suggest that the Ch/FM/SeNPs nanocomposite, similar to glibenclamide, may be used either as a standalone treatment or in combination with conventional antidiabetic therapies to reduce the potential risk of cardiovascular disease.

## Biological Context
Nanocomposites of Opuntia ficus-indica mucilage-mediated selenium nanoparticles and nanochitosan as antidiabetic candidates in streptozocin-induced diabetic rats
