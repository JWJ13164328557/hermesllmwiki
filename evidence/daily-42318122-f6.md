# AC2 seems to be a multifunctional protein that also induces other silencing suppressors …

## Claim
AC2 seems to be a multifunctional protein that also induces other silencing suppressors like WEL1 (Werner’s exonuclease-like 1), rgs-CaM (Regulator of gene silencing – calmodulin), RAV2 (RELATED TO ABI3 and VP1) and interacts with ADK (adenosine kinase), SAMDC1 (S-adenosyl methionine decarboxylase1) and other host proteins (Veluthambi and Sunitha, 2021).
