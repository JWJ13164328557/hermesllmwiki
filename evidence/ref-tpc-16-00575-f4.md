# We found that the gravitropic phenotype of lzy1 lzy2 lzy3 stems was fully rescued by SCRp:LZY2 and S

## Claim
We found that the gravitropic phenotype of lzy1 lzy2 lzy3 stems was fully rescued by SCRp:LZY2 and SCRp:LZY3 constructs (Figures 2A and 2B; Supplemental Figure 6A) but was only partially rescued by the SCRp:LZY1 construct (Supplemental Figure 6B), possibly owing to nonoptimal expression of LZY1 when under control of the SCR promoter.

## Biological Context
The Arabidopsis LAZY1 Family Plays a Key Role in Gravity Signaling within Statoc
