# Collectively, these data establish the prognostic value of the OAS family members in cancer and spec

## Claim
Collectively, these data establish the prognostic value of the OAS family members in cancer and specifically highlight OAS1 as a key driver associated with poor prognosis and lipid metabolic dysregulation in PDAC.

## Biological Context
The OAS1-FASN axis promotes pancreatic cancer by coordinating lipogenic stress and the unfolded protein response
