# MdWRKY11 binds to the W-box cis-elements in the promoter of MdUFGT in apple to induce its expression

## Claim
MdWRKY11 binds to the W-box cis-elements in the promoter of MdUFGT in apple to induce its expression for regulation of anthocyanin biosynthesis (Liu et al., 2019c).
