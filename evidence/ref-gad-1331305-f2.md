# In this study, we identified VND6 and VND7, which belong to plant-specific transcription factors, NA

## Claim
In this study, we identified VND6 and VND7, which belong to plant-specific transcription factors, NAC-domain proteins that can induce transdifferentiation of various types of cells into metaxylemand protoxylem-like vessel elements, respectively.

## Biological Context
Transcription switches for protoxylem and metaxylem vessel formation.
