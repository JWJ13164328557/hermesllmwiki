# ROP2 orchestrates the balance of plant growth and senescence under N starvation We found that CA-ROP

## Claim
ROP2 orchestrates the balance of plant growth and senescence under N starvation We found that CA-ROP2 maintained plant growth during early-stage N starvation but induced premature cotyledon senescence at the later stage (Figures 1 and 2).

## Biological Context
Active ROP2 triggers leaf senescence and orchestrates the growth-senescence trade-off under nitrogen starvation
