# In the cytosol, calcium-dependent effectors such as calcium-dependent protein kinases (CPKs), CBL-CI

## Claim
In the cytosol, calcium-dependent effectors such as calcium-dependent protein kinases (CPKs), CBL-CIPK complexes, and MAPK cascades are activated and propagate signals to the nucleus, where they phosphorylate transcriptional regulators to modulate the expression of cold-responsive genes, including CORs, COOL1, and OsPRO1.

## Biological Context
Plant responses to cold stress: molecular and physiological adaptive mechanisms with breeding advances
