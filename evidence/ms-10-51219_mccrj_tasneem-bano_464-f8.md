# Strategies to overcome fratricide include: (i) CRISPR/Cas9-mediated knockout of target antigens in C

## Claim
Strategies to overcome fratricide include: (i) CRISPR/Cas9-mediated knockout of target antigens in CAR-T cells before engineering with anti-target CARs; (ii) use of allogeneic (donor-derived) CAR-T cells from HTLV-1- negative donors; and (iii) NK cell-based CARs that do not share the T-cell surface markers of the malignancy71.
