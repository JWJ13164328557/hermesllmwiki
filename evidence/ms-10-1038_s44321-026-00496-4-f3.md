# Using both syngeneic and xenograft models, incorporating different timing of miRNA induction and rou

## Claim
Using both syngeneic and xenograft models, incorporating different timing of miRNA induction and routes of tumour cell administration, we show that miR-342 speciﬁcally suppresses metastatic outgrowth of TNBC cells.

## Biological Context
Metastasis of triple negative breast cancer is regulated by a targetable miR-342-E2F network
