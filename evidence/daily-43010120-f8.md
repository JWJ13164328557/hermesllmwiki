# We identified a colocalized locus, S2_41652294, which was a strong eQTL for GRMZM2G325131 …

## Claim
We identified a colocalized locus, S2_41652294, which was a strong eQTL for GRMZM2G325131 ( p = 8.10 × 10 −12 ) and was significantly associated with root Trp abundance ( p = 4.92 × 10 −7 ), suggesting that variation at this locus may influence Trp metabolism by modulating GRMZM2G325131 expression (Figure ).
