# Loss of CHK1, using a CRISPR/Cas9-mediated CHK1 knockout- rescue model, induces apoptosis in S-phase

## Claim
Loss of CHK1, using a CRISPR/Cas9-mediated CHK1 knockout- rescue model, induces apoptosis in S-phase Ewing sarcoma cells Off-target effects are a well-recognized limitation of many kinase inhibitors, including those targeting CHK1 [50].

DNA replication stress and translational repression converge to drive CDK1- and caspase-dependent apoptosis in Ewing sarcoma
