# Mechanistically, excess intracellular copper directly binds to lipoylated components of the tricarbo

## Claim
Mechanistically, excess intracellular copper directly binds to lipoylated components of the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle, triggering aggregation of lipoylated proteins and destabilization of iron–sulfur (Fe–S) cluster proteins, ultimately leading to proteotoxic stress and cell death (Tsvetkov et al., 2022).
