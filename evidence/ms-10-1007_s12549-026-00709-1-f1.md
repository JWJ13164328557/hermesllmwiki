# Despite the limited number of samples, this palynological data significantly enhances our understand

## Claim
Despite the limited number of samples, this palynological data significantly enhances our understanding of the Middle Jurassic vegetation and its palaeoenvironments in Jordan, one of the very few palaeotropical floras from that time period for which both macro- and microflora are known.

## Biological Context
Palynology of Bathonian macroflora-bearing beds from NW-Jordan – expanding a rare ‘peephole’ into Middle Jurassic palaeotropical vegetation
