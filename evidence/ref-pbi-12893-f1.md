# Taken together, these results suggest that PeCHYR1 plays a crucial role in enhancing drought toleran

## Claim
Taken together, these results suggest that PeCHYR1 plays a crucial role in enhancing drought tolerance via ABA-induced stomatal closure caused by hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) production in transgenic poplar plants.

## Biological Context
PeCHYR1, a ubiquitin E3 ligase from Populus euphratica, enhances drought toleran
