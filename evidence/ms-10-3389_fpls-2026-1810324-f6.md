# In rice, COLD1 interacts with the G-protein a subunit RGA1 to activate the plasma membrane Ca2+ chan

## Claim
In rice, COLD1 interacts with the G-protein a subunit RGA1 to activate the plasma membrane Ca2+ channel, leading to a sudden increase in the cytoplasmic Ca2+ concentration (Ma et al., 2015).
