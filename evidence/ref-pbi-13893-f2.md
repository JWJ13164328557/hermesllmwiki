# For instance, Phytosulfokine (PSK) peptide may interact with PSK receptor gene 1 (PSKR1) and PSK rec

## Claim
For instance, Phytosulfokine (PSK) peptide may interact with PSK receptor gene 1 (PSKR1) and PSK receptor gene 2 (PSKR2) to regulate root growth in Arabidopsis (Kutschmar et al ., 2009 ), and the pathway of AtPep3 peptide and membrane‐receptor kinase gene PEPR1 is associated with salt tolerance in Arabidopsis (Nakaminami et al ., 2018 ).
