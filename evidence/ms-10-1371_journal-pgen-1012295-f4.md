# Introduction Rice (Oryza sativa L.), an annual gramineous crop, serves as the staple food for approx

## Claim
Introduction Rice (Oryza sativa L.), an annual gramineous crop, serves as the staple food for approximately 50% of the global population and plays a pivotal role in worldwide food security, especially in densely populated regions where it acts as the primary source of energy and nutrition [1,2].

## Biological Context
OsDUF1223 negatively regulates seedling drought tolerance and modulates nitrate-dependent root growth in rice
