# Besides, overexpression of MdbZIP44 promoted sucrose accumulation by regulating the activities of su

## Claim
Besides, overexpression of MdbZIP44 promoted sucrose accumulation by regulating the activities of sucrose metabolism-related enzymes and the expression of sugar metabolism-related genes in apple callus and tomato fruits.
