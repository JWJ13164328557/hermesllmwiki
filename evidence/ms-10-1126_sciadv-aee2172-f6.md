# Ca2+ binding to the CPK activation domain activates kinase activity, phosphorylating downstream targ

## Claim
Ca2+ binding to the CPK activation domain activates kinase activity, phosphorylating downstream targets such as transcription factors, facilitating their cytoplasm-­to-­nucleus translo- cation, which mediates gene expression and stress adaptation (15–17).

## Biological Context
Natural variation in <i>PtoCPK3</i> governs drought tolerance by orchestrating xylem remodeling and lignin metabolism in <i>Populus</i>
