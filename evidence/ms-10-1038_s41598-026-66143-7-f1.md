# Unlike necrotic cell death, apoptosis is a highly regulated process in the ischemic penumbra, making

## Claim
Unlike necrotic cell death, apoptosis is a highly regulated process in the ischemic penumbra, making it an attractive therapeutic target for limiting secondary brain injury after stroke13,14.

## Biological Context
Recombinant CXCL16 reduces brain injury by modulating microglial phenotype and attenuating apoptosis in acute ischemic stroke
