# On the basis of sets of 100-bp simulated and 101-bp real reads that were used in our evaluation (Sup

## Claim
On the basis of sets of 100-bp simulated and 101-bp real reads that were used in our evaluation (Supplementary Note 1), we found that 2.6–3.4% and 1.4–1.8% of the reads were mapped to ≥5 locations and ≥100 locations, respec- tively.

## Biological Context
Graph-based genome alignment and genotyping with HISAT2 and HISAT-genotype.
