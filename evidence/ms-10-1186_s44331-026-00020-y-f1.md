# Unlike Cas9, which performs a single, site-specific cut [13], Cas12 and Cas13 undergo a conformation

## Claim
Unlike Cas9, which performs a single, site-specific cut [13], Cas12 and Cas13 undergo a conformational acti- vation once they recognize their target sequence—DNA in the case of Cas12 and RNA in the case of Cas13 [18, 19].
