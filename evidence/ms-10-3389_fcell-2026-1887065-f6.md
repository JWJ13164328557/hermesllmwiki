# In addition, periostin secreted by CAFs contributes to ECM remodeling and immune escape, and its hig

## Claim
In addition, periostin secreted by CAFs contributes to ECM remodeling and immune escape, and its high expression is associated with poor prognosis in multiple cancers, suggesting that periostin and its downstream pathways may represent promising targets for overcoming resistance (De Oliveira Macena et al., 2024).
