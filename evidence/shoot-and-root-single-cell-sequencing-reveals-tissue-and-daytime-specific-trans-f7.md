# Although the final bolt height of mercy1 plants was substan- tially shorter compared with Col-0 plan

## Claim
Although the final bolt height of mercy1 plants was substan- tially shorter compared with Col-0 plants (Figure 5, L), we found that mercy1 flowered significantly earlier compared with Col-0 based on the RLN (P 5 0.01) and DTF (P 5 0.001) (Figure 5, I and J).
