# In the canonical pathway, stimulation of inflammatory pathways such as lipopolysaccharide (LPS), tum

## Claim
In the canonical pathway, stimulation of inflammatory pathways such as lipopolysaccharide (LPS), tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α), or interleukin-1β (IL-1β) activates the IκB kinase (IKK) complexes which then phosphorylate the inhibitory IκB proteins, causing their degradation by the proteasome.
