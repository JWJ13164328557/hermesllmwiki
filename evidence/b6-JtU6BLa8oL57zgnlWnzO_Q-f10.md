# Notably, the association of HY5 with the IAA19 promoter was significantly increased in tcp21-2 seedl

## Claim
Notably, the association of HY5 with the IAA19 promoter was significantly increased in tcp21-2 seedlings compared with Col-0 plants, implying that TCP21 inhibits the DNA-binding ability of HY5 (Figure 4I).

## Biological Context
TCP21 negatively regulates photomorphogenesis by integrating phyB- and HY5-media
