# Under such conditions, plants tend to retain old roots and reduce the production of new roots to con

## Claim
Under such conditions, plants tend to retain old roots and reduce the production of new roots to conserve photo- synthate reserves, which is essential for plant normal growth in the subsequent year (Luo et al., 2021).

## Biological Context
Light mowing supports fine-root production in a temperate saline-alkaline grassland soil
