## Claim
For example, while Rho GDP-dissociation inhibitor beta (RhoGDIβ) typically stimulates Rac1-dependent pathways to promote hypertrophic growth and cell migration, ZAK can directly bind and phosphorylate RhoGDIβ, thereby exerting a negative feedback counterbalance (Huang et al., 2009a; Huang et al., 2009b).
