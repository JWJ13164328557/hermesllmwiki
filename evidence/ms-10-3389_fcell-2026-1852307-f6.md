# Critically, disrupted Cu/Fe homeostasis directly regulates novel cell death pathways: excess Cu indu

## Claim
Critically, disrupted Cu/Fe homeostasis directly regulates novel cell death pathways: excess Cu induces cuproptosis through the aggregation of lipoylated tricarboxylic acid cycle proteins, whereas aberrant Fe metabolism drives ferroptosis through iron-dependent lipid peroxidation.
