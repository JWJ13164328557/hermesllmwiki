# Under both low CO 2 (200 μmol mol −1 ) and high CO 2 (800 μmol mol −1 ) concentrations …

## Claim
Under both low CO 2 (200 μmol mol −1 ) and high CO 2 (800 μmol mol −1 ) concentrations, overexpression of Rubisco activase resulted in a faster rate of Rubisco activation upon irradiation, leading to faster photosynthetic induction and greater photosynthetic response to longterm fluctuating light conditions.
