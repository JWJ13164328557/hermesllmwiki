# The IIIe subgroup bHLH TFs MYC2, MYC3, and MYC4 function redundantly to activate JA-induced leaf sen

## Claim
The IIIe subgroup bHLH TFs MYC2, MYC3, and MYC4 function redundantly to activate JA-induced leaf senescence in which MYC2 binds to and activates the promoter of target gene SAG29.

## Biological Context
Leaf senescence: progression, regulation, and application.
