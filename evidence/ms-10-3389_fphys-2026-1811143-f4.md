# We found that D–gal increased p53 phosphorylation and upregulated p21 and p16, whereas NaB dose–depe

## Claim
We found that D–gal increased p53 phosphorylation and upregulated p21 and p16, whereas NaB dose–dependently suppressed p53 activation and reduced p21/p16 expression.

## Biological Context
Sodium butyrate attenuates D-galactose-induced cellular senescence in WI-38 fibroblasts in vitro via modulation of HDAC activity, Nrf2/ARE pathway, and p53/p21/p16 signaling
