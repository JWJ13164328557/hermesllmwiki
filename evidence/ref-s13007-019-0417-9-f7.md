# In addition to the proxi- mal gradient, we found that initials divide nearly three- fold more slowly

## Claim
In addition to the proxi- mal gradient, we found that initials divide nearly three- fold more slowly than TA cells, which is slightly more pronounced than previously estimated rates [3], although growth conditions between the two experiments were different.

## Biological Context
Week-long imaging of cell divisions in the Arabidopsis root meristem.
