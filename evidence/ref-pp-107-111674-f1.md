# In summary, these results suggest that HDACs are involved in the repression of embryonic properties 

## Claim
In summary, these results suggest that HDACs are involved in the repression of embryonic properties after germination via repression of the embryo-speciﬁc transcription factors LEC1, FUS3, and ABI3.

## Biological Context
The Arabidopsis histone deacetylases HDA6 and HDA19 contribute to the repression
