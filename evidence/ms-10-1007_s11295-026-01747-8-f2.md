# Heterologous overexpression of EgrARF10 in hybrid poplar did not alter overall growth or morphology 

## Claim
Heterologous overexpression of EgrARF10 in hybrid poplar did not alter overall growth or morphology but resulted in a significant reduction in stem lignification, accompanied by relative increases in D-glucose, D-xylose and D-mannose, indicating altered SCW composition.

## Biological Context
Eucalyptus grandis Auxin Response Factor 10 (EgrARF10) is associated with the modulation of secondary xylem cell wall chemistry
