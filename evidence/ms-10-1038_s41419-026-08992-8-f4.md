# Taken together, these results demonstrate that the FASTIS phenotype induced by ND- 646 and TVB-3166 

## Claim
Taken together, these results demonstrate that the FASTIS phenotype induced by ND- 646 and TVB-3166 has a distinct transcriptomic signature that is functionally enriched for mitotic cell cycle, protein binding, and spindle/chromosome segregation processes.

## Biological Context
Fatty acid synthesis therapy-induced senescence (FASTIS) in cancer cells
