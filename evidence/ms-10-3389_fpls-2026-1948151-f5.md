# PLSR assumes an approximately linear relationship between latent spectral variables and the target v

## Claim
PLSR assumes an approximately linear relationship between latent spectral variables and the target variable, which limits its ability to capture threshold effects, saturation phenomena, and complex interactions among spectral bands.

## Biological Context
Transferable spectral models for high-throughput estimation of winter wheat net photosynthetic rate: a cross-scale approach using leaf and UAV hyperspectral data
