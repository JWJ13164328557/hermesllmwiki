# In conjunction with previous results showing that miR167a targets ARF8 (16), these results show that

## Claim
In conjunction with previous results showing that miR167a targets ARF8 (16), these results show that nitrogen represses levels of miR167a to permit the ARF8 transcript to accumulate in the pericycle upon nitrogen treatment.

## Biological Context
Cell-specific nitrogen responses mediate developmental plasticity.
