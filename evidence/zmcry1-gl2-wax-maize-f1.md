# Notably, we identify that ZmCRY1 directly interacts with GLOSSY2 (GL2), a key acyltransferase in ver

## Claim
Notably, we identify that ZmCRY1 directly interacts with GLOSSY2 (GL2), a key acyltransferase in very-long-chain fatty acid metabolism, in a blue light-strengthened manner to mediate blue light-promoted C32 aldehyde accumulation, shedding new light on the enigma of the aldehyde- forming pathway in plants.
