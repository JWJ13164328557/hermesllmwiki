# RT-PCR analyses of Bu-SCs puriﬁed at different hair cycle substages showed that neither WNT target A

## Claim
RT-PCR analyses of Bu-SCs puriﬁed at different hair cycle substages showed that neither WNT target Axin2 nor TGF-b2 target Tmeff1 was appreciably elevated at AnaII–III (Figure S1D).

## Biological Context
Transit-amplifying cells orchestrate stem cell activity and tissue regeneration.
