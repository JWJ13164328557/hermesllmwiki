# Our results suggest that mowing at the MS strikes a crucial physiological balance: it allows suffici

## Claim
Our results suggest that mowing at the MS strikes a crucial physiological balance: it allows sufficient accumulation of non-structural carbohydrates in the stubble to fuel bud germination, while preventing the excessive translocation of assimilates to the MC panicles, occurring during the full maturity stage.

## Biological Context
Sustainable forage-grain ratoon rice production: interactions between planting density and mowing time on forage and grain attributes
