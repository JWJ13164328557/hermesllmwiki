# Flowering in Arabidop sis thaliana (Arabidopsis) is controlled by a mechanism involving …

## Claim
Flowering in Arabidop sis thaliana (Arabidopsis) is controlled by a mechanism involving CONSTANS (CO) and its target gene FLOWERING LOCUS T (FT), where a sufficiently long day time causes light-activated stabilization of CO protein in the later part of a day, and induces expression of the florigen-encoding FT gene [6].
