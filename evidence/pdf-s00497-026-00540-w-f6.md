# Unlike antipodal cells, synergids are essential for reproduction since they control many steps of th

## Claim
Unlike antipodal cells, synergids are essential for reproduction since they control many steps of the double fertilization process, including pollen tube guidance, pollen tube growth arrest in the female gameto­ phyte, pollen tube discharge and the migration of sperm cells (Punwani and Drews 2008 and references therein).
