# Pivotal Phase III trials, including IMbrave150 (atezolizumab plus bevacizumab)[6], HIMALAYA (durvalu

## Claim
Pivotal Phase III trials, including IMbrave150 (atezolizumab plus bevacizumab)[6], HIMALAYA (durvalumab plus tremelimumab)[7], and the recent CheckMate-9DW (nivolumab plus ipilimumab)[8], have achieved objective response rates (ORRs) of approximately 20.1%-36%, compared with 5.1%-18.8% for multi-target kinase inhibitors, such as sorafenib and lenvatinib, in their respective pivotal trials.

Gut microbiota as predictors of immunotherapy response in hepatocellular carcinoma
