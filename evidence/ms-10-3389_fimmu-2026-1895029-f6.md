# Collectively, these data demonstrate that D-mannose triggers the accumulation of upstream hexose pho

## Claim
Collectively, these data demonstrate that D-mannose triggers the accumulation of upstream hexose phosphates while broadly suppressing glycolysis, the TCA cycle, and the PPP, thereby reducing NADPH generation and reshaping the carbon metabolic network of inﬂammatory macrophages (Figure 1D).

## Biological Context
D-mannose blocks S-adenosylmethionine generation to suppress macrophage IL-1β expression
