# Interestingly, some inhibitors or negative regulators, such as PII, CTIs, and BADCs, can be activate

## Claim
Interestingly, some inhibitors or negative regulators, such as PII, CTIs, and BADCs, can be activated by core TF WRI1, which is typically regarded as a positive regulator of FA biosynthesis (Baud et al., 2010; Liu et al., 2019b; Ye et al., 2020).
