# Guanosine monophosphate synthetase (GMPS) catalyzes the final step of the purine biosynthesis guanos

## Claim
Guanosine monophosphate synthetase (GMPS) catalyzes the final step of the purine biosynthesis guanosine monophosphate branch.6 In this reaction, glutamine is hydrolyzed, and the resulting ammonia is incorporated into xanthosine monophosphate (XMP) to form GMP.3 Therefore, GMPS plays a crucial role in normal cell proliferation and nucleotide biosynthesis in tumorigenesis.

## Biological Context
Downregulation of GMPS inhibited the proliferation of hepatocellular cancer cells &lt;i&gt;via&lt;/i&gt; the regulation of STAT3/c-Myc pathway
