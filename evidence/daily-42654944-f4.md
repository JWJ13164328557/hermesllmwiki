# ZD5 and ZD8 groups also showed enrichment in the thylakoid membrane and photosynthesis …

## Claim
ZD5 and ZD8 groups also showed enrichment in the thylakoid membrane and photosynthesis terms compared with ZD2; however, unlike ZD1 and ZB1, their enrichment was more focused on the inner thylakoid membrane system, with ZD5 additionally enriching the “photosynthetic membrane” and ZD8 specifically enriching “photosystem II”.
