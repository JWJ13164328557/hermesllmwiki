# PsMYB12 was reported to interact with bHLH and WD40 to activate PsCHS expression, which contributes 

## Claim
PsMYB12 was reported to interact with bHLH and WD40 to activate PsCHS expression, which contributes to the anthocyanin accumulation in petals of Paeonia suffruticosa ‘Qing Hai Hu Yin Bo’ [32].
