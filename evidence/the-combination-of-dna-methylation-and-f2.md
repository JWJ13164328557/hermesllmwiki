# The SG6 member PrMYBa3 interacts with two SG5 (IIIf) bHLHs to synergistically activate the late bios

## Claim
The SG6 member PrMYBa3 interacts with two SG5 (IIIf) bHLHs to synergistically activate the late biosynthetic genes (LBGs) PrDFR and PrANS, which is essential for anthocyanin accumulation in petal blotches.

## Biological Context
The combination of DNA methylation and positive 调控 of 花青素 生物合成 by MYB and bHLH transcription factors contributes to the petal blotch formation in Xibei tree peony
