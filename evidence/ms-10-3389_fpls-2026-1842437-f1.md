# In this experiment, we found that a facilitated process occurs between soybean and tobacco, as evide

## Claim
In this experiment, we found that a facilitated process occurs between soybean and tobacco, as evidenced by tobacco biomass and N uptake being signiﬁcantly greater than that of soybean in all root separation patterns, regardless of the status of inoculation.

## Biological Context
Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi and rhizobium facilitate nitrogen uptake and transfer in soybean/tobacco intercropping system
