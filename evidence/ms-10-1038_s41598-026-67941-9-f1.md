# One of the mechanisms of steroid-induced diabetes is that glucocorticoids down-regulate the expressi

## Claim
One of the mechanisms of steroid-induced diabetes is that glucocorticoids down-regulate the expression of phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase (PEPCK) in adipose tissue, and up- regulate PEPCK in the liver, increasing in blood fatty acids and an increase in insulin resistance in insulin target organs (7-9).

## Biological Context
Glucocorticoids directly inhibit insulin biosynthesis via the glucocorticoid receptor in pancreatic β-cells
