# One of the mechanisms of steroid-induced diabetes is that glucocorticoids down-regulate …

## Claim
One of the mechanisms of steroid-induced diabetes is that glucocorticoids down-regulate the expression of phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase (PEPCK) in adipose tissue, and upregulate PEPCK in the liver, increasing in blood fatty acids and an increase in insulin resistance in insulin target organs (7-9).
