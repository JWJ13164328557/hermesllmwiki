# While the treatments in our study are not replicated (Hurlbert 1984), we provide novel insights into

## Claim
While the treatments in our study are not replicated (Hurlbert 1984), we provide novel insights into how soil microorganisms are affected by wildfires in boreal Fennoscandia that would otherwise have been impossible to capture in laboratory experiments.

## Biological Context
Wildfire and post-fire management reshape soil microbial guilds and carbon dynamics at a boreal forest site in Sweden
