# Interestingly, inducible overexpression of PLT1 or PLT2 is sufficient for the formation of ectopic r

## Claim
Interestingly, inducible overexpression of PLT1 or PLT2 is sufficient for the formation of ectopic root stem cell niches106,107, which indicates that the auxin–PLT/BBM readout provides a mechanism for concentration-based specification and maintenance of root stem cells (BOX 2).

## Biological Context
Plant and animal stem cells: similar yet different.
