# The role of ROS, particularly in signal transduction under drought stress, is well studied in the sh

## Claim
The role of ROS, particularly in signal transduction under drought stress, is well studied in the shoot of plants, where H2O2 has been reported to play a key role in ABA- induced stomatal closure (Singh et al., 2017) and reduce functional efficiency of the photosynthesis system (Cruz de Carvalho, 2008).

## Biological Context
Shaping the root system architecture in plants for adaptation to drought stress.
