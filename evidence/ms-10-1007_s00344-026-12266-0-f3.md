# These results redefine heat tolerance as the capacity to prevent complex regulatory changes and prio

## Claim
These results redefine heat tolerance as the capacity to prevent complex regulatory changes and prioritize metabolic protection, identifying specific ncRNA-mRNA interactions, particularly novel_42/SPL14 and Albumin-2, as promising targets for breeding climate-resilient crops.

## Biological Context
Identification of the Regulatory Networks of coding and non-coding RNAs involved in Heat-Responsive Flowering in Common Beans
