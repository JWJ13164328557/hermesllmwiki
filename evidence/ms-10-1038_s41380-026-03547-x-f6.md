# We found that the cortical alteration patterns of both schizotypy dimensions were spatially related 

## Claim
We found that the cortical alteration patterns of both schizotypy dimensions were spatially related to those seen in schizophrenia, conﬁrming previous ﬁndings of a neuroanatomical continuum across the extended psychosis phenotype [31, 47, 50].
