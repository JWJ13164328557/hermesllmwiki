# Basipetal transport and lateral distribution of auxin are required for stem-cell replenishment, as p

## Claim
Basipetal transport and lateral distribution of auxin are required for stem-cell replenishment, as plants carrying mutations in three members of the PIN-FORMED (PIN) family of auxin- efflux carrier proteins have reduced meristem size [4].
