# The amount of water lost through evapotranspiration was replenished manually using distilled water, 

## Claim
The amount of water lost through evapotranspiration was replenished manually using distilled water, applied slowly to the soil surface until the target weight corresponding to each treatment (40% or 80% pc) was restored.

## Biological Context
Rhizobium spp. strains improve the physiological attributes of Mimosa tenuiflora (Willd.) Poiret seedlings under different water contents
