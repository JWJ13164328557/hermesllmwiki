# As an important second messenger signaling molecule, ceramide plays a pivotal role in the induction 

## Claim
As an important second messenger signaling molecule, ceramide plays a pivotal role in the induction of apoptosis in cancer cells, and ceramide-based therapies have been shown to provide opportunities for designing new approaches to treat cancer (Bizzozero et al., 2014; Morad and Cabot, 2013; Ogretmen, 2018).

## Biological Context
A novel prenylflavonoid with efficacy in cancer therapy by targeting ceramide-orchestrated signaling
