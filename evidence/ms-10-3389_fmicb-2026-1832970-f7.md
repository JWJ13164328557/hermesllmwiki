# For instance, Gram-negative bacteria produce small molecules that interact with cytoplasmic transcri

## Claim
For instance, Gram-negative bacteria produce small molecules that interact with cytoplasmic transcription factors or transmembrane two-component histidine sensor kinases, whereas Gram- positive bacteria typically produce oligopeptides and rely on transmembrane two-component histidine sensor kinases as receptors (Novick and Geisinger, 2008; Papenfort and Bassler, 2016).

Decoding the spatiotemporal heterogeneity of bacterial virulence gene expression using single-cell approaches
