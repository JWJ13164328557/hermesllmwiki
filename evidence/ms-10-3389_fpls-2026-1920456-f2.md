# Collectively, these studies support a working model in which BRs alleviate plastic-particle stress t

## Claim
Collectively, these studies support a working model in which BRs alleviate plastic-particle stress through pollutant-exclusion mechanisms, biochemical repair, and plant-soil stabilization, rather than via direct plastic degradation.

## Biological Context
Brassinosteroids as phytohormonal shields against micro- and nanoplastic stress in plants
