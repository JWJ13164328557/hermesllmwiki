# These results suggest not only that substantially more DAMs were detected in leaves than …

## Claim
These results suggest not only that substantially more DAMs were detected in leaves than in flowers, but also reveal a striking tissue-specific pattern: in leaves, the majority of DAMs were downregulated, whereas in flowers, most DAMs were upregulated.
