# In diterpenoid biosynthesis, the bHLH TF DPF in rice binds to the cis-acting element (N-box) located

## Claim
In diterpenoid biosynthesis, the bHLH TF DPF in rice binds to the cis-acting element (N-box) located in CPS2 and CYP99A2 promoter regions, consequently enhancing the synthesis of diterpenoid toxins [16].
