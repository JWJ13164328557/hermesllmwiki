# Using quantitative real-time PCR (QRT-PCR), we found SCR mRNA levels to be similarly induced 6 and 2

## Claim
Using quantitative real-time PCR (QRT-PCR), we found SCR mRNA levels to be similarly induced 6 and 24 h after Dex treatment in shr-2 pSHR::SHR:GR plants (Figure S1).

## Biological Context
Whole-genome analysis of the SHORT-ROOT developmental pathway in Arabidopsis.
