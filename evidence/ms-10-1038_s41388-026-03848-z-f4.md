# Lentiviral particles were produced by co-transfecting 6 μg of the target plasmid with 3 μg each of p

## Claim
Lentiviral particles were produced by co-transfecting 6 μg of the target plasmid with 3 μg each of pCMV-dR8.2 dvpr (packaging vector, Addgene, #8455) and pCMV- VSV-G (envelope vector, Addgene, #8454), which are components of a lentiviral packaging system, using Lipofectamine 3000 (Thermo Fisher Scientiﬁc, #L3000008) according to the supplier’s protocols.

## Biological Context
LCAL4–FUS cooperation switches on MMP13 and drives osteolytic bone metastasis in breast cancer
