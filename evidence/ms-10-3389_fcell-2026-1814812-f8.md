# Inflammasome-driven pyroptosis is sufficient to induce rapid HMGB1 release independent of de novo tr

## Claim
Inflammasome-driven pyroptosis is sufficient to induce rapid HMGB1 release independent of de novo transcription, positioning pyroptosis as a dominant mechanism of DAMP liberation (Nyström et al., 2013).

## Biological Context
Involvement of the pyroptosis–HMGB1 axis in systemic diseases
