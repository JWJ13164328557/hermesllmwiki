# We show that stable shRNA knockdown of UBE2F in SDH-loss cells can result in tolerance to UBE2F loss

## Claim
We show that stable shRNA knockdown of UBE2F in SDH-loss cells can result in tolerance to UBE2F loss over multiple cell doublings, but growth rate is severely attenuated and NOXA levels (mainly ubiq­ uitylated forms) increase.

## Biological Context
Unbiased CRISPR synthetic lethal screening for genetic vulnerabilities in a succinate dehydrogenase-loss model of paraganglioma.
