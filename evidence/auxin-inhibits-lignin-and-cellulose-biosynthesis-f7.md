# For example, SlARF6 regulates chlorophyll biosynthesis, photosynthesis, sugar accumulation and fruit

## Claim
For example, SlARF6 regulates chlorophyll biosynthesis, photosynthesis, sugar accumulation and fruit development in tomatoes (Yuan et al., 2019); in apples, MdARF5 binds to the promoters of MdACS3a, MdACS1 and MdACO1 to promote ethylene biosynthesis in fruit (Yue et al., 2020).
