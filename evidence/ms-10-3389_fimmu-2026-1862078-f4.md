# Unlike Lys48-linked ubiquitination, which targets proteins for proteasomal degradation, K63-linked p

## Claim
Unlike Lys48-linked ubiquitination, which targets proteins for proteasomal degradation, K63-linked polyubiquitination primarily serves as a scaffold for signal propagation, facilitating the activation of downstream kinases such as transforming growth factor-b-activated kinase1 (TAK1) and the IkB kinase (IKK) complex (15).
