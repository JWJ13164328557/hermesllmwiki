# Notably, within this group, the genus Azotobacter stands out as a Gram-negative, aerobic, free-livin

## Claim
Notably, within this group, the genus Azotobacter stands out as a Gram-negative, aerobic, free-living bacterium of the proteobacteria, renowned for its effective nitrogen fixation and ability to enhance plant stress tolerance by increasing levels of soluble sugars, proteins, and proline while boosting root and shoot growth [18, 19].

## Biological Context
Integrating Azotobacter inoculation with nano-NPK foliar application: a sustainable strategy for enhancing growth, physiology, and sugar yield in sugar beet (Beta vulgaris L.)
