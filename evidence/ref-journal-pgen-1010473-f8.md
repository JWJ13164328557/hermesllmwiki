# Both ENAP1 and ENAP2 interact with MYB44, a transcription factor that interacts with histone deacety

## Claim
Both ENAP1 and ENAP2 interact with MYB44, a transcription factor that interacts with histone deacetylase HDT4.

## Biological Context
MYB44-ENAP1/2 restricts HDT4 to regulate drought tolerance in Arabidopsis.
