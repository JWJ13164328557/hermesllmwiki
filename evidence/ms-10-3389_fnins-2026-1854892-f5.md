# A comprehensive chemical analysis of human mitochondrial tRNAs identiﬁed Q34 in mitochondrial tRNAs 

## Claim
A comprehensive chemical analysis of human mitochondrial tRNAs identiﬁed Q34 in mitochondrial tRNAs for Asp, His, Asn, and Tyr, and showed that QTRT1 and QTRT2 are required for Q34 formation in these mt-tRNAs (Suzuki et al., 2020).
