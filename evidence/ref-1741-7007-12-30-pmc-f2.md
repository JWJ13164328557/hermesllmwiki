# However, we found that transcripts of the putative NuRD components chd4a /Mi-2, hdac1 /HDAC1/2, rbb4

## Claim
However, we found that transcripts of the putative NuRD components chd4a /Mi-2, hdac1 /HDAC1/2, rbb4 /RBB4/7, and mta2 /MTA were specifically co-induced in the blastema during adult and embryonic fin regeneration, and displayed similar spatial and temporal expression patterns.
