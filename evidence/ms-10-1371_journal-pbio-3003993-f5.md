# We found a striking shift in the distribution of areal growth in jaw-D leaves along the proximal–dis

## Claim
We found a striking shift in the distribution of areal growth in jaw-D leaves along the proximal–distal axis, with growth occurring at a more constant rate all the way along the proximal–distal axis.

## Biological Context
Growth-rate coordination across the width of a leaf preserves its flatness
