# In this study, we found that both the BnaTT2 and BnaTT8 genes exhibited lower expression in Bnatts t

## Claim
In this study, we found that both the BnaTT2 and BnaTT8 genes exhibited lower expression in Bnatts than in the WT at 21–35 DAF, which was the most important stage for the activity of TT2 and TT8 (Xie et al., 2020; Zhai et al., 2020).
