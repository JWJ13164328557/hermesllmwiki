# Multicellular animals and plants emerged well after the split of these two lineages during evolution

## Claim
Multicellular animals and plants emerged well after the split of these two lineages during evolution, and thus, these major eukaryotic groups utilize largely independent mechanisms to deal with challenges of multicellularity, such as cell-to-cell communication, development coordination, and tissue patterning.
