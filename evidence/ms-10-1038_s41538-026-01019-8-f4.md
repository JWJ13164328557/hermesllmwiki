# In contrast, MYLIP (myosin regulatory light chain interacting protein; IDOL), an E3 ubiquitin ligase

## Claim
In contrast, MYLIP (myosin regulatory light chain interacting protein; IDOL), an E3 ubiquitin ligase that targets LDLR for degradation, was lower in formula-fed piglets in the pooled small-intestine analysis.

## Biological Context
Lower-cholesterol formula feeding alters the early-life cholesterol-bile acid axis in neonatal piglets
