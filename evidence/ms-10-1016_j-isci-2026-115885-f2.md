# Finally, we show that Sdhb−/− immortalized mouse chromaffin cells (imCCs), but not SDH-expressing ce

## Claim
Finally, we show that Sdhb−/− immortalized mouse chromaffin cells (imCCs), but not SDH-expressing cells, are selectively sensitive to two neddylation inhibitors, MLN4924 (Pevonedistat) and HA- 9104, believed to reduce UBE2F-CRL5 levels.

## Biological Context
Unbiased CRISPR synthetic lethal screening for genetic vulnerabilities in a succinate dehydrogenase-loss model of paraganglioma.
