# From a man­ agement perspective, these results suggest that early pruning—when trees are below appro

## Claim
From a man­ agement perspective, these results suggest that early pruning—when trees are below approximately 8–10 cm in diameter at breast height or 5–8 years of age—represents a strategic window to improve stem form while maintaining overall stand growth potential.

## Biological Context
Early stem and form pruning effects on short-term tree growth and stem quality in hardwood plantations: differing results in Nothofagus dombeyi and Nothofagus alpina in south-central Chile
