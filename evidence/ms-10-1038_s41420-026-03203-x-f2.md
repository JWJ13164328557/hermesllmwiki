# Collectively, these findings demonstrate that QA enhances MVA pathway activity in prostate cancer ce

## Claim
Collectively, these findings demonstrate that QA enhances MVA pathway activity in prostate cancer cells, potentially contributing to tumor metabolic reprogramming through both transcriptional upregulation of rate-limiting enzymes and accumulation of downstream intermediates.

## Biological Context
HAAO‑derived quinolinic acid fuels FDPS‑dependent AR signaling and sensitizes prostate cancer to combination therapy
