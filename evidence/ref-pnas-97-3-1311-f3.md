# Additional support for the idea that lipids are key factors in promoting the early events …

## Claim
Additional support for the idea that lipids are key factors in promoting the early events in the pollen-stigma interaction in Arabidopsis has come from work showing that the exogenous application of unsaturated triacylglycerides is sufficient to promote pollen tube growth on Arabidopsis leaves.
