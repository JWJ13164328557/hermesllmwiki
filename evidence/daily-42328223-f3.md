# These flavonoids have gained attention in preventive dentistry due to their role in …

## Claim
These flavonoids have gained attention in preventive dentistry due to their role in enhancing remineralization and inhibiting collagen degradation, especially in root caries management., PAs interact with the exposed collagen matrix of demineralized dentin, facilitating cross-linking of collagen fibers, which strengthens the dentin-resin interface and resists further enzymatic degradation.
