# Historical accounts of wild oryx also indicate preferential use of inter-dune depressions during dry

## Claim
Historical accounts of wild oryx also indicate preferential use of inter-dune depressions during dry periods (Gillet, 1964, Gillet, 1965; Newby, 1974, Newby, 1978); however, our results suggest that reintroduced populations exploit such habitats primarily during wetter conditions.

## Biological Context
Seasonal trade-offs between resources and human activity shape habitat selection in reintroduced scimitar-horned oryx in the Sahel
