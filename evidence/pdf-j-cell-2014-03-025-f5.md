# Similar to Tsix, we found that Linx was biallelically expressed in >80% of cells but was transcribed

## Claim
Similar to Tsix, we found that Linx was biallelically expressed in >80% of cells but was transcribed at variable levels among cells and between the two alleles in the majority of biallelically ex- pressing cells (Figure S5F).
