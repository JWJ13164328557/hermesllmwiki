# METTL7A interacts with SAPCD2 in HCC cells To further explore how METTL7A regulates PLIN5 and PI3K/A

## Claim
METTL7A interacts with SAPCD2 in HCC cells To further explore how METTL7A regulates PLIN5 and PI3K/AKT signaling, a preliminary MS screening was initially performed as an exploratory approach using control and Flag-METTL7A–expressing HepG2 cells, with candidates cross-referenced against PLIN5 interactors in BioGRID 25.

## Biological Context
METTL7A is a key regulator of hepatic lipid metabolism and nonalcoholic fatty liver disease progression
