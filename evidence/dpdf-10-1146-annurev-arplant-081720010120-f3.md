# A combination of a low count depth, low number of genes, and high number of counts from mitochondria

## Claim
A combination of a low count depth, low number of genes, and high number of counts from mitochondrial or chloroplast genes per barcode can indicate a low-quality cell, while an unexpectedly high number of counts and genes per barcode can indicate a potential mixture of two or more cells (multiplets).
