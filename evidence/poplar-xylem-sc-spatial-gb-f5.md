# Given the complementary nature of these two technologies—scRNA-seq offering …

## Claim
Given the complementary nature of these two technologies—scRNA-seq offering high-resolution transcriptomic profiles but restricted to thin-walled, wall-degradable cells, and snRNA-seq enabling access to a broader range of cell types, including those with thick SCWs—a combined approach is essential to reconstruct the full developmental trajectory of xylem formation [ ].
