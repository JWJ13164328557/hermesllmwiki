# Importantly, while we had to pool data from a limited postnatal age range (P5-7) and subsequent time

## Claim
Importantly, while we had to pool data from a limited postnatal age range (P5-7) and subsequent time in culture (DIV1-2) for logistical reasons, direct comparison of various mobility-related parameters between the different RIBEYE-xFP variants as well as assessment of the individual time in culture did not reveal any systematic differences between the experimental cohorts (Supplementary Figure S1)

Dynamic structural plasticity facilitates the developmental assembly of cochlear inner hair cell ribbon synapses
