## Claim
We further demonstrate that FATTY ACYL-CoA REDUCTASEs (FARs) FAR5 (At3g44550), FAR4 (At3g44540), and FAR1 (At5g22500) are required for the synthesis of C18, C20, and C22 alkyl hydroxycinnamates, respectively.

## Biological Context
Identification of an Arabidopsis fatty alcohol:caffeoyl-Coenzyme A acyltransfera
