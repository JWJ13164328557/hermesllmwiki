# We show that the heterochromatin mark H4K20me3 is more variable than H3K9me3 across normal and malig

## Claim
We show that the heterochromatin mark H4K20me3 is more variable than H3K9me3 across normal and malignant breast tissues, and that H4K20me3-enriched regions are linked to more open chromatin and active transcription.

## Biological Context
Histone H4 lysine 20 tri-methylation safeguards breast cancer lineage fidelity and unfolded protein response
