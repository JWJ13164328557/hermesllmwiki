# Overexpression of lncRNA43234 increased the content of crude protein in seeds, decreased the content

## Claim
Overexpression of lncRNA43234 increased the content of crude protein in seeds, decreased the content of oleic acid, and affected the content of alanine and arginine in free amino acids.
