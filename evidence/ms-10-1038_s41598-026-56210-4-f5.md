# Furthermore, endogenous co-IP assays confirmed that native METTL7A physically interacts with endogen

## Claim
Furthermore, endogenous co-IP assays confirmed that native METTL7A physically interacts with endogenous SAPCD2 under physiological conditions in HepG2 and Huh7 cells (Figure 5D).

## Biological Context
METTL7A is a key regulator of hepatic lipid metabolism and nonalcoholic fatty liver disease progression
