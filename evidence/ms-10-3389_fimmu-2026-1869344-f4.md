# Retaining the biological activity of CTLA-4 is a major advantage of selectively blocking CD28 versus

## Claim
Retaining the biological activity of CTLA-4 is a major advantage of selectively blocking CD28 versus blocking both CD28 and CTLA-4 (26) as is the case for CTLA-4-Ig (Abatacept) which binds to CD86/CD80-expressing antigen-presenting cells (27).

## Biological Context
Treatment with saturating dose of conventional anti-CD28 monoclonal antibody well tolerated in pig acute myocardial infarction model
