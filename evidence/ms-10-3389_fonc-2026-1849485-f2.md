# Conversely, for patients with biological residuum or indeterminate status, salvage hepatectomy is st

## Claim
Conversely, for patients with biological residuum or indeterminate status, salvage hepatectomy is strongly recommended to eliminate occult micrometastases, supplemented by multi-target adjuvant interventions directed against resistance mechanisms (e.g., MCT4, CTLA-4).
