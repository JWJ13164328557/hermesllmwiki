# A primary advantage of bacteriophages is their high specificity for target bacteria, which minimizes

## Claim
A primary advantage of bacteriophages is their high specificity for target bacteria, which minimizes the likelihood of secondary infections, in contrast to antibiotics that may affect both harmful and beneficial bacteria.

## Biological Context
Bacteriophages as emerging-therapeutic agents for overcoming antibiotic resistance in livestock infections
