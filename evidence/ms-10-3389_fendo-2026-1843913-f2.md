# T2DM is typiﬁed by progressive dysfunction of pancreatic b- cells, culminating in attenuated insulin

## Claim
T2DM is typiﬁed by progressive dysfunction of pancreatic b- cells, culminating in attenuated insulin secretion, a decline in b-cell mass (11), and the development of insulin resistance within peripheral target tissues.
