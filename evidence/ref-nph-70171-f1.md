# The CALMO- DULIN BINDING PROTEIN 60G (CBP60g), a transcriptional target of NPR3/4, is required for S

## Claim
The CALMO- DULIN BINDING PROTEIN 60G (CBP60g), a transcriptional target of NPR3/4, is required for SA-mediated defense response, but does not alter DNRR in its mutant form (Tran et al., 2023a).
