# Loss-of-function mutants of SCL30a displayed reduced ABA sensitivity in both root growth …

## Claim
Loss-of-function mutants of SCL30a displayed reduced ABA sensitivity in both root growth and seed germination assays, whereas complementation or overexpression of three stable isoforms of SCL30a ( SCL30a.1, SCL30a.4, and SCL30a.5 ) enhanced ABA responsiveness.
