# Although nitrogen is essential for tomato growth and fruit development, its excessive use has result

## Claim
Although nitrogen is essential for tomato growth and fruit development, its excessive use has resulted in serious environmental and agronomic problems, including nitrate leaching, soil degradation, and reduced microbial diversity (Odegard and Van der Voet, 2014; Suchithra et al., 2022; Agaba et al., 2023).

## Biological Context
Integrated use of chicken manure and seaweed extract increased tomato yield at reduced nitrogen fertilization in semi-arid conditions
