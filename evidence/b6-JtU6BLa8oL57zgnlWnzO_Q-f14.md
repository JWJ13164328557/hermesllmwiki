# Taken together, these findings strongly support the conclusion that phyB inhibits TCP21’s transcript

## Claim
Taken together, these findings strongly support the conclusion that phyB inhibits TCP21’s transcriptional activity, likely by interfering with TCP21’s capacity to bind to the promoters of its target genes.

## Biological Context
TCP21 negatively regulates photomorphogenesis by integrating phyB- and HY5-media
