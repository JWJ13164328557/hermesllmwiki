# Importantly, this Treg-inducing program is operative across multiple mucosal sites, and in the conte

## Claim
Importantly, this Treg-inducing program is operative across multiple mucosal sites, and in the context of nasal mucosal immunity, SCFAs drive the differentiation of peripheral Tregs that, upon recruitment to sinonasal tissue, actively suppress Th2 and Th17 effector responses through IL-10 and TGF-b pro- duction (39, 46–48).
