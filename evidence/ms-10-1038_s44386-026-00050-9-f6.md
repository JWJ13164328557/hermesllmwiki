# In conclusion, a systematic screening of 50 arthropod venoms against varroa mites revealed that acar

## Claim
In conclusion, a systematic screening of 50 arthropod venoms against varroa mites revealed that acaricidal/varroacidal activity is abundant among arachnid venoms.

## Biological Context
Spider venom peptides Ht1a and Gg1a are toxic to honeybee parasite <i>Varroa destructor</i> by topical application.
