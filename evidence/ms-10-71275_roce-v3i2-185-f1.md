# Proper nursery practices and nutrition management have been shown to strongly influence the quality 

## Claim
Proper nursery practices and nutrition management have been shown to strongly influence the quality and growth of oil palm seedlings, which are critical for sustainable production (Pratiwi et al., 2026; Gusmawartati et al., 2025).
