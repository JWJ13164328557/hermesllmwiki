# The core hypothesis asserts the following: the value of any educational element should be evaluated 

## Claim
The core hypothesis asserts the following: the value of any educational element should be evaluated not by the difficulty level or discriminatory power emphasized in classical psychometrics, but by its capacity to elicit a single, stable, and consistently accurate mental response in an extremely high proportion of the target audience (applicable target ≥ 95%; theoretical ideal 99%).
