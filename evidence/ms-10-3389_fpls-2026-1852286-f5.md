# These results suggest that the tetrahedral AC and the helically symmetric body plan are natural cons

## Claim
These results suggest that the tetrahedral AC and the helically symmetric body plan are natural consequences of self-renewing cell divisions, imposed by 3D geometry and/or mechanics.

## Biological Context
3D geometry and mechanics of a single apical stem cell ensure helically symmetric plant body in multicellular models
