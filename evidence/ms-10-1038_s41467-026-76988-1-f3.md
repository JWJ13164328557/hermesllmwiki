# We conclude that arginine 189 is a key residue for both phos- pholipid and acetate binding in STARD7

## Claim
We conclude that arginine 189 is a key residue for both phos- pholipid and acetate binding in STARD7 and propose that its electro- static interaction with acetate diffusing into the binding pocket from the native MS buffer is central to trapping the unusual acetate adduct observed previously.

## Biological Context
Differential lipid selectivity of StARD phospholipid transporters revealed by native MS
