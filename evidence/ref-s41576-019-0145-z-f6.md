# Poly(A) binding protein 1 (PABPN1) binds to the growing poly(A) tail, disrupting the inter- action b

## Claim
Poly(A) binding protein 1 (PABPN1) binds to the growing poly(A) tail, disrupting the inter- action between CPSF and the poly(A) polymerase when the tail is ~250 nucleotides33 and thus controlling poly(A) tail length (reviewed in ref.34).

## Biological Context
Alternative cleavage and polyadenylation in health and disease.
