# Notably, common DEGs (statistically signiﬁcant across all dose metrics) comprised 27.6% of primary D

## Claim
Notably, common DEGs (statistically signiﬁcant across all dose metrics) comprised 27.6% of primary DEGs in vapers, whereas partial DEGs (statistically signiﬁcant for at least one, but not all dose metrics) comprised 28.8% (Table 2; Supplementary Figure 1A).
