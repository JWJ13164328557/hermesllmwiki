# Together, these ﬁndings suggest that while both SPs and glutamatergic modulators ultimately converge

## Claim
Together, these ﬁndings suggest that while both SPs and glutamatergic modulators ultimately converge on increased synaptic plasticity, they appear to engage distinct cellular targets and molecular pathways to achieve these effects.

## Biological Context
Time-dependent effects of rapid-acting antidepressants in iPSC-derived neurons from treatment-resistant depression and healthy volunteers
