# Our results suggest that AP2 acts as a transcriptional activator to directly promote expression of M

## Claim
Our results suggest that AP2 acts as a transcriptional activator to directly promote expression of MYBL2, which in turn disrupts formation of MBW complexes to repress PA accumulation.

## Biological Context
The APETALA2–MYBL2 模块 抑制 原花青素 生物合成 by affecting formation of the MBW complex in seeds of Arabidopsis thaliana
