# Collectively, the negative correlation between Lactobacillus abundance and altitude and …

## Claim
Collectively, the negative correlation between Lactobacillus abundance and altitude and diurnal temperature difference, together with the enrichment of Loigolactobacillus and Latilactobacillus in high-altitude samples, indicates that altitude and thermal variability act as environmental filters shaping silage microbial community structure on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau.
