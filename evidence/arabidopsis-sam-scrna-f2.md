# As expected, we found that the mutations in AT5G22580 and AT1G25230, which encode an A/B barrel doma

## Claim
As expected, we found that the mutations in AT5G22580 and AT1G25230, which encode an A/B barrel domain-containing protein and a calcineurin-like metallo-phosphoesterase family protein, respectively, exhibited the same large branch angle phenotype as the sgr6 mutant (Figures 3J and S2H).

## Biological Context
A single-cell analysis of the Arabidopsis vegetative shoot apex.
