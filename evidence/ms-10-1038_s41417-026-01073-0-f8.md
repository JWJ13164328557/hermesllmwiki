# CD28-based CAR- T cells appear to be particularly effective at achieving rapid tumor control in pati

## Claim
CD28-based CAR- T cells appear to be particularly effective at achieving rapid tumor control in patients with bulky or rapidly growing tumors, while 4- 1BB-based CAR-T therapies are likely to be most effective in providing a response for patients requiring maintenance approaches to target MRD.

## Biological Context
Investigating the role of costimulatory domains in enhancing CAR-T cell persistence in TNBC
