# Abscisic acid (ABA) plays a central role in drought response by regulating stomatal closure and prom

## Claim
Abscisic acid (ABA) plays a central role in drought response by regulating stomatal closure and promoting leaf senescence to reduce transpiration, though this also limits photosynthesis and yield20.

## Biological Context
Enhancing wheat growth and yield under deficit irrigation using stress-tolerant plant-beneficial rhizobacteria
