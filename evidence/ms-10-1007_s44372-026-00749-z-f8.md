# One of the most crucial components of biofertilizer formulation is the carrier material which serves

## Claim
One of the most crucial components of biofertilizer formulation is the carrier material which serves as a medium to support survival of the beneficial Bacillus strains and facilitates their delivery to the target plant or soil environment.

## Biological Context
Biofertilization potential of Bacillus spp. in sustainable agriculture
