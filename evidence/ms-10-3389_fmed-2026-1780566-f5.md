# 6.3 Microbial dysbiosis activates pyroptosis via the TLR4/MyD88/NF-κB pathway The lipopolysaccharide

## Claim
6.3 Microbial dysbiosis activates pyroptosis via the TLR4/MyD88/NF-κB pathway The lipopolysaccharide (LPS) and endotoxins generated by microbial dysbiosis can bind to TLR4, activating the MyD88/NF-κB signaling pathway, which induces NLRP3 inflammasome assembly and IL-1β release (38).
