# ZF1 was capable of growth under both aerobic and anaerobic conditions; however …

## Claim
ZF1 was capable of growth under both aerobic and anaerobic conditions; however, quantitative Fe(III) reduction was significantly higher under anaerobic conditions (p < 0.01), consistent with its metabolic role as a facultative iron-reducing bacterium.
