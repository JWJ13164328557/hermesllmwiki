# Notably, ZF1 was capable of growth under both aerobic and anaerobic conditions; however, quantitativ

## Claim
Notably, ZF1 was capable of growth under both aerobic and anaerobic conditions; however, quantitative Fe(III) reduction was significantly higher under anaerobic conditions (p < 0.01), consistent with its metabolic role as a facultative iron-reducing bacterium.

## Biological Context
Microbial desalination using Advenella faeciporci ZF1 as a novel iron-reducing bacterium isolated from oil refinery sludge
