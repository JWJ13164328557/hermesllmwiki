# Strikingly, we found that compared to WT, CA-ROP2 maintained elevated expression of growth- and deve

## Claim
Strikingly, we found that compared to WT, CA-ROP2 maintained elevated expression of growth- and development-related genes in the early phase of N starvation, yet later drove a pronounced shift toward the upregulation of senescence-associated genes.

## Biological Context
Active ROP2 triggers leaf senescence and orchestrates the growth-senescence trade-off under nitrogen starvation
