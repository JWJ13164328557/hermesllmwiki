# Using preclinical SCLC models and patient-derived samples, we show that XPO1 drives TRIM21-dependent

## Claim
Using preclinical SCLC models and patient-derived samples, we show that XPO1 drives TRIM21-dependent IRF3 degradation, suppresses TNFSF15 expression and promotes the accumulation of immunosuppressive TREM2+ macrophages, thereby reducing MHC class I expression and CD8+ T-cell inﬁltration.

## Biological Context
XPO1-mediated TRIM21 nuclear export reprograms TREM2+ macrophage polarization by targeting IRF3 to augment anti-PD-1 efficacy in small cell lung cancer
