# Interestingly, post-necroptotic cells (so- called ‘zombie’ cells) can continue to release cytokines 

## Claim
Interestingly, post-necroptotic cells (so- called ‘zombie’ cells) can continue to release cytokines and DAMPs long after membrane rupture, prolonging the inflammatory consequences of necroptotic demise (Orozco et al., 2019).

## Biological Context
Leveraging ZBP1 signaling for cancer immunotherapy
