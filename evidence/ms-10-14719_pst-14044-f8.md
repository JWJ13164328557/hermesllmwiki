# Proper optimisation of spray pressure, nozzle type and drying conditions is essential to avoid uneve

## Claim
Proper optimisation of spray pressure, nozzle type and drying conditions is essential to avoid uneven coating, wax streaking or excessive restriction of gas exchange, which could otherwise lead to physiological disorders during storage (25, 52).
