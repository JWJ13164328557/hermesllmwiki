# Consequently, it is essential to develop applied treatments that contribute to improving growth effi

## Claim
Consequently, it is essential to develop applied treatments that contribute to improving growth efficiency and enhancing production sustainability, among which is foliar spraying with plant growth regulators, including thidiazuron (TDZ), which is considered one of the synthetic cytokinins belonging to the phenylurea group.

## Biological Context
Potential of thidiazuron and arginine foliar spray to enhance vegetative growth and chemical composition of Washington navel orange saplings
