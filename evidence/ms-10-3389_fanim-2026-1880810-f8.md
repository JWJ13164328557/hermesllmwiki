# Mycotoxins also interact with the gut microbiota and can shift microbial commu- nities toward more p

## Claim
Mycotoxins also interact with the gut microbiota and can shift microbial commu- nities toward more pro-inﬂammatory or mucin-degrading taxa and can reduce SCFA production, which may weaken the epithelial barrier (Mafe and Büsselberg, 2026).
