# The retino­ geniculate system is divided into discrete magnocellular, parvocellular and koniocellula

## Claim
The retino­ geniculate system is divided into discrete magnocellular, parvocellular and koniocellular divisions, which target discrete layers of the lateral geniculate nucleus (Martinez et al., 2024; Solomon, 2021).
