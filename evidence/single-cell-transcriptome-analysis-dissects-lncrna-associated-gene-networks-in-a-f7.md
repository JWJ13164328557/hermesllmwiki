# In total, we identified 184 representative regulons governed by 181 141 distinct TFs across all cell

## Claim
In total, we identified 184 representative regulons governed by 181 141 distinct TFs across all cell types (Figure 4A and Supplementary Figure 5), whereby regulons consisting 182 of TFs and their target genes (including PCs and lncRNAs) were visualized as integrated networks 183 (consisting of multiple regulons) in a cell-type specific manner (Supplementary Figure 6).
