# In Cucumis sativus, CsCHYR1 positively regulates drought adaptability through the ABA …

## Claim
In Cucumis sativus, CsCHYR1 positively regulates drought adaptability through the ABA signaling pathway, which can interact with protein kinase CsSnRK2.6 and mediate the ubiquitination and degradation of transcription factor CShTAF1, thereby promoting stomatal closure to enhance drought resistance [ ].
