# Overexpression of PagCRY1 substantially inhibited plant growth and promoted secondary xylem developm

## Claim
Overexpression of PagCRY1 substantially inhibited plant growth and promoted secondary xylem development in Populus, while CRISPR/Cas9‐ mediated knockout of PagCRY1 enhanced plant growth and delayed secondary xylem development.
