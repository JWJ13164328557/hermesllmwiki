# Interestingly, the effects of high-quality rhizobia are not just due to increased nitrogen availabil

## Claim
Interestingly, the effects of high-quality rhizobia are not just due to increased nitrogen availability as synthetic fertilizers do not benefit herbivore survival and growth to the same extent.

## Biological Context
Less cooperative belowground plant mutualists negatively affect aboveground herbivore growth and survival
