# In hepatocellular carcinoma, targeting the CAF-derived factor MFAP5 significantly suppresses angioge

## Claim
In hepatocellular carcinoma, targeting the CAF-derived factor MFAP5 significantly suppresses angiogenesis and reduces tumor burden by 80%, suggesting that CAF-associated vascular abnormalities are also a key component of resistance (Schneider et al., 2024).
