# As the primary tissue responsible for nutrient and water absorption, the root tip interacts with bio

## Claim
As the primary tissue responsible for nutrient and water absorption, the root tip interacts with biotic and abiotic microenvironments to regulate overall plant physiology, largely through root hair cells (Gilroy Jones, 2000 ).

## Biological Context
Single-cell multiome reveals root hair-specific responses to salt stress.
