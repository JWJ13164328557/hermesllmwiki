# We found highly enriched expression of ZmYAB14 and other YAB genes (Strable et al., 2017) in the FAC

## Claim
We found highly enriched expression of ZmYAB14 and other YAB genes (Strable et al., 2017) in the FACS-sorted cells, while negative control markers such as KN1 (Jackson et al., 1994) were signiﬁcantly depleted (Figure 2O), as expected.
