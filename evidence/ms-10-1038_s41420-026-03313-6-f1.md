# Strikingly, we show that GCS inhibition resulted in stalled autophagy marked by an increase in LC3-I

## Claim
Strikingly, we show that GCS inhibition resulted in stalled autophagy marked by an increase in LC3-II-positive autolysosomes and an accumulation of autophagolysosome-localized cellular products including ER-associated membranes, p62-positive cargo, and cholesterol.

## Biological Context
Targeting glucosylceramide generation induces cancer cell death through lysosomal dysfunction independent of ceramide accumulation
