# In adults, Rn knockdown resulted in disrupted ovarian development, hindered oocyte …

## Claim
In adults, Rn knockdown resulted in disrupted ovarian development, hindered oocyte maturation, and reduced expression of vitellogenin gene, mainly VgA and VgB, indicating that Rn is crucial for ovarian and wing development in locusts [33].
