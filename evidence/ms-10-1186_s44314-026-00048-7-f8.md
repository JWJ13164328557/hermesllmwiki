# In adults, Rn knockdown resulted in disrupted ovarian development, hindered oocyte maturation, and r

## Claim
In adults, Rn knockdown resulted in disrupted ovarian development, hindered oocyte maturation, and reduced expression of vitello- genin gene, mainly VgA and VgB, indicating that Rn is crucial for ovarian and wing development in locusts [33].
