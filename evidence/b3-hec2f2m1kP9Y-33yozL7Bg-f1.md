# Chromatin immunoprecipitation sequencing (ChIPseq) and transcriptome analyses revealed …

## Claim
Chromatin immunoprecipitation sequencing (ChIPseq) and transcriptome analyses revealed that SlGRF1 binds to the promoters of genes encoding SAM regulators, namely No Apical Meristem 1 (NAM1), EPIDERMAL PATTERNING FACTOR 4 (EPF4), and ERECTAlike 2 (ER2) (Souer et al., 1996; Uchida et al., 2012), with dualluciferase and ChIPqPCR analyses confirming direct activation.
