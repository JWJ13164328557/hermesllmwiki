# TIM-4, like other TIM family members, specifically binds to PS exposed on the surface of apoptotic c

## Claim
TIM-4, like other TIM family members, specifically binds to PS exposed on the surface of apoptotic cells and mediates their phagocytosis (Liu et al., 2020; Miyanishi et al., 2007).

## Biological Context
Multi-omics integration reveals TIM-4 as a master regulatory hub of neuroinflammation and neuronal cell death
