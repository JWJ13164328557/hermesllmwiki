# To reduce false positives, computational predictions were rigorously filtered by expression correlat

## Claim
To reduce false positives, computational predictions were rigorously filtered by expression correlation analysis; only miRNA– target pairs exhibiting significant anti-correlation (Pearson correlation |r| > 0.9, p < 0.01) were retained.

## Biological Context
Identification of the Regulatory Networks of coding and non-coding RNAs involved in Heat-Responsive Flowering in Common Beans
