# In HD, the mHTT pro­ tein fails to interact with REST/NRSF, leading to nuclear accumulation of REST/

## Claim
In HD, the mHTT pro­ tein fails to interact with REST/NRSF, leading to nuclear accumulation of REST/NRSF and downregulation of neurotrophin expression, such as BDNF [49, 104].
