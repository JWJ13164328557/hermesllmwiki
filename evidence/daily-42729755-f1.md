# BBR reduces the in vivo incorporation of L-[methyl- 14 C]methionine into the C-24 …

## Claim
BBR reduces the in vivo incorporation of L-[methyl- 14 C]methionine into the C-24 position of ergosterol and noncompetitively inhibits microsomal sterol 24-methyltransferase (24-SMT), suggesting that 24-SMT is one of the cellular targets of BBR.
