# The primary cause for the loss of the shoot meristem in zll seedlings appears to be the ac- cumulati

## Claim
The primary cause for the loss of the shoot meristem in zll seedlings appears to be the ac- cumulation of microRNA165/166 (miR165/166) and the consequent downregulation of their target HOMEODOMAIN-LEUCINE ZIPPER III (HD-ZIPIII) messenger RNAs (mRNAs) in the shoot meristem (Figure 1d ) (80).
