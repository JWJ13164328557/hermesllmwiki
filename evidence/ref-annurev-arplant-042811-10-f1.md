# The primary cause for the loss of the shoot meristem in zll seedlings appears to be the …

## Claim
The primary cause for the loss of the shoot meristem in zll seedlings appears to be the accumulation of microRNA165/166 (miR165/166) and the consequent downregulation of their target HOMEODOMAIN-LEUCINE ZIPPER III (HD-ZIPIII) messenger RNAs (mRNAs) in the shoot meristem (Figure 1d ).
