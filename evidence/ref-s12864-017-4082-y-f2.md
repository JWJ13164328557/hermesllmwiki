# In the current study, we found that four pairs of paralogous genes developed through …

## Claim
In the current study, we found that four pairs of paralogous genes developed through segmental duplication, whereas no evidence of tandem duplication was detected for any gene pair, indicating that segmental duplication rather than tandem duplication has played a prominent role in the expansion of the tomato ZHD gene family.
