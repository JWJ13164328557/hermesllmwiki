# Concurrently, this gene cluster has evolved a novel functional gene, OpAVT1, which annotated as a va

## Claim
Concurrently, this gene cluster has evolved a novel functional gene, OpAVT1, which annotated as a vacuolar transporter, mediates the transport of tryptophan from vacuole to the cytosol, thereby regulating camptothecin and other MIAs accumulation.
