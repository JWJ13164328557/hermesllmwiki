# In summary, kefir grains contains a large bacterial and yeast symbiotic community, that may remain r

## Claim
In summary, kefir grains contains a large bacterial and yeast symbiotic community, that may remain relatively stable when properly maintained (Magalhães et al., 2010); however, its composition, as well as the resulting metabolic and volatile- compound profiles, are influenced by the grain’s origin and the processing parameters, among other conditions (González-Orozco et al., 2022).
