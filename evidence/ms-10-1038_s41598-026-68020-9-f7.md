# Notably, grain yield increased by 18–24% compared to uninoculated controls under the same reduced fe

## Claim
Notably, grain yield increased by 18–24% compared to uninoculated controls under the same reduced fertilizer regime, while several key parameters (shoot biomass, chlorophyll content, and grain nutrient uptake) performed comparably to, or better than, the full fertilizer treatment.

## Biological Context
Enhancing wheat growth and yield under deficit irrigation using stress-tolerant plant-beneficial rhizobacteria
