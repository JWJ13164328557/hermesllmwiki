# Surprisingly, aliphatic GS-related MYB28–3 interacted with the indolic GS synthetic protein SOT16– 4

## Claim
Surprisingly, aliphatic GS-related MYB28–3 interacted with the indolic GS synthetic protein SOT16– 4 and CYP83B1, which also could be regulated by MYB34, indicating a possible transcription factor- mediated crosstalk between indolic and aliphatic GSs.
