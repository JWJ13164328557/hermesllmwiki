# Together, these results show that MutLα suppresses TRDMT1i sensitivity independently of MutSα/β, sug

## Claim
Together, these results show that MutLα suppresses TRDMT1i sensitivity independently of MutSα/β, suggesting an MMR-independent function of MutLα whose loss renders cancer cells increasingly dependent on TRDMT1 for survival.

## Biological Context
Suppression of transcription-replication conflicts by sequence-coordinated actions of TRDMT1 and MutLα
