# This frees up the p65/p50 heterodimer to enter the nucleus to activate transcription of target genes

## Claim
This frees up the p65/p50 heterodimer to enter the nucleus to activate transcription of target genes that encode COX-2, iNOS, TNF-α, IL-6, IL-1β, and matrix metalloproteinases (Lawrence, 2009).
