# 2.1 Phosphorylation triggered by Ca2+ signals regulates plant cold responses Under ambient condition

## Claim
2.1 Phosphorylation triggered by Ca2+ signals regulates plant cold responses Under ambient conditions, the concentration of Ca2+ in the cytosol ([Ca2+] cyt) is maintained at a low steady-state level, resulting in low basal expression levels of CBFs and their target genes.

## Biological Context
Post-translational modifications play indispensable roles in cold stress responses of plants
