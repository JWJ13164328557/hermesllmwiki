# These findings suggest that candidate ECMGs may serve as important mediators linking ECM remodelling

## Claim
These findings suggest that candidate ECMGs may serve as important mediators linking ECM remodelling to the onset of DR in diabetic patients, potentially by modulating tissue structure, epithelial barrier integrity, and inflammation-associated signaling pathways.
