## Claim
Studies have shown that flavonoids, phenolics, and alkaloids play a central role in the reduction of metal ions (Cu²⁺ and Ni²⁺) into their respective oxide nanoparticles, while functional groups such as hydroxyl (–OH) and carbonyl (C=O) enhance nucleation and stabilization of the nanostructures [20, 30, 31].

## Biological Context
