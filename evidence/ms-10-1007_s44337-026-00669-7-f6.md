# In lower-grade gliomas, IDH mutations are fundamental in the initial development of cancer, but subs

## Claim
In lower-grade gliomas, IDH mutations are fundamental in the initial development of cancer, but subsequent genetic alterations are required for progression for high-grade gliomas: in particular, 1p/19q codeletion in oligodendrogliomas, or mutations in ATRX, TP53, and TERT promoter [74].

## Biological Context
Isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH) as target for anticancer therapy in brain and gastrointestinal cancers
