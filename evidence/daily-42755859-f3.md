# The original point clouds had an average density of 1900 points m −2, with a range of …

## Claim
The original point clouds had an average density of 1900 points m −2, with a range of 1500-2300 points m −2, and were spatially homogenized to a target density of 1500 points m −2 using a 50-m grid; the resulting effective density after decimation was 1380 points m −2.
