# The superior performance of Zn₄ treatment, highlights the central role of Znc as a cofactor for enzy

## Claim
The superior performance of Zn₄ treatment, highlights the central role of Znc as a cofactor for enzymes involved in auxin synthesis, carbohydrate metabolism and protein synthesis, which are critical for crop production (12, 39).

## Biological Context
Optimising irrigation and zinc management improves wheat yield, quality and resource use-efficiency in the Indo-Gangetic Plains
