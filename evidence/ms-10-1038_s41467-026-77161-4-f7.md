# Together, these data suggest that elevated maternal sEV-miR-29a- 3p disrupts DNA methylation homeost

## Claim
Together, these data suggest that elevated maternal sEV-miR-29a- 3p disrupts DNA methylation homeostasis in the fetal liver, leading to aberrant Pgc-1α expression and premature activation of hepatic gluconeogenesis.

## Biological Context
Maternal obesity programs offspring metabolic dysfunction via small extracellular vesicle-mediated epigenetic remodeling
