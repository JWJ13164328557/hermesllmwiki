# SWEET11 and SWEET12 are marker genes of phloem parenchyma (PP) cells in Arabidopsis …

## Claim
SWEET11 and SWEET12 are marker genes of phloem parenchyma (PP) cells in Arabidopsis leaves, and they encode sugar transporters that efflux sucrose from PP cells into the apoplast as a prerequisite for phloem loading (Chen et al., 2012; Cayla et al., 2019).
