# Importantly, we uncover that RAF265-a compound already approved by the FDA-effectively targets USP10

## Claim
Importantly, we uncover that RAF265-a compound already approved by the FDA-effectively targets USP10, leading to decreased SCD1 levels.

## Biological Context
Targeting USP10/SCD1 axis by RAF265 suppresses lipogenesis and induced ferroptosis in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma
