# Collectively, these ﬁndings indicate that inhibition of the CXCR4–CXCL12 axis effectively restores T

## Claim
Collectively, these ﬁndings indicate that inhibition of the CXCR4–CXCL12 axis effectively restores T-cell inﬁltration into the tumor cell region, further supporting the role of CAF-driven CXCL12 signaling as a clinically relevant mechanism of immune exclusion in PDAC.

## Biological Context
Targeting stroma-mediated T-cell exclusion and functional exhaustion in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma through CXCR4 and PD-1 blockade
