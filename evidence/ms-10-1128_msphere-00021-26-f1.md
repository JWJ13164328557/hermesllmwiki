# Here, we show that tethering of Bdf3 to the EP promoter using a dCas9-Bdf3 fusion protein and a guid

## Claim
Here, we show that tethering of Bdf3 to the EP promoter using a dCas9-Bdf3 fusion protein and a guide RNA is sufficient to increase transcript levels of EP1 in bloodstream parasites, where the gene is normally silenced.

## Biological Context
The bromodomain protein Bdf3 is sufficient to activate expression of a procyclin gene in bloodstream stage African trypanosomes
