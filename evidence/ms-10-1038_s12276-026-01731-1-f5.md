# Finally, we show that inhibition of TfR1 shedding increases TfR1 cell surface expression, causing el

## Claim
Finally, we show that inhibition of TfR1 shedding increases TfR1 cell surface expression, causing elevated Tf and iron uptake, which then promotes higher sensitivity to ferroptosis.

## Biological Context
Transferrin receptor 1 shedding by the pro-inflammatory iRhom–ADAM17 complex and ADAM10 regulates cellular iron uptake and ferroptosis
