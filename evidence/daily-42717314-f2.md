# We show that R-region cells accumulate substantially more floridean starch despite having …

## Claim
We show that R-region cells accumulate substantially more floridean starch despite having lower photosynthetic capacity, and that this phenotype is associated with reduced carbon consumption, altered energy metabolism, enhanced UDP-glucose formation and reduced expression of starch-degradation genes.
