## Claim
Pleiotrophin (PTN), a signaling molecule secreted by astrocytes, binds to its receptors (e.g., Syndecan-3, RPTPβ/Z) and concurrently promotes neuronal survival, dendritic spine formation, and synapse maturation, while also regulating the migration and differentiation of OPCs37.
