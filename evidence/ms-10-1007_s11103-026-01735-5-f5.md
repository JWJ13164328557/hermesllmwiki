# To determine whether NifM proteins could use the NifHH dimers as substrates in the cytosol and still

## Claim
To determine whether NifM proteins could use the NifHH dimers as substrates in the cytosol and still help solubilize NifHH proteins targeted to the mitochondrial matrix, constructs to target NifM proteins to the cytosol were generated.

## Biological Context
Use of translational fusions to express functional Klebsiella oxytoca dinitrogenase reductase in plant mitochondria
