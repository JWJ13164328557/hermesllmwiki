# The non-redundant DE-APA genes identified in this study were compared to these two categories of gen

## Claim
The non-redundant DE-APA genes identified in this study were compared to these two categories of genes, and we found that ~99% of the DE-APA genes belonged to the APA gene group (Fisher’s exact test p < 2.2 × e−16, Figure 1(d)).
