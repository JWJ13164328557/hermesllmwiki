# A threshold amount of local free auxin in the AZ is required to prevent abscission (Kühn et al., 201

## Claim
A threshold amount of local free auxin in the AZ is required to prevent abscission (Kühn et al., 2016; Shi et al., 2017) and auxin transport regulates the local auxin distribution in the AZ region (Basu et al., 2013; Liang et al., 2020; Dong et al., 2021).

## Biological Context
SlBEL11 调控 类黄酮 生物合成, thus fine‐tuning auxin efflux to prevent premature fruit drop in tomato
