# We also provide evidence that PbrNSC positively regulates the expression of PbrMYB132, which encodes

## Claim
We also provide evidence that PbrNSC positively regulates the expression of PbrMYB132, which encodes a protein that can bind to the promoters of genes encoding cellulose synthase (PbrCESA4a/7b/ 8c) and laccase (PbrLAC5) to promote cellulose and lignin biosynthesis in stone cells.

## Biological Context
Auxin 抑制 木质素 and 纤维素 生物合成 in stone cells of pear fruit via the PbrARF13‐PbrNSC‐PbrMYB132 transcriptional regulatory cascade
