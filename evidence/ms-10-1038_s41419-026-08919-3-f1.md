# Additionally, we show that specifically blocking eccDNA generation causes tumor growth inhibition, t

## Claim
Additionally, we show that specifically blocking eccDNA generation causes tumor growth inhibition, thus offering a strong theoretical basis as well as a set of highly prioritized therapeutic targets for the design of personalized CRC therapies.

## Biological Context
EZH2 deficiency suppresses colorectal cancer progression by inhibiting the mismatch repair pathway and consequently reducing extrachromosomal circular DNA formation
