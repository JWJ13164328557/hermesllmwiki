# Importantly, pyroptosis itself is sufficient to drive rapid HMGB1 release, even in the absence of pr

## Claim
Importantly, pyroptosis itself is sufficient to drive rapid HMGB1 release, even in the absence of priming, underscoring lytic death as the dominant determinant of HMGB1 liberation (Volchuk et al., 2020).

## Biological Context
Involvement of the pyroptosis–HMGB1 axis in systemic diseases
