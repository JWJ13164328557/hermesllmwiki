# In summary, the hypocotyl transcriptional programme was pro- gressively activated in response to inc

## Claim
In summary, the hypocotyl transcriptional programme was pro- gressively activated in response to increasing auxin concentrations and displayed a similar pattern between dark- and light-grown hypocotyls.

## Biological Context
The apoplastic pH is a key determinant in the hypocotyl growth response to auxin
