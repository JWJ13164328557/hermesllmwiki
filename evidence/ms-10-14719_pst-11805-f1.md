# Our findings suggest that further studies on the use of NAA in sago palm ex vitro propagation should

## Claim
Our findings suggest that further studies on the use of NAA in sago palm ex vitro propagation should emphasise varying low concentrations around 1 ppm.

## Biological Context
Low NAA concentration appears effective in promoting roots and shoot in sago palm suckers under ex-vitro propagation
