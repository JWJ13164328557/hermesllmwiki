# Our results show that in both the USA and SSA, the potential profitability of a …

## Claim
Our results show that in both the USA and SSA, the potential profitability of a nitrogen-fixing maize crop is greater than that typically observed with regular nitrogen fertilizer additions, with a VCR of 44.3 vs 3.4 for the USA and a VCR of 10.7 vs 2.3 for SSA, respectively.
