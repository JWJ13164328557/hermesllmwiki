# In dorsal root ganglion (DRG) neurons, which are essential for sensory transmission, hyperglycemia-i

## Claim
In dorsal root ganglion (DRG) neurons, which are essential for sensory transmission, hyperglycemia-induced oxidative stress causes mitochondrial membrane potential loss and bioenergetic failure, resulting in axonal degeneration, Schwann cell apoptosis, and impaired nerve conduction[10].

## Biological Context
Single-cell and experimental analyses identify mitochondria-related genes Hmgcs2, Nudt5, and Cpt1c in painful diabetic peripheral neuropathy
