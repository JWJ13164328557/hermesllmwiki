# Overexpression and knockdown experiments of miR-1, together with dual-luciferase reporter assays, de

## Claim
Overexpression and knockdown experiments of miR-1, together with dual-luciferase reporter assays, demonstrated that miR-1 inhibits the mRNA expression of its downstream target ATG14 by binding to the 3’UTR of ATG14.

## Biological Context
MiR-1 mediates autophagy via ATG14 in sheep Leydig cells infected with Brucella melitensis strain BA0711
