# Humic substances, derived from the chemical and biological humification of plant and animal residues

## Claim
Humic substances, derived from the chemical and biological humification of plant and animal residues through microbial metabolism, function as multi-target bios­ timulants that mimic plant hormones, modulate signalling pathways, stimulate plasma- membrane transport systems, and enhance antioxidant enzyme activities, collectively improving root architecture, nutrient availability, stress tolerance,
