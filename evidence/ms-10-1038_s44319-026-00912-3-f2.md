# Taken together, these results suggest that maternal microbiome disruption increases DNA damage in fe

## Claim
Taken together, these results suggest that maternal microbiome disruption increases DNA damage in fetal germ cells, and this accumulated DNA damage may persist into adulthood in offspring, leading to elevated apoptosis and ultimately compromising oocyte maturation and fertilization capacity.

## Biological Context
Maternal microbiome promotes offspring ovarian reserve via bile acid metabolism
