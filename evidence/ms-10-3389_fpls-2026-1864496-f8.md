# In Arabidopsis thaliana, AtCAMTA3 and its partners AtCAMTA1/2 rapidly bind to CGCG-core …

## Claim
In Arabidopsis thaliana, AtCAMTA3 and its partners AtCAMTA1/2 rapidly bind to CGCG-core elements in the promoters of CBF1, CBF2, and ZAT12 upon cold exposure, before the canonical ICE1-CBF pathway is fully activated (Doherty et al., 2009).
