# These results suggest that stereotypic BCR clonotypes detected in the LG following FMT are more like

## Claim
These results suggest that stereotypic BCR clonotypes detected in the LG following FMT are more likely to contain germline BCR sequences, indicating ongoing local generation of stereotypic clonotypes within the LG.

## Biological Context
Gut microbiota transfer from autoimmune dry eye mice imprints stereotypic B cell receptor repertoires in the lacrimal gland and induces disease
