# These results suggest that moderate SA application, particularly as a pre- or post-stress treatment,

## Claim
These results suggest that moderate SA application, particularly as a pre- or post-stress treatment, may serve as an effective and cost-efficient strategy to improve spinach tolerance to salinity, with potential implications for the sustainable cultivation of salt-sensitive crops in saline-prone soils.

## Biological Context
Foliar-applied salicylic acid mitigates NaCl-induced morphological, anatomical, and biochemical damages in Spinacia oleracea
