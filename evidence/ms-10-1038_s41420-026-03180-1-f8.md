# Collectively, these findings confirm that RAF265 functions as a small-molecule inhibitor of USP10 an

## Claim
Collectively, these findings confirm that RAF265 functions as a small-molecule inhibitor of USP10 and effectively blocks the USP10/SCD1 signaling axis.

## Biological Context
Targeting USP10/SCD1 axis by RAF265 suppresses lipogenesis and induced ferroptosis in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma
