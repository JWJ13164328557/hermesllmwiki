# PTI tunes NLR activation via alternative splicing.

## 摘要
This Commentary highlights a newly uncovered layer of plant immune regulation, summarizing how surface immune signals license intracellular NLR receptors through alternative RNA splicing. This mechanism enables rapid activation of NLRs upon effector recognition, revealing that surface immunity not only amplifies defense and actively sets the threshold for intracellular immune triggering.
