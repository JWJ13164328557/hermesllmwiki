# ZmWRKY83 Negatively Regulates Waterlogging Stress in Maize.

- **作者**: Qiaolu Li, Jingwei Yan, Ya Liu

## 摘要
Waterlogging is a major abiotic stress that restricts maize growth and yield. WRKY transcription factors play important roles in plant stress responses; however, the functions of many maize WRKY members in waterlogging tolerance remain poorly understood. In this study, we investigated the role of ZmWRKY83 in maize responses to waterlogging.
