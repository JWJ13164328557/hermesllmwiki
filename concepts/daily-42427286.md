## 摘要
'Candidatus Phytoplasma ziziphi' infection causes severe damage to jujube (Ziziphus jujuba), inducing symptoms such as branch clustering, dwarfism, chlorosis and phyllody, leading to significant economic losses. However, the pathogenic mechanisms underlying jujube witches'-broom (JWB) disease remain largely unknown. In this study, we identified SJP8 as a key secreted effector protein of 'Ca. P. ziziphi'. Overexpression of SJP8 in jujube recapitulated the characteristic JWB-like symptoms, including dwarfism and leaf chlorosis.
