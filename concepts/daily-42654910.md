## 摘要
The flavor variation among different tea plant cultivars significantly influences their economic value. 'Huangjinya' (HJY), a typical light-sensitive etiolated tea cultivar, is prized for its fresh and mellow taste with low bitterness and astringency, and commands a high market value, serving as excellent germplasm for breeding superior-flavor tea varieties.
