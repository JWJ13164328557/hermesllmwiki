## 摘要
Multi-kingdom disease complexes, where fungi, bacteria, and viruses interact synergistically, are increasingly recognized as a threat to bamboo, a fast-growing Poaceae lineage of high ecological and economic value. However, the mechanisms regulating tri-kingdom disease synergy in bamboo remain poorly understood. This review addresses a central question: Through which molecular and ecological pathways do pathogens from three kingdoms cooperatively enhance bamboo disease severity?
