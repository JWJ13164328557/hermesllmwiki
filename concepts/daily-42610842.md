## 摘要
Prunus sibirica possesses substantial ecological and economic value and is recognized as an emerging woody energy crop; however, its regeneration and genetic transformation remain challenging. The present study established an efficient regeneration and Agrobacterium tumefaciens-mediated transformation system for P. sibirica.
