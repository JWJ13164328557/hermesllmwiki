## 摘要
Rice is a major staple crop worldwide, and variation in starch composition is a key determinant of grain quality, end-use functionality, and breeding value. Among starch components, amylose content (AC) plays a central role in defining the physicochemical properties of rice grains and reflects the coordinated regulation of starch biosynthesis during endosperm development. This review provides an integrated analysis of amylose variation in rice from a genetics-physiology-breeding perspective, based on a structured literature search using PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, and Google Scholar covering studies published between 2000 and 2026.
