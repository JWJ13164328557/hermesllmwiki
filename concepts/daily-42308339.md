Here, we use proteomic (LC-MS/MS) analysis of purified mitochondria and chloroplasts from angiosperm species with major differences in mitochondrial gene content (Arabidopsis thaliana and Silene conica). Overall, this analysis illustrates how the complex network of molecular interactions necessary for mitochondrial translation are perturbed by gene loss, transfer, and replacement.
