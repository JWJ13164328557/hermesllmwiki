## 摘要
Petal size is a critical agronomic trait influencing pollination efficiency and yield in field crops. However, the cellular basis and upstream hormonal interplay governing petal size in Brassica napus remain unclear. In this study, utilizing a natural variation population of "Zhongshuang 11", we investigated biological mechanisms underlying petal development through integrated phenotypic screening, paraffin section-based cytological analysis, targeted hormone metabolomics, and transcriptome.
