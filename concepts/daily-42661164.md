# Salt eustress modulates physiological responses and enhances yield, nutritional quality, and antioxidant capacity in sunflower microgreens.

- **PMID**: [42661164](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42661164/)

- **作者**: Bardees Mickky, Reham Shams Eldeen, Mustafa Elnajar

## 摘要
BACKGROUND: Microgreens have gained attention as nutritious superfoods and as model systems for studying plant stress responses. Salt stress can elicit hormetic effects by enhancing plant growth and quality at low concentrations (eustress) while inhibiting performance at higher levels (distress).

METHODS: Sunflower (Helianthus annuus L.) microgreens were hydroponically cultivated in a completely randomized design with three vessel replicates per treatment. Seedlings were grown for ten days using root-dipping technique in five NaCl concentrations (0-100 mM) under controlled natural environmental conditions. Growth parameters, physiological status, stress biomarkers, proximate composition, mineral content, fatty acid profile, and antioxidant capacity were assessed.

RESULTS: Low stress induced by 25 mM NaCl increased shoot fresh yield (58.7%), shoot dry yield (55.4%), shoot length (48.3%), root length (25.7%), leaf area (81.4%), and specific leaf area (19.7%). Mild stress by 50 mM NaCl also improved growth, but its stimulatory effects were less pronounced. Conversely, moderate and severe stress levels (75 and 100 mM) significantly reduced these growth parameters.
