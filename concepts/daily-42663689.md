## 摘要
Agriculture must achieve substantial yield gains while drastically reducing its environmental footprint to meet the food demands of a growing global population by 2050. Current crop losses to pests and pathogens account for 20-40% of annual production, a challenge predominantly addressed by chemical pesticides, which impose high ecological and health costs. RNA interference (RNAi) has emerged as a transformative, species-specific solution, demonstrating high efficacy (> 90%) against susceptible target pests under controlled laboratory conditions, though responsiveness varies considerably across taxa. However, the transition of RNAi to widespread field application has been slow. The first commercial RNAi-based crop trait targeting corn rootworm, MON 87411, was introduced as part of SmartStax PRO
