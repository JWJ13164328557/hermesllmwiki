Drought-driven plant mortality is closely linked to xylem embolism. We tested and advanced the pneumatic method for constructing xylem vulnerability curves (VCs) across contrasting growth forms to improve inference of drought resilience. Percent air discharged (PAD)-Ψ relationships were well captured by sigmoid functions.
