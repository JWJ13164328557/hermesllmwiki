## 摘要
Maize roots suffer severe growth inhibition under combined drought and cold, yet the underlying trigger of this damage remains elusive. To address this, seeds with 5-6 mm radicles were placed on moistened filter paper for stress treatments with or without 1 mM imidazole (IMZ) treatment, and we quantified NADPH oxidase (NOX) activity, reactive oxygen species (ROS), antioxidant enzyme activities, redox status, lipid peroxidation, stress-responsive gene expression, root viability, and root growth.
