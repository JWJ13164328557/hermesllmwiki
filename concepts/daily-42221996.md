High-pressure processing (HPP) is a non-thermal alternative to thermal pasteurization (Ps) for preserving cold-brewed green tea. This study compared HPP (300, 450, and 600 MPa for 5 and 10 min) with Ps for quality, microbial safety, andin vitrodigestive stability. This study compared HPP (300, 450, and 600 MPa for 5 and 10 min) with Ps for quality, microbial safety, and in vitro digestive stability.
