## 摘要
In salinized soils, high salt and pH inhibit iron availability via chelation and ion competition, inducing iron deficiency stress in plants. Compared with traditional iron fertilizers, nano-iron has characteristics like easy absorption and targeted slow release, showing great potential in improving plants' saline-alkali tolerance. Herein, a spherical-like nano-iron (average particle size of 20-50 nm, zeta potential of -25 mV, and with oxide layer) was synthesized.
