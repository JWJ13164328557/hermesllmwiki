For example, zein/Tremella fuciformispolysaccharides gels are dried to prepare the capsule shells (Tian et al., 2021), or pectin/whey protein assembly is spray-dried to fabricate the shell materials of folic acid-loaded core–shell encapsulation system (Assadpour et al., 2017), or solutions of faba bean protein/cellulose nanocrystal are casting-dried into films as edible food packaging material (Rojas-Lema et al., 2021). There are lots of drying methods including hot-air oven drying, contact drying, microwave convective drying, super-heated steam drying, and so on. These drying methods are classified into thermal conduction drying, thermal convection drying, and thermal radiation drying according to the heat transfer ways (Mohammadi-Moghaddam et al., 2024). Conduction drying usually uses sand, salt, or a hot metal surface to directly transfer heat to food through their interface (Bhattacharya, 2014). Convection drying transfers heat to food by hot air, causing water diffusion from the surface of food to the surrounding air by convection and inside food by diffusion, convection, or capillarity (Castro et al., 2018). ## 摘要
The effect of thermal radiation drying (TR) and thermal convection drying (TC) methods on drying kinetics and physicochemical properties of konjac glucomannan/soybean protein isolate (KGM/SPI) gels was comparatively evaluated. Upon heating, the shortened molecular distance and the restricted molecular movement ability promoted hydrogen bonding between components and buried hydrophobic groups on SPI, increasing hydrogen bonds and reducing hydrophobic interactions. Compared with TC drying process, the relative moderate drying rates (reflected by smaller drying rate and effective moisture diffusivity D eff ) during TR drying favored stronger hydrogen bonding and occurrence of smaller SPI aggregates with concentrated size distribution. These contributed to the formation of a denser and more compact network structure, thus improved mechanical properties and increased their water barrier properties (decreased water vapor permeability and increased water contact angle). They help understanding the drying process of polysaccharide/protein assembly and provide a reference for the rational choosing of drying methods.
