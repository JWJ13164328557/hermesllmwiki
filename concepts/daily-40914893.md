## 摘要
Wheat yellow mosaic virus (WYMV) is the main cause of wheat yellow mosaic disease. Although its regulation of protein translation and interactions with host proteins are well studied, independent regulation of the virus genome is poorly understood. This study performed in vitro experiments investigating replication regulation by the 5' and 3' untranslated regions of WYMV RNA2. The results confirm that the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (nuclear inclusion protein b [NIb]) can only recognize and catalyze the synthesis of 3' 190-nt complementary chains in vitro.
