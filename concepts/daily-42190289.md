# The Yin and Yang of plant chromatin remodeling complexes: How they regulate transcriptional output by switching and maintaining chromatin states.

## 摘要
Plant chromatin remodeling complexes (CRCs) play a key role in regulating developmental processes and orchestrating responses to environmental stress through modulating gene expression. The primary functions of CRCs are sliding, eviction, and altering the composition of nucleosomes, which play important roles in the activation and repression of genes. Nevertheless, the molecular mechanisms of how plant CRCs regulate gene expression by switching and maintaining chromatin states are only partially understood. In this review, we summarize recent studies that provide a deeper understanding of the molecular functions of CRCs and their roles in emerging epigenetic pathways that regulate transcriptional output by promoting the switch from facultative heterochromatin to euchromatin and vice versa. Furthermore, we discuss how recent technical advances can contribute to our growing understanding of gene regulation by CRCs.
