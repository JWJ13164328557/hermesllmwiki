Urbanization is an important factor that affects anuran phenotypic characteristics (including morphology), but how it impacts anuran SSD has been explored less widely. We also tested whether these variations in SSD supported Rensch's rule or not. Our results showed that in both sexes, the snout-vent length (SVL) in F. multistriata was higher in urban areas compared with suburban and rural areas.
