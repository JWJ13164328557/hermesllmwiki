## 摘要
BACKGROUND: Rescued stray cats may present with disease or traumatic injury, but early risk assessment is challenging because background information, vaccination history, and the duration of illness or injury are often unavailable. Evidence remains limited regarding presentation-based indicators that can support immediate clinical assessment in stray cats requiring emergency medical care. This study evaluated whether shock index (SI), calculated as the ratio of heart rate to systolic blood pressure, was associated with mortality in stray cats that were presented to a veterinary emergency medical center.

METHODS: The medical records of 63 stray cats that were presented to the Emergency Medical Center of Seoul National University Veterinary Medical Teaching Hospital between March 2021 and November 2024 were retrospectively reviewed. After excluding three cats with missing outcome data, 60 cats were included in the final study population.
