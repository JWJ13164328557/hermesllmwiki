## 摘要
In both natural and greenhouse environments, irradiance fluctuates frequently, differentially impacting leaves at various layers inside crop canopies. It is unclear whether leaf age affects the response of photosynthesis and photoprotection to fluctuations in irradiance, as in previous studies leaf age and other environmental factors were often entangled. Then, steady-state and dynamic photosynthesis were characterized in leaves of different ages within the same plant to isolate the effect of leaf age. We found that leaf expansion progressed as leaf age increased (range: 3-32 days), while age had little impact on steady-state photosynthesis.
