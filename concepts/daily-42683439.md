## 摘要
Horticultural plants synthesize diverse flavonoids (e.g. anthocyanins, proanthocyanidins, and flavonols) that shape key agronomic traits, including flower pigmentation, fruit flavor, stress resilience, and nutritional value. MYB transcription factors (TFs), particularly R2R3-MYBs, serve as core regulators of flavonoid biosynthesis; their functional divergence and distinct regulatory mechanisms drive the interspecific diversity of flavonoid-related traits. This review systematically summarizes the evolutionary and functional divergence of flavonoid-regulatory R2R3-MYBs across monocot (including Poaceae and non-Poaceae) and eudicot horticultural plants.
