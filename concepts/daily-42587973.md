## 摘要
Soybean dregs, one of the main byproducts of traditional soybean processing, are rich in insoluble dietary fiber (IDF), which possesses high mechanical strength and thermal stability. After surface reconstruction treatment, they have the potential to serve as a Pickering emulsion stabilizer, as these properties enable the particles to maintain their structural integrity at the oil-water interface.
