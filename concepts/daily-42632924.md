## 摘要
As sessile organisms, plants must continuously balance growth and stress adaptation in response to fluctuating environments. This balance is largely governed by two evolutionarily conserved kinase systems, the Target of Rapamycin Complex (TORC) and Snf1-Related Kinases (SnRKs). Under favorable conditions, plants adopt a "growth mode" in which sufficient nutrients, energy, and growth-promoting hormones activate TORC to drive anabolic metabolism, cell proliferation, and biomass accumulation.
