## 摘要
Soybean (Glycine max L. Merrill) productivity is strongly influenced by genotype × environment (G × E) interaction, while simultaneous improvement of yield, seed quality, and associated traits remains a major breeding objective. Twenty-two soybean genotypes were evaluated across eight environments representing four locations and two kharif seasons in Maharashtra, India, to identify genotypes combining desirable multi-trait performance, stability, and adaptability. Significant variation was found for all the traits studied, seed yield ranged from 0.26 to 1.68 kg plot
