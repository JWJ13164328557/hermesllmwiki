# CDP-DAG synthases regulate plant growth and broad-spectrum disease resistance.

## 摘要
Phosphatidic acid (PA) functions as a cell membrane component and signaling molecule in plants. PA metabolism has multiple routes, in one of which PA is converted into cytidine diphosphate diacylglycerol (CDP-DAG) by CDP-DAG synthases (CDSs).
