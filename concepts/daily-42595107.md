## 摘要
BACKGROUND: Cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of global morbidity and mortality, with pathogenesis extending beyond hemodynamic derangements to involve chronic, maladaptive immune-metabolic crosstalk. Macrophage functional identity has evolved beyond the M1/M2 paradigm toward a model of high heterogeneity and dynamic plasticity, wherein Triggering Receptor Expressed on Myeloid Cells 2 (TREM2) serves as a pivotal molecular hub.
