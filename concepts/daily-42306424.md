Meanwhile, public TM-1 transcriptomic expression data provided exploratory supporting evidence for the functional inference of some candidate genes. These findings provide new evidence for elucidating the genetic basis of seed vigor in upland cotton and offer valuable gene resources for the development of high-vigor germplasm and related molecular markers.
