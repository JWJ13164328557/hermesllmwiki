## 摘要
Low-temperature stress severely inhibits the growth and development of sweetpotato, restricts its planting geographical range, and frequently causes seedling death and yield reduction, posing a major threat to sweetpotato sustainable production. Nevertheless, the molecular regulatory mechanisms by which sweetpotato perceives and adapts to low-temperature stress have not been fully elucidated and deserve further in-depth investigation.
