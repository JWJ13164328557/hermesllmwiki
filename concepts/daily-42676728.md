## 摘要
INTRODUCTION: Gene expression in plants is inherently dynamic, shifting rapidly during development and in response to environmental stimuli. However, many essential cellular processes require remarkable transcriptomic stability. The identity, genomic features, and evolutionary conservation of stably expressed genes (SEGs) in plants across broad evolutionary scales remain poorly understood.
