## 摘要
Improving seed size and weight is a major breeding goal in mungbean (Vigna radiata (L.) R. Wilczek). Improved genomic resources and precision phenotyping may enable more efficient selection for seed trait improvement. In this study, we integrated a deep learning-based segment anything model phenotyping pipeline with genome-wide association studies (GWAS), comparative mapping, and genomic prediction (GP) to dissect the genetic architecture of seed size, shape, and weight traits in the Iowa mungbean diversity panel. Considering the growing global demand for mungbeans due to their rich nutritional profile, improving seed yield is crucial. Seed size and weight are major contributors to yield, and understanding their genetic architecture is essential to accelerate cultivar development. To precisely phenotype these size‐related traits, deep learning‐based high‐throughput phenotyping is needed to ensure accuracy and reduce labor costs. This research aimed to integrate high‐throughput image‐based phenotyping with genome‐wide association and prediction analyses to better understand the genetic architecture of seed traits. We found that this integrated approach successfully extracted seed dimensional traits that strongly correlate with manually measured seed weight. We identified several candidate genes associated with seed size and weight traits.
