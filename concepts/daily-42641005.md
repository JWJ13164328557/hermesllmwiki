## 摘要
The contact toxicity of thymol, alpha-terpinene, and beta-pinene against Leptinotarsa decemlineata (Say) (Coleoptera: Chrysomelidae) was evaluated by topical application. Thymol exhibited markedly higher insecticidal activity (51.67% mortality at 5%) than the other monoterpenes. Dose-mortality tests showed 100% third-instar larval mortality at 7.5% thymol.
