Keywords:apparent metabolizable energy, chickens, enzymatic hydrolysate gross energy, growth performance, prediction equation In broiler production, feed accounts for the highest proportion of total costs, and the energy and protein ingredients in diets play a major contribution to the feed cost. Corn, soybean meal, and wheat bran are the principal ingredients in diets (Liu et al.2017; Wu et al.2019). It is necessary to precisely evaluate the feed ingredients from different sources and species, which provide reliable data to feed formulations and nutrition research. Several approaches are used to evaluate the feed ingredients and nutrient digestibility of feed for poultry (Noel et al.2021; Barekatain et al.2024). The in vivo method based on animal response to the diets is the best assay to determine the nutritional value of feed ingredients. However, it is time‐consuming, inefficient, and laborious to determine available energy for ingredients using the in vivo method (Zaefarian et al.2021). Therefore, a rapid, reliable, and practical method is important to assess the nutritional value of feed ingredients and to predict available energy. ## 摘要

However, statistical analysis showed that prediction equations of SBM were not significant.
