## 摘要
Accurate pre-harvest mango yield prediction provides valuable insights for improving productivity, reducing food waste, enhancing food security, and supporting the farmer livelihoods. In this respect, nine mango orchards, which had different agricultural practices, were selected to attain the essential data to integrate yield and leaf nutrient variability using an artificial neural network model.
