## 摘要
Heat stress limits raspberry cultivation in tropical and subtropical regions, yet strategies to mitigate its effects on seedlings are scarce. Selenium (Se) and iodine (I) individually enhance plant tolerance to abiotic stress, but whether their combined application produces complementary effects on the physiological responses of heat-stressed raspberry seedlings remains unexplored. This study addressed this gap by evaluating the physiological, biochemical, and photosynthetic responses of 'Heritage' raspberry seedlings to foliar applications of Se and I, alone and in combination, under high-temperature conditions.
