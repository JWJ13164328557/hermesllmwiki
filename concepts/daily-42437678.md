Comparative transcriptomics analysis revealed putative distinct response strategies. K326 may activate a defense response program characterized by strong induction of mitogen‐activated protein kinase (MAPK) and ethylene/jasmonic acid signaling pathways, followed by large‐scale transcriptional reprogramming dominated by MYB and WRKY transcription factors. In contrast, NPB may adopt a steady‐state prioritization strategy, upregulating NAC transcription factors to enhance glutathione‐based detoxification while sustaining the core metabolic processes, including photosynthesis. Collectively, our work provides a systematic framework for understanding the molecular basis of the differential quinclorac response in rice and tobacco. Although the herbicidal properties of quinclorac have been well documented, the mechanistic basis for its selective toxicity between monocots and dicots remains poorly understood (Yang et al.2021). Quinclorac closely resembles the natural auxin indole‐3‐acetic acid (IAA) (McCauley et al.2020; Todd et al.2020). After root uptake, it stimulates 1‐aminocyclopropane‐1‐carboxylic acid (ACC) synthase activity, converting S‐adenosylmethionine (SAM) to ACC. ACC is then transported to the stem, where it induces autocatalytic ethylene production, promoting biosynthesis of ethylene and cyanide (Wang et al.2022). Although ACC‐mediated ethylene synthesis is well established, endogenous cyanide plays a more pivotal role in phytotoxicity, and its production is comparable to that of ethylene.
