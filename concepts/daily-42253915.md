      Smokeless tobacco (SLT) and non-tobacco chewable products such as Gutka, Khaini, and Pan Masalas are widely consumed in India but are rarely assessed for microbial safety, despite the presence of toxin-producing microorganisms that may increase carcinogenic risk.
