## 摘要
Winter rapeseed suffers severe damage under cold stress, which seriously restricts its growth and yield. However, differences among cultivars in leaf starch and soluble sugar, as well as their underlying molecular regulatory mechanisms under low-temperature stress, remain poorly understood. We hypothesized that winter rapeseed cultivars with differences in cold tolerance would vary in starch accumulation, soluble sugar contents, and expression of the starch and sugar metabolism-related genes under cold stress.
