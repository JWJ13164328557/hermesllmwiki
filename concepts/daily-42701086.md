# Emerging regulatory roles of small metabolites in plant development and adaptation.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [42701086](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42701086/)

- **第一作者**: Xuan Xia
- **作者**: Xuan Xia, Alisdair R Fernie, Tao Zhang

## 摘要
Once viewed mainly as metabolic intermediates, small metabolites are increasingly recognized as spatially and temporally regulated signals that coordinate plant development and adaptation. Understanding these metabolite-based regulatory processes could reveal new strategies to improve crop resilience, productivity, and sustainability under changing environmental conditions.
