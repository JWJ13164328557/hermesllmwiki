## 摘要
Several laboratories have shown that even UV-naive model plants can acclimate to relatively low doses of supplemental broad-band UV radiation. On the other hand, responses to supplemental narrow-band 311 nm UV-B radiation (outside of the main absorption of UVR8) was applied to study antioxidants responses that were unlike our previous findings in plants exposed to broad-band UV-B or sunlight. The results suggest that signalling pathways activated by 311 nm radiation are distinct from those stimulated by other wavelengths. In this study we are examining the photoreceptor dependence of photosynthetic, antioxidants and epidermal pigment responses.
