This study aimed to enhance the nutritional and functional properties of fresh pasta by incorporating 12.5% orange or purple carrot flour. Pasta containing orange (O) or purple (P) carrot flour and a conventional pasta (control) were characterized in terms of nutritional composition, sensory profile and microbiological stability.
