## 摘要
INTRODUCTION: Cold stress is a major abiotic threat to apple production. 

METHODS: In the present work, 19 MbWOX family members were identified and characterized at the genome-wide level. Evolutionary analysis, cis-element prediction, transcriptome profiling and real-time quantitative PCR (RT-qPCR) were performed to screen core cold-responsive genes. Overexpression vectors were constructed and transformed into Arabidopsis seedlings for functional verification.
