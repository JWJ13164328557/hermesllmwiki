## 摘要
Wheat infested with Diuraphis noxia (Russian wheat aphid: RWA) respond by inducing a range of defense processes at the morphological, physiological, molecular, and biochemical levels. The physiological response includes the cell wall modification, which is generally weakened by the RWA infestation in the susceptible cultivar, while in the resistant cultivar, the cell wall modification leads to a more robust and strengthened wall. Direct measurements of the cell wall have been a challenge, but in the last decade, extraction methods for cell wall components, i.e., cellulose, hemicellulose and lignin, have been measured accurately. In addition, the Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) and X-ray diffraction (XRD) analyses provide additional physicochemical analysis. The acid and alkaline solutions extractions of the cell wall major components quantify the cellulose, hemicellulose, and lignin changes (in percentages) that occur during RWA-wheat interaction.
