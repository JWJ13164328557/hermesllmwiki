## 摘要
Conventional scanning electron microscopy (SEM) preparation, relying on chemical dehydration, frequently induces structural artifacts such as shrinkage and distortion. While high-pressure freezing (HPF) offers superior preservation, its accessibility is limited by the requirement for specialized equipment. In this study, we evaluated a water freeze-drying (WFD) method using a general-purpose laboratory freeze-dryer for observing bacteria and protists.
