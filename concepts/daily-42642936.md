## 摘要
Drought recovery and submergence confer hypoosmotic stress that disrupts plant cellular homeostasis and compromises growth and survival. Although calcium signaling serves as a central hub for plant adaptation to osmotic perturbations, the molecular mechanisms underlying hypoosmotic responses remain poorly understood.
