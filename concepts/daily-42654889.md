## 摘要
Coastal sandy ecosystems face compounded abiotic stresses, including nutrient-poor soils, strong winds, and salt spray, making vegetation restoration highly challenging. Plant functional traits are key indicators of adaptive strategies under environmental stress, yet systematic studies on leaf, twig, and root functional traits of bamboo stands under different silvicultural regimes in coastal sandy land remain scarce.
