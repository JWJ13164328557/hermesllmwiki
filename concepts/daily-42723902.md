## 摘要
The albino leaves of cold-sensitive tea plants exhibited a marked reduction in chlorophyll content. However, the underlying regulatory mechanism remains elusive. In this study, we determined lower chlorophyll levels in tender leaves of cold-sensitive 'Baiye 1' compared to green leaves during the spring.
