## 摘要
Integrated physiological, biochemical, histochemical, and structural analyses demonstrated that exogenous kinetin effectively alleviates arsenic-induced oxidative damage in Ocimum basilicum. Arsenic (As) contamination poses a severe threat to crop productivity by inducing oxidative stress through excessive reactive oxygen species (ROS) accumulation. This study aimed to investigate the protective role of exogenous kinetin (KN), a synthetic cytokinin, in alleviating As-induced phytotoxicity in Ocimum basilicum L. through a comprehensive morphological, physiological, biochemical, histochemical, and structural approach.
