These results revealed that rancidity is directly linked to temperature and oil unsaturation. The quality of food products is likely to be modified by the biochemical and physical reactions induced by their preservation and processing, making it useful to study these reactions in order to pinpoint the factors that define their quality (Kong & Singh, 2016). Predominantly, one of the chemical reactions of lipids is oxidation (Vieira et al., 2017). This process has detrimental effects on the functionality of lipids, deteriorates their nutritional and sensory quality and ultimately leads to important economic losses (Gharby et al., 2016).
