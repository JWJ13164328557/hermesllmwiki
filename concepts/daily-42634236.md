## 摘要
Soil salinity severely threatens rice production worldwide, and root lignification-mediated apoplastic barrier formation is a key adaptive mechanism for rice salt tolerance. Here, we characterised DIR12, a plasma mnm embrane-localised dirigent-like protein in rice, which is transcriptionally induced by salt stress and highly expressed in root and stem tissues.
