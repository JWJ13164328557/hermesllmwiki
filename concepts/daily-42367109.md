## 摘要
Engineered nanoparticles (ENPs) are increasingly recognized as promising tools for modulating plant stress responses; however, their underlying mechanisms and associated risks remain under debate. This review integrates recent advances showing that ENPs can reprogram plant redox homeostasis through multiple pathways, including direct surface redox activity, nanozyme-like catalysis, ion release, and disruption of organellar electron transport. In addition, ENPs influence membrane physicochemical properties, transcriptional regulation, metabolic fluxes, hormonal crosstalk, epigenetic modifications, and the structure of the plant-associated microbiome.
