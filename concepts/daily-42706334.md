## 摘要
Root system development strongly influences nutrient acquisition, yield formation, and these processes are largely influenced by crop management strategies. Meagre information is available on functional traits of roots that regulate nutrient acquisition and productivity of pigeonpea under organic and natural farming systems in semi-arid regions. This study aimed to quantify the spatial and temporal dynamics of root morphological traits, nutrient acquisition and grain yield of pigeonpea under specific crop management strategies.
