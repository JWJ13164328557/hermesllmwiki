## 摘要
Northeast China's Geng rice (Oryza sativa subsp. japonica) dominates the high-value rice markets in China due to its superior eating quality. However, current evaluation methods rely on either labor-intensive, subjective sensory protocols or low-accuracy, calibration-heavy near-infrared spectroscopy (NIRS), constraining breeding for high eating quality and market development. Here, we report a vision-based deep learning framework combining multi-population fine-tuning with industrial vision-language model (VLM) pre-training for Geng rice eating quality prediction.
