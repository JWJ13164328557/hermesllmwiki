Protein–protein interactions (PPIs) are at the heart of most cellular processes but despite recent progress, their genome-wide analysis remains challenging. With this in mind, we have developed SoPPIs (sequencing of PPIs), a powerful method that facilitates parallelized PPI analyses using an innovative combination of the split-Cre/loxP system and high-throughput DNA sequencing. Sequential recombination of plasmids encodes information about pairs of interacting proteins in recombined DNA, facilitating their easy and cost-efficient identification by next-generation sequencing.
