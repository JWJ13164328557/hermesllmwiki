## 摘要
The productivity of chestnut trees (Castanea sativa Mill.) is strongly affected by high temperatures and reduced water availability, phenomena exacerbated by climate change. However, the physiological effects of heat and drought exposure in this species remain poorly characterised, particularly, in local varieties of national relevance.
