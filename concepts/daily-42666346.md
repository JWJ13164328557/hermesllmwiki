# Environmental stress and plant-plant interactions jointly shape intertidal cordgrass traits across broad spatial scales.

## 摘要
In intertidal ecosystems, plant functional traits are strongly shaped by environmental stress induced by e.g. hydrodynamic disturbance and salinity. Meanwhile, local-scale plant-plant interactions (such as facilitation) can also affect plant traits to a certain extent. However, it remains unclear how these abiotic and biotic factors jointly shape plant traits across broad geographic gradients.
