## 摘要
Cadmium-contaminated rice poses a serious threat to public health, necessitating rapid screening methods. Unfortunately, traditional cadmium detection techniques are often cumbersome and expensive, making them unsuitable for on-site applications. In this study, we developed a graphene field-effect transistor (GFET) biosensor with Deoxyribozyme (DNAzyme) probes in binding mode at the graphene channel. By rationally redesigning a traditional catalytic DNAzyme, we suppressed its autocatalytic cleavage activity while fully retaining its high-specificity binding affinity for Cd
