## 摘要
Citrus tristeza virus (CTV) infection can induce significant changes in growth patterns and cellular differentiation in citrus species, with severe strains causing visible symptoms and damage. In contrast, mild strains may result in asymptomatic infections. The mild strain CTV-T36 was used to build an infectious clone.
