# Imprecise Cas12a/ssODN-Mediated Editing of eIF4E1 Confers Dominant-Negative Resistance to Potato Virus Y in Solanum tuberosum.

## 摘要
Mutants in the eukaryotic initiation factor 4E (eIF4E) gene family provide recessive resistance to plant viruses. Previously, we demonstrated that knocking out SteIF4E1 potato alleles (A, B, B, B1) resulted in limited resistance to a necrogenic PVY isolate (NTN). Expanding on this, we used a non-transgenic method employing Cas12a and a single-stranded oligodeoxynucleotide (ssODN) as a repair template to introduce amino acid changes in SteIF4E1, attempting to mimic the Capsicum PVY resistance allele pvr2
