## 摘要
RNA interference (RNAi) is an effective mechanism for reducing gene expression, therefore enabling the exploration of gene function. Its application in hairy root transient transformation facilitates rapid preliminary evaluation of gene function, specifically tackling the difficulties linked to steady transformation in soybean. The mechanisms of RNAi have been thoroughly investigated in model plants.
