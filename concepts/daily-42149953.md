## 摘要
TALEs (transcription activator-like effectors) are an excellent example of how studying pathogen-host interactions can lead to significant biotechnology inventions. TALEs are bacterial effectors that are translocated into plant cells via a bacterial type III secretion system. Once inside the host cell, they are imported into the nucleus to bind specific promoters and induce expression of target genes, thereby supporting the bacterial infection.
