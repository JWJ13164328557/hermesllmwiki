## 摘要
Field high-temperature and high-humidity (HTH) stress causes soybean seed deterioration, including shrinkage, moldiness, browning, and reduced germination rate, thereby decreasing seed vigor, emergence performance, and commercial value. Clarifying the molecular mechanisms of seed vigor formation under HTH stress is critical for identifying key genes and improving spring soybean seed quality. Plant homeodomain (PHD) proteins are conserved zinc-finger transcription factors involved in chromatin remodeling and stress responses. GmPHD3, a soybean PHD family member, has only been studied via heterologous expression in Arabidopsis; its authentic function in soybean remains unclear. In this study, we functionally characterized GmPHD3 in soybean.
