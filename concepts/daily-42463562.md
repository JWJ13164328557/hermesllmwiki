## 摘要
This article reviews how histone acetyltransferases (HATs) regulate plant responses to salt, drought, temperature, and light/UV-B stresses through histone and non-histone acetylation. Abiotic stresses, including drought, salinity, temperature extremes, and light stress, severely constrain plant growth and crop productivity.
