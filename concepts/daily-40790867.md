## 摘要
Stem rot, caused by Sclerotium rolfsii Sacc., is a devastating soil-borne disease causing up to 80% yield losses in groundnut globally. To dissect the genetic basis of resistance, we evaluated a diverse minicore germplasm panel over 3 years in stem rot sick-field conditions.
