## 摘要
The rapid expansion of fast-growing plantations in subtropical regions is closely linked to silvicultural practices, however, improper practices often lead to soil acidification and reduced nutrient bioavailability. Phosphorus (P), one of the most critical elements for plantation tree growth, shows complex spatial distribution patterns in soil that are influenced by multiple factors, directly affecting plantation productivity.
