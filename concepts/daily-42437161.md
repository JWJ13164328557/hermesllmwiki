## 摘要

Increasing evidence indicates that dysregulation of this pathway promotes the development and progression of hematological malignancies. This includes leukemia, lymphoma, and multiple myeloma. Notably, the biological functions of YAP/TAZ appear to be highly context-dependent, with both oncogenic and tumor-suppressive roles reported in different hematopoietic lineages and tumor microenvironments. We further outline current challenges and future opportunities for translating Hippo-based precision therapies into clinical practice. Despite promising preclinical findings, hematological malignancy-specific clinical evidence remains limited. Future studies are required to validate Hippo-targeted therapeutic strategies and establish clinically actionable biomarkers.
