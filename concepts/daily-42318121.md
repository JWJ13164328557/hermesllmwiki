Among the N treatments, N2 consistently improved both yield and rice quality of two varieties at different altitudes. Optimizing temperature and light resource allocation during key growth stages-particularly increasing the effective accumulated temperature over the entire rice growth period, the diurnal temperature range from heading to maturity stage, the average daily sunshine hours, and the solar radiation—is critical for achieving high yield, high quality, and efficient resource utilization across altitudes.
