# Functional significance of microRNAs in plant regeneration.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [43094348](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43094348/)

- **第一作者**: Ignacio Gomez Zencich
- **作者**: Ignacio Gomez Zencich, Agustin Vasconi, Javier F Palatnik

## 摘要
Plant regeneration involves the activation and reorganization of developmental programs to form new tissues, organs, or somatic embryos. Depending on the regeneration system, this may require differentiated cells to acquire developmental competence in response to wounding and hormonal cues. MicroRNAs (miRNAs) are post-transcriptional regulators that shape developmental and environmental responses and are therefore well positioned to influence these processes. The present review examines current evidence that miRNA-target modules regulate plant regeneration, with emphasis on Arabidopsis. We distinguish functional evidence obtained by manipulating miRNAs or their target interactions in regeneration assays from hypotheses based on target-gene function or developmental expression patterns. Improved understanding of their spatial and temporal activity may help develop more precise strategies to enhance regeneration and transformation in recalcitrant crop genotypes.
