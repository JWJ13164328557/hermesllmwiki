# Knockout of TaCKX2.2 in Wheat Improved Grain Yield by Increasing Grain Number Per Spike and Grain Size Under Field Production Conditions.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant Cell & Environment (2026)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/pce.70676](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.70676)
- **作者**: Zhenzhen Zhu, Fangyao Sun, Guihua Bai, Yanpeng Ding, Fuping Zhang
- **来源**: openalex
- **概念**: Grain yield, Yield (engineering), Agronomy, Production (economics), Mathematics

## 摘要
Knockouts of cytokinin oxidase‐dehydrogenase (TaCKX2.2) homeologs increased grain number per spike, grain size, grain weight per spike, and final yield without negative impacts on other major agronomic traits, revealing a novel approach to improve grain yield by manipulating the TaCKX2.2 gene family in wheat.
