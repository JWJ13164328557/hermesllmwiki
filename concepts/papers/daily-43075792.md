# Integrated m6A epitranscriptomic and metabolomic analysis reveals drought-responsive gene modules and regulators in wheat.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43075792](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43075792/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag877](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag877)
- **第一作者**: Yihan Men
- **作者**: Yihan Men, Chenfei Jia, Chaowu Zeng, Na Li, Quanhao Song, Yuhui Fang, Emel Ozer, Altyn Shayakhmetova

## 摘要
Wheat (Triticum aestivum L.) is a major staple crop worldwide, yet drought stress severely limits its yield and quality. N6-methyladenosine (m6A) plays important roles in plant stress responses, yet its function in wheat drought adaptation remains largely unknown. Here, we integrated methylated RNA immunoprecipitation sequencing (MeRIP-seq) and widely targeted metabolomic analyses to characterize m6A modification dynamics in two wheat accessions with contrasting drought tolerance, L1773 (drought-tolerant) and L98 (drought-sensitive), under drought stress. L1773 exhibited drought-induced global m6A hypermethylation, whereas L98 showed no net change. Drought-responsive genes with differentially m6A-modified were enriched in photosynthesis, stress signaling, and protein homeostasis pathways underpinning the superior drought tolerance of L1773. Weighted gene co-expression network analysis (WGCNA) and two-way orthogonal partial least squares (O2PLS) identified a key co-expression module (MEbrown) associated with lignin biosynthesis. Virus-induced gene silencing (VIGS) assays revealed that two hub genes from this module, TaC3'H1 and TaERF160, negatively regulate drought tolerance, whereas TaNAC108 acts as a positively regulator. Furthermore, silencing the drought-responsive m6A reader gene TaECT3-2A significantly improved seedling survival rate, identifying it as a negative regulator of wheat drought tolerance. Collectively, this study delineates the m6A epitranscriptomic landscape of wheat under drought stress and identifies lignin biosynthesis as a key pathway targeted by m6A-mediated epitranscriptomic regulation, providing new insights into the epitranscriptomic contribution to crop drought adaptation.
