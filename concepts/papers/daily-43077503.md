# Structural basis of plastid Mg2+ transport by the magnesium channel MGT10 in Arabidopsis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America (2026)
- **PMID**: [43077503](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43077503/)
- **DOI**: [10.1073/pnas.2633248123](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2633248123)
- **第一作者**: Chao Wang
- **作者**: Chao Wang, Aiyan Huang, Lingjie Lin, Jinda Ou, Qiqi Zhang, Yingying Li, Chengcheng Liang, Xiumei Xu

## 摘要
Magnesium ions (Mg2+) are vital for photosynthesis, serving as central metal ions in chlorophyll molecules and activating RuBisCO. MGT10 is the only member of the CorA/MRS2 superfamily bound to the chloroplast envelope membrane, where it mediates Mg2+ uptake and homeostasis in chloroplasts. Despite its importance, the mechanism of MGT10-mediated Mg2+ transport remains unclear. Here, we report cryoelectron microscopy structures of Arabidopsis MGT10 in two conformations, along with functional characterizations. MGT10 assembles into a homopentameric structure with a central tunnel connecting the transmembrane and soluble domains. A constriction ring consisting of five conserved arginine residues arranged asymmetrically in the tunnel is crucial for Mg2+ conduction. Our structures reveal an unexpected gating mechanism involving a conformational transition from a symmetric closed state to an asymmetric, putatively open state. In addition, we identified an intramolecular disulfide bond within each soluble domain as a unique structural feature of MGT10. Structural and functional analyses show that this disulfide bond is required for MGT10's structural integrity and for regulating its function in photosynthesis. Taken together, our findings provide insights into Mg2+ transport and regulatory mechanisms of MGT10 in chloroplasts.
