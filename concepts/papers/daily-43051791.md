# Integrated multi-omics analysis reveals the remodeling of phenylpropanoid-flavonoid metabolome in Morus nigra leaves under UV-B elicitation.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.) (2026)
- **PMID**: [43051791](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43051791/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.foodres.2026.121100](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodres.2026.121100)
- **第一作者**: Jiahui Gong
- **作者**: Jiahui Gong, Wei Zhu, Shan Jiang, Wan Zeng, Rehmetulla Aybek, Amr M El-Hawiet, Shaimaa M Sallam, Samah M El Sohafy

## 摘要
Morus nigra leaves are increasingly recognized as a promising functional food ingredient due to their richness in phenolic constituents and associated antioxidant potential. Because this species is naturally distributed in regions with relatively high ultraviolet exposure, understanding how ultraviolet radiation influences its metabolic profile is of particular interest for both plant adaptation and food utilization. In this study, we performed integrated transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic analyses of M. nigra leaves exposed to simulated ultraviolet-B (UV-B) stress. UV-B induced a clear growth-defense trade-off, with coordinated suppression of growth-related genes and proteins and activation of phenylpropanoid- and flavonoid-associated metabolism. Multi-omics integration highlighted major remodeling of secondary metabolic pathways linked to the accumulation of bioactive compounds of nutritional relevance. We also characterized MnPT33 as the first UbiA prenyltransferase catalyzing C-6 prenylation of flavanones. Co-expression of MnPT33 with two upstream genes in Nicotiana benthamiana leaves produced 6-prenylnaringenin at levels approximately 10,000-fold higher than in M. nigra, highlighting its potential for sustainable production of prenylated flavonoids. These findings provide molecular insight into UV-B-driven phytochemical regulation and support controlled UV-B elicitation as a strategy to enhance the functional value of mulberry leaves.
