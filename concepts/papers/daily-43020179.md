# A single-nucleus transcriptomic atlas reveals the endodermal gene regulatory network underlying Arabidopsis stem gravitropism.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of advanced research (2026)
- **PMID**: [43020179](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43020179/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.jare.2026.10.061](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jare.2026.10.061)
- **第一作者**: Yuhan Cho
- **作者**: Yuhan Cho, Sandra Thibivilliers, Ulhas Sopanrao Kadam, Woo Sik Chung, Marc Libault, Jong Chan Hong

## 摘要
INTRODUCTION: Gravitropism redirects plant organ growth by converting gravity perception into asymmetric growth initiated in endodermal statocytes. Although the C2H2-type zinc finger transcription factor SHOOT GRAVITROPISM 5 (SGR5), also known as INDETERMINATE DOMAIN 15 (IDD15), is essential for shoot gravitropism, the transcriptional mechanisms linking gravity perception to directional growth remain unclear.

OBJECTIVES: We aimed to characterize endodermal transcriptional programs associated with early shoot gravitropism and determine how loss of functional SGR5 alters gravity-associated transcriptional-state organization.

METHODS: We generated a time-resolved single-nucleus RNA-sequencing (snRNA-seq) atlas of Arabidopsis inflorescence stems at 0, 20, 40, and 60 min after gravistimulation in wild-type Col-0 and the gravitropism-defective sgr5 mutant. These data were integrated with gene regulatory network (GRN) inference, publicly available SGR5 DNA affinity purification sequencing (DAP-seq) profiles, and transient dual-luciferase reporter assays for selected promoters.

RESULTS: We propose a two-component working model of SGR5-associated transcriptional regulation. The first component comprises DAP-seq-supported, genotype-associated candidate genes, including LAZY1, SGR9, and WDL7, linked to gravity signaling, amyloplast-actin dynamics, auxin-related regulation, and cytoskeletal organization. The second component comprises a distinct growth- and cell wall-associated GRN state marked by three-amino-acid-loop-extension (TALE) homeobox candidate hubs KNOTTED-like from Arabidopsis thaliana 1 (KNAT1), BEL1-like homeodomain 1 (BLH1), and BEL1-like homeodomain 2 (BLH2), together with the basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) factor CRY2-interacting bHLH 2 (CIB2). In sgr5, these programs showed reduced coordination, with reduced representation of gravity- and growth-associated states and increased representation of stress- and metabolism-associated states. Transient dual-luciferase assays showed that SGR5 enhanced WDL7, LAZY1, and SGR9 promoter-reporter activity, supporting SGR5-associated promoter regulation.

CONCLUSION: Our findings support an endodermis-resolved working model in which loss of functional SGR5 is associated with altered gravitropism-related candidate-gene expression and organization of a distinct growth- and cell wall-associated GRN state, with transient reporter assays providing functional support for SGR5-dependent regulation of selected promoters.
