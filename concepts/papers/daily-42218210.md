## 摘要

### 核心发现
1. Our data suggest that palygorskite’s fibrous, high-porosity structure creates an open, well-connected pore network that facilitates colonization, diffusion of dissolved organic compounds, and retention of extracellular enzymes on mineral surfaces.
2. Thus, our results support a mechanistic distinction: fibrous, porous clays tend to function as open, colonizable habitats that facilitate rapid processing of fresh litter, whereas swelling smectites tend to function as denser matrices that restrict access and enhance protection.
3. Taken together, these patterns indicate that high quality alfalfa litter amplifies the positive effect of palygorskite on both C mineralization and microbial biomass, whereas more recalcitrant wheat residues accentuate mineralogical constraints on decomposition and biomass build up.
4. Closing this gap is critical for improving process-based models of SOM turnover and underpins the multifactorial experimental design of this study, which explicitly tests how these factors interact to regulate litter decomposition and carbon fluxes.
