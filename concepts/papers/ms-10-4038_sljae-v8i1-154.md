### 核心发现
1. Conclusions In conclusion, the findings of this study highlight that the effectiveness of iodine biofortification in carrot production is strongly dependent on the application rate, with specific rates demonstrating superior performance in growth, yield, and nutritional quality across the two cropping seasons.
2. The crop is well known for its high content of β-carotene, a precursor of vitamin A, which is crucial for maintaining healthy vision, skin, and immune functions.
3. Notably, the application of 14 kgha-1 iodine consistently produced the most pronounced improvements in key parameters.
