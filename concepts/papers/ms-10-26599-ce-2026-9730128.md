# Recycling of agro-industrial wastes via foliar spray, seed imbibition and seed coating enhances crop plant growth and development

## 论文信息

- **DOI**: [10.26599/ce.2026.9730128](https://doi.org/10.26599/ce.2026.9730128)
- **作者**: Keerthana Yeduguru Reddy, Rooben Reji Punnalathu, Naveen Kumar Yarra, Jeevan R. Singiri, Nurit Novoplansky
- **来源**: crossref

## 摘要

                    Abstract
                    Around one billion tons of agro-industrial byproducts and wastes (AIBWs) are generated worldwide annually, and their improper management causes significant environmental damage. Focusing on AIBWs produced in large quantities annually, we investigated the impact of wheat bran (WB), wine pomace (WP), brewer’s spent grain (BSG), and garlic peels (GP), on the performance of agricultural crops when applied through foliar spray, seed imbibition, and seed coating. We found that all AIBW application methods had beneficial effects on plant growth. Foliar spray of mustard and lettuce highlighted WB and WP extracts as effective treatments for enhancing plant growth and development. Imbibition of mustard seeds with AIBW extracts had a positive effect on various growth parameters, while coating wheat seeds with carboxymethyl cellulose (CMC) supplemented with AIBW substances significantly enhanced post-germination growth. Thus, AIBWs represent a sustainable, ecofriendly, and cost-effective resource that can be applied extensively via various methods to improve crop plant performance while contributing to waste management within the framework of circular economy.
