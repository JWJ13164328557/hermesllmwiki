### 核心发现
1. Soil moisture readings were monitored throughout the stress period, and irrigation was adjusted accordingly to maintain the target moisture levels.
2. Collectively, these in-vitro assays revealed a more pronounced bacterial-mediated improvement in drought tolerance traits in bean compared with tomato.
3. 3.2.3 Analysis of genes expression in response to drought To gain insight into the effects of bacterial treatments following drought stress and subsequent recovery, expression levels of a panel of target genes were assessed by qRT-PCR.
4. These targets represent key components of ABA-dependent and ABA-indepen- dent pathways, including osmolyte accumulation and cellular protection mechanisms (Figure 5B).
5. In NC plants, the expression of all target genes was upregulated under drought stress compared with well-watered conditions.
6. Notably, these treatments did not differ signiﬁcantly from the NPK treatment.
7. Notably, RWC increased by 30.3%, 23.8%, and 23.4% in CONS.NPK50(w-), PPW-26(w-), and BS- 116(w-), respectively, compared with NC(w-) (Figures 3H, I).
