### 核心发现
1. (2001), auxins play a crucial role in achieving adequate rooting rates.
2. These compounds are essential for the formation of adventitious roots (Gaspar; Hoffinger, 1988).
3. Thus, an adequate balance between auxins and carbohydrates is essential for optimal root production, since during the rooting process there is continuous mobilization of starch and soluble sugars toward the basal portion of the cutting (Neto, 2005).
