# CvARF3 and CvARF5 antagonistically regulate ray floret morphogenesis in Chrysanthemum vestitum.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of integrative plant biology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43043063](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43043063/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/jipb.70444](https://doi.org/10.1111/jipb.70444)
- **第一作者**: Ya Pu
- **作者**: Ya Pu, Xinyu Liu, Minling Liao, Aoqin Xu, Xiang Song, Junzhuo Li, Bo Liu, Chenfei Lu

## 摘要
Flower shape is the most important characteristic of higher plants. The morphology of ray florets in chrysanthemum (Chrysanthemum × morifolium) critically determines the diversity of capitulum shapes. However, its regulatory mechanisms remain largely elusive due to the complex genetic background. C. vestitum, a hexaploid species closely related to chrysanthemum within the genus, exhibits two distinct lines characterized by fully flat and tubular phenotypes of ray florets, which serve as an ideal model material for investigating ray floret morphogenesis. Here, we report that N-1-naphthylphthalamic acid (NPA) treatment converted tubular ray florets to the flat type, accompanied by altered expression of AUXIN RESPONSE FACTOR 3 (CvARF3) and CvARF5. Functional validation showed that CvARF5 promoted the formation of tubular ray florets, while CvARF3 contributed to the development of flat ray florets. We further demonstrated divergent expression of petal identity regulators PISTILLATA (CvPI) and SEPALLATA 3 (CvSEP3) between the flat and tubular types. CvARF3 directly repressed the transcription of CvPI and CvSEP3, whereas CvARF5 activated these two genes, with co-factor CvARF6 enhancing this activation. Notably, CvARF3 and CvARF5 antagonistically regulate their transcription through shared AuxRE elements, suggesting potential competition for promoter occupancy. This transcriptional cascade revealed auxin-mediated regulation of morphological differences in ray florets, providing new insights into the regulatory networks that modulate ray floret development and inflorescence architecture.
