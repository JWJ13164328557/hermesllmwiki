### 核心发现
1. In this study, we found that germination treatment contributed the significant increase of nutrients, such as soluble protein, soluble sugar, phenols, flavonoids and vitamins content, especially lysine content.
2. In recent decades, with the rapid development of high-throughput omics technology (e.g., genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metab­ olomics etc.), comprehensive multi-omics analysis has become an important method to analyze the molecular mechanism of target traits (Yang et al., 2015).
3. Taken together, the protein level of proteolytic en­ zymes was increased significantly during quinoa germination, which might result in the degradation of storage globulin proteins and then increased the lysine content.
4. Taken together, germination treatment significantly improved nutrient quality in quinoa, especially lysine content.
5. Furthermore, aspartic acid semialdehyde dehydrogenase is essential for lysine synthesis in addition to aspartate kinase and dihydropyridine dicarboxylic acid synthase.
6. Notably, “metabolic pathways (ko01100, 597/1347/1371/1270/1305/532 proteins),” “biosynthesis of secondary metabolites (ko01110, 360/778/795/732/ 756/330 proteins)”, “photosynthesis (ko00195, 37/52/53/52/53/35 genes)” and ascorbate and aldarate metabolism (ko00053, 25/46/46/45/ 43/29) were significantly enriched in all comparison groups.
7. Notably, there are six screened-genes concentrated in lysine biosynthesis (Table S6): one AK (XP_02172577.1/AUR62028102), four ASADHs (XP_021735305-1/ AUR62017612, XP_021731769.1/AUR62032211, XP_021715314.1/ AUR62043314, XP_021737191.1/AUR62044193) and one DHDPS (XP_0217386501.1/AUR62016767).
