# HvMAPK5-Mediated Phosphorylation of HvWRKY75 Enhances Salt Tolerance in Barley.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant, cell & environment (2026)
- **PMID**: [42993047](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993047/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/pce.71037](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.71037)
- **第一作者**: Huanqiang Guo
- **作者**: Huanqiang Guo, Meini Song, Lirong Yao, Erjing Si, Na Li, Jinfeng Yu, Yaxiong Meng, Chi Song

## 摘要
Soil salinization is a significant constraint on crop productivity, necessitating the elucidation of the molecular mechanisms underlying salt tolerance in cereals such as barley. In this study, a novel regulatory module comprising the ion transporter HvHKT1;2, the transcription factor HvWRKY75, and the mitogen-activated protein kinase HvMAPK5 was identified. HvHKT1;2 localized to the plasma membrane, and its overexpression promoted Na+ efflux at the root apex, reduced Na+ accumulation in shoots, increased the K+/Na+ratio, and enhanced salt tolerance, whereas HvHKT1;2 knockdown produced the opposite phenotype. Furthermore, HvWRKY75 directly bound to the HvHKT1;2 promoter and activated its transcription. Under salt stress, HvMAPK5 interacted with and phosphorylated HvWRKY75 at Ser21, thereby enhancing HvWRKY75 protein stability and transcriptional activity toward HvHKT1;2. Consistently, HvMAPK5 overexpression increased HvWRKY75 protein abundance, upregulated HvHKT1;2 expression, and enhanced salt tolerance, whereas HvMAPK5 knockdown accelerated HvWRKY75 degradation and compromised salt tolerance. Collectively, our findings identify a regulatory module consisting of HvMAPK5, HvWRKY75, and HvHKT1;2 that maintains cellular Na+/K+ homeostasis during salt stress, providing a mechanistic framework for breeding salt-tolerant barley varieties.
