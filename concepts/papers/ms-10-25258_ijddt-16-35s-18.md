### 核心发现
1. Biology and Ecology of Brown Planthopper The brown planthopper is a highly specialized pest whose biology and ecological behavior play a crucial role in its success as a major threat to rice cultivation.
2. Pest management requires balanced fertilization to control infestations because host plant nutrition interacts with pest behavior (Lu et al., 2004; Lu et al., 2020).
3. 2.5 Environmental and Ecological Interactions The brown planthopper displays ecological behavior in rice agroecosystems because it interacts with all living organisms which include its host plants and their natural predators and the microbial organisms in the area (Heong et al., 2015; Horgan, 2020).
4. The rice plant resistance traits which differ between rice varieties determine how brown planthopper interacts with rice plants (Heinrichs, 1994; Horgan, 2020).
5. Role of Inorganic Nutrients in Pest and Disease Dynamics The interaction between plant nutrition and pest dynamics plays a critical role in determining crop health and productivity.
6. Potassium plays a crucial role in multiple biological functions of organisms that include activating enzymes and controlling water balance and sustaining cellular pressure (Marschner, 2012).
7. The presence of nutrient imbalances will interrupt how plants interact with beneficial soil microorganisms, which results in more plant health problems (Vessey, 2003; Chen et al., 2020).
8. Understanding its classification, physical traits, life cycle, and interaction with the environment is essential for developing effective and sustainable management strategies.
