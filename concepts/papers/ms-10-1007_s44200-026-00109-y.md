### 核心发现
1. Arterial stiffness, a main component of vascular ageing, is now recognised as a key player in both promoting CVD directly but also medi­ ating the progression of target organ damage.
2. Indeed, numerous studies have demonstrated that arterial stiff­ ness (e.g., determined via carotid to femoral pulse wave velocity, PWV) adds value to risk prediction beyond traditional cardiovascular risk factors including brachial blood pressure [4, 5, 12–18], and early vascular ageing has been highlighted as a target of therapeutic interven­ tions [19].
3. However, it is not pos­ sible to identify from our survey what the lack of knowl­ edge relates to (the overall concept of vascular ageing, which population to target or how to use various devices, etc.), as this was not asked.
4. Identifying individuals at high risk of developing CVD before it becomes apparent is essential to prevent disease progression and the asso­ ciated health and economic burden.
5. Importantly, even carotid-femoral PWV – the measure with the strongest prognostic evidence – lacks full standardisation across manufacturers.
