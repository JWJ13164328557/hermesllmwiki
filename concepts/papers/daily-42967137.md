# NtMYB1 and NtbZIP53 antagonistically regulate NtGA3ox2 and NtGA2ox8 mediating diverse GA biosyntheses and tiny-plant in Narcissus tazetta.

## 摘要
Miniaturization and multiple branching are characteristic features of horticultural plants. Gibberellins participate in regulating the size and branching of plant architecture. Gibberellin 3β hydroxylase (GA3ox) and gibberellin 2β hydroxylase (GA2ox) are a pair of key enzymes for the homeostasis of active GA. However, the transcriptional regulation of GA3ox and GA2ox and the synthesis of active GAs remain unknown. Here the roles of narcissus NtMYB1 and NtbZIP53 in regulating the biosynthesis of diverse active GAs were addressed. Overexpression of NtMYB1 in Arabidopsis and Narcissus shows a tiny-type phenotype. The NtMYB1 co-expression network reveals that NtMYB1 or NtbZIP53 is closely related to NtKS1, NtGA20ox, NtGA2ox, and NtGA3ox in the GA biosynthetic pathway of narcissus. NtMYB1 can interact with NtbZIP53. Overexpression of NtMYB1 and NtMYB1/NtbZIP53 in transgenic narcissus upregulated the expression of upstream genes NtKS1 and NtGA20ox in the GA biosynthesis pathway; however, NtMYB1 and/or NtbZIP53 directly targeted the promoters of NtGA3ox2 and NtGA2ox8 and antagonistically controlled NtGA3ox2 and NtGA3ox8 transcription, respectively, and both decreased GA1 accumulation. NtMYB1 recruiting NtbZIP53 enhanced the repression of NtGA3ox2 and the activation of NtGA2ox8, reducing the GA1/GA3 ratio and plant size in narcissus. This provides a conceptual basis for controlling the architecture of Chinese narcissus.

### 核心发现
1. Collectively, an NtMYB1–NtNCED1/2 module plays a crucial role in controlling ABA biosynthesis and the accumulation of β-carotene and ABA.
2. Overexpression of NtMYB1 or NtbZIP53 in transgenic lines of Arabidopsis and narcissus led to upregulation of the gene expression of upstream biosynthetic enzymes of the GA biosynthesis pathway, while oeNtMYB1 or oeNtbZIP53 antagonistically controlled GA3ox2 and GA2ox8 transcription, and NtMYB1 recruiting NtbZIP53 enhanced the repression of GA3ox2 by NtMYB1 and the activation of GA2ox8 by NtbZIP53,
3. Overexpression of NtMYB1 and NtMYB1 / NtbZIP53 in transgenic narcissus upregulated the expression of upstream genes NtKS1 and NtGA20ox in the GA biosynthesis pathway; however, NtMYB1 and/or NtbZIP53 directly targeted the promoters of NtGA3ox2 and NtGA2ox8 and antagonistically controlled NtGA3ox2 and NtGA3ox8 transcription, respectively, and both decreased GA 1 accumulation.
4. DELLA proteins activate the expression of AtGA20ox2 and AtGA3ox1 genes, but there has been no direct evidence showing that DELLA proteins bind to the AtGA20ox2 and AtGA3ox1 promoter.
5. Overexpression of YAB1 resulted in a semi-dwarf phenotype with decreased OsGA3ox2 transcript levels, whereas co-suppression of YAB1 in transgenic plants induced expression of OsGA3ox2.
6. YAB1 binds to the GA-responsive element (GARE) located in the GA3ox2 promoter, indicating that YAB1 regulates OsGA3ox2 directly [ ].
7. RSG, a bZIP transcription factor, binds to this sequence of the NtGA20ox1 promoter in vivo in response to a decrease in GA levels, and the binding is abolished by exogenous GA treatments [, ].
8. The tiny-plant phenotype of the oeNtMYB1 narcissus bulb and Arabidopsis partially recovered after 4 weeks of growth (; ), indicating that overexpression of NtMYB1 reduced the GA 1 level, resulting in a mini-bulb, GA-deficient like dwarfism plant phenotype, which can be partially rescued by exogenous GA 3 application.
