# Unlocking yield potential: TaDL-B.4-driven grain weight improvement in post-Green Revolution wheat.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of integrative plant biology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43049274](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43049274/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/jipb.70433](https://doi.org/10.1111/jipb.70433)
- **第一作者**: Dongxue Zhang
- **作者**: Dongxue Zhang, Jie Liu, Dejie Du, Sen Li, Ziyan Wu, Huanhuan Zhao, Jiazheng Yu, Yunjie Liu

## 摘要
DELLA proteins repress responses to gibberellins (GAs). The semi-dwarf plant architecture of modern wheat varieties, a hallmark of the Green Revolution, is caused by the Reduced height-B1b (Rht-B1b) and Rht-D1b alleles. Both alleles encode DELLA proteins that repress plant growth and negatively impact grain development, thereby limiting further yield improvements. Here, we demonstrate that Rht-B1b inhibits grain development by suppressing the activity of a specific isoform of the transcription factor DL, DL-B.4. NEK6‑mediated DL-B.4 phosphorylation neutralizes the inhibitory effect of Rht-B1b, thereby upregulating GA20ox3 expression and GA intermediates in grain. Overexpression and natural variants of DL-B.4 mitigate the adverse effects of Rht-B1b on grain weight without influencing plant height, confirming it as a critical regulatory node. This insight will facilitate further improvements in wheat yield using these modern varieties.
