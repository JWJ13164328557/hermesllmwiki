# Seg3 mutants in barley (Hordeum vulgare L.) show accelerated germination.

- **PMID**: [43080962](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43080962/)

## 摘要
Shrunken endosperm (seg) mutations in barley have been previously characterized for their effects on grain development, resulting in reduced endosperm volume and decreased yield by weight. However, their impact on germination remains poorly understood. As the endosperm serves as the primary energy source during early germination, reductions in storage tissue would be expected to constrain seedling establishment. Here, we investigated the effects of the seg3 mutation on germination morphology and early seedling development. Despite a substantial reduction in endosperm volume, seg3 mutant grains exhibited an increased germination index compared to wild-type controls. Furthermore, microcomputed tomography (µCT) analysis revealed distinct differences in root system architecture, indicating altered patterns of early growth. These findings demonstrate that reduced endosperm volume in seg3 mutants does not limit germination performance but is associated instead with accelerated germination and modified root development.
