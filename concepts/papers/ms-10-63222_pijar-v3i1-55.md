### 核心发现
1. Our data reveal that raw rice husk (M0) yielded an unsustainably low mean survival rate (33.33%).
2. Ultimately, this study demonstrates that combining a highly moisture-retentive substrate with a 200 ppm IBA application effectively resolves the survival-growth trade-off.
3. By establishing that combining a highly retentive matrix with a brief, targeted exposure to 200 ppm IBA forces rapid root organogenesis, subsequently driving canopy expansion and maximum biomass accumulation, this study provides a highly reproducible protocol.
4. Initially, transcriptomic analyses are necessary to elucidate the precise molecular mechanisms by which the 200 ppm IBA optimum induces rapid root organogenesis, and to identify the specific pathways mediating phytotoxicity at elevated concentrations.
5. Finally, rigorous field trials are essential to validate this methodology.
