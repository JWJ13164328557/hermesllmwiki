## 1. Scientific Context

Cotton improvement remains a major challenge in plant biotechnology because stable yield, fiber quality, and stress resilience must be delivered from complex allopolyploid genomes under variable environmental conditions. Genetic mapping has progressively transformed this challenge into deployable breeding knowledge, advancing from sparse marker systems to high-density SNP arrays, reference genomes, and, more recently, structural-variation-aware pan-genomes and graph genomes.
