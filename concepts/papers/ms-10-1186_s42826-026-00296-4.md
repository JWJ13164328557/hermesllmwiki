### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these findings suggest that adipocyte-specific MC2R overexpression is insufficient to protect mice from diet-induced obesity.
2. Laboratory Animal Research (2026) 42:34 Conclusions In summary, this study demonstrates that AAV-mediated adipocyte-specific MC2R overexpression produces only modest molecular changes in adipose tissues and does not confer clear metabolic benefits under basal, obeso­ genic, cold stress, or ACTH-stimulated conditions.
3. Results Our findings show that under basal, high-fat diet (HFD), and stress-induced conditions, MC2R overexpression in adipocytes has minimal effects on thermogenic and lipolytic markers and is insufficient to promote weight reduction in mice.
4. Moreover, adipocyte-specific overexpression of MC2R failed to sequester the ACTH and decrease elevated serum corticosterone levels after exogenous ACTH administration.
5. The melanocortin system plays a central role in the regulation of energy homeostasis, including satiety and energy expenditure, highlighting its relevance in obesity and related metabolic disorders [7, 8].
6. These findings suggest that targeting the lipolytic and thermo­ genic properties of MC2R could offer new approaches to treating lipodystrophy and obesity.
7. We next val­ idated the tissue specificity of the system by examining FLAG expression across multiple tissues to assess poten­ tial off-target expression.
8. These findings suggest that adipocyte-specific MC2R overexpression induces subtle downstream acti­ vation of lipolysis, particularly in BAT in vivo, although these alterations were insufficient to reduce body weight.
