# Occurrence of tomato brown rugose fruit virus infecting field tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) in Hawaii.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant disease (2026)
- **PMID**: [43071919](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43071919/)
- **DOI**: [10.1094/PDIS-09-26-1728-SC](https://doi.org/10.1094/PDIS-09-26-1728-SC)
- **第一作者**: Jing Zhou
- **作者**: Jing Zhou, Scott Nikaido, Kian Kajioka, Alexandra Kong, Josiah Marquez, Roshan Manandhar, Jensen Uyeda, Rosemary Gutierrez-Coarite

## 摘要
In this short communication, we describe the occurrence of the emerging tomato brown rugose fruit virus (ToBRFV, Tobamovirus fructirugosum) in Hawaii. Disease and pathogen surveys conducted across the state focusing primarily on field tomato production reveal the presence of ToBRFV in multiple islands, and consistent detection of the virus for several years at the same field suggests it can persist in the field. To our knowledge, this is not only the first report of ToBRFV in Hawaii, but also the first official report of its presence in commercial field for tomato production in the U.S. Analysis of Sanger sequencing data reveal a high identity (>99.4%) between the detected virus isolates and ToBRFV genome sequences reported in the database. Considering the devastating impact of the virus on tomato production, we will work with growers to identify the source of the virus infection and develop practical management strategies to mitigate its impact.
