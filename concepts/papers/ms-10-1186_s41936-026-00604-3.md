### 核心发现
1. Results Under laboratory conditions, PDP and STS exhibited elevated toxicity toward target snails, with nearly identical LC50 values of 8.67 and 8.99%, respectively.
2. Conclusion The tested compounds exhibited significant toxicity against target land snails.
3. How­ ever, their environmental compatibility and effects on non-target organisms require further investigation (El Khateeb et al., 2025).
4. One-way ANOVA revealed no significant differences in the FDI (%) of tested com­ pounds against target land snails.
5. Both tested compounds exhibited a prolonged effect over time on the target land snail population.
6. Although metaldehyde is highly effective, its use may pose environmental risks, including toxicity to non- target organisms and potential soil or water contamina­ tion (Liu et al., 2024).
7. It also plays a key role in maintaining cell turgor and enhancing nutri­ ent uptake efficiency (Jančaitienė et al., 2023).
8. In addi­ tion, the potential effects of the tested compounds on non-target organisms were not evaluated.
