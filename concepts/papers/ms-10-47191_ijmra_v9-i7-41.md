### 核心发现
1. Vegetative growth, characterized by the emergence of strong, long, leafy, and sturdy new shoots, plays a crucial role as the plant's primary architectural organ.
2. Short-chain peptides (oligopeptides) applied through the leaf canopy are readily absorbed through stomata and then bind to kinase receptors on the cell membrane to activate endogenous growth hormones such as auxins and brassinosteroids (Albert et al., 2022).
3. The mechanism behind the peptide's ability to enhance generative components is related to its role as a biological elicitor that modifies the balance of endogenous hormones, particularly the cytokinin and gibberellin groups, and stimulates the expression of target genes encoding flowering initiation (Franzoni et al., 2023).
