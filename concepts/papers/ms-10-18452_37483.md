### 核心发现
1. Many aspects of how particles interact with one another on this tiny quantum scale, and how this relates to macroscopic interactions of objects, remains a mystery (Bell & d’Espagnat, 1971; d’Espagnat, 1979; Einstein et al., 1935; Gosson, 2016).
2. This thesis displays that within-group variability is crucial to above-group dynamics; across space and time, coarser data resolution simply cannot capture this variability, obscuring evolutionary stories.
3. To move the field forward, I believe it is crucial to centre variability at every taxonomic, temporal and spatial scale in evolutionary thinking, viewing each empirical observation as a singular snapshot of much greater complexity.
