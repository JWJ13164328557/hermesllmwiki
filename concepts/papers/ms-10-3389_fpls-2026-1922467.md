### 核心发现
1. Selected target genes associated with nitrogen metabolism and auxin- and cytokinin-related pathways were analyzed, including NifH, AUX6, AUX/IAA, PVCK01, AIPT3, and LOG/LOG-like genes.
2. Collectively, the complete allele matches corresponded to the reported MLST allelic proﬁle of BS- 114, strongly supporting the recovery of BS-114 from internal bean root tissues.
3. Notably, Pseudarthrobacter was markedly abundant in the BS-114+BS-120 treatment compared with other treatments.
