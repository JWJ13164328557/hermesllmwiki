# GhILL8 Interacts With GhREF to Coordinate Jasmonate-Auxin Signalling and Positively Regulate Cotton Defense Against Verticillium Wilt.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant, cell & environment (2026)
- **PMID**: [43036229](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43036229/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/pce.71042](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.71042)
- **第一作者**: Lei Zhang
- **作者**: Lei Zhang, Chang Wang, Xinyi Li, Kaikuo Rong, Youzhong Li, Yongang Yu

## 摘要
Auxin homoeostasis is a critical determinant of plant growth and environmental adaptation, yet its precise role and regulatory mechanisms in Verticillium wilt resistance remain largely unexplored. Here, we systematically characterized the IAA amidohydrolase (ILL) gene family across cotton species and identified GhILL8, a gene strongly induced by Verticillium dahliae infection and jasmonic acid (JA) defense-related signalling molecules. Functional dissection through virus-induced gene silencing (VIGS) revealed that GhILL8 knockdown severely compromises cotton resistance to V. dahliae, as evidenced by elevated disease indices, exacerbated vascular browning, and significantly increased fungal colonization. Notably, the early transient burst of free indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) observed in wild-type plants at 1 h post-inoculation, a hallmark of pattern-triggered immunity was completely abolished in GhILL8-silenced plants, correlating with accelerated reactive oxygen species (ROS) accumulation and extensive cell death. RNA-Seq data analysis further revealed that GhILL8 positively regulates cotton resistance to V. dahliae by modulating both JA and auxin (IAA) signalling pathways. Conversely, heterologous overexpression of GhILL8 in Arabidopsis and tobacco markedly enhanced tolerance to V. dahliae, underscoring its evolutionarily conserved positive role in plant immunity. Mechanistically, yeast two-hybrid and luciferase complementation imaging assays demonstrated that GhILL8 physically interacts with the rubber elongation factor GhREF. Loss of GhREF phenocopied GhILL8 silencing, impairing defense gene activation and aggravating vascular wilt symptoms. Collectively, our findings establish GhILL8 as a pivotal metabolic rheostat that calibrates the free auxin pool during early fungal infection and uncover a previously unrecognized GhILL8-GhREF module that orchestrates JA and IAA signalling crosstalk to fine-tune crop immunity. This work provides new mechanistic insights and valuable genetic resources for breeding Verticillium wilt-resistant cotton cultivars.
