### 核心发现
1. Intracellularly, BAFF bind- ing to BAFF-R strongly activates the non-canonical nuclear factor kappa B subunit 2 (NF-κB2) pathway and weakly activates the classical NF-κB1 pathway, while also engag- ing phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)/protein kinase B (AKT)/mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR) signal- ling to support protein synthesis and metabolic fitness [42].
2. The innate immune system plays a pivotal role in initi- ating the disease through the accumulation of self-nucleic acids derived from aberrant apoptosis clearance or neutro- phil extracellular traps (NETs).
3. Multim- eric 60-mer BAFF preferentially binds BAFF-R and TACI, whereas soluble BAFF interacts with all three receptors (BAFF-R, TACI and BCMA) and APRIL selectively signals through TACI and BCMA.
4. These findings suggest that BAFF/APRIL signalling may influence regulatory B cell populations.
5. Notably, APRIL can also bind heparan sulfate proteoglycans (HSPG) [38].
6. 8. In contrast, TACI signalling involves TRAF2 and TRAF6, triggering MyD88 and canonical NF-κB1 pathways that are critical for immunoglobulin class-switch recombination.
