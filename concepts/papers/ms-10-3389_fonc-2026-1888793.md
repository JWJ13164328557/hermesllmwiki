### 核心发现
1. Rather than cataloging lipid metabolic targets alone, we integrate de novo lipogenesis, exogenous lipid uptake, cholesterol dependent mem- brane remodeling, lipid droplet mediated stress buffering, ferroptosis regulation, and immune microenvironment remodeling into a uniﬁed adaptive model.
2. Collectively, these metabolic adaptations support tumor growth, metabolic ﬂexibility, and survival under therapeutic stress in GBM.
3. Collectively, DNL, exogenous lipid uptake, and FAO form a ﬂexible lipid acquisition and utilization network in GBM.
4. Collectively, these ﬁndings demonstrate that cholesterol metabolism and membrane remodeling are tightly interconnected processes in GBM.
5. Collectively, LDs in GBM function as spatially organized metabolic platforms that coordinate stress adaptation, redox homeostasis, ferroptosis balance, therapy resistance, and stemness maintenance.
6. Collectively, lipid metabolic remodeling in GAMs should be understood as a reciprocal tumor-immune adaptation program rather than a simple metabolic consequence of macrophage polarization.
7. Collectively, lipid metabolic reprogramming promotes immune evasion and tumor progression in GBM.
8. Collectively, lipid metabolic remodeling contributes to T-cell exhaustion through both intrinsic and extrinsic mechanisms.
