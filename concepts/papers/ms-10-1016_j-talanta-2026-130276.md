# MgFe2O4/graphene nanocomposites for bifunctional wastewater remediation: A critical review of adsorption, visible-light photocatalysis, and machine learning-assisted optimization.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Talanta (2027)

- **来源**: crossref


## 摘要
MgFe2O4/graphene nanocomposites have emerged as promising bifunctional platforms combining magnetic adsorption and visible-light photocatalysis for wastewater remediation; yet no comprehensive quantitative review of their structure-performance relationships exist. This review critically evaluates MgFe2O4/graphene nanocomposites for simultaneous removal of heavy metal ions and synthetic organic dyes (2015-2025). A comparative evaluation of five synthesis methodologies are performed against four criteria that determine the performance (Brunauer-Emmett-Teller surface area (BET): 120-320 m2/g; Raman D/G ratio: 1.05-1.35) of the synthesized materials, and the hydrothermal and solvothermal methods are found to be optimal. This review proposes a preliminary first Pearson correlation framework for this material system has been supported by a compiled adsorption dataset (qmax: 35 to 930 mg/g). Statistical analysis determined that the sole factor significantly correlated with the photocatalytic rate constant is the identifying bandgap energy (Eg): r = -0.889, p = 0.003, n = 8. The best range of graphene loadings has been found to be between 5 and 10 wt%, in an empirical manner, because the required magnetic recovery (Ms > 10 emu/g) is met while being accompanied by the maximum adsorption capacity. A new transferable machine learning optimization protocol (TMLOP) is presented, which combines the elements of response surface methodology, artificial neural networks, and random forest feature importance. pH and initial concentration were identified as dominant operational predictors (combined importance >55%). Real wastewater matrix effects, regeneration mechanisms, nanoparticle leaching, and scale-up engineering gaps are critically assessed. Six evidence-based future research priorities are ranked by impact and feasibility, with MgFe2O4-specific ML modeling and Z-scheme heterostructure development identified as the highest priority directions.
