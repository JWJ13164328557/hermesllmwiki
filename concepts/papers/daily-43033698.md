# Grape seed proanthocyanidins-yeast β-glucan interaction: Physicochemical properties, bioactivity, and in vitro digestive characteristics.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food chemistry (2026)
- **PMID**: [43033698](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43033698/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.foodchem.2026.152030](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodchem.2026.152030)
- **第一作者**: Yanping Huang
- **作者**: Yanping Huang, Yuanyuan Li, Huanyu Shi, Xinyi Kang, Chunlong Yuan, Junjun Li

## 摘要
This study investigated the effects of yeast β-glucan (BG) on the stability, bioactivity, and in vitro digestion of grape seed proanthocyanidins (GSPs) differing in mean degree of polymerization (MDP). GSPs (PC; MDP = 5.13) were fractionated into low-, medium-, and high-MDP fractions (LPC, MPC, HPC; MDP = 3.14, 4.61, 6.04, respectively). Complexation with BG reduced hydrodynamic diameters by 3.1-56.4%. BG improved thermal stability, increasing retention after 11 h at 60 °C by 1.9-6.7 percentage points. During gastric digestion, BG increased flavonoid contents by 52.9-66.3% and enhanced ABTS, DPPH, and hydroxyl radical scavenging by 10.2-12.1, 7.1-13.0, and 1.3-4.3 percentage points, respectively. BG reduced α-amylase and lipase inhibition by up to 11.2 and 33.6 percentage points. Molecular docking suggested that BG altered binding orientations and residue-level interactions of GSP units with enzyme models, offering a possible explanation for the reduced enzyme inhibition. These findings may inform the development of GSP-BG functional foods.
