### 核心发现
1. This study provides two key implications for sustainable intercropping design: From a carbon-budget perspective, maize-legume intercropping systems, particularly maize/pea, achieve higher carbon assimilation per unit area with lower GHG intensity, making them more carbon-eﬃcient in semi-arid regions.
2. Therefore, there is an urgent need to explore cropping systems that can enhance both resource-use eﬃciency and system productivity while maintaining agroecosystem health, which is crucial for ensuring food security and achieving sustainable agricultural development.
