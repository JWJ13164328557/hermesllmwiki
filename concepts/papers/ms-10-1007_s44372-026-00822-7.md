### 核心发现
1. This study provides the first evidence that kiwi and peach WPEs can act as effective, low-cost, and eco-friendly alternatives to synthetic phytohormones, supporting efficient micropropagation of elite cherry rootstocks.
2. Collectively, these characteristics highlight peach-derived extracts as promising natural biostimulants for sustainable agriculture and in vitro plant tissue culture.
3. Collectively, these findings demonstrate that pollen extracts function as multifunctional natural biostimulants capable of supporting the entire micropropagation process while reducing reliance on synthetic PGRs.
