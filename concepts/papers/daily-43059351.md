# Coordinated immune, chloroplast and chemical defences underpin BYDV and aphid resistance in barley carrying a Ryd4Hb introgression.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: The New phytologist (2026)
- **PMID**: [43059351](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43059351/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/nph.71725](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.71725)
- **第一作者**: Amma L Simon
- **作者**: Amma L Simon, Dasuni P Jayaweera, Ilma A Qonaah, Daniel Verburg, Faith Clarke, Dong-Hyun Kim, Benjamin Urquhart, James P E Melichar

## 摘要
Barley yellow dwarf virus (BYDV), transmitted by the bird cherry-oat aphid (Rhopalosiphum padi L.), is among the most damaging diseases of barley (Hordeum vulgare), but the mechanisms underlying resistance to both the virus and its vector remain poorly understood. Here, we investigated resistance in the barley hybrid SY Kestrel, carrying an H. bulbosum-derived Ryd4Hb introgression. We integrated aphid behavioural and electrophysiological assays, virus quantification, Chl fluorescence, transcriptomics, metabolomics and small RNA profiling. Functional assays evaluated the effects of candidate defence metabolites on aphid survival. SY Kestrel exhibited constitutive volatile-mediated antixenosis, postsettlement antibiosis involving impaired phloem feeding, reduced aphid fitness and suppression of BYDV establishment. Multi-omics analyses revealed coordinated immune, chloroplast and metabolic responses associated with resistance. Maintenance of photosystem II function during viruliferous aphid challenge was accompanied by reprogramming of α-linolenic acid-derived oxylipin metabolism, while metabolites associated with phenylpropanoid and amino acid metabolism reduced aphid survival. These findings identify multiple barriers to aphid-mediated BYDV transmission in SY Kestrel, encompassing vector deterrence, disruption of compatible phloem feeding and postinoculation host defence. Our results distinguish core Ryd4Hb-associated resistance from additional defence phenotypes associated with the larger introgressed genetic background and provide a mechanistic framework for breeding durable resistance to aphid-transmitted cereal viruses.
