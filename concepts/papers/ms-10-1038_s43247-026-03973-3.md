### 核心发现
1. Here we show that bacterial quorum sensing (QS) genes are markedly enriched inside P.
2. Our results reveal that intra-colonial bacterial communities possess higher network connectivity and modularity despite reduced taxonomic diversity, a phenomenon that has not been documented in prior research.
3. Collectively, these metabolic shifts coincided with enhanced resource recycling and biosynthetic capacity within colonies, facilitating colony expansion despite reduced colony frequency.
4. It is there- fore plausible that QS plays a pivotal role in structuring algal-bacterial relationships during bloom events15,21.
5. Future research incorporating direct quantitative proﬁling of diverse QS signals is essential to complement and further extend the ﬁndings of the present study.
