# MdEF-Tu-associated translational reprogramming modulates apple disease resistance through the MdGAPC2-autophagy pathway.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: The New phytologist (2026)
- **PMID**: [43070322](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43070322/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/nph.71776](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.71776)
- **第一作者**: Runmin Che
- **作者**: Runmin Che, Rui Ma, Yanqi Li, Chang Liu, Qi Wang, Hao Feng, Xiaoqing Gong, Lili Huang

## 摘要
Elongation factor-Tu (EF-Tu) is a central component of the ribosomal translation machinery, and the ribosome-associated regulatory environment is an important determinant for translational control. However, the molecular mechanisms involving plant EF-Tu in immune-associated translational regulation remain largely unexplored. We show that apple EF-Tu (MdEF-Tu) associates with the ribosomal proteins MdRPL28 and MdRPS8 and modulates global translational activity during Valsa mali (V. mali) infection. Transcriptome and translatome analyses reveal widespread translational reprogramming. Polysome profiling and ribosome fractionation analyses show that altered MdEF-Tu abundance affects the translational output of MdGAPC2, which encodes a cytosolic glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase. MdGAPC2 interacts with the autophagy-related protein MdATG3a and interferes with autophagy. Notably, the V. mali effector Vm1G-1794 stabilizes MdEF-Tu, sustaining an MdEF-Tu-associated translational response involving MdGAPC2, thereby promoting autophagy suppression and compromising apple disease resistance. Collectively, our findings uncover a regulatory process linking MdEF-Tu-associated translational reprogramming to MdGAPC2-mediated autophagy during the apple immune response and reveal how V. mali hijacks this process to facilitate infection. This work provides novel insights into the autophagy modulation during the interaction between plants and pathogens.
