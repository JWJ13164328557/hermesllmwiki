# Functional analysis of SmCSN5 in salt stress tolerance of Salvia miltiorrhiza.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant science : an international journal of experimental plant biology (2027)
- **PMID**: [42906702](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42906702/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113539](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113539)
- **第一作者**: Shuai Shao
- **作者**: Shuai Shao, Mei Wang, Weijia Zhu, Pengda Ma

## 摘要
Salt stress limits plant growth, but the contribution of COP9 signalosome subunit 5 (CSN5) to salt tolerance in Salvia miltiorrhiza remains unclear. Here, we combined heterologous overexpression in Arabidopsis thaliana with overexpression and RNA interference in S. miltiorrhiza hairy roots. SmCSN5 transcript abundance increased with NaCl concentration and showed a transient response to 150 mM NaCl, peaking at 6 h among the sampled times. SmCSN5 overexpression improved germination and growth under salt stress and was associated with higher proline accumulation and antioxidant enzyme activities. Overexpressing Arabidopsis also maintained higher relative water content and chlorophyll content, with lower electrolyte leakage, malondialdehyde content, and ROS-associated staining. Separate ion measurements showed lower Na⁺ content, higher K⁺ content, and lower Na⁺/K⁺ ratios in overexpressing Arabidopsis and hairy roots. SmCSN5 RNA interference produced opposite physiological and ion-accumulation responses in hairy roots. Yeast two-hybrid and luciferase complementation assays identified SmCBL3 as a candidate SmCSN5-interacting protein. Collectively, these findings support a positive role for SmCSN5 in salt tolerance, accompanied by improved antioxidant defense, osmotic adjustment, and ion homeostasis. Whether these responses improve medicinal metabolite production under salinity remains unresolved.
