# Unraveling microbiome, metabolome and volatilome profiles of naturally fermented sauerkraut from six Chinese cabbage cultivars.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.) (2026)
- **PMID**: [42914326](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42914326/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.foodres.2026.120593](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodres.2026.120593)
- **第一作者**: Jiayi Cao
- **作者**: Jiayi Cao, Yuanlong Chen, Shuang Zhou, Huan Meng, Yushuai Wei, Chuanzhen Ma, Jianan Du, Haiyan Fu

## 摘要
This study examined the effects of six Chinese cabbage cultivars on quality development in sauerkraut. Successful fermentation was achieved for all six cultivars, but the final products showed clearly distinct flavor profiles. Lactiplantibacillus was the dominant genus in all cultivar groups. Differences in the distributions of Leuconostoc, Weissella, Lentilactobacillus, and Lactococcus were closely associated with cultivar-specific flavor characteristics. A total of 54 differential metabolites were screened, mainly including lipid oxidation intermediates, phenolic acids, and nucleotide related compounds. These metabolites were primarily mapped to α linolenic acid metabolism and phenylpropanoid biosynthesis. Volatilomic analysis further revealed distinct cultivar-specific flavor profiles. HJ was dominated by ester and terpenoid volatiles and exhibited floral and fruity notes. JH was enriched in 2-methoxy-4-vinylphenol and showed clove-like and smoky notes. LS was enriched in aldehydes and nitriles and showed green and fatty characteristics. YK, BA, and YF retained the typical pungent odors derived from glucosinolate degradation. Multi-omics association analysis further revealed links among key microorganisms, metabolites, and volatile compounds. These findings link cabbage cultivar to distinct sauerkraut flavor, microbial, and metabolic profiles, providing evidence for raw-material selection and improved product consistency.
