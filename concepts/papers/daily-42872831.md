# Complete genome sequence of Pseudomonas sp. koa227, an anti-oomycete endophytic isolate from the shoot xylem of the Japanese indigenous wine grape Vitis sp. cv. Koshu.

- **作者**: Toshiyuki Kano, Yoshinao Aoki, Takayuki Asada, Masutoshi Nojiri, Shunji Suzuki

## 摘要
Endophytic bacteria residing within grapevines represent a largely unexplored research frontier, highlighting the need to elucidate the physiological properties of individual strains. Here, we introduce a genome sequence of Pseudomonas sp. koa227, an anti-oomycete endophytic isolate from the shoot xylem of a Japanese indigenous wine grape.
