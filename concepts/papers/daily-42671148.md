## 摘要
Extreme weather conditions driven by climate change have resulted in higher average temperatures, more frequent intense heat waves, increased drought conditions, improper soil conditions, as well as increased pest and disease incidence, posing a unique challenge to plants. Until now, several studies have been performed to reveal how plants can acclimatize or cope with these unfavorable conditions. These studies have majorly focused on how plants respond to an individual stress at a particular point in time. But in nature, no stress occurs in isolation; rather, they act mostly in combination. This condition is referred to as "multifactorial stress combination" or, more precisely, it is termed as "stress matrix". The damages and corresponding responses from individual stresses and stress combinations may entirely be different, leading to complex downstream signaling. Through this review, we have made an attempt to decipher the physiological and molecular responses of different plant species to multiple stress combinations. It is further emphasized that more focus needs to be drawn towards the development of plants that are tolerant to multiple or co-occurring stress factors, keeping in mind the adversities of the consistently challenging environmental conditions.

### 核心发现
1. In addition to transcription factors and gene-mediated stress regulation, plant hormones have also been shown to play a key role in stress tolerance.
2. Transcription factors may alter the target gene's expression in these regulatory networks and coordinate a suitable defense strategy by binding to promoter-associated cis-elements of those genes.
3. Notably, while the presence of one or two stressors did not impact rice seedling survivability, the combination of four and five stressors reduced survivability to approximately 60% and 75%, respectively.
4. Notably, Xida 889 exhibited superior resilience to heat and drought stress combination as compared to Xida 319, which was attributed to its robust antioxidant defense system, increased osmolyte accumulation, and ability to maintain photosynthetic pigments and nutrient balance.
5. Understanding the mechanisms underlying the response of plants to combined HT and salinity stress is essential for creating resilient varieties of crops that may prosper in such challenging circumstances.
6. Hormones such as ABA are essential for plants to fine-tune their stomatal adjustments, ensuring effective acclimation to each unique stress combination within their environment.
7. The results obtained suggest that ROS homeostasis, which refers to the balance between ROS synthesis and scavenging, is essential for the survival of plants under multifactorial stress combination.
