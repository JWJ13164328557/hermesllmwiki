# Metabolic division of labor enables tunable biosynthesis of linalool and linalyl acetate.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Biotechnology letters (2026)
- **PMID**: [43102654](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43102654/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s10529-026-03807-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10529-026-03807-4)
- **第一作者**: Lijin Jiang
- **作者**: Lijin Jiang, Xuxu Li, Hong Liang, Mengyao Yuan, Dianqi Yang, Xiaoyuan Jing, Xiaoqiang Ma, Yan Feng

## 摘要
Linalool and linalyl acetate are high-value monoterpene alcohol-ester pairs widely used in the fragrance, food, and cosmetic industries. In this study, an efficient two-strain Escherichia coli co-culture system was constructed via metabolic division of labor for the entire pathway. The upstream strain harbored mevalonate synthesis pathway and linalool synthase for de novo linalool synthesis, while the downstream strain expressed a linalool alcohol acyltransferase to convert linalool into linalyl acetate. The esterification strain achieved nearly 91% of the theoretical linalyl acetate yield. At an optimized inoculation ratio of 15:1, the co-culture produced 2.73 g/L total monoterpenes, comprising 0.82 g/L linalool and 1.91 g/L linalyl acetate. Separately, the use of sunflower oil as a renewable in situ extraction phase generated a linalyl acetate-to-linalool ratio close to that reported for Bulgarian lavender essential oil, which enables flexible regulation of alcohol-ester ratio, providing a feasible strategy for the sustainable biosynthesis of high-value monoterpenes.
