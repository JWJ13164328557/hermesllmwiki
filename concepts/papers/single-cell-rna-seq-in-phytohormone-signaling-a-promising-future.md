### 核心发现
1. Phytohormones originate from distinct sources and target tissues, necessitating the involvement of numerous transporters in short-distance movements mediated by symplast, apoplast, and transcellular mechanisms or long-range communication through the plant vascular system.
2. scRNA- seq holds immense potential for investi- gating cell–cell molecular communication mechanisms and identifying function- speciﬁc target molecules within intercel- lular networks.
3. BRs promote the expression of HOMEOBOX FROM ARABIDOPSIS THALIANA 7 (HAT7) and GT-2-LIKE 1 (GTL1) family transcription factors, which are direct targets of BES1 and BZR1.
4. combined scRNA-seq and a digital 3D single cell shape approach to characterize root meristems and mature root segments of the bri1 brl1 brl3 triple receptor-null mutant and wild-type and found that BR signaling is essential for the orientation of cell division planes and the extent and timing of anisotropic cell expansion [11].
