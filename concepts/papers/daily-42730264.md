## 摘要
The flavonoid glycoside isoorientin has demonstrated antitumor, cardioprotective, and gut-protective activities, as well as potential applications as a natural biopesticide and antimicrobial agent. However, large-scale production and industrial development of isoorientin are limited by its low abundance in plants. In this study, we used metabolic engineering to construct an isoorientin-producing budding yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) cell factory. To enhance production of the intermediate naringenin, we overexpressed key genes in the shikimic acid and naringenin pathways, reinforced the cytosolic malonyl-CoA supply, and redirected subcellular acetyl-CoA flux. After screening enzymes from diverse sources, we identified an optimized enzyme combination for luteolin biosynthesis from naringenin: CYP93G1 (from Oryza sativa L.), ATR1 (from Arabidopsis thaliana [L.] Heynh.), and SbFBH2 (from Scutellaria baicalensis Georgi)-ATR2 (from A. thaliana). To address cytochrome P450 (P450)-related bottlenecks, we optimized gene dosage and engineered the P450 microenvironment by increasing the supply of NADPH and heme and expanding the size of the endoplasmic reticulum. Finally, introducing a flavone 6-C-glycosyltransferase, coupled with optimizing the culture medium and enhancing the UDP-glucose supply, enabled de novo isoorientin biosynthesis. Therefore, we established an engineered yeast platform for isoorientin synthesis by implementing a strategy for P450 optimization, providing a framework for microbial production of glycosylated natural products.

### 核心发现
1. These results suggest that multi-copy overexpression is a viable strategy for enhancing luteolin production.
2. These results demonstrate that engineering the P450 microenvironment and electron transfer chain significantly enhances the production of functional P450s in yeast, leading to improved luteolin production.
3. In summary, a final isoorientin titer of 3.17 mg/L was achieved in a 10-mL shake flask culture of engineered S.
4. Collectively, these modifications boosted luteolin production by 45.3%.
5. Promoter, terminator, and target locus fragments were cloned from S.
6. coli lacks an endomembrane system, making it difficult to produce the functional cytochrome P450 enzymes, which are required for de novo isoorientin biosynthesis when the complete pathway is reconstituted in this host.
7. Therefore, screening for enzymes with higher activities is crucial for luteolin production.
8. Notably, when we expressed the selected genes in plasmids and introduced them into yeast, we achieved high luteolin titers, but their integration into the genome resulted in a dramatic decrease in luteolin production.
