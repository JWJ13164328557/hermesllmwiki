### 核心发现
1. We found that there is a certain relationship between iron death and polarization of microglia, which may be a causal relationship.
2. In conclusion, PSMD14 suppresses microglial M2 polarization during TBI progression by promoting mitochondrial oxidation-induced ferroptosis through the activation of the BMP6/SMAD3 pathway.
3. Upon post-injury activation, SMAD1/5 translocate from the cytoplasm to the nucleus to modulate the transcription of target genes associated with neuronal survival, cell differentiation, and neuroinflammatory regulation[16].

5. Relative mRNA expression levels of the target gene PSMD14 were quantified using the 2<sup>−ΔΔCt</sup> method, with GAPDH adopted as the internal reference gene for normalization.
6. With bregma set as the coordinate origin, a small ~1 mm × 1 mm craniotomy was drilled over target brain regions (AcbC, VTA) per standard brain atlas coordinates, without damaging the underlying dura mater.
7. The microsyringe needle was vertically advanced through the dura to the predetermined target depth, and viral suspension was infused at a slow rate of 0.02 mL/min using a microinjection pump.
8. Collectively, these clinical and in vivo findings indicate that PSMD14 upregulation is closely correlated with TBI progression, suggesting its potential as a critical regulatory molecule in traumatic brain injury.
