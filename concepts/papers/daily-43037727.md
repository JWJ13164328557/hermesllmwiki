# Chinese cabbage BrCAMTA1 mediating Ca2+ and ABA signaling regulates drought resistance.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant cell reports (2026)
- **PMID**: [43037727](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43037727/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s00299-026-04049-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00299-026-04049-4)
- **第一作者**: Mengting Mao
- **作者**: Mengting Mao, Guoqing Du, Xijiao Kang, Xuanwen Gong, Lin Chen, Shanshan Nie

## 摘要
This study identified BrCAMTA1 as the key regulator in response to drought stress in Chinese cabbage. BrCAMTA1 positively regulates the drought resistance by mediating Ca2+-BrCaM2-3 pathway and ABA signaling. Calmodulin-binding transcription activators (CAMTAs) are a class of transcription factors that bind with calmodulin (CaM) and plays an important role in plant biotic and abiotic stress responses. Although considerable studies have reported the involvement of CAMTA genes in drought response, the roles of BrCAMTAs in regulating drought resistance remain unclear in Chinese cabbage. In this study, nine BrCAMTA family genes were identified in Chinese cabbage, and these BrCAMTAs contained highly conserved functional domains. Expression analysis showed that BrCAMTA1 gene exhibited significant upregulation after drought and ABA treatment, which was consistent with the expression pattern of BrCaM2-3 gene. The interaction between BrCAMTA1 and BrCaM2-3 protein was verified, occurred in the nucleus, and exhibited a Ca2+-dependent manner. Silencing BrCAMTA1 or BrCaM2-3 reduced drought tolerance of Chinese cabbage seedlings, decreased leaf Ca2+ fluorescence, and increased ROS level. Loss of Arabidopsis CAMTA1 function that is homologous to BrCAMTA1 caused a decrease in drought tolerance and sensitivity to ABA, while heterologous overexpression of BrCAMTA1 in Arabidopsis improved drought tolerance and ABA sensitivity. These results indicate that BrCAMTA1 serves as a positive regulator of drought resistance by modulating ABA signaling and participating in Ca2+-CaM pathway. These findings provide important insights into the roles of Ca2+-BrCaM2-3-BrCAMTA1 model in regulating drought resistance and the regulatory mechanisms of BrCAMTAs in response to drought and ABA in Chinese cabbage and other vegetable crops.
