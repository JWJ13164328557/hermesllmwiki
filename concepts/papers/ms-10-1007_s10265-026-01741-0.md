### 核心发现
1. Taken together, these results suggest that HDG4 contributes to the DNA damage-induced repression of PIN gene expression, potentially contributing to reduced auxin signaling in the root tip.
2. Further studies, including the identification of HDG4 target genes, are required to clar­ ify the molecular basis of the HDG4-dependent regulation of auxin transport.
3. Our results show that HDG4 has a detectable function under genotoxic stress conditions, suggesting that HD-Zip IV transcription factors may contribute not only to develop­ mental patterning but also to stress-responsive developmen­ tal regulation.
