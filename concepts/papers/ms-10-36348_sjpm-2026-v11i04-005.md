### 核心发现
1. The target was polio eradication up to 2026.
2. CD 155 is composed of three extracellular immunoglobulin like domains- a membrane distal V- type domain and 2 C2 type domains that bind to polio virus.
3. Cutting edge: CD96 [tactile] promotes NK cell- target cell adhesion by interacting with the poliovirus receptor [CD155].
4. Political instability, humanitarian crisis and most importantly, global health funding cuts are threatening the progress made.
5. Tracing the origin of isolates to determine their antigenicity is a prerequisite for all epidemiological surveillance and is essential for the study of vaccines too.
6. More research and live simulation models are required to study this novel genetic recombination.
7. This study postulates and reiterates the fact that it is essential to include such PID patients in poliomyelitis surveillance programs.
