# γ-Aminobutyric Acid Enhances the Effectiveness of Rhizobium Y406 in Alleviating Salinity Stress and Promoting Growth of Soybean.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of agricultural and food chemistry (2026)
- **PMID**: [43082117](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43082117/)
- **DOI**: [10.1021/acs.jafc.6c10791](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jafc.6c10791)
- **第一作者**: Dandan Wang
- **作者**: Dandan Wang, Mengguang Zhao, Hongyu Du, Zitian Pu, Delong Liu, Chao Wang, Guiliang Zheng, Ronghui Ma

## 摘要
Soybean-rhizobia symbiosis is critical for yield and soil fertility, yet saline-alkali stress severely impairs nodulation and plant growth. γ-Aminobutyric acid (GABA) enhances stress tolerance, but whether GABA promotes rhizobial performance remains unclear. This study isolated a strain of rhizobium bacterium, Sinorhizobium alkalisoli Y406, from nodules of salt-tolerant wild soybean, which exhibits halo-alkaliphilic tolerance and possesses plant-growth-promoting traits; moreover, it significantly enhances soybean nodulation. GABA enhanced the growth, chemotaxis, and stress tolerance of Y406 under salt stress conditions. The coapplication further enhanced soybean growth by increasing antioxidant enzyme activities, improving the soil NPK content, and promoting rhizosphere microbial richness and diversity. This study demonstrates that the combined application of biostimulant and rhizobia effectively alleviates saline-alkali stress in soybean, offering a promising strategy for improving stress tolerance and promoting the sustainable utilization of marginal lands in soybean cultivation.
