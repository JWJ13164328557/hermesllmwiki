### 核心发现
1. Taken together, these findings suggest that, although SVs mutational processes appear comparatively homogeneous in cHL, subtle differences in the activity of selected SVs signatures may reflect underlying disease biology or treatment vulnerability.
2. Despite representing only a small fraction of the total tumor cellularity, these cells play a central role in disease pathogenesis and progression, sustained by a combination of genetic alterations, immune escape mechanisms, and dynamic interactions with the tumor microenvironment5,10–13.
3. Taken together, these overlapping events reinforce the view that disruption of loci involved in immune regulation and B-cell signalling is a central feature of cHL biology 13.
4. Characterizing the full spectrum of genomic alterations in HRS cells is essential to understand the biological mechanisms driving tumor evolution and immune evasion in cHL.
5. Importantly, integrating chromosomal instability with relapse-associated CNVs into the TRACER-HL model improved baseline risk stratification and identified a subgroup of patients with a very low probability of relapse.
