### 核心发现
1. Well-managed nursery and healing conditions are critical for producing quality grafted seedlings.
2. 3.4 Establishment and post-graft Compatible rootstock-scion combinations are required for successful graft establish­ ment, as are carefully managed healing conditions immediately following grafting.
3. Since rootstock- scion selection is crucial for determining graft compatibility, plant vigour, stress toler­ ance, and crop productivity, it receives particular attention.
4. 4.1.1 Compatibility The compatibility of the rootstock and scion is essential for successful grafting and long- term plant performance.
5. 4.2 Healing and acclimatisation The healing phase, which typically lasts five to ten days depending on the crop spe­ cies and environmental factors, is essential for successful graft establishment.
6. Unifor­ mity in seedling height, stem thickness, and leaf count is essential for sustained growth performance.
7. Ster­ ilised, well-drained growing media and strict sanitation practices are essential to pre­ vent pathogen spread [54].
8. 6 Role of rootstocks in stress resistance The selection of appropriate rootstocks is critical to increasing vegetable crop tolerance to both biotic and abiotic stressors.
