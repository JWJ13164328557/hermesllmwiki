### 核心发现
1. Our data show that ZNF217, an oncogenic zinc finger transcription factor, promotes CRPC resistance to ferroptosis by transcriptionally activating prominin-2 (PROM2) and thereby modulating iron homeostasis and lipid peroxidation.
2. In this study, we identify the transcription factor ZNF217 as a direct downstream target of AR, which is aberrantly upregulated in prostate cancer and associated with poor clinical outcomes.
3. Collectively, these findings identify AR/ZNF217/PROM2 signaling as a central determinant of ferroptotic vulnerability in prostate cancer and suggest that targeting this pathway may enhance sensitivity to AR inhibition, providing a molecular rationale for combination therapy ARTICLE IN PRESS ARTICLE IN PRESS in castration-resistant prostate cancer.
4. In addition, AR directly activates the expression of the cystine transporter subunits SLC7A11 and SLC3A2, as well as GPX4, thereby enhancing glutathione-dependent antioxidant defenses and facilitating the clearance of lipid peroxides[12- 14].
5. The present study demonstrates that the AR/ZNF217/PROM2 axis plays a critical role in ferroptosis tolerance in CRPC.
6. Collectively, our study uncover an AR- dependent mechanism underlying ferroptosis tolerance and highlight the AR/ZNF217/PROM2 axis as a promising entry point for ferroptosis-oriented combination strategies in castration- resistant prostate cancer.
