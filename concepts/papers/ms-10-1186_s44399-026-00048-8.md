Notably, sugar-specific effects on aphid feeding are pronounced: in Myzus persicae, sorbose stimulates ingestion without being metabo­ lized, whereas mannose, sorbitol, xylose, and ribose nei­ ther promote feeding nor support utilization; rhamnose, arabinose, lactose, and cellobiose inhibit fluid uptake and reduce survival [24].
5. Notably, it remains unclear whether aphid performance is driven primarily by the total amino acid concentration in phloem sap or by the ratio of essen­ tial to non-essential amino acids, which is a distinc­ tion with practical implications for breeding crops with reduced aphid suitability.
6. Remarkably, this contribution is not fixed, as facultative symbionts have been shown to help improve aphid growth by changing the relative abundance of Buchnera under low-amino acid diets, dis­ playing how this interaction is evolving and dynamic in nature [66].
