# Computational guided improvement of thermostability of Arabidopsis thaliana UDP-sugar pyrophosphorylase for efficient synthesis of uridine 5'-diphosphate galactose.

- **作者**: 

## 摘要
Uridine 5'-diphosphate galactose (UDP-Gal) is a vital but costly precursor for oligosaccharide synthesis. A major bottleneck in its production is the poor thermostability of biosynthetic enzymes. Here, we engineered Arabidopsis thaliana UDP-sugar pyrophosphorylase (AtUSP) using consensus prediction (FireProt, PROSS, Pythia) followed by experimental screening and combinatorial assembly of five beneficial mutations. The resulting mutant AtUSPM5 (K561Y/L492F/S99T/N107F/S545T) showed significantly enhanced thermostability: The melting temperature elevated by 9.72 °C (from 50.25 °C to 59.97 °C), half-lives at 37 °C and 50 °C extended from 0.43 h and 0.20 h to 28.88 h and 10.62 h (67.2- and 53.1-fold), respectively, without compromising catalytic activity. Molecular dynamics simulations revealed a more compact structure stabilized by additional hydrophobic interactions and hydrogen bonds. Improved thermostability of AtUSPM5 facilitated gram-scale synthesis of UDP-Gal and subsequent synthesis of complex human milk oligosaccharides para-lacto-N-neohexosaccharide (pLNnH) and lacto-N-neohexosaccharide (LNnH), offering a practical tool for food carbohydrate research.
