### 核心发现
1. Mineralocorticoid receptors (MRs) play a pivotal role in regulating sodium and water balance, blood pres- sure, and inflammation.
2. The renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS) plays a critical role in these processes [18–20].
3. Aldosterone, a key hormone in this system, binds to MRs, leading to sodium retention, potassium excretion, and water re- tention, which contribute to hypertension and fluid overload [21].
4. This selectivity is thought to reduce the likelihood of off-target effects, thus improving the safety profile of these drugs [32].
5. This aspect is crucial for T2DM patients who are at a heightened risk of cardiovascular disease due to underlying meta- bolic disturbances.
6. Studies noted significant im- provements in systolic and diastolic blood pressures, which are essential for reducing the overall cardiovas- cular risk profile of these patients.
7. • Mecha- nisms and Implications: Monitoring and managing adverse events such as hyperkalemia are essential for long-term therapy adherence and effectiveness.
