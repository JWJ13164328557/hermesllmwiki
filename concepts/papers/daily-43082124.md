# Carotenoid-Loaded Chitosan Nanoparticles Reduce Phenanthrene Accumulation and Alleviate Phytotoxicity in Brassica rapa.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of agricultural and food chemistry (2026)
- **PMID**: [43082124](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43082124/)
- **DOI**: [10.1021/acs.jafc.6c09518](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jafc.6c09518)
- **第一作者**: Menghan Cui
- **作者**: Menghan Cui, Yibo Yang, Hui Huang, Diwu Fan, Yongli Zhu, Jiangang Han, Yu Shen

## 摘要
Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) suppress crop growth and accumulate in edible tissues, and remediation removes the pollutant without relieving the injury it causes. β-Carotene was loaded onto chitosan nanoparticles (CNPs) to give carotenoid-loaded chitosan nanoparticles (CCNPs) and compared with free β-carotene and bare CNPs in Brassica rapa under phenanthrene (PHE) stress. CNPs were 70-260 nm and removed 68-82% of dissolved PHE within 72 h; loading was indicated by FTIR and by a larger hydrodynamic diameter with a lower zeta potential. CCNPs lowered leaf and root PHE, restored biomass, preserved chloroplast ultrastructure and pigments, and reduced malondialdehyde by about 44%. Bare CNPs lowered pollutant uptake but restored growth only partially, and free β-carotene did the reverse. Metabolomics associated protection with a shift from phenylpropanoid defense toward ascorbate recycling, terpenoid and phenolic antioxidants, and tryptophan-linked growth signaling. Under the conditions tested, a single material combining pollutant interception with β-carotene-associated antioxidant protection therefore protected the crop while limiting its pollutant burden, supporting a design principle for multifunctional soil amendments.
