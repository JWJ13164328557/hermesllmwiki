### 核心发现
1. These results suggest that the trade- off between photosynthetic capacity loss associated with leaf area removal and improvements in stem quality and structure may be species dependent, likely driven by differences in light requirements, crown plasticity, and apical dominance control (Niinemets, 2010; Alcorn et al., 2013).
2. Hormonal regulation associated with apical dominance—particularly auxin–cytokinin–strigolactone interactions—also plays a central role in re-establishing a domi­ nant leader and suppressing competing shoots after pruning (Ferguson and Beveridge, 2009; Beveridge et al., 2023).
3. From a man­ agement perspective, these results suggest that early pruning—when trees are below approximately 8–10 cm in diameter at breast height or 5–8 years of age—represents a strategic window to improve stem form while maintaining overall stand growth potential.
