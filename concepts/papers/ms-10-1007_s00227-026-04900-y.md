### 核心发现
1. From the animal-borne video footage, we found slow feeding behaviours to be the most frequent.
2. The ethogram revealed whale sharks forage using different feeding types and movements, and may feed in a cost-effective manner across a very broad range of prey densities, in contrast to other large filter feeders such as baleen whales and basking sharks, which target and limit feeding to dense aggregations of prey.
