## 摘要
Plant immunity relies on dynamic reprogramming of the transcriptome, but the underlying regulatory mechanisms that modulate chromatin accessibility and thus transcriptional activity remain poorly defined. In this study, we explore how salicylic acid (SA) and the pathogen-associated molecular pattern (PAMP) peptide flg22 reprogram immune gene expression through epigenetic modifications in Arabidopsis thaliana. We integrated RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, and ATAC-seq data to show that exogenous SA and flg22 induce distinct, widespread patterns of chromatin remodeling and differential gene expression. SA predominantly promotes a coordinated increase in H3K4me3 and H3 acetylation (H3Ac), two histone marks that are strongly associated with enhanced chromatin accessibility, at defense-related loci. By contrast, flg22 mainly activates defense gene expression via H3Ac, with minimal changes in H3K4me3 levels, suggesting distinct epigenetic strategies. Greater chromatin accessibility was closely linked to the activation of immune-responsive genes and correlated positively with H3K4me3 and H3Ac levels. Network analysis highlighted central roles for NPR1, ICS1, PR1, and proteasome-related genes in SA-triggered immunity. Collectively, our results reveal that SA and flg22 modulate immune gene expression through shared and unique chromatin-based pathways, offering insight into the epigenetic regulation of plant defense.

### 核心发现
1. Collectively, our results reveal that SA and flg22 modulate immune gene expression through shared and unique chromatin-based pathways, offering insight into the epigenetic regulation of plant defense.
2. Collectively, these data reveal that immune signals such as SA and flg22 induce significant, target-specific changes in histone modifications.
3. Taken together, these results demonstrate that greater SA-induced chromatin accessibility plays a pivotal role in the transcriptional regulation of immune genes, and suggest the central gene networks that may coordinate this response.
4. Overall, our results support a model in which PTI signaling via flg22 promotes transcriptional regulation through targeted epigenetic modulation, which is distinct from, but also partially overlapping with, SA-mediated immune regulation.
5. Our data suggest that flg22-responsive transcriptional activation may rely more heavily on rapid chromatin opening and acetylation, bypassing the need for stable trimethylation of H3K4, a mark often associated with long-term transcriptional memory or poised states.
6. Our results suggest that flg22-triggered chromatin remodeling may extend beyond transcriptional regulation to influence the protein synthesis machinery, providing a potential mechanism for rapid immune protein production.
7. Together, our results support a model in which chromatin accessibility acts as a central integrator of immune signaling and transcriptional control.
8. SA treatment led to 1414 upregulated DEGs and 1082 downregulated DEGs, whereas flg22 treatment predominantly induced gene expression, with 1476 upregulated DEGs and only 236 downregulated DEGs, suggesting that flg22 treatment significantly activates the PTI signaling pathway ( B, ).
