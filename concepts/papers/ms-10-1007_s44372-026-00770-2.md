### 核心发现
1. These compounds contribute to osmotic adjustment and interact with hor­ monal signaling networks that enhance stress resilience.
2. Carotenoids are a specific class of tetraterpenoids which play a crucial role in protecting photosystems from photoinhibition [41], by dissipating excess light energy generated during drought.
3. However, comparative evi­ dence suggests that, although terpenoid metabolism plays a central role in hormonal sig­ naling and rapid stress responses, its contribution to drought adaptation is more variable across genotypes than phenylpropanoid-driven structural protection, indicating path­ way-specific specialization in stress resilience strategies.
4. Collectively, drought-responsive sec­ ondary metabolites act as ecological regulators that integrate plant physiology with soil microbial dynamics, nutrient cycling and interspecific competition under water-limited environments.
5. These findings suggest that drought adaptation should be viewed as a coordinated plant–microbiome trait rather than an exclusively plant-centered response.
6. Classical Page 2 of 26 Mutanda and Mafirakurewa Discover Plants (2026) 3:289 drought-adaptive traits such as root architecture, stomatal regulation and leaf morphol­ ogy are critical for drought adaptation [4, 5], but they do not fully explain the metabolic reprogramming that supports plant survival under water deficit conditions.
7. Importantly, trade- offs are not static but vary with stress duration and severity.
