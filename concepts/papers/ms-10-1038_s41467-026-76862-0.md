### 核心发现
1. Using preclinical SCLC models and patient-derived samples, we show that XPO1 drives TRIM21-dependent IRF3 degradation, suppresses TNFSF15 expression and promotes the accumulation of immunosuppressive TREM2+ macrophages, thereby reducing MHC class I expression and CD8+ T-cell inﬁltration.
2. However, clinical strategies that directly target TAMs have had limited success, and the immunor- egulatory TAM subsets that are most relevant to SCLC remain incompletely characterized.
3. To determine whether TNFSF15 directly activates downstream signaling in macrophages, we treated THP-1-derived M0 macrophages with recombinant TNFSF15 (1 μg/ml, 24 h) and performed bulk RNA- seq.
4. These data suggest that TNFSF15 responsiveness may differ across macrophage states and support macrophages as the more likely TNFSF15-responsive population in this co-culture system.
5. Collectively, these data establish that XPO1 facilitates IRF3 degradation via the ubiquitin-proteasome system.
