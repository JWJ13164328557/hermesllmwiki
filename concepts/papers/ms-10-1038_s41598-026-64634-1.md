### 核心发现
1. Biosynthetic study Here we report, for the first time, the elucidation of the structure of a 1-deoxyceramide arising from distinct acyl-CoA precursors.
2. This newly characterized compound constitutes a promising target for future studies addressing its biological properties and potential biotechnological applications.
3. The target metabolite, pseudoceramide A, was purified using chromatographic techniques and subsequently characterized by 1H and 13C NMR spectroscopy.
