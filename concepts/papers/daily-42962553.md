# Manipulating the banana holobionts through early endophyte inoculation of in vitro plantlets to combat Fusarium wilt TR4.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Microbiological research (2027)
- **PMID**: [42962553](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42962553/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.micres.2026.128792](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micres.2026.128792)
- **第一作者**: Ping He
- **作者**: Ping He, Libo Tian, Guangdong Zhou, Huacai Fan, Yongfen Wang, Baoming Yang, Guangyu Han, Yunyue Wang

## 摘要
Fusarium wilt of banana (FWB), caused by Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. cubense tropical race 4 (Foc TR4), poses a severe threat to global banana production. Early inoculation of banana tissue-cultured plantlets with beneficial endophytes may serve as a microbial "vaccination" strategy and offers a promising approach to establish enhanced resistance against FWB. In this study, Bacillus velezensis YN1282-2, isolated from tissue-cultured banana plantlets, enhanced disease resistance and promoted growth when inoculated during the tissue-cultured plantlet stage. Genomic analysis of YN1282-2 identified multiple genes related to biocontrol and colonization, including genes potentially involved in environmental sensing and host colonization. Fluorescent labeling confirmed successful colonization within banana vascular tissues, with peak colonization at 4 days post-inoculation in both resistant and susceptible banana cultivars. Inoculation with YN1282-2 conferred durable disease suppression and growth-promoting effects, with control efficacies against TR4 of 56.75% in Brazilian and 78.13% in Yunjiao No.1. Transcriptomic profiling revealed activation of hormone signaling and phenylpropanoid biosynthesis pathways, indicating that YN1282-2 primes host defense responses. These findings highlight early inoculation with YN1282-2 as a promising and sustainable approach for long-term FWB management.
