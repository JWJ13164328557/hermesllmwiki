# Natural variation in the evolutionarily conserved F-box gene SDEF promotes seed dormancy in rice.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [42958909](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42958909/)

- **作者**: Yuliang Zhou, Yueping Wang, Guangmiao Zhao, Chunbao Liu, Ruizhen Zeng, Wenxuan Liang, Ru Han, Hui Kong

## 摘要
Insufficient seed dormancy causes pre-harvest sprouting (PHS) and severe yield losses in rice; however, genetic resources with practical breeding value remain limited. Here, we isolated a major dormancy gene, SDEF, via map-based cloning using chromosomal single segment substitution lines (SSSLs), identifying a functional SNP (Arg-to-Trp) as the causal mutation. CRISPR/Cas9-mediated knockout of SDEF led to PHS susceptibility, while overexpression significantly enhanced dormancy. Importantly, SDEF enhanced seed dormancy without compromising grain yield in SSSLs, confirming it as a promising breeding target. Moreover, SDEF encodes an angiosperm-conserved, nuclear-localized F-box protein and is preferentially expressed in the embryo, with expression increasing during seed maturation and declining upon germination. Mechanistically, protein interaction assays confirmed that SDEF recruits OSK1, with the Trp-type SDEFK exhibiting a more stable interaction than the Arg-type SDEFH. SDEF is induced by abscisic acid (ABA) and appears to promote seed dormancy through the ABA pathway by activating ABA signaling genes and repressing α-amylase genes, as evidenced by the reduced ABA sensitivity of sdef mutants. Additionally, haplotype analysis revealed that natural variation in SDEF is associated with divergent dormancy levels, and reduced nucleotide diversity in cultivated rice indicates that SDEF was selected during domestication. Collectively, our results provide insights into the molecular basis of SDEF-mediated dormancy, providing an ideal target for breeding PHS-resistant rice.

### 核心发现
1. Collectively, our results provide insights into the molecular basis of SDEF‐ mediated dormancy, providing an ideal target for breeding PHS‐resistant rice.
2. In summary, these results confirm that SDEF is the causal gene of qSD7‐1 and functions as a positive regulator of seed dormancy in rice.
3. Collectively, these findings confirm the robust expression of SDEF in seeds, particularly in the embryos, suggesting a potential role in modulating seed dormancy.
4. CRISPR/Cas9‐mediated knockout of SDEF led to PHS susceptibility, while overexpression significantly enhanced dormancy.
5. F‐box proteins mediate the degradation of target proteins through the ubiquitin‐26S proteasome system by forming SKP1‐Cullin‐F‐box (SCF) E3 ubiquitin ligases (Ban & Estelle, ).
6. In this study, we successfully cloned the target gene SDEF underlying this major locus using map‐based cloning.
7. We further confirmed that SDEF interacts with OSK1 ( LOC_Os11g26910 ), a member of the SCF‐type E3 ligase, and provide evidence supporting a model in which SDEF may modulate seed dormancy through the ABA signaling pathway.
8. Finally, haplotype analysis indicated that SDEF harbors abundant natural variations, suggesting that it can serve as a potential target for enhancing crop resistance against PHS.
