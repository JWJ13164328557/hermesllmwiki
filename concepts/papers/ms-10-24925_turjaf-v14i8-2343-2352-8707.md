### 核心发现
1. These findings show that moderate concentrations of IAA, particularly 25 ppm, significantly improved vegetative propagation efficiency and establishment of vetiver, with tillers being the best cutting type.
2. Among these substances, auxins play a central role in inducing adventitious root formation in cuttings and in regulating morphogenesis during plant propagation (Abdalla et al., 2022; Wei et al., 2019; Zheng et al., 2020).
3. This trend indicates that IAA plays a critical role in regulating tiller initiation and growth in vetiver cuttings and follows the characteristic bell-shaped auxin response curve reported in many plant species (Rout, 2006; Wang et al., 2022).
4. Identifying the optimal auxin concentration in relation to cutting type is essential for improving propagation efficiency and scaling up vetiver production.
5. By simultaneously assessing auxin concentration and cutting type, this research provides new insights into the optimization of propagation conditions for vetiver and identifies the most effective combination for its rapid rooting and growth.
6. Overall, the concurrent increases in leaf and root dry weights at optimal IAA concentration indicate improved source–sink balance and greater physiological vigor of vetiver cuttings, which are critical for successful vegetative propagation and field establishment.
