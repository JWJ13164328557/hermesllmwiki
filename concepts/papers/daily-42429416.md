## 摘要
Previously, we showed that altered sulfite homeostasis led to higher water loss in sulfite oxidase (SO) RNA interference (Ri) plants than in Arabidopsis wild type (WT) after sulfite infiltration into rosette leaves. In contrast, SO overexpression (OE) and adenosine-5'-phosphosulfate reductase 2 (apr2) knockout (KO) resulted in lower water loss than in WT. Accordingly, sulfite homeostasis and drought tolerance under prolonged drought in Arabidopsis and tomato (Rheinlands Ruhm) were investigated. SO Ri mutants displayed the most severe wilting and water loss, accompanied by sulfite accumulation due to reduced SO expression. In contrast, SO OE, apr2 KO, apr2 KO/SO OE, and gene-edited lines, including sulfite reductase (SiR) OEC2+SO OEC2 and SiR OEC4/SO OE, maintained higher relative water content by enhancing sulfite oxidation and reduction. Elevated sulfite in SO Ri mutants acted as an antagonist to abscisic acid (ABA), as despite accumulating higher ABA than all other genotypes, SO Ri plants displayed the lowest relative water content, consistent with sulfite suppressing ABA signal transduction through impaired by Pyrobactin resistance 1-like 5 (PYL5) and enhanced ABA negative feedback by Highly ABA-induced PP2C gene 2 (HAI2). SO and SiR overexpression also increased hydrogen sulfide and cysteine levels, contributing to drought resilience. Tomato lines also reflected these patterns in SO OE, SiR OE, and SiR OE/SO OE, showing reduced sulfite and improved relative water content compared with WT. Overall, coordinated regulation of SO, SiR, and APR2 maintains sulfite homeostasis, mitigating drought-induced damage and enhancing water retention across species by directly limiting sulfite accumulation and relieving sulfite-driven antagonism of ABA signal transduction.

### 核心发现
1. Overall, these results suggest that overexpression of SO and SiR improved sulfite detoxification and increased drought tolerance in Arabidopsis in the SlSiR OE/ SO OE, SiR OE C2 + SO OE C2, and SiR OE C4 / SO OE mutants compared with the WT plants.
2. Collectively, these metrics demonstrate that elongation factor 1-α ( EF1-α ) exhibits stable expression across genotypes, treatments, and independent experiments, supporting its suitability as a reference gene for normalization under the tested conditions, in line with MIQE recommendations (, ).
3. These results suggest that the SO Ri mutant has the highest susceptibility to drought stress, followed by WT, with the SO OE, apr2 KO, and apr2 KO/ SO OE mutants having a relatively higher tolerance ( ).
4. These results show a direct correlation between enhanced sulfite accumulation and increased water loss in SO Ri plants under drought stress, as the result of SO, SiR, and APR2 expression levels in Arabidopsis mutants.
5. Collectively, these data showed that SO Ri under drought is characterized by suppressed ABA perception, the strongest ABA negative feedback, and reduced expression of key guard cell ion transport components relative to its irrigated control, consistent with most likely impaired stomatal regulation.
6. Overall, these results suggest that as in the Arabidopsis mutant plants, the decrease in the sulfite levels through oxidation resulted in an increased sulfate level and sulfate/sulfite ratio in the SO OE and SiR OE/ SO OE tomatoes.
7. Taken together, the convergence of results across multiple independent genetic manipulations and two plant species provides strong confidence in the robustness of the present findings.
8. The uptake of available sulfate from the soil system is essential for plant growth and development ( ).
