### 核心发现
1. Nanoparticles interact with cellular organelles (mitochondria, chloroplasts, and peroxisomes) to influence the production of reactive oxygen species (ROS), including O₂⁻, H₂O₂, •OH, NO, and ONOO⁻.
2. Seed Priming and its Effects in Agriculture Seed is the main input used in agricultural production, hence, ensuring its quality is crucial to increasing crop productivity.
3. While seedling growth is inhibited during the drying process, metabolic activity which is essential for metabolite synthesis, antioxidant activity, solubilization of stored material, priming and stress memory, and repair mechanisms remains active.
4. (2017) [24], nano priming promoted the induction of aquaporin genes and reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation in germinated seeds, both of which are crucial for improving seed germination.
5. It is essential to establish standardized guidelines regarding nanoparticle dosage and treatment conditions to ensure safe application.
