### 核心发现
1. Taken together, we conclude that TTC33 levels are regulated in vivo by proteolytic decay, and WDR61 is key to safeguarding TTC33 from decay, by direct interaction.
2. Using a combina- tion of biochemical assays, structural modeling and molecular dynamics we show that TTC33 directly recruits WDR61 and PHF5A to assemble into a tri- meric core complex (TANC), which then forms distinct interactions with either UNG1/2 or SF3B–CCDC97.
3. In sum, we conclude that TTC33 is a conserved vertebrate-speciﬁc structural protein.
