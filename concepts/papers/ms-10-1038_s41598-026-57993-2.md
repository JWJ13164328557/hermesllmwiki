### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these observations highlight a lack of standardised methods for processing PC samples, particularly those preserved in RNAlater, which is common in routine clinical practice.
2. Collectively, these observations demonstrate that bead-assisted homogenisation and high-ionic strength lysis can generate snRNA-seq libraries from RNAlater-preserved prostate tissue resections within < 150 min.
3. While cross-dataset correlation cannot definitively exclude all potential preservation-related shifts, these results suggest that our workflow largely minimizes heat- shock or dissociation artefacts.
