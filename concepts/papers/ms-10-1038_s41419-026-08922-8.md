### 核心发现
1. In this study, we show that OAS1 is the key member of the OAS family whose expression is consistently associated with adverse clinical outcomes in PDAC.
2. Mechanistically, OAS1 binds to fatty acid synthase (FASN), maintaining its functional protein levels and thereby promoting FASN-dependent lipid synthesis.
3. Collectively, these data establish the prognostic value of the OAS family members in cancer and specifically highlight OAS1 as a key driver associated with poor prognosis and lipid metabolic dysregulation in PDAC.
4. These results confirm that OAS1 is up-regulated in malignant pancreatic cells and serves as an independent marker for predicting poor prognosis in PDAC patients.
5. Collectively, these results demonstrate that OAS1 is a critical driver of malignant phenotypes in PDAC cells in vitro.

8. Notably, within the PDAC cohort, OAS1 emerged as the only member whose high expression was significantly correlated with both poor OS and recurrence-free survival (RFS).
