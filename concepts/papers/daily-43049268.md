# A LINE1-associated circular RNA circRLn1 modulates apple Alternaria blotch resistance by sequestering mdm-miR398b to tune ROS homeostasis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of integrative plant biology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43049268](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43049268/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/jipb.70445](https://doi.org/10.1111/jipb.70445)
- **第一作者**: Yunfei Yu
- **作者**: Yunfei Yu, Ling Xiang, Manyu Zhang, Wenting Huang, Shengnan Wang, Wei Li, Shengyuan Wang, Qiulei Zhang

## 摘要
Alternaria blotch causes severe apple yield loss. Circular RNAs (circRNAs) participate in plant stress defense, but their antifungal pathways remain poorly understood. Retrotransposons modulate gene transcription; yet, Long interspersed nuclear element 1 (LINE1)-associated circRNA formation is rarely characterized in fruit trees. We combined circRNA sequencing, RNA pull-down, RNA immunoprecipitation (RIP), fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH), dual-luciferase assays, stable/transient transformation, and pathogen inoculation to dissect the biogenesis of a LINE1-associated circRNA, circRLn1, and its role in apple immunity against Alternaria blotch. circRLn1 overexpression restrained lesion expansion and pathogen growth under Alternaria alternata infection. Minigene assays suggest that LINE1-derived intronic inverted repeats promote circRLn1 production. Cytoplasmic circRLn1 may act as a sponge for mdm-miR398b, thereby de-repressing MdCSD1, MdCSD2 and MdCCS, elevating copper/zinc superoxide dismutase (Cu/Zn-SOD) activity to balance reactive oxygen species (ROS) and reduce oxidative injury. Functional dependency assays indicate that mdm-miR398b is required for circRLn1-mediated resistance. We identify a LINE1-circRLn1-mdm-miR398b-CSD/CCS regulatory module, revealing a potential retrotransposon-associated circRNA biogenesis pathway contributing to fungal immunity in apple.
