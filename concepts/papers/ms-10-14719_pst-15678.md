Keywords: arginine; citrus; foliar application; phenylurea cytokinins; vegetative growth OSAMAH & ATHEER 2 https://plantsciencetoday.online Arginine, which is considered one of the main important amino acids in plant metabolism, plays a central role in regulating vegetative growth and physiological processes.
2. It is considered a vital precursor for a group of important compounds, most notably polyamines and nitric oxide, which play a pivotal role in regulating cell division and elongation and interacting with plant hormones such as auxins and cytokinins.
3. Consequently, it is essential to develop applied treatments that contribute to improving growth efficiency and enhancing production sustainability, among which is foliar spraying with plant growth regulators, including thidiazuron (TDZ), which is considered one of the synthetic cytokinins belonging to the phenylurea group.
