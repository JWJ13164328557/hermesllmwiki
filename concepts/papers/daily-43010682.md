# Arabidopsis HMGA protein HON5 antagonizes ABF transcription factors to negatively regulate ABA signaling and stress tolerance.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant communications (2026)
- **PMID**: [43010682](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43010682/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.xplc.2026.102195](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xplc.2026.102195)
- **第一作者**: Pan Wang
- **作者**: Pan Wang, Ranze Zhao, Chunmei Yin, Aiqing Sun, Yuanda Wang, Yuda Fang

## 摘要
Plants inevitably face abiotic stresses such as drought and salinity. While the abscisic acid (ABA) signaling pathway has been extensively characterized, the involvement of chromatin structural factors in hormone responses remains poorly understood. This study investigates the roles of Arabidopsis Globular Domain of Linker Histone-High Mobility Group A (GH1-HMGA) proteins, specifically GH1-HMGA1 (HON4) and GH1-HMGA2 (HON5), in modulating ABA signaling and abiotic stress tolerance. Genetic analysis reveals that the loss-of-function hon45 double mutant exhibits hypersensitivity to ABA, salt, and osmotic stress, suggesting that HON4/5 function as negative regulators of the ABA and stress response signaling genes like RAB18 and RD29B. Mechanistically, HON4 and HON5 physically interact with the ABA-responsive element (ABRE)-binding factors (ABFs). Functional assays indicate that HON4 and HON5 impair the ability of ABFs to activate downstream stress-responsive genes. Genetic experiments demonstrate that HON4 and HON5 attenuate the ABF response to ABA and drought stress. Importantly, we identify a negative regulatory feedback loop in which the transcription factor ABF4 directly binds to the HON5 promoter and represses its transcription. Collectively, these findings establish HON4 and HON5 as novel nuclear co-repressors that attenuate ABA-mediated stress signaling by inhibiting the transcriptional activity of ABFs, revealing a previously unrecognized chromatin-associated layer of regulation in plant stress responses.
