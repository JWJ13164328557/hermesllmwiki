# OsNAC32 interacts with the AP2/ERF transcription factor RSR1 to promote seed germination by suppressing the expression of OsFIE1 encoding a core PRC2 component in rice.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant communications (2026)
- **PMID**: [43099930](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43099930/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.xplc.2026.102204](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xplc.2026.102204)
- **第一作者**: Xue-Feng Yao
- **作者**: Xue-Feng Yao, Qun Zhong, Mingyang Su, Xiaojun Cheng, Lifeng Liu, Jun Du, Chen Chen, Chun-Ming Liu

## 摘要
Pre-harvest sprouting (PHS) commonly reduces grain quality and yield and is influenced by hormone signaling, including ABA and GA levels in mature seeds. In recent years, several transcription factors (TFs) that control ABA and GA biosynthesis have been identified, including SD6, ICE2, OsABI3, and OsABI5. Nevertheless, how TFs directly coordinate the epigenetic machinery to modulate germination remains largely elusive. Here, we mapped the landscape of loss-of-function variants across all TF families in a diverse collection of natural rice varieties and uncovered numerous gene-loss events in the NAC TF family. Tissue expression profiling of NAC-family genes revealed high OsNAC32 expression in seeds, shoots, and roots. Genome-wide association study analysis, together with characterization of OsNAC32-knockout alleles, further suggested that loss of OsNAC32 function delays seed germination. Transcriptome and RT-qPCR analyses revealed upregulation of OsFIE1 in OsNAC32-knockout seeds, and electrophoretic mobility shift assays, ChIP-qPCR, and Y1H assays identified it as a downstream target gene. Furthermore, Y2H, Co-IP, pull-down, and Bi-FC assays demonstrated that OsNAC32 physically interacts with RSR1 (AP2/ERF family), forming a complex. Notably, RSR1 enhanced OsNAC32 stability. As expected, phenotypic analysis showed significantly delayed seed germination in the osnac32;rsr1 double mutant. Genome-wide profiling using CUT&Tag and ATAC-seq data revealed elevated H3K27ac levels near TSS and enhanced chromatin accessibility within and around gene bodies. These epigenetic changes led to upregulation of OsNCED1/3 and increased ABA levels in the osnac32 and osnac32;rsr1 mutants. Our findings indicate that a transcriptional-epigenetic module reduces ABA levels and promotes seed germination through OsNAC32 targeting of OsFIE1, offering potential targets for PHS-resistant breeding and illustrating precise regulation at multiple transcriptional and epigenetic levels.
