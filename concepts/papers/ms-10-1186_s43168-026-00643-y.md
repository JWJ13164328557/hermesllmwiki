### 核心发现
1. Our data suggest that the fundamental problem is not inadequate mechanical removal but rather an intrinsic epigen­ etic reprogramming of local fibroblasts.
2. This axis provides potential therapeutic targets and explains why inflammation-driven fibroproliferation persists despite mechanical resection.
3. The complete differential expression results for all five target genes are summarized in Table 2.
4. Discussion This study provides the first evidence, to our knowledge, that dysregulation of the ROBO2-EID1-EP300 epigen­ etic regulatory axis contributes to recurrent granulation tissue formation at tracheostomy sites.
5. From a therapeutic perspective, our data identify EP300 as a candidate target for pharmacological intervention.
6. Our findings identify EID1 as a potential biomarker and EP300 as a tractable therapeutic target for tracheostomy granula­ tion, a condition with no current targeted therapy.
