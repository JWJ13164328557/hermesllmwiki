### 核心发现
1. In summary, these preliminary ﬁndings suggest that KXA may alleviate symptoms of acute mountain sickness, may reduce cardiac workload, and might support cogni- tive function during acute hypoxic stress, although conﬁrmatory trials are needed.
2. This study demonstrates that KXA aerosol exhibits potential for preventing AMS during simulated high-altitude exposure.
