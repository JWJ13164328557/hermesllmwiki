# The Sequence of Photoperiod Exposure Determines Hypocotyl Development in Arabidopsis Seedlings.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant & cell physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43055645](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43055645/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/pcp/pcag160](https://doi.org/10.1093/pcp/pcag160)
- **第一作者**: James Ronald
- **作者**: James Ronald, Sarah C L Lock, Will Claydon, Zihao Zhu, Kayla McCarthy, Elizabeth Pendlington, Ethan J Redmond, Gina Y W Vong

## 摘要
Sensing and responding to photoperiod changes is essential for plants to adapt to the changing seasons. The elongation of the hypocotyl, the seedling stem, is directly controlled by the photoperiod through a coincidence model. However, these models were built on observations from growth under a static photoperiod. It is unknown if changes in the duration of the photoperiod during development influence hypocotyl elongation. To investigate this, we designed a photoperiod shift assay where seedlings were exposed to an initial photoperiod before being moved to a second photoperiod. Whilst hypocotyl elongation primarily coincided with exposure to the second photoperiod, we found that photoperiod exposure earlier in development also influenced final hypocotyl length. The response to these photoperiod shifts was dependent on the Arabidopsis ecotype, the ambient temperature of the growth assay and the developmental age of the seedling when exposed to the photoperiod shift. We also found that phyB and ELF3 have a critical role in responding to the photoperiod shift. It is unclear if the activity of phyB and ELF3 is circadian clock-dependent, as the circadian clock quickly re-phased to the second photoperiod. This suggests that additional mechanisms contribute to integrating photoperiod information across development. In summary, our work has revealed that the integration of photoperiodic information during seedling emergence and early development determines hypocotyl elongation.
