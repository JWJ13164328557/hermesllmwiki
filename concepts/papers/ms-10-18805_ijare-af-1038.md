# Impact of Water Stress Timing on Potato Yield Components: A Comparative Assessment of Two Cultivars (Solanum tuberosum L.) in Arid Regionnt of Two Cultivars (Solanum tuberosum L.) in Arid Region

- **作者**: Amel BOURAHLA
- **来源**: crossref


## 摘要
Background: Water scarcity is a principal constraint to potato production and stress timing mediates yield outcomes by affecting stage-specific processes. Methods: Field study (2023-2024, semi-arid Algeria) using split-plot RCBD with four irrigation regimes (full irrigation vs. deficit at 45% field capacity during vegetative growth, tuber initiation, or bulking) and two cultivars (Belete and Gudenie). Result: The largest losses occurred when stress coincided with tuber initiation and bulking; initiation stress reduced tuber number (≈37-44%), while bulking stress reduced average tuber weight (≈41-52%) and increased small, non-marketable tubers (≈28-35%), with total yield reductions up to ≈43-58% T1 (control).
