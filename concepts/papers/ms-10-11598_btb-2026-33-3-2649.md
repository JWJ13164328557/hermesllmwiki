### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that yeast extract supplementation within the tested concentration range does not impact the biomass growth of A.
2. Notably, 2,3-Butanediol was present at the control treatment (0 mg/L) but absent at higher yeast extract concentrations.
3. Notably, the 150 mg/L treatment, despite exhibiting the highest content of n-Hexadecanoic acid (30.72%), yielded the lowest total number of detected compounds (8), indicating that excessive yeast extract concentration may trigger metabolic selectivity or even inhibit the biosynthesis of certain metabolites.
4. Therefore, selecting the appropriate elicitor concentration is crucial to achieving a targeted and effective plant defense response.
