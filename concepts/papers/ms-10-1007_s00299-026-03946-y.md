### 核心发现
1. Functional analysis demonstrated that overexpression of NtMADS91 significantly promoted axillary bud growth, increasing both the number and length of axillary buds.
2. This study provides a theoretical basis for elucidating the regulatory mechanism of the NtMADS-box gene family in axillary bud development and also offers important candidate genes for research related to tobacco axillary bud development.
3. Overexpression of NtMADS91 promoted the growth and development of tobacco axillary buds.
4. Collectively, this promoter analysis revealed that NtMADS-box family genes are extensively involved in mediating plant hormonal responses, potentially through abscisic acid and methyl jasmonate signaling pathways.
5. These findings reveal distinct tissue-specific expression profiles among NtMADS-box subfamily genes.
6. These results suggest that ABA participates in the regulation of tobacco axillary bud development by repressing the expression of NtMADS-box genes.
7. Overexpression of NtMADS91 significantly increased the number and length of tobacco axillary buds, indicating that it acts as a positive regulator of axillary bud growth.
8. Overexpression of NtMADS91 significantly increased the number of tobacco axillary buds and promoted axillary bud elongation, indicating that this gene acts as a positive regulator of tobacco axillary bud Page 14/30 development.
