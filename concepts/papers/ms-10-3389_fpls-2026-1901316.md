### 核心发现
1. Further research is required to achieve a comprehensive un- derstanding of the underlying mechanisms and to determine the optimal combination of ADY, SW, and GT extracts with conven- tional mineral fertilizers.
2. Notably, the 75% NPK dose resulted in the highest signiﬁcant values of PH (51.13 and 53.30 cm), NB (11.93 and 12.80 branches/plant), AL (46.14 and 47.73 cm2), CI (17.67 and 18.22 SPAD units), FWS (95.76 and 96.20 g/plant), DWS (24.00 and 24.70 g/plant), FWR (40.01 and 41.05 g/ plant), and DWR (12.41 and 12.70 g/plant) (P ≤0.05) in both seasons, respectively.
3. Notably, plants sprayed with 3 g L-1 ADY + 1.5 ml L-1SW + 0.5 g L-1 GT had the highest TCS (13.44 and 13.45%), N (2.80 and 2.88%), P (0.67 and 0.70%), and K (2.11 and 2.51%) compared with the other natural extract treatments in both seasons.
