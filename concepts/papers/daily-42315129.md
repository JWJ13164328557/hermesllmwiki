## 摘要
Plant circadian clocks govern the timing of physiological and developmental processes that determine growth, reproduction, and seasonal phenology. As a result, natural variation in clock genes can directly influence local adaptation, but the components of the clock that have already been targeted by selection are unclear. We found that naturally occurring variation in the core clock gene EARLY FLOWERING 3 (ELF3) determines circadian period length and has been selected for in hot, seasonal climates. We measured the circadian rhythms of 287 global Arabidopsis (Arabidopsis thaliana) accessions using a seedling transformation protocol with a circadian luciferase reporter. Genome-wide association studies identified multiple single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) in ELF3, and their identities were used to define 3 haplogroups associated with seasonal variability in temperature. We found strong evidence of a selective sweep in haplogroups from continental climates, which is estimated to coincide with the most recent de-glaciation period in Europe. One of the SNPs is located within intron 2 of ELF3 in the region upstream of an alternative transcription start site affecting the expression of a shorter ELF3β transcript. Our results indicate the important role of subtle variation in a core circadian clock gene in adaptation to a changing climate.

### 核心发现
1. We found that naturally occurring variation in the core clock gene EARLY FLOWERING 3 (ELF3 ) determines circadian period length and has been selected for in hot, seasonal climates.
2. We found strong evidence of a selective sweep in haplogroups from continental climates, which is estimated to coincide with the most recent de-glaciation period in Europe.
3. We show that variation in ELF3 has been selected for and probably aided the spread of Arabidopsis to continental climates.
4. Using at least 3 independent transgenic lines for each, we found that all natural ELF3 alleles complemented the arrhythmic elf3-1 phenotype (; ).
5. We found no significant difference in reporter signal between the haplogroup alleles ( ), suggesting differences in post-transcriptional gene expression are unlikely to explain functional differences between these ELF3 alleles.
6. In warm temperature, we found significantly longer hypocotyl for ELF3 TA and ELF3 TT transgenics than in ELF3 AA transgenics or Col-0 and a temperature response in ELF3 TA that was intermediate to the ELF3 AA and ELF3 TA transgenics for fold change of hypocotyl length ( and ).
7. In keeping with previous studies that have identified thermosensory functions of ELF3, we found evidence that the forces driving the spread of ELF3 variants are related to elevated temperatures.
8. SNP1 converts the underrepresented Gly-encoding GGC codon to a more optimal GGU codon ( ), which could potentially affect translation efficiency or protein folding; however, we found no difference in 35S:ELF3-LUC2 protein abundance between haplogroups ( ).
