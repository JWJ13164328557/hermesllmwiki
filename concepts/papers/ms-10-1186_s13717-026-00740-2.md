### 核心发现
1. Discussion Our results show that reference conditions in Nothofagus pumilio forests are better represented by the multidimen- sional natural range of variation across forest develop- mental stages than by a single condition.
2. By integrating forest structure, soil, understory vegetation, and animal use, we found that developmental stages represent dif- ferent ecosystem conditions, with ecological dimensions differing across forest development.
3. Management practices often target forest structure because it is relatively easy to define and assess in the field (Oliver and Larson 1996).
4. Importantly, the definition of reference condi- tions depends on the spatial scale and management objective; our framework considers the forest land- scape as the reference system, where multiple develop- mental stages collectively represent the natural range of variation.
