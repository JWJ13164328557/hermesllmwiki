# Identification of key pathways and functional modules associated with multiple abiotic and biotic stress conditions in barley.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [43068816](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43068816/)
- **DOI**: [10.1071/FP25381](https://doi.org/10.1071/FP25381)
- **第一作者**: Bahman Panahi
- **作者**: Bahman Panahi, Nahid Hosseinzadeh, Amir Ghaffar Shahriari

## 摘要
Barley (Hordeum vulgare L.) is frequently exposed to diverse abiotic and biotic stresses that threaten its productivity and quality. This study aimed to identify key pathways, gene modules, and hub genes associated with multiple stress conditions in barley using a weighted gene co-expression network analysis (WGCNA). A total of 515 RNA-Seq samples representing 14 different stress types were integrated and analyzed to construct a comprehensive co-expression network. Eleven distinct gene modules were identified, five of which showed significant correlations with abiotic and/or biotic stresses. Functional enrichment and protein-protein interaction (PPI) analyses revealed that these modules are primarily involved in energy metabolism, amino acid biosynthesis, carbon metabolism, and protein processing-pathways critical for stress adaptation. The black and pink modules were associated with metabolic regulation and structural stability under abiotic stress, whereas the blue and green modules were linked to protein turnover and pathogen response during biotic stress. The red module highlighted the downregulation of oxidative phosphorylation under both stress types, indicating energy conservation mechanisms. Collectively, these findings provide novel insights into the coordinated transcriptional networks that regulate stress resilience in barley and identify potential molecular targets for breeding and biotechnological improvement of multi-stress tolerance.
