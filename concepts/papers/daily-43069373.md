# Dissecting the genomic and transcriptomic basis of heterosis in elite indica-japonica hybrid rice Zheyou 18.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Functional & integrative genomics (2026)
- **PMID**: [43069373](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43069373/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s10142-026-02135-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10142-026-02135-0)
- **第一作者**: Yongtao Cui
- **作者**: Yongtao Cui, Yang Lv, Rongbiao Jiang, Jianjun Wang

## 摘要
Elite indica-japonica hybrid rice varieties such as Zheyou 18 exhibit significant yield advantages; however, the molecular mechanisms underlying their heterosis remain incompletely elucidated. In this study, we conducted an integrated genomic and transcriptomic analysis of Zheyou 18 and its parental lines (Zhehui 818 and Zhe 08 A) to dissect the genetic and transcriptional basis of its heterosis. Whole-genome resequencing revealed substantial divergence between the parents, with the paternal indica line Zhehui 818 contributing approximately 60% (106/177) of the identified quantitative trait locus (QTL) haplotypes, including key alleles for yield and architecture such as IPA1 and Ghd7. Transcriptome profiling of stem and flag leaf tissues demonstrated tissue-specific regulatory patterns: while gene expression in stems predominantly followed additive inheritance, 3,444 genes in leaves exhibited significant transgressive overexpression ([H2P; significantly higher than both parents, FDR ≤ 0.01]), and these genes were notably enriched in plant-pathogen interaction pathways. We propose a dual mechanism for the yield heterosis of Zheyou 18: genomic complementarity, in which superior alleles from both parents are pyramided, and transcriptional innovation, particularly the activation of novel regulatory networks in leaf tissues. This study provides a molecular framework for understanding heterosis in intersubspecific hybrid rice and offers insights for optimizing breeding strategies aimed at high yield and resilience.
