### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that TC-HR, and TRDMT1 in particular, may be a good therapeutic target in cancers relying on this specific repair pathway, prompting us to develop inhibitors of TRDMT1 and test their effects in cancer cells.
2. Notably, we found that BRCA1-deficient cancer cells are highly sensitive to TRDMT1i 16, suggesting that homologous recombination deficiency (HRD) is a cause of TRDMT1 dependency in cancer and the consequent TRDMT1i sensitivity.
3. These results show that the cytotoxicity of TRDMT1i is selective to certain subsets of cancers.
4. Moreover, this result suggests that the effects of TRDMT1i in cancer cell lines are associated with its target TRDMT1.
5. These results suggest that the MSI caused by MLH1 deficiency, but not general MSI, is associated with high TRDMT1i sensitivity.
6. Together, these results show that loss of MLH1 is indeed a cause of TRDMT1i sensitivity.
7. These results suggest that MutLα, but not MutLβ, has a specific role in suppressing TRDMT1i sensitivity.
8. Together, these results show that MutLα suppresses TRDMT1i sensitivity independently of MutSα/β, suggesting an MMR-independent function of MutLα whose loss renders cancer cells increasingly dependent on TRDMT1 for survival.
