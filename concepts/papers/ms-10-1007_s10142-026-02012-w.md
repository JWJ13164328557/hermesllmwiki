### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that the 79 non-enriched TCs primarily represent non-biological batch effects.
2. Other DFS-associated TCs were enriched for genes involved in diverse biological processes, including MYC targets and the mitotic spindle.
3. These findings suggest that these TCs capture transcriptional effects from multiple biological processes that may influence the risk of LRR in breast cancer patients.
4. Notably, TC350 was highly active in adipose tissue from common reconstructive donor sites (abdomen, omentum, subcutis) but not in native breast adipose tissue.
