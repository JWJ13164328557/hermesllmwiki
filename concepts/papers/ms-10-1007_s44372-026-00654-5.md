### 核心发现
1. The phenylpropanoid pathway plays a central role in the biosynthesis of diverse sec­ ondary metabolites, including lignin, rutin, and chlorogenic acid.
2. Collectively, the data suggest that nanoparticles can stimulate transcriptional regulators of secondary metabolism while maintaining normal vegetative and reproductive growth.
3. From an agricultural perspective, these findings suggest that appropriately optimized nanoparticle formulations may serve as potential tools for improving the accumulation of valuable phytochemicals such as capsaicinoids and antioxidant compounds in chilli.
4. Additional health- promoting effects include gastroprotective and hypoglycemic properties; however, fur­ ther studies are necessary to fully understand their metabolic benefits [4, 26].
