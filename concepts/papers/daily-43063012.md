# SRPKIIs modulate HAB1 alternative splicing to counteract ABA-induced suppression of seedling establishment in Arabidopsis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43063012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43063012/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag860](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag860)
- **第一作者**: Shuangshuang Zhao
- **作者**: Shuangshuang Zhao, Tongtong Wang, Wenrong Yang, Xinrui Shi, Xiaofeng Wang, Yiwu Zhao, Haiyan Wang, Yuan Zheng

## 摘要
The phytohormone abscisic acid (ABA) serves as a master regulator of plant development and stress adaptation. In plants, alternative splicing (AS) dynamically expands transcriptome diversity and critically coordinates ABA-mediated physiological processes, however, the molecular mechanisms governing ABA-induced AS remain elusive. The serine/arginine-rich (SR) protein-specific kinase II family (SRPKIIs) modulates transcriptional programs and AS events through phosphorylation of specific substrates, but their role in ABA signaling has not been established. Through genetic and molecular analyses, we demonstrate that Arabidopsis SRPKIIs orchestrate ABA responses during seedling establishment by regulating HAB1 pre-mRNA splicing. The srpkii triple mutants exhibit hypersensitivity to ABA during early development. Genome-wide splicing analysis reveals that SRPKIIs differentially regulate AS patterns of core ABA signaling components, particularly by enhancing HAB1.1 isoform production while suppressing HAB1.2. Genetic complementation confirms that HAB1.1 overexpression restores ABA sensitivity in srpkii mutant. Notably, ABA treatment downregulates SRPKIIs expression via direct transcriptional repression by ABSCISIC ACID INSENSITIVE4 (ABI4), an AP2-domain transcriptional repressor that physically associates with SRPKII promoters. Genetic epistasis analysis positions ABI4 upstream of SRPKIIs, with the srpkii mutation partially rescuing ABA insensitivity in abi4. Mechanistically, SRPKII-mediated phosphorylation of SR45 modulates HAB1 splicing, thereby fine-tuning ABA signaling. Collectively, our findings uncover a transcriptional-phosphorylation regulatory circuit in which SRPKIIs integrate ABA signaling by modulating HAB1 AS, while being reciprocally controlled through ABI4-mediated feedback regulation.
