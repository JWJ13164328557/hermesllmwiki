# Functional analysis of the U-box ubiquitin E3 ligase gene Gracilariopsis lemaneiformis GlPRP19 in heat stress responses.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant science : an international journal of experimental plant biology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43033926](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43033926/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113592](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113592)
- **第一作者**: Zhenyu Jin
- **作者**: Zhenyu Jin, Yijing Chen, Shiji Li, Zhijun Xiong, Wencheng Gong, Xue Sun, Nianjun Xu, Shixia Liu

## 摘要
High temperature has become a major environmental factor affecting the growth and development of marine algae, including the red macroalga Gracilariopsis lemaneiformis. U-box type E3 ligases are well-known for regulating plant growth and stress responses. In this study, we found that GlPRP19, a PRP19 domain-containing E3 ligase, increased heat sensitivity in the model microalgae Phaeodactylum tricornutum. Heterologous overexpression of GlPRP19 reduced algal growth, photosynthetic activity, and activities of superoxide dismutase (SOD) and ascorbate peroxidase (APX), while increasing MDA content. In addition, compared with wild-type, GlPRP19 overexpression in transgenic P. tricornutum significantly downregulated the transcript levels of heat-responsive genes HSP70A, HSP70B, HSP90, HSF and HSF2 under heat stress. Moreover, metabolomic analysis revealed that the levels of several TCA cycle intermediates, ornithine and derivatives of γ-aminobutyric acid (GABA) were significantly suppressed in OE lines compared with WT under heat stress. Additionally, fatty acid profiling further demonstrated that overexpression of GlPRP19 significantly decreased saturated fatty acids and induced a marked accumulation of polyunsaturated fatty acids including C16:3, C18:3, C18:4, and C20:5, indicating an impaired capacity to reduce membrane lipid unsaturation in response to heat stress. Collectively, these results indicated that GlPRP19 might be a negative regulator of thermotolerance. This study lays a foundation for the direct functional characterization of GlPRP19 in G. lemaneiformis using CRISPR/Cas9-mediated knockout, which represents a promising strategy for breeding heat-tolerant algal strains in the future.
