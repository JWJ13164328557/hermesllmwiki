### 核心发现
1. Our findings suggest that LSD1 is a novel epigenetic regulator of coordinated pro-remodeling gene programs and may serve as a potential therapeutic target for the ACCEPTED MANUSCRIPT ARTICLE IN PRESS ARTICLE IN PRESS prevention and treatment of AF.
2. Collectively, these findings identify LSD1 as an upstream epigenetic regulator that promotes Ang II-induced atrial remodeling by independently activating Wnt/β-catenin-dependent hypertrophic signaling and oxidative stress- driven inflammatory pathways, including NLRP3 inflammasome activation, thereby contributing to increased AF susceptibility.
3. Collectively, these findings demonstrate that Ang II infusion induces substantial atrial injury and hypertrophic remodeling, which are associated with enhanced AF susceptibility.
4. LSD1 is up-regulated in Ang II-induced cardiomyocytes To determine whether Ang II directly regulates LSD1 expression in cardiac muscle cells, HL-1 cardiomyocytes were exposed to increasing concentrations of Ang II (0-2 μM) for 24 h.
