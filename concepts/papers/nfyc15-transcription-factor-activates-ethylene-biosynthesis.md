## 摘要
SummaryThe nuclear factor Y (NF‐Y) transcription factors play important roles in plant development and physiological responses. However, the relationship between NF‐Y, plant hormone and plant stress resistance in tropical crops remains unclear. In this study, we identified MeNF‐YC15 gene in the NF‐Y family that significantly responded to Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. manihotis (Xam) treatment. Using MeNF‐YC15‐silenced and ‐overexpressed cassava plants, we elucidated that MeNF‐YC15 positively regulated disease resistance to cassava bacterial blight (CBB). Notably, we illustrated MeNF‐YC15 downstream genes and revealed the direct genetic relationship between MeNF‐YC15 and 1‐aminocyclopropane‐1‐carboxylic acid (ACC) oxidase (MeACO1)‐ethylene module in disease resistance, as evidenced by the rescued disease susceptibility of MeNF‐YC15 silenced cassava plants with ethylene treatment or overexpressing MeACO1. In addition, the physical interaction between 2C‐type protein phosphatase 1 (MePP2C1) and MeNF‐YC15 inhibited the transcriptional activation of MeACO1 by MeNF‐YC15. In summary, MePP2C1‐MeNF‐YC15 interaction modulates ethylene biosynthesis and cassava disease resistance, providing gene network for cassava genetic improvement.

### Introduction
Cassava ( Manihot esculenta Crantz) is not only an important food crop that feeds about one‐seventh of the world's population, but also a special cash crop for starch production (Sonnewald et al ., 2020 ). However, cassava bacterial blight (CBB) is caused by Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. manihotis ( Xam ) is one of the most serious bacterial diseases during cassava growth, resulting in a great threat to loss of yield and quality (Lin et al ., 2019 ). The use of cassava resistance genes to cultivate disease‐resistant varieties is an important way to control CBB, especially transcription factors (TFs) that modulate transcriptional reprogramming in plant defence responses (Zhang et al ., 2023 ). Nuclear factor Y (NF‐Y) is a ubiquitous transcription factor in higher eukaryotes (Oldfield et al ., 2019 ; Pan et al ., 2023 ). NF‐Y is also known as CCAAT‐binding factor (CBF) because of its specific binding to CCAAT‐box (Dolfini et al ., 2012 ; Laloum et al ., 2013 ). NF‐Y transcription factors usually function as heterotrimeric forms, including NF‐YA, NF‐YB and NF‐YC subunits (Priest et al ., 2021 ; Zhou et al ., 2020 ), and they bind to CCAAT‐box and interact with regulatory factors to regulate downstream gene expression (An et al ., 2020 ).
NF‐Y subunits are widely involved in plant growth, development and stress response regulation (Wang et al ., 2021 ). In Arabidopsis, NF‐Y transcription factor COTYLEDON1 (LEC1) regulates seed development by interacting with several transcription factors (Song et al ., 2021 ). In rice, overexpression of OsNF‐YB9 leads to defects in seed development and smaller grains (Niu et al ., 2021 ). Meanwhile, NF‐Ys as flowering activators bind to SUPPRESSOR OF OVEREXPRESSION OF CO1 (SOC1) via interacting with DELLAs, resulting in down‐regulation of SOC1 expression and inhibition of flowering (Hou et al ., 2014 ). In order to explore whether MeNF‐YC15 is involved in regulating the disease resistance of cassava, we analysed its transcription in different cassava varieties with contrasting disease resistance (Ma et al ., 2023 ). Herein, disease‐resistant cassava varieties showed higher transcription of MeNF‐YC15 (Figure 1a ). Using MeNF‐YC15 ‐silenced and ‐overexpressed cassava plants, we clarified that MeNF‐YC15 positively regulated disease resistance to CBB, as evidenced by leaf bacterial number and lesion area during pathogen infection (Figure 1c–h ).

### Discussion
With the change of global warming that promotes disease susceptibility, it is essential to ensure the stability of cassava production for world food security (Wei et al ., 2021 ). Among the top 10 plant pathogens in molecular plant pathology, CBB is listed as the sixth most serious bacterial pathogen in the world (Mansfield et al ., 2012 ), resulting in about 50%–75% of root yield loss (Wydra and Verdier, 2002 ). The use of resistance genes is an effective and sustainable way to control crop diseases (Ma et al ., 2023 ). Although previous studies have identified several genes in the disease resistance of cassava ( MeHSP90 , MeLSD3 and MeERF72 ) (Ma et al ., 2023 ; Wei et al ., 2021 ; Zeng et al ., 2023 ), the disease gene network remains far from clear. In this study, we explored MeNF‐YC15‐ MeACO1 ‐ethylene module in disease resistance and revealed MePP2C1‐MeNF‐YC15 interaction in fine‐tuning MeACO1 ‐ethylene, providing gene network for cassava genetic improvement.
Plant NF‐Y subunit is not only involved in plant development but also important for plant disease resistance. In rice, overexpression of OsNF‐YA2 could improve the disease resistance to rice blast (Alam et al ., 2015 ), and several OsNF‐YAs could interfere with JA signalling to decrease rice viral disease (Tan et al ., 2022 ). In Arabidopsis, Qua‐Quine Starch (QQS) and NF‐YC4 have the potential to overcome the infection of viral and pathogen‐caused diseases (Qi et al ., 2019 ). Here, we revealed that MeNF‐YC15 positively regulated disease resistance and ethylene biosynthesis. NF‐Y family are involved in plant hormone biosynthesis and signal transduction, which might be various for different NF‐YCs and processes. In Arabidopsis, NF‐YC3, NF‐YC4 and NF‐YC9 interact with DELLA protein to regulate GA‐mediated seed germination (Liu et al ., 2016 ).
