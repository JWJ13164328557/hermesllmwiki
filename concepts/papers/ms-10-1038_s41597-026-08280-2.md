# Multi-tissue mRNA-seq, miRNA-seq and single-cell RNA-seq data from heat-stressed lactating Holstein cows

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Scientific Data ()
- **DOI**: [10.1038/s41597-026-08280-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-08280-2)
- **作者**: Zitai Guo, Shengtao Gao, Lu Ma, Dengpan Bu
- **来源**: crossref


## 摘要
Abstract
                  We present a multi-tissue, multi-omics transcriptome dataset from lactating Holstein cows under heat stress (HS) or pair-fed thermoneutral (PFTN) conditions. The dataset comprises: (i) bulk mRNA-seq from five tissues (rumen epithelium, liver, mammary gland, subcutaneous adipose tissue and skeletal muscle); (ii) small RNA-seq (miRNA-seq) from adipose tissue and skeletal muscle; and (iii) single-cell RNA-seq (scRNA-seq) of peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs). Data were generated from three independent experiments using comparable controlled HS versus PFTN designs in environmentally controlled chambers; arteriovenous blood metabolite and hormone measurements are also provided. In total, the dataset includes bulk mRNA-seq from 37 tissue samples across five organs, miRNA-seq from 16 samples, scRNA-seq from six PBMC samples (82,013 cells), and blood biochemistry over a 14-day treatment period. Raw sequencing data and processed matrices newly generated in this study have been deposited in the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO), while the previously published mammary gland and rumen epithelium data remain available under their original SRA accessions; arteriovenous blood biochemistry and additional phenotypic data are available from figshare. This resource enables investigation of tissue-specific and systemic molecular responses to heat stress in dairy cattle.
