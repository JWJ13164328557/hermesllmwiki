# Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi regulate cadmium uptake and detoxification in winter wheat via Cd dose-dependent molecular and cellular mechanisms.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [42217931](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42217931/)

- **作者**: 

## 摘要
Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) enhance cadmium (Cd) tolerance and promote wheat growth, yet their regulatory mechanisms for Cd uptake and detoxification require further elucidation. A pot trial with Cd gradients (0-4 mg/kg) in soil, based on China's environmental quality standard, was conducted to investigate AMF-mediated modulation of Cd uptake, transport and detoxification in winter wheat. Compared to non-inoculation (Nm), AMF significantly (p < 0.05) enhanced wheat biomass and antioxidant enzyme activities, while reducing hydrogen peroxide (8.28 %-27.51 %) and malondialdehyde (7.97 %-25.12 %) contents under high Cd levels (2-4 mg/kg). AMF regulated Cd uptake in dose-dependent manners: under low Cd levels (0-0.6 mg/kg), AMF upregulated TaNramp5 and TaHMA2 expression in roots, increasing Cd concentration in roots (5.05 %-8.12 %) and shoots (10.65 %-24.44 %); whereas under high Cd levels, AMF downregulated these genes, decreasing Cd concentration in roots (18.22 %-23.91 %) and shoots (10.26 %-16.73 %). Moreover, AMF upregulated TaTM20 (25.47 %-68.07 %) and TaHMA3 (32.11 %-114.68 %) in roots, facilitating Cd2+ converted into inactive pectate- and protein-bound forms via enhanced cell wall immobilization and vacuolar compartmentalization. Hierarchical cluster and structural equation modeling confirmed that AMF coordinated multi-detoxification pathways (transcriptional regulation, chemical speciation, subcellular fixation) to improve Cd tolerance. By simulating realistic farmland Cd contamination scenarios, this study elucidated the mechanisms of AMF-mediated Cd detoxification and underscored the potential of AMF inoculation as a cost-effective and ecologically sustainable approach for soil management and crop protection in Cd-contaminated agroecosystems.
