# Interactive Effects of Salicylic Acid and Sowing Dates on Growth and Yield Attributes of Potato (&lt;i&gt;Solanum tuberosum&lt;/i&gt; L.)

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Planta Animalia ()
- **DOI**: [10.71454/pa.005.02.0434](https://doi.org/10.71454/pa.005.02.0434)
- **作者**: Mohammad Ilyas, Muhammad Ismail, Muhammad Amir, Shumaila Siraj, Navid Iqbal
- **来源**: crossref


## 摘要
Optimizing sowing time and exogenous application of salicylic acid (SA) is essential for enhancing growth, tuberization, and yield of potato under abiotic stress conditions, particularly low temperature. A field experiment was conducted during the 2024–2025 growing season to evaluate the interactive effects of different sowing dates and foliar-applied salicylic acid on vegetative growth, tuber size distribution, yield, and plant survival of potato (Solanum tuberosum L.). The experiment consisted of three sowing dates (October 01, October 15, and October 29) and four SA concentrations (0, 1.0, 1.5, and 2.0 mM), arranged in a factorial design. Analysis of variance revealed that sowing dates (SD), salicylic acid levels (SA), and their interaction (SD × SA) had significant effects on all measured parameters. Early sowing on October 01 resulted in significantly higher plant height (50.1 cm), greater leaf number (35 plant⁻¹), increased number of large tubers (2.7 plant⁻¹), maximum total tuber yield (22.0 t ha⁻¹), and highest plant survival (94%). In contrast, delayed sowing on October 29 led to reduced growth, lower yield, decreased survival, and increased production of small-sized tubers. Foliar application of salicylic acid significantly enhanced growth and yield attributes up to an optimum concentration of 1.5 mM. Application at this level increased leaf number, reduced the number of small tubers, improved large tuber formation, enhanced total tuber yield (20.1 t ha⁻¹), and increased plant survival (83%). However, a higher concentration (2.0 mM) exhibited inhibitory effects on several growth and yield traits. The interaction between sowing date and salicylic acid demonstrated that early sowing (October 01) combined with 1.5 mM SA consistently produced superior results across all growth, yield, and survival parameters. These findings suggest that early October sowing coupled with foliar application of salicylic acid at 1.5 mM is an effective agronomic strategy for improving potato productivity and stand establishment under similar agro-ecological conditions.
