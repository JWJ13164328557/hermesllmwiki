### 核心发现
1. These results reveal a novel regulatory network mediated by the miR156a-StSPL9 module, which plays a crucial role in the synchronized regulation of flavonoids and anthocyanins accumulation in potato tubers.
2. Through transient expression assays, we confirmed that StSPL9 is a target of miR156a.
3. These results suggest that key downstream flavonoid pathway genes (e.g., CHS2, F3H, HST, CHS1A, BEATH, CHIL2, F3'5'H, FGGT1, and U79B6) may be regulated by miR156a-StSPL9 module, potentially contributing to the increased accumulation of hesperetin, kaempferol, and quercetin in potato tubers.
4. MicroRNAs (miRNAs) are a class of endogenous, non-coding small RNAs (19-23nt) that primarily regulate the expression of target genes at the post-transcriptional level, playing a crucial role in plant development [15].
5. Furthermore, miR156 delays the vegetative phase transition of plants by controlling its target gene SPL (squamous promoter binding protein-like) [19].
6. Overexpression of miR156 was found to decrease the expression of its target SPL genes, resulting in delayed flowering in plants overexpressing miR156.
