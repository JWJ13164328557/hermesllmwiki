### 核心发现
1. Taken together, heavy metals pose a multifaceted threat to plant systems, driving oxidative stress, metabolic dis­ ruption, and physiological injury.
2. Activation of stress-responsive signalling pathways Redox-dependent signalling cascades play a central role in coordinating systemic responses to heavy metal stress.
3. Redox signals play a central role in coordinating these responses at the plasma membrane, cell wall, and apoplastic interfaces, where metal accumulation is first encountered and regulated (Considine and Foyer, 2014; Hirata, 2019).
4. Taken together, phytohormones integrate redox signals into developmental, metabolic, and transcriptional program­ ming, allowing plants to convert oxidative stress perception into coordinated long-term tolerance strategies.
5. 6. Because heavy-metal expo­ sure results in substantial ROS accumulation, a robust anti­ oxidant response is required to prevent oxidative damage.
7. Preservation of photosynthetic performance Because heavy metals impair photosystem activity and disrupt electron transport, maintaining photosynthetic effi­ ciency is essential for long-term survival.
