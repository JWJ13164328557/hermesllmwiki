### 核心发现
1. Cytokinins, particularly 6-benzylaminopurine, play a central role in promoting shoot organogenesis, but optimal concentrations differ across genotypes and experimental conditions (Koné et al., 2013).
2. Collectively, the protocol provides a technical foundation for breeding support, trait improvement, and scaling of planting material for climate resilient agriculture in Kenya.
3. Callus induction occurred only in media supplemented with auxins, confirming that exogenous plant growth regulators were necessary to initiate dedifferentiation in both landraces.
4. Similar observations have been reported in Bambara groundnut, where auxin supplementation is required for callus initiation (Konaté et al., 2013; Koné et al., 2009).
5. Importantly, the superiority of Yellow with White Eye was consistently maintained across all cytokinin treatments, with higher shoot numbers and faster development compared to Cream with Black Eye.
