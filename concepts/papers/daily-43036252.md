# Diverse Functions of the Phytohormone Cytokinin Across the Plant Kingdom.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [43036252](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43036252/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/jxb/erag559](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erag559)

- **作者**: Eva Mei Shouse, Joseph J Kieber

## 摘要
Cytokinin, an adenine-derived compound, is a phytohormone found throughout the plant kingdom, including species ranging from green algae to land plants. Although its importance in algae is not yet clear, cytokinin is a major regulator of growth and development in land plants, playing diverse and sometimes opposing roles in different species and/or organs. The availability of genome sequences across the plant kingdom, coupled with powerful new tools to analyze gene expression and to edit target genes has led to a major expansion in our understanding of cytokinin function across the plant kingdom beyond the previously well studied model Arabidopsis thaliana (Arabidopsis). In this review, we focus on the developmental functions of cytokinin in a range of non-Arabidopsis species. We discuss how cytokinin acts to modulate growth and differentiation, often in concert with other phytohormones and signals, with many species regulating cytokinin metabolism and/or signaling to promote the development of various structures, including shoots, axillary branches, seeds, vasculature, and epidermal outgrowths.
