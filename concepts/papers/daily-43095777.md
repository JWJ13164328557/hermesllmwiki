# Natural variation in NifU and NifS reveals differences in chloroplast compatibility for nitrogenase engineering.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of experimental botany (2026)
- **PMID**: [43095777](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43095777/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/jxb/erag582](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erag582)
- **第一作者**: Manuel Ene-Ordorica
- **作者**: Manuel Ene-Ordorica, Cristina Vaca-Sanz, Germán Alonso-Tolo, Natalia Makarovsky-Saavedra, Adrian O Sanchez, Francesco Blasio, Leonardo Curatti, Elena Caro

## 摘要
Reconstitution of functional nitrogenase in plants requires an adequate supply of [Fe-S] clusters. NifU and NifS. However, the extent to which heterologous NifU and NifS affect endogenous Fe-S metabolism and affect plant physiology remains unclear. Here, we compared NifU and NifS homologs from diverse diazotrophs to identify candidates for chloroplast nitrogenase engineering. Selected variants from Azotobacter vinelandii, Fischerella thermalis, and Marinobacterium lutimaris were characterized by transient expression in Nicotiana benthamiana and stable transformation in rice. Plant-produced NifU had low apparent [Fe-S] cluster occupancy when isolated but supported in vitro cluster reconstitution and apo-NifH activation with activities following Ft > Av > Ml. Our results establish the biochemical competence of plant-produced NifU after reconstitution but also show limited cluster loading in planta or cluster loss during isolation. Rice plants co-expressing the NifUS pairs differed in growth and proteome profiles, with A. vinelandii-expressing lines exhibiting severe defects, F. thermalis lines showing intermediate phenotype, and M. lutimaris lines being similar to wild type. As small differences in transgene expression levels remain a possible contributor, these associations identify promising NifUS combinations for further testing at tightly controlled expression levels. Thus, trade-offs between the biochemical activity of each NifUS pair and their compatibility with host metabolism must be considered in nitrogenase engineering.
