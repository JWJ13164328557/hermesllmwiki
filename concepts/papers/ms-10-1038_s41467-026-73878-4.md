### 核心发现
1. More recently, the frequency-tagging approach has been introduced, which rapidly presents target stimuli at a fixed rate, e.g., frequency f, so that the stimulus-related neural response also repeats at the same rate.
2. In conclusion, our study demonstrates that the EEG response to familiar names, measured using bedside EEG and automatically analysed using the frequency-tagging method, is related to the outcome of comatose ICU patients.
3. Accurate prediction in the intensive care unit (ICU) is crucial for decision-making regarding the continuation or withdrawal of life-sustaining treatment (WLST).
4. Importantly, backward speech preserves low-level acoustic properties while removing intelligibility and personal salience; therefore, the enhanced tracking for familiar names more plausibly indexes higher-order processing linked to name recognition.

6. Notably, while EEG is a standard prognostic tool in cardiac arrest, its utility in TBI has traditionally been viewed as limited due to lesion heterogeneity.
7. Finally, larger prospective multicentre studies are required to confirm generalizability across aetiologies, define the optimal recording window after injury, and quantify the effects of sedation and peripheral auditory impairment.
