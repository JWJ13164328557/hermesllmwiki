## 摘要

### 核心发现
1. – Cortisol follows a circadian secretion pattern regulated by the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis and plays a central role in the stress response.
2. Consistent with prior literature, these findings suggest that the added burden of repeated transmeridian travel, specifically eastward time-zone crossings, with associated circadian misalignment, places unique stress on athletes that extends beyond the demands of training and competition alone.
3. Collectively, these results emphasize that the cardiovascular and endocrine systems appear vulnerable to travel-related stress, highlighting the need for strategies to mitigate these effects during competitive seasons.
4. An overall participation rate of at least 80% in seasonal competitions was required for travel-group study participants.
5. Notably, at the post-season timepoint (T2), RHR remained significantly higher in the travel group compared to the non-travel group.
