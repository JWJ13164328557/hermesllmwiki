# Functional analysis and breeding application of the ZmDLE1 for maize plant height.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant science : an international journal of experimental plant biology (2027)
- **PMID**: [42918343](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42918343/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113543](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113543)
- **第一作者**: Wenqi Zhou
- **作者**: Wenqi Zhou, Jieshan Liu, Yongsheng Li, Haijun He, Xiaorong Lian, Xiaoyun Dong, Zengke Ma, Kehui Huang

## 摘要
Plant height (PH) and ear height (EH) are critical agronomic traits in maize breeding, directly affecting lodging resistance, mechanical harvesting and yield potential. In this study, we identified a stable dwarf and low ear mutant, zmdle1-1, from an EMS-mutagenized population of the inbred line LY8405. Genetic mapping and allelism tests, together with a single nucleotide variant mutant (zmdle1-2) and a CRISPR-Cas9 knockout mutant (zmdle1-3), confirmed that the candidate gene ZmDLE1 (Zm00001d033231), which encodes Expansin-B4, is responsible for the reduced PH and EH. Compared with wild-type plants, zmdle1 mutants showed 35% lower PH and 63% lower EH, primarily due to a decreased number of internodes below the ear and shortened internode length. Transcriptome analysis revealed differential expression of hormone responsive genes, and exogenous hormone treatments demonstrated that GA₃, IAA and BR could promote PH in the mutants. Endogenous hormone measurements showed significantly reduced GA₁ and GA₃ levels in the mutants, suggesting that ZmDLE1 may regulate PH through GA biosynthesis or signaling. Importantly, when zmdle1-1 was crossed with five elite inbred lines, the resulting hybrids consistently exhibited markedly reduced PH and EH without compromising ear morphology or yield-related traits. Collectively, our findings establish ZmDLE1 as a key regulator of maize plant architecture and provide a valuable dwarfing resource for breeding high-density tolerant and mechanically harvestable maize hybrids.
