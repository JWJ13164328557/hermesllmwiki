### 核心发现
1. Finally, we show that beta-carotene production from formate is possible in our engineered strain, opening the door to formatotrophic eukaryote bioprocesses.
2. Finally, we show that metabolic engineering of cofactor metabolism and oxidative stress resulted in a twofold higher final OD, as well as allowing strong carotene biosynthesis.
3. We used Mitotracker Deep Red, a dye that binds to active mitochondria50, to assess whether NYF1 had more functional mitochondria.
4. Together, these findings suggest that NYF1 has evolved greater degradation of ROS, leading to lower oxidative stress and preserving mitochondrial function when formate is added.
5. Oye2 is required for hydrogen peroxide generation in S.
6. This did not occur with NYF1, suggesting that lowering ROS was required to exploit increased cel- lular reducing power.
