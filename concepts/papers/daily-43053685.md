# The miR164-NAC module differentially regulates drought and salt tolerance in creeping bentgrass.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43053685](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43053685/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag866](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag866)
- **第一作者**: Xiaotong Chen
- **作者**: Xiaotong Chen, Wensong Zhang, Zhaohui Chen, Ryan Watts, Dayong Li, Rui Che, Yu Liu, Zihe Zhu

## 摘要
MicroRNA164 (miR164) has been implicated in boundary formation and stress regulation in model annual species such as Arabidopsis and rice. However, experimental functional and mechanistic studies of miR164 in perennial grasses remain limited, and its broader impact on plant growth and stress performance is unclear. Creeping bentgrass (Agrostis stolonifera L.) is a C3 cool-season perennial turfgrass of major environmental and economic importance. In this study, we generated miR164 overexpression and knockdown creeping bentgrass transgenic lines and profiled their physiology, anatomy, hormone composition, and transcriptomes. We show that miR164 remodels plant architecture and water use. MiR164 overexpression reduces structural rigidity but enhances drought resistance, whereas miR164 knockdown through target mimicry increases biomass and structural rigidity, enhances salt tolerance, but exacerbates drought sensitivity. Direct manipulation of one of the miR164 targets, AsNAC2, reveals a narrow dosage window for normal plant development, as AsNAC2 overexpression and knockdown both severely stunted growth, with the knockdown resulting in a complete developmental arrest. The dwarfism observed in the AsNAC2 overexpression lines required a low water consumption and conferred a superior plant performance under drought. In addition, salt tolerance consistently improved in the miR164 knockdown and the NAC overexpression lines, through the upregulation of a Na⁺/H⁺ antiporter (AsNHX1) by an NAC-dependent stress-regulating binding motif. These findings establish the miR164-NAC module as a potential dosage-sensitive regulatory module that couples morphology, hormones, and ion transport, highlighting its potential for precision tuning to produce perennial grasses with optimized growth and stress resilience.
