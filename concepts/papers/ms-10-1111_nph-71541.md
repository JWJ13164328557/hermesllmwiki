# Elevated CO2 increases C allocation to CaCO3 mineralization relative to net organic C fixation in Ceratostigma willmottianum.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: New Phytologist (2026)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/nph.71541](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.71541)
- **作者**: Long Guo, Cailei Liu, Ting Lei, Jiani Li, Maoqiang Ye
- **来源**: openalex
- **概念**: Mineralization (soil science), Chemistry, Caco-2, Carbonic anhydrase, Carbon fixation

## 摘要
Ceratostigma willmottianum, which can mineralize atmospheric CO2 directly into CaCO3 for long-lasting C sequestration, has both organic and inorganic C sequestration functions. However, how elevated CO2 affects CaCO3 mineralization and the allocation of C between the two C sequestration remains unclear. Therefore, C. willmottianum was exposed to five CO2 concentrations (400, 550, 700, 850, and 1000 ppm) in combination with 13C isotope labeling, carbonic anhydrase (CA) inhibitor treatments, and transient overexpression assays to systematically investigate these effects. Results showed that 700 ppm CO2 was the most effective in promoting plant growth, photosynthesis, and CaCO3 accumulation, whereas 1000 ppm CO2 exerted an inhibitory effect. 13C tracing revealed that elevated CO2 increased C allocation to CaCO3 mineralization from 3.03% (400 ppm) to 4.62% (700 ppm), while decreasing that to net organic C fixation from 63.40 to 60.51%. CA inhibitor experiments further indicated that extracellular CA plays a key role in promoting more C allocation to CaCO3 mineralization. Gene expression and functional validation identified CwβCA2 as a key gene responding to elevated CO2 and promoting CaCO3 mineralization. This study provides a theoretical basis and candidate gene resources for developing biomineralization-based C capture and storage technologies to cope with rising CO2 in the future.

岷江蓝雪花(Ceratostigma willmottianum)能够将大气中的CO2直接矿化为CaCO3，实现长期碳封存，因此兼具有机固碳与无机矿化固碳两种功能。然而，关于升高CO2如何影响CaCO3矿化，以及碳在两种固碳途径之间的分配机制，目前尚不清楚。 为此，本研究设置五个CO2浓度处理(400、550、700、850和1000 ppm)，结合13C同位素标记、碳酸酐酶(CA)抑制剂处理和瞬时过表达实验，系统探究上述问题。 结果表明，700 ppm CO2对促进植物生长、光合作用和CaCO3积累效果最为显著，而1000 ppm CO2则表现出抑制作用。13C示踪显示，CO2浓度升高使分配到CaCO3矿化的碳比例从400 ppm时的3.03%增至700 ppm时的4.62%，而分配到净有机碳固定的比例则从63.40%降至60.51%。CA抑制剂实验进一步表明，胞外CA在促进更多碳分配至CaCO3矿化中起关键作用。基因表达与功能验证实验鉴定出CwβCA2是响应CO2升高、促进CaCO3矿化的关键基因。 本研究为发展基于生物矿化的碳捕集与封存技术、应对未来大气CO2升高提供了理论依据和候选基因资源。.
