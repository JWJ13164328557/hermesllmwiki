### 核心发现
1. The need for eucalyptus-specific risk indicators is further underscored by the temperature targets established under the Paris Agreement, which aims to limit global warming to 1.5–2 °C above pre-industrial levels (UNFCCC 2015).
2. Developing risk indicators is essential to quantify vulnerabilities and anticipate potential losses in Eucalyptus plantations.
3. For these reasons, assessing risks to Eucalyptus across different GWLs (1.5 °C to 4 °C) is essential to support cli­ mate-resilient forest planning and to underpin adaptation measures aimed at reducing vulnerabilities and economic losses.
