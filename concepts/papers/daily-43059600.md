# Dual role of FpSBT1 from Fusarium pseudograminearum in inducing plant immunity and promoting virulence.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Pest management science (2026)
- **PMID**: [43059600](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43059600/)
- **DOI**: [10.1002/ps.71511](https://doi.org/10.1002/ps.71511)
- **第一作者**: Yuxin Xie
- **作者**: Yuxin Xie, Xiu Wang, Jiabao Xu, Ji Wu, Mingyu Xu, Jinli Xiong, Jiawei Wang, Yeqiang Xia

## 摘要
BACKGOUND: Fusarium pseudograminearum, the causal agent of Fusarium crown rot, secretes numerous proteins during infection, but the mechanisms by which these proteins regulate plant immunity and fungal virulence remain largely unknown.

RESULTS: Herein, we identified a secreted subtilisin-like protease, FpSBT1, which induces cell death in Nicotiana benthamiana. The cell death-inducing activity of FpSBT1 requires both the I9 and S8 domains, as well as its three catalytic residues (Asp169, His200 and Ser356), and functions independently of the immune co-receptors BAK1 and SOBIR1. Furthermore, FpSBT1 triggered multiple immune responses. Deletion of FpSBT1 significantly reduced fungal pathogenicity, conidiation and hyphal penetration, whereas deletion of its homolog FpSBT2 alone had no such effects. However, the FpSBT1 and FpSBT2 double deletion mutant exhibited additional growth defects beyond those observed in the FpSBT1 single mutant. Abiotic stress tolerance assays revealed that neither FpSBT1 nor FpSBT2 participates in the fungal response to abiotic stress.

CONCLUSION: Collectively, our findings demonstrate that FpSBT1 functions dually to elicit plant immunity and as a virulence factor essential for full pathogenicity, providing new insights into the F. pseudograminearum-wheat interaction. © 2026 Society of Chemical Industry.
