### 核心发现
1. Most importantly, we found that all spermatozoa displaying hyperactivated motility main- tained their quadrilateral CatSper Ca2+ domains and were tyro- sine phosphorylated (Figures 6D–6F).
2. We conclude that intact CatSper domains and focused P-Tyr are required for sustained hyperactivated motility and suc- cessful fertilizing capacity.
3. CatSper Is Required for Efﬁcient Sperm Migration in the Oviduct Thus far, our results suggest that a population of in vitro capaci- tated sperm represent both intact and degenerating and/or nonfertilizing spermatozoa.
4. Indeed, we found a CatSper-like distribu- tion for the constitutively active form of CaMKII (phosphorylated at threonine 286, P-CaMKII; Figure 3A) in the principal piece.
5. We conclude that manipulating Ca2+ inﬂux recapitulates the extent of P-Tyr, but proper sperm motility requires a spatially localized network.
6. Here, we report 62 P-Tyr peptides on 45 proteins common to both WT and CatSper1-null spermatozoa, the most compre- hensive list of P-Tyr sperm proteins to date.
7. We found that capacitation induces P-Tyr of nine peptides on six metabolic proteins.
8. These data suggest that disruption of the CatSper channel complex deregulates the capacitation-initiated tyrosine phosphorylation pathway.
