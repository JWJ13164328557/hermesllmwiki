### 核心发现
1. Here we show that RNF220 ablation triggers severe synapsis disruption at pachytene and germ cell apoptosis, thereby recapitulating the phenotype of Syce1 haploinsufficient mice [13].
2. Mechanistically, SYCE1, a core component of the synaptonemal complex central element essential for synapsis, was identified as a testicular target of RNF220.
3. Ring finger protein 220 (RNF220), a RING-type E3 ubiquitin ligase, has been shown to be widely involved in neural development and human diseases through different target proteins[16].
4. RNF220 functions through mediating either K48-linked polyubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of its target or K63-linked ubiquitination or monoubiquitination[17–22] of the substrates to regulate their localization or activity.
5. In this study, we generated a germ cell-specific conditional knockout mouse model to investigate the physiological function and potential molecular target of RNF220 during spermatogenesis.
6. Peanut agglutinin (PNA) binds to the acrosome and can be used to identify the stages of sperm morphogenesis and classify the corresponding cell types during the seminiferous epithelial cycle.
7. In summary, RNF220 deficiency also impairs chromatin condensation during spermiogenesis.
