### 核心发现
1. We show that EgrARF10 is nucleus-localised and strongly associated with SCW biosynthetic genes and transcription factors across co- expression and multi-omic networks.
2. Heterologous overexpression of EgrARF10 in hybrid poplar did not alter overall growth or morphology but resulted in a significant reduction in stem lignification, accompanied by relative increases in D-glucose, D-xylose and D-mannose, indicating altered SCW composition.
3. Since PB1 domains of classes B and C PB1 are thought to have a reduced capacity, to interact with Aux/IAAs (Guilfoyle & Hagen, 2012), they are generally regarded as auxin-independent or non-canonical with some documented exceptions.

7. Together, these results suggest that EgrARF10 may play roles in vari­ ous aspects of post-embryonic development.
