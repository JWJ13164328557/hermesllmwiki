### 核心发现
1. Analysis of epididymal white adipose tissue (eWAT), a previously identified target of endostatin, revealed that endostatin lowered the expression of several genes that are critical to the metabolic activities of adipocytes, including the Pparg2 isoform that regulates adipogenesis44 (Pparg2, Srebf1, Lipe, Mgll, Fabp4).
2. Clock gene expression profiles confirmed efficient synchronization, and we found that endostatin secretion peaked earlier in Bmal1hep-/- hepatocytes and was increased at several timepoints compared to WT hepatocytes (Fig.
3. The mouse liver circadian proteome is enriched for well-characterized secreted proteins6,7 and a recent study showed that at the transcript and protein level, the clock protein BMAL1 specifically targets and regulates proteins annotated as secreted8.
4. Collectively, multiple approaches can facilitate mechanistic insights into hepatokine regulation and function.
5. These data show that an acute ex vivo approach is suitable to interrogate hepatic protein secretion.
6. These results show that the hepatocyte clock regulates protein secretion tied to hepatic and systemic functions.
7. These findings suggest that the repressive arm of the clock, or CRY proteins in concert with other transcription factors, temporally regulate Col18a1 transcription.
8. An active classical secretory pathway is required for the generation of a robust population-level rhythm among a group of non-neuronal peripheral cells11.
