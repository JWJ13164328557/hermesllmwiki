### 核心发现
1. In summary, the main purpose of this study is to use conven- tional serological indicators to screen independent predictors of MASLD in T2DM patients and construct a non-invasive, efﬁcient and low-cost diagnostic model.
2. In summary, as a core marker reﬂecting hepatic lipotoxicity, the high weight of TG in this model has a solid pathophysiological basis.
3. This study shows that BMI is signiﬁcantly higher in the MASLD group than in the Non-case group, providing clinical data support for the above mechanism.
4. By incorporating BMI, a simple anthropometric indicator, the diagnostic model constructed in this study provides clinicians with an intuitive risk stratiﬁcation tool.
5. In summary, this study successfully constructs and internally validates a high-precision nomogram model (AUC > 0.9) for diagnosing T2DM complicated by MASLD, based on BMI and seven routine serological indicators.
6. Notably, these studies collectively highlight that chronic low-grade inﬂammation is the shared pathophysiological basis connecting metabolic disorders, cardiovascular events and liver disease.
