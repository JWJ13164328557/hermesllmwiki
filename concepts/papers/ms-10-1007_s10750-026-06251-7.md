### 核心发现
1. We found a crossed response to rainfall: sediment properties differed most strongly between rainfall periods in A.
2. Conclusions Our results reveal a crossed response to rainfall vari- ability across mangrove types.
3. We predicted that rainfall effects would interact with mangrove dominance, producing a crossed response in which sediment properties and crab assemblages would not necessarily vary in parallel.
4. Collectively, these patterns are con- sistent with rainfall-period differences interacting with local hydrodynamics and site exposure, poten- tially contributing to the redistribution of fine sedi- ments and organic carbon within or among sites.
5. Notably, the largest shifts in sediment properties occurred in A.
