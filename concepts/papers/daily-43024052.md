# Integrating k-mer-based markers and oligo-FISH to uncover chromosome translocations associated with DNA hypomethylation in sugarcane × Tripidium arundinaceum hybrids.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: TAG. Theoretical and applied genetics. Theoretische und angewandte Genetik (2026)
- **PMID**: [43024052](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43024052/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s00122-026-05441-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00122-026-05441-0)
- **第一作者**: Zehuai Yu
- **作者**: Zehuai Yu, Zetian Cui, Li Xue, Jiarui Liu, Zilan Zhuang, Yiying Qi, Qilong Ling, Fei Qin

## 摘要
Chromosomal translocations play a pivotal role in generating structural diversity during sugarcane breeding, yet the molecular mechanisms underlying these events remain elusive. Here, we developed k-mer-based whole-genome molecular markers (WGMMs) to precisely track Tripidium arundinaceum (2n = 4x or 6x = 40, 60) chromosomal segments in sugarcane hybrids. WGMMs successfully identified targeted T. arundinaceum chromosomal segments in sugarcane hybrids, and these findings were validated by oligonucleotide fluorescence in situ hybridization (Oligo-FISH) cytology and sequencing analyses. Combined WGMM and Oligo-FISH analyses revealed a recurrent translocation between chromosomes 4 and 6 of T. arundinaceum, with the breakpoints coinciding with pericentromeric regions enriched in autonomous long terminal repeat retrotransposons (LTR-RTs). Whole-genome bisulfite sequencing further revealed significantly reduced CG and CHG methylation levels within these hotspots, suggesting an association between local hypomethylation, LTR-RT enrichment, and chromosomal instability. These findings provide a framework for investigating how repetitive sequence architecture and epigenetic variation may contribute to chromosome rearrangements in polyploid sugarcane.
