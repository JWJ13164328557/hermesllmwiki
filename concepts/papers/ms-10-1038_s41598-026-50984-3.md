### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that the combined use of CQD-NPs and AMF can alleviate Cd-induced stress and serve as a sustainable strategy for the eco-friendly production of medicinal plants.
2. The application of arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) as a biofertilizer significantly enhances plant physiological performance under both optimal and stress conditions.
3. They play a crucial role in metal tolerance and accumulation, improve plant growth, facilitate pollutant removal, reduce fertilizer requirements in crop production, and contribute to soil structure and health25.
4. Nanomaterials are believed to play a key role in investigating these agricultural challenges in the future.
5. These results confirm the presence of oxygen-containing surface functional groups, essential for CQD-NPs stability and reactivity in aqueous solutions.
6. Notably, the maximum EO content (1.90%) was recorded under moderate Cd stress when plants were treated with AMF + CQD-NPs20.
7. Notably, within the Mentha genus, grapefruit mint is one of the few species in which linalool constitutes the dominant EO component6.
8. Additionally, this association enhances photosynthetic activity by promoting nutrient acquisition, particularly phosphorus and nitrogen, which are essential for Chl biosynthesis and photosynthetic function.
