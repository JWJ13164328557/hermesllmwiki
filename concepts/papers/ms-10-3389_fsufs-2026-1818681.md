### 核心发现
1. Rich in carbohydrates, proteins, fats, miner­ als (calcium and iron), and vitamins A, B, and C, okra plays a crucial role in human nutrition (El-Kader et al., 2010; de Oliveira et al., 2014).
2. These results suggest an inherent genetic character­ istic which may favor Konni responsivity.
3. These findings suggest that plants only absorb the nutrients they need, making excess inputs unnecessary.
4. Notably, none of the treatments increased soil organic carbon or available phosphorus, an unexpected result given the high carbon content of the PM applied.
5. Acknowledging this gap and aware of the competitive promotional environments of these two varieties, their comparative evaluation across different soil fertility management strategies is necessary to support their adoption and significantly enhance okra productivity in Benin.
6. Interestingly, the enhanced photosynthetic surface may have increased competition among vegetative organs for water, nutri­ ents, and assimilates, limiting the flowering and fruiting of AVOK1504.
7. Notably, sole poultry manure (T2) increased soil pH, total nitrogen, calcium, and mag­ nesium, highlighting its role in soil fertility improvement.
