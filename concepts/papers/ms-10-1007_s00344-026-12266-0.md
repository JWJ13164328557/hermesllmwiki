### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that lncRNAs play a key role in driving the divergent physiolog­ ical outcomes observed between the tolerant and sensitive genotypes.
2. Interestingly, while novel_47 (targeting the DNA repair gene BRCA2) was upregulated in both genotypes, its silencing effect appeared specific to the sensitive cultivar, implying that Perola possesses down­ stream buffering mechanisms—potentially via target site masking or ceRNA decoys—that prevent this miRNA from compromising genomic integrity.
3. These results redefine heat tolerance as the capacity to prevent complex regulatory changes and prioritize metabolic protection, identifying specific ncRNA-mRNA interactions, particularly novel_42/SPL14 and Albumin-2, as promising targets for breeding climate-resilient crops.
4. In this model, lncRNAs and circRNAs “compete” for shared miRNA bind­ ing sites, acting as molecular sponges that sequester miR­ NAs, thereby releasing repression of their target mRNAs (Salmena et al.
5. This study establishes a robust framework for breeding heat-tolerant legumes to meet the demands of climate-resilient agriculture (Mukankusi et al.
6. Potential miRNA–target interactions were predicted using psRNATarget (expectation ≤ 3.0) and TargetFinder (score ≤ 4) (Bo and Wang 2005).
7. To reduce false positives, computational predictions were rigorously filtered by expression correlation analysis; only miRNA– target pairs exhibiting significant anti-correlation (Pearson correlation |r| > 0.9, p < 0.01) were retained.
8. The predicted targets of these miRNAs suggest their pivotal involvement in regulating flowering time and mediating heat-stress signaling pathways.
