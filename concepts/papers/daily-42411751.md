# Domain compositions of Arabidopsis Toll/Interleukin-1 Receptor/Resistance domain-containing TX14 proteins affect localization and induction of the hypersensitive response.

## 摘要
Plant Toll/Interleukin-1 Receptor/Resistance (TIR) domains produce small signaling molecules that activate immune responses. In this study we show that the Arabidopsis TX14 gene encodes four distinct protein isoforms that are generated through alternative splicing. These isoforms differ in their overall domain composition and the integrity of the TIR domain. Among them, TX14 2IR, an isoform composed solely of an intact TIR domain, was found to be both necessary and sufficient to induce the hypersensitive response (HR). Mechanistically, TX14 2IR localizes to both the cytoplasm and the nucleus, whereas the two other functional isoforms, TX14 Full and Fusion, localize exclusively to the cytoplasm. Furthermore, simultaneous mutation of four clustered basic amino acids in TX14 2IR abolished its deoxyribonuclease activity and delayed HR induction. Collectively, our findings demonstrate that the domain architecture of TIR domain-containing proteins influences their subcellular localization and their dependency on ENHANCED DISEASE SUSCEPTIBILITY 1 in mediating the HR phenotype.

### 核心发现
1. These results suggest that HR induction by TX14 in Arabidopsis is coordinated by AS, which targets the catalytically versatile TIR domains to multiple locations in the cell.
2. In this study we show that the Arabidopsis TX14 gene encodes four distinct protein isoforms that are generated through alternative splicing.
3. In this study, we report that TX14 encodes four distinct transcripts through AS: three resulting from AS of TX14 -derived sequences and the fourth produced by transcripts from both TX14 and CHIQUITA1-like 7 ( CHIQL7 ), located downstream of TX14.
4. We found that gAtTX14 transgenic plants, in which 1.3 kb of genomic DNA covering from the start to the stop codon of TX14 was overexpressed under the control of the constitutive CaMV 35S promoter, showed stunted growth leading to dwarf phenotypes ( ).
5. Consistent with a previous report that defense responses and associated growth defects induced by TX14 depend on EDS1 in Arabidopsis ( ), we found that HR responses induced by TX14 2IR and Full were abolished in an the Nbeds1 mutant (; ), which was generated by the CRISPR/Cas9 system ( ).
6. We found that TX14 2IR, Full, and Fusion with the intact TIR domain formed homodimers when fused to bait and prey proteins, allowing the growth of co-transformed yeast cells on selective media ( ).
7. We found that AtTX14 2IR could be exclusively directed to either the cytosol or the nucleus when YFP-NES or YFP-NLS was translationally fused to its C-terminus ( ).
8. Collectively, our findings demonstrate that the domain architecture of TIR domain-containing proteins influences their subcellular localization and their dependency on ENHANCED DISEASE SUSCEPTIBILITY 1 in mediating the HR phenotype.
