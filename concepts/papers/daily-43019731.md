# Ultrasound-assisted deep eutectic solvent two-phase system for enhanced simultaneous recovery of lycopene and polysaccharides from tomato pomace: Process optimization, stability, and mechanistic insights.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food chemistry (2026)
- **PMID**: [43019731](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019731/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.foodchem.2026.152082](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodchem.2026.152082)
- **第一作者**: Ting Di
- **作者**: Ting Di, Jinyu Wu, Genlin Zhang, Yan Zhang, Yanyan Dang

## 摘要
This study developed an ultrasonic-assisted deep eutectic solvent two-phase system (U-DES-TP) for simultaneous extraction and enrichment of lycopene and polysaccharides from tomato pomace. After optimization, the optimal system was 40% (w/w) Dec-Oct-EG (1:1:2) with 5% (w/w) sodium citrate. Under the optimal conditions (solid-to-liquid ratio 1:39, 296 W, 35 °C, 40 min), yielded 108.93 mg/100 g lycopene and 174.62 mg/g polysaccharide, increases of 89.41% (vs. n-hexane) and 70.59% (vs. water). The polysaccharide was an acidic heteropolysaccharide rich in galacturonic acid and rhamnose. Both extracts showed strong antioxidant activities (lycopene: ABTS 434.72, DPPH 91.79 mg TE/g; polysaccharide: ABTS 386.19, DPPH 149.79 mg TE/g). Lycopene retention in DES (84.89%, 40 °C, 120 min) exceeded n-hexane (64.13%). Mechanistic analysis (FT-IR, 1H NMR, COSMO-RS) indicated that ethylene glycol strengthened hydrogen bonding within the DES, improved solute-solvent interactions, and reduced activity coefficients, thereby enhancing lycopene solubility. This work provides an efficient green strategy for valorizing tomato pomace.
