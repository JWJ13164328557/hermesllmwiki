### 核心发现
1. To further develop ATK as a new vertebrate model for cardiac aging, in this study, we report the first scRNA-seq dataset generated from young (8 weeks-old; week 8 hereafter) and old (16 weeks-old; week 16 hereafter) female GRZ strain ATK hearts (equivalent to 30 and 60 years of age in humans, respectively).
2. We found ten different cell clusters in this dataset and age- associated cell composition changes particularly in macrophages.
3. In summary, this dataset captures numerous cell-type specific genetic factors associated with ARTICLE IN PRESS ARTICLE IN PRESS cardiac aging and lays a solid foundation for ATK to serve as an emerging experimental model for interrogating normative cardiac aging.
