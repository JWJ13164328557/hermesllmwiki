4. In addition to known phenotypic and physiological responses in other model plants (Shanker et al., 2014), we found that fewer DEGs were positively asso­ ciated with higher drought tolerance in N.
5. Focusing on the major diterpenoids produced in trichomes, we found that the expression of key DEGs was associated with diterpenoid levels under drought stress.
