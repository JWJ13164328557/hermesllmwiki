## 摘要
Strigolactones (SLs) were initially identified as rhizosphere signals that trigger germination of parasitic weeds and promote branching in arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi. More recently, SLs have been characterized as a class of carotenoid-derived plant hormones that regulate plant architecture and stress responses. This review systematically summarizes their diverse functions in shaping shoot architecture and root development, as well as their ability to mediate acclimation to various abiotic and biotic stresses. It also discusses the canonical signaling module composed of D14, MAX2/D3, and D53/SMXLs and its extensive interactions with other hormonal pathways. Finally, this review suggests that future research should focus on elucidating the dynamic responses to environmental stress mediated by the SL pathway, decoding the functional diversification of SLs in different plant species, and leveraging SLs as rhizosphere signals to control parasitic weeds. Precise spatiotemporal modulation of SL activity is crucial for balancing its functional complexity and will contribute to designing crops with optimized plant architectures and enhanced stress resilience.

### 核心发现
1. In summary, SLs play a pivotal role in regulating various aspects of root system architecture, including primary root growth, lateral root formation, root hair elongation, and adventitious root development ( ).
2. Interestingly, the loss of Short Hypocotyl 2 (SHY2), a key regulator of auxin and CKs, abolished the ability of SLs to regulate lateral roots (;;; ), indicating that SHY2 plays a pivotal role in the interaction SL, CK and auxin signaling pathways ( ).
3. The essential role of the F‐box protein suggests that SL signaling involves ubiquitination‐dependent degradation of target proteins.
4. Upon SL perception, D14 interacts with D3/MAX2 and D53/SMXL6, 7, 8 in an SL‐dependent manner, leading to the ubiquitination and degradation of the repressors D53/SMXL6, 7, 8 and the activation of downstream transcriptional responses (;;;; ).
5. Among these processes, D14 phosphorylation plays a key role in low‐nitrogen‐repressed tillering ( ).
6. The D14–D3/MAX2–D53/SMXL6, 7, 8 module plays a central role in SL signaling (;;;, ).
7. The transcription factors SQUAMOSA PROMOTER BINDING PROTEIN‐LIKE 9 (SPL9), SPL15, BES1 ( bri1 ‐EMS‐SUPPRESSOR 1), and TCP4 interact with SMXL6, 7, and 8 and directly bind the BRC1 promoter to regulate its transcriptional activation (;; ).
8. BRC1 directly activates the expression of Homeobox Protein 21 ( HB21 ), 40, and 53, thereby stimulating abscisic acid (ABA) biosynthesis and repressing outgrowth in axillary buds (;;; ).
