### 核心发现
1. Five priority targets were identified, each characterized by distinctive structural– hydrothermal signatures indicative of porphyry-style mineral systems.
2. However, identifying new deposits has become increasingly challenging as easily detectable surface mineralization is progressively depleted and exploration targets become deeper and more complex [4].
3. Geochemical layer The geochemical layer of stream sediments—one of the most effective direct indicators for identifying areas susceptible to mineralization—captures geochemical signals derived from hydrothermal and mineralizing systems in upstream regions and therefore plays a crucial role in exploration studies [58-60].
4. Consequently, identifying and analyzing the mechanical and geochemical behavior of these units is essential for targeted exploration and delineating Cu mineralization potential within the Urmia–Dokhtar magmatic arc.
5. Therefore, it is essential to convert all layers to a common scale prior to integration.
