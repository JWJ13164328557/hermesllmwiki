# Sugarcane mosaic virus HC-Pro-triggered hypersensitive cell death is modulated by interaction with P3N-PIPO.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43053684](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43053684/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag859](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag859)
- **第一作者**: Guangyuan Cheng
- **作者**: Guangyuan Cheng, Zongtao Yang, Zhe Chai, Xingyu Liu, Kang Zeng, Haoming Liu, Lu Wang, Guoqiang Huang

## 摘要
Sugarcane mosaic virus (SCMV) causes heavy yield losses in both sugarcane and maize. However, the hypersensitive cell death (HCD) induced by SCMV helper component proteinase (HC-Pro), an RNA silencing suppressor (RSS), has not been characterized. Here, we show that transient expression of HC-Pro of sugarcane isolate (SCMVSc), but not HC-ProZm of maize isolate (SCMVZm), induces dose-dependent HCD in Nicotiana benthamiana and other plant species. HC-Pro-induced HCD is independent of salicylic acid, reactive oxygen species, and receptor kinases BAK1/SOBIR1, but dependent on Ca2+ influx and helper nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat receptors ADR1/N NRG1. The conserved F345 in HC-Pro is key to HCD induction, whereas the corresponding residue in HC-ProZm is Y345. HC-ProZmY345F induces HCD in N. benthamiana leaves. Correspondingly, SCMVZm-HC-ProY345F-GFP demonstrated markedly decreased infectivity on N. benthamiana and maize compared with SCMVZm-GFP. Furthermore, we show that the interaction between SCMVSc movement protein P3N-PIPO and HC-Pro enhances the RSS activity of HC-Pro and inhibits the intercellular diffusion of silencing signals and HC-Pro-induced HCD, and that the RSS activity of HC-Pro is not required for HCD induction. Together, these findings reveal that SCMV employs different strategies to circumvent HC-Pro-induced HCD in plants, shedding light on the co-evolution mechanisms between SCMV and its hosts sugarcane and maize.
