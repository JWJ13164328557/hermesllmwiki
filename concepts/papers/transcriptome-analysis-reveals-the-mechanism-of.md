### 核心发现
1. Weighted Gene Co-expression Network Analysis (WGCNA) was performed to screen gene modules or core genes linked to phenotypic traits of interest or target samples, using the WGCNA package (v1.51) in R (Liu et al., 2022).
2. SuS downregulation has minimal effect on sucrose content; however, this downregulation significantly inhibits starch synthesis in transgenic potato tubers (Zhao et al., 2022).
3. Overall, these results suggest that nitrogen fertilization significantly upregulated the amylo­ pectin synthesis pathway, which was more active in both varieties.
4. Notably, no large granules (50–300 μm) were formed under the high‑nitrogen treatment (N3).
5. Notably, the N0 treatment was significantly different from that of other treatments in the S2 and S3 stages in both varieties, whereas N1 and N2 treatments had a high similarity in their transcrip­ tional programs.
6. Notably, nitrogen fertilization-regulated modules primarily occurred in the S2 and S3 stages, consistent with the PCA results.
