# Cell-type-specific dosage reprogramming reveals epidermal barrier-based gray mold defense strategy in autotetraploid strawberry.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: The New phytologist (2026)
- **PMID**: [43026182](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43026182/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/nph.71754](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.71754)
- **第一作者**: Qiang Cao
- **作者**: Qiang Cao, Shipeng Yang, Ni Zhu, Jie Lu, Lingchun Kong, Bo Yuan, Xinyue Zhang, Yushan Liu

## 摘要
Whole-genome duplication (WGD) triggers genetic and epigenetic remodeling that confers stress adaptation in plants, but how these responses act at single-cell resolution to shape adaptive phenotypes remains unclear. Here, we generated autotetraploid Fragaria nilgerrensis via colchicine treatment, which showed gigantism (enlarged leaves and stomata) and enhanced gray mold resistance. Integrated multi-omics analysis showed that while the expression of many genes remained close to the diploid levels post-WGD, defense pathway genes displayed dosage sensitivity contributing to adaptive traits. Genome-wide transposable element (TE) hypermethylation stabilized the genome and may contribute to dosage by suppressing adjacent gene expression, and differential promoter/gene-body methylation may contribute to distinct gene-dosage responses. Single-nucleus transcriptomics revealed cell-type heterogeneity in dosage responses, with epidermal cells undergoing regulatory network reprogramming toward enhanced physical barriers through increased expression of genes involved in cell wall and cuticle reinforcement. Functional validation confirmed LAC9 as a core gene governing gray mold resistance: Its overexpression improved resistance in diploids, whereas its silencing weakened resistance in tetraploids. LAC9 may increase lignin content to strengthen cell walls and restrain pathogen adhesion and penetration. This work elucidates how WGD enhances adaptive potential through coordinated gene dosage, DNA methylation, and cell-specific reprogramming, offering mechanistic insights and targets for polyploid-based crop improvement.

全基因组加倍(WGD)能够驱动遗传和表观遗传重塑，赋予植物适应胁迫的能力，但这些响应如何在单细胞水平塑造适应性表型目前仍不清楚。本研究利用秋水仙素处理创制了黄毛草莓(Fragaria nilgerrensis)同源四倍体，该材料呈现器官巨大化特征(叶片和气孔增大)，同时灰霉病抗性显著增强。多组学整合分析表明，全基因组加倍后，大量基因的表达水平与二倍体基本持平，而防御通路相关基因表现出明显的剂量敏感性，可能推动适应性性状的形成。全基因组转座元件(TE)发生超甲基化，既维持了基因组稳定性，并可能通过抑制邻近基因的表达参与基因剂量调控；启动子和基因体甲基化的差异则可能驱动了剂量响应基因与剂量补偿基因的形成。单核转录组分析揭示，基因剂量响应存在细胞类型异质性: 表皮细胞发生明显的调控网络重编程，通过上调细胞壁和角质层强化相关基因的表达，增强物理屏障。功能验证证实，LAC9 是调控灰霉病抗性的核心基因: 在二倍体中过表达该基因可提高抗性，而在四倍体中沉默该基因则削弱抗性。LAC9 可能通过提高木质素含量来强化细胞壁，进而限制病原菌的附着和侵入。本研究阐明了全基因组加倍如何通过基因剂量、DNA甲基化及细胞类型特异性重编程的协同作用，增强植物的适应潜力，为利用多倍体作物改良提供了理论机制与基因靶标。.
