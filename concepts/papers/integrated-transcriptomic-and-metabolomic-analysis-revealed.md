1. These results suggest that the tubers accumulated significantly different flavonoids at moderate altitudes, compared to tubers grown at higher or lower altitudes.
2. Notably, the pelargonidin derivatives were uniquely accumulated in red fleshed potatoes, suggesting that they were the primary sources of the red pigments.
