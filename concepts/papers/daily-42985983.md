# SlMED2 regulates locule number through the SlTPL-SlKNU floral meristem module.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of integrative plant biology (2026)
- **PMID**: [42985983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42985983/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/jipb.70437](https://doi.org/10.1111/jipb.70437)
- **第一作者**: Sufu Gan
- **作者**: Sufu Gan, Yong Kang, Can Luo, Ying Li, Ying Gao, Jianghua Cai, Leilei Zhou, Mondher Bouzayen

## 摘要
The Mediator complex subunit SlMED2 controls carpel number in tomato through promoter-proximal and long-range genomic interactions with the SlTPL-SlKNU floral meristem module, which represses SlWUS and SlCLV3, resulting in termination of stem cell activity.
