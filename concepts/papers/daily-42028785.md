# Development of a qualitative detection method for the pSIM1278 construct in genetically modified potato using real-time PCR.

## 摘要
Innate® genetically modified (GM) potato events have been developed to confer desirable traits such as black spot resistance and decreased acrylamide levels. The first-generation event E12 and second-generation events X17, Y9, and Z6, which possess additional traits, including further sugar reduction and late blight resistance, have been approved for food use in several countries. Considering that these GM potatoes may be in commerce, they need to be appropriately monitored and labeled to ensure consumers' right to informed choice. All of these events share the genetic construct pSIM1278. We developed a real-time PCR method targeting pSIM1278 that enables simultaneous screening of all Innate® GM potato events in a single reaction. The assay's sensitivity and specificity were evaluated, and its practical utility was demonstrated through a distribution survey of imported potato products in Japan. This method provides a useful tool for monitoring GM potatoes containing the pSIM1278 sequence in foods.

### 核心发现
1. Notably, these assays identified only the target GM events (i.e., other GM events were undetectable) (Figure S4).
2. Given the tetraploid nature and high heterozygosity of the potato genome, RNAi-mediated gene silencing is an effective strategy for modulating target gene activity.
3. Because the inserted sequences in Innate® GM potatoes were derived from the potato genome ( ), DNA-based detection of these GM events requires methods that target either the junctions between the insert and the host genomic DNA or the junctions within synthetic gene cassettes that do not occur naturally.
4. A control plasmid containing the pSIM1278 target sequence (Figure S1) was synthesized (Eurofins Genomics K.K., Tokyo, Japan) and linearized using the restriction enzyme ScaI (New England Biolabs, Massachusetts, USA).
5. The pSIM1278 target sequence was then analyzed by ddPCR for the absolute quantification of the control plasmid.
6. These results suggest that the lowest detectable copy number for the pSIM1278 detection method (LOD 95% ) is between 5.0 and 10 copies per reaction ( ).
7. This satisfies the performance criterion that LOD 95% of a qualitative real-time PCR method should not exceed 20 copies of the target sequence.
8. In conclusion, the pSIM1278-targeting real-time PCR assay developed in this study is sensitive, specific, and practical, and it may serve as a reliable tool for monitoring Innate® GM potatoes in food products in Japan and potentially in other countries where these events have been approved.
