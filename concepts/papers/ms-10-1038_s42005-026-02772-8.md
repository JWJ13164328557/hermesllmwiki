### 核心发现
1. Here, we show that this is not the whole story: gen- uine phase transitions can occur even when all equi- libria remain spectrally stable.
2. We show that, by extending such models to include explicit non-normality, one can reconcile bistability with fast transitions, thus providing a mechanistic explanation for rapid epigenetic responses at physiological temperature.
3. Building on the bistable CpG dyad model of [27], which captures epigenetic memory through deterministic switching be- tween unmethylated, hemimethylated, and fully methy- lated states, we show (SM ) that incorporating stochastic fluctuations reveals additional structural features of the dynamics.
4. Our results show that regimes of large κ naturally ac- count for the fast epigenetic responses observed experi- mentally [8], while remaining consistent with the slower, progressive demethylation dynamics seen in development and aging.
5. ARTICLE IN PRESS ARTICLE IN PRESS In summary, our work establishes that phase transi- tions need not be spectrally induced.
6. Importantly, once non-normality is identified as the mechanism underlying the observed fast switching, its contribution can be re- lated back to underlying molecular processes.
