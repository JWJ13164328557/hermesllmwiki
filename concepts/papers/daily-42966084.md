# Quorum sensing-mediated rhizosphere engineering: Synthetic microbial consortia as a next-generation strategy to induce soybean resistance against Heterodera glycines.

- **作者**: Jiatu Li, Baiyan Cai, Jingping Ge

## 摘要
Soybean cyst nematode (Heterodera glycines, SCN) severely threatens global soybean yields. Traditional genetic resistance relying on Rhg1 and Rhg4 loci is increasingly compromised by virulent SCN populations. This review synthesizes current knowledge on the molecular interplay among soybean (Glycine max), SCN, and rhizosphere microbiome with a specific focus on quorum sensing (QS) as a central regulatory mechanism enabling novel biotechnological interventions. Emphasis is placed on how soil molecular microbiology approaches can facilitate the transfer of disease-suppressive microbiomes from resistant to susceptible soybean genotypes through strategic inoculation. QS molecules, including N-acyl homoserine lactones (AHLs) and autoinducer-2 (AI-2), prime plant immune responses, while root exudates mediate bidirectional plant-microbe feedback loops. It is proposed that rationally designed synthetic microbial consortia (SynComs), microbiome-smart breeding, and optimized inoculation technologies offer complementary strategies to conventional genetic resistance for sustainable SCN management.
