### 核心发现
1. These observations support that karyotypic differentiation and polyploidy play a crucial role in species delimitation and evolutionary divergence within this group.
2. Notably, our new AGS estimates represent the lowest 2C values recorded to date for hexaploid representatives of A.
3. Broader phylogenetic analyses with expanded Anatolian sampling are required to clarify the relationships between the two Aegean polyploids and eastern representatives of A.
