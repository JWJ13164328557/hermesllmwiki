# Differential Metabolic and Transcriptional Responses to the Novel Fungicide Candidate Triathiazole (TTA) in Cucumber Seedlings and Fruits.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Bulletin of environmental contamination and toxicology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43049801](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43049801/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s00128-026-04378-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00128-026-04378-w)
- **第一作者**: Mingqin Yang
- **作者**: Mingqin Yang, Shixian Wang, Ming Zhuang, Lijun Han, Mengyuan Cao, Zhijin Fan, Liangfu Tang, Lai Chen

## 摘要
Triathiazole (TTA) is a newly identified fungicide targeting pyruvate kinase, but its effects on plant and fungal metabolism remain unclear. This study employed targeted metabolomics and transcriptomics to investigate the impact of TTA on cucumber and Botrytis cinerea. TTA increased the stachyose content in cucumber fruits, but decreased the levels of guanine and salicylic acid. In the leaves of cucumber seedlings, TTA increased putrescine, salicylic acid, and betaine concentrations, while reducing proline. Transcriptomic analysis showed no effect on pyruvate kinase expression, but confirmed the activation of stress-related pathways in seedling leaves. Given that TTA reduces stress resistance in fruits, seedling-stage application of TTA is recommended. In Botrytis cinerea, TTA significantly disrupted the pathway of arginine and proline metabolism, which may represent an alternative mechanism through which TTA inhibited fungal growth. These findings provide insight into the physiological effects of TTA in cucumber and Botrytis cinerea.
