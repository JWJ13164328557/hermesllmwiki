### 核心发现
1. Collectively, the results demonstrate that increasing fertilizer concentration is the primary driver of structural growth, whereas the addition of Moringa extract selectively enhances physiological and biochemical attributes, with the most effective treatment varying according to the targeted trait.
2. Collectively, the PCA indicates that the combined treatment of 3 mL L⁻¹ CF + mL L-1 ME (2.2) is the most comprehensive and high-performing treatment, improving both floral and biochemical traits.
3. Therefore, it is necessary to take help from sustainable and environmentally friendly agricultural systems to provide optimal nutrition for plant production without imperiling the environment (Di Sario et al., 2025; Galindo et al., 2025; Mandal et al., 2023).
