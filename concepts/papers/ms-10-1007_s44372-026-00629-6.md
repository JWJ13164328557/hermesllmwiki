### 核心发现
1. Overall, this study demonstrates that SA can effectively enhance spinach tolerance to salt stress by integrating physiological, anatomical, and biochemical mechanisms.
2. 5 Conclusion This study demonstrates that NaCl-induced salinity stress markedly impairs the growth and anatomical development of Spinacia oleracea, causing reductions in fresh and dry biomass, shoot and root length, plant height, stem area, cortex cell area, epidermal thickness, epidermal cell area, and vascular region area.
3. These results suggest that moderate SA application, particularly as a pre- or post-stress treatment, may serve as an effective and cost-efficient strategy to improve spinach tolerance to salinity, with potential implications for the sustainable cultivation of salt-sensitive crops in saline-prone soils.
