# Integrated Transcriptomic and Metabolomic Analyses Reveal Early Freezing Responses in Populus alba × P. tremula var. glandulosa Clone 84K.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Tree physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43083123](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43083123/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/treephys/tpag176](https://doi.org/10.1093/treephys/tpag176)
- **第一作者**: Qiushuang Zhao
- **作者**: Qiushuang Zhao, Ling Jin, Xinyu Wang, Kewei Cai, Meiqi Lu, Zhanchen Ren, Siyuan Li, Yuanfu Liu

## 摘要
Freezing stress (below 0°C) restricts the geographic distribution and productivity of poplar, but the early molecular responses of poplar to freezing stress remain incompletely understood. Here, transcriptomic and metabolomic profiles were jointly analyzed in Populus alba × P. tremula var. glandulosa Clone 84K leaves exposed to -4°C for 0, 3, and 6 h. Time-series analyses revealed coordinated transcriptional and metabolic changes involving glycolysis. Under freezing stress, the fructose-1,6-bisphosphate aldolase encoding genes, PagFBA1a and PagFBA1b, were upregulated, whereas phosphoenolpyruvate abundance was decreased. Co-expression analysis identified a MYB-family transcription factor, PagLHY, as a candidate transcriptional regulator of PagFBA1a and PagFBA1b. Subsequent dual-luciferase reporter assays showed that PagLHY enhanced the promoter activities of PagFBA1a and PagFBA1b in Nicotiana benthamiana. Furthermore, heterologous transient assays showed that PagFBA1a/1b overexpression significantly enhanced freezing stress tolerance in N. benthamiana by increasing antioxidant enzyme (POD, CAT) activities and proline content. Together, these findings revealed a putative PagLHY-PagFBA regulatory module involved in the response of poplar to freezing stress.
