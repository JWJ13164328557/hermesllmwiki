1. When incorporated into chitosan matrices, these nanoparticles interact with chitosan’s amino and hydroxyl groups through coordination and hydrogen bonding, forming stable complexes that improve structural integrity and packaging performance.
2. Solvent polarity also plays a critical role in polyphenol extraction eﬃciency; methanolic extracts of Nigella sativa exhibited superior bioactivity compared to ethanolic extracts, owing to methanol’s high polarity and hydrogen-bonding capacity, which enhance the solubilization of plant phenolics (Karkar et al., 2023).
3. These compounds interact with microbial cell walls, leading to structural disruption and leakage of intracellular contents.
4. Optimizing extract concentration plays a critical role in determining the functional performance of bioactive ﬁlms.
5. Collectively, these ﬁndings highlight the potential of polyphenol- enriched chitosan ﬁlms as multifunctional packaging materials.
6. Furthermore, the timing of cold plasma application plays a critical role in composite ﬁlm performance.
7. Time- temperature indicators (TTIs) integrate thermal exposure over time through an irreversible chemical, enzymatic, or physical reaction whose kinetics are matched to those of the spoilage reaction of the target food.
8. Notably, certain bioactives (e.g., anthocyanins) can additionally confer pH-responsive color changes, enabling dual active-intelligent packaging behavior.
