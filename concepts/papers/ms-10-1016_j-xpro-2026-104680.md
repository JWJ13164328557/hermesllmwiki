# LOTR-Seq: A protocol for large-scale simultaneous single-cell long-read genotyping of transcripts.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: STAR Protocols (2026)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.xpro.2026.104680](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xpro.2026.104680)
- **作者**: Julian Grabek, Leanne Cooper, Rohit Haldar, Jenny Quiatchon, Nadine Fitzpatrick
- **来源**: crossref


## 摘要
Single-cell RNA sequencing enables analysis of intra-tumoral transcriptional heterogeneity but has limited coverage of mutational hotspots. Here, we present LOTR-Seq, a protocol combining short-read whole-transcriptome analysis with long-read genotyping. We describe steps for hematopoietic stem cell isolation from human bone marrow samples, generation of full-length single-cell barcoded cDNA, target enrichment, and long-read sequencing for variant calling. For complete details on the use and execution of this protocol, please refer to Grabek et al.1.
