### 核心发现
1. • Long-term reconstruction of air pollution: This study provides a rare 60-year (1962–2021) temporal record of cobalt, nickel, and lead deposition using Tilia tomentosa, demonstrating its applicability for retrospective air quality assessment.
2. To mitigate the impact of air pollution, it is necessary to identify pollutant sources and implement effective control strategies.
3. Nevertheless, further research incorporating broader spatial coverage and biological replication is necessary to confirm its suitability for environmental monitoring applications.
