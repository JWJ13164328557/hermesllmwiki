### 核心发现
1. Four target genes were higher in FIBP-high AML after transcriptome-wide false-discovery correction: FTH1, GPX4, AIFM2/FSP1, and LPCAT3.
2. Spearman correlations were calculated between continuous FIBP expression and each target gene; FDR was controlled across the 11 correlations.
3. Spearman correlations were calculated between each immune score and the 11 target genes, with FDR correction within each 11-gene family.
4. (B) Spearman correlations between continuous FIBP and target genes.
5. None of the targets met both |Δ|>1 and q<0.05.
6. The model does not establish that FIBP directly regulates these genes, that ferroptosis is functionally suppressed in individual tumors, or that the survival association is mediated by ferroptosis.
7. FIBP interacts with transcription factor STAT3 to induce EME1 expression and drive radioresistance in lung adenocarcinoma.
8. Basic mechanisms and novel potential therapeutic targets for ferroptosis in acute myeloid leukemia.
