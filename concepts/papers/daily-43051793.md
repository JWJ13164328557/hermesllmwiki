# Regional variation in taste profiles and non-volatile metabolites of raw Anhua dark tea revealed by metabolomics and machine learning.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.) (2026)
- **PMID**: [43051793](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43051793/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.foodres.2026.121103](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodres.2026.121103)
- **第一作者**: Yixiong Wang
- **作者**: Yixiong Wang, Jing Wang, Jiaqi He, Zhi Chen, Wanjing Zhong, Yeqing Huang, Hengbin Yao, Fangmei Liu

## 摘要
Raw Anhua dark tea (RADT) from different production regions has its own unique flavor characteristics, this study systematically investigated taste differences and their metabolic basis in RADT from key four production regions in Anhua County. Sensory evaluation and electronic tongue (E-tongue) analysis revealed distinct regional variations in taste characteristics. Non-targeted metabolomics identified 269 non-volatile metabolites, including 31 differential metabolites mainly comprising amino acids, phenolic acids, flavonoids, and flavonoid glycosides. Correlation analysis indicated that EGCG, ECG, and epigallocatechin 3-O-(3-O-methylgallate) were primarily associated with bitterness, aftertaste-A, and richness, whereas flavonoid glycosides and kaempferol derivatives were positively correlated with the E-tongue sweetness response and astringency modulation. Phloridzin and carbohydrates, including sucrose and trehalose, were also potentially associated with regional taste variation. Machine learning models further demonstrated the feasibility of discriminating RADTs from different production regions based on metabolic profiles, with random forest (RF), support vector machine (SVM), and k-nearest neighbor (KNN) showing better classification performance. These findings reveal the metabolic basis of regional taste differentiation in RADTs and deepen the understanding of how metabolites contribute to tea taste formation.
