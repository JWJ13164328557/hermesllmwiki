### 核心发现
1. 3.3 Genetic diversity analysis To target desirable qualities in flax, genetic indices such as variance, covariance, broad- sense heritability, and genetic advancement are essential.
2. Understanding the anatomical structures of flax is essential for enhancing fiber production and developing new cultivars.
3. Further agronomic trials are necessary to confirm their performance.
4. Understand­ ing the fiber’s properties while still attached to the plant is crucial for evaluating its suit­ ability for various applications.
5. To increase fiber yield and identify the most promising genotypes for fiber production, it is essential to develop a comprehensive understand­ ing of the ultrastructural features of the stem tissues that produce these fibers.
6. Notably, the outer wall of the epidermal parenchyma cells is generally thicker than the anticlinal and inner cell walls.
7. To ensure accuracy, it is crucial to include a range of standard measurements alongside an aver­ age figure in diagnostic descriptions.
8. Changes in fiber properties and the xylem core are essential for stem stiffness.
