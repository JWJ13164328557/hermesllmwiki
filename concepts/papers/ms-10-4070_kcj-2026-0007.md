### 核心发现
1. By leveraging large-scale single-cell atlases and computational inference of ligand-receptor interactions, we uncover systems-level alterations in signaling activities across diverse cardiac cell types and propose novel mechanisms of pathogenesis driven by miscommunication between cellular compartments.
2. ABSTRACT Cell-cell interactions play a critical role in maintaining cardiovascular homeostasis by coordinating communication among cardiomyocytes, endothelial cells, fibroblasts, smooth muscle cells, and immune cells.
3. Dysregulated SMC behavior plays a central role in atherosclerosis, hypertension, and vascular remodeling in cardiac disease.
4. NicheNet NicheNet15) represents one of the first computational frameworks to link ligand-receptor interactions with downstream transcriptional responses in target cells.
5. By estimating ligand–target regulatory potential scores, the method prioritizes ligands most likely responsible for observed differentially expressed genes in a particular cell population.
6. MultiNicheNet MultiNicheNet18) extends NicheNet’s mechanistic ligand-target inference to multi-sample and multi-condition datasets, addressing an important limitation of earlier tools.
7. By modeling how ligand-target regulatory relationships vary across patient samples or disease states, MultiNicheNet quantifies differential communication programs that distinguish pathological from healthy conditions.
8. These findings indicate that macrophages orchestrate fibroblast activation through distinct yet complementary pathways; TGFB1 acts as a primary driver of ECM-related gene programs, while TNF upregulates specific targets such as JUNB and PLAU.
