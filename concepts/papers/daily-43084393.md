# Lipid phosphate phosphatase α3 contributes to seed oil accumulation in Brassica napus.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Cell reports (2026)
- **PMID**: [43084393](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43084393/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118382](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118382)
- **第一作者**: Chenxi Jia
- **作者**: Chenxi Jia, Yang Zhang, Yueyun Hong

## 摘要
Triacylglycerol (TAG) biosynthesis relies on diacylglycerol (DAG). However, the molecular and genetic basis of DAG sources for TAG synthesis remains poorly understood. Here we show that DAG produced by lipid phosphate phosphatase α3 (LPPα3) is important for seed TAG synthesis. Among 35 BnaLPPs in Brassica napus, BnaLPPα3 is highly expressed in developing seeds, and its encoded protein localizes to the endoplasmic reticulum. The lppα3 mutant exhibits a 9.8% reduction in seed oil content with a diminished DAG flux toward TAG compared with wild-type. Biochemical assays show that BnaLPPα3 dephosphorylates phosphatidic acid (PA) to DAG in an Mg2+-independent manner. Furthermore, BnaLPPα3 is important for both the DGAT1 (acyl-CoA: diacylglycerol acyltransferase 1)- and PDAT1 (phospholipid: diacylglycerol acyltransferase 1)-mediated pathways of TAG synthesis. Haplotype analysis reveals that a higher expression level of BnaLPPα3 is associated with higher seed oil content, which may facilitate the improvement of seed oil production in rapeseed.
