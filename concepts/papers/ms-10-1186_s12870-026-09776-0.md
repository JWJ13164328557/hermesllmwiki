### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that nano-fertil­ izers can effectively supplement conventional nutrient management in sugar beet production, though optimal formulations and application strategies require further investigation.
2. Notably, total chlorophyll content increased by approximately 122.8%, carotenoids by 82.6%, leaf area index by 95%, and sugar yield showed the most dramatic improvement, increasing by 94% (from 3.03 to 5.87 t ha⁻¹).
3. Notably, within this group, the genus Azotobacter stands out as a Gram-negative, aerobic, free-living bacterium of the proteobacteria, renowned for its effective nitrogen fixation and ability to enhance plant stress tolerance by increasing levels of soluble sugars, proteins, and proline while boosting root and shoot growth [18, 19].
4. Partial nano-formulations and single applications yielded inferior results, confirming that the complete combina­ tion is essential for optimizing leaf efficiency and growth velocity in sugar beet.
5. Notably, rhizo­ bacteria alone raised chlorophyll by 64% and carotenoids by 48%, while nano-NPK alone gave lower increases— demonstrating clear synergy.
6. Notably, the complete N + P+K formulation gave significantly higher leaf potassium (49.9 mg g⁻¹ d.w.) than the N + K formula­ tion (42.5 mg g⁻¹ d.w.), indicating that balanced nutrition enhances potassium uptake.
7. Notably, the magnitude of nutrient increase was smaller in roots than in leaves (reported in Table 7), suggesting that under improved nutrition; plants preferentially allo­ cate essential elements to photosynthetic tissues.
