### 核心发现
1. In this study, the regulatory networks of miRNAs and targets were examined by combining mRNA, miRNA, and degradome.
2. A total of 3066 target genes were associated with 228 miRNAs via degradome sequencing.
3. Collectively, our results demonstrate for the first time that miRNAs are identified in I.
4. These RNAs can regulate gene expression by guiding cleavage or translational reduction of targets at transcriptional or post- transcriptional levels [1].
5. In Fusa­ rium oxysporum, pmi-miR530, pmi-miR6173, pmi-miR6300, and pmi- Nov_13 were predicted to be involved in the mevalonate pathway (MVA), while miR396a, and miR398f/g were predicted to target tran­ scripts in the methylerythritol-4-phosphate (MEP) pathway [4].
6. In Salvia miltiorrhiza, miR5027 targets AACT, and participates in the regulation of tanshinone biosynthesis [5].
7. In Ginkgo biloba, miR167 targets AACT, miR457 targets HMGR, and they all regulate the biosynthesis of terpenoid lactones [7].
8. In Artemisia annua, miR414 targets FPS and HMGR, and participates in regulating artemisinin synthesis [8].
