### 核心发现
1. Given the extensive phylogenetic, developmental, and metabolic diversity of marine invertebrates, rigorous validation is essential to ensure that cultured cells represent the intended target cells and accurately reﬂect their in vivo counterparts.
2. In particular, researchers have relied on culture condi- tions adapted from mammalian or non-marine invertebrate sys- tems that fail to meet the physiological requirements of target species.
3. Marine invertebrate tissues often harbor diverse microbial commu- nities, and co-cultured microeukaryotes can outcompete host cells, becoming the predominant cell type in culture that can be mistak- enly identiﬁed as target cells (Nowotny et al., 2021; Walsh et al., 2025).

6. Cell-cell and cell-extracellular matrix interactions also play a central role in determining cell behavior.
