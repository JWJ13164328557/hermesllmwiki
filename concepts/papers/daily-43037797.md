# Lytic polysaccharide monooxygenase (LPMO) expression in Arabidopsis reduces cell wall recalcitrance and boosts immunity against Pseudomonas syringae.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant molecular biology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43037797](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43037797/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s11103-026-01776-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11103-026-01776-w)
- **第一作者**: Rajan Kumar Sah
- **作者**: Rajan Kumar Sah, Lavi Rastogi, Bhagwat Prasad Dewangan, Anant Mohan Sharma, Prashant Anupama-Mohan Pawar

## 摘要
Lytic Polysaccharide Monooxygenases (LPMOs) can enhance the efficiency of cell wall degrading enzyme cocktails for processing lignocellulosic biomass in vitro. However, the role of LPMO after its expression in planta is not explored. Therefore, we investigated the effect of heterologous expression of fungi Pycnoporus coccineus (PcAA14) in planta on cell wall composition, saccharification and plant immunity. To study this, we expressed PcAA14 in Arabidopsis thaliana under the control of constitutive (35S) and xylem specific (CINNAMATE 4-HYDROXYLASE) promoter. Arabidopsis transgenic lines expressing PcAA14 showed slight increase in LPMO activity against synthetic substrates, and more accumulation of xylan derived oxidised oligosaccharides after xylanase digestion as compared to control plants. Furthermore, PcAA14 plants exhibited increased cellulose content and reduced lignin content compared to wild-type. Importantly, the cell wall from PcAA14-expressing plants released more glucose and xylose after digestion with cellulase and xylanases respectively. Notably, the expression of PcAA14 did not result in any observable morphological or anatomical abnormalities. In fact, PcAA14 plants exhibited enhanced immunity against Pseudomonas syringae along with increase in expression of immunity related markers. Overall, this study demonstrated PcAA14 expression in planta is a promising strategy to improve lignocellulosic properties without compromising plant growth and immunity.
