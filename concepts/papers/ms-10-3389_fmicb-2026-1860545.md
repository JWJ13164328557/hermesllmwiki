### 核心发现
1. In summary, the increase in chlorophyll under uranium stress is a combined outcome of stress response and concentration eﬀects.
2. In conclusion, under moderate uranium stress, plant intrinsic tolerance dominates, and microbes indirectly support plant health rather than direct regulation of uranium uptake and distribution.
3. Notably, Massilia increased in sterilized- inoculated bulk soil, Bacillus in several uranium-treated groups, and Paracoccus in uranium-stressed original rhizosphere.
