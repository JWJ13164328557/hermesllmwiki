# Comparison of fruit quality and metabolome differences between three table grape varieties and their parents.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food chemistry: X (2026)
- **PMID**: [42926806](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42926806/)
- **DOI**: [10.3390/analytica6040043](https://doi.org/10.3390/analytica6040043)
- **第一作者**: Liu Zhenghai
- **作者**: Liu Zhenghai, Xiao Feng, Wang Chenyu, Qiao Qinghe, Liu Jinpin, Tan Min, He Jinyu, Zhang Yanxia

## 摘要
To elucidate the differences in fruit quality and metabolome between table grape hybrid varieties and their parents, this study used 'Kyoho' (KG) as the female parent, 'Zaoheibao' (ZHB) as the male parent, and their three hybrid offspring 'Baoguang' (BG), 'Cuiguang' (CG), and 'Miguang' (MG) as materials. Basic fruit quality traits, total phenolic and anthocyanin contents were measured, sensory evaluation was conducted, and 30 pairs of SSR fluorescent primers were employed to verify the genetic relationships between parents and offspring. Meanwhile, LC-MS/MS-based untargeted metabolomics combined with multivariate statistical analysis was used to compare the metabolic profiles of the five varieties.The results showed that CG exhibited high soluble solids content (19.26%) and low titratable acidity (3.78 g·L-1), demonstrating excellent sugar-acid balance; BG exhibited the highest anthocyanin content (14.56 mg/100 g); MG possessed a strong rose aroma and achieved the highest overall sensory score. SSR molecular markers confirmed that all three offspring were true hybrids, and each variety was assigned a unique SSR barcode-based molecular identity. A total of 2038 metabolites were putatively annotated by metabolomics analysis. PCA and OPLS-DA confirmed clear metabolomic separation among varieties, with flavonoids and anthocyanins identified as key differential metabolites. The up- and down-regulation patterns of metabolites in the volcano plot indicate that the inheritance from the two parents in the hybrid progeny is not simply additive. The Euclidean distance between the parents KG and ZHB was the largest (77.70), whereas the distance between CG and KG was the smallest (62.17), indicating that CG had a metabolome more similar to that of the female parent KG. KEGG enrichment analysis indicated that differential metabolites were mainly enriched in amino acid biosynthesis and flavonoid metabolism pathways. Correlation analysis revealed that flavonol glycosides showed strong positive correlations with anthocyanins and total phenolics, while WGCNA analysis revealed that anthocyanins and total phenolics exhibited divergent dynamic trends. Redundancy analysis (RDA) showed that the first two axes cumulatively explained 74.39% of the covariance, revealing an opposing differentiation pattern between appearance quality and internal quality traits at the metabolic level. In conclusion, the three hybrid cultivars exhibit distinct genetic characteristics at both the fruit-quality and metabolomic levels, and amino-acid metabolic remodeling represents a plausible factor underlying their quality divergence.

### 核心发现
1. Differences in the flavonoid and anthocyanin synthesis pathways were key to distinguishing the metabolomes of the five varieties, and the specific upregulation of anthocyanins such as Peonidin-3-O-galactoside in MG was consistent with its purple-red skin phenotype.
2. In conclusion, the three hybrid cultivars exhibit distinct genetic characteristics at both the fruit-quality and metabolomic levels, and amino-acid metabolic remodeling represents a plausible factor underlying their quality divergence.
3. These results confirm at the metabolome level that hybrid offspring are not simple additive combinations of parental traits but rather form specific traits through metabolic network remodeling, which is an important molecular basis for heterosis.
4. KEGG enrichment analysis indicated that differential metabolites were mainly enriched in amino acid biosynthesis and flavonoid metabolism pathways, suggesting that amino acid metabolic remodeling is strongly associated with quality differentiation in hybrid offspring, thereby providing metabolic targets for improving grape fruit flavor, color, and nutritional quality through breeding.
5. This study provides a theoretical basis for parental selection in table grape cross-breeding, while the SSR markers developed herein can be used for variety identification and intellectual property protection.
6. Elucidating the metabolic basis for quality divergence among hybrid grape cultivars is critical for breeding cultivars with higher nutritional value, superior flavor characteristics, and improved suitability for fresh consumption, juice processing and functional-food applications.
7. Notably, DENTICULAFLAVONOL exhibited positive correlations with anthocyanins, total phenolics, and flavonoids simultaneously (all r > 0.65), suggesting that it may serve as a hub metabolite in the anthocyanin–phenolic metabolic network.
8. Understanding metabolic trade-offs through integrative correlation analysis is critical for formulating breeding strategies that balance multiple traits.
