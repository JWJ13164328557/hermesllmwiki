### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these findings suggest that POU2F2 promotes the activation of the JAK/STAT pathway, thereby enhancing cell viability, reducing apop­ tosis, and increas-ing invasiveness in clear cell renal cell carcinoma.
2. SPI1 plays a central role in the differentiation of myeloid and lymphoid lineages by controlling the expression of its target genes, thereby influencing cellular fate determination, proliferation, and Fig.
3. This study reveals the upregulation of POU2F2 in ccRCC and its role in promoting tumor progression through the activation of the JAK/STAT signaling pathway via SPI1.
4. Additionally, WB analysis showed that overexpression of POU2F2 led to increased SPI1 expression, while downregulation of POU2F2 resulted in decreased SPI1 expression (Fig.
5. Conclusions This study reveals a significant upregulation of POU2F2 in ccRCC and its role in promoting tumor cell prolifera­ tion and invasion through interaction with SPI1, leading to the activation of the JAK/STAT signaling pathway.
6. Through KEGG, GO, and GSEA analyses, we found that overexpression of POU2F2 is associated with the JAK/STAT pathway.
7. In summary, POU2F2 promotes the progression of ccRCC through the activation of the JAK/STAT signaling pathway via SPI1.
8. Our study sug-gested that POU2F2 may serve as a potential therapeutic target for ccRCC.
