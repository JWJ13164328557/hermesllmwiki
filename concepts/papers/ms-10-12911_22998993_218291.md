### 核心发现
1. 99 INTRODUCTION Nitrogen nutrition plays a key role in maize productivity; however, the inefficient use of nitro­ gen fertilizers remains one of the major environ­ mental challenges in modern agriculture.
2. These findings suggest that the environmental and agronomic outcomes of in­ hibitor application may depend strongly on soil conditions, climatic factors, and crop physiologi­ cal responses (Lasisi et al., 2021).
3. These results confirm that nitrogen supply optimization alone is insufficient without stabi­ lization of soil nitrogen transformations under water-limited conditions.
4. Therefore, optimizing ni­ trogen management strategies is essential to im­ prove fertilizer efficiency while minimizing ad­ verse environmental impacts (Muller et al., 2023; Alonso-Ayuso et al., 2016).
