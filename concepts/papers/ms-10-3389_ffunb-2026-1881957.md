### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these results reveal that H2O2 exerts distinct effects on different autophagic processes, highlighting the complexity of autophagy regulation.
2. Functioning as a cysteine protease, AaAtg4 directly interacts with the AaAtg8 ubiquitin-like protein and is indispensable for AaAtg8 processing and autophagosome formation, with its enzymatic activity modulated by oxidative cues.
3. Furthermore, AaAtg4 interacts with the stress-responsive mitogen-activated protein kinase AaHog1, which modulates its phosphorylation under conditions less conducive to autophagy and thus, reinforces a dynamic signaling axis.
4. TYPE Original Research PUBLISHED 22 July 2026 DOI 10.3389/ffunb.2026.1881957 Introduction Autophagy plays a critical role in cell survival in all eukaryotes under starvation or other stress conditions.
5. Atg4 is a cysteine protease that plays a central role in the Atg8 ubiquitin-like protein conjugation system (Kirisako et al., 2000; Ichimura et al., 2000).
6. Atg4 directly interacts with Atg8, regulating its processing and deconjugation.
7. In yeast, Atg1 has been shown to phosphorylate Atg4 at the site of autophagosome biogenesis, thereby inhibiting Atg4 protease activ- ity and facilitating autophagosome formation (Sánchez-Wandelmer et al., 2017).
8. Atg1 interacts with Atg4 at the PAS, a crucial site for spatial regulation of Atg4 activity, ensuring proper Atg8 processing during autophagosome formation.
