### 核心发现
1. They maintain the delivery of nutrients at the target destination.
2. They interact with the soil and roots of plants and enhance the availability and uptake of nutrients.
3. It is required to synthesize different enzymes and regulate photosynthesis and respiration.
4. These changes can be linked with the role of Zn, as it is required for chlorophyll production and auxin biosynthesis and plays an essential role in germination, soil fertility, and pollen performance.
