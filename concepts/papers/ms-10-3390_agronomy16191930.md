## 摘要
This study evaluated mulch-film degradation, soil conditions, and the growth of soybean and maize under polyethylene film (PEF) and biodegradable (BD) mulch films in South Korea in 2023. Both crops received identical treatments: non-mulching (NM) and four black mulch films (PEF and three BD films: BFI, BFII, and BFIII). In the soybean field, film weight maintenance at 120 days after transplanting (DAT) was lowest for BFII (20.3%), followed by BFIII (29.4%) and BFI (46.1%). Soil water potential values were −136.2 kPa in BFII, −125.6 kPa in BFI, −75.3 kPa in NM, −70.4 kPa in PEF, and −62.2 kPa in BFIII. Soil electrical conductivity (EC) increased in PEF plots. Bulk density decreased under mulching treatments, corresponding to increased soil porosity. PEF significantly increased root and stem fresh weight (FW), and total FW. BFII increased pod number and seed FW, with the second-highest values in BFI- and BFIII-treated plants. In the maize field, film weight maintenance declined in BFII and BFIII or in BFI between 30 and 60 DAT. Soil moisture decreased in BFII plots and, on some June sampling dates, in BFI and BFIII plots. PEF significantly reduced soil hardness, with similarly low values in BD treatments. Mulching increased soil P2O5 and K2O concentrations. PEF produced the highest vegetative FW (817.2 g), while BD films increased ear height and ear length and FW. Overall, the BFII mulching film with readily decomposable PBAT and PBS components was likely to have influenced mild drought stress during the late growth stage of soybean, producing moderate vegetative growth and promoting yield.
