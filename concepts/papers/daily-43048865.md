# A KNOX-BELL-Lignin Module Regulates Leaf Rolling and Drought Tolerance in Rice.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43048865](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43048865/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag862](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag862)
- **第一作者**: Lei Qiao
- **作者**: Lei Qiao, Zhongyu Zhang, Qinying Li, Jinrong Li, Dingbang Da, Mingjia Lin, Yuguo Chen, Junhan Mi

## 摘要
Leaf rolling is a critical agronomic trait in rice that directly shapes canopy architecture and photosynthetic efficiency, yet the underlying molecular mechanisms remain to be fully elucidated. Here, we identify RLC3 (OsBLH4), a BELL-type homeodomain transcription factor, as a key regulator of leaf rolling and drought tolerance. Genetic validation using rlc3-1, rlc3-2, and CRISPR/Cas9-generated rlc3-ko lines, supported by allelism tests, confirms the critical regulatory role of RLC3 in leaf rolling. Paraffin section analysis revealed that RLC3 loss-of-function mutations reduced bulliform cells (BCs) dimensions. Biochemical assays, ultrastructural observations and vascular permeability measurements further showed that these mutations disrupt lignin biosynthesis and secondary cell wall (SCW) formation, leading to aberrant midrib vascular patterning. This structural defect accelerates leaf water loss, which in turn ‌reduces BC turgor pressure, ultimately resulting in leaf rolling and reduced drought tolerance. Mechanistically, RLC3 directly binds to and activates the promoters of core lignin biosynthetic genes (OsCCR4, OsCAld5H1, OsPAL5, and OsCOMT5). Furthermore, RLC3 forms heterodimers with KNOX transcription factors OSH1/45/71, which differentially bind to and co-activate a subset of these targets: OSH1/71 specifically co-activate OsCCR4 and OsCAld5H1, whereas OSH45 additionally co-activates OsPAL5. Loss-of-function mutations in these KNOX genes result in leaf morphology alterations, with reduced lignin content and SCW thickness, ‌ which are partially consistent with the defects observed in rlc3 mutants‌‌. Together, our results reveal a KNOX-BELL-lignin regulatory module that connects transcriptional activation to SCW fortification and water loss regulation, enabling molecular design of drought-tolerant rice with balanced water retention and ideal plant architecture.
