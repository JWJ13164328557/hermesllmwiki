### 核心发现
1. This study provides a sensitive RT-qPCR assay for SCYLV detection further validated using field collected samples, confirms the TVD is adequate tissue for detection throughout the growing season, and emphasizes the criticality of thorough collection methods for reliable detection of this economically important virus.
2. These data show that sampling a single leaf from a single cane could result in an extreme underestimation of incidence.
3. Taken together, the developed assay combined with the recommended sampling strategies should be considered for routine detection and certification programs to reduce SCYLV introductions via seed cane at field establishment.
4. This work will provide new insight into virus detection strategies in sugarcane and contribute to the development of improved sampling protocols for SCYLV management for the sugarcane industry.
5. Importantly, the assay detected multiple SCYLV genotypes (BRA, CUB, and REU), with clear genotype-specific melt- ing temperatures.
6. Results show that SCYLV accumulation within the TVD was sufficient for detection, regardless the month of collection, no drastic decline in titer was observed.
7. Addi- tional work is required to investigate the differing accumu- lation patterns exhibited by the two cultivars.
8. More importantly, grow- ers can select the TVD at any time in the growing season for detection, though collections later in the season may better capture the occurrence of new infections.
