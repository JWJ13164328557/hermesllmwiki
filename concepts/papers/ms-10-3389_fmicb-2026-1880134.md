### 核心发现
1. Here we report the half-life extension of LSN and LSN-containing chimeric lysins by fusion to a human immunoglobulin G1 (IgG1) Fc, using a previously described chassis which incorporates a helical linker (Badarau et al., 2026).
2. The bactericidal activity of Fc-CHAP-LSN is driven by the LSN-catalyzed and target cell-dependent release of free LSN.
3. Elution fractions were analyzed by sodium dodecyl sulfate–polyacrylamide gel elec­ trophoresis (SDS-PAGE) followed by Coomassie staining and frac­ tions containing the target protein were pooled.
4. Elution fractions were analyzed by SDS–PAGE followed by Coomassie staining and fractions containing the target protein were pooled.
5. Elution fractions were analyzed by SDS–PAGE followed by Coomassie staining and monomer fractions containing the target protein were pooled and supplemented with HRV 3C pro­ tease (produced in-house) at a 1:100 protease:substrate molar ratio, followed by overnight incubation at 4 °C.
6. Fractions containing the target pro­ tein were pooled and analyzed by SDS–PAGE.
7. Survey scans were acquired from 375–2000 m/z with lock mass enabled, normalized AGC target of 300%, resolution of 60,000.
8. Selected ions were analyzed with a maximum fill time of 200 ms, normalized auto­ matic gain control target of 200%, and resolution of 30,000 after higher-energy collisional dissociation fragmentation with nor­ malized collision energy of 30%.
