# DNA hypomethylation licenses cell-cycle re-entry and accelerates coelomocyte replenishment after evisceration in Apostichopus japonicus.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Comparative biochemistry and physiology. Part A, Molecular & integrative physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43014656](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43014656/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.cbpa.2026.112093](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cbpa.2026.112093)
- **第一作者**: Mingmei Xu
- **作者**: Mingmei Xu, Yinan Wang, Lin Cui, Lirui Huang, Xiaoli Xu, Jiong Wu, Qiang Li

## 摘要
Evisceration in the sea cucumber Apostichopus japonicus causes a substantial loss of coelomocytes, potentially affecting its innate immune defense. Studies have shown that epigenetic regulation, particularly DNA methylation, may play a critical role in coelomocyte replenishment. In this study, we investigated the role of DNA methylation dynamics in coelomocyte replenishment following evisceration. Intracellular SAM concentrations declined from 53.5 ± 1.4 ng/mL before evisceration to 22.7 ± 5.6 ng/mL at 6 h, representing a 57.6% reduction. DNMT1 expression decreased to 0.20-fold of the pre-evisceration level at 12 h, whereas TET2 expression increased to 2.32-fold the pre-evisceration level. Following pharmacological inhibition of DNMT activity with 5-azacytidine, the expression levels of E2F, Cyclin A, and Cyclin B in the DNMTiEVI group were 7.43-fold, 5.69-fold, and 18.0-fold higher than those in the EVI group at 0, 24, and 24 h, respectively. Flow cytometric analysis revealed that, following DNMT inhibition, the proportion of S-phase cells in the DNMTiEVI group increased from 10.04% at 0 h to 17.85% at 72 h, whereas that in the EVI group rose from 8.58% at 0 h to 12.53% at 72 h. The proportion of EdU-positive cells in the DNMTiEVI group increased from 5.3% at 0 h to 13.5% at 72 h, whereas that in the EVI group rose from 3.3% at 0 h to 10.2% at 72 h. At 72 h, coelomocyte density reached 3.6 × 105 cells/mL in the DNMTiEVI group compared with 2.0 × 105 cells/mL in the EVI group, and cell viability was 1.91-fold higher following DNMT inhibition. These findings suggest that DNMT inhibition is associated with increased expression of cell-cycle-related genes, enhanced cell-cycle progression, and accelerated coelomocyte replenishment following evisceration in A. japonicus.
