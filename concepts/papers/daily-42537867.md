# Rice husk-derived nanosilica alleviates aluminum accumulation and toxicity in Brassica napus by regulating cellular metabolism and antioxidant defense.

- **PMID**: [42537867](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42537867/)

- **作者**: 

## 摘要
Aluminum (Al) toxicity poses a serious threat to agricultural crops and impedes plant growth in acidic soils. Although Si-based nanomaterials have previously been investigated for alleviating metal induced stress, their role in mitigating Al-induced toxicity in Brassica napus under acidic conditions remains poorly understood. Therefore, this study evaluated the efficacy of rice husk-derived nanosilica (SiO2-NPs) in modulating physico-biochemical, cellular, and molecular responses of Brassica napus exposed to Al (100 μM) stress under acidic conditions (pH 4.5). The application of nanosilica showed significant enhancement in nutrient uptake (Mg, K, Zn, Mn, and Fe) and reduced Al accumulation (42%/41.9%), H2O2 (27.5%/27%), O2•- (32.2%/20.8%), and malondialdehyde (MDA) (22/18%) levels in root/shoot tissues, respectively, compared with plants exposed to Al stress under acidic conditions. In addition, nanosilica treatment restored membrane stability, alleviated cellular and ultrastructural damages, enhanced antioxidant enzyme activities, and regulated stress-related phytohormones levels. Nanosilica further modulated the expression of genes associated with Al transport (BnALS3), hormone regulation (BnNCED, BnBCB), and defense. Overall, this study reveals that rice husk-derived nanosilica alleviated Al-induced phytotoxicity by limiting Al accumulation, maintaining nutrient and hormonal homeostasis, and strengthening antioxidant and molecular defense systems. These findings indicate the potential of rice husk-derived nanosilica as a sustainable approach for improving the performance of Brassica napus in Al enriched acidic conditions.
