### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that the gene signature may complement clinical parameters and improve discrimination when integrated with clinical variables.
2. These findings suggest that altered purine metabolism is not simply a secondary feature of systemic inflammation, but may reflect a broader immunometabolic state associated with pediatric bacterial infection.
3. In summary, a purine metabolism-related host gene signature was identified in pediatric bacterial infection.
4. These findings suggest that purine metabolism-related changes may be involved in the host response to infection.
