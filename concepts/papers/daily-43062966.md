# Phosphate Availability Controls Shoot Magnesium Allocation through Local Root Phosphate Sensing in Arabidopsis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43062966](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43062966/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag875](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag875)
- **第一作者**: Yan-Li Chu
- **作者**: Yan-Li Chu, Jiang-Tao Yu, Huai-Zeng Liu, Rui Chen, Shuai-Lei Zhang, Yu-Qi Wei, Shu-Ting Xue, Bin Wang

## 摘要
Phosphate (Pi) and magnesium (Mg2+) are essential macronutrients that function together in numerous cellular processes. In natural soils, however, Pi scarcity is a far more pervasive constraint on plant productivity than Mg2+ limitation. How plants coordinate the allocation of these two nutrients under such imbalanced conditions remains largely unknown. Here, we uncover a nutrient-prioritization mechanism that allocates Mg2+ according to Pi availability in Arabidopsis thaliana. Elevated Pi supply alleviates Mg2+ deficiency, whereas Pi starvation renders Mg2+ physiologically ineffective for sustaining shoot growth, demonstrating that Pi is prioritized over Mg2+ in growth regulation. Mechanistically, local root Pi status controls the Pi-responsive transcription factors PHR1 and PHL1, which directly repress MGR4, the major transporter responsible for xylem Mg2+ loading. Under low Pi supply, activated PHR1/PHL1 restrict MGR4-dependent Mg2+ transport, preventing futile Mg2+ accumulation in Pi-starved shoots with arrested growth. As Pi availability increases, this repression of MGR4 is relieved, enhancing xylem Mg2+ loading and enabling Mg2+ to cooperate with Pi in sustaining shoot growth even under Mg2+-limiting conditions. Together, our findings identify local root Pi sensing as a nutritional rheostat that dynamically matches systemic Mg2+ allocation to Pi availability, thereby prioritizing Pi-dependent growth and maintaining shoot Pi-Mg2+ homeostasis under widespread Pi-limiting conditions.
