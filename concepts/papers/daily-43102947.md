# qRL7 orchestrates a kinase-glycosylation cascade to promote rice primary root growth.

## 摘要
Primary root growth is critical for seedling establishment in direct seeding of rice, yet the regulatory mechanisms underlying its elongation during seed germination remain poorly understood. Here, we identify quantitative trait locus (QTL) qRL7 as encoding the DNA-binding with one finger (DOF) transcription factor OsDOF22, which serves as a central regulatory hub influencing primary root elongation during seed germination. We demonstrate that OsDOF22 triggers the transcriptional activation of its downstream target, OsHSL1, which encodes a leucine-rich repeat receptor-like kinase. Subsequently, OsHSL1 phosphorylates and stabilizes the glucosyltransferase OsUGT75A. Collectively, this tripartite OsDOF22-OsHSL1-OsUGT75A module regulates the levels of abscisic acid (ABA), jasmonic acid (JA), and indole-3-acetic acid (IAA), thereby influencing primary root elongation. Our findings establish qRL7 as central regulatory hub that integrates phosphorylation and glycosylation signaling in primary root development, providing fundamental insights for breeding rice varieties with root architectures optimized for direct-seeding cultivation.
