# Genomic characterization, synteny, and cold-responsive expression of Soybean HD-ZIP IV transcription factor candidates.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: PloS one (2026)
- **PMID**: [43050425](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43050425/)
- **DOI**: [10.1371/journal.pone.0363148](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0363148)
- **第一作者**: Kenzhen Zhao
- **作者**: Kenzhen Zhao, Huilong Hong, Xiulin Liu, Xueyang Wang, Chunlei Zhang, Fengyi Zhang, Rongqiang Yuan, Sobhi F Lamlom

## 摘要
Homeodomain-leucine zipper (HD-ZIP) transcription factors regulate plant development and responses to environmental stress. This study characterized selected soybean (Glycine max) HD-ZIP IV candidates by integrating domain screening, genomic mapping, phylogenetic comparison, evolutionary analysis, general co-expression networks, and secondary analysis of cold-stress expression data. A broader domain audit recovered 95 loci with family-associated architectures, comprising 54 with I/II patterns, 12 with III patterns, and 29 with IV patterns. Comparison with a published 88-gene inventory established correspondences for 86 entries, of which 82 satisfied the joint-domain criteria; recovery therefore remained conditional rather than demonstrating completeness. The 29 selected IV-pattern candidates were distributed across 15 chromosomes and encoded representative proteins of 713-836 amino acids. Domain architecture and placement alongside Arabidopsis IV reference proteins supported their classification. Of 160 candidate pairs, 122 yielded finite NG86 estimates, all with Ka/Ks ratios below 1.0, consistent with purifying constraint. However, 79 finite pairs had Ks > 2, limiting duplication dating. The general co-expression network contained 617 nodes and 802 edges; candidate rankings differed between connectivity and betweenness metrics. Cold-stress expression statistics were estimable for 22 candidates. At adjusted P < 0.01 and |log2 fold change| ≥ 1, genotype-dependent temporal contrasts identified 11, 8, and 8 candidates at 2, 4, and 8 h, respectively. Gm.HDZ6, Gm.HDZ19, and Gm.HDZ22 met these criteria at all three times. These findings identify candidates for functional investigation while distinguishing evolutionary constraints and expression associations from complete family recovery, reliable duplication chronology, and causal mechanisms of cold tolerance.
