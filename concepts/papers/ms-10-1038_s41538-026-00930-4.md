### 核心发现
1. These findings confirm that the yellowing process plays a crucial role in the development of characteristic flavor profile of MDBYT.
2. Processing techniques play a key role in the formation of MDBYT quality, with yellowing step representing the core operation.
3. Collectively, these reactions enhance sweetness and refreshness while shaping the unique flavor profile of MDBYT.
4. Collectively, the attributes of sweetness, umami, mellowness, thickness, and acceptability gradually increased during processing, while the grass taste and fired flavor progressively decreased.
5. These results suggest that amino acids accumulate in the early stages of processing and may be consumed during later stages, possibly through Maillard reactions or thermal degradation at elevated temperatures.
6. These results suggest that the accumulation of umami- and sweet-tasting amino acids during yellowing contributes substantially to the characteristic taste of MDBYT 12, while the increase in bitter amino acids during later stages may enhance thickness and flavor complexity.
7. Notably, the first two of the three yellowing stages contributed most substantially to the improvement in quality scores.
