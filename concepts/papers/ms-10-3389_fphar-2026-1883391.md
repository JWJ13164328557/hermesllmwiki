### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these findings suggest that although conventional mechanical debridement effectively reduces the microbial burden, it is insufficient to completely eliminate persistent infectious foci, thereby compromising long-term therapeutic outcomes.
2. 2 Targets of LDDSs in the periodontal microenvironment The therapeutic performance of LDDSs in periodontitis is constrained by the complex and dynamic microenvironment within periodontal pockets.
3. Collectively, these barriers constrain the therapeutic efficacy of periodontal local drug delivery systems (LDDSs).
4. These characteristics represent the fundamental cause of the limited penetration efficiency of most LDDSs and have consequently become a primary target for the development of next- generation nanodelivery strategies (Lv et al., 2023).
5. Collectively, these findings provide direct evidence that insufficient penetration into the deeper biofilm layers represents a critical factor limiting the therapeutic efficacy of LDDSs (Kang et al., 2024).
6. Collectively, these approaches aim to improve drug delivery efficiency while enabling the precise eradication of mature biofilms.
7. These results suggest that conventional static in vitro models may overestimate the local retention performance of drug delivery systems and further highlight persistent GCF- mediated clearance as a critical physiological barrier limiting the efficacy of local periodontal drug delivery (Makkar et al., 2023).
8. Collectively, the complex anatomical architecture of the periodontal pocket, persistent GCF-mediated clearance, and the protective mucus barrier synergistically restrict the intralesional distribution, local retention, and tissue penetration of LDDSs.
