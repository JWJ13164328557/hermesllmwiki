# Uncoupling hypersensitive cell death response and disease resistance activated by effector-triggered immunity.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Cell reports (2026)
- **PMID**: [43019053](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019053/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118232](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118232)
- **第一作者**: Himanshu Chhillar
- **作者**: Himanshu Chhillar, Henk-Jan Schoonbeek, Bruno Pok Man Ngou, Jonathan Dg Jones, Pingtao Ding

## 摘要
Effector-triggered immunity (ETI) is frequently accompanied by the hypersensitive response (HR), a localized form of programmed cell death considered closely associated with disease resistance. However, the mechanistic relationship between HR and effective immunity remains incompletely understood. We show that the Arabidopsis cbp60g sard1 double mutant exhibits exaggerated ETI-associated HR while retaining partial resistance to bacterial pathogens, providing a genetically tractable system uncoupling disease resistance from cell death without visible pleiotropic defects. Transcriptome profiling reveals that CBP60g and SARD1 coordinate positive and negative immune regulatory programs, including activation of the Nudix hydrolase NUDT7, a downstream target that partially restrains HR. The incomplete restoration of the mutant phenotype by NUDT7 overexpression suggests that additional CBP60g/SARD1-dependent negative regulators buffer excessive cell death. Our findings demonstrate that hypersensitive cell death is genetically separable from effective disease resistance and reveal how CBP60g/SARD1 coordinates transcriptional networks to balance immunity with protection against self-inflicted damage.
