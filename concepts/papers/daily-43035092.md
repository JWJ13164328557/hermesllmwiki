# Mn3O4 nanoparticles enhance drought tolerance in cotton via ROS scavenging-dependent ABA signaling and ROS scavenging-independent osmotic homeostasis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology and biochemistry : PPB (2026)
- **PMID**: [43035092](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43035092/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plaphy.2026.111960](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plaphy.2026.111960)
- **第一作者**: Yao Wang
- **作者**: Yao Wang, Miao Zhao, Lei Zheng, Min Li, Guangjing Li, Yukun Wang, Yiping Sui, Xunyangbin Shi

## 摘要
Drought stress severely impairs crop growth, yield, and quality, posing increasing threats to agricultural sustainability. Developing eco-friendly strategies to improve plant drought resilience is therefore critically important. Poly(acrylic) acid-coated manganese oxide (Mn3O4) nanoparticles (PMO), a functional nanomaterial, can scavenge reactive oxygen species (ROS) and strengthen plant stress tolerance; yet, the underlying molecular regulatory mechanisms remain largely unclear. In this study, physiological, transcriptomic, and biochemical analyses were performed on drought-stressed cotton seedlings with or without 400 mg/L PMO application. The results demonstrated that, under drought stress condition, PMO increased the above-ground fresh biomass by 58.6% and above-ground dry biomass by 5.8%, improved photosynthetic efficiency by 141.6%-239.6%, and enhanced water-use efficiency by 43.4%-166.8%. Moreover, PMO facilitated abscisic acid (ABA) accumulation and stomatal closure via repressing the expression of UDP-glucosyltransferases (GhUGTs) and protein phosphatase 2Cs (GhPP2Cs) in a ROS scavenging-dependent pathway. In addition, PMO downregulated the proline degradation gene GhProDH2 and upregulated the sucrose synthase gene GhSus and sugar transporter GhSWEET family genes to promote osmolyte biosynthesis in a ROS scavenging-independent manner. Collectively, PMO improves cotton drought tolerance by coordinating ROS scavenging-dependent ABA signaling and ROS scavenging-independent osmotic adjustment, which offers an effective strategy for cultivating drought-tolerant crops.
