# Blue light photoreceptor cryptochrome 1 促进 wood formation and 花青素 生物合成 in Populus

> Blue light photoreceptor cryptochrome 1 promotes wood formation and anthocyanin biosynthesis in Populus

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant, Cell &amp; Environment (2024)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/pce.14866](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.14866)

- **作者**: Xiaoman Chen, Yiting Fan, Ying Guo, Shuyi Li, Bo Zhang, Hao Li

## 物种
Arabidopsis thaliana, Populus

## 摘要
AbstractBlue light photoreceptor cryptochrome 1 (CRY1) in herbaceous plants plays crucial roles in various developmental processes, including cotyledon expansion, hypocotyl elongation and anthocyanin biosynthesis. However, the function of CRY1 in perennial trees is unclear. In this study, we identified two ortholog genes of CRY1 (PagCRY1a and PagCRY1b) from Populus, which displayed high sequence similarity to Arabidopsis CRY1. Overexpression of PagCRY1 substantially inhibited plant growth and promoted secondary xylem development in Populus, while CRISPR/Cas9‐mediated knockout of PagCRY1 enhanced plant growth and delayed secondary xylem development. Moreover, overexpression of PagCRY1 dramatically increased anthocyanin accumulation. The further analysis supported that PagCRY1 functions specifically in response to blue light. Taken together, our results demonstrated that modulating the expression of blue light photoreceptor CRY1 ortholog gene in Populus could significantly influence plant biomass production and the process of wood formation, laying a foundation for further investigating the light‐regulated tree growth.
