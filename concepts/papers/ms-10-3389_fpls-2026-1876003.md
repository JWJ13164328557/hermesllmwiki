### 核心发现
1. In summary, the combination of callus formation, healthy tissue maintenance, and the emergence of new growth can serve as reliable markers for evaluating the success of grafting in olive and other horticultural practices.
2. 5 Conclusion This study demonstrates the feasibility and efﬁciency of in vitro micrografting using clonal rootstocks in olive, providing the ﬁrst evidence of its successful application within a fully clonal system.
3. Therefore, the development of more efﬁcient and standardized grafting systems, particularly involving selected clonal rootstocks, is essential to accelerate the availability of elite cultivars with improved agronomic performance and abiotic and biotic stress tolerance.
4. Notably, this timeframe could be further reduced when clonal material is already available in vitro, and because the system is independent of seasonal constraints.
