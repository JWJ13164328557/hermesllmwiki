# The WRKY transcription factor NtWRKY11 confers cold tolerance in tobacco via an ABA-associated regulatory pathway.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant science : an international journal of experimental plant biology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43025614](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43025614/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113594](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113594)
- **第一作者**: Yong Luo
- **作者**: Yong Luo, Lianjun Feng, Zongze Geng, Wei Du, Lili Wu, Qiwei Yu, Haiyang Wen, Yongxia Yang

## 摘要
WRKY transcription factors contribute substantially to plant responses toward diverse abiotic stresses. Nevertheless, little is known regarding the molecular mechanism of tobacco NtWRKY11 under cold stress conditions. Herein, we identified that NtWRKY11, a nuclear-localized transcription factor, is markedly induced by cold and drought stresses. Overexpression of NtWRKY11 significantly improved tobacco cold tolerance, as evidenced by reduced electrolyte leakage, lower reactive oxygen species (ROS) accumulation, higher seed-germination rate, and better-maintained photosynthetic capacity under cold stress. Transcriptome and physiological analyses revealed that NtWRKY11 may promotes the transcription of ABA biosynthetic genes NCED3 and AAO3, facilitating endogenous ABA accumulation under cold stress. NtWRKY11-associated ABA may further modulate CNGC-mediated calcium signaling. Both ABA and calcium-CPK signaling converge and may modulate the CBF-COR regulatory module. Meanwhile, NtWRKY11 enhances antioxidant defense by up-regulating antioxidant-related genes and elevating antioxidant‑enzyme activities. Collectively, NtWRKY11 confers enhanced cold tolerance in tobacco by coordinating ABA-associated calcium signaling, the CBF-COR cold-responsive cascade and antioxidant defense systems.
