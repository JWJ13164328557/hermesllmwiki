### 核心发现
1. Leaf angle, defined as the angle between the vertical stem and the midrib of the leaf blade, is therefore a major breeding target for improving maize productivity, as it directly determines canopy architecture and light interception.
2. These QTLs and genes provide reliable targets for MAS and CRISPR in erect-leaf-angle breeding.
3. Under low-density planting conditions, ZmLG1 interacts with phyB-Pfr to suppress ZmHB53, which regulates adaxial-abaxial axis development.
4. Hormone signalling pathways Hormone signalling pathways play a central role in regulating maize LA by integrating gibberellin (GA), BR, auxin, abscisic acid (ABA), ethylene and jasmonic acid (JA) signalling networks.
5. Collectively, these hormonal pathways modulate cell elongation, division and ligule development, ultimately shaping plant architecture and canopy structure.
6. RAMYA ET AL 4 https://plantsciencetoday.online At the transcriptional level, ZmLG2 targets key transcription factors involved in BR signalling, including BZR1 and ZmBEH1, which further bind to the promoter of GRAS42 (SCARECROW-LIKE 28 ortholog) to regulate LA.
7. Exogenous BR application plays a key role in modulating lamina joint growth and leaf inclination.
8. Collectively, these findings highlight that exogenous hormonal treatments and chemical regulators are effective tools for fine-tuning maize LA and canopy Sl.
