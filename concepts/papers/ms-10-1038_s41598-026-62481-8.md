### 核心发现
1. For AAV9, the residues N470, D271, N272, Y446, and W503 interact with the terminal N-linked galactose as primary receptor13.
2. For tumor targeting, the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) is a validated target due to its high expression rates in tumors.
3. These results confirm the role of EGFR for transduction of cell lines with Affibody ZEGFR:1907−decorated rAAVs.
4. Taken together, AAV9M-453Affi clearly outperformed Fig.
5. introduced the first application of the tropism-diminished AAV9 capsid variant W503A, finding reduced off-target transduction in peripheral organs19.
6. Considering the potentially reduced off-target effects in therapeutic applications, the tropism-diminished AAV9 variants represent a refined platform for generating highly specific AAVs capable of effective brain tumor transduction.
7. The Affi-decorated capsids presented in this study target the natural EGFR, which is also expressed in healthy brain tissue68.
8. EGFR is a validated target for systemic therapy of solid tumors as demonstrated by the approved antibodies Cetuximab (Erbitux), Panitumumab (Vectibix), and Necitumumab (Portrazza)70.
