### 核心发现
1. Notably, we found that the presence of endometrial lymphoid aggregates was strongly associated with adverse pregnancy outcomes, with a predictive value superior to that of plasma cell counts.
2. Collectively, we establish a detailed immune cell atlas of the cycling human endometrium and deﬁne a pathogenic mechanism for adverse preg- nancy outcomes.
3. CD11c+ NK cells expand during the secretory phase and interact with EVTs in early pregnancy To further verify the temporal dynamics of the three primary NK subsets, we performed in situ quantitative analysis.
4. By constructing a high-resolution immune cell atlas of the human endometrium across the menstrual cycle, we identiﬁed CD11c+ NK cells as a specialized functional subset that exhibits dynamic accumulation during the secretory phase and pregnancy, and demonstrated their unique ability to physically interact with EVTs.
5. Collectively, in this mouse model, the genetic depletion of Tfh cells reduced lymphoid aggregate formation and partially rescued pregnancy outcomes, providing experimental evidence for a causal contribution of Tfh-dependent lymphoid aggregates to compromised establishment and maintenance of pregnancy in mice.
6. Discussion This study establishes a high-resolution immune cell atlas of 53 healthy endometria across eight precisely timed menstrual stages.
