### 核心发现
1. Epiphytic diatoms, as a component of broader epiphytic assemblages on marine macrophytes, play a central role within these communities due to their high abundance and ecological and functional importance (Chung and Lee, 2008).

3. Taken together, these ﬁndings indicate that macroalgae represent dynamic and ecologically relevant hosts whose architecture, persistence, and environmental setting can strongly inﬂuence the organization of epiphytic diatom assemblages.
4. Moreover, light, which is essential for photosyn- thesis, creates vertical zonation on leaves: more exposed areas host adherent forms while shaded portions show less structured com- munities (Oh et al., 2009).
