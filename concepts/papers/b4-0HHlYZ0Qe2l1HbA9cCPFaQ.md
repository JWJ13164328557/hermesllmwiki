## 1. Scientific Context

Cis-regulatory elements (CREs) are essential for regulating gene expression, yet their evolutionary dynamics in plants remain elusive. Here we constructed a single-cell chromatin accessibility atlas for Oryza sativa from 103,911 nuclei representing 126 cell states across nine organs.
