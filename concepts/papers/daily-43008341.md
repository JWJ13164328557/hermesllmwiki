# The virulent milRNA Foc-milR87 manipulates host hormone signaling to promote root infection by Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. cubense.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Microbiological research (2027)
- **PMID**: [43008341](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43008341/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.micres.2026.128808](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micres.2026.128808)
- **第一作者**: Chengcheng He
- **作者**: Chengcheng He, Siman Cheng, Ruling Gan, Rong Huang, Guanghui Kong, Pinggen Xi, Zide Jiang, Junjian Situ

## 摘要
Banana (Musa spp.), a critical global staple and cash crop, is facing severe threats from Fusarium wilt caused by Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. cubense Tropical Race 4 (Foc TR4). Deciphering the molecular pathogenesis of Foc TR4 and its immune regulatory network with the host is essential for developing novel control strategies targeting host resistance genes. Foc-milR87 is a virulent milRNA that targets the banana AP2/ERF family transcription factor MaPTI6L, but how it regulates downstream immune network in banana is still mysterious. Here, we analyzed transcriptomic data from banana roots infected with wild-type Foc TR4 (XJZ2) and Foc-milR87 knockout mutant (ΔFoc-milR87) to investigate Foc-milR87's regulatory role in pathogenesis. Knockout of the Foc-milR87 precursor significantly impaired Foc TR4's root infection and colonization capability. Transcriptomic analysis and experimental evidence revealed that Foc-milR87 modulates banana salicylic acid, ethylene, and abscisic acid pathways to promote root infection. Bioinformatic analysis identified numerous transcription factors from AP2/ERF, WRKY, and bHLH families as potential downstream targets, suggesting that Foc-milR87 manipulates these regulators to reprogram the host transcriptome and disturb plant immunity. This study elucidates the molecular role of fungal milRNA in early pathogenesis, offering novel insights for banana wilt resistance breeding.
