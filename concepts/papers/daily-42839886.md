# OsDRP1C mediates root development by modulating OsPIN1 cycling in rice.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: The New phytologist (2026)
- **PMID**: [42839886](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42839886/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/nph.71625](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.71625)
- **第一作者**: Mengzhen Li
- **作者**: Mengzhen Li, Jianshu Zhu, Jiming Xu, Yuelin Luo, Keju Zhao, Meiyan Ren, Yu Liu, Yanlin Shao

## 摘要
Root length plays a crucial role in plants' ability to absorb water and nutrients from the soil. However, the molecular mechanisms underlying root elongation in rice (Oryza sativa) remain unclear. Here, we identified a short root mutant, short root-31 (sr31), and cloned the causal gene by map-based cloning, which carries a point mutation in the coding sequence of DYNAMIN-RELATED PROTEIN 1C (OsDRP1C). Genetic complementation analyses demonstrated that OsDRP1C is responsible for the phenotypic defect of sr31. OsDRP1C is widely expressed in roots, with higher expression in root meristem, epidermal cells and vascular bundles. Colocalization analysis revealed that OsDRP1C is present in the plasma membrane (PM) and endomembrane system. FM4-64 internalization assay showed that endocytosis is inhibited in sr31. Further study indicated that OsDRP1D and OsDRP1E are also involved in root elongation; OsDRP1C interacts directly with OsDRP1D/E, and they function additively on root elongation. Furthermore, we showed that the trafficking and recycling of OsPIN1b between endomembrane and PM localization are attenuated in sr31, which affects auxin polar transport. These results provided the first insights into the molecular mechanism of OsDRP1C modulating OsPIN1b cycling in regulating root elongation, expanded our understanding of OsDRP1s' association with membrane protein trafficking in rice.

根长在植物从土壤中吸收水分和养分的过程中起着至关重要的作用。然而，水稻(Oryza sativa)根系伸长的分子机制仍不够清楚。 本研究鉴定了一个短根突变体short root‐31 (sr31)，并通过图位克隆方法克隆了其调控基因DYNAMIN‐RELATED PROTEIN 1C (OsDRP1C)，sr31中该基因的编码序列中产生了一个点突变。遗传互补和功能缺失分析表明OsDRP1C是导致sr31表型缺陷的基因。 OsDRP1C在根中广泛表达，在根分生组织、表皮细胞和维管束中表达较高。共定位分析表明OsDRP1C定位于质膜和内膜系统中。FM4‐64内吞实验表明sr31中内吞作用受到抑制。进一步研究表明，OsDRP1D和OsDRP1E也参与根伸长，OsDRP1C与OsDRP1D/E直接互作，并且它们在根伸长方面具有加性作用。此外，我们证明在sr31中OsPIN1b在内膜系统与质膜之间的运输和循环减弱，从而影响生长素极性运输。 这些结果首次揭示了OsDRP1C通过调控OsPIN1bOsDRP1s与膜蛋白运输之间关系的理解。.
