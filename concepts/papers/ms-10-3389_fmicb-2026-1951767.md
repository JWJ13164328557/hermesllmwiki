### 核心发现
1. Beneficial microorganisms also interact with pollinators and natu­ ral enemies of pests.
2. Collectively, these microbial interactions transform the coffee root system into a highly efficient nutrient acquisition network capable of sustaining plant growth under nutrient-poor tropi­ cal soils.
3. Abscisic acid (ABA) plays a central role in drought responses by regulating stomatal closure.
