# Comparative Genome-Wide Association Studies of Metabolites and Grain-Related Traits in Common Wheat.

## 摘要
The metabolome is highly diverse and the closest layer to phenotype; therefore, it is commonly regarded as a bridge between the genome and phenome in plants. Here, we performed large-scale metabolome analysis using liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) and 33 grain-related traits in a diverse panel of natural accessions and a recombinant inbred line (RIL) population. We identified a new network of 2286 associations between 947 metabolites and 33 grain-related traits. Systematic integration of metabolic genome-wide association study (mGWAS) and metabolic quantitative trait locus (mQTL) analyses identified 33 566 significant single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) and 3128 mQTL. Thirteen annotated metabolites co-localized within a physical interval on 7A. Integration of metabolite-based and phenotype-based GWAS and QTL revealed an overlapped region for gibberellin A4 (GA4) content and grain roundness on 4A. Phenotyping of an ethyl methanesulfonate (EMS)-induced mutant confirmed the role of TaSDR in regulating GA4 content and grain morphology. These findings provide novel insights into the metabolic pathways influencing key grain-related traits and advance our understanding of the complex molecular mechanisms regulating grain metabolites and phenotypes in wheat. The identified metabolic markers and candidate genes provide valuable targets for molecular breeding programs aimed at improving wheat yield and quality.

### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these findings reveal some genomic regions showing associations between metabolic pathways and agronomically important grain‐related traits, and provide promising targets for identifying candidate genes with pleiotropic effects or closely linked regulatory functions.
2. Taken together, our findings support that TaSDR is involved in GA4 accumulation and grain morphology.
3. Collectively, this study reveals substantial phenotypic diversity and highlights a trade‐off between starch pasting properties and dough strength, providing insights for targeted wheat improvement.
4. Collectively, these findings suggest that plants have evolved intrinsic regulatory mechanisms to coordinate diverse metabolic pathways with grain‐related traits.
5. Furthermore, we found that metabolites associated with the flavonoid pathway (mr1070, mr1149) and the phytohormone and its derivative (mr2098) pathway also showed negative correlations with grain width in both the AP and XX‐RIL populations (Tables and ).
6. Our findings reveal novel genetic determinants and provide mechanistic insights into grain‐related and metabolic traits in wheat.
7. The identified metabolic markers and candidate genes provide valuable targets for molecular breeding programs aimed at improving wheat yield and quality.
8. Collectively, these studies demonstrated that integrating metabolomics with other high‐throughput technologies can substantially enhances the toolkit available for genomics‐assisted selection (Fernie and Schauer ).
