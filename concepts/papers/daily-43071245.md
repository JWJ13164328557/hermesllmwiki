# Genome-metabolome integration reveals NRPS-associated peptides and redox-active metabolites correlated with antioxidant and cyanide-detoxifying functions of Pediococcus acidilactici LP05 in cassava fermentation.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Biotechnology letters (2026)
- **PMID**: [43071245](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43071245/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s10529-026-03801-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10529-026-03801-w)
- **第一作者**: Andri Frediansyah
- **作者**: Andri Frediansyah, Salfa Athallah Agtari Nabillah, Nodali Ndraha, Lucia Dhiantika Witasari

## 摘要
Pediococcus acidilactici LP05, isolated from modified cassava flour (MOCAF) fermentation, was characterized through integrated genomic and metabolomic analyses. Whole-genome sequencing yielded a high-quality assembly (1.96 Mb, 99.3% complete; GenBank JBUZKT000000000) confirmed as P. acidilactici by ANI (99.9%) and dDDH (91.7%) metrics. Functional annotation revealed enrichment in carbohydrate metabolism, proteostasis, oxidative stress defense (katA, trxA/B, msrA/B), and an antiSMASH-predicted NRPS cluster. In vitro validation demonstrated statistically significant 2.9-fold DPPH and 2.6-fold ABTS radical scavenging enhancement (p < 0.001) compared to unfermented controls, while rhodanese-like sulfurtransferases genomically identified in LP05 corresponded with a moderate but statistically significant 26% cyanide reduction (1.00 to 0.74 ppm KCN; p = 0.0002). UPLC-HRMS putatively identified several cyclic dipeptides (including Cyclo(Phe-Pro) and Cyclo(Tyr-Pro)) whose detection is temporally associated with the predicted NRPS cluster and indole metabolites, which may mechanistically contribute to antioxidant bioactivity. Collectively, these findings suggest that LP05 has potential as a multifunctional starter culture for enhancing cassava fermentation safety, oxidative stability, and nutritional value, although further functional validation of NRPS activity and cyanide detoxification mechanisms is warranted.
