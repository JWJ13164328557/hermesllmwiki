### 核心发现
1. LAG-3 interacts with MHC class II molecules, interfering with effective T-cell receptor (TCR) signaling and dampening T-cell activation (29).
2. Collectively, these mechanisms reduce effector function of CD4+ T-cells and impair the cytotoxic activity of CD8+ T-cells against tumor cells (28, 29).
3. Collectively, these ﬁndings highlight the pronounced dysfunctional phenotype ac- quired by T-cells within the PDAC tumor microenvironment.
4. Collectively, these data demonstrate that despite its simplicity, the co-culture 3D PDAC model robustly reproduces key features of checkpoint upregulation observed in inﬁltrating T-cells in PDAC tumors (Figure 2).
5. Collectively, these results recapitulate the checkpoint molecule expression signature previously observed in tumor inﬁltrating T-cells (Figure 2).
6. Collectively, these ﬁndings indicate that inhibition of the CXCR4–CXCL12 axis effectively restores T-cell inﬁltration into the tumor cell region, further supporting the role of CAF-driven CXCL12 signaling as a clinically relevant mechanism of immune exclusion in PDAC.
7. Cytotoxic lymphocytes (CD8+ T- cells, (CTL)) are crucial for the elimination of tumor cells, medi- ating killing through the secretion of granzyme B, perforin, IFN-g, and TNF-a, or of mediating FasL-induced apoptosis (12).
8. Notably, a similar shift in the CD4/CD8 ratio toward CD4+ T-cell predominance was detected in peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMC) from the same patients, with a 3.5-fold increase.
