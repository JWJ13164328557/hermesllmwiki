### 核心发现
1. Reasonably dense planting is required for the achievement of high yields in cotton.
2. Source-sink balance is necessary for vigorous plant growth and high-yielding cotton.
3. Adequate photosynthetic carbohydrates produced by cotton leaves are essential to attain fiber quality approaching genetic maximums (Petti- grew 2001; Zhao and Oosterhuis 1998).
4. (1965) reported that an LAI of 5.0 is required to sustain the growth of early-maturing bolls for achieving maximal cotton yield.
5. Journal of Cotton Research (2026) 9:36 Wells and Meredith (1984a, b) found that an LAI of 3.9– 6.3 is necessary to attain the desirable carbon assimilation rates.
