### 核心发现
1. Light naphtha fractions in the C₅–C₆ range represent the primary feedstock for isomerization units and play a crucial role in increasing gasoline octane number while complying with environmental regulations.
2. Therefore, selective separation of light hydrocarbons (C₄–C₆) plays a crucial role in gasoline pool optimization, vapor pressure control, and the production of high-octane blending components [26].
