## 摘要
Drought stress is one of the most severe constraints affecting wheat production worldwide. 1. In addition, SA priming increases the accumulation of osmolytes such as proline and glycine betaine, which play a crucial role in mitigating the adverse effects of abiotic stresses [, ].
2. During water deficit, ABA plays a pivotal role in drought adaptation by inducing stomatal closure to reduce transpiration, stimulating the accumulation of osmolytes, and modulating root architecture to improve water-use efficiency [ ].
3. Gene-level studies also reinforce the role of IAA: overexpression of the TrIAA27 gene in Arabidopsis thaliana increased biomass, drought tolerance, and stress-responsive signaling pathways [ ].
