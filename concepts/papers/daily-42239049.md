## 摘要
Transposable elements insert non-randomly into host genomes, generating mutations whose consequences depend on the tissue and developmental stage in which transposition occurs. We analyzed 3.2 million de novo Mutator (Mu) insertions across maize leaf, root, pollen, and endosperm to map target site preferences and their tissue specificity at fine-scale resolution.
