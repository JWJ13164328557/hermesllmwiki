### 核心发现
1. In summary, this study demonstrates that diazotrophic bacterial inoculation enhances growth, biomass accumu­ lation, N uptake, and NUE in sugarcane supplied with amino acid-based nutrition in a genotype-dependent manner.
2. 4 Discussion This study demonstrates, for the first time, that the combina­ tion of plant growth-promoting bacteria (PGPB) and amino acid-based N nutrition enhances the utilization of organic N in sugarcane, resulting in improvements in plant growth, biomass accumulation, N accumulation, and nitrogen use efficiency (NUE).
3. Together, these findings suggest that the superior perfor­ mance of RB966928 resulted from integrated effects of bac­ terial inoculation on plant development and N utilization.
4. Taken together, these findings indicate that PGPB modulate N metabolism in a cultivar and N source-dependent manner rather than uniformly enhanc­ ing N acquisition.
5. Importantly, the similar performance observed between OR-N + PGPB and mineral N treatments indicates that the combination of amino acids and bacterial inoculation was Fig.
6. Therefore, our findings provide new evidence that bacterial inoculation can modulate the utilization of amino acid-derived N, promot­ ing cultivar-dependent improvements in growth and NUE.
7. Importantly, NUE was also greater under OR-N + PGPB, particularly in RB966928, demonstrating that inoculation improved not only biomass production but also the efficiency with which plants converted absorbed N into plant growth.
8. Therefore, further studies under soil-based and field conditions are necessary to determine whether the interactions observed between amino acids and PGPB can translate into agro­ nomic benefits under production systems.
