# A conserved role for the transcription factor SUPERMAN in seed development across eudicots.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of experimental botany (2026)
- **PMID**: [43069734](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43069734/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/jxb/erag574](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erag574)
- **第一作者**: Edelín Roque
- **作者**: Edelín Roque, Ana L Rodas, María D Gómez, Yosapol Harnvanichvech, Jiangqi Wen, Kirankumar S Mysore, Dolf Weijers, José P Beltrán

## 摘要
Seed development depends on the coordinated growth of maternal and filial tissues, yet how conserved regulatory genes contribute to this process across species remains unclear. Here, we reveal a role for the C2H2 transcription factor SUPERMAN (SUP) in seed development. Using a comparative approach in Arabidopsis thaliana, Medicago truncatula, and Solanum lycopersicum, we show that SUP and related orthologs are consistently associated with integumentary tissues and the micropylar and chalazal regions of the developing seed endosperm. While this spatial pattern is conserved, the timing and tissue context of their activity differ between species. Functional analyses show that these genes contribute to early seed development, with mutations causing altered embryogenesis and increased seed abortion in Arabidopsis, impaired early seed development in Medicago, and ovule defects in tomato. In Arabidopsis, these phenotypes are associated with altered auxin responsiveness, suggesting that SUP modulates hormone-regulated processes between maternal and filial seed tissues. Overall, our findings identify SUP and SUP-like genes as conserved regulators of seed development and suggest that developmental diversification may arise through changes in the timing and spatial context of their activity.
