# Genetic and Transcriptome-Level Dissection of the Powdery Mildew Resistance in Spelt Accession L251.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Phytopathology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43037365](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43037365/)
- **DOI**: [10.1094/PHYTO-05-26-0159-R](https://doi.org/10.1094/PHYTO-05-26-0159-R)
- **第一作者**: Yuting Liang
- **作者**: Yuting Liang, Guohao Han, Jiadong Zhang, Hongxing Xu, Yumei Zhang, Ningning Yu, Lige Geng, Yanmin Qie

## 摘要
Wheat (Triticum aestivum) powdery mildew, caused by Blumeria graminis f. sp. tritici (Bgt), is a devastating foliar disease worldwide. Spelt (T. spelta), as an ancient cultivated wheat type, harbors untapped genetic diversity for wheat improvement against powdery mildew. In this study, we characterized the powdery mildew resistance in spelt accession L251, which exhibited resistance to two Bgt isolates. Genetic analysis revealed that the resistance to these two Bgt isolates is controlled by a single dominant gene, tentatively designated PmL251. Using bulked segregant analysis and linkage mapping, PmL251 was mapped to a 2.52-Mb physical interval (701.81 to 704.33 Mb) on chromosome arm 2BL. Mapping interval and sequence alignment demonstrated that PmL251 is identical to the Pm6 gene. Expression pattern analysis revealed that PmL251 was rapidly upregulated in L251 following Bgt inoculation, peaking at 12 h postinoculation with a sixfold increase, whereas no expression was detected in susceptible L211. Transcriptomic analysis further identified differentially expressed genes enriched in defense response to fungus, oxidative stress, chitin catabolism, and phenylpropanoid biosynthesis. We also validated four closely linked markers as effective tools for marker-assisted selection across 46 susceptible wheat cultivars. Collectively, this study identifies Pm6 as the functional gene conferring powdery mildew resistance in the spelt background, provides molecular markers for efficient selection, and offers valuable genetic resources for wheat breeding.
