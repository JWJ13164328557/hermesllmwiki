# ZmFLP Controls Symmetric Division of Guard Mother Cells and Associates with BZU2/ZmMUTE during Stomatal Development in Maize.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of experimental botany (2026)
- **PMID**: [43042620](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43042620/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/jxb/erag579](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erag579)
- **第一作者**: Bolun Meng
- **作者**: Bolun Meng, Wenhui Li, Yusen Zhou, Qingzeng Li, Yuhua Chang, José Ramón Botella, Pengtao Wang, Siyi Guo

## 摘要
Stomata in grasses such as maize consist of dumbbell-shaped guard cells (GCs) produced by a single symmetric division of the guard mother cell (GMC), a process requiring strict regulation for proper function. Here, we show that the maize R2R3-MYB transcription factor ZmFLP is essential to terminate GMC division. CRISPR-Cas9 generated zmflp null mutants undergo additional longitudinal or transverse divisions after the initial symmetric GMC division, leading to aberrant GC clusters and abnormal subsidiary cells (SCs). As a consequence, mutants show elevated leaf temperature and seedling lethality. Transcriptome profiling revealed extensive changes in zmflp mutants, including upregulation of cell cycle and MAPK-associated genes and downregulation of key stomatal development regulators, including ZmFAMA, ZmSCRM2, and ZmTMM. ZmFLP physically interacts with the bHLH transcription factor BZU2/ZmMUTE in the nucleus, and dual-luciferase assays reveal that co-expression of BZU2 enhances ZmFLP transcriptional repression. Furthermore, introduction of an additional native-promoter-driven VENUS-BZU2 transgene reduced the frequency of abnormal stomatal complexes in zmflp mutants. Together, these findings establish ZmFLP as a key regulator of GMC division and suggest a functional link between ZmFLP and BZU2 in the final stages of stomatal development in maize.
