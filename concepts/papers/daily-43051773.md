# Construction of electrospun zein-based nanofibers mediated by multi-arm active sites of tea polyphenols and their application in room-temperature preservation of avocado.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.) (2026)
- **PMID**: [43051773](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43051773/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.foodres.2026.121071](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodres.2026.121071)
- **第一作者**: Fang Sun
- **作者**: Fang Sun, Xin Fan, Yujun Xian, Kai Li, Junrong Huang

## 摘要
Avocado, a high-value climacteric fruit, undergoes rapid post-harvest deterioration due to spontaneous ethylene release. Although electrospun zein nanofibers possess the capacity to adsorb ethylene, their restricted spinnability (which necessitates a zein content of ≥24 wt%) and high cost impede their practical application. This study utilized the active phenolic hydroxyl groups of tea polyphenols (TP) to create multi-arm active sites, which enabled specific hydrogen bonding with zein amino acid residues. This enhancement of zein molecular entanglement and improvement in spinnability ultimately promoted the controlled production of fine nanofibers at a low zein concentration (15 wt%). Molecular simulations and FTIR analysis confirmed the existence of intermolecular hydrogen bonds. In comparison with pure zein films (15Z), the 15Z-11TP composite displayed a 98.20% increase in ethylene adsorption. Furthermore, it exhibited superior hydrophobicity and mechanical strength. After 21 days of storage at room temperature, the total phenol content of avocado flesh in 15Z-11TP packaging was 0.48 ± 0.01 μmol GAE/g DW, and the antioxidant activity was 68.63 ± 1.41%. These values were elevated by 48.67% and 92.89% respectively in comparison with the control group. This research offers a low-cost and high-performance ethylene-scavenging film for avocado preservation.
