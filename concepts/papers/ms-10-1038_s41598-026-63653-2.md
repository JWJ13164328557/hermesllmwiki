### 核心发现
1. Key physical characteristics such as sphericity, geometric mean diameter, density, and surface area play a crucial role in postharvest handling, grading, packaging, and transportation.
2. Characterizing these native genotypes is crucial for selecting superior varieties suitable for fresh consumption and functional food production and for supporting genetic improvement initiatives that enhance fruit quality, antioxidant capacity, and adaptability to local environmental conditions5.
3. No specific institutional, national, or international permits were required for this study.
4. Notably, several genotypes in the present study exhibited fruit dimensions comparable to or exceeding previously reported accessions from Tunisia and Turkey, emphasizing the promise of Iranian germplasm for breeding higher-yielding cultivars5,9,22.
5. These physical traits are essential for mechanized grading, packaging design, and determining appropriate harvest and postharvest technologies23.
