### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these results suggest that auto- phagy induction in XPA deﬁciency is at least partially attributed to activation of AMPK and inhibition of mTOR.
2. Taken together, these data suggest that PARP activation drives an accelerated aging phenotype in XPA and that pharmacological intervention with PARP inhibitors or compounds that increase NAD+ can partially normalize this phenotype.
3. More importantly, we found that the lifespan defect in xpa-1 worms could be rescued by treatments with both the NAD+ precursors NR and NMN and through PARP inhibition using AZD2281 (olaparib).
4.
