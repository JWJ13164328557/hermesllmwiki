### 核心发现
1. Interestingly, ubiquitination of L28 ribosomal protein differs from previously described cell-cycle-dependent ubiquitination events in that it does not serve to target the substrate for degradation.
2. eIF4G also interacts with the ribosome through eIF3, a multisubunit initiation factor (Figure 1a).
3. One important target for both eIF4E and eIF3 is the CLN3 mRNA, whose translation is dramatically decreased when the activity of either of these factors is compromised [9,10].
4. This complex interacts with and inhibits the PI3K-like kinases TOR1 and TOR2 (tar- get of rapamycin) in yeast, and a single kinase, FRAP/mTOR (FKBP12–rapamycin-associated protein/mammalian target of rapamycin) in mammals (Figure 2; [11]).
5. It has therefore been suggested that the TOR kinases play a critical role in a signaling pathway that activates eIF4E-dependent protein synthesis and, thereby, induce G1 progression [13].
6. FRAP/mTOR has been shown to directly phosphorylate a subset of sites in the S6 ribosomal protein kinase, p70S6k, and in the 4E-BPs (Figure 2; [3,15]).
7. MAPK phosphorylates and activates Mnk1, which in turn phosphorylates eIF4E [17–19].
8. Mnk1 does not interact with eIF4E directly but functions by dock- ing to eIF4G [19,20].
