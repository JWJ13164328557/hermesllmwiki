### 核心发现
1. Keywords Rosa chinensis, Jasmonic acid, RcMYC2, Histone deacetylation, Flower size Core This study reveals a jasmonic acid (JA)-mediated epige- netic mechanism controlling rose flower size.
2. The tran- scription factor RcMYC2 interacts with Mediator subunit RcMED25 and recruits histone deacetylase RcHDA8 to repress expansin gene RcEXPA8 transcription via histone deacetylation, thereby restricting petal cell expansion and reducing flower diameter.
3. These results suggest that chromatin modifications and histone deacetylases play critical roles in the develop- ment of plant organs, including petals, by regulating cell expansion.
4. Collectively, these data indicate that MeJA treatment inhibits floral organ devel- opment in roses, resulting in smaller flowers.
5. These results suggest a negative correlation between RcMYC2 and flower size.
6. RcMYC2 directly binds to the RcEXPA8 promoter and represses its transcription Expansins are a class of cell wall proteins that loosen the cell wall, thereby promoting cell elongation and expan- sion and determining cell size (Cosgrove 2005).
