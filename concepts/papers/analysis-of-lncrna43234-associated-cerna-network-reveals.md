# 分析 of LncRNA43234-相关的 ceRNA 网络 揭示 Oil Metabolism in Soybean

**方法**: CRISPR/Cas9, RNA-seq

### 核心发现

- Our results suggest that lncRNA43234 may be involved in the pathway of some key enzyme genes participating in oil synthesis through the ceRNA mechanism, affecting oil synthesis.
- transcriptome sequencing and bioinformatics analysis of young soybean pods 30 and 40 d after flowering.25 A total of 10 lncRNAs regulated target genes through miRNAs to influence oil synthesis.
- It was found that lncRNA43234 may be involved in lipid regulation by competitively binding to miRNA10420 and affecting the expression of the target gene XM_014775786.1.
- In addition, the miRNAs predicted to interact with these targeted protein-coding genes showed upregulated expression levels.
- The lncRNAs MSTRG.33053 and targeted protein-coding genes showed upregulated expression levels, and the miRNAs that were predicted to interact with MSTRG.33053 showed down- regulated expression levels
