### 核心发现
1. In contrast, RNAi was less effective at targeting nuclear lncRNAs.30 In previous experi- ments, we found that lncRNA43234 was enriched in the cytoplasm.
2. Our results suggest that lncRNA43234 may be involved in the pathway of some key enzyme genes participating in oil synthesis through the ceRNA mechanism, affecting oil synthesis.
3. Overexpression of lncRNA43234 increased the content of crude protein in seeds, decreased the content of oleic acid, and affected the content of alanine and arginine in free amino acids.
4. transcriptome sequencing and bioinformatics analysis of young soybean pods 30 and 40 d after flowering.25 A total of 10 lncRNAs regulated target genes through miRNAs to influence oil synthesis.
5. It was found that lncRNA43234 may be involved in lipid regulation by competitively binding to miRNA10420 and affecting the expression of the target gene XM_014775786.1.
6. The target genes were individually ligated into the WMV002-bar-RNAi vector using a homologous recombination protocol.
7. In addition, the miRNAs predicted to interact with these targeted protein-coding genes showed upregulated expression levels.
8. The lncRNAs MSTRG.33053 and targeted protein-coding genes showed upregulated expression levels, and the miRNAs that were predicted to interact with MSTRG.33053 showed down- regulated expression levels.
