# Dissecting the genetic architecture for maize prolificacy by combining QTL mapping and genome-wide association study.

## 摘要
Maize prolificacy (ears per plant) is a vital domestication trait in maize that profoundly determines grain yield potential. Deciphering its genetic regulatory network is essential for improving yield performance and expanding germplasm diversity. Here, we performed multi-environment phenotypic evaluation on two RIL populations and one association panel. Integrative QTL mapping and GWAS uncovered 13 QTLs and 8 QTNs associated with maize prolificacy. The stable locus qP9-2 harbored two tissue differentiation-associated genes (Zm00001eb395530, Zm00001eb395480), while the significant SNPs within qP5-1 and qP7-1 target Zm00001eb245490 and Zm00001eb311180, respectively. RT-qPCR assays verified the functional involvement of these four candidate genes in modulating maize multi-ear formation. Further bioinformatic analyses revealed that the ethylene signalling pathway acts as a central regulator of maize prolificacy.
