# Exogenous β-glucan activates systemic acquired resistance (SAR) conferring dual resistance to SRBSDV and Sogatella furcifera in rice.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Pest management science (2026)
- **PMID**: [43070037](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43070037/)
- **DOI**: [10.1002/ps.71486](https://doi.org/10.1002/ps.71486)
- **第一作者**: MengYan Li
- **作者**: MengYan Li, MengTing He, JianBin Chen, Jiao Du, XiaoHua Du, Yue Chen, DeYong Zhang, XiaoBin Shi

## 摘要
BACKGROUND: Rice is one of the most important staple crops, feeding more than half of the global population. Southern rice black-streaked dwarf virus (SRBSDV), which is persistently transmitted by the white-backed planthopper (WBPH, Sogatella furcifera), poses a serious threat to rice production. β-Glucan is a potent plant immune elicitor that induces systemic acquired resistance (SAR). However, the specific signaling pathways through which exogenous β-glucan regulates rice resistance against SRBSDV infection and WBPH infestation remains unclear.

RESULTS: Exogenous β-glucan enhanced photosynthetic capacity and antioxidant defense in healthy rice plants, and reduced the SRBSDV transmission rate and transmission load by WBPH. In SRBSDV-infected rice, β-glucan reduced SRBSDV accumulation and alleviated physiological damage. Exogenous β-glucan promoted mixed-linkage β-glucan (MLG) production and induced cell-wall remodeling by up-regulating OsCslF6, manifested as increased lignin content, thickened sclerenchyma cell walls, and a broadened mechanical-tissue zone. These were accompanied by immune modulation including up-regulated expression of callose-biosynthesis-related genes as well as activation of the salicylic acid (SA) pathway and defense pathways. In vector-insect-related bioassays, β-glucan treatment altered the feeding behavior and virus acquisition amount of WBPH, inhibited its oviposition, and shortened its lifespan.

CONCLUSION: Exogenous β-glucan promote MLG production to induce physical reinforcement of cell walls, accompanied by activation of SA-mediated immune responses, thereby enhancing rice resistance against WBPH and SRBSDV. This study reveals a novel β-glucan-induced defense mechanism in rice and provides a promising biological control strategy for the sustainable management of planthopper-transmitted viral diseases. © 2026 Society of Chemical Industry.
