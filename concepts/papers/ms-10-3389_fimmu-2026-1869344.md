### 核心发现
1. Molecularly, mAb 3D11 infusion led to a downmodulation of CD28 expression on porcine T cells in vivo with the remaining CD28 molecules blocked from binding natural ligand proteins CD80 and CD86, as we show in this publication.
2. Our data show that infusion of mAb 3D11 was well tolerated by the animals and that it did not negatively affect cardiac function during the ﬁrst week after MI.
3. This means that conventional anti-CD28 mAb harbour clones which bind to or close to the binding site of CD80 and CD86 on the tip of the CD28 molecule and are, thus, capable of blocking ligand binding to CD28.
4. Retaining the biological activity of CTLA-4 is a major advantage of selectively blocking CD28 versus blocking both CD28 and CTLA-4 (26) as is the case for CTLA-4-Ig (Abatacept) which binds to CD86/CD80-expressing antigen-presenting cells (27).
