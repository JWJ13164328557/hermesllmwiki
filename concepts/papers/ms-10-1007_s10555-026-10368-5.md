### 核心发现
1. In fumarate hydratase-deficient cells, fumarate directly inhibits TET-mediated demethylation of a regulatory region controlling the miR-200ba429 cluster, thereby acti- vating EMT-associated transcription factors and migratory behavior [68].
2. Collectively, these mechanisms can reshape the acces- sibility of epithelial, mesenchymal, and stress-response pro- grams, thereby influencing not only the metabolic fitness of an EMT state but potentially also its stability and reversibil- ity.
3. For example, EMT in mesenchymal breast carcinoma cells reroutes glutamine toward IDH2-dependent reductive carboxylation and lipid synthesis, reduces its contribution to glutathione production, and increases sensitivity to mechanistic target of rapamy- cin kinase (mTOR) inhibitors [71].
4. These findings support evaluating measurable metabolic dependencies as therapeu- tic targets alongside, or instead of, direct inhibition of core EMT transcription factors; however, candidate dependencies should be validated longitudinally and with sufficient cell- type resolution to distinguish carcinoma cell requirements from those of stromal and immune populations.
5. Collectively, these studies demonstrate that EMT enhances plasticity and stemness, enabling persister cells to survive therapeutic pressure, reconstruct intratumoral het- erogeneity, and sustain therapy resistance [93, 94].
6. The multiplicity of pro-EMT inputs across multiple cell types and molecular axes accounts for why single-target interventions frequently fail to dismantle adverse EMT configurations Cancer and Metastasis Reviews (2026) 45:63 Page 11 of 27 63 (CXCL2) expression recruiting M2 macrophages to support lung metastasis [102].
7. These intricate regulatory systems account for the clinical failure of single- target interventions and underscore the necessity of combi- natorial therapeutic strategies.
8. Collectively, these observations suggest that adverse car- cinoma cell states and microenvironmental programs may jointly support therapeutic persistence and protection from immune elimination, although not all these mechanisms are necessarily driven by EMT itself.
