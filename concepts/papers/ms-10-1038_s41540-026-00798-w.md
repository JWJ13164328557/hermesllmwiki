### 核心发现
1. Several genes from the MAS (GOT1, GOT2, MDH2) are differentially regulated and are the target of therapeutic interventions, further providing the relevance of this network.
2. Several MAS-associated proteins showed strong correlations with patient survival, suggesting that this pathway may play a pivotal role in NSCLC progression.
3. These ﬁndings not only highlight the importance of MAS in NSCLC metabolism but also propose it as a potential target for future therapeutic exploration.
4. The MYC transcription factor plays a central role in metabolic reprogramming by coordinately upregulating key glycolytic genes, includ- ing those encoding glucose transporters, hexokinase, phosphofructokinase- 1, enolase, and lactate dehydrogenase.
5. c-MYC also targets monocarboxylate transporter 4 that extrudes lactic acid produced from glucose.
