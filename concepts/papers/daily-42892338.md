# The bZIP transcription factor ZmbZIP111 contributes to drought tolerance and regulates the expression of ZmEULD1b in maize.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant science : an international journal of experimental plant biology (2027)
- **PMID**: [42892338](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42892338/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113532](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113532)
- **第一作者**: Haidong Yan
- **作者**: Haidong Yan, Dongxu Zhao, Chen Liu, Xueru Zhu, Xueshi Zhang, Zhongxian Ma, Qing Ma, Ronghao Cai

## 摘要
Drought stress severely constrains maize productivity, and elucidating the molecular mechanisms underlying drought tolerance is crucial for developing drought-tolerant cultivars. In this study, we characterized the function of the drought-inducible bZIP transcription factor ZmbZIP111 and found that the bzip111 mutant exhibited increased sensitivity to drought stress. Compared with the wild type, the bzip111 mutants exhibited more pronounced inhibition of root growth under water-deficit conditions, accompanied by significantly reduced survival rates and relative water content. Physiological assays further demonstrated that the mutant accumulated higher levels of malondialdehyde and exhibited decreased superoxide dismutase activity. To elucidate the underlying molecular mechanism, we performed transcriptome analysis and identified ZmEULD1b, a gene previously reported to positively regulate root development and drought tolerance in maize, as a candidate downstream target of ZmbZIP111. Notably, the transcript level of ZmEULD1b was significantly reduced in the bzip111 mutant compared with that in the wild type. Collectively, our findings suggest that ZmbZIP111 contributes to drought tolerance in maize and is associated with the regulation of ZmEULD1b expression. This study provides a valuable candidate gene for the genetic improvement of drought tolerance in maize.
