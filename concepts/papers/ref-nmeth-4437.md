### 核心发现
1. Here, we applied sin­ gle-cell mRNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) to muscle stem cells, and we found a subpopulation that is strongly affected by the widely used dissociation protocol that we employed.
2. Our results show that the SC isolation procedure induces transcriptome-wide changes in a subpopulation of these cells.
3. Taken together, our results highlight the importance of single-cell resolved experiments and validation by orthogonal methods.
4. Importantly, we detected similar subpopu­ lations in other single-cell data sets, suggesting that cells from other tissues may be affected by this artifact as well.
