### 核心发现
1. Here, we report that HEAT SHOCK TRAN- SCRIPTION FACTOR A7b (HSFA7b) plays a crucial role in this process in Arabidopsis, as the absence of functional HSFA7b results in the temporal suppression of SAM activity after thermopriming.
2. We found that HSFA7b directly regulates ethylene response at the SAM by binding to the promoter of the key ethylene signaling gene ETHYLENE-INSENSITIVE 3 to establish thermotolerance.
3. Taken together, our data show that HSFA7b controls ethylene homeostasis during thermopriming, enabling plants to estab- lish thermotolerance.
4. Collectively, our data suggest that HSFA7b, with a minor effect from HSFA7a, regulates expression of ethylene biosynthesis and signaling genes, and therefore ethylene response, at the SAM during thermomemory.
5. Taken together, these results reveal a crucial and tissue-speciﬁc role for HSFA7b in thermomemory at the Arabidopsis SAM.
6. Importantly, our data show that HSFA7b also regulates ethylene response by controlling ethylene biosynthesis.
7. Interestingly, we found that expression of HSFA7b, but not HSFA7a, is mediated by ethylene during HS, as evidenced by reduced expression of HSFA7b in plants treated with AIB or in PT acs mutants.
8. In summary, the combined observations demonstrate that HSFA7a and HSFA7b are required for maintenance of thermomemory.
