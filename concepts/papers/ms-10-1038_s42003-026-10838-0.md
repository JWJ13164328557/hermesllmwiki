### 核心发现
1. Taken together, the decoupling of rAI from ACC and LOC reﬂects a reconﬁguration of how salience and affective networks interact with cortical visual processes during subjective unpleasantness under N₂O.
2. Notably, one connection between the superior sensorimotor cortex and the right posterior parietal node of the FPN reached edge-level signiﬁcance, suggesting it may play a pivotal role in the broader reorganization.
3. We found that N₂O reduces functional network modularity and dis- rupts hierarchical network organization during visual stimulation, with the strongest effects in higher-order integrative systems and relative preserva- tion of visual networks.
4. More broadly, our results support the view that psychedelic compounds exert their inﬂuence by redistributing connectivity from locally segregated, unimodal systems toward more transmodal, integrative networks, thereby reshaping the functional architecture that supports altered conscious experience12,38,39.
5. Importantly, our group has previously demonstrated that rAI responses are sensitive to sensory processing demands during this same visual task, providing task-speciﬁc motivation for its selection as an a priori region of interest22.
6. Notably, although the clustering procedure was agnostic to canonical resting-state network labels, the resulting consensus clusters closely resembled established large-scale functional systems, indicating that canonical functional network organi- zation was preserved during task performance under placebo.
7. Notably, VN regions remained a partial exception, maintaining internal cohesion but at substantially higher dissimilarity values than under placebo andinproximitytocerebellarnodes,suggestingpartialratherthancomplete resilience of visual network organization.
8. This aligns with evidence from pain and affective neuroscience showing that insula–ACC connectivity is critical for the attribution of affective valence to sensory experiences28–30.
