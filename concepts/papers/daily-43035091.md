# VvMYB4-like-VvPIN1/VvCSN5 regulatory module contributes to the grapevine cold-stress response.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology and biochemistry : PPB (2026)
- **PMID**: [43035091](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43035091/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plaphy.2026.111950](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plaphy.2026.111950)
- **第一作者**: Baozhen Zeng
- **作者**: Baozhen Zeng, Houmin Gou, Guangling Shi, Jiying Bao, Zonghuan Ma, Guoping Liang, Baihong Chen, Juan Mao

## 摘要
Cold stress limits grapevine growth and yield, but the contribution of auxin transport to cold tolerance remains unclear. We examined VvPIN1, VvCSN5, and VvMYB4-like in the grapevine cold response. Co-immunoprecipitation (Co-IP) indicated that the corresponding VvPIN1 and VvCSN5 proteins occur in the same complex in grapevine calli. VvPIN1-OE reduced cold tolerance in leaves and calli, whereas VvPIN1-RNAi calli retained more fresh weight, showed higher peroxidase (POD), superoxide dismutase (SOD), and catalase (CAT) activities and proline (Pro) content, accumulated less hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) and malondialdehyde (MDA), and showed stronger cold induction of VvCBF1-VvCBF4, VvICE1, and VvKIN2 than wild-type (WT) calli. VvCSN5-OE in Arabidopsis and VvPIN1/VvCSN5 co-expression in grapevine were associated with improved cold-tolerance phenotypes. VvMYB4-like bound the VvPIN1 promoter and repressed reporter activity, while VvMYB4-like-OE enhanced leaf cold tolerance. These findings support the involvement of a VvMYB4-like-VvPIN1/VvCSN5 module in the grapevine cold response, but they do not establish the biochemical mechanism linking VvCSN5 to VvPIN1 or the causal relationships with downstream cold-response pathways. Because matched VvPIN1-OE and VvCSN5-OE single-gene controls were not included under every condition, the co-expression data do not demonstrate synergy, rescue.
