### 核心发现
1. Our findings suggest that rejuvenation driven by aging-down/development-up genes might be more effec- tive to develop a safer path toward anti-aging interventions.
2. These results suggest that genes whose expression levels altered during the ag- ing and development could contribute in maintaining young and matured status of cells.
3. Collectively, these data indicate that embryonic devel- opmental transcriptional states are characterized by the activation of translational programs enriched in ADDU genes, together with suppression of pathways linked to ag- ing-associated transcriptional dysregulation and oncogenic susceptibility.
4. Collectively, these factors delineate a rejuvenation potential that enhances cellular function while maintaining cell identity.
5. Collectively, the ADDU strategy offers an effective approach for isolating safe rejuvenation-in- duction factors.
6. In conclusion, we suggest that the genes in the ADDU category could be considered as appropriate fac- tors for reverse-aging interventions that can replace the Yamanaka factors and circumvent safety concerns.
7. Interestingly, the signaling pathways and transcriptional programs that orchestrate em- bryonic growth often exhibit antagonistic pleiotropy, func- tioning as drivers of development while potentially con- tributing to dysfunction in later years (10).
8. Notably, epigenomic and phenotypic analyses re- vealed substantial overlap between aging-related genes and those essential for stemness and embryonic development.
