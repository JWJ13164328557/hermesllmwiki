### 核心发现
1. Drawing on the electric vehicle sector as a case study, we show how persistent investment bias, industrial scaling, and control over critical inputs have strengthened China’s position in global value chains despite limited progress in consumption-led rebalancing.
2. All this has led the Chinese government to increasingly embrace the notion of ‘hedged integration’ (Blanchette and Polk 2020), with the aim to interact with the global community on its own terms.
