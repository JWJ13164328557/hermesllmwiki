# Discovery and Optimization of MoEno1-Targeting Antifungal Compounds for Rice Blast Control.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of agricultural and food chemistry (2026)
- **PMID**: [42980443](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42980443/)
- **DOI**: [10.1021/acs.jafc.6c11543](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jafc.6c11543)
- **第一作者**: Wei Yu
- **作者**: Wei Yu, Chaoqi He, Fan Zhong, Bocong Cai, Bi Wang, Jiaxing Yan, Yaguang Zhang, Shu Xu

## 摘要
Developing fungicides with novel modes of action is essential for sustainable rice blast management. Here, we investigated the glycolytic enzyme MoEno1 in Magnaporthe oryzae as a potential antifungal target. Deletion of MoEno1 impaired vegetative growth, melanin production, conidiation, and pathogenicity. Deep-learning-assisted affinity prediction and structure-based virtual screening identified isobavachalcone (IBC) as a MoEno1-binding hit. Structure-guided optimization of IBC yielded 59 derivatives, among which II-24 showed enhanced antifungal activity, stronger MoEno1 binding, and greater inhibition of enolase activity. II-24 treatment was associated with reduced melanin accumulation and acetyl-CoA levels, together with perturbations in energy metabolism. II-24 also showed in planta efficacy, broad-spectrum antifungal activity, low phytotoxicity, low toxicity to zebrafish, predicted low mammalian toxicity, and enhanced activity in combination with tebuconazole. These findings support MoEno1 as a potential antifungal target and II-24 as an early stage lead for rice blast control.
