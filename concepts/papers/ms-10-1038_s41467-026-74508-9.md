### 核心发现
1. We found that the densities of both TEs and segmental duplications (SDs) were significantly enriched at inversion breakpoints compared to the internal regions of the inversions.
2. While deletions remained the dominant factor in altering gene expression in both tissues, we found that insertions exhibited a higher proportion of expression in leaf than stems (35% in leaf vs 20% in stem) (Supplementary Figure 13).
3. In contrast, we found little evidence to support TE activity as a primary driver of SV formation in these regions.
4. Collectively, our work provides comprehensive insights into the evolutionary landscape of SV and establishes a blueprint for SV informed genomic breeding in long-lived woody plants.
5. Collectively, these results highlight the potential role of adaptive introgression in shaping the SV landscape of P.
6. For example, SVs have been directly linked to complex trait variations like tomato flavor5, driven cold tolerance in tea plants6, and remarkably, major phenotypic divergences such as pod color through large genomic deletions7.
7. Notably, population structure analysis revealed distinct genetic background.
8. Notably, chromosome 17 exhibited the highest SV density, while chromosome 14 had the lowest.
