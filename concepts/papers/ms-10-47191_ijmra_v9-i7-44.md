### 核心发现
1. These results confirm that soaking seeds with germination stimulants plays a crucial role in overcoming physiological dormancy caused by the hard structure of Arabica coffee bean hulls, which are not very permeable to water.
2. The number of root hairs can serve as an indicator supporting seed viability and vigor, as the presence of root hairs indicates that the seed’s initial physiological processes are proceeding optimally and play a crucial role in the absorption of water and nutrients from the growing medium.
3. Both of these root development strategies are regulated by auxin, which plays a central role in regulating primary root meristem activity, root elongation, and lateral root formation (Roychoudhry and Kepinski, 2022).
4. Root hairs are extensions of epidermal cells that play a crucial role in water and nutrient uptake; the better development of root hairs in treatments B1 and B2 indicates that the seedlings in these two treatments are physiologically better prepared to absorb water and nutrients from the growing medium immediately after germination.
5. This is because Golden Grow and coconut water contain auxin hormones, which play a crucial role in enhancing cell division and differentiation in the embryo, thereby accelerating the growth and development of cotyledons.
