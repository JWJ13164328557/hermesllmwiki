### 核心发现
1. Taken together, these results reveal that the least area division-driven AC rotational self- similarity ensures the consecutive rotational divisions in 3D.
2. Furthermore, by incorporating biologically plausible cell growth mechanics, we show that the tetrahedral AC and its rotational division spontaneously emerge from a spherical cell.
