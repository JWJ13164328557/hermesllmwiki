### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that the commercial application of specific strains not only can illustrate an eco-friendly solution to induce resistance against fungal pathogens but also improve the quality properties of food plants with lower nitrate content.
2. To our knowledge, this is the first report about the induction of UDP by Streptomyces strains in tomato plants probably to detoxify the fungal pathogen.
3. Collectively, developing specific PGP-based synthetic prod­ ucts to promote and protect fresh vegetables such as tomato plants can lead to more positive results on quality, nitrate content, biosafety, and resistance against fungal diseases.
4. Here, we show that these strains can protect tomato plants from the invasion of Alternaria by eliciting the plant’s immune system and nitrate reduction that confirming the potential of these strains to manage two important diseases of tomato plants.
