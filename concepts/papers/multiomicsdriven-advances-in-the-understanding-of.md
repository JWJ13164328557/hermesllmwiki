# Multi‐omics‐driven advances in the understanding of triacylglycerol biosynthesis in oil seeds

## 物种
Plant (unspecified)

## 摘要
SUMMARYVegetable oils are rich sources of polyunsaturated fatty acids and energy as well as valuable sources of human food, animal feed, and bioenergy. Triacylglycerols, which are comprised of three fatty acids attached to a glycerol backbone, are the main component of vegetable oils. Here, we review the development and application of multiple‐level omics in major oilseeds and emphasize the progress in the analysis of the biological roles of key genes underlying seed oil content and quality in major oilseeds. Finally, we discuss future research directions in functional genomics research based on current omics and oil metabolic engineering strategies that aim to enhance seed oil content and quality, and specific fatty acids components according to either human health needs or industrial requirements.

### 核心发现
1. Interestingly, Arabidopsis hmACCase can be targeted to plastids in rapeseed, and OA and the SOC were signiﬁcantly increased by At-hmACCase overexpression (Roesler et al., 1997), implying the functional heterogeneity of hmACCase due to alterations in target subcellular organelles.
2. Subsequently, we emphasize the progress made in the biological roles of key genes involved in FA and TAG biosynthesis in major oilseeds, thereby providing targets for genetically engineered oil plants (Figure 1).
3. In cotton, overexpression of GhBCCP1, GhBC1, or GhCTb resulted in increased CSO content by 17% to 22%, whereas overexpression of GhCTa2 in transgenic cotton seeds showed no signiﬁcant change in oil content (Cui et al., 2017).
4. In addition, htACCase interacts with many protein modulators to regulate its activity (Figure 3).
5. Overexpression of ZmSAD1 in transgenic maize resulted in a reduced SA content by 1.57% and a lower SFA-to-USFA ratio by 20.40% (Du et al., 2016).
6. Homozygous mutations in single and/or double allele(s) of GmFAD2-1A and GmFAD2-1B in soybean, which are created by transcription activator-like effector nucleases (TALENs) that target induced local lesions in genomes (TILLING), RNAi, and CRISPR/Cas9, resulted in substantial increases in OA and decreases in LA and LAN (Do et al., 2019; Knoll et al., 2011; Wen et al., 2018; Yang, Xing, et al., 2018)
7. In cotton, knockout of GhFAD2 genes using the same approach increased the OA level to 77.7%, with concomitant decreases in LA (from 58.6% to 6.9%) and PA (from 23.95% to 13.18%) (Chen et al., 2021).
8. Overexpression of the GmDGAT1 variant, which was created by amino acid substitutions, increased the SOC in soybean by 16%.
