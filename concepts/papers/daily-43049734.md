# Dimethyl Trisulfide Exhibits Toxicity against Diverse Phytopathogens and Reduces Fusarium Wilt Severity in Banana Plants.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant disease (2026)
- **PMID**: [43049734](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43049734/)
- **DOI**: [10.1094/PDIS-07-26-1430-RE](https://doi.org/10.1094/PDIS-07-26-1430-RE)
- **第一作者**: Jinkang Song
- **作者**: Jinkang Song, SuiPing Huang, Ruyun Qin, Qili Li, Xiaolin Chen, Yu Zhang, Lihua Tang

## 摘要
Fusarium wilt, caused by Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. cubense, poses a critical threat to global banana production. The overuse of synthetic fungicides has raised environmental and resistance concerns. In this study, we evaluated dimethyl trisulfide (DMTS), a natural volatile organic compound, for its antifungal activity and its potential to reduce Fusarium wilt severity under controlled greenhouse conditions. In vitro assays against 11 fungal pathogens isolated from seven plant hosts revealed concentration‑dependent inhibition, with half‑maximal effective concentration (EC₅₀) values ranging from 0.34 to 100.37 μL/L, indicating marked pathogen‑specific susceptibility. In greenhouse trials, soil fumigation with DMTS at 80 μL/L (a non‑phytotoxic dose) significantly suppressed Fusarium wilt. Compared with the untreated control, this treatment resulted in a 94.26% reduction in wilt index and an 80.81% reduction in vascular discoloration index. These findings demonstrate that DMTS is a promising eco‑friendly alternative for targeted disease control, while underscoring the importance of adjusting the dose according to the susceptibility profile of the target pathogen to ensure both high efficacy and plant safety in sustainable agricultural systems.
