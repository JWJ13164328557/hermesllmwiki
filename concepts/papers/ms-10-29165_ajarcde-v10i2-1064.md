### 核心发现
1. Among these regulators, gibberellic acid (GA₃) plays a central role in promoting early fruit initiation by relieving DELLA-mediated repression of cell division and expansion pathways [20][21].
2. However, GA often acts downstream of auxin and interacts with cytokinin, abscisic acid (ABA), and ethylene pathways, indicating that its effectiveness is contingent upon broader hormonal context and species-specific regulation [16][25][22].
3. Synthesis and Research Gap Collectively, prior studies establish that parthenocarpy in cucumber is hormonally regulated, genetically modulated, and strongly influenced by GA₃ concentration and developmental timing.
4. This result indicates that GA₃ application at 300 ppm during anthesis significantly enhances fruit biomass accumulation.
5. These findings confirm the critical role of GA₃ in improving yield-related traits in cucumber.
6. Fertilization or exogenous auxin/GA application triggers large-scale transcriptional reprogramming in maternal ovary tissues, activating genes related to the cell cycle, cell wall modification, and sink strength, which collectively are sufficient to initiate fruit growth without zygote formation [17][18][19].
7. Therefore, factorial evaluation of dose and timing is essential to disentangle additive and interactive effects on parthenocarpy outcomes.
8. Therefore, GA₃ application is crucial for achieving desirable fruit size and uniformity.
