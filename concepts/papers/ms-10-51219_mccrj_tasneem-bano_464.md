7. The primary challenge is the shared expression of target antigens (CD7, CD25, CCR4, CD30) between malignant ATL cells and normal T cells, which risks fratricidal killing of the CAR-T product during manufacturing and T-cell aplasia in vivo.
8. Strategies to overcome fratricide include: (i) CRISPR/Cas9-mediated knockout of target antigens in CAR-T cells before engineering with anti-target CARs; (ii) use of allogeneic (donor-derived) CAR-T cells from HTLV-1- negative donors; and (iii) NK cell-based CARs that do not share the T-cell surface markers of the malignancy71.
