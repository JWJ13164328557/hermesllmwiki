### 核心发现
1. This study establishes an optimized protocol for indirect shoot organogenesis from leaf lamina explants through callus culture, followed by shoot induction, rooting, and acclimatization.
2. Therefore, this study provides a direct comparison between a com­ post-based photoautotrophic rooting system and conventional agar-solidified medium.
3. Discover Plants (2026) 3:227 5 Conclusion This study demonstrates that indirect organogenesis in Ficus lyrata can be effectively achieved through controlled auxin-cytokinin interactions, with callus morphology serv­ ing as practical indicator of regenerative competence.
4. Importantly, BAP has also been extensively adopted in commercial-scale micropropagation, where its strong shoot- inductive effect, cost-effectiveness, and ease of use make it the preferred regulator for managing the callus-to-shoot transition.
5. Importantly, bypassing the agar rooting stage reduced the overall production cycle by approximately four weeks under the conditions tested, a practical advantage aligning with Kozai’s argument that photoautotroph system reduces labor intensity and acclimatization bottlenecks.
6. A very recent study on pistachio rootstocks (Pistacia vera ‘Qazvini’ and ‘UCB1’) further supports our findings by showing that ventilation without additional CO₂ enrichment is sufficient to enhance in vitro performance.
