# RhWUS1 integrates antagonistic phytohormonal cues to regulate axillary bud outgrowth in rose (Rosa hybrida).

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43072416](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072416/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag797](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag797)
- **第一作者**: Qi Fu
- **作者**: Qi Fu, Feifei Gong, Xue Bai, Dan Wang, Yue Yang, Yangchao Jia, Lihua Wang, Huichun Wang

## 摘要
Axillary bud outgrowth is a major determinant of plant architecture, yet the molecular basis by which antagonistic hormonal signals are integrated remains poorly resolved. Here, we identify the rose WUSCHEL-related homeobox transcription factor RhWUS1 as a key regulator of the transition from bud dormancy to activation. Functional analyses using transient overexpression and virus-induced gene silencing demonstrate that RhWUS1 is required for axillary bud outgrowth. Transcriptomic and biochemical evidence further show that RhWUS1 acts as a transcriptional repressor by directly binding the promoter of RhNCED3, a rate-limiting gene in abscisic acid (ABA) biosynthesis. Repression of RhNCED3 by RhWUS1 reduces local ABA accumulation in axillary buds (Abs), thereby promoting bud outgrowth. To define upstream regulatory network of RhWUS1, yeast one-hybrid screening identified two transcription factors from antagonistic hormonal pathways: the cytokinin response regulator RhRR11 and the auxin response factor RhARF3. Subsequent Y1H, chromatin immunoprecipitation qPCR (ChIP-qPCR) and dual-luciferase reporter (DLR) assays confirmed that RhRR11 directly activates RhWUS1 transcription, whereas RhARF3 directly represses its promoter activity. Together, these findings reveal that RhWUS1 integrates cytokinin and auxin signals to fine-tune local ABA biosynthesis in axillary buds, providing a molecular framework for improving budbreak capacity in rose.
