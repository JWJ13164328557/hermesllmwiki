### 核心发现
1. We found that ~90% (6901 peaks) of pwr hyperacetylated peaks over- lapped with those increased peaks in hda9 (Figure 2C), suggesting that PWR and HDA9 target at similar genomic regions.
2. We found that the enrichment of HDA9 at WRKY57, APG9, NPX1, and four other randomly selected loci was substantially decreased in pwr (Figure 6D, Figure 6—figure supplement 1C), suggesting that HDA9 binding to these targets requires PWR.
3. Surprisingly, we found that HDA9 is preferentially enriched in the pro- moters of active genes but not silent genes (Figure 3C).
4. Together, these results suggest that PWR binds to the same genomic regions as HDA9 at HDA9 targets.
5. Consistent with the delayed leaf senescence phenotype in hda9, we found the downregulation of hallmark SAGs including SENES- CENCE4 (SEN4), SAG12, and SAG113 in hda9 (Figure 5A).

7. We found that HDA9 acts in complex with a SANT domain-containing protein POWERDRESS (PWR) and transcription factor WRKY53.
8. Furthermore, we found that PWR is important for HDA9 nuclear accumulation.
