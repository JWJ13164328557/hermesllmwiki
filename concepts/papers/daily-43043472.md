# Comprehensive analysis of the soybean GPAT gene family and functional characterization of GmGPAT3C.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Planta (2026)
- **PMID**: [43043472](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43043472/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s00425-026-05220-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00425-026-05220-7)
- **第一作者**: Bo Hu
- **作者**: Bo Hu, Min Yu, Lijuan Ma, Jiufeng Zhao, Yongqin Hou, Jun Zhong, Yuqi Wang, Yanming Zhou

## 摘要
Genome-wide identification of the soybean GPAT family, elite haplotypes of GmGPAT9D and GmGPAT3C associated with oil content, and demonstration that GmGPAT3C overexpression increases oil content and modulates the fatty acid profile, consistent with a proposed role in the Kennedy pathway. Soybean is a crucial source of edible oil and protein. Glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase (GPAT) plays a significant role in seed oil biosynthesis; however, research on the soybean GPAT family remains limited. In this study, we identified 26 GPAT members in soybean, which were classified into two phylogenetic groups. All members contain a conserved PlsC acyltransferase domain and exhibit tissue-specific expression. Haplotype analysis of GmGPAT9D and GmGPAT3C, both of which are highly expressed during seed oil accumulation, revealed that GmGPAT3C possesses four polymorphic sites forming three haplotypes, with Hap1 identified as the predominant high-oil haplotype. For GmGPAT9D, a promoter single-nucleotide polymorphism delineates two haplotypes with Pro1 associated with higher oil content and predominant across both wild and cultivated soybean. Overexpression of GmGPAT3C significantly increased seed oil and linoleic acid content while decreasing protein content. GmGPAT3C was highly expressed during seed development, and its protein was localized to the endoplasmic reticulum. This study systematically characterizes the soybean GPAT family and reveals the function of GmGPAT3C, providing a valuable target for high-oil breeding.
