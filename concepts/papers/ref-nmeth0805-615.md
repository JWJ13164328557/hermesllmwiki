### 核心发现
1. Genomics methods have tremendous potential to infer developmental circuits and, in combination with genetic tools, to discover the unknown downstream targets of known developmental regulators.
2. Note the dense cluster of target protoplasts, which are delimited by the sorting gate, emitting more intensely in green than red wavelengths.
3. These are the GFP-positive targets of sorting.
4. Typical gates set in forward versus side scatter plots to eliminate dead animal cells or debris may exclude some target protoplasts in plants.
5. Empirically determine a higher forward scatter threshold that eliminates debris, but not potential target protoplasts.
