### 核心发现
1. This study shows that patients with type 2 diabetes can be classified into meaningful groups with different progression patterns.
2. Notably, 18.6% of patients did not purchase any diabetes medication dur- ing the follow-up period.
3. Interestingly, the percentages of patients using any of these medications were lower in C1 (best progno- sis) and C5 (worst prognosis), than in C2–C4 (Figure S5).
