### 核心发现
1. In conclusion, DHA:ARA ratios of 1:1 and 1:4 significantly improve abnormal glucose and lipid metabolism and hepatic metabolites caused by GDM.
2. Notably, HFD groups exhibited body ARTICLE IN PRESS ARTICLE IN PRESS weight reductions from the 3rd week onward, which were consistent with the symptom of weight loss in diabetes.
3. Notably, DHA:ARA = 1:1 demonstrated lowest LDL- C level, statistically indistinguishable from control group.
4. Notably, DHA, DHA:ARA = 4:1, and metformin groups exhibited comparable restorative effects, achieving near-normalization of hepatic histology.
