# Root transcriptional dynamics reveal root tissues influenced by developmental timing and genotype in Phaseolus vulgaris.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: The plant genome (2026)
- **PMID**: [43086585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43086585/)
- **DOI**: [10.1002/tpg2.70344](https://doi.org/10.1002/tpg2.70344)
- **第一作者**: Viviana Ortiz Londoño
- **作者**: Viviana Ortiz Londoño, Karen A Cichy, C Robin Buell, Miranda J Haus

## 摘要
Phaseolus vulgaris is a staple crop arising from two independent domestication events. The resulting gene pools exhibit distinct resilience to biotic and abiotic stress, which may be linked to differences in root development and function. Many resilience-associated traits are mediated by specialized root tissues, yet the transcriptomic programs underlying their functional specialization and association with stress response remain poorly understood. We present a comprehensive root transcriptome atlas, generated by profiling eight root tissues in two representative genotypes. Transcriptomic variation was primarily driven by tissue identity, with gene expression patterns varying across developmental stages and genotypes. Gene expression clustering of adjacent tissues suggests coordinated transcriptional programs, with transcription factors from the AP2/ERF (APETALA2/ethylene-responsive factor transcription factor family), and WRKY (WRKY transcription factor family) families among those associated with tissue-specific gene expression networks. Developmental transitions from protein synthesis to transcriptional regulation were marked by conserved spatial gene expression and tissue-specific regulatory modules, highlighting coordinated root maturation across species. Genes differentially expressed between genotypes were enriched for biotic-response functions. A subset of genes encoding nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat (NB-LRRs) proteins, peroxidases, and cytochrome P450-encoding genes showed higher expression in each genotype. Stampede showed the strongest enrichment of ADP-binding NB-LRR genes, whereas Red Hawk showed comparatively higher expression of peroxidase and oxidative-stress-related genes. Our findings provide insight into the transcriptional programs shaping root function in P. vulgaris and highlight tissue-, stage-, and genotype-associated variation, establishing a reference atlas to support legume root biology and breeding.

Plant roots are critical interfaces for sensing and responding to edaphic stress and pathogens, yet how responses are coordinated across specialized root tissues remains poorly understood, particularly in Phaseolus vulgaris, a globally important legume crop. By profiling gene expression across eight distinct root tissues in two genotypes with distinct root architecture, we uncovered developmental and genotype‐specific transcriptional programs that shape root tissue specialization and stress responses. This high‐resolution root transcriptome atlas provides a foundational resource for understanding early root biology and accelerating breeding for resilient legume crops.
