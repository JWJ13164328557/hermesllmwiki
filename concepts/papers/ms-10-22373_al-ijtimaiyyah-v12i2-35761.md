### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that humanitarian da'wah on Instagram functions not only as a medium for disseminating Islamic values but also as an effective instrument of public communication capable of building trust, strengthening social solidarity, and enhancing public participation in fundraising for victims of the Aceh Tamiang flash floods.
2. Importantly, every fundraising appeal was accompanied by visual documentation of aid implementation and program outcomes, demonstrating transparency and accountability.
3. Therefore, continuous innovation in message diversification is necessary to ensure that humanitarian da'wah can reach broader audiences and sustain public engagement over the long term.
