### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these findings support the transition of Sargassum from a coastal nuisance to a viable/valuable agricultural input, positioning biostimulants within an emerging framework of seaweed-based valorization strategies.
2. Collectively, the study aimed to provide an integrated evaluation of the agronomic, pathological, and molecular effects of a Sar­ gassum-derived biostimulant in a tropical agricultural context.
3. However, an efficient and system­ atic Sargassum network is necessary to ensure that the seaweed biomass is transformed into a marketable product.
4. Importantly, work on liquid Sargassum stimulants promotes its use in agriculture by demonstrating increased soil macronutrient availability to the plant and no detectable short-term accumulation of metals of concern (As, Cd, Pb, Ni, Cr) in the soil–plant-fruit system [48].
5. Therefore, with any plan to valorise Sargassum, careful testing and pro­ cessing are necessary to ensure that these harmful elements are not introduced into the food chain which would lead to significant harm to the environment and human health.
6. Notably, rotational application of OJA1 with conventional fungicides produced the high­ est yields and lowest disease levels, highlighting its value within integrated crop manage­ ment systems.
7. Collection complied with applicable local environmental and coastal management guidelines, and no specific permits were required for the removal of the stranded Sargassum biomass.
