# A mild SCMV isolate-based vector and an enhanced agroinfiltration-mediated viral multiplication system for efficient gene function studies in maize.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43056223](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43056223/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag873](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag873)
- **第一作者**: Dezhi Peng
- **作者**: Dezhi Peng, Wenqi Jing, Liyang Xie, Zhengxing Song, Yan Zhang, Hao Wang, Fei Jiang, Caifu Jiang

## 摘要
Functional genomics in maize (Zea mays) and sorghum (Sorghum bicolor) is constrained by the low efficiency and high cost of stable transformation, as well as the lack of robust and user-friendly transient expression tools. Virus-based overexpression (VOX) vectors offer a promising alternative; however, currently available viral vectors for these crops are limited by complex inoculation methods and the severe symptoms often caused by the viral infection. Here, we report a newly identified, mild sugarcane mosaic virus isolate, SCmDu, and its development into an efficient, agroinoculation-based VOX vector. By co-expressing the optimized tomato chlorosis virus-encoded silencing suppressor P22 (TP22) and the pro-viral maize eukaryotic initiation factor 4A-III (ZmEIF4A3) in Nicotiana benthamiana leaves, we achieved high viral titers, enabling simple mechanical transmission to maize seedlings. The resulting pSCmDu vector supports strong, systemic protein expression for over four weeks across diverse maize inbred lines, accommodates large protein fusions, and is also functional in sorghum. Its utility was further demonstrated by triggering a hypersensitive response with a fungal effector and by identifying maize gibberellin 2-oxidases 6 (ZmGA2ox6) as a regulator of plant height. Overall, this study establishes and validates a user-friendly, efficient VOX platform for maize and sorghum, thereby paving the way for accelerated functional genomics and trait discovery in these key gramineous crops.
