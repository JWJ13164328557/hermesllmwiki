# Purine Synthesis Is Required for Pokkaliibacter plantistimulans to Interact Effectively with Pokkali Rice Under Brackish Conditions.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [43063376](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43063376/)
- **DOI**: [10.1094/MPMI-07-26-0076-R](https://doi.org/10.1094/MPMI-07-26-0076-R)
- **第一作者**: Sanoj Mohan B

## 摘要
Limited knowledge exists regarding beneficial plant-microbe interactions in brackish environments. Here, we demonstrate that the marine taxon Pokkaliibacter plantistimulans L1E11T promotes plant growth, mitigates salt stress, and modulates multiple defense- and abiotic stress-responsive genes in Pokkali rice under brackish conditions. Notably, L1E11T exhibits more robust root colonization under brackish conditions than under zero-brackish conditions. To identify novel genetic determinants underlying this mutualistic interaction, we screened a transposon mutant library of L1E11T for defects in root colonization under brackish conditions. This screen identified a mutant, pp-Tn5-22, carrying an insertion in purN, which encodes glycinamide ribonucleotide transformylase N, a key enzyme in the third step of bacterial de novo purine biosynthesis. Disruption of purN affects the expression of purM and purN and causes a previously unreported purine-thiamine auxotrophy. In vitro transcriptomic analysis of pp-Tn5-22 revealed significant downregulation of several genes associated with purine synthesis, energy production, oxidative stress responses, and other central metabolic pathways. purN disruption also severely impairs several in vitro root-colonization traits, host colonization, and plant growth-promoting ability under brackish conditions. Consistent with these defects, pp-Tn5-22 failed to induce host defense- and stress-responsive genes or modulate key root metabolites essential for stable bacterial colonization and improved host salt tolerance. In planta bacterial transcriptomics further revealed that purine synthesis is essential for L1E11T active proliferation during early root colonization. Overall, these findings establish purN-mediated purine biosynthesis as a key metabolic determinant of the mutualistic interaction between L1E11T and Pokkali rice under brackish conditions.
