### 核心发现
1. Taken together, GSE130970 provided the strongest concordant evidence for the female-higher LEPR pat­ tern, weaker same-direction evidence for P4HA1 and PNPLA3, and no corresponding sex-associated signal for BCL2L11.
2. Taken together, GSE167523 provided complementary evidence for sex-dependent PNPLA3 behavior at the cat­ egorical disease-stage level, whereas LEPR was associ­ ated with Diagnosis without detectable sex dependence.
3. Notably, PNPLA3 remained FDR-supported only in males, con­ sistent with the regression analyses showing stronger coupling between PNPLA3 expression and histological disease activity in male liver tissue.
