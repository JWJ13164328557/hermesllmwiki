# Effects of different shaping processes on the quality, volatile and non-volatile metabolites of Xinyang Maojian green tea.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food chemistry: X (2026)
- **PMID**: [42997838](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42997838/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.fochx.2026.104722](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fochx.2026.104722)
- **第一作者**: Jinlei Luo
- **作者**: Jinlei Luo, Yi Chen, Lingzhi Chen, Peng Yin, Guiyi Guo, Zhihui Wang

## 摘要
Shaping processes critically influence green tea sensory quality. This study examined four shaping methods - No Shaping (NS), Chao Tiao (CT), Pai Ba (PB), and Li Tiao (LT) - on Xinyang Maojian green tea using sensory evaluation, physicochemical assays, GC-MS, and LC-MS. CT induced the most profound reconfiguration: it enhanced chestnut/beany aromas and umami while reducing bitterness/astringency. CT had the highest water extract and free amino acids, with ester-type catechins elevated by approximately 15% relative to NS. GC-MS showed CT up-regulated heterocyclic compounds (e.g., pyrazoles) and down-regulated terpenoids. LC-MS revealed CT elevated umami-enhancing di/tri-peptides and reduced astringent phenolic compounds (e.g., the phenolic acid caffeic acid). LT broadly accumulated small peptides/phenolic glycosides, supporting fuller mouthfeel and sweet aftertaste. PB and NS showed limited changes. These results demonstrate CT as a precise regulator linking physical disruption to metabolic reprogramming for desirable chestnut/beany characteristics, providing a basis for targeted green tea quality improvement.
