# Genomic characterization of a novel WVMV isolate and development of a sensitive, field-deployable RT-RPA-CRISPR/Cas12a lateral flow detection assay.

- **PMID**: [43071937](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43071937/)
- **DOI**: [10.1094/PDIS-09-26-1855-RE](https://doi.org/10.1094/PDIS-09-26-1855-RE)

- **作者**: Yao Luo, Tingyu Hou, Mingyu Yuan, Jingwen Cai, Tengfeng Peng, Ni Ma, Lei Lei, Meng-Ao Jia

## 摘要
Wisteria vein mosaic virus (WVMV), a potyviral pathogen, severely compromises the ornamental quality of wisteria plants, yet conventional diagnostic tools fail to support rapid on-site screening under field conditions. Herein, we characterized a novel WVMV isolate designated WVMV-GZWf, recovered from symptomatic wisteria foliage in Guizhou Province, China. Using its full genomic sequence as a reference, we developed a visual molecular diagnostic assay integrating reverse transcription recombinase polymerase amplification (RT-RPA) and CRISPR/Cas12a collateral cleavage. Single-variable gradient assays were performed to optimize core reaction components, yielding optimal concentrations of 10 nM crRNA, 12.5 nM LbCas12a and 400 nM ssDNA-FQ reporter. The established assay achieved a limit of detection (LOD) of 1.55 copies μL⁻¹, corresponding to a 105-fold higher sensitivity than standard RT-PCR. No cross-reactivity was detected against five prevalent plant viruses: potato virus Y, tobacco vein banding mosaic virus, chilli veinal mottle virus, sugarcane mosaic virus and cucumber mosaic virus. The complete workflow takes less than 1 h, with readouts obtainable via portable UV transilluminators or disposable lateral flow dipsticks (LFDs). Testing of field-collected wisteria leaf samples generated diagnostic results fully concordant with RT-PCR. Overall, our RT-RPA-CRISPR/Cas12a-LFD platform enables sensitive, specific and operationally straightforward point-of-care detection of WVMV for field surveillance.
