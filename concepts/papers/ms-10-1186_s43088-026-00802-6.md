### 核心发现
1. Primers were prepared per the manufacturer’s instructions, and the housekeeping gene (GAPDH) served as a normalizer for calculating relative gene expression or fold change in the target gene.
2. Therefore, the critical threshold (Ct) of the target gene was normalized with quantities (Ct) of GAPDH using the 2⁻∆∆Ct method of Zhang et al.
3. Notably, AJLE- CuO-NPs exhibited superior performance compared to ZSCLE-CuO-NPs.
4. Notably, nanoparticle-enhanced formulations (G6, G9) alongside diclazuril (G3) achieved minimal pathology (score = 1), while AJLE monotherapy demonstrated mod­ erate amelioration (score = 2).
