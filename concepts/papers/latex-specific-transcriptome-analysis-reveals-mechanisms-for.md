### 核心发现
1. We found at least one or two genes of each family in the MVA pathway being highly expressed in latex.
2. Among multi-subunit RING finger-type E3, the anaphase promoting complex (APC complex) presented the most apparent tissue specificity; its subunits APC13 with the RING finger domain and cell division cycle 20 (CDC20) with the target recognizing sub-unit were expressed in the latex.
3. There were also another two latex-specific com­ plexes, Skp-cullin-F-box protein (SCF) and cullin 3 (Cul3), that shared the RING box protein 1 (RBX1) in many transcripts, but had different target recognizing proteins F-BOX and DDB1- and CUL4-associated factor (DCAF) with latex-specific expression.
4. Taken together, those results indicated that the JA signaling pathway was more active in the laticifer than the ET signaling pathway.
5. As a recognition signal for proteasomal degradation, a ubiquitin chain is covalently tagged to the target protein, the processing of which requires multi-step catalytic reactions mediated by three types of en­ zymes.
6. The ubiquitin ligase (E3) interacts with the target protein and adds ubiquitin molecules derived from the ubiquitin-activating enzyme (E1) and the ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme (E2) (Schwartz and Cie­ chanover, 2009).
7. Research has shown E3 to be the key enzyme for specific ubiquitination for proteasomal degradation, by direct binding with the target protein.
8. Taken together, these results indicate that the ubiquitination is involved in latex-specific metabolism although no systematic research has been conducted so far.
