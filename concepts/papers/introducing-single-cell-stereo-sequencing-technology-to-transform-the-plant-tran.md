### 核心发现
1. We conclude with an outlook of how such integra- tion will enhance crop improvement.
2. Thus, tools for systematic research are required to unravel how distinct cell types are regulated or communicate to form strong functional networks in plant tissues.
3. Importantly, the authors showed that Stereo-seq is a powerful tool that incorporates single cell location and transcriptome information for understanding plant biological processes.
4. In addition, further advancements in sectioning are required to generate high-quality RNA content in case of increased cell number.
5. Nevertheless, despite these limitations, technical improvements in Stereo-seq are expected to provide new insights into tissue composition and function by com- bining single cell transcriptomics with chromatin accessibility in the same biological sample.
