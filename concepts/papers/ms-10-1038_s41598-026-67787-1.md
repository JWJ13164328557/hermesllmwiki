### 核心发现
1. Methods: We applied a drug-target Mendelian randomization (MR) framework to evaluate genetically proxied lower HDAC11 expression.
2. DM and its associated complications ultimately result in damage to target organs, significantly reducing patients' quality of life and often leading to a loss of working capacity.
3. Although the prevention and treatment of DM and its complications are constantly being revised, there remains a need to explore new therapeutic targets for DM and its complications.
4. This phenotype-informed prioritization followed a previous drug-target MR framework(18) and selected HDAC11 expression variants with glycaemic relevance to the hypothesized intervention.
5. The primary threshold of r² < 0.8 was selected with reference to a previous drug-target MR framework(18), removing variants in very high LD while retaining multiple variants within the HDAC11 cis region.
6. In this study, we used a drug-target MR framework to evaluate associations between genetically proxied lower HDAC11 expression and diabetes-related outcomes, and integrated mediation, colocalization, PPI, enrichment, and molecular docking analyses to explore possible biological links.
7. Drug-target MR can help prioritize potential therapeutic targets by using genetic variants as proxies for target modulation.
8. Docking evaluates geometric and energetic compatibility, whereas direct target engagement was not measured in this study.
