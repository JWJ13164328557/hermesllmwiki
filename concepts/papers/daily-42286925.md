## 摘要
Although herbicides are among the most effective tools for weed control in rice cultivation, they often cause phytotoxicity. Herbicide safeners can mitigate such damage; however, commercial safeners may have adverse effects on non-target organisms. Natural safeners such as gibberellin A3 (GA3) are considered safer alternatives, but new strategies are needed to improve the utilization efficiency of GA3 because its poor solubility and limited potency restrict its practical application. Here, taking advantage of the self-assembly capacity of a hydrophilic-lipophilic diblock polymer (HLDP) to formulate poorly soluble natural products, GA3 was assembled with HLDP to generate GA3@HLDP. The results demonstrated that GA3@HLDP was successfully constructed through hydrogen bonding and hydrophobic association. Its nanoscale particle size significantly improved permeability and safening activity, thereby alleviating pendimethalin (PND)-induced phytotoxicity in rice seedlings without affecting the herbicidal activity of PND. Compared with GA3, GA3@HLDP more effectively activated phenylpropanoid biosynthesis, glutathione metabolism, and starch/sucrose metabolism pathways, while also restoring the activities of multiple antioxidant enzymes. Furthermore, GA3@HLDP displayed favorable biosafety in various model organisms, and its application had negligible effects on soil microorganisms. This work shows that HLDP can comprehensively improve the performance of GA3 through multifaceted synergistic mechanisms, providing a feasible strategy to bridge the gap between the theoretical development and practical application of natural safeners.
