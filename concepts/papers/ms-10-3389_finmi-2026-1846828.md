### 核心发现
1. Phytate, tannin, and oxalate are considered antinutritional factors because they bind to minerals and proteins, leading to decreased protein digestibility and impaired mineral bioavailability (Gautier et al., 2022; Liang et al., 2025; Seol and Yeo, 2026.
2. Understanding these parameters is essential for supporting its development at both small-scale and industrial levels.
3. Calcium supports bone development and cellular functions in children, iron is required for hemoglobin synthesis and anemia prevention, while zinc is vital for immune function, enzyme activity, and growth (Uche et al., 2025; Yusuf et al., 2025).
4. For example, thiamine in its active form, (thiamine pyrophosphate), is essential for the decarboxylation of pyruvate to acetyl-CoA during carbohydrate metabolism for energy generation in fermenting microorganisms.
5. Riboﬂavin and niacin serve as precursors of redox cofactors (i.e., FAD/FMN and NAD+/NADP+), which are essential for energy metabolism, while vitamin C contributes to antioxidant defense by scavenging reactive oxygen species and regenerating other antioxidants such as a-tocopherol.
