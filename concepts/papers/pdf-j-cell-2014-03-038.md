### 核心发现
1. Strikingly, GroEL/ES accelerates DapA folding more than 30-fold, with the conﬁning environment of the chaper- onin cage lowering the entropic component of the activation barrier by promoting segmental structure formation in the TIM barrel.
2. Strikingly, GroEL/ES accelerated the folding reaction 30-fold to a rate of 6.0 min1 (t1/2 7 s) at 25C and 16-fold to a rate of 1.5 min1 (t1/2 28 s) at 10C, with 100% yield (Figure 1D).
3. Notably, the Hsp70 chaperone system (DnaK/DnaJ/GrpE) allowed only very slow refolding (t1/2 > 1 hr), even at 37C, but efﬁciently maintained DapA in a nonaggre- gated state for transfer to GroEL/ES and completion of folding within seconds (Figure S1A available online).
4. Importantly, GroEL/ES accelerated the folding of DapA-293C-Alexa (100 pM) more than 50-fold at 20C (Figure 2C).
5. Remarkably, MsNanA renatured spon- taneously with a half-time similar to that measured for the assis- ted refolding of EcNanA (Figure 3A).
6. Interestingly, MsNanA contains more than twice the number of lysines (34) as the chap- eronin-dependent EcNanA and DapA proteins (16 and 13, respectively) and less than half the number of arginines (5 compared to 12 and 13, respectively).
7. Strikingly, GroEL/ES accelerated the rate at which P1–8 acquired protection by at least 50-fold (t1/2 12 s, the duration of the D2O pulse) compared to spontaneous folding (t1/2 9 min) (Figures 5A and 5C).
8. Importantly, 50- to 130- fold accelerated folding (at 25C–37C) was measured by FCS at a very low concentration of DapA (100 pM), conditions excluding intermolecular association (aggregation or assembly) as determined by dual-color FCCS.
