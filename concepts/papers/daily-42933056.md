# Genomic resequencing of low nicotine tobacco germplasm identifies novel allelic variations contributing to reduced alkaloid accumulation in tobacco.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Biochemical and biophysical research communications (2026)
- **PMID**: [42933056](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42933056/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.bbrc.2026.154797](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2026.154797)
- **第一作者**: Cheng Yuan
- **作者**: Cheng Yuan, Zhijun Tong, Xuejun Chen, Yihan Zhang, Yingtong Di, Yuehu Wang, Ruixue Wang, Xueyi Sui

## 摘要
In response to proposed regulations on non-addictive nicotine levels in tobacco products, researchers are screening tobacco germplasm for natural alleles associated with reduced alkaloid accumulation. Here, we re-evaluated total alkaloid contents in 15 low-nicotine accessions and identified associated allelic variations through whole-genome resequencing. Two novel nic1 alleles, nic1-5 and nic1-6, were identified and associated with suppressed nicotine biosynthetic gene expression and reduced alkaloid accumulation. Furthermore, we also identified a single-nucleotide deletion in NtMYC2a and a C-to-A substitution in NtNUP2, both causing frameshifts and resulting in premature termination codons. The NtMYC2a mutant was associated with dramatically reduced alkaloid levels in low-nicotine accessions, whereas CRISPR/Cas9-mediated knockout of NtNUP2 did not significantly reduce nicotine content in the edited lines. These findings expand our known nic1 allelic diversity and facilitate allele pyramiding as a strategy for breeding ultra-low-nicotine tobacco cultivars.
