# On-Column RNA Preservation Enables Cold-Chain-Free Virome Analysis of Chili Peppers.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant disease (2026)
- **PMID**: [43059585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43059585/)
- **DOI**: [10.1094/PDIS-07-26-1437-RE](https://doi.org/10.1094/PDIS-07-26-1437-RE)
- **第一作者**: Tomoyuki Nabeshima
- **作者**: Tomoyuki Nabeshima, Ryota Aramaki, Sota Yamamoto, Elly Kesumawati, Sabaruddin Zakaria, Sota Koeda

## 摘要
High-throughput sequencing (HTS) has become an essential tool for plant virus surveillance and discovery, but its application in field settings is often limited by the need to preserve high-quality RNA under cold-chain conditions. This challenge is particularly acute for chili pepper (Capsicum spp.), which has strong endogenous RNA-degrading activities. To address this limitation, we developed an on-column RNA-preservation workflow that enables cold-chain-free transport of extracted RNA for virome analysis. Laboratory preservation tests showed that RNA extracted using conventional methods degraded rapidly during ambient-temperature storage, whereas RNA that was retained and dehydrated directly on a silica column remained stable. The workflow was validated using RNA extracted from symptomatic chili pepper samples collected in Indonesia. RNA remained highly intact after extraction in a laboratory and subsequent 3-day transport at ambient temperature. Using HTS of the preserved RNA, we identified a diverse virome, including near-complete genome sequences of Capsicum annuum amalgavirus 1, pepper cryptic virus 2, and chili veinal mottle virus as well as begomovirus- and polerovirus-related sequences. These detection results agreed with RT-PCR assays for individual RNA viruses and PCR assays for DNA viruses. These findings demonstrate that on-column RNA preservation enables reliable, cold-chain-free transport of extracted RNA for virome analysis and provides a practical framework for plant virus surveillance and molecular diagnostics in regions where cold-chain infrastructure is limited.
