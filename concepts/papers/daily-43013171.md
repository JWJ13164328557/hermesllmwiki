# Genome wide identification, characterization and differential expression profiling of Soil Surface Rooting1-like gene family in soybean (Glycine max (L.) Merr.).

- **PMID**: [43013171](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43013171/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s11033-026-13069-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11033-026-13069-y)
- **第一作者**: Nisha Agrawal
- **作者**: Nisha Agrawal, Giriraj Kumawat, Anjali Choudhary, Shreya Verma, Kamini Patidar, Prince Choyal, Milind B Ratnaparkhe, Gyanesh K Satpute

## 摘要
BACKGROUND: In plants, root traits are governed by complex genetic networks whose natural variation critically shapes root system architecture (RSA). Soybean breeding efforts aim to develop varieties with robust root systems to enhance water and nutrient use efficiency. To elucidate the genetic basis of root development, homologs of the rice Soil Surface Rooting 1 (OsSOR1) were identified in soybean. Seventeen GmSOR1-like genes were characterized for gene structure, conserved motifs, protein properties, cis-regulatory elements (CREs), phylogenetic relationships, and tissue-specific expression patterns.

METHODS AND RESULTS: In this study, soybean germplasm accessions were phenotyped under hydroponic culture and soil culture systems for five key root traits: primary root length (PRL), total root length (TRL), surface area of roots (SAR), root diameter (RD), and root volume (RV). Across multiple experiments conducted under hydroponic and soil culture systems, the soybean genotypes NRC2396, EC528623, HAL, and HARDEE, consistently demonstrated high TRL. In contrast, PS1024, TGX 780-5 A, JS80-21, and Pusa 37 consistently exhibited low TRL across all experiments. Contrasting genotypes exhibited inverse root angle patterns, with low-rooting lines displaying wider angles and high-rooting lines narrower ones, except NRC2396. Differential gene expression analysis in four high-rooting-low root angle, and four low rooting-high root angle genotypes revealed distinct transcriptional patterns in lateral root tissues analysed at four different time points under hydroponic culture conditions.

CONCLUSIONS: Notably, Glyma01g097900, Glyma09g203400, Glyma10g210051, Glyma12g077333, Glyma14g185600, and Glyma20g180551, showed elevated expression in the lateral roots of low rooting-high root angle genotypes at two-weeks and four-weeks stages. These findings suggest that these genes may play key roles in regulating soybean root architecture and offer promising targets for functional validation and root trait improvement.
