### 核心发现
1. In summary, among NA-experienced, HBeAg-positive patients with CHB and baseline HBsAg <3,000 IU/mL, Peg- IFNα-2b add-on therapy was associated with a rapid early decline in qHBsAg followed by a modest rebound, whereas continued NAs alone showed minimal change.
