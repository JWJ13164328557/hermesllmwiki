### 核心发现
1. Here, we show that a thyroid hormone surge activates the IGF-1/IGF-1-R/Akt pathway on postnatal day 15 and initiates a brief but intense proliferative burst of predominantly binuclear cardiomyocytes.
2. Here, we show a 1.4-fold increase in CPN during the preado- lescent period, which occurs as a discrete proliferative burst on P15 initiated by a surge in thyroid hormone (T3).
3. We found 5- to 12-fold increases in mRNA levels (p < 0.05) of all these genes on the morning (9:00 a.m.) of P15, with levels on P16 falling to near-P13 levels.
4. In some mitotic binuclear CMs, we found sarcomeric struc- tures on the periphery (e.g., Figure 3G shows a binucleate CM in anaphase, labeled ‘‘A’’).
5. We found that IGF-1 mRNA and IGF-1 expression are increased 2.3-fold (p < 0.05; Figure 6A) and 39-fold (p < 0.05; Figures 6B–6F), respectively, in P15 relative to P10 hearts.
6. Here, we show that over a precisely timed interval during the third week of life, 500,000 CMs are added to the preadolescent mouse heart.
7. Here, we show that numerous CMs reenter the cell cycle on the night of P14; this culminates in cell division during P15.
8. In support of this notion, we found extensive nuclear localization of aurora B, indicating that these CMs have just passed the G2/M phase transit point by the morning of P15.
