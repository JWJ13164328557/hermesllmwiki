### 核心发现
1. Collectively, plant functional traits play a signiﬁcant role in enhancing carbon sequestration and supporting carbon neutrality, thereby mitigating climate change.
2. It is evident that seasonal climatic variabil- ity strongly controls plant physiological processes, with monsoon moisture and temperature enhancing carbon uptake.
3. Notably, advances in plant functional trait-based assessments at the plant and landscape levels underscore the importance of linking plant func- tional traits to ecosystem services, particularly in the context of climate change (Madani et al., 2018).
4. Furthermore, functional traits may provide critical insights into the regulation of bamboo productivity and carbon sequestration poten- tial (Malik et al., 2024; Kanta et al., 2024).
5. Notably, plant functional trait-based assessments that identify climatically adaptive, high-performing bamboo species and clones with enhanced carbon sequestration capacity and productivity can substantially contribute to carbon neutrality, sustainable forestry, and a broader understanding of bamboo’s role in achieving the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
