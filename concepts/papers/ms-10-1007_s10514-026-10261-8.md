### 核心发现
1. We show that the pheromone intensity can be used by swarms of simple robots to form groups of speciﬁc sizes, without the requirement of direct communication between the robots via, e.g., WiFi or Bluetooth.
2. To this end, a leader robot lays a phosphorescent trail by exciting the arena surface with ultraviolet light; the brightness of the trail decays over time, and its initial intensity encodes the target group size.
