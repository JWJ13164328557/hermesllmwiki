### 核心发现
1. The core hypothesis asserts the following: the value of any educational element should be evaluated not by the difficulty level or discriminatory power emphasized in classical psychometrics, but by its capacity to elicit a single, stable, and consistently accurate mental response in an extremely high proportion of the target audience (applicable target ≥ 95%; theoretical ideal 99%).
2. This principle is defined at two levels: the formation of a single, consistent semantic equivalent by 95% of the target audience is the applicable (operational) design target; 99% is positioned as the theoretical ideal to be approached.
3. Nevertheless, it must be noted that the empirical effect of mastery learning does not directly prove PIF’s specific clarity thresholds (95%–99%); these thresholds are targets that must be tested through pilot studies (Bloom, 1968; Guskey, 2024).
4. • Objective: To create a “Common Denominator” in the entire target audience; to eliminate “conceptual confusion” before it begins.
5. Its primary and most urgent application targets the STEM fields, where conceptual precision is paramount.
