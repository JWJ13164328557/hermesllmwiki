### 核心发现
1. Despite its central role, the sector has struggled to meet growth targets; during 2018–19, agricultural expansion reached only 0.85% against a target of 3.8%, with wheat contributing merely 0.5%, far below the expected 4.9%10.
2. Abscisic acid (ABA) plays a central role in drought response by regulating stomatal closure and promoting leaf senescence to reduce transpiration, though this also limits photosynthesis and yield20.
3. In all drought stress treatments (both pot and field), irrigation was scheduled based on the target field capacity levels.
4. Soil moisture levels were maintained by weighing the pots daily and adding water to restore the target field capac­ ity.
5. Soil moisture content was monitored every 2–3 days using a calibrated soil moisture meter, and irrigation was applied to maintain the target field capacity levels.
6. Soil moisture was periodically monitored using a soil moisture meter to verify that the target deficit levels were maintained.
7. Notably, grain yield increased by 18–24% compared to uninoculated controls under the same reduced fertilizer regime, while several key parameters (shoot biomass, chlorophyll content, and grain nutrient uptake) performed comparably to, or better than, the full fertilizer treatment.
8. Importantly, we assessed their performance under reduced fertilizer applications (80% of the recom­ mended NPK dose), a scenario highly relevant to resource-limited farming systems.
