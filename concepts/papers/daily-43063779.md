# SlbHLH123 acts as a positive regulator of bacterial wilt resistance in tomato.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Biochemical and biophysical research communications (2026)
- **PMID**: [43063779](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43063779/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.bbrc.2026.154891](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2026.154891)
- **第一作者**: Jia-Le Li
- **作者**: Jia-Le Li, Kai-Yue Yu, Rui-Wen Wang

## 摘要
Bacterial wilt caused by Ralstonia solanacearum is a devastating soil-borne disease that severely threatens global tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) production. This study characterized the role of the bHLH transcription factor SlbHLH123 in tomato defense against R. solanacearum. Quantitative real-time PCR showed that SlbHLH123 was rapidly and transiently transcriptionally induced upon pathogen infection. Transgenic tomato lines overexpressing SlbHLH123 exhibited significantly enhanced bacterial wilt resistance relative to wild-type plants. Physiological assays revealed that SlbHLH123 overexpression attenuated pathogen-induced reactive oxygen species accumulation and membrane lipid peroxidation, and elevated superoxide dismutase and peroxidase activities. Furthermore, SlbHLH123 overexpression upregulated transcript levels of pathogenesis-related genes SlPR1b, SlPR4b and SlPR5. Collectively, SlbHLH123 positively regulates tomato bacterial wilt resistance by enhancing antioxidant defense and activating defense gene expression, providing a candidate gene for resistant tomato molecular breeding.
