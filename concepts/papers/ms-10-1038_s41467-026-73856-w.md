### 核心发现
1. Using murine and human models, we show that hepatic polarity and bile canaliculi (BC) formation initiate at sites of cell division during the hepatoblast stage, and that BC elongation is temporally associated with oriented cell division during hepatocyte differentiation.
2. We show that hepatocyte polarization and primordial BC formation initiate during the hepatoblast stage, with BC elongation accompanying hepatocyte differentiation.
3. Together, these findings reveal a stage-dependent mechanism linking liver architecture to functional maturation and provide insights into liver development, regeneration, and potential therapeutic strategies for metabolic liver disease and liver failure.
4. Mechanistically, we identify a polarity–signaling axis in which PAR3 interacts with IQGAP1—a scaffold that bridges ERK to its upstream activators RAF and MEK 27,28, and also regulates ARTICLE IN PRESS ARTICLE IN PRESS adherens junctions, TJs, actin organization, and Cdc42/Rac signaling 27-29.
5. These findings suggest that hepatic cell polarity and the formation of primordial BCs—initial BC structures formed between polarizing hepatoblasts—begin during the early hepatoblast stage.
6. These results suggest a conserved developmental trajectory of hepatic polarity and BC biogenesis between mouse and human models.
7. Collectively, these findings indicate that PAR3 and YAP may function within a shared regulatory pathway, with their co-localization at TJs playing a pivotal role in BC elongation and hepatocyte development.
8. This regulation is required for efficient BC elongation and hepatocyte maturation.
