### 核心发现
1. In this paper, we show that background QED processes severely limit the study of elastic light-by-light scattering at e+e−colliders.
2. Notably, this behavior holds true for both e+e−and e−e− colliders.
3. Notably, these background processes are absent in ultra-peripheral heavy-ion collisions.
