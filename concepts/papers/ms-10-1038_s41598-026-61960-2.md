### 核心发现
1. Notably, although these formulations differed and achieved different organoid formation efficiencies, they all included Noggin and RSPO1, growth factors that are essential to maintain stemness.
2. This study provides a practical workflow for generating EOC PDOs, with potential applications in disease modeling, drug screening, and translation cancer research, while recognizing the need for broader validation.
3. Because EOCOM3 was evaluated in a limited number of samples, additional validation is required to determine its performance across different EOC histologic subtypes, sample sources, tumor cellularity levels, treatment histories, and low-input biopsy specimens.
