### 核心发现
1. This natural wax layer plays a crucial role in regulating gas exchange and minimising transpiration.
2. The construction and working principles of waxing equipment play a critical role in achieving uniform coating thickness, reducing material wastage and ensuring hygienic application under commercial packing conditions (3).
3. These coatings play a critical role in extending shelf life and maintaining market quality during storage and transportation.
4. Therefore, the adoption of effective post-harvest technologies is essential to extend shelf life and maintain quality during storage and distribution.
5. However, careful selection of coating materials, formulation and application rate is essential to achieve desirable quality retention without inducing adverse effects.
6. Thus, appropriate selection of wax type, formulation and application method is essential for optimising post-harvest quality, shelf life and marketability of vegetables.
7. Therefore, careful control of shellac concentration and coating thickness is essential to balance moisture retention, respiration regulation and overall fruit quality during storage and marketing.
8. Proper optimisation of spray pressure, nozzle type and drying conditions is essential to avoid uneven coating, wax streaking or excessive restriction of gas exchange, which could otherwise lead to physiological disorders during storage (25, 52).
