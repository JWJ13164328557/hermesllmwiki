### 核心发现
1. This formulation ensures that competence dimensions receiving frequent observational updates adapt quickly, while dimensions with sparse recent evidence retain prior estimates — a property we found essential during pilot experiments where learners engaged irregularly across skill domains.
2. The core idea — that each organism thrives within a specific niche defined by multiple environmental dimensions — maps onto the educational setting where each learner occupies a competence niche defined by proficiency dimensions, and each resource addresses a target niche defined by difficulty, topic, and pedagogical function [12].
3. A niche fitness score, computed as a multidimensional overlap between the learner's estimated competence niche and each candidate resource's target niche, drives both the ranking of individual resources and the sequencing of personalized learning paths.
4. This KG-grounded prompting strategy — where the LLM receives not just raw text but also the prerequisite chain and difficulty metadata surrounding a target knowledge node — constrains the model's inference to pedagogically coherent outputs rather than unchecked free-form generation [36].
