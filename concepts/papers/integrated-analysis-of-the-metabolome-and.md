### 核心发现

- Collectively, these findings strongly suggest that the notion that these 13 anthocyanins play a pivotal role as major contributors to the red pigmentation in cashew apple peels.
- These findings suggest that the multiple gene families involved in anthocyanin biosynthesis may play distinct and specialized roles in the pathway.
- In summary, our integrated transcriptomic and metabolomic anal­ ysis provides a comprehensive understanding of the molecular basis of anthocyanin biosynthesis in cashew apple peels.
- MdCOP1 ubiquitin E3 ligases interact with MdMYB1 to regulate light-induced anthocyanin biosynthesis and red fruit coloration in apple.
- Ectopic expression of VvMybPA2 promotes proanthocyanidin biosynthesis in grapevine and suggests additional targets in the pathway.
