### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these findings strongly suggest that the notion that these 13 anthocyanins play a pivotal role as major contributors to the red pigmentation in cashew apple peels.
2. On the other hand, UFGT plays a critical role in the final step of the anthocyanin biosynthetic pathway, facilitating the glycosylation and stabilization of unstable anthocyanins.
3. These findings suggest that the multiple gene families involved in anthocyanin biosynthesis may play distinct and specialized roles in the pathway.
4. In summary, our integrated transcriptomic and metabolomic anal­ ysis provides a comprehensive understanding of the molecular basis of anthocyanin biosynthesis in cashew apple peels.
5. MdCOP1 ubiquitin E3 ligases interact with MdMYB1 to regulate light-induced anthocyanin biosynthesis and red fruit coloration in apple.
6. Ectopic expression of VvMybPA2 promotes proanthocyanidin biosynthesis in grapevine and suggests additional targets in the pathway.
7. Remarkably, over 7 million metric tons of cashew apples are produced annually based on global cashew nut production (https://www.cashews.org/).
8. Notably, only 12 of these metabolites were detected in the Ye fruits.
