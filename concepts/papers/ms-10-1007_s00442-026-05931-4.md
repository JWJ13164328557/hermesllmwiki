### 核心发现
1. We found that the addition of honeydew at accumulated levels reduced total inorganic soil nitrogen by 48.8% relative to controls.
2. These results suggest the possibility of a positive plant-herbivore-soil feedback for milkweeds, mediated by aphid honeydew.
3. The reciprocal nature of plant-soil interactions is key to this conceptual model, and it may be unclear how the deposition of aphid honeydew fits into this framework.
