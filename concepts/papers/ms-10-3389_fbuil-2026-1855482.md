### 核心发现
1. Frontiers in Built Environment frontiersin.org 01 As individuals spend most of their time indoors, the built environment plays a crucial role in the spread of pathogens and the promotion of healthy and efficient buildings (González-Lezcano, 2023).
2. Traditional strategies, such as routine cleaning, environmental maintenance, and hand hygiene, continue to play a central role in infection control (Gozdzielewska et al., 2022).
3. These approaches target multiple transmission pathways, such as surfaces and air, using mechanisms ranging from antimicrobial materials to ventilation and automated systems.
4. HVAC and air purification Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems also play a key role in infection prevention (Nikoopayan and Mousavi, 2025).
5. Taken together, the literature suggests that while microbiological reductions are well established, translation into consistent health benefits remains uncertain.
6. To support informed decision-making, it is essential to consider both the costs and benefits of human-independent IH solutions, as well as their sensitivity to environmental and social conditions.
