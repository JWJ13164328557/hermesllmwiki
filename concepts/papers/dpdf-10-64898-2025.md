1. Collectively, these results provide evidence that CRY1 physically interacts with ARF3 in a 331 light-independent manner.
2. Collectively, these findings suggest that CRY1 498 could have suppressive effects on ARF3 under both light and dark conditions, implying that 499 the regulatory mechanisms of CRY1 toward ARF3 differs from those previously described for 500 ARF6 and ARF8.
3. Notably, ARF3 and ARF4, 280 which are transcription factors in the response to auxin, were selected as major candidate 281 downstream targets of CRY1 due to their well-characterized roles in auxin response (Cheng et 282 al., 2013; Guan et al., 2017).
4. Integrating RNA-seq with cis-motif analysis identifies stress-responsive ARF3 targets within 51 the CRY1 network.
5. 60  ARF3 acts downstream of CRY1 and CRY1 physically interacts with ARF3.
6. In Oryza sativa (rice), ARF3 was recently found to 159 participate in an immune signaling module: a pathogen-induced A Leaf Expressed and 160 Xoo-induced lncRNA 1 (ALEX1), binds to the IDR of ARF3 and modulates ARF3’s phase 161 separation behavior, converting it into a functional state to enhance JA-mediated defense and 162 disease resistance (Lei et al., 2025).
7. Exposure of dark-grown seedlings to light, ETHYLENE 163 RESPONSE FACTOR 72 (ERF72) is translocated to the nucleus, and interacts with ARF6 164 and BRASSINOZOLE-RESISTANT1 (BZR1) to attenuate the transcriptional regulation of 165 target genes of ARF6 and BZR1 (Liu et al., 2018).
8. Taken 208 together, these results suggest that CRY1 has the function of both enhancement of shoot 209 regeneration and suppression of root regeneration.
