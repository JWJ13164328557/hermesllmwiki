### 核心发现
1. Our findings suggest that further studies on the use of NAA in sago palm ex vitro propagation should emphasise varying low concentrations around 1 ppm.
2. In conclusion, a low concentration of 1 ppm NAA appears effective in promoting the subsequent growth.
3. Phytohormones play a crucial role in mediating carbohydrate transformations by activating enzymes, as reported in sorghum grain (38).
4. These data were required to determine the availability of suckers in the clusters that were mostly used by the farmers, as well as farmers’ preferences in selecting them based on the extraction process and handling.
5. Concentrations of 2000 and 4000 ppm are required to achieve the most effective root formation in apple cuttings (33).
