### 核心发现
1. Importantly, neutralizing ele- vated miR-29a-3p in maternal sEVs ameliorated metabolic dysfunction in offspring of dams with obesity, suggesting that this pathway may represent a candidate target for limiting obesity-associated inter- generational metabolic programming.
2. We found increased mature miR-29a-3p levels in the liver and pancreatic islets of DIO pregnant mice (Supplementary Fig.
3. Together, these data show that maternal plasma-derived sEVs traverse the placenta and deliver miRNA cargo—speciﬁcally miR-29a- 3p—to the fetal liver.
4. To further conﬁrm that miR-29a-3p directly suppresses these enzymes at the post-transcriptional level, we per- formed luciferase reporter assays.
5. These results indicate that miR-29a-3p directly binds to and represses these epige- netic regulators at the post-transcriptional level.
6. Together, these data suggest that circulating corticosterone, gluca- gon, and hepatic sympathetic innervation are unlikely to explain the phenotype observed at this stage.
7. Together, these data suggest that elevated maternal sEV-miR-29a- 3p disrupts DNA methylation homeostasis in the fetal liver, leading to aberrant Pgc-1α expression and premature activation of hepatic gluconeogenesis.
