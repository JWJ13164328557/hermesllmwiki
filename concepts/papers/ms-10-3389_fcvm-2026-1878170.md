### 核心发现
1. reported that Jmjd3 profoundly influences macrophage transdifferentiation by binding to target genes and regulating H3K27me3 levels (31).
2. observed that using I-BET151 significantly regulates cytokine expression in DCs and T cells and alleviates graft-vs.-host disease (34).
3. During DNA replication, DNMT1 binds to nascent DNA and methylates it, precisely replicating the methylation pattern present prior to replication (41).
4. This process directly determines whether transcription factors and other molecules can bind to target DNA by precisely regulating the accessibility of specific genomic regions.
5. The CHD family of remodeling complexes interacts with factors involved in chromatin extension and modification (70).
6. miRNAs primarily suppress gene expression and translation by forming base pairs with the 3′-UTR of target mRNAs (73).
7. circRNAs can specifically bind to microRNAs via complementary binding sites, thereby sequestering and inhibiting miRNA activity (82).
8. Furthermore, circRNAs can interact with RNA-binding proteins and function as transcriptional regulators (83, 84).
