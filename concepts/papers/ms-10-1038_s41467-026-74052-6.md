### 核心发现
1. Inspired by the helical nanoarchitecture of cherry bark19,20, we report a bioinspired nano-orientation engineering strategy to fabricate ultra-tough regenerated cellulose fibers with a biaxially oriented configuration via a scalable microfluidic spinning system.
2. Collectively, these findings highlight the critical importance of the complex, multi-directional flow dynamics within the microfluidic channels for inducing and stabilizing the biaxial nano-orientation structure in cellulose fibers.
3. Collectively, these findings verify that bioinspired fibers engineered via nano-orientation techniques can be processed into fabrics exhibiting exceptional mechanical performance, combining ultrahigh toughness and high strength.
