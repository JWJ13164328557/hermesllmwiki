### 核心发现
1. Interestingly, we found the Gata6 locus to have silencing chromatin modiﬁcations (H3K27me3) in BMDMs and inﬂammatory macrophages, whereas Gata6 locus in LPMs has an H3K4me3 mark associated with active chromatin (Figures 6C and S5D).
2. Impor- tantly, we found that GATA6 is not essential for the development of peritoneal macrophages but is required for their maintenance in the proper tissue compartment.
3. In addition, we found that GATA6 expression and other peritoneal macrophage-speciﬁc gene expression pro- grams are induced by local tissue-derived retinoic acid.
4. Lastly, we show that GATA6 in peritoneal macrophages regulates gut IgA response mediated by peritoneal B-1 cells.
5. Consistent with the previous study (Gautier et al., 2012), we found that zinc ﬁnger transcription factor GATA6 is uniquely expressed at a high level in peritoneal macrophages compared to tissue-derived, bone-marrow-derived (BMDM) and fetal- liver-derived macrophages (Figure 1B).
6. We found that perito- neal macrophages had an abundant expression of retinoic acid nuclear receptor RARb (Figures 2A, S2B, and S2C).
7. We found that, following LPS injection, GATA6+ macrophages rapidly accumu- lated around milky spots in omentum (Figures 5C and 5D).
8. Here, we show how local tissue-derived signals, including retinoic acid, control peritoneal macrophage-speciﬁc transcriptional program.
