### 核心发现
1. One of the mechanisms of steroid-induced diabetes is that glucocorticoids down-regulate the expression of phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase (PEPCK) in adipose tissue, and up- regulate PEPCK in the liver, increasing in blood fatty acids and an increase in insulin resistance in insulin target organs (7-9).
2. Glucocorticoids are associated with an increase in insulin resistance in insulin target organs.
3. Glucocorticoids cause steroid-induced diabetes through a complex mechanism involving both direct effects on pancreatic -cells and indirect effects via their impact on insulin target organs.
4. Taken together, 2 weeks of glucocorticoid administration to C57BL/6 mice did not cause any obvious changes in islet morphology but significantly altered the quality of insulin granules in pancreatic -cells.
