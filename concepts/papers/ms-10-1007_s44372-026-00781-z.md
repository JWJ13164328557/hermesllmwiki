### 核心发现
1. In summary, treatment of a rocket crop with low-intensity red light applied as EoP resulted in an increase in the content of compounds of nutritional value (phenols, antho­ cyanins and carotenoids) without alteration of the morphology or other nutrients in the leaves.
2. Light stimulates primary metabolism, not only because it is essential for photosynthesis and photoassimilate production, but also because it stimulates amino acid and chlorophyll biosynthesis [8].
3. The low-intensity red light treatment was sufficient to achieve normal leaf growth with improved nutritional quality and to significantly increase the contents of Ca, P, carotenoids, anthocyanins and total phenolics in rocket leaves.
