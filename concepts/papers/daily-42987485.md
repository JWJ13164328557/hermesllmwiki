# Inhibition of ROS-induced MiIDA1 and MiIDA2 gene expression by sodium hydrosulfide mitigates mango fruitlet abscission.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [42987485](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42987485/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.jplph.2026.154915](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jplph.2026.154915)
- **第一作者**: Pawan Kumar
- **作者**: Pawan Kumar, Avinash Chandra Rai, Eti Keinan, Vered Irihimovitch

## 摘要
Fruitlet abscission in mango (Mangifera indica L.) is associated with enhanced ethylene perception in the abscission zone (AZ), reduced indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) transport, and increased expression of two INFLORESCENCE DEFICIENT IN ABSCISSION (MiIDA) genes. Furthermore, although reactive oxygen species (ROS) are recognized as important regulators of abscission, their specific role in mango fruitlet drop remains unclear. Hydrogen sulfide (H2S) has recently emerged as a signaling molecule and antioxidant that suppresses ROS, enhancing stress tolerance while delaying senescence and inhibiting floral organ abscission in tomato. Here, we investigated the role of ROS in mango fruitlet abscission and evaluated the potential of sodium hydrosulfide (NaHS), an H2S donor, to mitigate fruitlet shedding. Using mango fruitlet-bearing explants, we monitored ROS dynamics in the AZ and their relationship with MiIDAs expression. Ethephon-induced abscission was associated with rapid ROS burst, upregulation of ROS-producing genes, and subsequent induction of MiIDA1 and MiIDA2. Similarly, exogenous hydrogen peroxide enhanced MiIDA transcripts expression and accelerated abscission. In contrast, NaHS pre-treatment significantly reduced ethephon-induced abscission and ROS accumulation in the AZ, accompanied by suppression of ROS-related genes and decreased MiIDA transcripts expression. Notably, NaHS also attenuated the induction of ethylene signaling-related genes, while not preventing the downregulation of auxin-related genes. Together, our findings suggest that NaHS mitigates mango fruitlet abscission primarily by modulating ethylene signaling, limiting ROS accumulation and repressing MiIDA genes induction. This study provides new insight into the role of ROS in abscission regulation and suggests a potential strategy for reducing fruitlet drop in mango orchards.
