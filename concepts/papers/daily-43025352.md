# Vacuolar aquaporin TIP1;1 binding with small auxin-up RNA 30 influences Arabidopsis cold responses.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology and biochemistry : PPB (2026)
- **PMID**: [43025352](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43025352/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plaphy.2026.111961](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plaphy.2026.111961)
- **第一作者**: Yue Guo
- **作者**: Yue Guo, Shan Du, Weijuan Zhou, Qiang Li, Guojing Liang, Yimin Fan, Yaxian Zhang, Zhen Liang

## 摘要
Cold stress is a major threat for global food security impacting agricultural productivity by influencing cellular processes including photosynthetic efficiency, nutrient and water translocation in plants. Plant response to cold stress involves the regulation of complex transcriptional networks, including the SMALL AUXIN UPREGULATED RNA (SAUR) gene family. Canonically, SAURs inhibit clade PROTEIN PHOSPHATASE 2C, CLADE D (PP2C.D) phosphatases for control of plasma membrane H+-ATPase phosphorylation and activity. The plasma membrane H+-ATPases are primary transporters energizing osmotic ion and water transport across plant cell membranes. Here we uncover unexpected mechanisms involving SAUR30 protein in cold stress response that encompass both transcriptional regulation and post translational control to influence cold sensitivity in plants. Using yeast two-hybrid and mating-based split-ubiquitin screens, ratio BiFC, Xenopus oocyte swelling assays, and Arabidopsis loss-of-function/overexpression lines, we show that under cold stress, increased SAUR30 abundance downregulates aquaporin TONOPLAST INTRINSIC PROTEIN 1;1 (TIP1;1) expression. Remarkably, we found that SAUR30 interacts with TIP1;1 resulting in suppression of TIP1;1 water channel activity. Physiologically, disrupting this interaction-either by saur30 knockout or by expressing a non-binding SAUR30 chimera, restores root elongation and cellular water content under cold stress. We propose that cold-induced SAUR30 restricts vacuolar water transport by suppressing both the abundance and the intrinsic activity of TIP1;1, thereby modulating plant growth and cold resilience.
