### 核心发现
1. Notably, most target genes of DELs were enriched downstream of the terpenoid biosynthesis pathway, such as TPS genes.
2. Many target genes of differentially expressed lncRNAs (DELs) were enriched in terpenoid biosynthesis pathways, and 17 DELs were related to monoterpenoid and sesquiterpenoid biosynthesis.
3. We also identified the differentially expressed lncRNAs (DELs) and predicted their target genes.
4. Identification of differentially expressed lncRNAs and target genes To identify DELs and differentially expressed mRNAs (DEGs), the expression levels of lncRNAs/mRNAs were estimated using the method of fragments per kilobase per million reads (FPKM) with the program Cuffdiff (Trapnell et al., 2012).
5. Because of the regulation of lncRNAs on target genes by cis- and trans-actions, cis-targets were classified according to location relation­ ships (colocation: 100 kb up- or downstream) between lncRNAs and their target PCgenes, and trans-targets were determined via expression correlation (coexpression).
6. GOseq R packages (v1.10.0) (Young et al., 2010) and KOBAS (v2.0.12) (Mao et al., 2005) were used to perform GO and KEGG enrichment analyses of DEGs and lncRNA target genes, respectively.
7. Based on their location relationships, 20,335 cis-target genes of 8662 lncRNAs were predicted and formed 81,663 lncRNA- mRNA pairs.
8. A total of 2066 PCgenes were classified as cis-target genes of 242 CT-specific lncRNAs.
