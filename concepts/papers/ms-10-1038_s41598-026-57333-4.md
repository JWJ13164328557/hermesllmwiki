### 核心发现
1. These findings suggest that the integration of SA with HWT is an effective strategy for ACCEPTED MANUSCRIPT ARTICLE IN PRESS ARTICLE IN PRESS delaying ripening and preserving the postharvest biochemical quality of mango fruits under ambient conditions.
2. In contrast, this study provides a mechanistic and integrative evaluation of their combined application by linking oxidative stress, membrane stability, and cell wall-degrading enzymes within a unified framework.
3. Therefore, modulating oxidative stress is essential for maintaining postharvest quality and extending shelf life.
4. Importantly, the study was conducted under ambient storage conditions (20-25 °C), reflecting real supply chain environments in tropical regions where cold storage is limited.
5. Notably, the combined treatments HWT + SA 1 mM and HWT + SA 2 mM exhibited a continuous increase throughout the storage period.
