### 核心发现
1. First, the effect of these agents on cardiovascular outcomes is only partially captured by changes in glycemia or weight; therefore, dose adjustment and treatment continuation should not be guided by glycemic targets alone.
2. Trial registration numbers, target enrolment, and projected completion dates reﬂect publicly available information at the time of search; tags marked [VERIFICAR] should be reconﬁrmed against clinicaltrials.gov before submission.
3. Notably, in SELECT, which enrolled patients without diabetes, the curves for the primary cardiovascular endpoint began to separate within months of randomization — well before any meaningful weight loss had accrued — suggesting that mechanisms beyond weight reduction are operative (9).
4. Notably, SUMMIT did not require elevated natriuretic peptides for entry — a methodological detail with clinical relevance: in routine practice, a normal NT-proBNP in a patient with obesity and dyspnea should not be interpreted as ruling out HFpEF.
5. Importantly, these ﬁndings apply speciﬁcally to the obesity-related HFpEF phenotype.
6. Importantly, no dedicated cardiovascular outcome trial has yet demonstrated improved clinical outcomes in established HFrEF.
