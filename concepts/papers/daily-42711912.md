# Iron Excess Stress Promotes Adventitious Root Formation at the Root-Hypocotyl Junction in Arabidopsis.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [42711912](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42711912/)

- **作者**: Poonam Kanwar, Britta Möllmann, Stan Altmeisch, Petra Bauer

## 摘要
Fe excess triggers auxin‐mediated adventitious root formation at the root–hypocotyl junction (J‐ARs), helping Arabidopsis maintain root growth when primary root function is compromised under Fe toxicity.
