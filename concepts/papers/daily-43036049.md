# The OsERF39/OsNAC6 Transcription Factor Module Contributes to Rice Quality and Yield by Maintaining Methylglyoxal Homeostasis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Advanced science (Weinheim, Baden-Wurttemberg, Germany) (2026)
- **PMID**: [43036049](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43036049/)
- **DOI**: [10.1002/advs.78765](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.78765)
- **第一作者**: Liuyang Ma
- **作者**: Liuyang Ma, Luna Chen, Yingqing Duan, Guiai Jiao, Zihang Wang, Long Chen, Tianxiao Chen, Baohua Feng

## 摘要
The biosynthesis of starch largely determines rice quality and yield, and is regulated by numerous transcription factors (TFs). Methylglyoxal (MG), a toxic byproduct of glycolysis and the Calvin cycle, accumulates during starch biosynthesis and requires efficient glyoxalase-mediated detoxification for normal grain development. However, how TFs regulate MG homeostasis and modulate rice quality and yield remains largely unexplored. Here, we show that two transcription factors, OsERF39 and OsNAC6, physically interact to form a complex that directly binds to and activates OsGLYI7, encoding a plastid-localized glyoxalase I (GlyI). Loss-of-function mutations in OsERF39, OsNAC6, or OsGLYI7 markedly reduce GlyI activity, trigger MG overaccumulation and redox imbalance in leaves and developing endosperm. These metabolic perturbations compromise transitory and storage starch biosynthesis, reduce nitrogen (protein) accumulation, aggravate grain chalkiness, and decrease grain weight and yield. Collectively, our findings establish the OsERF39/OsNAC6-OsGLYI7 pathway as a critical regulatory node for MG detoxification, sustaining MG and reactive oxygen species (ROS) homeostasis to support carbon and nitrogen assimilation in both source and sink organs, uncovering a TF-mediated mechanism linking MG metabolism to rice grain quality and yield and advancing our understanding of its underlying regulatory basis.
