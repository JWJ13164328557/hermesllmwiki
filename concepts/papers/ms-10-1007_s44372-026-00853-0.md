### 核心发现
1. Nutrient solutions were formulated using established hydroponic concentration ranges adapted for vanilla [6, 10, 25, 26], with target EC and nitrogen concentration based on func­ tional ranges established for V.
2. Together, these results suggest that ammonium stress affected organ development rather than carbon partitioning between above- and belowground compartments.
3. Importantly, nitrogen-form effects are not static, and temporal dynamics have been documented in several species.
4. Notably, plants grown under exclusive ammonium supply (0.0061 g g⁻¹ d⁻¹) achieved the lowest RGR numeri­ cally of any nitrogen treatment, significantly below both the pure nitrate and 75:25 treatments.
5. Notably, apical SPAD values followed a quadratic rather than a linear response (R2 adj = 0.078, pquadratic = 0.007), with the predicted opti­ mum at approximately 54.5% NH4 +-N (i.e., an approximate NO3 –-N: NH4 +-N ratio of 45:55).
6. In Phalaenopsis, nitrate proportions of at least 50% are required for satisfactory vegetative growth and flowering, and a 75:25 NO3 –-N: NH4 +-N ratio has been recommended [20].
