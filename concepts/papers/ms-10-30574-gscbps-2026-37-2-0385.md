# IN VITRO GROWTH OF POTATO (SOLANUM TUBEROSUM L.) CULTIVAR GRANOLA PLANTLETS UNDER DROUGHT STRESS INDUCED BY VARIOUS CONCENTRATIONS OF MANNITOL

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: GSC Biological and Pharmaceutical Sciences ()
- **DOI**: [10.30574/gscbps.2026.37.2.0385](https://doi.org/10.30574/gscbps.2026.37.2.0385)
- **作者**: Mayridha Dwi Andini, Endang Nurcahyani, Yulianty Yulianty, Sri Wahyuningsih
- **来源**: crossref

## 摘要
Potato (Solanum tuberosum L.) is one of the important food commodities with high economic value in Indonesia, with Granola being the most widely cultivated cultivar. The productivity of this cultivar often decreases due to drought stress. This study aimed to determine the growth response of potato plantlets of the Granola cultivar to various concentrations of mannitol as a simulation of drought stress and to determine the mannitol concentration that can still be tolerated by the plantlets. This study employed a Completely Randomized Design (CRD) with one factor, namely the addition of mannitol at concentrations of 0 M, 0.1 M, 0.2 M, 0.3 M, and 0.4 M. Each concentration was replicated five times. The research data consisted of qualitative and quantitative data. Qualitative data were presented using a comparative descriptive approach and supplemented with photographic documentation, while quantitative data were tested for homogeneity of variance using Levene’s test at a 5% significance level. If the data were homogeneous, an analysis of variance (ANOVA) was performed. When significant differences were detected, the analysis was followed by Tukey’s HSD test at the 5% significance level. The results showed that the application of mannitol as a simulation of drought stress induced changes in the morphological and physiological responses of potato plantlets of the Granola cultivar. Increasing mannitol concentrations tended to decrease plantlet height, number of leaves, and number of shoots, accompanied by changes in plantlet appearance from green to greenish-brown and eventually brown, as well as reductions in chlorophyll a, chlorophyll b, and total chlorophyll contents. Potato plantlets of the Granola cultivar were still able to maintain growth under 0.1 M and 0.2 M mannitol treatments, whereas increasing the concentration to 0.3 M and 0.4 M resulted in greater growth inhibition.
