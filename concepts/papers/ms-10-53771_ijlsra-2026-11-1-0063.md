### 核心发现
1. For instance, many biopesticides target specific pests and have little negative impact on the surrounding ecosystem and human health [101-117].
2. The commercially used synthetic pesticides have been proven to be toxic not only to humans and other animals, but also to non-target plant, the surrounding organisms around the plant, and the environment [101-117].
3. Biopesticides play a crucial role in promoting climate-smart agriculture (CSA) by offering effective pest management solutions, improving crop yield and quality, and reducing pest resistance while minimizing negative impacts on human health and the environment [101-114].
4. Bopesticides may, however, not be equally effective against all pests, and their efficacy can vary depending on the target species.
5. Biopesticides offer several advantages over conventional chemical pesticides, including lower toxicity to non-target organisms, reduced environmental impacts, and less likelihood of developing resistance to pests [101-114].
6. Novel microbial strain creation with improved features, such as greater efficacy and larger target spectra, should be prioritized in future research and the use of biopesticides [101-114].
7. A number of studies have reported the ability of CBD to modulate different biochemical targets in insects, likely through the interaction with other G-protein-coupled receptors.
8. Importantly, many of them are non-toxic to beneficial organisms and wildlife [101-114].
