### 核心发现
1. PBZ targets ent-kaurene oxidase, an enzyme crucial for GA biosynthesis, by blocking the oxidation of ent-kaurene to ent-kaurenoic acid21,22.
2. These results suggest that higher PBZ concentrations combined with closer spacing enhance antioxidant enzyme activity possibly due to stress conditions in these treatments.
3. Adequately spaced onion plants in the field are essential to maximize the number of plants per unit area while simultaneously improving the quality of the seeds produced.
