# Timing the feast: Circadian control of rhizosphere nitrogen supply.

## 摘要
The challenge of nitrogen use efficiency stems from a "handshake" problem between soil supply and plant demand. In this issue, Du and colleagues reveal how the rice circadian regulator Nhd1 uses rhythmic chemical signals to synchronize microbial nitrogen cycling with plant uptake and assimilation, providing an example of "chronoculture."
