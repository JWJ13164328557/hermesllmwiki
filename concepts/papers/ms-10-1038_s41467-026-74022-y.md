### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these results demonstrate that cholesterol binds to CHMP4B at CRM2 motif to increase its PM localization and promote PM repair.
2. Collectively, these results suggest the potential of HMGCS1 expression as a biomarker to predict the efficacy of immunotherapies.
3. Together, these results suggest that membrane-localized CHMP4B is essential for cholesterol-mediated promotion of tumor immune evasion.
4. Our findings reveal a tumor immune evasion mechanism wherein HMGCS1 drives cholesterol-dependent PM repair by activating the cholesterol synthesis.
