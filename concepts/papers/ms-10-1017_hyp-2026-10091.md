### 核心发现
1. While altered abilities to engage with, and move towards, possibilities are important aspects of some disenfranchised grief, we argue that this characterisation glosses over important distinctions that are key to characterising the phenomenology and intentionality of disenfranchised grief.
2. Therefore, it does not seem to be the case that we can easily separate out which harmful and disenfranchised possibilities are extrinsic, and which are essential to an experience of grief.
