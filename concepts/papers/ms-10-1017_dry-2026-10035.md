### 核心发现
1. Production methods may target single isolates (Giraldo-Silva et al., 2019), populations of a target species (Doherty et al., 2018), or whole community cultures (e.g., Velasco-Ayuso et al., 2020; Antoninka et al., 2018).
2. Different production targets and systems are appropriate in different settings and for different groups of biota, depending on the environment being treated, the goals and scale of the project and the type of biocrust to be regenerated.
3. At harvest the rows are cut to 3 m length and rolled into tightly packed cylinders for transport to the target site, where they can be unrolled directly on the application surface while maintaining greater intactness compared to scraping and crumbling (Figure 2 a-c).
