### 核心发现
1. Results and discussion: We found that plant NSC content was consistently higher in aboveground parts than in belowground parts across growth stages, indicating an aboveground−prioritized carbon strategy.
2. 5 Conclusion Our results reveal that plant NSC and its components, C, N stoichiometry and their determining soil properties varied with plant tissues and growth stages.
3. This strong negative correlation persisted across all tissues and growth stages, indicating that N plays a central role in modulating the C:N ratio throughout the entire life cycle.
4. Soil C:N ratio directly regulates root nitrogen uptake and carbon partitioning through its inﬂuence on nitrogen availability.
5. Therefore, unraveling how NSC, carbon (C), nitrogen (N) and their ratios respond to salt stress, and how these responses are coupled, is essential for understanding plant adapta- tion to saline environments.
6. NSC, composed primarily of soluble sugars and starch, repre- sents the carbon storage form in plants and are critical for plant growth, metabolism and stress responses (Signori−Müller et al., 2021).
7. Therefore, elucidating the coupling between plant NSC and C, N stoichiometry is critical for understanding how plants coordinate carbon metabolism with nutrient balance.
