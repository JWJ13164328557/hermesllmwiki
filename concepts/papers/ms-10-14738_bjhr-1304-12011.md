### 核心发现
1. Tumour consolidation after chemotherapy using transpupillary thermotherapy (TTT) or cryotherapy plays a key role, significantly reducing the risk of recurrence.
2. The following section presents local treatment methods that play a key role in this process.
3. Visual rehabilitation plays a key role and should be initiated as soon as possible after tumour control is achieved [39].
4. Two mutations are necessary for tumour development.
5. Enucleation involves the removal of the eyeball along with a section of the optic nerve at least 1 cm long, which is crucial for assessing potential tumour infiltration [27].
6. The degree of differentiation and the presence of high-risk factors, such as choroidal or optic nerve invasion, are crucial for prognostic significance [1].
