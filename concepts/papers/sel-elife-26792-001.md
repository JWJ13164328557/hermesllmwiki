### 核心发现
1. TipTracker can interact with the graphical user interfaces of various commercial micro- scope software programs.
2. In summary, we developed a vertical microscope setup with controlled illumination that enables multi-day live imaging.
3. TipTracker interacts with the microscope control software (e.g.
4. For each new system, the AutoIt scripts must be modified to correctly interact with the crucial control buttons of the software GUI, and we provide an illustration of this process within the commented AutoIt scripts (Supplementary file 2).
5. In summary, our root tip-tracking program TipTracker allows for online long-term tracking of root tips or other moving samples and can be easily implemented on a wide range of microscopes.
6. Illumination of the leaves is crucial to keep the plant in a photosynthetically active state, in particular when performing long-term experiments.
7. This is essential for the acquisition of multiple positions during time-lapse experiments and also for the tracking of the root tips, as described below.
8. Importantly, TipTracker makes it possible to observe multiple samples indepen- dently over long periods of time regardless of the individual behaviour.
