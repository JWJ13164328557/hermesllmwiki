3. We report age-related changes in total and regional brain volumes, with sex differences (total cortex, piriform area, amygdala, cingulum), and other regions only changed in males (motor and retrosplenial corti­ ces) or in females (whole brain, cerebrum, CA1, total white matter, corpus callosum).
4. We found no significant cross-sectional differences between males and females within each age group.
5. We found sex-specific changes in the whole brain and cere­ brum volumes of normal aging C57BL/6N mice.
6. In the motor cortex, we found a significant age effect (2way ANOVA, p = 0.0034) but no sex differences (p = 0.905).
7. Broadly, except for body weight, we found no significant differences between males and females within specific age groups.
8. In contrast to the human studies, we found increased total white matter and corpus callosum volumes, but only in females.
