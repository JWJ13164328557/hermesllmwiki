### 核心发现
1. Overall, our findings suggest that hippocampal atrophy in astronauts is not driven by mechanical compression, but it may be affected by reduced vestibular input, aligning with observations in terrestrial vestibular loss.
2. The hippocampus plays a central role in spatial navigation and receives vestibular inputs.
3. Together, these results provide new evidence linking vestibular input to hippocampal integrity in both terrestrial and microgravity conditions.
