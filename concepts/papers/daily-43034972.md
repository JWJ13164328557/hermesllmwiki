# Integrated cytological and transcriptomic analysis of male sterility in a Brassica rapa × Orychophragmus violaceus hybrid allodiploid.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant cell reports (2026)
- **PMID**: [43034972](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43034972/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s00299-026-04043-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00299-026-04043-w)
- **第一作者**: Md Mostafijur Rahman
- **作者**: Md Mostafijur Rahman, Feng Zhu, Dauglas Ochiel, Yixin Huangfu, Long Sha, Jiayin Liu, Asif Ali, Gongyao Shi

## 摘要
Integrated cytological and transcriptomic analyses suggest that male sterility in a Brassica rapa × Orychophragmus violaceus allodiploid is associated with meiotic irregularities, altered spindle organization, impaired pollendevelopment, and changes in reproductive gene expression. Intergeneric hybridization drives Brassica crop evolution and innovation but is hindered by hybrid sterility. To elucidate the transcriptional and cytological basis of pollen failure, we synthesized a novel allodiploid (2n = 22) by crossing Brassica rapa (AA, 2n = 20) with Orychophragmus violaceus (OO, 2n = 24). The hybrid exhibited near-complete male sterility, with a pollen stainability of only about 1%. Scanning electron microscopy revealed aberrant pollen morphology, and its cytological analysis revealed severe meiotic defects at multiple stages of meiosis. Immunolocalization analysis of the allodiploid revealed abnormal microtubule organization and uneven chromosome distribution during meiosis. To characterize transcriptional changes associated with male sterility, we performed comparative transcriptome sequencing of meiotic-stage anthers from the two parental species and the allodiploid. The transcriptomic profile of the allodiploid was clearly distinct from those of both parents, indicating extensive transcriptional reprogramming following allodiploid formation. Selected genes associated with pollen development, cell wall and callose remodeling, pollen tube signaling and polarity, cytoskeletal organization and trafficking, and meiosis/chromosome organization showed marked expression changes in the allodiploid. In particular, bHLH66, GSL5, UAM1, UAM2, Ca2⁺-ATPase9, PIP5K6, PIP5K6-2, ANN7, EXO70B1, ACTIN-1, ACTIN-11, MYOSIN-8, KIN-14O, and SMC4 showed reduced expression in one or both parental comparisons, whereas SEC15B was increased relative to both parents. NPG1 and DYAD showed contrasting expression patterns between the two parental comparisons. Together, the cytological and transcriptomic data show that male sterility in this intergeneric hybrid is accompanied by severe meiotic abnormalities, defective pollen development, and extensive transcriptional reprogramming, providing candidate cellular processes and genes for further investigation.
