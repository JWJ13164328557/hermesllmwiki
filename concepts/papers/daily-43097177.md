# Transcriptional changes induced during drought stress by the overexpression of the chickpea Metallothionein 1 (MT1) gene in Brassica juncea (L.).

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [43097177](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43097177/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s10142-026-02147-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10142-026-02147-w)
- **第一作者**: Swati Lal
- **作者**: Swati Lal, Nikita Tiwari, Uma Gupta, Mehar Hasan Asif, Indraneel Sanyal

## 摘要
Drought is one of the most calamitous environmental stressors, seriously threatening the stability of global crop yields. Brassica juncea (L.) is an essential oilseed crop that experiences drought stress at various stages of its life cycle. In this study, RNA sequencing was performed in transgenic mustard overexpressing the chickpea Metallothionein 1 (MT1) gene following drought stress induction. During the analysis, 3949 differentially expressed genes (DEGs) with P < 0.05 were identified in response to drought stress compared to the control. After applying an additional fold-change threshold, 811 and 863 genes were upregulated and downregulated, respectively. Moreover, these genes were classified via Gene Ontology (GO), KEGG, MapMan, and Pageman pathway analysis. GO analysis showed that DEGs were involved in numerous crucial processes, including cellular and metabolic processes, as well as stress responses. Further, during our study, KEGG enrichment analysis identified 123 enriched pathways. These were found to be significantly associated with glucosinolate biosynthesis, biotin metabolism, lysine biosynthesis, and secondary metabolism. qRT-PCR analysis was performed for selected DEGs, and the results were consistent with the transcriptomic data. Thus, our data provide a platform for identifying novel genes, their functional aspects, and their involvement in several biological pathways, which can assist molecular breeding in developing agronomically superior crops.
