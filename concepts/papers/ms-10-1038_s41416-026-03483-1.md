### 核心发现
1. ROR2 is an interesting target for the L122R mutant MYOD1 rhabdomyosarcoma.
2. Considering targets with existing clinical agents, antibody-drug conjugates targeting cell-surface antigen B7-H3/CD276 are showing preclinical promise in other paediatric cancers and are also deemed a high priority for evaluation in rhabdomyosarcoma.
3. Altered methylation patterns play a key role.
4. The MYOD1L122R mutations preclude normal differentiation of myogenic progenitor cells by gaining the ability to bind to and activate regulatory motifs typically bound by MYC [209].
5. Mutant MYOD1 loses the ability to regulate physiological target genes, resulting in a pathology driven by both gain-of- function and loss-of-function mutations, associated with a very poor prognosis [39].

THE RHABDOMYOSARCOMA PAEDIATRIC THERAPEUTIC DEVELOPMENT WORKSHOP To identify unmet needs and research priorities in rhabdomyo- sarcoma, a detailed diligence review of rhabdomyosarcoma epidemiology, biology, preclinical tools, potential targets and existing commercial developments was prepared by LifeArc and circulated to experts ahead of the workshop.

This included a list of therapeutic targets produced from the GlobalData Drugs Data- base, supplemented with literature searches, to identify preclinical and clinical evidence for each target’s association with rhabdo- myosarcoma (Supplementary Table 1) and the list of drugs in development (Supplementary Table 2).
8. The workshop shared and discussed the scientiﬁc evidence and drug development status of the different therapeutic targets, prioritised targets based on their level of validation and tractability and identiﬁed potential drug combinations to progress into clinical trials.
