NFAT expression is upregulated by microenvironmental stimuli and is elevated in cases with high CLL-PD score.32 Interestingly, higher levels of miR-155 have also been associated with a shorter time to relapse during Ibrutinib treatment in CLL.23 Altogether, these findings suggest that miR-155 may be an interesting target in CLL therapeutics.
2. Importantly, we found that LINC00152 expression correlated with shorter time-to-treatment, in contrast to previous reports Page 8/20 suggesting no clinical impact.27 Our analysis of a large CLL cohort confirmed its poor prognostic value independent of Binet stage and other clinical or biological factors, underscoring its functional relevance.
3. In conclusion, the evidences reported in this study supports the biological and clinical relevance of some ncRNAs in CLL, particularly LINC00152, highlighting the regulatory role that lncRNAs play in controlling both coding and non-coding targets within pathways essential for the proliferation and survival of CLL cells, especially under microenvironmental stimulation.
