6. One of the most extensively studied path- ways is the NLRP3 inflammasome, which plays a critical role in linking metabolic stress to inflammatory responses within atherosclerotic plaques (Stitham et al., 2020).

8. Consequently, effective therapeutic strategies must target not only lipid accumulation but also inflammatory signaling pathways and cellular mecha- nisms responsible for plaque progression.
