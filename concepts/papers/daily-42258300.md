# Description of two novel non-pathogenic tomato-associated Clavibacter species: Clavibacter seminis sp. nov. and Clavibacter quasicaliforniensis sp. nov.

## 摘要
The genus Clavibacter currently comprises ten recognized species associated with diverse plant hosts, including both pathogenic and non-pathogenic members. Here, we describe three tomato-associated strains, A6099T, A4868T and A6308, whose taxonomic positions were evaluated together with three previously unclassified strains (LMG 26808, CFBP 7493 and VKM Ac-2542). Whole-genome analysis of the newly sequenced strains (A6099T, A4868T and A6308) revealed genome sizes ranging from 3.25 to 3.27 Mb, with a single plasmid detected in strain A6099T, whereas no plasmids were identified in strains A4868T and A6308. Phylogenomic reconstruction based on 808 core genes resolved 2 distinct and well-supported clades within the genus, closely related to Clavibacter michiganensis and Clavibacter californiensis. Average nucleotide identity and digital DNA-DNA hybridization values between these clades and their closest relatives were below the accepted species thresholds of 96 and 70%, respectively, confirming their genomic distinctiveness. Comparative genomic analyses demonstrated the absence of major virulence-associated determinants, including the chp/tomA pathogenicity island and key plasmid-borne genes (celA and pat-1), consistent with a non-pathogenic lifestyle. Phenotypically, cells of the newly sequenced strains produced yellow to orange pigmented colonies, grew at 28-33 °C and displayed distinct biochemical and enzymatic profiles, including the ability to utilize glycerol as a carbon source and exhibit esterase, esterase lipase, naphthol-AS-BI-phosphohydrolase, pyrazinamidase and pyrrolidonyl arylamidase activities, traits not observed in closely related species such as C. michiganensis and C. californiensis. Based on the combined genomics, phylogenomics and phenotypic evidence, two novel species are proposed: Clavibacter seminis sp. nov., formed by the type strain A6099T (=ICMP 26023T=LMG 34062T), along with LMG 26808 and CFBP 7493, and Clavibacter quasicaliforniensis sp. nov., composed of the type strains A4868T (=ICMP 26022T=LMG 34064T), A6308 and CFBP 7493. The two new species were non-pathogenic on tomato seedlings, their isolation host.
