### 核心发现
1. Among NP-hard ordering CSPs defined by a single predicate of arity 4, we show that at least 15 051 CSPs admit nontrivial approxima- tion in the satisfiable regime.
2. We show that this fraction is at least p(Π′ →Π, σ) s, where p(Π′ →Π, σ) is characterized in terms of certain restricted pattern densities of permutations in σ.
3. We show that each strong IDU transformation can be represented as a random permuton constructed from the basic permutons I (identity), D (decreasing), and U (uniform) via a simple operation called up-combination, which we introduce in this paper.
4. As a corollary, we show how to find a nearly optimal strong IDU transformation ˆσ such that p(Π′ →Π, ˆσ) ≥p(Π′ →Π, σ) −ε for every weak IDU transformation σ, in time f(k, 1/ε), where k is the maximum arity of predicates in Π.
5. Each random permuton defines a weak IDU transformation, and although the converse is not true, we show that such transformations are as powerful for our purposes as arbitrary weak IDU transformations.
6. First, we show that profiles can be expressed using quasisymmetric polynomials (see Section 9 for details).
7. We show in Appendix A that every shuffle-closed ordering CSP is a Not-First ordering CSP.
8. We now introduce the notion of a transformation that, together with its strengthening, plays a key role in our framework.
