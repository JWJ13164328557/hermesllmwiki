### 核心发现
1. A third tumor-relevant axis is GSDMB: cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL)–derived granzyme A cleaves GSDMB in target tumor cells, providing a CTL/natural killer (NK)–driven pyroptotic effector arm distinct from caspase- or chemotherapy-driven routes (32).
2. Preclinical work suggests that combined IL-1b plus IL-18 blockade may inhibit tumor progression more effectively than blocking either alone (56), point- ing toward dual-target strategies.
3. Mitochondrial dysfunction with cytosolic mtDNA release acts as a key upstream NLRP3 trigger (21).
