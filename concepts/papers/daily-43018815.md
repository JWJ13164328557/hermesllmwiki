# Distinct genomic variant and DNA methylation patterns in Arabidopsis lineages exposed to heat, cold, or UVC for 25 generations : Research Article.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Molecular biology reports (2026)
- **PMID**: [43018815](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43018815/)
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s11033-026-13138-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11033-026-13138-2)
- **第一作者**: Narendra Yadav
- **作者**: Narendra Yadav, Boseon Byeon, Yaroslav Ilnytskyy, Igor Kovalchuk

## 摘要
BACKGROUND: Environmental conditions can influence genome stability and epigenetic regulationacross generations. This study compared genomic variants and DNA methylation in Arabidopsis thaliana lineages propagated for 25 generations under control, heat, cold, or UVC conditions.

METHODS AND RESULTS: Five plants per group were profiled by whole-genome and whole-genome bisulfite sequencing. F2C, F25C, and F25Cd were sampled from two independently propagated lineages (2 + 3 plants), whereas F25H and F25UV were sampled from three lineages (2 + 2 + 1 plants). Variants identified relative to TAIR10 were more abundant in the stress-exposed groups than in the profiled controls. F25H had the largest variant burden, although this pattern was driven primarily by two plants, and showed a higher proportion of synonymous annotations. DNA methylation differed comparatively little in the CG context but more extensively in the CHG and CHH contexts. UVC produced the greatest divergence from the early-generation F2C control, whereas cold and UVC showed greater non-CG methylation divergence than heat. Comparisons with the generation-matched F25C control indicated that some differences also accompanied multigenerational propagation under control conditions.

CONCLUSIONS: Prolonged heat, cold, and UVC exposure was associated with distinct genomic and methylomic signatures. The findings are descriptive and do not establish de novo mutation rates, selection, or specific methylation mechanisms.
