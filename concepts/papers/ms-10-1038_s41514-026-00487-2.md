### 核心发现
1. By uniting aging biology and disease mechanisms through an interpretable systems framework, this study provides new opportunities for early risk prediction, mechanistic stratiﬁcation, and pre- cision intervention in chronic respiratory diseases18–20.
2. Collectively, these results demonstrate that AgeAcc provides a quantitative molecular index connecting environmental exposure, inﬂammation, and clinical outcomes.
3. Discussion This study establishes a mechanistically interpretable framework that links molecular aging to chronic respiratory disease susceptibility through a pathway-level analysis of DNA methylation.
4. Although these convergent pathways highlight biologically plausible processes associated with respiratory ageing and disease progression, they should be regarded as candidate mechanistic targets rather than validated therapeutic targets.
5. In summary, this work advances a mechanistic and interpretable view of molecular aging in chronic respiratory diseases.
6. Notably, PathwayAge consistently out- performed established clocks in capturing biologically coherent patterns aligned with known tissue physiology, validating that its pathway-based framework enhances both predictive accuracy and mechanistic relevance.
