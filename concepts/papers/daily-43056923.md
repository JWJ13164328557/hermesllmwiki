# CsAHL17 activates the chloroplast-associated invertase CsINV12 to modulate sucrose metabolism and cold response in tea plants.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Journal of plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43056923](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43056923/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.jplph.2026.154920](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jplph.2026.154920)
- **第一作者**: Xuxu Lang
- **作者**: Xuxu Lang, Bo Li, Huimin Zhang, Chao Zhou, Dapeng Song, Zixin Yu, Huan Wang, Kai Fan

## 摘要
Neutral/alkaline invertases (A/N-INVs) govern carbohydrate partitioning and abiotic stress adaptation, yet the function and transcriptional regulation of organelle-targeted isoforms, particularly chloroplast-associated ones, during cold stress in perennial woody crops remain largely unknown. Here we identified and functionally characterized CsINV12, a chloroplast-associated α-subfamily A/N-INV from tea plant. Recombinant CsINV12 hydrolyzes sucrose optimally at pH 7.5 and 50-55°C in vitro, and its transcript abundance is induced by low temperature and by exogenous sugars. Transient silencing and overexpression in tea leaves, together with stable heterologous expression in Arabidopsis, indicated that CsINV12 promotes sucrose cleavage and reshapes the sucrose-hexose balance under cold stress, which was associated with osmotic adjustment, maintained photosynthetic performance and reduced membrane lipid peroxidation. Overexpressing CsINV12 enhanced freezing survival in transgenic Arabidopsis and was accompanied by lower transcript abundance of canonical cold-responsive marker genes, a pattern consistent with sugar-mediated modulation of stress-responsive transcription rather than with suppression of the cold-response pathway. In addition, promoter haplotype analysis across a tea germplasm panel identified a haplotype (Hap-P3) associated with potentially superior cold hardiness, nominating a candidate cis-regulatory resource that awaits functional validation. Mechanistically, the cold-inducible AT-hook transcription factor CsAHL17 directly transactivates CsINV12 by binding to A/T-rich matrix attachment region-like motifs in its promoter, defining a CsAHL17-CsINV12 module that links organellar sucrose metabolism to cold acclimation and providing a promising molecular target for breeding cold-tolerant tea cultivars.
