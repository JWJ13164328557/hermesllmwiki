# Phosphorylation of the transcription factor TgWRKY47 by TgSnRK1 modulates cone enlargement in the gymnosperm Torreya grandis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: New Phytologist (2026)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/nph.71525](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.71525)
- **作者**: Ya Liu, Ruoman Wang, Shuya Wang, Tongtong Wang, Jinwei Suo
- **来源**: openalex
- **概念**: Phosphorylation, Transactivation, Biology, Cell biology, WRKY protein domain

## 摘要
Reproduction is essential for seed plant survival, and in gymnosperms, cone enlargement plays a pivotal role in seed production and reproduction. However, the molecular mechanisms regulating cone enlargement after fertilization remain poorly understood. Here, using Torreya grandis, a representative gymnosperm species with persistently low cone enlargement efficiency, we elucidated the molecular regulation of cone enlargement and identified sucrose non-fermenting 1-related protein kinase 1 (TgSnRK1) as a novel negative regulator of this process. TgSnRK1 physically interacted with the WRKY transcription factor TgWRKY47 both in vitro and in vivo. Functional analyses revealed that TgSnRK1 acted upstream of TgWRKY47 and suppressed cone enlargement in T. grandis. Phosphorylation assays revealed that TgSnRK1 directly phosphorylated TgWRKY47 at Thr-75. This phosphorylation event markedly reduced the transactivation activity of TgWRKY47, thereby attenuating its activation of the cell wall-remodeling gene TgEXPA2, a key regulator of cell expansion during cone enlargement. Together, these findings establish a phosphorylation-dependent TgSnRK1-TgWRKY47-TgEXPA2 regulatory pathway in which developmental attenuation of TgSnRK1-mediated phosphorylation of TgWRKY47 at Thr-75 enhances TgWRKY47 transactivation activity, thereby promoting TgEXPA2-dependent cone enlargement. This study advances our understanding of reproductive development in gymnosperms and identifies potential molecular targets for improving reproductive efficiency and seed yield.

繁殖是维持种子植物生存和延续的关键过程。在裸子植物中，球果膨大是种子发育和繁殖过程中的关键环节。然而，受精后调控球果膨大的分子机制目前仍知之甚少。本研究以球果膨大效率长期较低的典型裸子植物香榧 (Torreya grandis) 为材料，研究了球果膨大的分子调控机制，并鉴定发现蔗糖非发酵型蛋白激酶1 (sucrose non‐fermenting 1‐related protein kinase 1，TgSnRK1) 是抑制该过程的重要调控因子。 研究发现，TgSnRK1可在体外和体内与WRKY转录因子TgWRKY47发生互作。功能分析表明，TgSnRK1作用于TgWRKY47上游负调控香榧球果膨大过程。 磷酸化实验分析揭示，TgSnRK1作为上游激酶，可直接催化TgWRKY47蛋白在Thr‐75位点发生磷酸化。该磷酸化修饰降低了TgWRKY47的转录激活能力，从而削弱其对细胞壁重塑相关基因TgEXPA2的激活作用。 综上，本研究揭示了一个由磷酸化修饰介导的TgSnRK1‐TgWRKY47‐TgEXPA2调控模块。在球果发育过程中，随着TgSnRK1介导的TgWRKY47 Thr‐75位点磷酸化水平降低，TgWRKY47的转录激活活性增强，进而增强TgEXPA2的表达，促进球果膨大。本研究揭示了裸子植物生殖发育过程中球果膨大的调控机制，为深入理解裸子植物繁殖发育调控网络提供了新的理论依据，并为提高繁殖效率和种子产量提供了潜在的分子调控靶点。.
