### 核心发现
1. Here, we report that PUB22, a plant U-box type E3 ubiquitin ligase, plays a critical role in the regulation of plant resistance against Helicoverpa armigera and other JA re- sponses in tomato.
2. In agreement with these ﬁndings, we found that tomato COI1 physically interacts only with JAZ1/2/5/7 (Figure 3), whereas PUB22 physically interacts with JAZ1/3/4/6 (Figure 2).
3. Taken together, these re- sults indicate that PUB22 plays a crucial role in plant growth and defense responses, together with COI1- regulated JA signaling, by targeting speciﬁc JAZs.
4. Collectively, these results demonstrated that PUB22 physically interacts with JAZ1/3/4/6, whereas COI1 physically interacts with JAZ1/ 2/5/7.
5. Moreover, we found that expression of PUB22 can be transcriptionally activated by MYC2, thus forming a positive feedback circuit in JA signaling.
6. We found that PUB22 expression is sharply upregulated upon herbivory and that loss of PUB22 results in plant sensitivity to Helicoverpa armigera, a worldwide pest.
7. Us- ing in vitro ubiquitination assays, we found that GST-JAZ1/3/4/6 were all polyubiquitinated by PUB22 (Figure 4A).
8. Here, we found that expression of the JA-responsive genes ARG2 and TD2 was decreased in pub22 mutants upon exposure to herbivores and in response to exogenous JA but increased by deletion of JAZ4, which was degraded by PUB22 (Figures 1G, 1H, 5D, and 5E and Supplemental Figures 20B and 20C).
