### 核心发现

2. Micronutrients such as zinc (Zn) and iron (Fe), along with beneficial elements like silicon (Si), play a crucial role in enhancing growth, yield formation and stress tolerance in rice.
3. Iron plays a crucial role in photosynthesis and respiration through its involvement in electron transport systems and it supports antioxidant activity that reduces oxidative stress in plants, thereby contributing to enhanced yield (8).
4. Micronutrients are critical for physiological processes and maximising the efficiency of Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P) and Potassium (K) (NPK) fertilisers.
