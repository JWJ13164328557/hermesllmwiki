### 核心发现
1. In summary, this study demonstrates that, for RKC, a 3-month live holding period with raw herring and shrimp as feed impacts only marginally the organoleptic properties of its cooked leg meat.
2. Although the clusters in the live holding group were cooked for 30 s longer than their wild counterparts to reach the same target temperature (91 °C) in the walking leg core, it is likely that, in the wild crabs, the leg meat located in proximity to the exoskeleton was subjected to higher thermal exposure.
3. In crustaceans, the hepatopancreas is the metabolic centre and lipid storage organ and plays a key role in regulating nutrient status and enzymatic activity (Albalat et al., 2021); thus, the HSI can be used as a valuable indicator of the bio- logical and nutritional status of RKCs (Lian et al.
4. In summary, the sensory differences between the wild and live holding groups were minimal and unlikely to be perceptible to untrained consumers.
5. Concluding remarks This study demonstrates that a 3-month live holding period using raw herring and shrimp as feed has only a marginal impact on the sensory properties of cooked leg meat from red king crab (Paralithodes camtschaticus).
6. Notably, in our study, textural juiciness was positively correlated with cooked cluster yield and the lightness of the cooked leg meat, but negatively correlated with textural fibrousness.

8. Interestingly, the hepatosomatic index (HSI) was sig- nificantly negatively correlated with sensory attributes of negative connotation, namely the trimethylamine flavour as well as the chemical odour and flavour.
