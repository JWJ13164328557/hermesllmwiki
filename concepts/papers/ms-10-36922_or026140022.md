### 核心发现
1. We analyze material-based strategies for modulating senescence-associated phenotypes, including mechanobiological approaches, such as tunable stress relaxation to attenuate pathological mechanical memory, and biochemical interventions that target reactive oxygen species via nanozyme catalysis and modulate SASP signaling through spatiotemporal delivery control.
2. Dynamic topological remodeling and spatiotemporal decoupled delivery The mechanobiological and biochemical strategies described above each target a single pathological axis of the senescent microenvironment.
3. In summary, the programmable hydrogel toolbox has grown well beyond the static scaffolds of a decade ago.
4. Notably, few hydrogel systems combine these two arms into a single matrix.
5. Programmable matrices may counteract this senescence-associated epigenetic silencing through mechanotransduction- mediated chromatin remodeling, as discussed in Section 2.52 Notably, the mechanical drivers of this silencing differ sharply across oral tissues, reflecting the distinct anatomical demands and loading environments of each niche.
