# The ZmDLR8/ZmRTEL1 Is Required for Lateral Root Formation by Mediating DNA Damage Repair in Maize.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant, cell & environment (2026)
- **PMID**: [43036225](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43036225/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/pce.71045](https://doi.org/10.1111/pce.71045)
- **第一作者**: Yan Zhang
- **作者**: Yan Zhang, Yang Li, Yanbin Zhu, Feng Wei, Zhubing Hu, Chunfei Wang

## 摘要
Maize possesses a complex root system architecture, in which the lateral root (LR) plays a key role in the uptake of water and mineral nutrients, which affects yield. In this study, we characterized the maize mutant dlr8-1 (defective in lateral root 8-1). The dlr8-1 mutant exhibited reduced cell proliferation, consequently impairing both lateral root primordium initiation and subsequent primordium morphogenesis, leading to a reduced LR number. ZmDLR8/ZmRTEL1 encodes a DNA helicase that is mainly localized in the nucleus. The ZmDLR8 mutation (dlr8-1) activates DNA damage responses and leads to increased DNA damage, which is exacerbated upon treatment with a DNA-damaging agent, indicating a role of ZmDLR8 in DNA damage repair. Furthermore, the dlr8-1 mutation also reduces plant height and kernel size, resulting in decreased grain yield. In summary, our results indicate that ZmDLR8 is involved in DNA damage repair to maintain genome stability and promote LR formation, and that dysfunction of ZmDLR8 compromises cell proliferation, thus impairing LR development and ultimately affecting maize yield.
