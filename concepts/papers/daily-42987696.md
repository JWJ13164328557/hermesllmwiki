# pGpp emerges as a novel alarmone in Bradyrhizobium diazoefficiens.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Microbiological research (2027)
- **PMID**: [42987696](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42987696/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.micres.2026.128809](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micres.2026.128809)
- **第一作者**: Delfina Colla
- **作者**: Delfina Colla, Ornella Falduti, Antonio Lagares, Abril Luchetti, Esteban T Iturralde, Lucas G Castellani, Ezequiel G Mogro, Nicole Paczia

## 摘要
The stringent response is a key mechanism of bacterial stress adaptation and plays an important role in symbiosis. Notably, environmental conditions that trigger the stringent response also enhance rhizobial infectivity and symbiotic efficiency in soybean. The role of (pp)pGpp in Bradyrhizobium diazoefficiens was investigated during carbon and nitrogen starvation, as well as its contribution to symbiosis with soybean plants, combining multi-omics approaches, genetic mutants, alarmone quantification, and symbiotic assays. Under nutrient limitation, the wild-type strain USDA 110 displayed a pleiotropic phenotype characterized by decreased ppGpp levels and accumulation of pGpp, a small alarmone described here for the first time in rhizobia. Our data suggests that pGpp accumulation is associated with RSH activity and is consistent with a possible conversion of ppGpp to pGpp, indicating a dynamic balance between these alarmones. These findings suggest a potential role for pGpp in stress adaptation and symbiotic performance, providing new insights into its possible functions.
