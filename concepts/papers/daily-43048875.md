# Cyanobacterial Films via Biomass Deconstruction and Supramolecular Reassembly for Multifunctional Agromaterials.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: ACS applied materials & interfaces (2026)
- **PMID**: [43048875](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43048875/)
- **DOI**: [10.1021/acsami.6c12130](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.6c12130)
- **第一作者**: Jaya Baranwal
- **作者**: Jaya Baranwal, Jonatan Niño-Sanchez, Danila Merino

## 摘要
The extensive reliance on plastics, fertilizers, and antimicrobial agents in agriculture underscores the need for integrated, sustainable material platforms that simultaneously deliver structural, nutritional, and protective functions. Here, we transform Spirulina (SP) biomass into fully biobased, multifunctional agro-bioplastics through a biomass deconstruction-reassembly strategy. Controlled alkaline hydrolysis deconstructs SP into functional macromolecular building blocks, which are reassembled into cohesive films using a natural deep eutectic solvent (NADES; choline chloride-urea) and ε-poly-l-lysine (PL), enabling programmable mechanical and physicochemical properties. The regenerated films exhibit negligible water solubility, rapid soil biodegradation (∼80% mass loss in 20 days), and potent antimicrobial activity against Escherichia coli and Fusarium oxysporum. Beyond protection, the materials significantly enhance tomato seedling growth by up to 5.5-fold increase compared to untreated controls, demonstrating their capability as solid-state growth-promoting platforms. Mechanistically, PL imparts antimicrobial activity while plasticizing the polymer network, whereas the NADES modulates polymer mobility and provides intrinsic fertilizer functionality through urea and choline chloride components. This work establishes cyanobacterial biomass as a regenerative feedstock for solid-state agro-materials that unify fertilization, pathogen protection, and growth stimulation within a single biodegradable platform, offering a blueprint for circular and regenerative agricultural materials.
