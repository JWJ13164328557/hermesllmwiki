### 核心发现
1. As an intermediate phenotype connecting genotype and target traits, the heritability of gene expression enables its analysis via quantitative trait locus (QTL) mapping, similar to conventional trait phenotypes, thereby leading to the identiﬁcation of expression quantitative trait loci (eQTL) (3, 4).
2. Blood serves as the central medium for substance transport in animals, through which various hormones, nutrients, and signaling molecules reach their target organs to coordinate growth, development, and physiological functions (11).
3. In summary, the breed diﬀerence in eQTL discovery is primarily attributable to diﬀerences in LD architecture rather than technical artifacts or statistical false positives.
4. First, as discussed above, diﬀerences in LD architecture play a key role: higher LD in Xinjiang brown cattle causes a single causal variant to drag many tagging SNPs into signiﬁcance, inﬂating eQTL counts on certain chromosomes, while the lower LD in Chinese Simmental cattle yields sparser but more reﬁned signals.
5. Collectively, these genes may indirectly contribute to the regulation of lactation traits via hormone signal transduction, mammary development, and immune-metabolic regulation.
6. In summary, leveraging the accessibility of blood tissue and its representation of systemic regulatory information, we performed a genome-wide screening of candidate genes potentially associated with lactation.
7. This study provides a valuable foundation for further screening of candidate genes related to milk production traits and oﬀers an important theoretical basis for the breeding of milk production traits in Xinjiang brown cattle and Chinese Simmental cattle.
8. Notably, some of these animals were purebred and expanded at the Hutubi Cattle Farm in Xinjiang (2).
