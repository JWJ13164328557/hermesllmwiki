### 核心发现
1. We conclude by outlining how artificial intelligence may help integrate the data into diagnostic algorithms and identifying areas requiring further investigation to advance these diagnostics into routine clini- cal practice.
2. Each modal- ity targets distinct aspects of tumor biology: genomics reveals mutational landscapes; methylomics captures epi- genetic reprogramming; proteomics quantifies tumor and tumor microenvironment-derived proteins; phosphoprot- eomics identifies active signaling states; glycoproteomics detects differentially glycosylated proteins; metabolomics reveals metabolic dysregulation; and lipidomics 
3. Taken together, cytology, FISH, and CA 19-9 form the core of current diagnostic practice, but none reliably resolves the central clinical problem in pCCA, that is accurately dis- tinguishing malignancy from benign inflammatory/obstruc- tive strictures (especially in PSC-associated disease) in a minimally invasive manner.
4. These findings suggest CLU and POSTN may seamlessly integrate into cur- rent diagnostic algorithms involving CA19-9 to enhance the specificity and sensitivity of the test.
5. Collectively, proteomics offer a functional readout of pCCA biology that can overcome low tumor cellularity and sampling constraints that limit current clinical assays, par- ticularly by enabling detection of tumor-associated molecu- lar changes in accessible samples, such as bile and serum.
6. In summary, metabolomic and lipidomic profiling high- lights that pCCA arises within a bile acid rich, fibroinflam- matory niche in which bile composition is highly dynamic and affected by cholestasis, inflammation, and malignancy.
7. Collectively, these studies provide proof-of-concept that AI can identify disease-specific signatures from high-dimensional datasets, integrate orthogonal omics layers, and classify tumors with high accuracy, supporting its emerging role as a critical com- putational framework for pCCA diagnosis.
8. This diagnostic ambiguity can significantly impact clinical outcomes as timely detection and accurate classifica- tion are critical for referral and curative-intent treatment.
