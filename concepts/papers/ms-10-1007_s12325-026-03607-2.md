### 核心发现
1. Overall, these data suggest that all individuals with phenylketonuria should be considered for treatment with sepiapterin to assess potential benefits.
2. Collectively, the evi­ dence supports sepiapterin as a well-tolerated therapy that can be efficacious in individuals with diverse PKU genotypes and phenotypes, including individuals with classic PKU and those considered to be nonresponsive to ­BH4.
3. Collectively, these complementary dual mecha­ nisms lead to a greater proportion of individuals who are responsive to sepiapterin versus existing oral therapy, typically with a greater magnitude in the reduction of blood Phe levels, including in some ­BH4-nonresponsive individuals with more severe disease.
4. This lowered mean Phe levels to within target guidelines (≤ 360 µmol/L) [9, 10, 34].
