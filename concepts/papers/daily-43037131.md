# Melatonin and Selenium Nanoparticles Alleviate Salt Stress by Modulating MAPK-Signaling-Related Transcriptional Changes, Hormonal Responses, and Lipid Metabolism.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant physiology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43037131](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43037131/)
- **DOI**: [10.1093/plphys/kiag856](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag856)
- **第一作者**: Shahid Ali
- **作者**: Shahid Ali, Jia Meng, Muhammad Faheem Jan, Yu Xin Chi, Muhammad Ali Shah, Haseeb Ahmad, Xun Bo Zhou

## 摘要
Salt stress is a major constraint on soybean (Glycine max L.) productivity, largely through disruption of redox balance, hormone signaling, and membrane homeostasis. This study investigated the protective role of melatonin (75 µM), selenium nanoparticles (SeNPs; 50 mg/L), and their combined application in soybean seedlings subjected to 160 mM NaCl stress. Salt exposure significantly reduced shoot length, biomass accumulation, and photosynthetic pigment content, while increasing hydrogen peroxide and malondialdehyde accumulation. Melatonin and SeNP treatments alleviated salt-induced damage, with the combined treatment showing the greatest improvement in growth, chlorophyll content, and oxidative stress mitigation. Integrated transcriptomic and metabolomic analyses revealed that salinity altered MAPK signaling-related gene expression, disrupted phytohormone homeostasis, and induced extensive lipid metabolic remodeling. Salt stress increased abscisic acid accumulation (30-fold), altered indole-3-acetic acid and jasmonate metabolism, and modified membrane lipid composition, including phosphatidic acid accumulation. Combined melatonin and SeNP treatments partially restored hormone balance, reduced ABA accumulation by 43-52%, promoted jasmonate-isoleucine accumulation, and shifted lipid profiles toward control-like patterns. Expression analysis further confirmed the regulation of key genes associated with MAPK signaling, hormone pathways, and lipid metabolism. Together, these findings demonstrate that melatonin and SeNPs enhance soybean salt tolerance through coordinated regulation of antioxidant defense, hormone signaling, and membrane lipid remodeling. Their combined application provides a promising approach for improving salinity resilience by modulating interconnected molecular and physiological responses.
