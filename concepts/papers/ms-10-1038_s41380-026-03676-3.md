### 核心发现
1. FinnGen plays a key role in this analysis by providing a large, well-ascertained national cohort with long follow-up and registry-based diagnoses, yielding PAF estimates that are both precise and broadly consistent across the cohort.
2. These data suggest that this genetic proxy marker can help identify potential C9-HRE carriers without labor-intensive testing in large population-based cohorts, such as GWAS, where complex genetic alterations, such as large repeat expansions or deletions cannot directly be detected.
3. These ﬁndings may provide novel biomarker and therapeutic target candidates and underline potential mechanistic pathways in ALS.
4. Importantly, the FinnGen cohort structure favors older individuals and hospital‑derived samples, increasing representation of brain disorders, such as AD and idiopathic normal pressure hydrocephalus (iNPH), a disorder frequently accompanied by AD‑like pathological features.
5. These variants can provide critical insights into underlying disease mechanisms, therapeutic targets, and biomarker discovery.
