### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these results suggest that ARID1A directly regulates SCIN.
2. Collectively, these findings suggest that ARID1A directly regulates SCIN transcription, which influence mucin secretion and thereby affect the development of SRCC.
3. And mucin secretion is impaired by the downregulation of Scin, a direct target of Arid1a in SRCC.
4. Here, we report that AT-rich interactive domain-containing protein 1A (ARID1A), one of the most frequently mutated genes in SRCC, is a bona fide tumor suppressor gene in gastric SRCC.
