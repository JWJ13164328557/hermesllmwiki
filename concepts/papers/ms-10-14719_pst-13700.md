### 核心发现
1. Bacterial endophytes play a pivotal role in promoting plant growth and development.
2. Indole-3-acetic acid IAA is a major plant hormone that plays a key role in plant growth and development.
3. Beneficial roles of endophytes Fertile soil and a stable climate are essential for agriculture.
4. Twitching motility is essential for endophytic rice colonization by the N₂-fixing endophyte Azoarcus sp.
