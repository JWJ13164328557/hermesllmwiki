### 核心发现
1. In conclusion, we report for the first time the neuropathologic and 56 biochemical properties of PrP in the retina of individuals carrying F198S and compare them with those in the 57 brain of the same individuals.
2. Thus, our data show that PrP in F198S retina is composed of full- 348 length, detergent-insoluble glycosylated PrP with a glycosylation pattern distinct from that of control retina.
