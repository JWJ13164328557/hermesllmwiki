# Light-Signalling Regulators GmSTF3/4 Enhance Soybean Susceptibility to Cyst Nematodes via Activation of Sugar Transporters.

## 论文信息

- **PMID**: [40898604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40898604/)
- **DOI**: [10.1111/pbi.70349](https://doi.org/10.1111/pbi.70349)

## 摘要
Soybean cyst nematode (SCN; Heterodera glycines Ichinohe) is a plant-parasitic nematode that causes substantial yield losses in soybean production. Light signalling is a critical environmental factor that influences photomorphogenesis and carbohydrate metabolism. However, its transcriptional regulation under pathogen-induced stress remains unclear. In this study, the biological function and regulatory mechanism of TGACG-motif binding factor 3/4 (GmSTF3/4), a shoot-to-root mobile protein in soybean (Glycine max), were investigated during H. glycines infection. Evidence was provided that light signalling modulated soybean susceptibility to cyst nematode, marked by light-enhanced nematode infection and upregulation of photoreceptor gene expression post-infection. GmSTF3/4 interacted with CONSTITUTIVE PHOTOMORPHOGENIC1a (GmCOP1a) and mediated its degradation. The nematode was identified to accelerate the shoot-to-root translocation of GmSTF3/4. Phenotypic analysis revealed that GmSTF3/4 promoted nematode development, whereas GmCOP1a exerted an antagonistic effect. Furthermore, the integrated analyses of Cleavage Under Targets and Tagmentation (CUT&Tag) and RNA sequencing (RNA-seq) indicated that GmSTF3/4 bound to the promoters of multiple sugar transporter genes, including GmSWEET8, GmSWEET10b, GmSWEET13d, GmSUC8, and GmERD6-like. The subcellular localization confirmed their plasma membrane targeting, and functional validation in yeast demonstrated the sucrose transport activity of four of these genes. Transient expression assays of five candidate genes during nematode infection supported the positive regulatory role of GmSWEET10b in facilitating nematode infection and development, a result further supported by the Gmsweet10b mutant. Collectively, this study revealed that GmSTF3/4 enhanced soybean susceptibility to cyst nematodes by transcriptionally activating sugar transporter genes, offering a new avenue for SCN-resistance research.

### 核心发现
1. Collectively, these results suggest that GmSTF3/4 positively modulates soybean cyst nematode development.
2. Collectively, these results suggest that GmSWEET10b functions as a dual sucrose and hexose transporter, whereas GmSWEET8, GmSWEET13d, and GmSUC8 are specific to sucrose transport.
3. Taken together, these data suggest that soybean employs GmCOP1a to counteract GmSTF3/4‐mediated susceptibility through its degradation mechanism.
4. Interestingly, both overexpression and silencing of GmSWEET8 enhanced resistance to nematode infection without affecting developmental progression (Figure ).
5. Notably, GmSWEET10b exhibited significant susceptibility to cyst nematodes, suggesting that it may serve as a promising target for improving SCN resistance in soybean.
6. Unlike light‐enhanced plant resistance to pathogens in leaf tissues, we found that light promotes soybean susceptibility to the SCN, including facilitating infection and development.
7. In this study, we reveal that soybean employs GmCOP1a to counteract GmSTF3/4‐mediated susceptibility through its degradation mechanism, demonstrating a functionally conserved role of GmCOP1a in nematode stress.
8. Furthermore, the integrated analyses of Cleavage Under Targets and Tagmentation (CUT&Tag) and RNA sequencing (RNA‐seq) indicated that GmSTF3/4 bound to the promoters of multiple sugar transporter genes, including GmSWEET8, GmSWEET10b, GmSWEET13d, GmSUC8, and GmERD6‐like.
