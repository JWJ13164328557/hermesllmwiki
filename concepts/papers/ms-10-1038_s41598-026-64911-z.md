### 核心发现
1. The JAZ domain is not only a key region for specific binding to the COI1 receptor, but also a major target for ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of JAZ proteins upon activation of JA signaling.
2. Collectively, these results enhance our understanding of the evolutionary mechanisms and functional potential of the maize JAZ gene family under drought stress and provide a valuable genomic resource for future functional characterization and molecular breeding of drought-tolerant maize varieties.
3. These results show that all 26 maize genomes of ZmJAZ19 contained the conserved motifs Motif 1–4, while some members of other conserved motifs were not complete.
4. The C-terminal JAZ domain is highly conserved and is often referred to as the ‘JAs’ domain, which is essential for the regulation of JAZ proteins.
