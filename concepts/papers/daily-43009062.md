# An ER-retained misfolded brassinosteroid receptor triggers selective transcriptional reprogramming with distinct roles of ERdj3B and PDI10 in Arabidopsis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Plant science : an international journal of experimental plant biology (2026)
- **PMID**: [43009062](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43009062/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113588](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plantsci.2026.113588)
- **第一作者**: Limeng Yuan
- **作者**: Limeng Yuan, Zexuan Wu, Jiayi Luo, Dianxiang Xing, Peiqi Huang, Jishu Wei, Mengxu Yang, Juan Mao

## 摘要
The endoplasmic reticulum (ER) is the central compartment for protein folding. Most studies of ER stress rely on chemical inducers, which cause widespread off-target effects. Thus, how plant cells respond specifically to ER-retained misfolded proteins remains poorly defined. Here, we overexpressed a kinase-dead, ER-retained misfolded brassinosteroid receptor, bri1-5K911E, as a model to dissect these responses without activating BR signaling. Transcriptome profiling identified 2019 differentially expressed genes. Although our data do not exclude activation of other UPR subpathways, this misfolded receptor induced a specialized transcriptional program characterized by upregulation of specific ER quality control (ERQC) and ER-associated degradation (ERAD) components and downregulation of a subset of canonical HSP chaperones. Based on BIP3 transcript levels and undetectable TIN1 protein, we observed no detectable activation of the IRE1-bZIP60 branch, and our data do not provide evidence for induction of a broad canonical unfolded protein response (UPR). Among the prominently induced factors, the ERQC components ERdj3B and PDI10 were selected for functional validation. Loss of ERdj3B severely exacerbated the bri1-5 dwarf phenotype, whereas PDI10 mutation enhanced tolerance to ER chemical stress but increased salt sensitivity. These distinct phenotypes point to divergent physiological roles for ERdj3B and PDI10 in plant growth and stress responses. Collectively, our findings demonstrate that ER-accumulated misfolded proteins trigger a specific, non-canonical transcriptional response. They also reveal divergent and context-dependent physiological functions of ERdj3B and PDI10 in maintaining ER proteostasis. These findings provide novel mechanistic insights into plant ER protein quality control.
