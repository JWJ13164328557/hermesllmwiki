# Experimental context shapes interpretation of immune outputs in Arabidopsis.

- **作者**: Alba Moreno-Pérez, Hanxu Sha, Gitta Coaker

## 摘要
Pattern recognition receptors (PRRs) activate plant immunity through recognition of extracellular immunogenic patterns, including microbe-associated molecular patterns (MAMPs). PRR activation is commonly assessed using different experimental systems, including sterile-grown seedlings and soil-grown rosettes. We systematically compared Arabidopsis PRR immune outputs triggered by the bacterial MAMPs elf18 and flg22 across a range of concentrations in seedlings and rosettes using assays for reactive oxygen species (ROS), cytosolic calcium influx, mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) activation, and seedling growth inhibition (SGI). Rosettes exhibited greater sensitivity than seedlings in ROS and MAPK assays, whereas calcium responses measured using the Aeqcyt/pMAQ2 reporter were stronger in seedlings, correlating with reduced reporter transcript accumulation in rosette tissue. Despite flg22 inducing stronger early signaling outputs, SGI assays showed greater sensitivity to elf18. These findings demonstrate that canonical PRR-mediated immune outputs are differentially sensitive to experimental context and should not be interpreted as interchangeable measures of immune activation. This study provides a framework for selecting appropriate assay systems and MAMP concentrations in plant immunity studies.
