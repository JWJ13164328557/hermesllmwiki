### 核心发现
1. Several studies suggest that changes in gene expression linked to environmental variability may play a crucial role in adaptive evolution [23], based on observed genotype-by-environment (GxE) interactions in the expression level of specific genes associated with fitness in a given environment.
2. Importantly, origin-specific methylation marks persist even under identical laboratory cultivation conditions, suggesting transgenerational epigenetic priming in kelp.
