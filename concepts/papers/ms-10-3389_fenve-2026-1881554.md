### 核心发现
1. These results confirm that system behavior cannot be represented by a single operating point or steady-state assumption.
2. Instead, sequential ambient condition-driven simulations are required to capture path-dependent behavior (Young et al., 2021; Sendi et al., 2022).
3. Importantly, the CO2 loading at the end of adsorption does not exactly match the loading at the start of the subsequent vacuum stage (t = 0), indicating that the system does not fully return to an identical state after a single cycle.
