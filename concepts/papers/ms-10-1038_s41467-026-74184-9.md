### 核心发现
1. In summary, our findings support a model in which Corylin binds ESR1 to induce Sirt3 expression, thereby enhancing mitochondrial resilience and antioxidant defenses in a sex-dependent manner and contributing to longevity benefits observed in females.
2. In summary, these findings suggest that Corylin promotes a molecular state consistent with enhanced mitochondrial metabolic capacity, potentially through SIRT3- associated deacetylation of key enzymes.
3. We found that mid-life administration of Corylin, a flavonoid derived from Psoralea corylifolia, improves metabolic function, muscle integrity, and physical performance in mice maintained on a standard diet.
4. Here, we show that integrated multi-omics analyses across multiple tissues reveal coordinated age-associated molecular changes modulated by Corylin.
5. Here, we show that Corylin is a candidate geroprotective intervention and uses it as a tool to probe sex-dependent molecular responses.
6. Our findings suggest that Corylin did not affect appetite or feeding behavior in aged mice, and thus the observed lifespan extension is unlikely to result from an aversive response to the supplemented diet.
