### 核心发现
1. These results suggest that the application of ZnO-NPs at appropriate concentrations favored biomass allocation and overall plant development.
2. These results suggest that the application of ZnO-NPs can influence the morpho­ logical development, physiology, and vigour of Paliavana sericiflora, optimizing the growth and productivity of the species.
3. These findings suggest that plant performance was more strongly associated with nutritional balance and nutrient-use efficiency than with foliar zinc concentration alone.
4. These results suggest that the application of ZnO-NPs in adequate concentrations can influence the growth and development of Paliavana ser­ iciflora, optimizing the plant’s characteristics for propaga­ tion and field management purposes.
5. In conclusion, this research demonstrated the successful synthesis and incorporation of zinc oxide nanoparticles (ZnO- NPs) into filaments to produce microcontainers.
6. However, these mechanisms were not investigated in the present study, and further studies are required to clarify the factors responsible for the observed differences among treatments.
7. Leaf size is crucial for the survival of Paliavana serici­ flora of hardening off.
8. However, it is crucial to consider that excessive use of ZnO-NPs can lead to toxicity.
