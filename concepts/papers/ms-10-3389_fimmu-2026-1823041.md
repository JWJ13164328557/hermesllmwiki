### 核心发现
1. Checkpoint blockade interacts with organ-speciﬁc immune architectures that can condition the quality and trajectory of the immune response (12, 20, 26).
2. Thus, although not all steatotic or inﬂamed HCC will undergo HPD after checkpoint blockade, these results show that metabolic liver immune states can impair antitumor immunity and checkpoint responsiveness (12, 50–52).
3. Importantly, these organ states are not mutually exclusive as a single tumor-bearing organ can combine surveillance- prone, chronically inﬂamed, ﬁbrotic, myeloid-rich, or tolerogenic features.
4. Importantly, these features may exist indepen- dently of tumor mutational burden or PD-L1 expression, which remain widely used but incomplete biomarkers for ICI response prediction (72–74).
