### 核心发现
1. We found that at high tidal positions, where hydrodynamic stress was relatively weak, plants at patch centers had 15.6% higher LDMC and 13.3% lower SLA than those at patch edges.
2. Importantly, how these abiotic and biotic factors jointly shape plant traits across broad geographic gradients remains unclear.
