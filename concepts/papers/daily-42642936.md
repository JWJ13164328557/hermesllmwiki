# Identification of COSR1 and COSR2 as hypoosmotic-activated Ca2+-permeable channels that are required for submergence tolerance and post-drought recovery in Arabidopsis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Molecular plant (2026)
- **PMID**: [42642936](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42642936/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.molp.2026.08.019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molp.2026.08.019)
- **第一作者**: Zebin Liu
- **作者**: Zebin Liu, Qi Niu, Yuxin Dong, Menghao Xu, Jianghao Wu, Lei Gao, Changxin Feng, Yujia Sun

## 摘要
Drought recovery and submergence confer hypoosmotic stress that disrupts plant cellular homeostasis and compromises growth and survival. Although calcium signaling serves as a hub for plant adaptation to osmotic perturbations, the molecular mechanisms of hypoosmotic responses remain poorly understood. In this study, we identified COSR1 (calcium-permeable osmotic stress response 1) and its homolog, COSR2, as Ca2+-permeable channels activated by hypoosmotic stimuli. Genetic analyses showed that COSR1 plays a dominant role, with COSR2 serving a secondary compensatory role, as the cosr1 cosr2 double mutant exhibited substantially more severe phenotypes than either single mutant under post-drought recovery and submergence. Calcium imaging further demonstrated that COSR1 and COSR2 collectively mediate the second spike of hypoosmotic-induced Ca2+ influx, which is closely associated with TCH2/TCH4 induction and stress tolerance. Collectively, our work establishes COSR1 and COSR2 as crucial components of the hypoosmotic-responsive signaling machinery that translates post-drought recovery and submergence-induced osmotic stress into Ca2+ signals, providing potential targets for improving crop resilience.
