### 核心发现
1. These results confirm that PGR-free MS medium is suitable for routine in vitro propagation of C.
2. These results demonstrate that precise control of explant water content is critical for successful cryopreservation, and the D-cryoplate technique is an efficient and chemically safe strategy for the long-term preservation of elite C.
3. Notably, the D-cryoplate technique avoids ex- posure to vitrification solutions by inducing dehydra- tion through controlled airflow or silica gel.
4. As previously reported, the use of healthy stock plants combined with appropriate disinfection strategies is essential to minimize losses during culture initiation (Cassells 2001).
5. In plant tissues, the “vital water” fraction, typically 20–33%, comprises nonfreezing water bound to membranes and macromolecules and is essential for maintaining the structural stability and functional protein conformation (Barraco et al.
6. The optimal water contents identi- fied in this study fall within this physiological range, supporting the notion that the preservation of bound water is essential for successful cryopreservation.
