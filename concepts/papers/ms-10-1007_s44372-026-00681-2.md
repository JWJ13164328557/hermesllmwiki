### 核心发现
1. This study provides the first field assessment of proline content and chlorophyll concentration responses to foliar-applied humic acid (HA) and seaweed extract (SWE) under drought conditions in custard apple.
2. Humic substances, derived from the chemical and biological humification of plant and animal residues through microbial metabolism, function as multi-target bios­ timulants that mimic plant hormones, modulate signalling pathways, stimulate plasma- membrane transport systems, and enhance antioxidant enzyme activities, collectively improving root architecture, nutrient availability, stress tolerance,
3. This represents a critical knowledge gap, as understanding the physiological mecha­ nisms through which biostimulants enhance stress tolerance is essential for optimizing application protocols, predicting treatment efficacy under varying environmental condi­ tions, and developing cultivar-specific recommendations.
