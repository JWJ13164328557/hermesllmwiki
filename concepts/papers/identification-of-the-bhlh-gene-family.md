1. Taken together, these results suggest that DcbHLH2 and DcbHLH46 may be involved in JA-regulated regulate ﬂavonoid biosynthesis.
2. Here, we found that DcbHLH2 and DcbHLH46, which belonged to the III(d + e) subfamily, may be involved in ﬂavonoid biosynthesis based on results from the tran- scriptomic analysis of plants exposed to the inducer and UVB.
3. Furthermore, DcbHLH5 could bind to the promoters of ﬂavonoid biosynthesis genes and activate their expression.
4. Furthermore, DcbHLH5 could bind to the promoters and regulate the transcription of ﬂavonoid biosynthesis genes.
5. These results suggest that DcbHLHs in the subfamilies III(d + e) and IIIf may regulate ﬂavonoid biosynthesis in D.
