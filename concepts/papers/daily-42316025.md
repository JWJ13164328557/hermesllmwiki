# Integrated physiological and transcriptomic measurements reveal changes in the accumulation and partitioning of storage reserves after prevention of pollination in maize.

## 摘要
Reduced sink demand, such as that caused by grain or ear removal, is known to accelerate senescence in maize; nonetheless, how diminished sink strength mechanistically triggers senescence and disrupts grain development remains poorly understood. In particular, the transcriptional and metabolic regulatory networks that link reduced sink demand to the onset of senescence have not been clearly defined.

We grew the maize inbred line 08LF, which exhibits early senescence in response to grain removal, under non-pollinated (NONPOL) and normally pollinated (POL) conditions. We then collected phenotypic, physiological, and transcriptomic data from developing grains (6 to 24 days after silking) to gain insight into the molecular mechanisms responsible for the changes induced under NONPOL conditions. Pollination prevention induced early senescence, and major transcriptomic divergence between POL and NONPOL grains occurred between 14 and 18 DAS, indicating a key time window associated with the onset of senescence under reduced sink demand. Key pathways involved in starch, sucrose, and trehalose metabolism were disrupted in nonpollinated grains, and gene co-expression network analysis identified five co-expressed modules linked to energy metabolism, photosynthesis, and stress responses. These modules were correlated with changes in sugar accumulation and oxidative stress indices. Importantly, 20 hub genes, including genes encoding MYB transcription factors and heat shock proteins, were identified as potential regulators of the senescence process triggered by reduced sink demand. These genes may represent key components for dissecting the regulatory mechanisms that underlie senescence triggered by reduced sink demand in maize.

This study links reduced sink demand to coordinated metabolic and transcriptional changes associated with senescence, providing novel insights and genetic resources for understanding maize grain development under stress.

### 核心发现
1. These results suggest that zein synthesis increased sharply at 21 DAS, when 08LF grains entered the milky stage, and that this period was critical for zein accumulation.
2. Taken together, our RNA-seq analysis indicated that we had obtained sufficient transcriptome coverage for subsequent analysis of gene co-expression networks and also suggested that pollination prevention was associated with a genome-wide transcriptional change at around 14 DAS relative to pollinated plants.
3. These results suggest that trehalose biosynthesis genes may be involved in abnormal ovary development under NONPOL conditions.
4. In summary, expression of genes in the black, blue, brown, salmon, and yellow modules appeared to be most closely associated with physiological differences between NONPOL and POL grains.
5. These hub genes represent candidate regulatory genes that could serve as study targets for grain development to alter specific gene programs after different pollination treatments.
6. These findings suggest that proper regulation of zein biosynthesis is closely associated with coordinated reserve accumulation during grain development.
7. Importantly, 20 hub genes, including genes encoding MYB transcription factors and heat shock proteins, were identified as potential regulators of the senescence process triggered by reduced sink demand.
8. Notably, 6 DAS corresponded to approximately 0 days after pollination in POL plants, as artificial pollination was performed at 6 DAS.
