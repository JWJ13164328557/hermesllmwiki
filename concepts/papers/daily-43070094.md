# Comparative Functional Characterization of Plant Squalene Synthases and Application of Olive Squalene Synthase for Squalene Production in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Biotechnology and bioengineering (2026)
- **PMID**: [43070094](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43070094/)
- **DOI**: [10.1002/bit.70447](https://doi.org/10.1002/bit.70447)
- **第一作者**: Haiyang Chi
- **作者**: Haiyang Chi, Zhimin Li, Shucan Liu, Qiaoyun He, Xiai Yang, Xiushi Yang, Shitao Sun, Yanchun Deng

## 摘要
Numerous studies have focused on studying enzymes involved in squalene biosynthesis, yet comparative functional characterization of squalene synthases (SQSs) in squalene-rich plants, such as Olea europaea, Zea mays, and Ginkgo biloba, remains largely unexplored. Moreover, the strategic implementation of plant-derived SQS genes for optimized squalene biosynthesis in engineered microbial hosts remains poorly understood. In this study, we systematically investigated function of the three-plant homologous SQSs through genes clone, heterologous expression, and comprehensive enzymatic characterization, and explored their efficient utilization for squalene production in Saccharomyces cerevisiae through metabolic engineering. Following successful recombinant expression and purification of the three homologous proteins OeSQS1, ZmSQS, and GbSQS, in vitro enzyme activity analysis demonstrated OeSQS exhibiting relatively high enzyme activity-more than 1.5-fold higher than that of Z. mays and G. biloba counterparts. Remarkably, metabolic engineering strategies combining OeSQS1 overexpression with precursor pathway optimization ultimately enabled a squalene titer of 704 mg/L in shake-flask cultures, corresponding to a 478-fold enhancement relative to the parental BY4742 strain (1.47 mg/L). This study complemented previous findings on the industrial potential of plant-derived enzymes for the heterologous production of terpenoids in microbial hosts.
