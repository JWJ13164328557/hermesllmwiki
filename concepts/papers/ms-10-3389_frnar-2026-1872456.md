2. For the m6A modification specifically, m6A- Atlas v2.0 consolidated 797,091 high-confidence base-resolution m6A sites across multiple species, integrating single-cell profiles and providing writer-eraser-reader target association tools that facilitate direct functional hypothesis generation (Liang et al., 2024).
3. Expanding beyond modification site catalogues, RMBase v3.0 now integrates over 1,880 high-throughput datasets to annotate 73 distinct modification types across 62 species, with modules linking modification sites to RNA-binding protein interactomes, miRNA target overlaps, GWAS- derived disease variants, and histone modification co-occurrence patterns (Xuan et al., 2024).
4. CRISPR-CAS9 represent an extremely valuable and flexible technology to generate such models while adoption of high-fidelity Cas9 variants (e.g., HiFi Cas9), combined with clonal validation strategies, minimizes off-target effects (Vakulskas et al., 2018; Ortega-Batista et al., 2025).
5. Collectively, these genetic models remain indispensable reference frameworks for benchmarking computational modification callers and distinguishing true epitranscriptomic signals from technical noise.
