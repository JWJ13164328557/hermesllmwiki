# Resistance monitoring and molecular mechanisms of the rice false smut pathogen Ustilaginoidea virens to demethylation inhibitor fungicides in major rice-growing regions of China.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Pest management science (2026)
- **PMID**: [43035260](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43035260/)
- **DOI**: [10.1002/ps.71495](https://doi.org/10.1002/ps.71495)
- **第一作者**: Shiyun Zhang
- **作者**: Shiyun Zhang, Lindong Wang, Wenhua Wang, Guodong Zhang, Qingdong Yuan, Yang Yang, Jing Wang, Binnian Tian

## 摘要
BACKGROUND: Rice false smut (RFS), caused by Ustilaginoidea virens, is a devastating fungal disease in China. Demethylation inhibitor (DMI) fungicides are widely used for RFS control, yet nationwide resistance monitoring in U. virens is still lacking, and resistance to hexaconazole, epoxiconazole, and difenoconazole remains unexplored.

RESULTS: In this study, large-scale resistance monitoring of 2381 U. virens isolates collected from 48 regions across 17 provinces/municipalities in China's major rice-growing areas to four DMIs was performed. We identified three moderately and two low-level hexaconazole-resistant isolates (0.21%), three moderately and four low-level epoxiconazole-resistant isolates (0.29%), one low-level propiconazole-resistant isolate (0.04%), and two low-level difenoconazole-resistant isolates (0.08%). Jiangxi and Jilin exhibited relatively higher resistance frequencies, at 4.29% and 3.64%, respectively. Notably, no significant differences in mycelial growth, sporulation, or abiotic stress responses were observed between resistant and sensitive isolates. Except for positive cross-resistance between epoxiconazole and propiconazole, no cross-resistance was found among tested DMIs, pyraclostrobin and carbendazim. No mutations were detected in CYP51 or its promoter; however, CYP51 was significantly overexpressed in resistant isolates under hexaconazole or epoxiconazole treatment. Both resistance and CYP51 overexpression declined during subculturing without fungicide selection, indicating that the resistance phenotype is predominantly associated with CYP51 overexpression.

CONCLUSION: This first nationwide survey suggests that U. virens resistance to hexaconazole and epoxiconazole is mainly linked to induced CYP51 overexpression. Reducing application frequency and rotating with fungicides of different modes of action may effectively delay resistance development, particularly in high-risk regions. © 2026 Society of Chemical Industry.
