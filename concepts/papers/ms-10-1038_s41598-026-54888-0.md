1. Taken together, our data suggest a coordinated dual-mode metabolic response consistent with the principles of metabolic hormesis33 (Figure 2).
2. Our data show a significant downregulation of both pyroglutamic acid (FC = 0.2, −log10(q) = 3.2) (Supplementary Table S1, Figure 1, C) and glutamic acid, concomitant with an upregulation of glycine.
3. Located within the medial temporal lobe, this structure plays a crucial role in various cognitive and neurological functions, including memory formation, spatial navigation, emotional regulation, pattern separation, and both spatial and episodic memory3.
4. These newly generated neurons play a crucial role in replacing neural populations damaged by disease or injury, and they integrate into pre-existing neural circuits6.
5. Notably, the hippocampus retains the ability to generate new neurons throughout adulthood4,5.
