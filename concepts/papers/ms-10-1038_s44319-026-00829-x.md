### 核心发现
1. Here, we report that lactate-induced cholesterol accumulation activates mammalian target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1) signalling under hypoxic conditions, thereby promoting hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) progression.
2. Taken together, our results reveal a previously unrecognized mechanism through which HCC cells adapt to hypoxic conditions through the reprogramming of cholesterol metabolism.
