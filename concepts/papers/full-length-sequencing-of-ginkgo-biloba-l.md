### 核心发现
1. Our findings confirm the advantages of SMRT, which facilitated the construction a rich transcript data-set for research on the development of ginkgo seeds, enriching the annotation of the ginkgo genome, and enhancing our understanding of gene regulation of terpene biosynthesis in ginkgo seeds.
2. These results suggest that further research on the synthesis of terpenoids in ginkgo seeds would be worthwhile.
