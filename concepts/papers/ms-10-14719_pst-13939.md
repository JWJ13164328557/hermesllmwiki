### 核心发现
1. The ABA synthesised in the plant root and shoot system under drought is transported via xylem to guard cells, where it binds to a family of ABA receptors, i.e., pyrabactin resistance/PYR-like/regulatory component of ABA receptor (PYR/PYL/RCAR) (22).
2. 3. Further, ABA triggers cytosolic Ca2+ elevations by activating Ca2+- permeable plasma membrane and tonoplast-located channels, which activate Ca2+-dependent protein kinases (CDPKs) that phosphorylate SLAC1 and regulate guard cell ion fluxes (25, 26).
4. In summary, cell wall thickening and compositional changes, decline in intercellular airspace and chloroplast retraction are among the drought-induced modifications that constitute a coordinated, balanced structural strategy in plants, allowing them to conserve water while still carrying out some photosynthetic activity.
5. Without a doubt, possessing a carbon-concentrating mechanism enables C₄ plants to change the main limitation target under extreme drought conditions from CO₂ diffusion to biochemical and metabolic activities.
6. Further blue light, mainly through phototropins (PHOT1/ PHOT2), plays a crucial role in plant stomatal regulation by stomatal, mesophyll, chloroplast regulatory continuum.
7. Hence, an in-depth understanding of the cross-talk among stomatal, mesophyll and chloroplast processes is crucial for unravelling mechanisms of drought acclimation and improved crop WUE (5, 16).
8. As a result, photoprotective processes like NPQ and alternative electron sinks are required to release surplus energy and keep photosynthesis working properly (2, 3).
