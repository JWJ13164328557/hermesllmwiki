## 摘要
Canker is a destructive fungal disease that adversely affects plant growth, triggering leaf drop, branch dieback and even plant death. For the blueberry industry, fungal canker reduces blueberry yield and quality. In this study, diseased branches of the 'Bluegold' blueberry cultivar collected from open-field plantations in Jilin Province, China, were used to isolate and identify the pathogen, characterize its biological properties, and investigate the physiological and metabolomic responses of blueberry shoots to pathogen infection.
