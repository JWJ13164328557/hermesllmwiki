Circular RNAs can originate from one or more exons, from introns, from both exons and introns, or from intergenic regions. Most circRNAs are derived from one or more exons of protein-coding genes known as parental genes [2]. Furthermore, circRNAs are conserved and widely distributed in a variety of eukaryotic organisms, including mammals and plants [3].
