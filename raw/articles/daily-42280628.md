Rice depends on its root system to perceive drought, a major environmental constraint that leads to severe yield losses worldwide. To dissect the underlying molecular basis, we conducted a comparative analysis of drought-sensitive (WAB) and drought-tolerant (IR65) rice genotypes that exhibited divergent drought tolerance at the seedling stage. After exposure to 15% PEG6000 (-0.4 MPa) for two days, the shoot and root architectural traits of IR65 were better than those of WAB seedlings.
