# A fungal natural product that inhibits plant cellulose biosynthesis by disrupting cellulose synthase complexes.

Cellulose, a primary component of plant cell walls, is synthesized by cellulose synthase complexes (CSCs) at the plasma membrane. Targeting this process with cellulose biosynthesis inhibitors (CBIs) has significantly advanced our understanding of plant cell wall formation and provided valuable compounds for herbicide development. Here, we identified a fungal natural product, 8-methyldichlorodiaporthin (MDD), as a broad-spectrum plant CBI. Structure-activity relationship analyses demonstrate that methylation modifications on the isocoumarin ring and chlorination of the side chain are crucial for MDD-induced growth inhibition.
