# Integrating demographic characteristics, settling preference, and field investigation for resistance evaluation of four potato cultivars to Myzus persicae.

BACKGROUND: Myzus persicae is one of the most common potato pests. We evaluated the resistance of four potato (Solanum tubersum) cultivars (Evely Lucette, Burbank, Innovator, and Lucinda V7) to M. persicae by linking the age-stage two-sex life table, computer simulation, settling preference, and field investigation.
