# Optimizing Sowing Date and Nitrogen Management to Trade Off Yield and Nitrate Leaching in Maize-Soybean Intercropping Under CMIP6 Climate Scenarios in the North China Plain.

Climate change threatens nitrogen cycling in agricultural ecosystems. Optimizing sowing dates and nitrogen management for maize-soybean intercropping is critical for sustainable production in the North China Plain (NCP). Using a calibrated Agricultural Production Systems Simulator (APSIM) model driven by three representative global climate models (GCMs) selected from 20 Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6) GCMs, we evaluated management strategies under two Shared Socioeconomic Pathway scenarios (SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5) across three climatic zones for near-term (2030-2059) and long-term (2070-2099) periods.
