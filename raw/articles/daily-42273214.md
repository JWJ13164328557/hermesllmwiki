# Integrated transcriptomic analysis reveals transcriptional changes associated with begomovirus infection in the medicinal plant 

METHODS: To reveal gene expression changes and enriched biological pathways in response to begomovirus infection in the medicinal plant 

RESULTS: A total of 195 differentially expressed genes (DEGs) were detected, and enriched in pathways related to reactive oxygen species (ROS) scavenging, cell wall remodeling, hormone signaling, transcriptional regulation, as well as stress and immune related responses. Reverse transcription quantitative polymerase chain reaction (RT-qPCR) validations revealed that gene expression was group-dependent and tissue-specific.
