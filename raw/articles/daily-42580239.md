# Stage-specific optimization of light intensity enhances rice seedling quality and yield potential.

Light intensity critically determines rice seedling growth and yield, yet the stage-specific photoacclimation mechanisms and optimal light regimes remain unclear. We investigated optimal light intensity combinations across developmental stages to enhance seedling quality, post-transplant growth, and yield.
