# Biochar and zinc oxide nanoparticles partnership: a multi-faceted strategy to enhance rice productivity and remediate antimony polluted soils.

BACKGROUND: Antimony (Sb) is a toxic metalloid that poses a significant threat to living organisms and ecosystem sustainability. Biochar (BC) and zinc oxide nano-particles (ZnO-NPs) are promising amendments to mitigate heavy metals toxicity. However, the potential role of BC and ZnO-NPs in alleviating the Sb toxicity remains unexamined. To address this gap, the present study investigated how BC and ZnO-NPs mitigate Sb toxicity in rice.
