# Comparative radiosensitivity of soybean genotypes to electron beam and gamma ray irradiation for optimisation of GR

Mutation breeding provides a valuable tool for generating variability and enhancing agronomic traits in crop plants that are vital for food security. However, it demands precise implementation and thorough screening to promote higher mutation frequencies without excessive lethality.
