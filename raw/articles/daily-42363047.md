# Integrated physiological, biochemical, and transcriptomic analyses reveal lead-induces stress responses and tolerance of bread wheat (Triticum aestivum L.).

BACKGROUND: Lead (Pb) pollution is a severe environmental condition that inhibits the growth of plants by causing oxidative stress and interference with cellular and molecular functions. Genotypic variation in stress tolerance has been identified in wheat varieties (Triticum aestivum L.), but there has been limited evidence of integrated physiological, biochemical, and transcriptomic responses to Pb.

RESULTS: Pb exposure (0-3 mM lead acetate) had significant effect on shoot height and root length in all wheat varieties with root being more sensitive than shoot.
