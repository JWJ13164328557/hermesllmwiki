# Impacts of steam explosion treatment on qualities, protein network development and digestion-resistant properties of dried sorghum noodles.

Moderate steam explosion (SE) treatment (1.2 MPa, 60-120 s) significantly (p < 0.05) enhanced the physicochemical properties of sorghum-wheat dough, the quality and digestion-resistant properties of dried sorghum noodles (DSN). Compared with the untreated control, SE treatment increased the water absorption of the sorghum-wheat dough from 51.97% to 91.63%, and reduced the cooking loss of DSN from 8.73% to 6.77%, while also enhancing DSN's textural properties. Although SE treatment increased the free sulfhydryl content in DSN from 2.59 μmol/g to 3.18 μmol/g, decreased the glutenin macropolymer content from 1.61% to 1.39% and induced the depolymerization behavior of high-molecular-weight glutenin subunits, the starch-protein network structure became denser according to SEM and WSS results. These results confirm that SE treatment enhanced physical entanglement between gelatinized starch and denatured protein. Furthermore, moderate SE treatment significantly increased resistant starch content from 37.54% to 48.18% while decreasing rapidly digestible starch content from 37.30% to 28.67%.
