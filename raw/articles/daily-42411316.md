# Distinct Chemical Profiles and Gut Microbiota Modulation of Ancha Tea Compared with Green and Black Teas from the Same Cultivar.

Ancha tea (AT) is a type of dark tea processed through dew wetting. This study compared green tea (GT), black tea (BT), and AT, all derived from the same tea cultivar, to examine how processing methods influence chemical composition and health-promoting function. Nontargeted and targeted metabolomics revealed distinct chemical profiles among the three teas.
