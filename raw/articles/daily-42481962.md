# Effects of stage-specific plant growth regulators application on lodging resistance and yield formation in maize under high planting density.

In response to the growing issue of maize lodging caused by typhoons shifting northward into Northeast China, the rational application of plant growth regulators (PGRs) offers a strategy to safeguard maize yields and mitigate economic losses. To elucidate how PGRs applied at different growing stages affect lodging resistance and grain yield, a two-year field experiment (2019-2020) was conducted at the Gongzhuling Field Experimental Station, Jilin Province.
