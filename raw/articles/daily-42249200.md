# Impact of land uses on soil pollution in a part of eastern Indian Terai region.

Urbanization, faulty waste disposal, and improper management contribute to soil pollution in many Indian cities, while excessive fertilizer use contaminates the cropland and plantation soils. This study on soil pollution was conducted in the Jalpaiguri district of West Bengal, located in the eastern Terai region of India. This region features diverse land uses, including urban peri-urban areas (UPA), extensive croplands, tea gardens, and several forests.
