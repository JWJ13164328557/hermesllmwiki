# QTLs for heat-induced stomatal anatomy underpin gas exchange variation in field-grown wheat.

Stomata are central to leaf gas exchange, governing carbon uptake, water loss, and, ultimately, crop performance. However, how stomatal anatomy and physiology integrate to determine wheat heat tolerance under field conditions remains poorly understood. Here, we examined the contribution of stomatal anatomical and physiological traits in shaping wheat responses to heat stress.
