# Characterization of the blueberry AUXIN RESISTANT 1/LIKE-AUX1 gene family and regulatory roles of VcAUX1/LAX12 in flower bud burst.

Dormancy is a necessary stage for most perennial woody plants before flowering. This process largely determines the flowering time of plants. The regulation of auxin in flowering has always been a research hotspot and still remains controversial. As the influx carrier, the role of AUXIN RESISTANT 1/LIKE-AUX1 (AUX1/LAX) in blueberry flower bud burst remains unclear. This study identified a total of 23 VcAUX1/LAX family members in blueberry and performed a comprehensive bioinformatics analysis of them. In dormant flower buds, VcAUX1/LAX12 expression was significantly lower than that in germinating flower buds in both spring and autumn. Therefore, VcAUX1/LAX12 was selected as the key gene for functional characterization. Subcellular localization experiments confirmed that VcAUX1/LAX12 was localized at the cell membrane. In VcAUX1/LAX12-overexpressing (VcAUX1/LAX12-OE) blueberry spring new shoots with formed flower buds, the germination rate of flower buds was about 2.4-fold higher than that in the control group, and the total indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) content of flower buds was significantly higher than that in the control group. Correspondingly, the expression of VcLFY, VcSOC1, and VcFT was significantly upregulated in both flower buds and leaves, but VcFUL expression was significantly upregulated only in flower buds. The overexpression of VcAUX1/LAX12 delayed flower development in tobacco, accompanied by an increased total IAA content and the decreased expression of NtLFY, NtSOC1, NtFUL, NtAP1, NtFT4, and NtFT5 in flower buds and leaves. The overexpression of VcAUX1/LAX12 also inhibited vegetative growth, as manifested by the significant decrease in leaf length and width, plant height, and ground diameter. Furthermore, exogenous IAA delayed flower development in both WT and VcAUX1/LAX12-OE plants, accompanied by increased total IAA content and the downregulation of the aforementioned flowering-related genes. Exogenous 2,3,5-triiodobenzoic acid (TIBA) accelerated flower development in both WT and VcAUX1/LAX12-OE plants, along with the upregulation of these flowering-related genes. In summary, this study demonstrated that VcAUX1/LAX12 promoted blueberry flower bud burst but delayed flowering development in tobacco. These results provide a theoretical foundation for revealing the regulatory mechanism of VcAUX1/LAX12-mediated auxin transport in flower bud development.
