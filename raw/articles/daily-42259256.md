# Plant development: RBOH genes.

The roles of respiratory burst oxidase homolog (RBOH)-derived reactive oxygen species in plant development remain poorly documented. Evidence from the liverwort Marchantia polymorpha demonstrates crucial requirements in cuticle-cell wall integrity and morphogenesis, a likely key trait in land plant evolution.
