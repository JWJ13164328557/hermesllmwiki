# From byproduct to bioresource: Antioxidant potential, flavor profile, and microbiological quality of recovered water from freeze-dried and rotary evaporation dried Muscat grapes.

Grape drying generates substantial condensate (recovered water) containing soluble aroma and bioactive compounds, offering potential for value-added reutilization. This study characterized the antioxidant capacity, volatile profile, and microbiological quality of the water recovered during freeze-drying (FD) and rotary evaporation drying (RED) of Muscat grapes. Both condensates retained measurable grape-derived bioactives and volatiles (e.g., linalool, squalene).
