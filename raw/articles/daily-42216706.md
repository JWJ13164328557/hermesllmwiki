# Optimizing Spinach Shelf Life and Nutritional Quality Using Targeted LED Light Treatments.

Postharvest lighting can modify deterioration rates and nutritional profiles of leafy greens during cold storage. This study evaluated the effects of narrow green light-emitting diode (LED) spectra on spinach quality during 18 days of storage at 4°C and 98% relative humidity.
