# A Thermo-Sensitive OsMYC2 Allele Coordinates Spikelet Development With Temperature via Proteasome-Mediated Protein Stability.

Elevated temperature disrupts spikelet development and threatens rice yields; however, the underlying molecular mechanisms are not completely elucidated. Although the core jasmonic acid (JA) signalling transcription factor OsMYC2 is essential for spikelet development, it remains unclear whether and how OsMYC2 mediates temperature-responsive regulation of this process. Here, we identified a thermo-sensitive panicle defect mutant tspd4, which exhibits various spikelet abnormalities under high temperature (HT) that are largely reversed under low temperature (LT).
