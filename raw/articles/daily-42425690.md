# Interactive effects of temperature and water activity on growth, mycotoxin production, and transcriptomic profiles of Aspergillus flavus, Aspergillus parasiticus, and Fusarium verticillioides.

Maize is frequently contaminated with aflatoxins (AFs) and fumonisins (FBs), posing a significant global food safety threat.
