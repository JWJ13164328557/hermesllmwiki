# Layered Nitrogen Fertilization Enhances Wheat Productivity by Reshaping Rhizosphere Processes via Root-Metabolite-Bacteria Coupling.

Layered nitrogen (N) fertilization has been proposed to improve wheat productivity, yet its rhizosphere mechanisms remain unclear. In a four-year field experiment, we compared layered fertilization (N1-2-1), surface application (N8), and no N input (NCK). Layered fertilization increased grain yield, anthesis-stage N accumulation, root biomass, and soil nutrient availability relative to N8.
