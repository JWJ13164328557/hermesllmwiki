# Functional Characterization of Wheat TaAPC11-5B in Regulating Grain Size and Weight.

The anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) is a conserved multisubunit Cullin-RING-type E3 ubiquitin ligase complex. It regulates mitotic progression by ubiquitinating key cell cycle regulators, thereby modulating plant growth and development. In this study, the RING domain-containing subunit TaAPC11-5B of the APC/C E3 ligase was functionally characterized as a critical regulator of wheat grain development.
