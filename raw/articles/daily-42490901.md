# Foliar biostimulant boost physiological resilience and reproductive growth, increasing cotton yield.

INTRODUCTION: Cotton is a crop of major economic importance, often cultivated under environmental conditions that expose plants to abiotic stresses such as heat and water deficit, which negatively affect physiological performance and yield. Biostimulants have emerged as promising tools to enhance plant resilience and productivity under such conditions.
