# Integrative Insights Into UV-B-Induced Growth Regulation, Physiological Responses, Antioxidants, and Essential Oil Remodeling in Ocimum americanum L.

Ocimum americanum L. (American basil or lime basil), an aromatic annual herb of the Lamiaceae family, is widely recognized for its medicinal properties. Traditionally, it has been used in treating cough, respiratory disorders, rheumatism, and renal complications. In the context of global climate change and stratospheric ozone depletion, the increase in ultraviolet B (UV-B) radiation poses a significant threat to plant health and productivity.
