# The Biosynthesis and Functions of Flavonoids: Recent Advances From Studies Across Land Plant Diversity.

Over the last decade there have been significant advances in genome sequencing and model species development for ferns, lycophytes, and the bryophyte lineages-mosses, liverworts, and hornworts.
