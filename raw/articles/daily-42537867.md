# Rice husk-derived nanosilica alleviates aluminum accumulation and toxicity in Brassica napus by regulating cellular metabolism and antioxidant defense.

Aluminum (Al) toxicity poses a serious threat to agricultural crops and impedes plant growth in acidic soils.
