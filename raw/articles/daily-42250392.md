# The narrow-spectrum circadian rhythm promotes the photosynthesis and pigment accumulation of celery within two days (light/dark: 12 h/12 h).

Light is both the primary energy source for photosynthesis and a key regulator for circadian rhythms, influencing the comprehensive quality and the production cycle of plants. Light quality is a critical parameter of light parameters, and the response patterns of photosynthesis and circadian rhythm to different light qualities remain to be explored. Here, white (broad-spectrum), red, blue and purple lights were each applied to celery for two days (light/dark: 12 h/12 h), and stomatal openness, photosynthetic parameters, photosynthetic pigments contents, and the expression levels of photoreceptor-related genes were measured.
