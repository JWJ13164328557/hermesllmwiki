# Isolation, structural elucidation, and antioxidant potential of phytomelatonin from tomato (

Melatonin (N-acetyl-5-methoxytryptamine) is an indoleamine compound present in both animal and plant kingdoms. Melatonin, a potent antioxidant, is currently produced synthetically, which is often associated with the presence of toxic by-products.
