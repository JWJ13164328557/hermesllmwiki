# Microbial community assembly and functional potential response driven by soil phosphorus gradients.

Understanding how long-term phosphorus (P) fertilization reshapes soil microbiomes and their functional interplay with P cycling is crucial for sustainable agriculture. This study investigated the effects of different P levels on soil physicochemical properties, phosphatase activities, and the bacterial community across three geographically distinct tobacco-growing sites (YD, SH, and ZS) in Fujian Province, China.
