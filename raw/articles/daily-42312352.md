# Systematic Revealing of Plant-Derived Nerve Agent Exposure Markers by In-Source Fragmentation-Enabled HRMS Nontargeted Screening.

Identifying diverse, uncharacterized plant-derived nerve agent adducts is a formidable challenge for environmental exposure confirmation and forensic attribution.
