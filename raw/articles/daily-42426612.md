# Intraspecific variation, trait coordination and resource-use strategies of leaf functional traits in Suzhou tea landraces (Camellia sinensis).

BACKGROUND: Understanding intraspecific variation in leaf functional traits is essential for revealing plant resource-use strategies and adaptive mechanisms.
