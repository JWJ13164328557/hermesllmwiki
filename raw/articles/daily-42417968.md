# Low Sorption and High Desorption of Pretilachlor Metabolites Indicated Greater Mobility Potential than Parent Compound.

Pretilachlor herbicide is extensively used in rice cultivation. In open environment, it degrades into different metabolites. Information on the environmental mobility of these metabolites is limited. This study aimed to assess the potential movement risks associated with two principal metabolites of pretilachlor. To achieve the objectives, the sorption-desorption behaviour and the thermodynamic properties of pretilachlor (P), metabolite 2',6'-diethyl-N-(2-hydroxyethyl)aniline (M1) and 2-chloro-2',6'-diethylacetanilide (M2) were studied in different soils governing their mobility.
