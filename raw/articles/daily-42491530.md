# Biochar co-applied with silicon mitigates combined chromium and salinity stress in barley by upregulating antioxidant defense, hormonal balance, and nutrient homeostasis.

Barley was grown for 150 days under controlled greenhouse conditions in pots containing 8 kg of soil. This study aimed to investigate the effects of co-applied biochar (BC) and silicon (Si) in mitigating combined chromium (Cr) and salinity stress (SS). Biochar was produced from sugarcane husks pyrolyzed at 500 °C and applied at 2% (w/w). Silicon (as sodium silicate) was applied at 150 mg kg
