# Multi-generational effects of salicylic acid on Lipaphis erysimi and induced defense in Brassica juncea.

Turnip aphid, Lipaphis erysimi Kalt. (Hemiptera: Aphididae), is an insecticide-resistant pest of Brassica spp. SA-salicylic acid, a hormonal regulator of systemic acquired resistance in plants, might prove to be a better option against resistant pests. Therefore, SA was applied exogenously in the four treatments with the concentrations of 0, 0.5, and 1 mM 24-hours before infesting the Brassica juncea (Chinese leaf mustard) plants with L. erysimi.
