# Exogenous Pyrroloquinoline Quinone Application Enhances Low-Phosphorus Adaptation in Rice by Modulating Redox Homeostasis Under Variable Light.

Phosphorus (P) bioavailability in soil is often limited, restricting plant growth. Pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ) is a natural quinone compound with potent redox-cycling capabilities. In this study, we investigated the physiological effects of exogenously applied PQQ on rice adaptation to P deficiency.
