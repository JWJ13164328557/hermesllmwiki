# Triple-Helix Biomolecules Construct Self-Adaptive Multi-Phase Interfaces for Dendrites Immunized Zinc Metal Anodes.

Aqueous zinc (Zn) metal cells are promising candidates for large-scale energy storage owing to their intrinsic safety and low cost. However, their practical application remains limited by uncontrolled Zn dendrite growth and hydrogen-evolution-induced corrosion. Here, we present a collagen-engineered strategy that harnesses the natural triple-helix architecture of collagen to construct collagen-coated cotton fiber (COL@COT) separators capable of continuous collagen release and dynamic interfacial regulation.
