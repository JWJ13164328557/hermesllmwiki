# Efficacy of entomopathogenic fungi against Tuta absoluta (Lepidoptera: Gelechiidae). A systematic review and meta-analysis.

The South American tomato pinworm, Tuta absoluta (Meyrick) (Lepidoptera: Gelechiidae) (syn. Phthorimaea absoluta), is a highly destructive invasive pest of tomato crops worldwide. Its management has relied heavily on repeated applications of synthetic insecticides, resulting in widespread resistance development and environmental concerns. Entomopathogenic fungi (EPF) offer an eco-friendly biological control alternative. This systematic review and meta-analysis quantitatively assessed the global efficacy of EPF against T. absoluta by synthesizing data from the available literature. Based on 27 independent effect sizes drawn from 11 studies selected for quantitative synthesis, the pooled mortality under a random-effects model was 73.66%, indicating substantial overall pathogenic effectiveness. Subgroup analysis showed that Metarhizium anisopliae (81.06%) exhibited higher pooled mortality than Beauveria bassiana (69.83%), although both genera demonstrated strong virulence.
