# Efficacy of entomopathogenic fungi against Tuta absoluta (Lepidoptera: Gelechiidae). A systematic review and meta-analysis.

The South American tomato pinworm, Tuta absoluta (Meyrick) (Lepidoptera: Gelechiidae) (syn. Phthorimaea absoluta), is a highly destructive invasive pest of tomato crops worldwide. Its management has relied heavily on repeated applications of synthetic insecticides, resulting in widespread resistance development and environmental concerns. Entomopathogenic fungi (EPF) offer an eco-friendly biological control alternative. This systematic review and meta-analysis quantitatively assessed the global efficacy of EPF against T. absoluta by synthesizing data from the available literature. Based on 27 independent effect sizes drawn from 11 studies selected for quantitative synthesis, the pooled mortality under a random-effects model was 73.66%, indicating substantial overall pathogenic effectiveness. Subgroup analysis showed that Metarhizium anisopliae (81.06%) exhibited higher pooled mortality than Beauveria bassiana (69.83%), although both genera demonstrated strong virulence. EPF treatments were also relatively fast-acting, with a pooled median lethal time (LT50) of 2.97 days. Given the high variability observed across studies, a mixed-effects meta-regression was conducted and revealed that host developmental stage explained 71.97% of between-study heterogeneity, with early larval instars showing significantly greater susceptibility. Beyond direct mortality, EPF exhibited endophytic capabilities within tomato plants, contributing to systemic protection and significant reductions in key life-table parameters, including fecundity, adult longevity, and intrinsic rate of increase. Field and greenhouse evaluations further demonstrated reductions in leaf mining and fruit damage, accompanied by improvements in marketable yield. These findings support entomopathogenic fungi as effective and sustainable biological control agents suitable for integration into integrated pest management (IPM) programmes for T. absoluta.
