# Optimized Irrigation and Fertilization Reduce Luxury Transpiration While Improving GRAIN Yield, Water Use Efficiency, and Economic Benefits of Winter Wheat in the Arid Region of Xinjiang.

Winter wheat production in the extremely arid oasis region of Xinjiang relies heavily on irrigation and fertilization, but conventional high-input management can induce luxury transpiration and non-productive water consumption, limiting the coordinated improvement of grain yield, water use efficiency (WUE), and economic benefits. To identify the threshold at which water-fertilizer inputs shift from efficient use to inefficient water consumption and to define a robust management range, a two-year field experiment was conducted in southern Xinjiang during the 2022-2023 and 2023-2024 growing seasons.
