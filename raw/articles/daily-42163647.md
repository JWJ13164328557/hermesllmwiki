# Zinc-Doping Trap Engineering and FRET Synergy Enable Multicolor Room-Temperature Phosphorescence in Rice-Derived Carbon Dots Confined in Amorphous Alumina for Advanced Encryption.

The development of multicolor room-temperature phosphorescence (RTP) materials with tunable afterglow is crucial for advanced information encryption. Herein, we propose a synergistic strategy that integrates trap engineering and Förster resonance energy transfer (FRET) process for dynamically adjusting the emission color and lifetime.
