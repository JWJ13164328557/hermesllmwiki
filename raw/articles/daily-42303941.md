# Optimizing nitrogen fertilization for shaded cultivation of the medicinal plant Panax ginseng: implications for photosynthesis, root growth, and carbon accumulation in two-year-old plants.

Panax ginseng (C.A. Meyer) is an important medicinal plant cultivated under heavy shade, and stable production of high-quality roots requires appropriate nutrient management. Nitrogen (N) fertilization remains difficult to optimize because excessive N can promote shoot growth disproportionately, impair root development, and increase the risk of root quality deterioration and soil-related problems.
