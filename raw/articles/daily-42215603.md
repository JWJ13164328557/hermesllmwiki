# Data fusion of EnMAP and sentinel-2 for high-resolution soil fertility assessment in wheat cultivation of central Khuzestan plain.

This study aimed to develop a Soil Fertility Index (SFI) map for agricultural lands in central Khuzestan Province, specifically focusing on irrigated wheat cultivation.
