# Yield Performance, Combining Ability and Stability of Early- to Medium-Maturing Doubled-Haploid Maize Lines in Eastern Africa.

Maize is a staple food crop in sub-Saharan Africa, where increasing productivity is essential for food security. This study assessed testcross performance and combining ability of early- to medium-maturity doubled haploid (DH) maize lines adapted to eastern Africa. A total of 732 testcross hybrids derived from 244 DH lines crossed with six testers, along with six commercial checks, were evaluated across eight locations.
