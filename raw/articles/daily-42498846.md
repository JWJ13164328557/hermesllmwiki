# Soil heavy metal uptake and distribution in silver birch plantations on former agricultural land.

Afforestation of former agricultural land with silver birch (Betula pendula Roth) is increasingly promoted in Northern and Eastern Europe, yet its long-term effects on soil-plant heavy metal dynamics remain insufficiently understood. We assessed heavy metal accumulation in soil and woody biomass (stemwood and bark) 20 years after afforestation of former agricultural land at 11 plantations. Eleven elements (As, Cd, Co, Cr, Cu, Fe, Mn, Mo, Ni, Pb, and Zn) were initially determined, but subsequent analyses focused on five elements (Cr, Cu, Fe, Mn, and Zn) consistently detected above the limit of quantification.
