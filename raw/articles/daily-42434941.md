# Substrate Carbon Controls Regional Divergence of Wetland Biofilm Carbon Sinks under Climate Warming.

Microbial biofilms at soil-water interfaces mediate carbon exchange between soils and the atmosphere, yet whether substrate state alters their climate sensitivity remains poorly understood. Here, we analyzed 1080 rice-paddy sites across 26 regions of China to test whether soil organic carbon (SOC) regulates periphytic biofilm organic carbon (PB-TOC) responses to warming.
