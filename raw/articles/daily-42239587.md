# Harnessing wetting transitions to program dual-mode antibacterial textiles.

Anti-biofouling and antimicrobial treatments often face intrinsic trade-offs between short-term surface repellence and long-term bactericidal activity. Herein, a time-sequenced dual-defense textile platform is presented based on Cu/Zn bimetallic imidazolate frameworks grown on cotton fabrics
