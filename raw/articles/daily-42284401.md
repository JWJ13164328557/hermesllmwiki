# Regenerative macrophages enhance stem cell-derived β cell function and engraftment.

The generation of insulin-producing, stem cell-derived β cells (SC-β cells) from human embryonic or induced pluripotent stem cells holds promise for treating type 1 diabetes. Transplantation of SC-β cells is already in clinical testing, but generating mature cells with insulin-secreting properties similar to endogenous cells has been challenging. Given that macrophages are essential for islet development, we hypothesized that they could enhance SC-β cell differentiation and function.
