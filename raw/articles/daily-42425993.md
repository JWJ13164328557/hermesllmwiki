# Bound states in doped charge transfer insulators.

Understanding the physics of doped charge transfer insulators is the most important problem in high-temperature superconductivity. In this work, we show that an in-gap bound state emerges from the localized hole of the doped charge transfer insulator. We propose an approximate ground state wavefunction based on one localized Zhang-Rice singlet and the Néel state. By calculating the excitation states with one hole added and removed from this ground state, we successfully identify the existence of bound states inside the charge transfer gap.
