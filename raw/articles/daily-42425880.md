# The octameric shift in plant immune signaling.

Nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat (NLR) receptors are ubiquitous intracellular sensors. Guo et al. recently revealed that the wheat CCG10-NLR, Wheat Autoimmunity 3, forms a previously undescribed plant octameric resistosome to mediate cell death and immunity. This discovery uncovers a distinct configuration, highlighting the remarkable structural plasticity in plant immune signaling.
