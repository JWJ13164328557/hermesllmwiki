# Patient Comfort and Acceptance of Different Isolation Techniques for Silver Diamine Fluoride Application: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

BACKGROUND: Efficient isolation is critical for effective silver diamine fluoride (SDF) application in pediatric dentistry. However, conventional methods like cotton roll isolation may be uncomfortable and compromise cooperation. This study compares patient comfort and acceptance of cotton roll, OpalDam, and OptraDam during SDF application.

AIM: To evaluate and compare patient discomfort and satisfaction associated with cotton roll, OpalDam, and OptraDam isolation techniques during SDF application in primary mandibular molars of children.
