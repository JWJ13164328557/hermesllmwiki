# Effect of green tea kombucha fermentation on phenolic content and antioxidant activity in Vero cells.

Kombucha, a fermented beverage, is gaining attention for its antioxidant potential. Here, we investigated the effects of green tea kombucha fermentation on the phenolic profile and in vitro antioxidant capacity, both in a standardized inoculum and an industrialized kombucha.
