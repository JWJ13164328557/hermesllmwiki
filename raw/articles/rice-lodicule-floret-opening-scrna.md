# Single-cell transcriptome sequencing uncovers the regulatory role of lodicule cells in rice floret opening

## 论文信息

- **题目**: Single-cell transcriptome sequencing (ScRNA-seq) uncovers the regulatory role of lodicule cells in rice floret opening (FO)
- **期刊**: Communications Biology (2026)
- **DOI**: [10.1038/s42003-026-10191-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-10191-2)
- **第一作者**: Zhiqiang Yan
- **作者**: Zhiqiang Yan, Ruyue Deng, Huihui Tang

## 摘要

Floret opening (FO) in rice is triggered by lodicule swelling. The regulatory mechanisms underlying lodicule-mediated FO remain largely unclear — in particular whether distinct floral cell types coordinately regulate lodicule osmotic potential and cell wall remodeling. This study performed single-cell transcriptome sequencing on spikelets sampled at pre-floret opening and FO stages, demonstrating strong communication between phloem and lodicule cells during FO, potentially involving transport of calcium ions, soluble sugars, and water. These osmolytes contribute to lowering lodicule osmotic potential, thereby facilitating swelling. Multiple FO genes involved in lodicule cell wall remodeling showed heterogeneous activation, suggesting fine regulation of cell wall remodeling and cell deformation.

## 核心发现

1. **水稻颖花开颖(FO)** 由浆片(lodicule)细胞膨大触发
2. **scRNA-seq** 分析颖花开颖前和开颖期的小穗
3. **韧皮部-浆片细胞通讯**: 钙离子/可溶性糖/水分运输 → 降低浆片渗透势 → 促进膨大
4. **细胞壁重塑**: FO相关基因在浆片细胞中异质性激活，精密调控细胞壁重塑和细胞形变
