# A multi-indicator evaluation of cadmium tolerance in wheat seedlings identifies low-accumulating cultivars for contaminated areas.

BACKGROUND: The Huang-Huai region is a major wheat-producing area in China, contributing significantly to national food security. However, cadmium (Cd) contamination in local soils threatens both crop productivity and human health through food chain transfer. Selecting wheat cultivars with low Cd accumulation is a cost-effective strategy, yet a rapid screening protocol for Cd tolerance at the seedling stage remains lacking.

RESULTS: This study aimed to establish a comprehensive evaluation system based on seedling growth traits and screen wheat cultivars suitable for Cd-contaminated farmlands in the Huang-Huai region. Seven widely cultivated wheat cultivars were hydroponically grown under Cd0 (0 mg L

CONCLUSION: The TOPSIS with EW ranking confirmed Zhengmai 366 and Zhoumai 36 as the most suitable cultivars for Cd-contaminated areas.
