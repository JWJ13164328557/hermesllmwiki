# Molybdate Causes Target and Nontarget Inhibitory Effects on Microbial Arsenic Methylation in Paddy Soil.

Microbial arsenic (As) methylation is enhanced in flooded paddy soils, producing mainly dimethylarsenic (DMA), which can cause rice straighthead disease and large yield losses.
