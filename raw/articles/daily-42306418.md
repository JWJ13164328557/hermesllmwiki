INTRODUCTION: Herbaceous plants regulate the generation of runoff and sediment and the anti-erosion ability of soil by altering vegetation coverage and root systems, thereby influencing soil erosion. However, the variation in soil erosion reduction benefits and the contributions of stem-leaf and roots under diverse vegetation spatial distribution patterns on the Loess Plateau have not yet been understood.
