# Identification of a Novel Glycosyltransferase CaUGT Specifically Targeting the C-30 Position of Glycyrrhetinic Acid and Development of an Efficient Enzymatic Biotransformation System.

Glycyrrhetinic acid (GA) is a major bioactive ingredient of licorice with extensive pharmacological functions, while its strong hydrophobicity and low permeability severely restrict practical application. Enzymatic glycosylation serves as a mild method to enhance GA solubility and bioactivity, yet few reported glycosyltransferases can specifically modify the C30 carboxyl group of GA.
