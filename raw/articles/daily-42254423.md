# Sensitive Spectral and Temporal-Spatial Characteristic Analysis of Leaf SPAD in Maize Under Variety and Nitrogen Coupling Effects.

Accurate estimation of leaf SPAD is crucial for maize growth and yield formation. Many methods for monitoring SPAD currently lack the analysis of sensitive leaf position in different stages of maize. In this paper, the spectra and temporal-spatial characteristics of maize leaf SPAD were analyzed to describe the sensitive stage and leaf position. After exploring the dynamic growth effects of SPAD in maize leaves, the sensitive stage of SPAD was determine. Several preprocessing methods and spectral vegetation indices were used to analyze the spectral reflectance of typical leaf positions in sensitive stages. The function regression methods based on single vegetation index and the random forest regression (RFR) based on multi-vegetation indices were employed. The results showed that the twelve-leaf (V12) and the silking (R1) were the sensitive stages.
