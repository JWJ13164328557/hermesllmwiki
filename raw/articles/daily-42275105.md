# The E3 Ubiquitin-Protein Ligase ZmBOIR3 Is a Key Regulator of Low-Temperature Tolerance in Maize Seedlings.

Low-temperature stress seriously affects the growth and development of maize seedlings, endangering their quality and yield. In this study, the low-temperature tolerance at the seedling stage of 299 maize inbred lines was evaluated by using the membership function method.
