# Tomato Silicon Influx Transporter SlLsi1 Participates in Selenite Uptake and Selenomethionine Transport and Improves Fruit Quality.

The molecular mechanism of plant selenium (Se) uptake is poorly understood. Here, we demonstrated that the silicon (Si) influx transporter Lsi1 mediates selenite uptake and selenomethionine transport in tomato.
