# Virus-induced transgene- and tissue culture-free heritable genome editing in tomato.

Genome editing has emerged as a powerful tool for genome manipulation and trait improvement in crops. However, most commonly used approaches rely on tissue culture and transgenic materials, which are time-consuming, labor-intensive, and often strongly genotype-dependent. Here, we developed a Tobacco rattle virus (TRV)-based system to deliver the compact ISYmu1 TnpB endonuclease, coupled with in planta shoot regeneration, to achieve somatic and heritable genome editing across different tomato cultivars without tissue culture.
