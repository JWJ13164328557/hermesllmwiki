# Transcriptomic and physiological analyses reveal the mechanism of acetylene-induced flowering in Guzmania 'Denise'.

In Bromeliaceae cultivation, flowering regulation remains a major challenge, and calcium carbide is widely used as a flowering stimulant because of its low cost and ease of application. Upon contact with water, calcium carbide releases acetylene, which subsequently induces flowering in plants. However, to date, no systematic studies have elucidated the mechanisms by which acetylene triggers floral bud differentiation in bromeliads.
