# Synergistic effects of zinc and iron to reduce cadmium uptake in wheat: Insights into soil immobilization and physiological regulatory mechanisms.

Soil heavy metal pollution tends to cause excessive accumulation of cadmium (Cd) in wheat grains, posing serious threats to food safety and human health. Therefore, it is urgent to develop strategies for effective remediation of Cd-contaminated soils. Iron (Fe) and zinc (Zn) can antagonize Cd uptake for safer agriculture production.
