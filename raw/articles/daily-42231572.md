# Heat shock-induced zinc efflux repurposes Arabidopsis E3 ligase EMR as a molecular chaperone.

EMR, an Endoplasmic Reticulum Associated Degradation (ERAD)-Mediating RING finger E3 ligase initially identified as an ER-resident protein, was unexpectedly detected in the cytoplasm under ER stress conditions. This unanticipated subcellular distribution prompted us to explore the uncharacterized functions of cytoplasmic EMR.
