# Spatiotemporal transcriptome atlas reveals the dynamic cellular and molecular characteristics of ovule development in gymnosperms.

Ovule, as the developmental precursor to seed, represents a key evolutionary innovation in seed plants. However, its origin and evolutionary trajectory have been debated for over a century, largely due to the disconnects between morphological information gained from fossils and the regulatory mechanisms of ovule development inferred from extant angiosperms. The core regulatory networks shared by gymnosperms and angiosperms may serve as a bridge in terms of the ovule developmental process.
