# One-step preparation of polymeric guanidine-modified straw 

A solvent-free, one-step internal mixing strategy was developed to graft polyhexamethylene guanidine hydrochloride (PHMG) onto straw using epoxidized soybean oil (ESO) as a bio-based crosslinker. This high-temperature, high-shear process concurrently achieves surface functionalization and thermal sanitization of the biomass.
