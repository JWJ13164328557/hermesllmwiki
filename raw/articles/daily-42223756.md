# Sesamol derivatives bearing a quinazolin moiety: synthesis, antifungal efficacy against tea plant pathogens, and mechanistic insights.

Pestalotiopsis sp. and Colletotrichum camelliae are two devastating fungal pathogens that cause significant yield losses and quality degradation in tea plants (Camellia sinensis). The increasing resistance of these pathogens to commercial fungicides necessitates the development of novel fungicidal agents with distinct modes of action.
