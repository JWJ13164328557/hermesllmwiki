# Techno-Economic and Environmental Assessment of Magnesium-Impregnated Rice Husk Biochar for Nutrient Removal: A Scale-Up and Prospective Soil Application Approach.

This study evaluates the techno-economic and environmental performance of a sequential system based on fixed-bed column adsorption using magnesium-impregnated rice husk biochar (RHB-Mg) for nutrient removal from wastewater, coupled with a prospective assessment of its reuse as a soil amendment in irrigated rice systems.
