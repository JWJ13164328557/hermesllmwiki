# Physiological Tolerance to Heat and Drought Are Decoupled Across Common Woody Urban Greening Species in North China.

Urban vegetation is increasingly exposed to the compound heat and drought stress due to global warming and urban heat island effects, yet heat tolerance and its linkage with drought resistance or photosynthesis remain unclear for plants in urban environments. We evaluated corresponding traits in nine woody species common to northern China's highly urbanized megacity cluster dominated by temperate continental monsoon climate.
