# Organic fertilizer substitution in saline-alkali paddy fields: trade-offs among soil quality, greenhouse gas emissions, yield, and profitability.

Balancing crop productivity with greenhouse gas (GHG) mitigation is a major challenge in saline-alkali rice systems. We conducted a three-season field experiment in Shanghai, China, to evaluate four fertilization regimes: no fertilizer (CK), full chemical fertilization (CT), 50% organic substitution (MF), and 100% organic substitution (OF).
