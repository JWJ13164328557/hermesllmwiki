# Detection of soybean oil in commercial edible oil products using a rapid, specific and sensitive real-time loop-mediated isothermal amplification assay.

BACKGROUND: Soybean oil extracted from the seeds of Glycine max plants is an economically important edible oil in the food industry. Due to its lower price, soybean oil is often used as an adulterant in more valuable edible oils.

PURPOSE: In this study, real-time loop-mediated isothermal amplification (rtLAMP) assay was developed for the detection of soybean oil DNA to support edible oil authentication.

METHODS: The LAMP primers of rtLAMP were designed based on the ITS2 sequence and further were used for validating the specificity and sensitivity.

CONCLUSION: In conclusion, we successfully established a soybean-specific rtLAMP assay for authenticating soybean DNA in edible oil. This developed rtLAMP assay not only demonstrated enhanced specificity and speed but also holds strong potential for authenticating soybean DNA and preventing adulteration in edible oil products.
