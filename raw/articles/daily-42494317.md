Microplastic contamination and cadmium (Cd) pollution are increasingly recognized as co-occurring environmental threats in agricultural ecosystems, where their combined presence may exert more severe effects on crop growth and productivity than either stressor alone. Although previous studies have separately examined the effects of microplastics, heavy metals, and ZnO nanoparticles on plants, investigations addressing the mitigation of combined cadmium-microplastic stress through nano-enabled approaches remain scarce. Furthermore, antioxidant defense systems and Cd accumulation under simultaneous exposure to these contaminants are not yet well understood.
