# Revealing the Molecular Network of Pattern-Triggered Immunity (PTI) Signal Transduction.

Pattern-triggered immunity (PTI), the first line of defense in plants, is activated when host pattern recognition receptors (PRRs) detect pathogen-associated molecular patterns (PAMPs) or damage-associated molecular patterns (DAMPs) released during microbial invasion. Over the past decade, major advances have clarified PTI's complex signaling network, revealing its role as a dynamic regulator of plant immune responses.
