# Morphological and molecular characterization of Cordyceps javanica (Frieder. & Bally) Kepler and its in-vitro and in-vivo efficacy against tea mosquito bug, Helopeltis theivora waterhouse.

Tea mosquito bug, Helopeltis theivora Waterhouse (Hemiptera: Miridae), is a major pest of tea, Camellia sinensis (L.) O. Kuntze, causing up to 50% yield losses in the North Eastern region of India. The management of this pest is challenging owing to its rapid multiplication and development of resistance to conventional insecticides. Therefore, the present study was made to assess the efficacy of naturally occurring native entomopathogenic fungus (EPF), which was isolated from Archips, a leaf feeder pest of Lac host plant, Flemingia semialata. In vitro efficacy of this EPF against H. theivora confirmed its virulence. Hence, the isolate was further characterized at molecular level by using 18 S rDNA primer and identified it as Cordyceps javanica (Gene bank accession number, OM321438).
