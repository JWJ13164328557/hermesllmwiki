# Rapid Detection of Deoxynivalenol in Soybeans by NIR Spectroscopy Coupled With ETs-PLS Feature Selection Method.

This study developed a hybrid feature selection approach combining Extra Trees (ETs) with partial least squares (PLS) regression for rapid and accurate deoxynivalenol (DON) quantification in soybeans using near-infrared (NIR) spectroscopy. The ETs algorithm ranked spectral variables by importance, and the top 50 wavelengths were selected as inputs for the PLS model.
