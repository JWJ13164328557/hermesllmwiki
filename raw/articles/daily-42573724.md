# GsIRT3: a zinc-iron transporter gene from Glycine soja, enhancing alkali stress tolerance in Arabidopsis thaliana.

ZIP family genes are known to play important roles in the transport of divalent metal ions such as zinc and iron. However, their roles in alkali stress have not been well characterized so far. In this study, a ZIP (Zrt, Irt-like Protein) cDNA was isolated from wild soybean (Glycine soja) by reverse transcription PCR (RT-PCR) and named as GsIRT3. GsIRT3 displayed differential gene expression levels at different time points under alkali treatment in both roots and leaves. Heterologous expression of GsIRT3 in yeast (pYES2-GsIRT3) conferred enhanced tolerance to both iron- and zinc-deficiency stress conditions. In addition, GsIRT3 overexpression lines (OX) of Arabidopsis thaliana were created by floral dip method for functional characterization of GsIRT3 under stress treatments. The results displayed that GsIRT3 OX lines performed better under alkali stress than wild-type (WT) plants in terms of higher root lengths and fresh biomass.
