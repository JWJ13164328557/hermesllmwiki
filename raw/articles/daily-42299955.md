Bacterial infection remains a major challenge in wound management, often leading to delayed healing, prolonged treatment, and increased healthcare costs. Antimicrobial dressings provide an effective approach to prevent and control wound infections, thereby supporting faster wound repair. Although many antimicrobial dressings are commercially available and widely studied, most are either loaded with antibiotics, which raises concerns about antibiotic resistance, or contain silver nanoparticles (Ag NPs), which increase the overall cost.
