# Sustainable Extraction of Collagen from Tannery Raw Trimmings Using Ginger Peel-Derived Zingibain Enzyme: Process Optimization with Box-Behnken Design.

The traditional chemical-based extraction methods of collagen pose significant environmental and health risks. This study presents a sustainable alternative by employing zingibaina plant-based proteolytic enzyme derived from ginger peelfor the hydrolysis of tannery waste, minimizing resource consumption and environmental impact. To maximize the output of the extraction process, Box-Behnken Design (BBD) under the Response Surface Methodology was employed. The design analysis revealed that the optimal hydrolysis using crude zingibain enzyme achieved a yield of approximately 92% at a temperature of 57.83 °C, neutral pH, and a reaction time of 5.36 h.
