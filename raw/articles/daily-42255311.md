# Balancing individual trade-offs for population gain: optimizing nitrogen and density secures fresh ear yield in rain-fed waxy maize.

CONTEXT: Fresh waxy maize is a unique type of maize harvested at the late R3 stage. Both fresh ear yield and weight per ear determine its economic value. Nitrogen (N) application and planting density are the two major agronomic practice factors influencing maize growth and yield; however, optimal values for fresh waxy maize remain poorly defined, particularly in rain-fed areas.

OBJECTIVE: The purpose of the present study was to clarify the effects of planting density and N application rate on fresh ear yield, in addition to dry matter (DM) and N accumulation and partitioning, and to identify the primary drivers of the effects in fresh waxy maize.
