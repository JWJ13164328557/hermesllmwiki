# CARK3-mediated phosphorylation of bHLH106 inhibits iron uptake by repressing the expression of IRT1 in Arabidopsis.

Iron homeostasis is fundamental to plant survival, as it balances the dual challenges of iron deficiency and toxicity. This intricate equilibrium is orchestrated by transcription factors including bHLH TFs. To date, numerous bHLH TFs have been identified as positive regulators of iron uptake in plants. However, negative regulators remain largely unknown. In this study, we identified a novel transcription factor bHLH106 that can bypass the FIT-centered transcriptional regulatory network and directly bind to the promoter of IRT1 (Iron Regulated Transporter 1) thus inhibiting the expression of IRT1. FIT-IRT1-mediated iron uptake pathway is activated under iron deficiency, leading to continuous accumulation of intracellular iron. As intracellular iron levels rise, CARK3 (Cytosolic ABA Receptor Kinase 3) kinase phosphorylates bHLH106 to promote its recruitment to IRT1 promoter thereby enhancing the transcription repression of IRT1 by bHLH106. bHLH106 represses the expression of IRT1 in order to negatively regulate iron uptake. However, the transcription level of bHLH106 is suppressed, leading to a reduced protein accumulation under iron-deficient conditions. Consequently, the transcriptional repression of IRT1 by bHLH106 is alleviated, facilitating FIT-mediated upregulation expression of IRT1 and promoting iron uptake. In summary, the CARK3-bHLH106-IRT1-mediated response mechanism enables plants to rapidly adapt to fluctuations in iron levels.
