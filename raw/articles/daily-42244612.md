# Genomic history and ecology of local adaptation in sky-island 

Adaptation to local environments in isolated habitats may produce independent solutions to similar ecological challenges, yielding insight into the predictability of evolution. Alpine plants on tropical sky-islands, in particular, must cope with extreme diurnal fluctuations in temperature and radiation. These populations may undergo parallel local adaptation to high elevation, or alternatively, distinct local adaptations due to intermountain heterogeneity.
