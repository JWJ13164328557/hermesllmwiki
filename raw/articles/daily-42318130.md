METHODS: This study combined transcriptomic and genetic analyses to characterize the moderate BPB resistance of Jupiter. Comparative RNA-seq analysis of Jupiter and Bengal (a BPB susceptible medium-grain variety) was performed using panicle samples collected 24 hours post pathogen-inoculation at the flowering stage. We also conducted quantitative trait locus (QTL) mapping of the BPB resistance, using a recombinant inbred line (RIL) population from Jupiter and Bengal.
