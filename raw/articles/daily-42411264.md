# Gas-phase recognition by a dopamine-selective cyclic tetrapeptide in the analysis of plant extracts.

A dopamine-selective oligopeptide is verified to possess superior binding performance to model ligands over rice matrix-derived molecules in gas phase, which represents a step forward for promoting the development and application of gas-phase recognition chemistry.
