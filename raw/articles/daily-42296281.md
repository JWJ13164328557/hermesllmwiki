# Steam Pop During Lattice-Tip Radiofrequency Ablation: Simultaneous Intracardiac Echocardiography and Electroanatomical Mapping Recordings.

We report a case of steam pop occurring during lattice-tip radiofrequency ablation. An 82-year-old woman underwent cavotricuspid isthmus ablation using a 9-mm lattice-tip catheter. Intracardiac echocardiography (ICE), electroanatomical mapping, and radiofrequency parameters were recorded simultaneously on a single system, allowing precise temporal correlation. A steam pop occurred at 4 s with a sudden microbubble shower on ICE at 65°C. The highest temperature was detected at the proximal thermocouple, suggesting nonuniform catheter-tissue contact. This case highlights limitations of temperature monitoring and the importance of spatial catheter orientation during lattice-tip ablation.
