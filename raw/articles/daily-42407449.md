# Plant evolution: The early fossil record of phloem and xylem.

Phloem and xylem co-occur in all living vascular plants. A new study on plants from the Rhynie chert, a spectacularly preserved early terrestrial ecosystem, shows that these primary tissues have distinct fossil records exhibiting unique evolutionary patterns.
