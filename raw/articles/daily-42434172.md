# Molecular dynamics and predictive toxicity insights into theaflavin as a potential inhibitor of MMP-2 and MMP-9 in breast cancer.

Breast cancer is one of the most commonly diagnosed malignancies in women and remains a leading cause of cancer-related mortality worldwide. Matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs), particularly Matrix Metalloproteinase-2 (MMP-2) and Matrix Metalloproteinase-9 (MMP-9), play critical roles in cancer invasion, metastasis, and angiogenesis, making them attractive therapeutic targets.
