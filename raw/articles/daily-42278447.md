The optimal timing and therapeutic role of dexamethasone for neuroprotection in neonatal hypoxic-ischemic (HI) brain injury remain unclear. We investigated whether dexamethasone-mediated neuroprotection is time-dependent and explored its underlying molecular mechanisms in a neonatal HI mouse model.
