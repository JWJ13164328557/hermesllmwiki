# A long-distance signaling loop promotes soybean nodulation and productivity.

Legume nodulation is initiated when soil bacteria rhizobia infect root hairs and is tightly regulated by host-derived mechanisms that restrict nodule numbers to balance the benefits of symbiotic nitrogen fixation with the plant's growth and metabolic demands.
