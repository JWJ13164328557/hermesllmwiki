# Exogenous pipecolic acid ameliorates lead-induced phytotoxicity in chili (

Heavy metal toxicity disrupts plant cellular homeostasis by inducing oxidative stress and impairing the key signaling networks that regulate plant growth and defense. Small signaling metabolites, such as pipecolic acid (PipA), a known regulator of systemic acquired resistance, may play a critical role in modulating plant responses to abiotic stresses.
