# Plasma ball milling-assisted synthesis of silico-manganese heterojunction adsorbent from manganese slag and rice husk biochar for efficient removal of Cu(II) and Ni(II) from aqueous solutions.

Heavy metal pollution from industrial activities poses severe threats to aquatic ecosystems and human health, urging the development of low-cost, high-efficiency adsorbents via resource reuse. Herein, a silico-manganese heterojunction adsorbent (RHB&MS) was fabricated by plasma ball milling (PBM) using manganese slag (MS) and rice husk biochar (RHB) as raw materials. The synthesis mechanism, structural characteristics, and adsorption performance of RHB&MS for Cu(II) and Ni(II) were systematically investigated via multiple characterizations and batch adsorption experiments. The results indicated that PBM induced the formation of stable Si-O-Mn covalent bonds at the interface, constructing a "crystalline silica framework-amorphous carbon matrix-nano manganese oxide active site" heterojunction structure.
