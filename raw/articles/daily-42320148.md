# Iron-catalyzed degradation of atrazine with persulfate from synthetic oxides and highly weathered soils with contrasting mineralogy.

The intensive use of herbicides in agriculture has caused contamination of soil, surface waters and underground waters. The presence of atrazine (ATZ), an herbicide widely used in soybean, wheat, and corn crops, is particularly relevant. This study evaluated the degradation of ATZ by persulfate (PS), using Fe oxide activation of two highly weathered soils (Oxisol and Alfisol), with contrasting hematite (Hm) and goethite (Gt) contents. To better predict the mechanisms involved in PS activation and ATZ degradation, tests were also conducted with synthetic ferrihydrite (Fh), Gt, and Hm.
