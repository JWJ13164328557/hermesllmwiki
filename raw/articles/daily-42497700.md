# Computational guided improvement of thermostability of Arabidopsis thaliana UDP-sugar pyrophosphorylase for efficient synthesis of uridine 5'-diphosphate galactose.

Uridine 5'-diphosphate galactose (UDP-Gal) is a vital but costly precursor for oligosaccharide synthesis. A major bottleneck in its production is the poor thermostability of biosynthetic enzymes. Here, we engineered Arabidopsis thaliana UDP-sugar pyrophosphorylase (AtUSP) using consensus prediction (FireProt, PROSS, Pythia) followed by experimental screening and combinatorial assembly of five beneficial mutations.
