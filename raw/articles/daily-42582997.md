# Stress phenotyping of wild desert legume 

Plants encounter multiple abiotic stresses. Among them, heat and drought stress play a substantial role in reducing the agricultural productivity of commercial plants. Hence, wild and underutilized plants can be a potential alternative as they are naturally tolerant to extreme climatic conditions and are a rich source of nutrition. Manual stress and disease detection is a laborious and expensive process, and hence automation in this field is required to reduce agricultural losses.
