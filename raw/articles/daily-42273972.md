# Interfacial engineering of rice bran wax-lard oleogels by polyglycerol fatty acid esters: Polymerization degree-dependent modulation of β'-crystal networks and bread quality.

BACKGROUND: The objective of this study was to evaluate the effects of polyglycerol fatty acid ester (PGFE) concentration and degree of polymerization on the structure of rice bran wax-lard composite oleogel (RLCO) and its application in bread. PGFE interfacial behavior was characterized by assessments of interfacial tension, adsorption kinetics, and dilatational rheology, while oleogel structure was evaluated using differential scanning calorimetry, Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy, X-ray diffraction, and polarized light microscopy. Functional assessments included rheology and measurements of hardness, oil-binding, and bread-making application.

RESULTS: PGFEs were found to lower the interfacial tension and Gibbs free energy at oil-water interfaces while stabilizing interfacial films. Fourier transform infrared analysis suggested that PGFEs adsorbed at the rapeseed oil-lard interface through hydrogen bonding, promoting the orderly crystallization of rice bran wax.
