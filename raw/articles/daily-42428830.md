# Metal-Free Analcime Derived from Rice Husk Ash for Visible-Light-Enhanced Heterogeneous Activation of H

The development of sustainable, metal-free advanced oxidation processes is crucial for efficient wastewater treatment. In this study, analcime zeolite was rapidly synthesized from agricultural waste-derived rice husk ash via a facile 5 min microwave-assisted route.
