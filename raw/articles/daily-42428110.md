# Determinants of Repetitive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation Efficacy in Tobacco Use Disorder: A Pre-Registered Study.

Repetitive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (rTMS) is a promising treatment for tobacco use disorder (TUD). Although at a group level, active stimulation outperforms sham, at an individual level, variability exists in clinical response.
