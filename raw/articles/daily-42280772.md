# Alternative Splicing of SCL30a Generates Distinct Isoforms to Modulate ABA Signaling in Arabidopsis.

Alternative splicing (AS) coupled with nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) is an important post-transcriptional mechanism that regulates the expression of many genes, including serine/arginine-rich (SR) proteins across eukaryotes. In plants, SR proteins participate in diverse developmental processes and stress responses, particularly in abscisic acid (ABA) signaling.
