# Antifungal activity and biochemical characteristics of natamycin against Fusarium graminearum.

Fusarium head blight (FHB) caused by Fusarium graminearum is a destructive fungal disease of wheat. Natamycin is a natural polyene macrolide antifungal compound exhibiting broad-spectrum antifungal activity, but its efficacy and mechanism against F. graminearum remain unclear.
