# AhIRX7, a novel glycosyltransferases (GTs) gene, confers salt and drought tolerance in transgenic peanut.

BACKGROUND: Peanut (Arachis hypogaea L.) is an important oilseed crop whose yield is threatened by various abiotic stresses. Glycosyltransferases are crucial for diverse plant functions, including the regulation of plant growth and development, biotic and abiotic stress response, and the biosynthesis of secondary metabolites. However, the mechanism of glycosyltransferases relates to abiotic stresses remains unclear in peanut.

RESULTS: In this study, we isolated a novel gene, AhIRX7, from a salt-tolerant mutant of peanut. The expression of AhIRX7 was strongly induced by NaCl and PEG6000. Overexpression of AhIRX7 led to increased, whereas silenced of AhIRX7 resulted in decreased tolerance of peanut seedlings to salt and drought stresses. Compared to wild-type (WT), the overexpression lines showed significantly increased chlorophyll fluorescence parameters and reduced photodamage under salt and drought stress.
