# Chlorophyll fluorescence imaging for early visual detection and measurement of postharvest discoloration: Potato greening and broccoli yellowing.

Chlorophyll serves as both physiological basis and fundamental pigment underlying healthy coloration of numerous fruits and vegetables. Yet conventional visual inspection, point-level color measurement, and destructive chlorophyll assays lack sensitivity for early, non-destructive detection of discoloration driven by abnormal chlorophyll metabolism in postharvest produce. This study developed a simple chlorophyll fluorescence imaging system to enable early detection and measurement of two representative postharvest discoloration phenomena: light-induced greening in potatoes and senescence-driven yellowing in broccoli.
