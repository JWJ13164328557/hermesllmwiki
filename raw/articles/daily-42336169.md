# Two fluffy family proteins, CgrFlbA and CgrFlbC, coordinately regulate hyphal growth, oxidative stress, asexual development, and pathogenicity in Colletotrichum graminicola.

Colletotrichum graminicola, the causal agent of maize anthracnose, is responsible for substantial global crop losses. While the fluffy family proteins (FlbA-FlbE, FluG) are known as key upstream activators of asexual development in Aspergillus nidulans, their functions in C. graminicola remain largely unexplored. Here, we systematically characterized the biological roles of the fluffy gene homologs in this plant pathogen. Deletion of CgrflbA, which encodes a regulator of G-protein signaling (RGS) protein, resulted in a fluffy phenotype, reduced oval conidiation, and a complete block in falcate conidium formation. The ΔCgrflbA mutant also demonstrated enhanced melanin deposition, increased hyphopodium formation, improved oxidative stress tolerance, and consequently, elevated virulence.
