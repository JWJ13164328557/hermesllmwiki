# Combined associations of tea consumption and smoking with accelerated biological aging among oilfield workers.

OBJECTIVES: This study aimed to examine the individual and combined associations of tea consumption and cigarette smoking with accelerated biological aging among oilfield workers.

METHODS: Logistic regression was used to assess the associations of tea consumption and cigarette smoking with accelerated biological aging, defined using the PhenoAge algorithm, with results reported as odds ratios (ORs) and 95% confidence intervals (CIs). Interaction and stratified analyses by shift work status were performed to evaluate additive, multiplicative, and context-specific effects. Sensitivity analyses were conducted to assess the robustness of the observed associations.
