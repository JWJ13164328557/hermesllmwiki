Molybdenum (Mo) serves an important biological role as part of cofactors of various enzymes in all domains of life. In plants, it is critical for nitrogen metabolism and integral to rhizobial enzymes such as nitrogenase, which is essential for plant-rhizobia symbiosis. However, like other transition metals, Mo can be toxic at excess concentrations.
