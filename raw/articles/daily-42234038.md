# CeO

Climate change-induced abiotic stress, including heat, cold, salinity, and drought, increasingly threaten global crop productivity. These challenges highlight the need for complementary strategies alongside conventional genetic improvement. Reactive oxygen species (ROS) act both as cytotoxic molecules causing oxidative damage and as essential signaling regulators controlling redox homeostasis and stress adaptation. This review examines recent advances in engineering nanozymes (NZs) for targeted regulation of plant ROS networks.
