# Separating intrinsic from extrinsic stomatal control in different soils.

The regulation of stomata in response to environmental changes is one of the most dynamic and important behaviours in vascular plants, playing a central role in determining gas exchange and vulnerability to drought. Yet whether this regulation is determined by intrinsic species physiology or by soil hydraulic properties or a combination of both remains unsolved.
