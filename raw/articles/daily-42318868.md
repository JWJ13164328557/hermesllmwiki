Cadmium (Cd) is a highly toxic and mobile heavy metal in soils, adversely affecting rice quality and productivity. Innovations in technology/techniques are needed to reduce Cd accumulation in rice while improving its resilience to contamination. The current study focused on preparing, characterizing, and evaluating silicon dioxide nanoparticles (SIONPs), iron oxide nanoparticles (IONPs), and iron-doped silicon nanoparticles (IDSNPs) for their potential to improve rice resilience to Cd contamination.
