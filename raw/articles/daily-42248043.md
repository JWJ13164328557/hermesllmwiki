# Zinc and ascorbic acid synergistically alleviate cadmium toxicity in wheat by modulating transporter genes and antioxidant defense.

This study investigated the synergistic effects and mechanisms of exogenous zinc (Zn) and ascorbic acid (AsA) in reducing Cd toxicity in wheat (Triticum aestivum L. cv. Xinong 979).
