# Genetic Variability, Trait Association, and Multi-Trait Selection of New Indeterminate Tomato Genotypes Under Protected Cultivation.

Tomato is an important vegetable crop suited to both open-field and protected cultivation. Indeterminate genotypes with high yield potential and desirable quality traits are especially suited to off-season production under protected cultivation. The present study evaluated 57 indeterminate tomato genotypes over two consecutive years under protected conditions to assess genetic variability, genetic divergence, and trait associations across 16 important yield-attributing and quality traits. The analysis of variance depicted significant differences among genotypes for all traits under study. The traits, viz., fruit weight and number of fruits per cluster, exhibited high heritability and high genetic gain, suggesting the predominance of additive gene action and the possibility of direct selection.
