# Effects of increasing soybean meal in late nursery, grower, and finishing pig diets.

Six experiments were conducted to determine the effects of increasing soybean meal (SBM) in late nursery and finishing pig diets on growth performance, fecal characteristics, and carcass traits. In Exp. 1, 266 pigs (initially 10.1 ± 0.17 kg) were randomly assigned to one of four corn-based diets with SBM levels of 25.0, 28.9, 32.5, or 36.2%. In Exp. 2, 340 pigs (initially 13.5 ± 0.18 kg) were randomly assigned to one of five corn-based diets with SBM levels of 25.0, 28.9, 32.5, 36.2, or 40.0%.
