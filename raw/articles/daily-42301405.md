# Ciprofloxacin and glyphosate co-exposure alters soybean development and reprograms metabolic pathways.

The increasing occurrence of pharmaceutical residues and herbicides in agricultural soils raises concerns about their impacts on crop development. Here, we first screened the effects of 22 emerging contaminants on soybean (Glycine max L.) germination and subsequently assessed the individual and combined effects of ciprofloxacin (CIP) and glyphosate (G) during early development.
