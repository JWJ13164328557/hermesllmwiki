# Wheat Functional Traits and Photosynthetic Responses to Dynamic Shading in Agrivoltaic Systems.

The growing demand for food and renewable energy has stimulated interest in agrivoltaic systems, yet their effects on crop physiology remain poorly understood. This study investigates how dynamic shading from high biaxial solar trackers influences wheat photosynthesis and functional traits. Across 2 years and eight field sites in western France, nine leaf-level traits related to carbon and nitrogen economy, together with plant height and plant density, were measured at anthesis and analysed against 14 environmental and management variables.
