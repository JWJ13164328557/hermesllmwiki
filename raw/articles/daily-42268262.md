# Comparative efficacy of foliar-applied biogenic copper and iron nanoparticles for mitigating salinity stress in chickpea (

Plant synthesized iron oxide nanoparticles (FeO NPs) and copper nanoparticles (Cu NPs) were evaluated to promote chickpeas growth under salt stress. The characterization of the FeO NPs and Cu NPs was carried out by X-ray Diffraction (XRD), Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) and Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) analysis. Foliar applications of FeO (100 mg/L) and Cu NPs (75 mg/L) significantly improved chlorophyll (47% & 40%), carotenoids (59% & 56%), and total soluble sugars (36% & 30%) compared to the control. Growth parameters increased 31% to 53% by 100 mg/L FeO and 27% to 53% by 75 mg/L Cu NPs compared to the control.
