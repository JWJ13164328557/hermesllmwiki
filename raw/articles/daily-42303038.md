# Legacy-buffered groundwater nitrate limits predictability from short-term hydroclimatic windows across intensive wheat-maize croplands.

Groundwater nitrate contamination in intensive croplands often persists despite strong short-term weather variability, suggesting that near-surface signals may not translate directly to aquifers. We tested this across a four-site south-to-north transect in Henan Province, China, under a wheat-maize rotation.
