# Status of insecticide resistance and detoxification enzymes activity in pink bollworm, Pectinophora gossypiella (Saunders) populations from India.

The pink bollworm, Pectinophora gossypiella (Saunders), is one of the most destructive pests of cotton, causing substantial yield losses and threatening the long-term sustainability of cotton cultivation in India. The introduction of transgenic Bt cotton expressing Cry1Ac and subsequently stacked Cry1Ac+Cry2Ab toxins initially provided effective control of bollworm infestations. However, over time, pink bollworm has developed significant resistance, not only to Bt toxins but also to several classes of chemical insecticides, posing a major challenge to effective management.
