# Luminescent Wearable Sensor on Anions from Cotton Fabric Grafted with Cu-In-Zn-S Colloidal Quantum Dots.

Negatively charged molecules and anions are widely present in the natural environment and can pose a threat to aquatic life, affecting their survival and reproduction. As the understanding of the hazards of negatively charged molecules and ions deepens, the need for real-time monitoring governs the development of highly sensitive and convenient sensing materials.
