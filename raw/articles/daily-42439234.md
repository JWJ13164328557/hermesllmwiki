# Chromosome-Level Genome Assembly of Tea Cultivar "Aijiaowulong" Elucidates the Molecular Mechanism of Osmanthus-like Aroma Formation.

Beiyuan Tribute Tea is produced mainly from the cultivar "Aijiaowulong" (AJWL), noted for its distinctive osmanthus-like aroma, yet its molecular basis remains unclear. We generated a chromosome-level AJWL genome (3.16 Gb; contig N50 130.03 Mb). Structural-variation analyses indicated AJWL is closer to Rougui (RG) and Tieguanyin (TGY) than to Shuchazao (SCZ), which shows clearer structural divergence.
