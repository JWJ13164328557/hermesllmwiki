# A circadian rheostat drives proton electrochemical gradients to optimize cell-type-specific growth in Arabidopsis.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Cell (2026)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.cell.2025.12.056](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.12.056)
- **PMID**: 41713419
- **第一作者**: Lu Xiong

## 摘要
Plant growth relies on the activity of key transcription factors. Here, we uncover a mechanism for organ-specific growth driven by opposing electrochemical signals that propagate in a cell-type-specific manner. Using a genetically encoded pH sensor and a pH-sensitive dye, we show that apoplastic pH in epidermal cells oscillates antiphasically relative to phloem pH. The clock component CCA1 lowers apoplastic pH in hypocotyl epidermal cells while increasing it in companion cells. This opposing regulation promotes hypocotyl growth but inhibits root elongation.
