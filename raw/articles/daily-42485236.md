# Genetically modified rice in Iran: scientific progress, policy constraints, and prospects for sustainable food security.

Iran's rice security faces acute threats from drought, salinity, and pests. A sustained national research program has developed genetically modified (GM) rice to address these challenges. This critical review synthesizes over 15 years of research, applying a systematic literature search and thematic analysis to map the trajectory from gene discovery to field trials.
