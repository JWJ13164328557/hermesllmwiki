# Integrating gene expression and morphological traits for drought stress adaptation in maize hybrids.

Maize, the third major crop in Pakistan, faces yield challenges due to climate changes agronomic environment, and genetic factors. There is an urgent need for high-yielding hybrid varieties to adapt to different agro-climactic conditions to meet the day-by-day increasing demand of maize.
