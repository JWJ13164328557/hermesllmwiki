STAGE OF TECHNICAL EFFICACY: Stage 1.

E‐cigarettes contribute to endothelial dysfunction, an early step in atherogenesis. This study used MRI to quantify and compare the acute vascular effects of tobacco cigarette smoking, nicotinized e‐cigarette vaping, and non‐nicotinized e‐cigarette vaping in cigarette smokers and e‐cigarette users. Participants underwent MRI examinations before and after smoking or vaping challenges. Both smoking and vaping significantly reduced resting femoral vein oxygen saturation and femoral artery blood velocity. These findings demonstrate the acute deleterious effects of smoking and vaping on MRI markers of vascular function.
