# The transcription factor LSD-one-like 2 binds to a novel identified cis-element LBE (GCCAAAAC) and regulates molecular response of Populus davidiana×P. bolleana to salt stress.

Salt stress is an adverse environmental factor limiting the distribution range of Shanxin poplar (Populus davidiana×P. bolleana). In our study, the LSD1 gene (PdbLOL2) in Shanxin poplar was identified and characterized, and its expression was markedly induced by salt. Compared with wild-type poplar, overexpression of PdbLOL2 exhibited a more sensitive phenotype to salt stress. Physiological measurements showed that PdbLOL2-OE decreased T-AOC (total antioxidant capacity) and ROS scavenging capacity, thus increasing the salt sensitivity of Shanxin poplar. TF-centered yeast one-hybrid (Y1H), GUS and EMSA assays revealed that PdbLOL2 specifically interacts with a newly identified cis-element LBE (GCCAAAAC). ChIP-PCR, qRT-PCR, EMSA and LUC assays confirmed that PdbLOL2 suppressed PdbWAT1 expression by binding to this element on its promoter. Further analysis revealed that overexpression of PdbWAT1 reduced cellular damage by increasing peroxidase (POD) activity and proline content, thereby enhancing Shanxin poplar salt tolerance. This study provided a theoretical foundation for further understanding the role of the LOL2 TF and breeding highly resistant plants through genetic engineering.
