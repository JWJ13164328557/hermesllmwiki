# Exploring wild rices for photosynthetic efficiency improvement in rice.

UNLABELLED: Photosynthesis is a key determinant for plant productivity, understanding its genetic basis will aid its improvement. The photosynthesis trait is primarily measured in terms of carbon and light utilization, using gas exchange and chlorophyll fluorescence methods. We report in this study, the extent of variability for photosynthetic trait in wild rice relatives, comprising a total of 26 taxa (22 wild species), using an infrared gas analyzer.
