# SlROP4 negatively regulates drought tolerance in tomato.

Rho-like GTPases of plants (ROPs) regulate diverse cellular processes, but their roles in tomato drought responses are largely unknown. In this study, nine ROP genes were identified in tomato (Solanum lycopersicum). Among them, SlROP4, the sole tomato ortholog of Arabidopsis AtROP6, was highly expressed, localized to the plasma membrane, and induced by drought stress.
