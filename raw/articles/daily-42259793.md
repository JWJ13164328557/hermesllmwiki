# Design and evolution of the tetracycline repressor into sulfonylurea herbicide-responsive gene switches for field crops.

Chemically inducible expression systems enable transgene expression regulation in response to external small molecules. Tetracycline repressor (TetR)-based gene switches work in plants, but antibiotics are neither approved nor advisable for crop use.
