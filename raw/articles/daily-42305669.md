# The impact of microbial colonization on cadmium adsorption by rice husk biochar: microorganism-dependent outcomes in bioretention systems.

INTRODUCTION: The use of microorganisms and biochar, individually, has been proposed as a promising strategy for the bioremediation of heavy metals in contaminated water bodies. However, the impact of their combined application, specifically through the immobilization of microorganisms on biochar, on the efficiency of cadmium (Cd) removal and the physicochemical properties of water has not yet been fully assessed, creating uncertainty regarding their effectiveness in decontamination processes.
