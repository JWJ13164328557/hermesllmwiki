# Optimizing Soil Health and Productivity in Intensive Rice-Wheat Systems Using Conjoint Application of Sewage Sludge.

This study assessed the long-term sustainability of integrating sewage sludge (SSL) with recommended (100%) or reduced (75%) doses of chemical fertilizers to enhance soil health, crop productivity, and reduce dependency on synthetic fertilizer inputs in the intensive rice-wheat cropping system (RWCS). A field experiment, established in 2010 and evaluated from 2017 to 2020 in Uttar Pradesh, India, employed nine nutrient management treatments in a randomized complete block design with three replications.
