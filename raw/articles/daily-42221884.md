# Pan-genome analysis of soybean terpene synthase identifies 

Soybean terpene synthase (TPS) genes are pivotal to ecological adaptation and stress resilience, yet their genetic diversity, evolutionary history, and enzymatic mechanisms remain poorly understood. A pan-genomic survey of 27 soybean genomes was performed to identify and classify TPS loci by conservation status.
