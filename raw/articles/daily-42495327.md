# From Simplified Pretreatment to Sensitive Analysis: An Immobilization-Free Cathodic Photoelectrochemical Assay for Sulfonylurea Herbicide Residue and Its Accumulation.

Sensitive and selective detection of agricultural chemicals in complex matrices necessitates streamlined workflows without performance loss. By combining the inherent high sensitivity of photoelectrochemical (PEC) analysis with the high selectivity of enzymatic recognition, an immobilization-free PEC assay for the sulfonylurea herbicide chlorsulfuron (Chs) was developed, with purification steps omitted and analytical procedure simplified.
