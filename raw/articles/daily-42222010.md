# Pyramiding 

UNLABELLED: Breeding dwarf or semi-dwarf soybean varieties is an effective strategy to enhance lodging resistance and thereby increase yield potential. However, soybean plant height is co-determined by the number of nodes and internode length. Reducing plant height often leads to a decrease in node number, which subsequently reduces pod number and ultimately results in lower yield.
