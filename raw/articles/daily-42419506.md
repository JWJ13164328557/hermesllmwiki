# Co-recovery of cellulose nanofibers and lignin from rice straw for high-performance reusable insulating aerogels.

Rice-straw-derived cellulose nanofiber/lignin (CNF/lignin) aerogels were developed as microwave-passive insulating cores for reusable food-container walls. Cellulose and lignin co-recovered from Vietnamese rice straw were reassembled into aerogels containing 0-20 wt% lignin to define a practical formulation window for microwave-reheatable packaging. Moderate lignin addition preserved the cellulose I framework and improved the balance between pore-wall integrity, moisture tolerance, thermal insulation, and dielectric stability.
