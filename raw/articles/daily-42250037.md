# Human health risk assessment from potentially toxic elements (PTEs) in wild aesthetic plants (Cosmos): PTE concentration and distribution.

Anthropogenic activities and fast industrial growth influence the level of PTEs in the soil and the food chain. The contents of PTEs were examined in two wild Cosmos species (C. bipinnatus and D. cosmos) from Jiangsu City, using chemical and AAS methods to determine the risk to human health.
