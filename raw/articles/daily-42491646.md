# Ternary composite amendments of sludge-humic acid-fertilizer for mineral mud paddy field utilization.

To address severe challenges posed by the contamination of mineral mud, a ternary composite comprising sludge, humic acid, and silica-calcium-magnesium (Si-Ca-Mg) fertilizer was developed. The system aims to achieve multi-dimensional paddy field utilization through synergistic mechanisms with mining mud. Field experiment results demonstrated that the amended mineral mud exhibited a pH increase of 9.46%-13.07% and significant improvements in nutrition, thus showing higher rice yields compared with the control group.
