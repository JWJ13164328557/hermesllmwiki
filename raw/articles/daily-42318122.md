# Geminivirus-derived replicons: assessment for transient GFP expression in tobacco and tomato.

Global food security requires innovative strategies for sustainable crop improvement. Gene editing offers a precise and rapid approach to plant modification, but its success depends on efficient delivery and robust expression systems.
