# Debranching and OSA esterification of waxy maize starch: effects on nanoparticle properties and emulsion performance.

Waxy maize starch (WMS) was modified sequentially via two pathways: pullulanase debranching followed by octenyl succinic anhydride (OSA) esterification or the reverse sequence (esterification followed by debranching) to obtain OSA-esterified debranched starch (OS-DBS, 305.45 nm) and debranched OSA-esterified starch (DB-OSS, 166.56 nm) nanoparticles. Debranching transformed the A-type crystalline structures of WMS and OSS into amorphous states.
