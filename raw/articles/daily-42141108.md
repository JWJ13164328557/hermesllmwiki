# Irrigation-nutrient synergy improves productivity, soil fertility, and input use efficiency in winter maize under Gangetic plains of India.

In the Gangetic plains of West Bengal, where rice-wheat is the predominant cropping system, appropriate irrigation and nutrient management can assist to introduce and raise the productivity and quality of winter maize. For maize cultivation to operate at its peak under the unique agro-climatic conditions, these two essential inputs must be standardized. Furthermore, the water and nutrient use efficiencies in winter maize cultivation at varying primary nutrients and irrigation levels are seldom investigated.
