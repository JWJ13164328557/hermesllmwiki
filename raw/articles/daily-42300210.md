# Impact of tomatoes and tomato-derived products on obesity and cardiometabolic health: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Tomatoes and tomato-based products are central components of the Mediterranean diet and have been associated with improved cardiometabolic health, but their effects on anthropometric parameters remain unclear. This review aimed to assess the potential effects of tomato-based interventions on obesity-related and cardiometabolic outcomes in individuals with overweight or obesity.
