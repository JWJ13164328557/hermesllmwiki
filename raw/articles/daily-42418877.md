# Study on the identification, generation mechanism and coloring effect of 5,7-dihydroxycoumarin formed from the autoxidation of EGCG.

Epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG) was the most abundant catechin in tea. Its autoxidation reaction could result in significant coloring effect. In this study, based on UV-vis and UHPLC-HRMS, compounds with m/z 741.1117, 583.0742, and 177.0183 were screened as potential chromogenic products of EGCG autoxidation reaction. Compound with m/z 177.0183 was identified as 5,7-dihydroxycoumarin. The formation pathway of 5,7-dihydroxycoumarin generated from EGCG might be through reactions of deprotonation, o-quinone intermediate formation, C-ring opening and intramolecular lactonization. The solution of 5,7-dihydroxycoumarin mainly presented yellow and green hue. High concentration, alkaline and heating conditions could significantly enhance the yellow hue of 5,7-dihydroxycoumarin. The content of 5,7-dihydroxycoumarin was higher in black tea. This study provides theoretical foundation for the processing and quality control of tea leaves and tea products.
