# TEA-modified anthill clay for tannery wastewater treatment: COD and BOD adsorption.

Existing wastewater treatment methods often struggle to achieve slow, ineffective removal of complex organics, such as biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) and chemical oxygen demand (COD), particularly in challenging industrial applications, such as tannery wastewater treatment. Triethanolamine (TEA)-functionalized anthill clay was prepared as an effective adsorbent for the removal of BOD and COD from tannery wastewater. Raw anthill clay was purified by sedimentation, then subjected to acid activation and TEA functionalization. X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis confirmed the retention of the aluminosilicate structure, while Fourier transform infrared (FTIR) spectra confirmed the incorporation of the amine functions through the formation of C-N and N-H bonds.
