# Tea polyphenol-soy lecithin-beeswax composite oleogelator for soybean oil oleogel: multiscale structural characterization and performance validation in bakery matrices.

BACKGROUND: Unsaturated fatty acids based oleogels could present higher health benefits than shortening in baked goods, but poor oxidation stability limits their application. Regrettably, natural antioxidants, such as polyphenols, cannot be dispersed directly in grease-based systems. Herein, the composite gelator (TP-SL-B) was self-assembled by emulsifying with tea polyphenol (TP), soy lecithin (SL), and beeswax (B).
