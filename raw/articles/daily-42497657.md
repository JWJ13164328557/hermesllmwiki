# The mechanism of pyrite passivation in bioretention systems: Electron donor termination and electron transfer inhibition.

Pyrite-based bioretention system hold considerable promise for nitrogen removal from stormwater runoff. However, long-term performance attenuation, caused by surface passivation of the filter media, remains a critical bottleneck for practical engineering applications. This study elucidated the formation mechanism of the passivation layer and its inhibition of denitrification through a six-month continuous operation experiment, comparing a pure pyrite system (PB) with a pyrite-wheat straw composite system (WPB).
