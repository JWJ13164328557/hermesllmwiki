# Functional Wet Noodles Based on Modified Cassava Flour and Pumpkin Flour: Physicochemical, Bioactive, Structural, and Starch Digestibility Characteristics.

The use of local food ingredients in noodle products is increasing due to their potential to improve nutritional value and functional properties. This study evaluated the effect of partially substituting wheat flour with modified cassava flour (mocaf) and pumpkin flour on the physicochemical characteristics, bioactive compound content, starch digestibility fractions, structural properties, and sensory acceptance of fresh noodles.
