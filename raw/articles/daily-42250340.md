# The anion channel SLAH3 regulates flowering time in Arabidopsis thaliana.

Flowering is a critical developmental event for plants. Here, we identify that the nitrate efflux channel SLOW ANION CHANNEL HOMOLOG 3 (SLAH3) regulates flowering time in Arabidopsis thaliana. The slah3-4 mutant exhibited early flowering and reduced rosette leaf number under both long-day and short-day conditions, while SLAH3 overexpression plants exhibited delayed flowering.
