# Paper Analysis Specification

Version: 1.0

Purpose:

Define the standard for transforming a scientific publication into structured scientific knowledge.

This specification applies to all papers ingested into the Plant Single-Cell Research Operating System.

---

# 1 Core Principle

A paper page is not a summary.

A paper page is a reconstruction of scientific reasoning.

The goal is not to answer:

```text
What did the authors do?
```

The goal is to answer:

```text
Why did they do it?

What evidence did they generate?

How convincing is the evidence?

How does it change existing knowledge?
```

---

# 2 Extraction Levels

Every paper must be processed through eight levels.

```text
Level 1
Metadata

Level 2
Scientific Context

Level 3
Research Questions

Level 4
Experimental Logic

Level 5
Figure Analysis

Level 6
Evidence Extraction

Level 7
Knowledge Extraction

Level 8
Research Insight
```

No level may be skipped.

# 4 Scientific Context

# 5 Research Question Analysis

Purpose:

Identify the true scientific problem.

---

## Primary Question

One sentence.

Example:

```text
Does QC contain previously unrecognized cell states?
```

---

## Secondary Questions

List all supporting questions.

---

## Explicit Hypotheses

Directly stated by authors.

---

## Implicit Hypotheses

Not stated explicitly.

Inferred from experimental design.

---

## Success Criteria

What result would support the hypothesis?

---

## Failure Criteria

What result would refute it?

# 6 Experimental Logic Reconstruction

Purpose:

Recover scientific reasoning.

---

Never write:

```text

Step 2
Step 3
```

Instead reconstruct:

```text
Question

↓

Experiment

↓

Observation

↓

Interpretation

↓

New Question

↓

Experiment

↓

Conclusion
```

### Question

---

---

### Expected Outcomes

---

### Actual Results

### Interpretation
