# METTL3-mediated m6A modification supports Tcrα recombination center activity and thymic T cell development.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Cell reports (2026)
- **PMID**: [43038103](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43038103/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118328](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118328)
- **第一作者**: Keyi Li
- **作者**: Keyi Li, Hongyuan Pan, Wei Xue, Junwei Luo, Yongchang Zhu, Yanhong Chen, Litao Qin, Guangfu Wang

## 摘要
N6-methyladenosine (m6A) is the most abundant internal RNA modification, and METTL3 is the catalytic core of the methyltransferase complex. Although m6A regulates peripheral T cell homeostasis and effector differentiation, its contribution to thymocyte development and T cell receptor (TCR) V(D)J recombination is unclear. Here, conditional Mettl3 knockout mice and multi-omic profiling reveal that METTL3 supports thymocyte development and Tcrα rearrangement. Mettl3 deficiency reduces double-positive (DP) thymocytes and mature T cell output, skewing the Tcrα repertoire toward proximal Jα usage at the expense of distal Jα segments. Mettl3-deficient DP thymocytes also show reduced Jα germline transcripts, diminished Eα-T cell early α (TEA) chromatin interactions, elevated dsRNA and interferon-associated programs, increased γH2A.X, and reduced viability. METTL3-dependent m6A marks Jα germline transcripts, and Mettl3 loss reduces Tcrα translational output and surface TCR-CD3 expression. These findings establish a role for METTL3-mediated m6A in Tcrα recombination center activity and thymic T cell development.
