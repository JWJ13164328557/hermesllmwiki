# In our study, by comparing the expression levels of polyadenylation factors under control …

## Claim
In our study, by comparing the expression levels of polyadenylation factors under control and salt stress conditions in Arabidopsis and Eutrema, we found that five polyadenylation factors in Arabidopsis changed significantly in their expression levels when responded to salt stress, whereas only two polyadenylation factors in Eutrema showed significant changes (Figure 7).
