# The MF_IIB_3 myonuclear subtype was enriched for the oxidative phosphorylation pathway …

## Claim
The MF_IIB_3 myonuclear subtype was enriched for the oxidative phosphorylation pathway, while its marker gene encodes a key glycolytic enzyme that catalyzes the reversible conversion of pyruvate to lactate (Sharma et al., 2022), suggesting that MF_ IIB_3 myonuclei in 5-week-old mice may suggest a concurrent upregulation of both glycolytic and oxidative metabolism machineries.
