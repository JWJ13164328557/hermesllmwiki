# Biocontrol potential of bacteriophage CAU_YEP3 against Yersinia enterocolitica O3 in food-contact surfaces and refrigerated food systems.

## 论文信息
- **期刊**: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.) (2026)
- **PMID**: [42967686](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42967686/)
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.foodres.2026.120780](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodres.2026.120780)
- **第一作者**: Ha Rim Lee
- **作者**: Ha Rim Lee, Sangha Han, Soo-Jin Jung, Chaeryeong Oh, Dongryeol Lee, Sang-Do Ha

## 摘要
Yersinia enterocolitica O3 is an emergent foodborne pathogen often isolated from cold-chain foods, posing a threat to public health. In this study, a lytic bacteriophage, CAU_YEP3, which specifically targets Y. enterocolitica O3, was isolated from environmental. Its adsorption, phage persistence, stability, and host range on Enterobacteriaceae were determined. Whole-genome sequencing revealed that phage CAU_YEP3 represents a new phage lineage that is unassigned to any recognized bacteriophage family. The phage region contains no virulence or antibiotic-resistance genes. Notably, CAU_YEP3 effectively disrupted biofilms on food-contact surfaces, reducing viable cell counts by 2.84, 1.29, and 1.69 log10 CFU/cm2 on stainless steel, silicone rubber, and low-density polyethylene, respectively. The antimicrobial efficacy of CAU_YEP3 was further examined in pork, carrot, and milk matrices under different storage temperatures. In pork, moderate reductions occurred under both temperature conditions, with slightly higher reductions at 4 °C (1.60, 1.72, and 1.93 log10 CFU/g) than at 25 °C (1.30, 1.42, and 1.55 log10 CFU/g) at multiplicity of infection (MOI) of 1, 10, and 100, respectively. In carrots, reductions reached 0.64, 0.98, and 1.11 log10 CFU/g at 4 °C, whereas lower reductions (0.92, 0.96, and 1.14 log10 CFU/g) occurred at 25 °C. The milk sample exhibited no significant reduction at 4 °C and 10 °C but demonstrated substantial reductions at 25 °C, reaching 2.53, 3.67, and 4.35 log10 CFU/mL at MOIs of 0.1, 1, and 10, respectively. These findings highlight CAU_YEP3 as a promising phage-based tool for controlling Y. enterocolitica O:3 contamination and improving food safety in cold-chain and food-processing environments.
