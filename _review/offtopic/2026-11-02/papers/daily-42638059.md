# [Effects of soybean fermented bioactive peptides on intestinal health of bullfrogs].

- **期刊**: Sheng wu gong cheng xue bao = Chinese journal of biotechnology (2026)
- **PMID**: [42638059](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42638059/)

## 摘要
To address the challenges of antibiotic overuse and high feed costs in bullfrog aquaculture, this study aimed to systematically evaluate the effects of soybean fermented bioactive peptides (SFP) as a functional feed additive on the growth performance and intestinal health of bullfrogs (Lithobates catesbeianus). A total of 450 healthy bullfrogs with an initial body weight of (100.0±2.0) g were randomly assigned into three groups: group C (basal diet), the 2% SFP group (basal diet supplemented with 2% SFP), and the 3% SFP group (basal diet supplemented with 3% SFP). The feeding trial lasted for 6 weeks. After the experiment, growth performance, intestinal morphology, digestive enzyme activities, relative mRNA expression levels of inflammatory cytokines, and expression levels of the tight junction protein ZO-1 were systematically measured. Additionally, the intestinal microbiota structure was analyzed using 16S rRNA gene high-throughput sequencing, and the content of short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) in the intestine was determined by gas chromatography-mass spectrometry (GC-MS). The results showed that compared with the control group, dietary SFP supplementation significantly promoted bullfrog growth and improved intestinal health. Among the treatments, the 2% SFP group exhibited the most prominent effects, with significantly higher weight gain rate and specific growth rate than those in the control group and the 3% SFP group (P0.05). The 2% SFP supplementation also significantly increased the villus height in the jejunum and ileum (P0.05), enhanced intestinal digestive enzyme activities, remarkably downregulated the mRNA expression levels of intestinal pro-inflammatory cytokines (P0.05), and upregulated the expression of the tight junction protein ZO-1. Further analysis of the intestinal microbiota revealed that 2% SFP optimized the microbial community structure by increasing the relative abundance of beneficial bacteria such as Clostridium and Lactococcus. Although the 3% SFP group exhibited an increased relative abundance of Bacteroides, its modulatory effect on the intestinal microbiota structure was inferior to that of the 2% SFP group. Consequently, the intestinal content of acetic acid and n-butyric acid was significantly higher in the 2% SFP group than in the 3% SFP group (P0.05). Therefore, these findings demonstrate that dietary supplementation with 2% SFP enhances the growth performance and intestinal health of bullfrogs by optimizing the intestinal microbiota composition, strengthening intestinal metabolic capacity, improving intestinal barrier integrity, and facilitating better digestion and absorption. This study establishes an effective feeding strategy for feed additives to promote healthy bullfrog cultivation, thereby facilitating the green and sustainable development of the bullfrog aquaculture industry.

为应对牛蛙养殖中存在的抗生素滥用及饲料成本压力等问题，本研究旨在系统评估生物发酵大豆活性肽(soybean fermented bioactive peptides, SFP)作为功能性饲料添加剂对牛蛙生长性能及肠道健康的调控作用。选取450只初始体重为(100.0±2.0) g的健康牛蛙，随机分为3组:C组(饲喂基础饲料)、2% SFP组(基础饲料中添加2% SFP)及3% SFP组(基础饲料中添加3% SFP)，进行为期6周的养殖实验。实验结束后，系统测定了牛蛙的生长指标、肠道组织形态、消化酶活性、炎症因子mRNA相对表达水平、紧密连接蛋白ZO-1的表达水平，并采用16S rRNA基因高通量测序技术分析了肠道菌群结构，采用气相色谱-质谱联用技术测定了肠道短链脂肪酸的含量。结果表明，与对照组相比，饲料中添加SFP能显著促进牛蛙生长并改善肠道健康。其中，2% SFP添加剂量的改善效果最为显著，该组牛蛙的增重率、特定生长率均显著高于对照组和3% SFP组(P0.05)。同时2% SFP能显著改善空肠和回肠的绒毛高度(P0.05)，提升肠道消化酶活性，显著下调肠道促炎症因子的基因表达(P0.05)，增强紧密连接蛋白ZO-1的表达。肠道微生物区系分析进一步揭示，2% SFP可优化菌群结构，提高梭菌属和乳球菌属等有益菌的相对丰度。3% SFP虽提高了拟杆菌属的相对丰度，但其对肠道菌群结构的优化效果不及2% SFP。2% SFP组肠道短链脂肪酸乙酸、丁酸的含量均显著高于3% SFP组(P0.05)。研究表明，饲料中添加2% SFP能通过优化肠道菌群结构，加强肠道代谢能力，增强肠道屏障完整性，改善肠道消化吸收功能，从而提升牛蛙的生长性能和肠道健康水平。本研究为牛蛙健康养殖提供了一种高效的饲料添加剂投喂方案，有助于推动牛蛙养殖产业的绿色可持续发展。.
